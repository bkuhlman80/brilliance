# Alternative Personality Subscales Reference
# Non-NEO-PI-R and non-BFAS subscale data extracted from domain files

This file catalogs all subscale-level data from BFI-2, HEXACO, IPIP variants, MPQ,
and other instruments found in the corpus. The purpose is to map these constructs
onto the NEO-PI-R facets and BFAS aspects that have been deeply characterized
in the Bright Triad hypothesis tests.

================================================================================
## 1. BFI-2 (Big Five Inventory-2)
================================================================================

Source: Soto & John (2017), XL structural quality
Design: 60 items, 15 facets (3 per domain), 5 domains
Convergence subsample: N=438 completed BFI-2 + NEO-PI-R + BFAS concurrently

### BFI-2 Facet List

EXTRAVERSION:
  - Sociability
  - Assertiveness
  - Energy Level

AGREEABLENESS:
  - Compassion
  - Respectfulness
  - Trust

CONSCIENTIOUSNESS:
  - Organization
  - Productiveness
  - Responsibility

NEUROTICISM (Negative Emotionality):
  - Anxiety
  - Depression
  - Emotional Volatility

OPENNESS (Open-Mindedness):
  - Aesthetic Sensitivity
  - Intellectual Curiosity
  - Creative Imagination

### BFI-2 Structural Findings

1. BFI-2 deliberately stripped anger/irritability from Neuroticism. Emotional
   Volatility emphasizes emotional lability without anger content.

2. BFI-2 Energy Level shows phenotypic cross-loadings on C (.23–.24) and is
   structurally a blend — not a pure E facet.

3. BFI-2 Assertiveness shows phenotypic cross-loadings on C (.23–.24) and O
   (.24–.24) across Swiss German, French, and Italian samples (Zakrisson 2025, L).

4. In Zakrisson (2025), Energy Level's between-domain correlation with
   C-Productiveness exceeded its within-E correlation with Assertiveness in 2 of
   3 Swedish language samples.

5. BFI-2 explicitly excluded: A5 Modesty, O6 Values, E5 Excitement-Seeking.
   O4 Actions content is not directly represented in BFI-2 Openness facets.

## 2. HEXACO
================================================================================

Sources: Ludeke et al. (2019), Ashton & Lee (2016), Lewis & Bates (2014, M)
Design: 6 domains × ~4 facets each = 24-25 facets

### HEXACO Domain-Level Mappings to BFAS Aspects

Source: Ludeke et al. (2019), N=1,586 across 4 samples

| HEXACO Domain | Primary BFAS Correlate | r | Secondary | r | R² from BFAS |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Honesty-Humility | Politeness | .51 | Compassion | lower | ~.40 |
| Emotionality | Withdrawal | .53 | Compassion | .38 | ~.42 |
| eXtraversion | Assertiveness + Enthusiasm | high | — | — | ~.45 |
| Agreeableness (vs. Anger) | Volatility (reversed) | -.61 | — | — | ~.42 |
| Conscientiousness | Industriousness + Orderliness | .48/.42 | — | — | ~.40 |
| Openness | Openness (BFAS) | .63 dominant | Intellect | .27 | ~.45 |

CRITICAL FINDING: BFAS aspects capture only R² = .40-.45 of HEXACO domain
variance. ~55-60% of HEXACO variance is NOT captured by Big Five aspects.

### HEXACO Key Structural Differences from Big Five

1. HEXACO Agreeableness ≠ Big Five Agreeableness.
   - HEXACO A is primarily Volatility-reversed (anger management, patience)
   - Big Five A is primarily Compassion + Politeness
   - HEXACO explicitly moved anger/irritability from N into A domain

2. HEXACO Emotionality ≠ Big Five Neuroticism.
   - HEXACO E splits off anger (→ A domain) and adds sentimentality
   - HEXACO E = Withdrawal + some Compassion content
   - NEO N = Withdrawal + Volatility (anger stays)

3. Honesty-Humility has no Big Five equivalent.
   - Best BFAS correlate is Politeness (.51) — but captures < half the variance
   - HH content: sincerity, fairness, greed avoidance, modesty

### HEXACO Facet List with NEO/BFAS Mappings

HONESTY-HUMILITY:
  - Sincerity (~A2 Straightforwardness content, but ethical emphasis)
  - Fairness (no clear NEO equivalent; anti-exploitation)
  - Greed Avoidance (no clear NEO equivalent; anti-materialism)
  - Modesty (~A5 Modesty, but more social equality emphasis)

EMOTIONALITY:
  - Fearfulness (~N1 Anxiety, but physical fear emphasis)
  - Anxiety (~N1 Anxiety, social/general worry)
  - Dependence (~N6 Vulnerability, interpersonal dependency)
  - Sentimentality (~A6 Tender-Mindedness; emotional bond emphasis)

EXTRAVERSION:
  - Social Self-Esteem (~reversed N4 Self-Consciousness)
  - Social Boldness (~E3 Assertiveness)
  - Sociability (~E2 Gregariousness)
  - Liveliness (~E4 Activity + E6 Positive Emotions blend)

AGREEABLENESS (vs. Anger):
  - Forgivingness (~reversed N2 Angry Hostility, holding grudges)
  - Gentleness (~A4 Compliance, non-harsh)
  - Flexibility (~A4 Compliance, willingness to compromise)
  - Patience (~reversed N2 Angry Hostility, temper control)

CONSCIENTIOUSNESS:
  - Organization (~C2 Order)
  - Diligence (~C4 Achievement Striving + C1 Competence)
  - Perfectionism (~C2 Order, high standards emphasis)
  - Prudence (~C6 Deliberation, careful decision-making)

OPENNESS:
  - Aesthetic Appreciation (~O2 Aesthetics)
  - Inquisitiveness (~O5 Ideas)
  - Creativity (~O1 Fantasy + "Creative Imagination" in BFI-2)
  - Unconventionality (~O6 Values + O4 Actions blend)

### HEXACO Developmental Trajectories (Ashton & Lee 2016)

Cross-sectional, N=97,583, ages 14-74

| HEXACO Facet | Trajectory | d-value | NEO equivalent trajectory comparison |
|---|---|---|---|
| Honesty-Humility (domain) | ~1.0 SD increase 18→60s | ~1.0 | No NEO domain equivalent; largest effect ever documented |
| Emotionality-Sentimentality | Increases ~0.33 SD teens→60 | ~0.33 | A6 Tender-Mindedness: increases (convex) — CONVERGENT |
| Emotionality-Anxiety | Decreases ~0.5 SD 20s→70s | ~-0.50 | N1 Anxiety: flat/slight increase — DIVERGENT |
| Emotionality-Fearfulness | Flat across lifespan | ~0.00 | N1 Anxiety (physical component): — |
| HEXACO Agreeableness (domain) | Nearly flat/stable | ~0.00 | N2 reversed: increases — PARTIALLY DIVERGENT |
| C-Perfectionism | Flatlines after teen increase | — | C2 Order: increase-then-flat — CONVERGENT |
| C-Prudence | Steep continued rise ~0.4 SD | ~0.40 | C6 Deliberation: monotonic increase — CONVERGENT |
| C-Organization | Moderate continued rise ~0.2 SD | ~0.20 | C2 Order: moderate increase — CONVERGENT |
| C-Diligence | Moderate continued rise ~0.2 SD | ~0.20 | C4 Achievement Striving: mixed/flat — DIVERGENT |

### HEXACO Genetic Data (Lewis & Bates 2014, M quality)

N=948 twin pairs, HEXACO-60 (2-3 items per facet, low reliability α ≈ .38-.74)
Design limitations: ~90% female, short scales, age M=61

Key finding: HEXACO C-Prudence shows genetically independent profile from other
C facets. Loaded on a unique genetic factor separate from Organization/Diligence.
Maps to C6 Deliberation (also genetically distinctive in NEO-PI-R data).

## 3. IPIP Variants
================================================================================

### IPIP-300 (Goldberg 1997)

Source: 2009 Facets and Wellbeing study; Soto & John 2017 Study 1
300 items, maps to NEO-PI-R 30-facet structure
Used as NEO-PI-R proxy in studies where NEO copyright prevented use

### IPIP-AB5C Conscientiousness Scales (9 facets)

Source: Jackson et al. (2009), Studies 1 & 2
9 scales for C domain specifically:
  Conscientiousness, Efficiency, Cautiousness, Dutifulness, Rationality,
  Purposefulness, Perfectionism, Organization, Orderliness

### Roberts et al. (2004) 5-Facet C Model (using IPIP/CAC items)

Alternative conscientiousness facet structure:
  - Industriousness
  - Orderliness
  - Impulse Control
  - Reliability
  - Conventionality

### IPIP-AB5C Developmental Trajectories (Jackson et al. 2009, M quality)

N=274 (Study 1, 2 samples: self + observer); N=613 (Study 2, representative)
Cross-sectional, ages 19-94, 3 age bins

| IPIP/Roberts Facet | Age Trajectory | d (self) | d (observer) | NEO equivalent |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Industriousness | Increases young→middle, plateaus | 0.42 | — | C1/C4 blend |
| Orderliness | Minimal change | 0.07 | 0.24 | C2 Order |
| Impulse Control | Steep monotonic increase | **0.88** | 0.59 | Reversed N5 Impulsiveness |
| Reliability | Increases across lifespan | 0.79 | 0.58 | C3 Dutifulness / A boundary |
| Conventionality | Flat young→mid; increases old | 0.38 | — | Partial O6 Values (reversed) |

Key finding: Impulse Control shows the LARGEST developmental effect (d=0.88) of
any C-domain facet — larger than Industriousness, Orderliness, or Reliability.
This maps to reversed N5 Impulsiveness, which the Bright Triad places in the
center triangle (v2).

### IPIP Goldberg Markers Stability Data (Seifert et al. 2023)

6 items per trait (reduced from 10), LISS panel Netherlands
Used for domain-level stability only; no facet-level data.

## 4. MPQ (Multidimensional Personality Questionnaire)
================================================================================

Source: Schwaba et al. (2022), used alongside NEO-PI-R
155-item short form, 11 primary facets

### MPQ Facet List with NEO/BFAS Mappings

| MPQ Facet | Big Five Domain | NEO-PI-R Equivalent | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Well-being | E | E6 Positive Emotions | Positive affect core |
| Social Potency | E | E3 Assertiveness | Dominance/leadership |
| Achievement | C | C4 Achievement Striving | Goal-directed effort |
| Social Closeness | E/A | E1 Warmth / A3 Altruism | Interpersonal warmth |
| Stress Reaction | N | N1 Anxiety / N6 Vulnerability | General distress |
| Alienation | N/A | N3 Depression + low A1 Trust | Mistrust + negativity |
| Aggression | N/A | N2 Angry Hostility | Physical/verbal aggression |
| Control | C | C6 Deliberation / C5 Self-Discipline | Impulse inhibition |
| Harm Avoidance | — | reversed E5 Excitement-Seeking | Physical risk avoidance |
| Traditionalism | — | partial O6 Values (reversed) | Conventional values |
| Absorption | O | O1 Fantasy + O3 Feelings blend | Imaginative engagement |

### MPQ Developmental Data (Schwaba et al. 2022)

From Figure 2 of Schwaba et al., ages 30-70:

| MPQ Facet | Trajectory | Matches NEO equivalent? |
|---|---|---|
| Social Potency | Steep decline (~-0.50 SD) | E3: increases midlife — DIVERGENT (tracks E4 instead) |
| Well-being | — | E6: stable/slight decline — — |
| Aggression | Declines | N2: declines — CONVERGENT |
| Achievement | — | C4: mixed — — |
| Control | — | C6: increases — — |
| Harm Avoidance | — | E5 reversed: E5 declines, so HA increases — — |
| Absorption | — | O1: declines — — |

Critical finding: MPQ Social Potency trajectory (steep decline) tracks E4 Activity
trajectory, NOT E3 Assertiveness (which increases through midlife). This suggests
Social Potency captures E4-like energy/vitality content despite its assertiveness
label.

### MPQ Comaturation Data (Schwaba et al. 2022)

Within-domain codevelopment correlations:
  N mean r_change = .33
  C mean r_change = .31
  A mean r_change = .21
  E mean r_change = .15
  O mean r_change = .15
  CMHS (Cook-Medley) mean r_change = .17

Note: These are from Figure 4 of Schwaba et al. and represent between-person
correlated change among NEO-PI-R + MPQ + CMHS facets jointly.

## 5. Cook-Medley Hostility Scale (CMHS)
================================================================================

Source: Schwaba et al. (2022)
50 items, 5 narrow facets, all classified as unhealthy/maladaptive

### CMHS Facets

| CMHS Facet | NEO-PI-R Equivalent | Content |
|---|---|---|
| Cynicism | Low A1 Trust | Suspicious, mistrustful worldview |
| Hostile Affect | N2 Angry Hostility | Irritability, resentment |
| Aggressive Responding | Low A4 Compliance | Antagonistic behavior |
| Hostile Attribution | Low A1 Trust + N2 | Interpreting others' motives negatively |
| Social Avoidance | Low E1 Warmth + low E2 | Interpersonal withdrawal |

Internal consistencies acceptable (ωt = .52-.82, α = .34-.79) except Social
Avoidance (α = .20) — very low internal consistency.

## 6. Other Instruments (Brief References)
================================================================================

### Original BFI (44 items, 10 facets)
- Soto & John (2011): N=1,267,218, ages 10-65
- 10 BFI facets (2 per domain: Assertiveness+Activity for E, etc.)
- Superseded by BFI-2

### Mini-Markers (Saucier 1994)
- 40 adjectives, 5 domains, no facets
- Mean r = .80 with BFI-2

### EPQ (Eysenck)
- 3 domains (E, N, Psychoticism)
- Used in meta-analyses (Briley & Tucker-Drob 2014; Roberts et al. 2006)
- Harmonized to Big Five for comparative analyses

### HiPIC, EAS, BIS/BAS, CBCL, 16PF, MMPI, CPI
- All mentioned in meta-analyses (Briley & Tucker-Drob 2014; Roberts et al. 2006)
- Coded to Big Five domains; no facet-level details in corpus
- Some mappings explicitly debatable (BIS/BAS as N/E; MPQ absorption as O)

### Roberts 5-Facet C Model (CAC)
- Industriousness, Orderliness, Impulse Control, Reliability, Conventionality
- Same structure as IPIP-AB5C analysis
- Observer-rated version available (Jackson et al. 2009)
- Facet trajectories closely parallel IPIP-AB5C results
