# Cross-Instrument Subscale Mapping — BFI-2 Anchored

## Purpose

Each row anchors on a BFI-2 facet and maps outward to NEO-PI-R, BFAS, HEXACO, MPQ, and IPIP/Roberts equivalents. Because BFI-2 has 15 facets (3 per domain) while NEO-PI-R has 30 (6 per domain), each BFI-2 facet typically absorbs content from 2+ NEO facets. This mapping shows what each BFI-2 facet captures, what it misses, and how it relates to the Bright Triad v2 zone structure.
