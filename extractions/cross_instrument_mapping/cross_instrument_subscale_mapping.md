# Cross-Instrument Subscale Mapping Inventory

## Purpose

Map subscales from BFI-2, HEXACO, IPIP/Roberts, and MPQ onto the deeply characterized NEO-PI-R facets and BFAS aspects. Each row anchors on a NEO-PI-R facet (with its v2 Bright Triad zone) and shows which subscales from other instruments capture similar construct space.

### The Honesty-Humility question

HEXACO's 6th factor (Honesty-Humility) has no Big Five equivalent. Its strongest BFAS correlate is Politeness (r=.51), capturing < half the variance. H-H content includes:
- Sincerity (≈A2 ethical component)
- Fairness (no NEO equivalent)
- Greed Avoidance (no NEO equivalent)
- Modesty (≈A5, relocated from A)

H-H shows the largest developmental effect ever documented (~1.0 SD increase, 18→60s). In the Bright Triad framework, H-H content is partially captured by:
- ES×E intersection (A2 Straightforwardness → Politeness aspect)
- Edge/outside (A5 Modesty)
- But Fairness and Greed Avoidance are NOT represented in any BT zone

This suggests the Bright Triad model (and the Big Five generally) has a blind spot for ethical/integrity content that HEXACO captures through its 6th factor.
