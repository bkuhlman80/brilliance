## **Structural Observations**

### **1\. The Internalizing/Externalizing divide is an Autonomy divide**

HiTOP’s central structural feature — the split between internalizing and externalizing spectra — maps onto the presence vs. absence of Autonomy in the triple intersections.

* **Internalizing** (Neurotic triple) \= the ONLY triple that LACKS Autonomy  
* **All three externalizing/antagonistic spectra** (Dominant, Impulsive, Deceptive) HAVE Autonomy

Prediction: primate gPFC lesion studies should selectively elevate externalizing without elevating internalizing, or the reverse. The internalizing/externalizing divide is not a factor-analytic convenience — it reflects a genuine architectural boundary at the System 2 (mentalizing) level.

### **2\. No primary extreme is a true disorder**

All four primary extremes produce recognizable personality profiles but not clinical disorders in the conventional sense:

| Primary | Extreme high | Social reception |
| ----- | ----- | ----- |
| Mastery | ASD / systemizer | Pathologized (social friction) |
| Boldness | Stunt man | Celebrated |
| Affiliation | Church lady | Valued in community |
| Autonomy | Hermit | Eccentric at worst |

ASD is only pathologized because extreme Mastery allocation to non-social domains creates friction in a species that treats social reciprocity as mandatory. The “disorder” is the mismatch between the individual’s allocation pattern and the species-typical social environment, not an intrinsic dysfunction. This gives the neurodiversity argument a structural basis rather than a purely political one.

Implication: all clinical pathology lives at intersections, triples, and the center. Primaries produce personality extremes that may or may not be socially functional depending on context.

### **3\. The DSM-5 collapsed two architecturally distinct conditions into “ASD”**

* High-functioning systemizer \= extreme high Mastery primary (not a disorder)  
* Nonverbal / profound support needs \= extreme low Open/Intellect (Au×B) — cognitive disorder

Same behavioral surface (restricted interests, social communication deficits), completely different architecture underneath. The Mastery-extreme *can* model social situations but doesn’t prioritize them. The cognitive-disorder person *cannot* model them with sufficient complexity.

### **4\. Mania and dopamine-deficiency anhedonia are exact architectural inverses**

Both sit on the Extraverted diagonal (Af×Au). Mania \= extreme high. Anhedonia (motivational) \= extreme low. Bipolar cycling \= oscillation between extreme high and extreme low on the same architectural region.

### **5\. BPD is an attachment disorder, not a Machiavellian one**

Borderline sits at extreme high Agreeable (M×Af), not at the Deceptive triple (M×Af×Au). The “manipulation” historically attributed to BPD in clinical literature is better understood as desperate attachment behavior (Af-driven) amplified by emotional dysregulation (M-driven), not calculated strategic deception (which requires Autonomy).

### **6\. Dissociation is self-directed deception**

The Deceptive triple’s clinical expression (dissociative disorders) and its normal-range trait expression (Machiavellianism) look phenomenologically opposite — one is self-fragmentation, the other is hyper-strategic self-presentation. The architectural link: both involve information management (Au×M \= self-monitoring diagonal) \+ social calibration (Af) without the Boldness to confront material directly. Machiavellianism directs the deception outward. Dissociation directs it inward. Same absent primary (B), same computational operation (hiding information from a target), different target (others vs. self).

### **7\. The covered diagonals have clinical relevance**

**Core Self-Evaluation / Sociometer (Af×B):** This diagonal is covered by Neurotic \+ Dominant \+ Worldly. CSE combines self-esteem, generalized self-efficacy, locus of control, and low neuroticism — it asks “am I valued AND capable in social space?” Social standing (Af) meets risk tolerance (B). Every trait touching this diagonal involves self-evaluation relative to others.

**Self-Monitoring (Au×M):** This diagonal is covered by Impulsive \+ Deceptive \+ Worldly. The capacity to model yourself as others model you and update your behavior based on that model. High \= strategic impression management. Low \= authenticity/transparency. Shame is the *failure mode* of this diagonal — the affective signal when self-monitoring detects a discrepancy between modeled-self and ideal-self.

Connection to NV/Shame/Toxicity work: shame as the failure mode of the Au×M diagonal explains why Negative Valence loads diffusely across personality disorders. Every PD involves some disruption of the self-monitoring channel.

## **Unplaced / Open Questions**

1. **Extreme low Agreeable (M×Af)** — no clinical assignment yet. Candidates: schizoid PD? Alexithymia?  
2. **Paraphilias** — placed with sexual dysfunction under Somatoform/extreme high Conscientious. But paraphilias might be better understood as extreme high Openness (novel object-relations) or as a Mastery-primary allocation pattern (non-normative reinforcement targets). Worth revisiting.  
3. **Where does the Deceptive triple’s clinical mapping (dissociative disorders) sit in HiTOP?** HiTOP doesn’t have a clean dissociative spectrum — dissociation cross-loads on Internalizing and Thought Disorder. This may be a genuine gap in HiTOP that the ring architecture exposes.  
4. **Severity gradient for ASD:** The architecture predicts that severity tracks how many intersections are suppressed by primary over-allocation, not just how extreme the primary is. Testable.  
5. **Is Machiavellianism a stable normal-range trait, or is it always a frequency-dependent strategy that fluctuates with population composition?** The architecture predicts both could be true — stable individual differences in the Deceptive triple, frequency-dependent fitness of deploying them.  
6. **Extreme high on primaries as a potential Trap placement:** The observation that no primary extreme is a “true disorder” connects to the Fingerprint Fallacy — the temptation to pathologize every deviation from the mean. Could be developed as a sub-point under Fingerprint in the Traps document.

## **Phylogenetic Prediction (from Worldliness §2.2)**

The Dark Triad should be specifically primate-level (System 2+) phenomena, because all three Dark Triad triples include Autonomy. Non-primate mammals lack Autonomy and cannot produce the three Autonomy-linked triples.

**Neuroticism — the only triple that doesn’t require Autonomy — should be observable in non-primate mammals.** This is testable against the animal personality literature.

Extension: the internalizing/externalizing distinction should not exist in non-primate mammals, because the externalizing spectra require Autonomy-linked triples that don’t exist at the endotherm level. Non-primate mammalian “personality disorders” (if observable) should all be internalizing-like or primary-extreme-like.
