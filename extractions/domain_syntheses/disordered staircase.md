# **Disorders by Staircase Level**

---

## **Principle**

A disorder can only exist at a phylogenetic level if the architectural region it occupies is available at that level. If Autonomy doesn’t exist until apes, then no Autonomy-linked region can produce pathology in mammals.

A secondary principle: **no primary extreme is a true disorder.** All four primary extremes produce recognizable personality profiles (systemizer, stunt man, church lady, hermit) but not clinical pathology. Disorders live at intersections, triples, and the center. This distinction matters at lower staircase levels where fewer intersections exist.

---

## **§0.2 Bilaterian — Boldness only**

**Available architecture:** One primary (Boldness). No intersections.

**No pathology at this level.** There’s no nervous system complexity to produce disorders. There are just two causes of death:

* Too bold → death by accident or predation  
* Not bold enough → starvation

These aren’t disorders. They’re the selection pressure that calibrates the single parameter. A bilaterian doesn’t have a “behavioral pathology” — it has a fitness consequence. The failure mode is death, not dysfunction.

---

## **§0.3 Vertebrate — Boldness \+ Mastery**

**Available architecture:** Two primaries (B, M). One pairwise intersection: Conscientious (B×M).

**Possible pathology:**

| Region | Extreme high | Extreme low |
| ----- | ----- | ----- |
| Boldness (primary) | Reckless approach | Frozen avoidance |
| Mastery (primary) | Stereotypy / perseveration (proto-ASD) | Failure to learn from reinforcement |
| **Conscientious (B×M)** | **Proto-somatoform: rigid over-monitoring of interoceptive signals with compulsive behavioral response** | **Proto-ADHD: failure to sustain goal-directed behavior across reinforcement delays** |

**Not possible:** Anything involving Affiliation or Autonomy. No attachment disorders, no social pathology, no externalizing/internalizing distinction, no Dark Triad, no mania, no borderline.

**Character of vertebrate pathology:** The first intersection-based disorder appears: Conscientious pathology. A fish can be “too Conscientious” (rigid, perseverative, over-responsive to interoceptive signals) or “not Conscientious enough” (unable to sustain effort toward delayed rewards). But there’s no social dimension yet — all pathology is about the organism’s relationship with its own behavior and its environment, not with conspecifics.

**Testable prediction:** Stereotypy in captive fish and reptiles (repetitive swimming patterns, glass-surfing) should be the dominant form of behavioral pathology, mapping onto extreme high Mastery and/or extreme high Conscientious. Learned helplessness paradigms in fish should map onto extreme low Conscientious.

## **§1 Endotherm — Boldness \+ Mastery \+ Affiliation**

**Available architecture:** Three primaries (B, M, Af). Three pairwise intersections: Conscientious (B×M), Dominant (B×Af), Agreeable (M×Af). One triple: Neurotic (B×M×Af). No center (requires four primaries).

**Critical note from the doc:** At this level, Dominant is a **pairwise intersection** (B×Af), not a triple. It becomes a triple only when Autonomy arrives at the ape level and inserts between B and Af, breaking their adjacency. Endotherm Dominant \= approach tendency in a social/bonded context. Ape Dominant \= narcissism (absent Mastery). These are structurally different.

**Possible pathology:**

| Region | Extreme high | Extreme low |
| ----- | ----- | ----- |
| Boldness (primary) | Reckless approach | Frozen avoidance |
| Mastery (primary) | Stereotypy / perseveration | Failure to learn |
| Affiliation (primary) | Dependent attachment (the “church lady” extreme, now in an attachment-capable organism) | Social avoidance / failure to bond |
| **Conscientious (B×M)** | **Somatoform-like: rigid interoceptive monitoring** | **ADHD-like: failure to sustain goal-pursuit** |
| **Dominant (B×Af)** | **Social aggression: approach tendency \+ attachment \= bullying, dominance escalation** | **Social submission: avoidance \+ bonding need \= anxious attachment** |
| **Agreeable (M×Af)** | **Borderline-like: abandonment sensitivity \+ emotional dysregulation** | **Proto-ASD-social: competence without social calibration** |
| **Neurotic (B×M×Af)** | **Internalizing: coordination failure of all three systems \= generalized distress, fear, dysregulated eating** | — |

**Not possible:** Anything requiring Autonomy. No Dark Triad. No mania (requires Af×Au). No thought disorder / detachment (requires Au×B). No dissociative disorders (requires M×Af×Au). No Worldly center, therefore no p-factor in its full form.

**The big structural observation: no externalizing spectrum.** The internalizing/externalizing distinction doesn’t exist at this level because externalizing requires Autonomy-linked triples, and Autonomy hasn’t arrived yet. Endotherm pathology is either primary-extreme, pairwise-intersection, or Neurotic (triple) — and Neurotic IS internalizing. There is no structural location for externalizing pathology at this level.

**Prediction:** Non-primate mammalian behavioral pathology should be overwhelmingly internalizing-like: anxiety, stereotypy, learned helplessness, separation distress, compulsive behaviors. Genuine “externalizing” in non-primate mammals (e.g., redirected aggression in dogs) should be better understood as extreme Dominant (pairwise B×Af \= social aggression) rather than as a disinhibited externalizing spectrum. The difference matters: endotherm Dominant is reactive social aggression (hot), not strategic exploitation (cold). Dogs bite because they’re afraid or dominant, not because they’re Machiavellian.

**Testable against veterinary behavioral medicine:** The most common behavioral diagnoses in dogs and cats are separation anxiety, compulsive disorders, noise phobia, and aggression. These map cleanly onto: Neurotic (separation anxiety, noise phobia), extreme high Conscientious (compulsive disorders), and extreme high Dominant-pairwise (aggression). Notably absent from veterinary medicine: anything resembling narcissism, psychopathy, Machiavellianism, or mania.

## **§2 Ape — Boldness \+ Mastery \+ Affiliation \+ Autonomy**

**Available architecture:** Four primaries. Four pairwise intersections (ring-adjacent). Four triples. Domesticated center.

**Autonomy’s arrival transforms existing architecture:**

* Dominant (was B×Af pairwise) → absorbs Autonomy, becomes B×Af×Au triple (absent: Mastery) \= narcissism  
* New pairwise: Extraverted (Af×Au), Open (Au×B)  
* New triples: Impulsive (B×M×Au, absent Af), Deceptive (M×Af×Au, absent B)  
* New center: Domesticated (all four coordinated)

**Covered diagonals become structurally available:**

* Core Self-Evaluation (Af×B) — covered by Neurotic \+ Dominant \+ Worldly  
* Strategic Self-Concept (Au×M) — covered by Impulsive \+ Deceptive \+ Worldly

**New pathology that becomes possible at this level:**

| Region | Extreme high | Extreme low |
| ----- | ----- | ----- |
| Autonomy (primary) | Hermit / extreme self-sufficiency | — |
| **Extraverted (Af×Au)** | **Mania** | **Dopamine-deficiency anhedonia (motivational)** |
| **Open (Au×B)** | **Thought disorder, detachment** | **Cognitive disorder (intellectual disability)** |
| **Dominant triple (B×Af×Au, –M)** | **Narcissism / antagonistic externalizing** | — |
| **Impulsive triple (B×M×Au, –Af)** | **Psychopathy, antisocial PD, substance use** | — |
| **Deceptive triple (M×Af×Au, –B)** | **Dissociative disorders** | — |
| **Domesticated center** | — | **Proto-p-factor (failure of four-system coordination)** |

**Everything from the endotherm level persists,** plus:

* The externalizing spectrum appears for the first time (all three Autonomy-linked triples)  
* The internalizing/externalizing distinction now exists as an Autonomy boundary  
* The Dark Triad is possible for the first time  
* Mania and its inverse (anhedonia) become possible  
* Thought disorder becomes possible  
* Dissociative disorders become possible  
* The p-factor (general psychopathology) becomes possible as failure of the four-system center

**What changes vs. endotherm level:**

* Dominant transforms from reactive social aggression (pairwise, hot) to narcissistic exploitation (triple, cold). Same label, different architecture, different pathology.  
* Neurotic persists unchanged (B×M×Af, absent Au) — it was already complete at the endotherm level. Neurotic is the architecturally oldest triple, the only one that predates Autonomy.

**Prediction for primate behavioral pathology:** Great apes in captivity should show the full range of pathology, including externalizing-like behaviors (aggression \+ social manipulation, not just reactive dominance), depression/anhedonia (low Extraverted), stereotypy (Mastery-extreme), and social withdrawal (Detachment/high Open). The critical test: do captive primates show behaviors that look like narcissistic manipulation (Dominant triple) vs. merely reactive aggression (endotherm Dominant pairwise)? If yes, the ape-level transformation of Dominant from pairwise to triple has a behavioral signature.

## **§3 Human — Same topology, new medium**

**Available architecture:** Same as ape. System 3 (symbolic compression) doesn’t add a fifth circle. It transforms the existing architecture by changing the medium of coordination.

**Key transitions:**

* Open → Open/Intellect (adds the Intellect aspect via symbolic cognition)  
* Domesticated → Worldly (generational representativeness via symbolic cultural transmission)

**All ape-level disorders persist,** plus:

* **Worldly center replaces Domesticated center.** The p-factor at the human level is failure to coordinate all four primaries *within a culturally transmitted symbolic package*. This is a richer failure mode than the ape’s Domesticated failure, because the human can fail both at basic four-system coordination (Domesticated failure, shared with apes) AND at cultural calibration (Worldly failure, uniquely human).  
* **Open/Intellect’s Intellect aspect** means human thought disorder includes symbolic-cognitive disruption (word salad, loose associations between abstract concepts, referential delusions involving symbolic content) not just sensory-perceptual disruption.  
* **Extreme low Open/Intellect** at the human level \= cognitive disorder that includes symbolic processing deficits (language delays, reading difficulties, abstract reasoning failures) not just perceptual-motor integration failures.  
* **The Fingerprint Fallacy:** Pathology gets *labeled and reified* at the human level. This is unique. Apes have behavioral pathology but no DSM codes. Humans taxonomize their disorders, which creates a new failure mode: treating the label as the mechanism (the Fingerprint trap from the Traps doc).

**Uniquely human elaborations of existing disorders:**

* Neurotic (internalizing) gains symbolic rumination — the ability to suffer about abstract future scenarios, counterfactual pasts, and imagined social judgments. Ape internalizing is about present threats and attachment disruptions. Human internalizing is about symbolic representations of threats that may never materialize.  
* Dissociative disorders gain narrative fragmentation — the self being “deceived” can be a linguistically constructed narrative identity, not just an embodied behavioral pattern.  
* Mania gains grandiose ideation expressed in symbolic content — messianic delusions, business empire fantasies, creative output in language/writing/art.

## **Structural Observations**

### **1\. The externalizing spectrum is an ape invention**

No externalizing pathology exists before Autonomy arrives. The internalizing/externalizing distinction is a System 2 phenomenon. Non-primate mammals can suffer (Neurotic triple) but cannot exploit (Dark Triad triples). This is the strongest phylogenetic prediction in the mapping.

### **2\. Neuroticism is the oldest triple — and the only one conserved across all attachment-capable organisms**

Neurotic (B×M×Af, absent Au) exists from the endotherm level onward. It’s the only triple that doesn’t require Autonomy. Every warm-blooded animal can be neurotic. Only apes and humans can be narcissistic, psychopathic, or Machiavellian.

### **3\. Dominant transforms across levels**

* Endotherm: Dominant \= B×Af pairwise \= reactive social aggression (hot, approach-oriented, unsophisticated)  
* Ape: Dominant \= B×Af×Au triple (–M) \= narcissistic exploitation (cold, strategic, reputation-obsessed)

Same label, different architecture, different pathology. An aggressive dog and a narcissistic chimpanzee are both “dominant” but the mechanisms are structurally different. The dog’s dominance is a pairwise coordination of approach \+ social bonding. The chimp’s dominance adds Autonomy (strategic self-governance) and loses Mastery (can’t learn from feedback).

### **4\. Disorder complexity increases with staircase level**

* Bilaterian: 2 possible pathologies (one parameter, two extremes)  
* Vertebrate: 4 possible pathologies (two primaries \+ one intersection, high/low)  
* Endotherm: \~10 possible pathologies (three primaries \+ three pairwise \+ one triple)  
* Ape: \~17+ possible pathologies (four primaries \+ four pairwise \+ four triples \+ center)  
* Human: same count as ape, but each disorder gains symbolic elaboration

The architecture predicts that more complex brains don’t just get new disorders — they get exponentially more *ways* to be disordered. Each new primary doesn’t just add one; it multiplies the existing structure.

### **5\. The veterinary medicine test**

If the mapping is right, then veterinary behavioral medicine for non-primate mammals should show:

* Abundant internalizing-like pathology (separation anxiety, compulsive disorders, phobias) → maps to Neurotic triple \+ high Conscientious  
* Reactive aggression → maps to endotherm Dominant pairwise (B×Af)  
* Stereotypy → maps to extreme Mastery primary  
* **Absence of:** narcissistic manipulation, strategic deception, mania, thought disorder, dissociation, substance-seeking-as-strategy  
* Any behavior that *looks* externalizing in a non-primate mammal should, on closer examination, be reactive (hot) rather than strategic (cold)

### **6\. The captive primate test**

If the ape-level transformation is real, then captive great apes should show qualitatively different pathology from captive non-primate mammals:

* Social manipulation that goes beyond reactive aggression (Dominant triple vs. pairwise)  
* Anhedonia / social withdrawal that looks like depression (low Extraverted \= low Af×Au)  
* Possible dissociative-like fragmentation under extreme stress (Deceptive triple)  
* The critical observation: do captive chimps show behaviors interpretable as narcissistic (demands admiration, can’t update self-model from feedback) vs. merely aggressive?
