# **Origin of life 2010–2026: a research-grade reconnaissance**

The field has moved decisively from a landscape of rival monolithic theories toward a polycentric, experimentally grounded framework in which **LUCA is a relatively complex \~4.2 Ga H₂/CO₂-autotroph with a \~2.5 Mb genome** (Moody et al. 2024), **nonenzymatic RNA replication is mechanistically within reach but not yet closed-loop** (Szostak/Holliger 2021–2025), **geochemically plausible non-enzymatic analogs of Wood–Ljungdahl, rTCA and glycolysis now run in the laboratory** (Preiner 2020; Muchowska 2019; Keller 2014–2017), and **compartmentalization has splintered into three partially compatible programs** (fatty-acid vesicles, alkaline-vent mineral pores, peptide/RNA coacervates). The sharpest unresolved axis is venue — alkaline submarine vents vs. terrestrial geothermal hot springs — because it dictates which chemistry counts as prebiotic. The sharpest methodological development is the integration of systems chemistry (Sutherland, Krishnamurthy, Powner) with phylogenomic bioenergetics (Martin, Lane) and with ribozyme engineering (Holliger, Joyce), to the point where "RNA-first vs metabolism-first" is, in 2026, a minority framing among practitioners; most working programs are **co-emergence programs** that differ in which subsystem they privilege.

What follows is organised as the user requested: landscape → catalog → shared problems → convergence map → active disagreements → methodology. DOIs are given where the research agents verified them; items flagged "unverified" should be checked before citation.

## **1\. Landscape: how the field currently divides itself**

The taxonomic axes below do **not** decompose into independent bits; most living research programs occupy a cell in a six-dimensional lattice rather than a single box.

**Autotrophy vs heterotrophy at origin.** The **majority view among phylogenomically anchored workers** (Martin, Lane, Sousa, Moran, Russell, Preiner, and consistent with Weiss 2016 and Moody 2024\) is autotrophic: LUCA fixed CO₂ with H₂ via a proto-Wood–Ljungdahl pathway. A **substantial minority** (Bada/Cleaves, some prebiotic chemists, Sutherland's systems-chemistry tradition) treats the origin as heterotrophic on abiotically produced organics, and Ryugu/Bennu returns (2023–2025) have strengthened the ingredient-rich primordial-soup framing. The distinction has softened: most autotrophic-origin proponents now accept meteoritic and spark-discharge contributions to the secondary inventory, and most heterotrophic-origin proponents accept early Fe-catalysed redox chemistry.

**Surface vs submarine.** The Russell/Martin/Lane alkaline-vent programme competes with the Deamer/Damer hot-spring programme and Mulkidjanian's terrestrial K⁺/Zn/Mn geothermal-pond model. This is **the field's most publicly contested axis**, essentially unresolved: both camps have strong geochemical arguments and laboratory chemistry, and both have weaknesses the other exploits (vents: dilution, UV-free but polymer-unfriendly; ponds: wet–dry cycling works but no continuous redox drive, more problematic for chemiosmotic origins).

**Genetics-first vs metabolism-first vs co-emergence.** The classical Gilbert/Cech "RNA first" framing is no longer the dominant formulation, even among RNA-world researchers. Sutherland's 2015 *Nat Chem* synthesis (common cyanosulfidic precursors for ribonucleotides, amino acids and lipids) and the Carter–Wills bidirectional-coding work reposition the field around **co-emergence**. "Metabolism-first" in the strict Morowitz/Wächtershäuser sense (information is late) is a **minority position**; "metabolism-early" in the sense of Russell/Martin/Preiner (proto-metabolism precedes genetically encoded catalysts but co-exists with informational polymers) is closer to the working consensus.

**Hot start vs cold start.** Hot (≥70 °C, thermophilic LUCA) is consistent with Weiss 2016 and most vent models. Cold-adapted ribozyme chemistry, enabled by Attwater/Holliger's in-ice evolution of the "Z" RPR and down to –19 °C RNA synthesis, keeps a serious cold-start/eutectic line alive. Holliger's 2025 *Nat Chem* open-ended replication uses freeze–thaw cycling as the essential compartment/denaturation mechanism. The field has converged on the view that **cycling** (temperature, wet/dry, pH) matters more than mean temperature.

**Membrane-first vs mineral-pore vs coacervate.** Three living programmes coexist, and the most interesting 2022–2026 moves are **hybrids**: Tang-Mann membranized coacervates; Agrawal/Szostak 2024 rainwater-stabilised coacervates (Szostak's partial concession to LLPS); Jordan/Lane mixed SCA \+ isoprenoid vesicles that form in hot alkaline seawater.

**Continuous vs cycling environments.** Essentially every program now invokes environmental cycling — wet/dry (Deamer, Damer, Hud, Ross, Keating 2020), freeze/thaw (Holliger, Mutschler), pH oscillation (Möller/Braun microfluidics), day/night UV cycling (Sutherland, Pearce). Pure steady-state vent models (early Russell) are now rare; the Lane programme incorporates tidal and redox oscillations.

## **2\. Catalog of major theories**

### **2.1 Alkaline hydrothermal vent / iron–sulfur / metabolism-early (Russell–Martin–Lane)**

**Principal proponents.** Michael Russell (JPL, retired, originator 1989/1994/1997); William F. Martin (Düsseldorf); Nick Lane (UCL); Wolfgang Nitschke (CNRS Marseille); Elbert Branscomb (Illinois/NASA); Filipa Sousa (Vienna); Victor Sojo; Martina Preiner (MPI); Barry Herschy; Eloi Camprubí; Sean Jordan; Laura Barge (JPL).

**Key publications.**

* Martin & Russell 2003 *Phil Trans B* 358:59, 10.1098/rstb.2002.1183.  
* Martin, Baross, Kelley, Russell 2008 *Nat Rev Microbiol* 6:805, 10.1038/nrmicro1991.  
* Lane, Allen, Martin 2010 *BioEssays* 32:271, 10.1002/bies.200900131.  
* **Lane & Martin 2012 *Cell* 151:1406, 10.1016/j.cell.2012.11.050** — the canonical statement that LUCA had leaky membranes and used the natural geochemical proton gradient.  
* Sojo, Pomiankowski, Lane 2014 *PLoS Biol* 12:e1001926, 10.1371/journal.pbio.1001926 — bacteria/archaea lipid divide from bioenergetic transition.  
* Herschy et al. 2014 *J Mol Evol* 79:213, 10.1007/s00239-014-9658-4 — vent reactor, CO₂→formate/formaldehyde via Fe(Ni)S barriers.  
* Sojo, Herschy, Whicher, Camprubí, Lane 2016 *Astrobiology* 16:181, 10.1089/ast.2015.1406.  
* Branscomb & Russell 2013 *BBA Bioenerg* 1827:62, 10.1016/j.bbabio.2012.10.003 ("turnstiles and bifurcators").  
* Branscomb & Russell 2018 *BioEssays* 40:e1700179/e1700182, 10.1002/bies.201700179.  
* **Preiner et al. 2020 *Nat Ecol Evol* 4:534, 10.1038/s41559-020-1125-6** — H₂+CO₂ over greigite/magnetite/awaruite yields formate (up to 200 mM), acetate, pyruvate, methanol, methane non-enzymatically; the strongest experimental win for the programme.  
* Jordan et al. 2019 *Nat Ecol Evol* 3:1705, 10.1038/s41559-019-1015-y — mixed C10–C15 fatty acids \+ alcohols vesiculate at \~70 °C, alkaline pH, seawater ionic strength, answering the headline vesicle-stability objection.  
* Beyazay et al. 2023 *Nat Commun* 14:570, 10.1038/s41467-023-36088-w — ambient-T CO₂→pyruvate→citramalate on FeNi nanoparticles.  
* Wimmer et al. 2021 *Front Microbiol* 12:793664, 10.3389/fmicb.2021.793664 — 402-reaction autotrophic core is \~97% exergonic under serpentinizing conditions.

**Central claim.** Life originated within the micrometer mineral-pore network of Hadean serpentinite-hosted alkaline vents: thin Fe(Ni)S walls separating alkaline (pH 9–11) H₂-rich vent fluid from mildly acidic Hadean seawater functioned as natural analogs of chemiosmotic membranes; the natural proton-motive force and Fe–Ni–S surface catalysis drove a proto-Wood–Ljungdahl chemistry before genetic encoding.

**Strongest support.** (i) *Phylogenomic* — Weiss 2016 and Moody 2024 depict LUCA as an anaerobic H₂-dependent autotroph with Wood–Ljungdahl and Fe–S cofactors. (ii) *Geochemical* — Lost City (discovered 2000; long-lived, pH 9–11, \~70–90 °C) proves the geological setting existed. (iii) *Universal biochemistry* — no extant cell escapes chemiosmotic coupling; ATP synthase is universally conserved. (iv) *Laboratory* — Preiner 2020, Varma 2018, Beyazay 2023 have now closed several rungs of CO₂→acetyl-CoA→pyruvate chemistry under vent-like conditions. (v) *Thermodynamic* — Wimmer 2021 shows biosynthetic reactions are overwhelmingly exergonic in the serpentinizing regime.

**Primary criticisms.** (i) Jackson 2016 *J Mol Evol* 83:1 (10.1007/s00239-016-9756-6) and subsequent follow-ups argue inorganic barriers are too porous to hold proton gradients and that H₂/CO₂ reduction is thermodynamically disfavoured without electron bifurcation. (ii) Sutherland/Krishnamurthy prebiotic-chemistry objection: no demonstrated clean synthesis of ribonucleotides in vent conditions. (iii) Deamer/Damer: seawater ionic strength and absence of wet–dry cycling obstruct both vesicle formation and condensation polymerisation. (iv) The concentration problem — vent flow disperses products.

**Standing.** Majority view among biochemistry/microbiology-rooted origins workers; near-consensus for the *bioenergetic* framing (leaky membranes; universal ATP synthase ancestral; Lane–Martin 2012 is now standard even among critics). The *locational* claim (marine alkaline vent as *the* site) is a contested but well-supported minority-to-majority position. Icy-moon astrobiology (Enceladus, Europa) has strengthened interest since 2020\.

### **2.2 RNA world and post-Szostak refinements**

**Principal proponents.** Jack Szostak (Harvard → Chicago 2022); Gerald Joyce (Scripps → Salk); Philipp Holliger (MRC-LMB); David Bartel (Whitehead); Niles Lehman; Paul Higgs (McMaster); Aaron Engelhart; Lijun Zhou; Seohyun Chris Kim; Peter Unrau.

**Key publications.**

* Higgs & Lehman 2015 *Nat Rev Genet* 16:7 — synthesis review.  
* Walton & Szostak 2016 *JACS* 138:11996, 10.1021/jacs.6b07977 — imidazolium-bridged dinucleotide as the actual reactive intermediate in primer extension.  
* Li, Prywes, Tam, O'Flaherty, Lelyveld, Izgu, Pal, Szostak 2017 *JACS* 139:1810, 10.1021/jacs.6b13148 — 2-aminoimidazole (2-AI) activation; order-of-magnitude kinetic improvement and prebiotic plausibility link to 2-aminooxazole.  
* Adamala & Szostak 2013 *Science* 342:1098, 10.1126/science.1241888 — Mg²⁺/citrate solution enabling nonenzymatic copying inside fatty-acid vesicles.  
* Zhou, Ding, Szostak 2021 *RNA* 27:1, 10.1261/rna.077693.120 — virtual circular genome (VCG) model.  
* Ding, Zhou, Mittal, Szostak 2023 *JACS* 145:7504, 10.1021/jacs.3c00612 — experimental test of a 12-nt VCG.  
* Duzdevich et al. 2024 *NAR* 52:13515 — overcoming sequence bias.  
* Wochner, Attwater, Coulson, Holliger 2011 *Science* 332:209 — "Z" RPR transcribes an active ribozyme.  
* Attwater, Wochner, Holliger 2013 *Nat Chem* 5:1011 — in-ice RPR extends to 206 nt (longer than itself).  
* Attwater et al. 2018 *eLife* 7:e35255 — heterodimeric triplet polymerase ribozyme.  
* **Attwater, Augustin, Curran, Kwok, Ohlendorf, Gianni, Holliger 2025 *Nat Chem* 17:1129** — open-ended exponential RNA replication by TPR under pH/freeze–thaw cycles with triplet substrates.  
* **Gianni, Kwok, Wan, de Goeij, Clifton, Colizzi, Attwater, Holliger 2025/2026 *Science*, 10.1126/science.adt2760** — QT45, a 45-nt polymerase ribozyme from random pools that synthesizes itself and its complement.  
* Joyce cross-chiral: Sczepanski & Joyce 2014 *Nature* 515:440; Tjhung, Shokhirev, Horning, Joyce 2020 *PNAS* 117:2906 — RPR synthesises its own ancestor; Bare & Joyce 2021 *JACS* 143:19160 — cross-chiral exponential amplification.

**Central claim.** An informational-catalytic RNA regime preceded the DNA–protein world; ribozymes performed the key catalytic roles including their own replication; the modern apparatus evolved by take-over (proteins for catalysis, DNA for storage) with the ribosome as the surviving core.

**Strongest support.** (i) *Laboratory chemistry* — nonenzymatic template-directed RNA primer extension with 2-AI activation now operates at plausible rates and prebiotic concentrations; vesicle-encapsulated copying works; VCG circumvents the need for long-template fidelity. (ii) *In vitro evolution* — RPR lineage has gone from Bartel class-I ligase → Wochner Z ribozyme → Attwater TPR → Gianni QT45 self-replicator in 15 years; full sequence-general self-replication is no longer a philosophical matter but an engineering one. (iii) *Phylogenetic* — ribosome is an RNA machine, peptidyl-transferase centre is ribozymal, cofactors are RNA fossils (NAD, FAD, CoA, SAM).

**Primary criticisms/open problems.** (i) *Strand separation* — even when copying works, prebiotic denaturation of stable duplexes remains unsolved one-pot; freeze-thaw and thermal cycling are Holliger's current workaround. (ii) *Fidelity ceiling* — Eigen error threshold is still the shadow constraint; VCG sidesteps but does not eliminate it. (iii) *Prebiotic plausibility of ribose-phosphate* — Sutherland/Powner solved pyrimidines (Powner 2009 *Nature* 459:239) but purines remained harder; Xu et al. 2020 *Nature* 582:60 gave a selective prebiotic route. Ribose stability answered partly by borate (Ricardo/Benner 2004\) and by the Furukawa lab 2022–2026. (iv) *Mg²⁺ paradox* — pure fatty-acid vesicles are destroyed by the Mg²⁺ needed for RNA chemistry; citrate is not prebiotic; peptide/amino-acid chelators are candidates. (v) No one has yet demonstrated continuous, closed-loop, sequence-general nonenzymatic RNA replication in a single pot.

**Standing.** The "classical RNA world" as Gilbert conceived it — RNA doing everything first — is a **minority position** by 2026\. The *practical* RNA-world programme (RNA as the heritable–catalytic polymer at a well-defined stage, coexisting with peptides and lipids) is **dominant** in the prebiotic-chemistry community. 2025's Holliger and Gianni results have shifted the conversation toward "how did open-ended Darwinian RNA replication begin" rather than "whether" — a significant change.

### **2.3 RNA–peptide coevolution and proto-translation**

**Principal proponents.** Charles Carter (UNC); Peter Wills (Auckland); Richard Wolfenden (UNC); Ada Yonath, Matthew Belousoff, Ilana Agmon, Miri Krupkin (Weizmann); Loren Dean Williams, Anton Petrov, Jessica Bowman (Georgia Tech); Moran Frenkel-Pinter (Hebrew U.); Matthew Powner (UCL); Ramanarayanan Krishnamurthy (Scripps); John Sutherland (MRC-LMB).

**Key publications.**

* Carter & Wolfenden 2015 *PNAS* 112:7489 — tRNA acceptor stem encodes amino-acid size; anticodon encodes polarity (two codes layered).  
* Martinez-Rodriguez et al. 2015 *JBC* 290:19710 — functional Class I/II aaRS urzymes on opposite strands of the same ancestral gene (Rodin–Ohno bidirectional-coding confirmation).  
* Davidovich, Belousoff, Wekselman et al. 2010 *Isr J Chem* 50:29; Krupkin et al. 2011 *Phil Trans R Soc B* 366:2972 — vestige of prebiotic bonding machine in the contemporary ribosome.  
* Bose, Fridkin, Davidovich, Krupkin et al. 2022 *NAR* 50:1815 — 67-nt engineered proto-ribosome forms peptide bonds without coding.  
* Petrov et al. 2014 *PNAS* 111:10251; Bowman et al. 2020 *Chem Rev* 120:4848 — accretion/onion model of ribosomal evolution.  
* Frenkel-Pinter et al. 2020 *Nat Commun* (mutually stabilising proto-peptide–RNA interactions).  
* Canavelli, Islam, Powner 2019 *Nature* 571:546 — chemoselective α-aminonitrile peptide ligation in water.  
* Singh, Thoma, Whitaker, Satterly Webley, Yao, Powner 2025 *Nature* 644:933 — thioester-mediated RNA aminoacylation and peptidyl-RNA synthesis in water. **This is a major recent result** because it gives a prebiotic chemistry in which aminoacylated RNA emerges naturally, bypassing the synthetase problem.  
* Müller, Neu-Yilik, Hentze et al. 2022 *Nature* 605:279 — prebiotic RNA–peptide world scenario (peptidyl-RNA replicators).  
* Radakovic, Fang, Szostak 2024 *PNAS* 121:e2410206121 — RNA aminoacylation may precede its role in peptide synthesis.

**Central claim.** The genetic code and the translation apparatus co-arose with catalytic peptides and RNA; aminoacyl-tRNA synthetase Classes I and II descend from opposite strands of a single ancestral gene (Rodin–Ohno bidirectional coding), implying a very early dual-sense RNA template that specified two complementary enzymatic activities. A proto-ribosome comprising the symmetric pocket at the peptidyl-transferase centre existed before coded translation.

**Strongest support.** (i) Experimental functional urzymes on opposite strands; (ii) the symmetric-pocket proto-ribosome produces peptide bonds in vitro (Bose 2022); (iii) peptide-assisted RNA catalysis is now robust (Iglesias-Artola 2022 *Nat Chem* 14:407 on coacervates; Frenkel-Pinter mutual stabilisation); (iv) thioester-mediated aminoacylation on RNA is now a prebiotic reaction (Singh/Powner 2025).

**Criticisms/open problems.** The specific bidirectional-ancestor gene is inferred, not recovered from deep phylogeny; proto-ribosome experiments form peptide bonds but not coded translation; no experiment has yet produced a coded amino-acid sequence from a template via a plausible prebiotic intermediate.

**Standing.** Ascendant. By 2026, most serious prebiotic-chemistry research accepts some RNA–peptide coevolution; the fight is over *how early* and *how tightly coupled*. This framework increasingly replaces the pure RNA-world.

### **2.4 Lipid world / GARD and membrane-first**

**Principal proponents.** Doron Lancet, Daniel Segré (Weizmann → BU); David Deamer; David Bar-Even.

* Segré, Ben-Eli, Lancet 2000 *PNAS* 97:4112; Segré, Ben-Eli, Deamer, Lancet 2001 *OLEB* 31:119.  
* Vasas, Szathmáry, Santos 2010 *PNAS* 107:1470 — critique (compositional genomes lack evolvability).  
* Subsequent Lancet-group defences (composome clusters).

**Central claim.** Compositional assemblies (lipid/amphiphile aggregates) can carry heritable information as *composition* rather than *sequence*; catalytic networks of small molecules suffice to initiate life.

**Standing.** **Losing support** as a stand-alone origin model. The evolvability critique (Vasas/Szathmáry) is largely accepted; compositional heredity has not been experimentally realised in a clearly Darwinian form. Lipid vesicles remain central as *compartments* in Szostak/Deamer programmes, but "lipid world as genetic substrate" is a minority position.

### **2.5 Mineral-template / surface chemistry / mineral evolution (modern Cairns-Smith, Hazen)**

**Principal proponents.** James Ferris (d. 2016); Robert Hazen, Dimitri Sverjensky (Carnegie); Valentina Erastova, Chris Greenwell (Edinburgh/Durham); Steven Benner (FfAME); Hyman Hartman.

* Ferris 2006 *Phil Trans B* 361:1777, 10.1098/rstb.2006.1903; Aldersley et al. 2011 — montmorillonite RNA oligomerisation up to 50-mers.  
* Hazen & Sverjensky 2010 *CSH Perspect Biol* 2:a002162; Hazen 2008, 2013 mineral-evolution framework.  
* Erastova et al. 2017 *Nat Commun* 8:2033, 10.1038/s41467-017-02248-y — layered double hydroxides concentrate/polymerise amino acids under wet–dry cycling.  
* Jerome, Kim, Mojzsis, Benner, Biondi 2022 *Astrobiology* 22:629, 10.1089/ast.2021.0128 — basaltic glass catalyses RNA oligomers 100–200 nt from NTPs without enzymes.  
* Ozturk & Sasselov 2022 *PNAS* 119:e2204765119; Ozturk et al. 2023 *Sci Adv* — homochirality via CISS on magnetite.

**Central claim.** Mineral surfaces concentrate, align, and catalyse polymerisation of RNA/peptides; the mineral kingdom co-evolved with life; specific minerals (clays, LDHs, magnetite, pyrite, calcite, borate) selected for particular monomers and chiralities.

**Standing.** Accepted as a **contributing mechanism** rather than a stand-alone origin. Clay-/LDH-/basalt-catalysed oligomerisation is the gold-standard experimental demonstration of mineral templating; Cairns-Smith's strong form (clays as genetic substrate) is essentially abandoned, but the weak form — minerals as concentrators, chiral selectors, and redox catalysts — is near-universal.

### **2.6 Wet–dry / hot-spring / terrestrial geothermal (Deamer–Damer; Mulkidjanian)**

**Principal proponents.** David Deamer (UCSC), Bruce Damer; Armen Mulkidjanian, Eugene Koonin, Michael Galperin; Ben Pearce, Ralph Pudritz (McMaster); Martin Van Kranendonk (Pilbara field evidence); Nicholas Hud (Georgia Tech).

* **Mulkidjanian, Bychkov, Dibrova, Galperin, Koonin 2012 *PNAS* 109:E821, 10.1073/pnas.1117774109** — terrestrial anoxic geothermal fields, K⁺/Na⁺/Zn/Mn/Pi compositions of cytoplasm match vapor-condensate pools.  
* Damer & Deamer 2015 *Life* 5:872; **2020 *Astrobiology* 20:429, 10.1089/ast.2019.2045** — hot-spring hypothesis.  
* Pearce, Pudritz, Semenov, Henning 2017 *PNAS* 114:11327, 10.1073/pnas.1710339114 — warm-little-pond polymer-formation model, meteoritic delivery timing.  
* Rajamani et al. 2008 *OLEB* 38:57 — RNA-like polymers up to \~100 nt from AMP \+ lipid under wet–dry cycling at 85 °C.  
* Djokic, Van Kranendonk et al. 2017 *Nat Commun* 8:15263 — 3.48 Ga Dresser Fm terrestrial hot-spring biosignatures with geyserite.  
* Forsythe et al. 2015 *Angew Chem* (10.1002/anie.201503792) — depsipeptide cycling.

**Central claim.** Life began in fluctuating subaerial hot-spring/pond systems where wet–dry–moist cycles drive condensation polymerisation, amphiphiles template oligomers on drying, and K⁺/Zn/Mn/phosphate-rich vapor condensates match cytoplasmic composition.

**Strongest support.** (i) Laboratory: wet–dry cycling yields longer polymers than any steady-state scheme; (ii) ion-composition argument: modern cytoplasm does not resemble seawater; (iii) Pilbara 3.48 Ga geyserite is the oldest unambiguous *terrestrial* biosignature.

**Criticisms.** (i) No continuous chemiosmotic drive (Lane–Martin objection); (ii) K⁺/Na⁺ ratio may be evolved, not ancestral (Szostak); (iii) UV flux problematic without shielding; (iv) Pilbara hot-spring interpretation accepted, but "origin-of-life venue" is a stronger claim than "life was there by 3.48 Ga."

**Standing.** The main living rival to alkaline vents. Strong in the prebiotic-chemistry community (because wet-dry cycling is experimentally productive), growing since 2015\. Still contested on energetics.

### **2.7 Heterotrophic / primordial-soup / meteoritic delivery**

**Principal proponents.** Jeffrey Bada, H. James Cleaves, Antonio Lazcano (Miller school); Sandra Pizzarello; Daniel Glavin, Jason Dworkin (NASA Goddard); Yasuhiro Oba (Hokkaido); Hiroshi Naraoka; Dante Lauretta (OSIRIS-REx).

* Johnson, Cleaves, Dworkin, Glavin, Lazcano, Bada 2008 *Science* 322:404, 10.1126/science.1161527 — Miller volcanic-spark reanalysis: 22 amino acids.  
* Parker et al. 2011 *PNAS* 108:5526 — Miller H₂S spark: 23 AA \+ amines.  
* **Ryugu (Hayabusa2):** Naraoka et al. 2023 *Science* 379:eabn9033; Oba et al. 2023 *Nat Commun* 14:1292 (uracil); Parker et al. 2023 *GCA* 347:42; Furukawa et al. 2026 *Nat Astron* 10.1038/s41550-026-02791-z (all 5 canonical nucleobases, **unverified DOI**).  
* **Bennu (OSIRIS-REx):** Glavin et al. 2025 *Nat Astron* 9:199, 10.1038/s41550-024-02472-9 — 14 of 20 protein amino acids, all 5 nucleobases, abundant NH₃, \~10,000 N-bearing species, brine-evaporite minerals; McCoy et al. 2025 *Nature* (brine chemistry, phosphates, **DOI flagged unverified**).

**Central claim.** The prebiotic molecular inventory — amino acids, nucleobases, sugars, ammonia, PAHs, phosphate — was delivered abundantly by carbonaceous chondrites and IDPs, and augmented locally by spark-discharge and photochemistry; the origin of life is heterotrophic, feeding on this abiotically produced pool.

**Standing.** Ingredient-delivery **is consensus**; heterotrophic origin as a **causal mechanism** is a minority position. Ryugu (2023) and Bennu (2025) returns have been the strongest recent empirical support. The principal unresolved question is concentration: absolute abundances are large, but ocean-scale concentrations remain nanomolar without a concentrating mechanism.

### **2.8 Sutherland cyanosulfidic systems chemistry**

**Principal proponents.** John Sutherland, Matthew Powner (UCL/MRC), Dougal Ritson, Shaun Islam, Jianfeng Xu.

* Powner, Gerland, Sutherland 2009 *Nature* 459:239 — activated pyrimidine nucleotide synthesis.  
* **Patel, Percivalle, Ritson, Duffy, Sutherland 2015 *Nat Chem* 7:301, 10.1038/nchem.2202** — common precursors of ribonucleotides, amino acids, and lipid precursors from HCN/H₂S/UV chemistry.  
* Xu et al. 2017, 2020 *Nature* 582:60 — selective prebiotic purine nucleosides.  
* Sutherland 2017 *Nat Rev Chem* 1:0012 — "opinion" article arguing for co-emergence and a land-based photoredox venue.

**Central claim.** The three informational/structural classes (nucleic acids, proteins, membranes) emerged *simultaneously* from reductive homologation of hydrogen cyanide and derivatives under UV photoredox with Cu(I)/Cu(II)/H₂S; the preferred venue is terrestrial (meteoritic-impact-regolith leachates in streams/ponds), not submarine.

**Standing.** Highly influential. The 2015 paper is one of the single most-cited origin-of-life papers of the decade. Sutherland's programme has won the argument that monomer synthesis is no longer the field's bottleneck; it has not yet demonstrated unification with phylogenomic LUCA reconstruction, and its rejection of vent chemistry is a genuine disagreement with the Martin/Lane camp.

### **2.9 Krishnamurthy mixed-ancestry / cyanide proto-metabolism**

**Principal proponents.** Ramanarayanan Krishnamurthy, Greg Springsteen (Scripps/Furman), Nicholas Hud (Georgia Tech), Subhendu Bhowmik, Megha Karki.

* Bhowmik & Krishnamurthy 2019 *Nat Chem* 11:1009 — RNA/DNA backbone chimeras support co-emergence.  
* Jiménez, Gibard, Krishnamurthy 2020 *Angew Chem* — diamidophosphate (DAP) prebiotic phosphorylation.  
* Fialho, Karunakaran, Greeson, Martínez, Schuster, Krishnamurthy, Hud 2021 *JACS* 143:13525 — depsipeptide nucleic acids.  
* Springsteen, Yerabolu, Nelson, Rhea, Krishnamurthy 2018 *Nat Commun* 9:91 — metal-free glyoxylate α-ketoacid rTCA analog.  
* Stubbs, Yadav, Krishnamurthy, Springsteen 2020 *Nat Chem* 12:1016 — metal-free ancestral Krebs analog.  
* **Yadav, Pulletikurti, Yerabolu, Krishnamurthy 2022 *Nat Chem* 14:170, 10.1038/s41557-021-00878-w** — cyanide as primordial reductant in a reductive glyoxylate pathway.  
* Pulletikurti et al. 2022 *Nat Chem* 14:1142 — α-amino acids \+ orotate from α-ketoacids.

**Central claim.** Iron-mediated prebiotic networks are too messy; cyanide- and metal-free α-ketoacid/glyoxylate pathways can reproduce the skeletal logic of the TCA without Orgel's selectivity objection; RNA and DNA co-emerged via backbone heterogeneity that was pruned by selection.

**Standing.** Rising since 2020\. Directly competes with the Moran/Martin Fe-catalysed programme on mechanism; converges with it on the importance of small-molecule redox network chemistry.

### **2.10 Moran/Ralser non-enzymatic metabolic reconstitution**

**Principal proponents.** Joseph Moran, Kamila Muchowska, Sreejith Varma (Strasbourg); Markus Ralser, Markus Keller (Crick/Berlin).

* **Muchowska, Varma, Moran 2017 *Nat Ecol Evol* 1:1716, 10.1038/s41559-017-0311-7** — Zn²⁺/Cr³⁺/Fe⁰ drive 6/11 reactions of rTCA.  
* **Muchowska, Varma, Moran 2019 *Nature* 569:104, 10.1038/s41586-019-1151-1** — Fe²⁺ \+ pyruvate/glyoxylate → 9/11 TCA intermediates, all 5 universal precursors, 4 amino acids with NH₂OH \+ Fe⁰.  
* Varma, Muchowska, Chatelain, Moran 2018 *Nat Ecol Evol* 2:1019 — native Fe/Ni/Co reduce CO₂ to acetate \+ pyruvate (acetyl-CoA analog).  
* **Keller, Turchyn, Ralser 2014 *Mol Syst Biol* 10:725, 10.1002/msb.20145228** — 28/29 expected non-enzymatic glycolysis/PPP reactions in Archean-ocean conditions.  
* Keller et al. 2016 *Sci Adv* 2:e1501235; 2017 *Nat Ecol Evol* 1:0083 (sulfate-radical Krebs-like chemistry).

**Central claim.** Core metabolism's topology predates enzymes; non-enzymatic analogs of the universal biosynthetic precursor networks run in plausible Hadean geochemistries (Fe, Zn, Ni, H₂O₂/sulfate radicals) at high step-completeness, albeit with modest yields.

**Standing.** The single most important experimental programme bridging "metabolism-first" and phylogenomic LUCA biochemistry. By 2026 it is widely cited as the empirical answer to Orgel 2008, with caveats (conditions still mismatched across steps; side reactions dominant in some regimes).

### **2.11 Modern Wächtershäuser iron–sulfur world**

Günter Wächtershäuser and Claudia Huber's programme (Huber & Wächtershäuser 1997 *Science* 276:245; 1998 *Science* 281:670; Huber et al. 2003 *Science* 301:938) — activated-acetate and peptide-bond formation on (Ni,Fe)S surfaces — is **historically seminal but programmatically superseded**. Its experimental pillars remain foundational, but the research agenda has been absorbed by Russell/Martin/Lane (vent setting) and Moran/Ralser (non-enzymatic metabolism), with Preiner 2020 and Varma 2018 as direct modern descendants. FeS catalysis as a *theme* is near-consensus; "iron-sulfur world" as a branded rival is effectively dormant.

### **2.12 Coacervate / LLPS protocell programme**

**Principal proponents.** Stephen Mann (Bristol); Christine Keating (Penn State); T-Y Dora Tang (MPI-CBG/Sheffield); Evan Spruijt (Radboud); Philip Bevilacqua (Penn State); Tommaso Fraccia (ESPCI); Tony Jia (ELSI); Hannes Mutschler; Moritz Kreysing.

* Koga, Williams, Perriman, Mann 2011 *Nat Chem* 3:720.  
* Aumiller & Keating 2016 *Nat Chem* 8:129 — phosphorylation-driven dynamic LLPS.  
* Drobot, Iglesias-Artola, Le Vay, Mayr, Kar, Kreysing, Mutschler, Tang 2018 *Nat Commun* 9:3643 — active hammerhead ribozyme inside coacervates.  
* Poudyal et al. 2019 *Nat Commun* 10:490 — template-directed RNA polymerisation inside membraneless compartments.  
* **Iglesias-Artola et al. 2022 *Nat Chem* 14:407, 10.1038/s41557-022-00890-8** — ribozyme activity requires cationic heteropeptides (RGG-type), not homopolymer Kn/Rn; peptide sequence tunes Mg²⁺ partitioning.  
* Abbas, Lipiński, Wang, Spruijt 2021 *Chem Soc Rev* 50:3690 — review.  
* **Agrawal, Radakovic, Vonteddu, Rizvi, Huynh, Douglas, Tirrell, Karim, Szostak 2024 *Sci Adv* 10:eadn9657, 10.1126/sciadv.adn9657** — rainwater (distilled water) forms a polymer-rich skin on coacervates, slowing RNA exchange from minutes to days; Szostak's partial embrace of LLPS.

**Central claim.** Membrane-free liquid–liquid phase-separated droplets, formed from cationic peptides \+ anionic RNA/polyphosphate, provided the first compartments: they concentrate biomolecules 10²–10⁴-fold, tolerate high salt/Mg²⁺, and accelerate ribozyme catalysis. They are the natural precursors of modern biomolecular condensates.

**Standing.** Rising rapidly since 2018\. The 2024 Agrawal/Szostak rainwater paper is widely read as the "handshake" between the fatty-acid vesicle programme and the LLPS programme; the dominant near-term synthesis is **hybrid** (LLPS core \+ membrane envelope). Weak on bioenergetics; open on prebiotic plausibility of the cationic peptide component, though short heteropeptides (Abbas/Spruijt) are candidates.

### **2.13 LUCA reconstructions themselves (a "theory" in their own right)**

* **Weiss, Sousa, Mrnjavac, Neukirchen, Roettger, Nelson-Sathi, Martin 2016 *Nat Microbiol* 1:16116, 10.1038/nmicrobiol.2016.116** — 355 protein families, anaerobic thermophile, H₂/CO₂ autotroph, Wood–Ljungdahl, Fe–S/radical-SAM biochemistry.  
* Weiss, Preiner, Xavier, Zimorski, Martin 2018 *PLoS Genet* 14:e1007518 — autotrophic LUCA defence.  
* Berkemer & McGlynn 2020 *MBE* 37:2332, 10.1093/molbev/msaa089 — \~82% of Weiss 2016's 355 families fail A/B separation when homolog sampling is expanded; methodological caveat.  
* Betts, Puttick, Clark, Williams, Donoghue, Pisani 2018 *Nat Ecol Evol* 2:1556, 10.1038/s41559-018-0644-x — molecular clock \>3.9 Ga LUCA; LECA \~1.84 Ga.  
* **Moody et al. 2024 *Nat Ecol Evol* 8:1654, 10.1038/s41559-024-02461-1** — \~2.75 Mb genome, \~2,657 proteins, \~4.09–4.33 Ga, acetogen-grade, 19 inferred CRISPR-Cas loci, embedded in a heterotrophic ecosystem.  
* Wimmer et al. 2021 *Front Microbiol* 12:793664; *Microorganisms* 9:458 — 402/404 autotrophic-core reactions, exergonic under serpentinisation.  
* Xavier, Hordijk, Kauffman, Steel, Martin 2020 *Proc R Soc B* 287:20192377 — RAFs recoverable from acetogen+methanogen metabolism.  
* Sojo, Pomiankowski, Lane 2014 *PLoS Biol* 12:e1001926 — leaky-membrane transition explaining archaea/bacteria lipid divide.  
* Williams, Cox, Foster, Szöllősi, Embley 2020 *Nat Ecol Evol* 4:138 — 2-domain tree of life.

**Central claim.** LUCA was a complex (but not eukaryotic-grade) anaerobic chemolithoautotroph with DNA, a complete ribosome and genetic code, Wood–Ljungdahl CO₂ fixation, Fe-S/radical-SAM cofactors, ATP synthase using chemiosmotic coupling, leaky membranes of mixed/heterochiral lipids, dated \~4.09–4.33 Ga.

**Standing.** The 2024 Moody synthesis is the current best-available integrated reconstruction. The \~4.2 Ga date, \~2,600-gene complement, and embedded-ecosystem claim are the most contested elements. Autotrophy at LUCA is majority consensus; nitrogen fixation at LUCA (claimed by Weiss 2016\) is now **contested** and likely a later LBCA acquisition (Pi et al. 2022, **unverified primary citation**). CRISPR-Cas at LUCA is novel and not independently confirmed.

## **3\. Shared problem-set**

The questions every serious theory must answer. In each case I name theories that have good, partial, or no answer.

**Energy and disequilibrium.** *Good answers:* alkaline-vent programme (continuous H₂/CO₂ redox \+ pH gradient; Preiner 2020 experimentally; Wimmer 2021 thermodynamically). *Partial:* hot-spring models (wet–dry cycling \+ UV photoredox; Sutherland cyanosulfidic photochemistry); Moran/Ralser non-enzymatic reductive chemistry with Fe. *Weak/absent:* pure coacervate LLPS (no explicit free-energy mechanism); classical RNA world (externally supplied activated monomers, no free-energy flux).

**Concentration.** *Good:* wet–dry cycling in ponds (Damer/Deamer, Hud, Ross), thermophoresis/microfluidic pore concentration (Braun lab, Möller 2017), coacervate sequestration (10²–10⁴-fold). *Partial:* mineral adsorption (Ferris montmorillonite, Erastova LDHs, Benner basalt glass). *Weak:* steady-state vent flow disperses (explicit Lane-camp acknowledgement that pore evaporation/thermophoresis is needed); steady-state ocean chemistry.

**Compartmentalization.** *Good for growth/division:* fatty-acid vesicles (Szostak lab); hybrid membranized coacervates (Tang 2014, rainwater-stabilised Szostak 2024). *Good for concentration and ribozyme catalysis:* coacervates (Iglesias-Artola 2022). *Good for bioenergetics but not for free-living cellularity:* alkaline-vent mineral pores. *Weak:* lipid-world-as-genome (GARD) essentially abandoned.

**Information storage and replication.** *Good within its scope:* nonenzymatic RNA copying with 2-AI activation (Szostak/Walton 2016, 2017); VCG model (Zhou 2021, Ding 2023); triplet polymerase and QT45 exponential replication (Holliger 2025, Gianni 2026). *Partial:* pre-RNA worlds — TNA (Chaput/Maola 2024 *Nat Catal* 7:1173, \>99% fidelity TNA polymerase), PNA, GNA — demonstrate the polymer space is larger than RNA but don't resolve how RNA won. *Weak:* GARD compositional heredity (Vasas/Szathmáry 2010 critique largely accepted); metabolism-first in its strict form has no heritability mechanism — acknowledged openly by Muchowska, Moran, Ralser, who now pair metabolism with early RNA–peptide chemistry.

**Homochirality.** *Partial answers only, no consensus.* Blackmond/Soai autocatalytic amplification; Sutherland cyanosulfidic chemistry with UV (some enantioselectivity in photochemistry); Ozturk & Sasselov 2022/2023 (CISS on magnetite); crystallisation-driven enrichment of RAO (Sasselov). Meteoritic enantiomeric excess is too small and the wrong sign in some cases. **This is a problem no theory fully solves as of 2026**; the best candidates are physical (CISS, crystallisation) rather than strictly chemical.

**Genotype–phenotype bridge.** *Best answers:* Carter/Wolfenden dual-code and Rodin–Ohno bidirectional aaRS ancestry; Krupkin/Yonath proto-ribosome symmetric pocket (Bose 2022 proto-ribosome makes peptide bonds without coding); Singh/Powner 2025 thioester-mediated RNA aminoacylation gives the *chemistry* of aminoacyl-tRNA prebiotically. *Partial:* RNA-peptide coevolution synthesis (Frenkel-Pinter, Müller 2022). *Open:* no experiment has produced coded synthesis of a defined peptide sequence from a template under prebiotic conditions. The genetic-code *origin* remains the field's deepest unsolved problem.

**Chemistry-to-biology transition.** Working definitions differ. Martin/Lane: appearance of genetically encoded chemiosmotic ATP synthesis. Szostak: Darwinian selection on encapsulated replicating RNA. Sutherland: emergence of a systems-chemistry attractor with informational feedback. None is operationally closed; this is a conceptual rather than experimental impasse.

**LUCA complexity gap.** Moody 2024's \~2,600-gene LUCA implies hundreds of millions of years (or a geologically short but chemically dense evolution) between first life and LUCA. No current theory explains *quantitatively* how the gap between "first replicator" and LUCA was bridged; the Martin programme argues it was short (vent-continuous selection pressure); Sutherland/Krishnamurthy imply a longer pre-LUCA evolution of increasing chemical specificity. **This is an acknowledged open problem across all programmes.**

**Timing.** Habitability established by \~4.4 Ga (Jack Hills zircons, Wilde 2001; Harrison 2020). Moody 2024 places LUCA at 4.09–4.33 Ga; Betts 2018 gives \>3.9 Ga. Earliest widely accepted biosignatures: Dresser 3.48 Ga (Djokic 2017 terrestrial hot springs); Strelley Pool 3.43 Ga (Allwood 2007). Contested older claims: Nutman 2016 Isua 3.7 Ga stromatolites (Allwood 2018 *Nature* 563:241 and Zawaski 2020 *EPSL* 545:116409 argue deformation artefact); Dodd 2017 Nuvvuagittuq 3.77–4.28 Ga hematite tubes (Papineau 2022 *Sci Adv* 8:eabm2296 defends, critics cite abiotic mimics); Tashiro 2017 Labrador 3.95 Ga graphite (Whitehouse 2019 *Precambrian Res* 331:105409 and Zhang 2024 *Nat Commun* 15:5938 argue secondary/abiotic); Bell 2015 Jack Hills 4.1 Ga zircon graphite (contested single inclusion). **Best-case interval habitability→LUCA ≈ 100–300 Myr; conservative ≈ 800 Myr with many undiscovered earlier organisms. Life appears to have arisen geologically quickly; exactly how quickly is a function of which contested biosignatures one accepts.**

## **4\. Convergence map**

Concrete convergences across named theories.

**Natural proton gradients as primordial energy currency.** Converged on by Russell, Martin, Lane, Nitschke, Sousa, Preiner, Branscomb (submarine alkaline-vent programme). Terrestrial models offer no strict analogue: Mulkidjanian invokes vapor-condensate pH/ion gradients but not chemiosmotic proton-motive force per se; Damer/Deamer invoke wet–dry cycles rather than ion gradients. **Proton-gradient primacy is a defining commitment of the vent programme and distinguishes it sharply from terrestrial programmes.**

**Fatty-acid / single-chain amphiphile vesicles as compartments.** Szostak, Deamer, Damer, Mansy, Chen, Jordan, Lane (post-2019). Alkaline-vent programme has partially converged here via Jordan 2019 (mixed C10–C15 \+ alcohols \+ isoprenoids in hot alkaline seawater) and Lane lab 2024–2025 vesicle work; this is a non-trivial move because Russell's original position invoked mineral pores, not lipid vesicles.

**Mineral micropores as initial compartments.** Russell, Martin, Lane, Koonin 2005, Branscomb. Not held by Szostak/Deamer/Keating/Mann; **this is a first-order disagreement** with the lipid-vesicle and coacervate camps.

**Coacervates as compartments.** Mann, Keating, Tang, Spruijt, Bevilacqua, Kreysing, Mutschler, increasingly Szostak (Agrawal 2024). Not held by Russell/Martin/Lane axis, but not explicitly rejected; **most likely near-term synthesis is "coacervate stage preceding membrane-bound cell."**

**Fe–S / (Ni,Fe)S / transition-metal clusters as primordial catalysts.** Converged on by Wächtershäuser, Russell, Martin, Lane, Moran (Muchowska 2019, Varma 2018), Ralser (Keller 2014), Barge, Preiner (2020), Sousa. Krishnamurthy partly dissents (metal-free cyanide/glyoxylate) but his own programme uses Fe for some steps. **FeS catalysis is one of the broadest near-consensus convergences in the field.**

**Co-emergence of RNA, peptides, and lipids from shared precursors.** Sutherland cyanosulfidic (Patel 2015), Powner (2019, 2023, 2025), Krishnamurthy (Bhowmik 2019), Frenkel-Pinter, Müller 2022\. Szostak-Chicago \+ Krishnamurthy cyclophospholipids (Toparlak 2023 *ACS Nano* 17:23772) converge here. **Co-emergence is now the majority framing among prebiotic chemists**, displacing pure RNA-first.

**Wet–dry / freeze–thaw / pH cycling as essential.** Deamer, Damer, Hud, Ross, Keating (Fares 2020), Mulkidjanian, Pearce, Holliger (pH-freeze-thaw for TPR replication 2025), Iglesias-Artola (coacervate dynamics 2022). **Environmental cycling is near-consensus**, though which cycle matters is model-dependent.

**Wood–Ljungdahl pathway as ancestral.** Sousa & Martin 2014, Weiss 2016, Moody 2024 (phylogenetic); Preiner 2020, Varma 2018, Beyazay 2023 (experimental); Wimmer 2021 (thermodynamic). Near-consensus among vent and non-enzymatic-metabolism programmes; **the single most robust convergence between phylogenomics and laboratory chemistry**.

**Cyclic environmental chemistry \+ autocatalytic set formalism.** Xavier/Hordijk/Kauffman/Steel/Martin 2020 RAF analysis of acetogen+methanogen metabolism converges mathematically with Moran's experimental programme; this is an underappreciated formal convergence.

**UV photoredox \+ HCN/H₂S chemistry as monomer synthesis.** Sutherland, Powner, Krishnamurthy, Ritson. Not the dominant view among Martin/Lane camp (who favour FeS redox in vents) but not excluded by them; converges with meteoritic organic delivery (Bennu, Ryugu) to provide the building-block inventory.

## **5\. Active disagreements**

**Venue: alkaline vent vs terrestrial geothermal.** The most public disagreement. Vent camp points to LUCA phylogenomics, thermodynamics, chemiosmosis universality, Preiner 2020, Beyazay 2023\. Pond/hot-spring camp points to cytoplasmic K⁺/Zn/Mn/Pi ratios, wet–dry polymer chemistry, Pilbara 3.48 Ga geyserite, UV photoredox monomer synthesis, Mg²⁺–vesicle compatibility. **What would tilt the balance:** (i) demonstration of continuous non-enzymatic polymerisation of RNA \+ protometabolic chemistry in a single vent or pond simulation (neither has achieved this yet); (ii) direct isotopic/paleoenvironmental evidence of life in Eoarchean submarine vs subaerial settings (currently both sites show signals, interpretations contested); (iii) resolution of whether K⁺/Na⁺ cellular ratios are ancestral (Mulkidjanian) or evolved post-LUCA (Szostak, Lane).

**Autotrophy vs heterotrophy at LUCA.** Majority autotrophic (Weiss 2016, Moody 2024). Heterotrophic-LUCA minority position (Crits-Christoph and others, some cited via secondary sources, **primary reference partly unverified**). **What would tilt the balance:** better taxon sampling at the A/B domain boundary with model-heterogeneous phylogenetics; direct chemistry demonstrating a self-sustaining heterotrophic network on abiotic organics.

**Whether chemiosmosis is primordial or evolved later.** Lane/Martin (primordial) vs Jackson 2016 and Deamer (evolved later). Central to whether the vent programme's bioenergetic core is right. **What would tilt the balance:** a non-phylogenetic demonstration that non-chemiosmotic energy coupling is sustainable in plausible prebiotic conditions, or conversely, geochemical demonstration of stable proton gradients in a real Hadean analog system (Jordan 2019 and Möller 2017 partly address this but not dispositively).

**Whether a fully non-enzymatic rTCA is achievable.** Moran/Ralser optimism (9/11 steps of TCA; near-complete glycolysis/PPP) vs Krishnamurthy/Stubbs skepticism (Fe chemistry is messy; propose cyanide/metal-free alternatives) vs Orgel-descendants (partial cycles are not cycles). **What would tilt the balance:** a demonstration of a self-sustaining, catalytically closed non-enzymatic cycle at a single set of conditions, still absent in 2026\.

**Heredity mechanism before RNA replication.** Pure RNA-world camp (Szostak, Joyce, Holliger) — nonenzymatic \+ ribozymal. Coacervate camp — composition-based heredity with partial selection. GARD — compositional genomes (largely abandoned). Pre-RNA polymers (TNA, PNA, GNA) — heredity in alternative backbones (Chaput 2012, 2024). **What would tilt the balance:** demonstration of a sequence-general closed-loop replicator, in any polymer, under prebiotic conditions.

**Primordial compartment type.** Fatty-acid vesicle vs mineral pore vs coacervate. Likely to be resolved by hybridisation rather than victory of one; the Agrawal/Szostak 2024 rainwater paper and Tang-Mann membranized coacervates are already hybrid moves. **What would tilt the balance:** demonstration of Darwinian selection (with heritable variation) on encapsulated chemistry in any one of the three — Holliger 2025 and Gianni 2026 are close to achieving this for RNA replication but not yet in an evolutionarily closed vesicle or coacervate.

**Homochirality origin.** Physical (CISS, Sasselov/Ozturk) vs chemical (Soai/Blackmond) vs crystal-face (RAO on magnetite) vs meteoritic (Pizzarello). No convergence. **What would tilt the balance:** identification of a prebiotically plausible mechanism producing large enantiomeric excess (\>10%) with a specific hand, reproducibly, under conditions compatible with downstream chemistry.

**Age of LUCA.** Moody 2024 (\~4.2 Ga) vs Betts 2018 (\>3.9 Ga) vs conservative fossil-only estimates (\<3.5 Ga). **What would tilt the balance:** independent validation of disputed Hadean/Eoarchean biosignatures (Bell 2015 zircon graphite, Dodd 2017 Nuvvuagittuq microfossils, Tashiro 2017 Labrador graphite) with multiple independent lines of evidence.

## **6\. Methodological notes**

**Geochemical evidence.** Strong for: Hadean habitability by 4.4 Ga (Jack Hills zircons); existence of serpentinite-hosted alkaline vents throughout Hadean–Archean (Lost City proxy); Pilbara 3.48 Ga terrestrial hot-spring life; carbonaceous-chondrite inventories on Ryugu (Naraoka 2023\) and Bennu (Glavin 2025). Suggestive: Moon-forming impact timing and the implication that magma-ocean solidification preceded habitability by \~100 Myr (Mrnjavac 2023). Speculative/contested: Hadean atmospheric composition (mildly vs strongly reducing); specific venue of Eoarchean life. The strongest Bayesian update in the decade is the **Ryugu/Bennu confirmation** that carbonaceous asteroids carry a nearly complete prebiotic organic toolkit, including all five canonical nucleobases and 14/20 protein amino acids.

**Phylogenetic evidence.** Strong: 2-domain tree of life (Williams 2020), Asgard archaea–eukaryote relationship (Spang 2015; Zaremba-Niedzwiedzka 2017; Eme 2023 *Nature* 618:992). Strong-but-methodology-dependent: LUCA gene content (Weiss 2016 vs Berkemer & McGlynn 2020 on clustering sensitivity; Moody 2024 with ALE gene-tree reconciliation as the current state of the art). Suggestive: molecular clock estimates of LUCA age (Betts 2018, Moody 2024\) sensitive to calibration choice. Ribosomal accretion (Petrov 2014; Bowman 2020\) is strong internal evidence for proto-ribosome precedence.

**Laboratory chemistry.** Strong: nonenzymatic primer extension with 2-AI (Walton 2016, Li 2017); TPR exponential replication (Holliger 2025, Gianni 2026); Fe-catalysed non-enzymatic metabolic chemistry (Muchowska 2019, Keller 2014, Varma 2018, Preiner 2020); Sutherland cyanosulfidic synthesis; cross-chiral ribozymes (Joyce); basalt-glass RNA oligomerisation up to 100–200 nt (Jerome/Benner 2022). Suggestive: coacervate ribozyme activity (Iglesias-Artola 2022); rainwater-stabilised coacervates (Agrawal/Szostak 2024); Jordan 2019 vent vesicles. Gaps: no closed-loop self-replication in a single pot; no coded peptide from a template in a prebiotic system; no prebiotic mechanism for \>10% homochiral amplification demonstrated under downstream-compatible conditions.

**Theoretical modeling.** Strong: RAF autocatalytic-set analyses (Xavier 2020\) recovering acetogen/methanogen cores; Wimmer 2021 thermodynamic demonstration that 402/404 biosynthetic reactions are exergonic in serpentinising conditions; Higgs RNA-world thermodynamics; Eigen error-threshold framework as an active constraint on any replicator proposal. Suggestive: Pearce 2017 warm-pond kinetic model; Vasas/Szathmáry 2010 evolvability analysis of GARD.

## **Conclusion**

The defining change between the 1990s field and the 2026 field is that the question "which theory is right?" has been displaced by the question "which subsystem is the right focus for each experimentally tractable piece of the problem?" The phylogenomic LUCA reconstruction (Moody 2024\) is heavier, older and more autotrophic than the Woesean progenote; the non-enzymatic metabolic chemistry (Preiner 2020, Muchowska 2019, Keller 2014\) runs well enough to undercut Orgel 2008 without closing the full cycle; the RNA-replication programme (Holliger 2025, Gianni 2026\) has crossed from philosophy into engineering; and Sutherland's co-emergence chemistry has made "RNA-first vs metabolism-first" feel anachronistic.

The three biggest open questions — the origin of the genetic code, the origin of homochirality, and the resolution of vent-vs-pond venue — are not methodologically alike. The code problem is a bioenergetics-of-information problem and will most likely fall to Carter/Wills/Powner-style chemistry. Homochirality is most likely to yield to physics (CISS, crystallisation) rather than pure chemistry. The venue question will likely *not* yield to a clean winner; the highest-probability endgame is that distinct subsystems (monomer synthesis, bioenergetics, compartmentalization) occurred at different sites and were concatenated by transport — which would render both camps partly right and neither fully so.

Two striking facts sit uncomfortably with all current programmes. First, **LUCA is far more complex than any extant protocell experiment reaches**; several hundred million years, or an implausibly fast evolution, lies unexplained between first replicator and LUCA. Second, **no current theory offers a compelling quantitative account of why life arose once, rather than many times with distinct biochemistries**. The absence of serious answers to either is informative: the field has matured enough to know what it does not know.
