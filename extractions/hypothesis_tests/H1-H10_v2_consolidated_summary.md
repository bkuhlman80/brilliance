## Detailed Summaries

### H1: Univariate Heritability by Zone

**Data:** Weighted h² from Jang (1996, M), Jang (1998, L), Pilia (2006, XL)
**Zone means:** Primary .42, Intersection .40, Center .38, Edge .42
**Assessment:** Predicted ordering technically holds (.42 > .40 > .38), but effect sizes are negligible. Within-zone variance exceeds between-zone variance by 2–3×. Zone membership explains essentially no variance in heritability.
**V1→V2 change:** Total spread improved from .02 to .04, driven by A1 (.30) and N5 (.34) lowering center mean.

### H2a: Facet-Specific Genetic Variance by Zone

**Data:** Residual h² from Jang (1998, L)
**Zone means:** Center .282, Primary .268, Edge .26, Intersection .20
**Assessment:** Prediction completely reversed — center has the HIGHEST specific variance, not the lowest. All 6 v2 center facets have comparable AE-model residual h² (.27–.30). This uniformity is actually consistent with center facets sharing "Brilliance" variance beyond the Big Five, though the prediction was that they'd have the LEAST specificity.
**V1→V2 change:** Moving CE facet A3 (zero specificity) out of center and adding AE facets A1/N5 made the reversal stronger.

### H3: Cross-Domain Genetic Loading Ratios

**Data:** Pilia (2006, XL) S3 genetic factor loadings for all 30 facets
**Zone means:** Center 2.17, Intersection 1.09, Primary 1.02, Edge 0.85
**Assessment:** The model's strongest result. Center-triangle facets are dramatically more genetically diffuse than any other zone. All 6 center facets occupy the top 6 positions among the 30 facets (N5 2.80, E3 2.75, E4 2.53, A1 1.75, E6 1.64, A6 1.55). The gap between center and intersection doubled from v1 to v2.
**V1→V2 change:** Adding N5 (2.80) and A1 (1.75) to center while removing low-ratio C3 (.58) dramatically increased center mean.

### H4: Direction of Cross-Domain Genetic Loadings

**Data:** Yamagata (2006, XL), Pilia (2006, XL) genetic cross-loadings
**Assessment:** ES and E parent predictions work (~67% combined hit rate). The Intelligence parent prediction fails — only 1 of 10 intersection facets shows the predicted C↔O mutual cross-loading (10% hit rate, down from 22%). The C→O and O→C genetic bridges predicted by the I parent simply do not exist in the data.
**V1→V2 change:** Removing N5 (the only facet with a positive O cross-loading) from I×ES made the Intelligence prediction worse. A3's E loading (.37) improved ES×E slightly.

### H5: Facets Co-Develop Within BT Zones

**Data:** Developmental trajectories from Terracciano (2005, L), Brandt (2023, L), Schwaba (2022, M)
**Assessment:** Only 1 of 4 test cases (N2 → ES×E) shows stronger zone-mate than domain-mate developmental similarity. The v2 center triangle is developmentally heterogeneous — E3 increases, E4/N5 decline, E6/A6 are mixed, A1 has an inverted-U. The N5–C6 developmental mirror (strongest cross-domain signal in the corpus) is no longer a zone-mate comparison in v2.
**V1→V2 change:** Reassigning N5 from I×ES to center broke the N5–C6 mirror relationship. Reassigning A1 from ES core to center added another ambiguous case.

### H6: Developmental Rank-Order Stability by Zone

**Data:** Brandt (2023, L) Tables S13–S17 — latent 4-year retest correlations for all 30 NEO-PI-R facets (previously untestable)
**Zone means:** Primary .886, Intersection .873, Center .869, Edge .926
**Assessment:** Monotonic ordering matches prediction (Primary > Intersection > Center), but differences are trivially small (.017 primary-vs-center, d ≈ 0.35). Two outlier facets drive the pattern: A1 Trust (.779, lowest) depresses center; E2 Gregariousness (.943, highest) elevates primary. Without A1, center = primary (.886). Edge/outside facets are unexpectedly the most stable zone (.926), contradicting the idea that primary cores should be most environmentally resistant.
**Supplementary:** Ringwald (2024, L) BFI-2 facet stability (ages 14–23) shows convergent patterns: Sociability (≈E2) is most stable (.80); Depression (≈N3) is least stable early (.32).

### H7: Brilliance Composite vs Openness for Intelligence

**Data:** Stanek & Ones (2023, XL) personality–GMA meta-analysis
**V2 center composite:** (.11 + .23 + .04 + .02 + -.05 + .10) / 6 = .075
**Openness benchmark:** .26 (Intellect aspect: .40)
**Assessment:** Center composite (.075) is far below Openness (.26). Intelligence signal concentrates in intersection zones: Intellect .40 (I×E), Industriousness .32 (I×ES). E4 Activity (.23) is the only center facet with a meaningful intelligence signal.
**BFI-2 confirmation:** Rammstedt et al. (2026, XL, N=67,927, 12 countries) shows identical pattern with BFI-2 facets. Center-zone facets average β = .003; O-intersection facets average β = .067.

### H8: Center-Triangle Predicts Life Satisfaction

**Data:** Haehner (2025, L), Schimmack (2004, unrated), Røysamb (2018, M)
**V2 zone means (|r|):** Primary .332, Intersection .303, Center .236, Edge .025
**Assessment:** Prediction completely inverted. Primary cores (Depression -.52, N4 -.35, N6 -.31) dominate LS prediction. E6 Positive Emotions (.40) is the one strong center facet. The center triangle as a group does not concentrate well-being variance.
**BFI-2 confirmation:** Danner et al. (2019, L, N=1,224) shows identical pattern with BFI-2 facets. Depression β = -.50 (primary) >> Energy Level β = .13 (center).

### H9: Outside Facets Show Weakest Biological Signatures

**Data:** All corpus studies, 12 biological indicators per facet
**V2 outside set:** O4, O6, A5, E5 (was A5, O6, C5 + E5 quasi-outside in v1)
**Per-facet hit rates:** O6 82%, A5 64%, O4 ~63%, E5 55%
**Assessment:** Replacing C5 (33% — biologically well-integrated) with O4 (~63% — most cross-culturally unstable) strengthened the verdict. The v2 outside set is more biologically coherent: all 4 are BFI-2-excluded, none have molecular associations, all have near-zero criterion correlations. Different anomaly types: O6 = near-zero heritability; A5 = zero specificity + reversed MZ/DZ; E5 = extreme genetic specificity; O4 = cross-cultural instability.
**BFI-2 convergence:** All 4 v2 outside facets are excluded from BFI-2 (4-for-4 hit).

### H10: Cross-Cultural Genetic Congruence

**Data:** Yamagata (2006, XL) 3-country loadings, Pilia (2006, XL) VCC
**V2 VCC means:** Edge .953, Primary .940, Intersection .923, Center .920
**Unstable top-10:** Primary 0%, Intersection 33%, Center 50%, Outside 50%
**Stable top-10:** Primary 80%, Intersection 33%, Center 0%, Outside 25%
**Assessment:** Primary cores are clearly the most stable by proportional analysis (0% unstable, 80% stable). But by VCC mean, primary (.940) is no longer highest — edge/outside (.953) surpasses it because E5 (.99 VCC) moved to outside. Center-vs-Intersection ordering corrected in v2 (center now lower, as predicted). Domain-level prediction still fails: C (intersection) ≥ E (primary).
**Cross-national BFI-2 data:** Rammstedt et al. (2026) shows Assertiveness (center) has the most cross-nationally stable intelligence association (SD=.03), while C and O facets show the most variation (SD=.07–.08).

## Overall Scorecard

| Category | Hypotheses | V2 Results |
|---|---|---|
| **Supported** | H3 (cross-domain ratios) | Strongly supported — model's best result |
| **Partially/weakly supported** | H4, H5, H6, H9, H10 | Mixed evidence; some strengthen, some weaken with v2 |
| **Not supported** | H1, H2a, H7, H8 | Predictions wrong or effect sizes negligible |

### What the model gets RIGHT:
1. **Center-triangle facets are genetically multi-domain** (H3) — this is the single strongest empirical finding
2. **ES and E function as genetic parent factors** for intersection zones (H4)
3. **Outside facets are biologically anomalous** (H9) — confirmed by BFI-2 exclusions
4. **Primary cores are cross-culturally stable** (H10, by proportions)

### What the model gets WRONG:
1. **Intelligence is not a third primary** — C↔O genetic bridge does not exist (H4); center triangle does not concentrate intelligence variance (H7)
2. **Center triangle does not predict well-being** (H8) — ES core dominates
3. **Center facets have MORE genetic specificity than predicted** (H2a) — reversed from prediction
4. **Heritability and stability differences between zones are trivially small** (H1, H6)

### The structural core:
The model's strongest contribution is the H3 finding: the v2 center-triangle facets (E3, E4, E6, A1, A6, N5) are genuinely and dramatically more genetically diffuse than all other facets. This is not a zone-assignment artifact — these 6 facets sit in the top 6 of all 30 facets by genetic diffuseness, regardless of how zones are drawn. Whether this diffuseness reflects a "Brilliance" construct or simply measurement impurity is the unresolved question.
