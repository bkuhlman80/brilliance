### The structural implication

The v2 center-triangle facets (E3, E4, E6, A1, A6, N5) all have moderate residual h² (.27–.30) — meaning they each retain meaningful genetic variance AFTER removing all five Big Five domain factors. This residual could represent:
1. "Brilliance" variance — a sixth factor not captured by the Big Five rotation
2. Facet-specific genetic content unrelated to any domain
3. Measurement-specific genetic variance

The data cannot distinguish these interpretations. But the finding that ALL six v2 center facets have comparable, non-zero residual h² is consistent with them sharing something genetically beyond the five-factor structure.
