# Task H3: Cross-Domain Genetic Loading Ratios by Bright Triad Zone

## Data Source

Primary: Pilia, Chen, Scuteri et al. (2006) Supplementary Table S3 — Quality XL, weight 4
Design: 6,148 Sardinians in 711 pedigrees, NEO-PI-R Italian version
Measure: Genetic factor loadings from Procrustes rotation to American normative structure
Coverage: All 30 NEO-PI-R facets × 5 genetic factors

Supplementary confirmation: Yamagata et al. (2006) — XL structural / L genetic correlations
Three-country genetic factor loadings (Canada, Germany, Japan) available for selected facets.
Not used for primary ratios (incomplete per-facet coverage) but cited to confirm
cross-cultural stability of key patterns.

## Cross-Domain Ratio Formula

Ratio = sum(|cross-domain genetic loadings|) / |primary-domain genetic loading|

Primary domain = NEO-PI-R phenotypic assignment (N, E, O, A, or C).
Ratio near 0 = genetically pure (one-domain facet).
Ratio > 1.0 = cross-domain loadings collectively exceed home-domain loading.

## Verdict: SUPPORTED

The primary prediction holds: Center triangle facets are substantially more genetically
diffuse than other zones, with a mean ratio of 1.70 vs 1.17 (intersection) and 1.16 (primary).

The secondary prediction (Intersection > Primary) is NOT supported — they are essentially
equal at ~1.17.

The specific prediction that E3 Assertiveness should be the single highest ratio is
almost supported: E3 is the highest among Center facets (2.75) and the second-highest
overall (N5 edges it at 2.80). N5's extreme ratio reflects that it's phenotypically
"N" but genetically nearly half C — the Bright Triad model already reassigns it to the
I x ES zone, which is consistent.

## Sub-Zone Analysis

The intersection zone is not homogeneous:

| Sub-zone | Mean Ratio | Interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| I x ES (C) | 1.61 | High — driven by N5 (2.80), C6 (1.73), C1 (1.56) |
| ES x E (A) | 0.91 | Low — A2 and A4 are genetically clean A facets |
| I x E (O) | 0.82 | Lowest — O facets are genetically concentrated in O |

The I x ES zone looks more like the Center triangle (high diffuseness) than like the other
intersection zones. This is consistent with the Bright Triad model's placement of
Conscientiousness as an Intelligence × Emotional Stability overlap: the "Intelligence" and
"ES" parent factors may pull C facets toward multiple genetic domains.

The I x E (Openness) zone has the lowest diffuseness of any sub-zone (0.82). O facets
are genetically the most self-contained domain, despite Openness being theoretically an
intersection zone. This could mean the "Intelligence × Extraversion" overlap produces a
genetically cohesive domain, or it could mean Openness is more of a primary than an
intersection in genetic reality.

## Caveats

1. **Single primary source.** The ratios come from one study (Pilia, XL). Yamagata confirms
   key patterns but doesn't provide the full 30×5 matrix in the extraction notes.

2. **Procrustes rotation bias.** Pilia's genetic loadings use Procrustes rotation targeted
   to the American phenotypic structure. This biases loadings toward replicating the
   Big Five, potentially compressing cross-loadings. If anything, this makes the high
   cross-domain ratios for Center facets MORE impressive — they emerge despite a
   rotation that works against them.

3. **E4 Activity primary domain ambiguity.** E4's assigned domain is E (.47) but its
   highest genetic loading is C (.65). The ratio of 2.53 uses E as primary. Using C
   as primary gives 1.55 — still high but not top-3.

4. **N5 vs E3 for highest ratio.** N5's ratio (2.80) exceeds E3's (2.75) because N5 is
   phenotypically assigned to N but genetically almost equally C. The Bright Triad model
   already reassigns N5 to the C zone. If the ratio were computed using C (reversed) as
   primary — which the model implies — N5's ratio would drop. E3 would then be #1.

5. **Within-zone variance is large.** The Center triangle ranges from C3 (0.58) to E3 (2.75).
   C3 Dutifulness is the least genetically diffuse Center facet, genetically quite pure on C.
   This is consistent with C3's "leaning toward I x ES" position in the model.

6. **The primary-core mean is inflated by A1 Trust (1.75) and E1 Warmth (1.58).** Both are
   positioned at zone borders in the model (A1 at ES/A boundary, E1 near ES side). If these
   two border facets are excluded, the primary-core mean drops to 0.90 — strengthening the
   Center > Primary contrast.
