### Structural observations across H7–H10

1. **The v2 center triangle is weaker than v1 for criterion prediction.** Moving A3 (Compassion), C3 (Dependability), and O4 (Variety Seeking) out of the center — and replacing them with A1 (Trust) and N5 (Impulsiveness reversed) — makes the center triangle less intelligence-correlated (H7) and less LS-predictive (H8). The v2 center is psychologically more coherent (all facets are genetically multi-domain), but less criterion-valid.

2. **The v2 outside set is more biologically coherent.** Replacing C5 (biologically well-integrated in C) with O4 (most cross-culturally unstable primary loading) creates a cleaner outside category. The v2 outside facets {O4, O6, A5, E5} are all BFI-2-excluded (or likely excluded) and lack molecular associations.

3. **H7 and H8 converge on the same conclusion in both v1 and v2.** The "Brilliance" triple intersection does not concentrate criterion-relevant variance. Intelligence and life satisfaction signals sit in the intersection zones (Industriousness, Intellect) and primary cores (Emotional Stability), not the center triangle. This conclusion is robust to zone reassignment.

4. **H9 is the only hypothesis that improves from v1 to v2.** The v2 zone assignments better delineate which facets are biologically anomalous (outside) versus well-integrated (intersection). C5's reclassification from outside to intersection is the single most impactful v2 change for any hypothesis — it eliminates a clear misclassification.

5. **H10 shows a tension between VCC means and proportional analysis.** By proportions (% in unstable/stable top-10), primary cores remain clearly the most stable. But by VCC means, the v2 primary zone is no longer the absolute highest because its three highest-VCC members (A1, E1, E5) were redistributed.
