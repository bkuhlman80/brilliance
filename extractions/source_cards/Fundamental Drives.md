# Theories of Fundamental Motivation & Drives

### 1\. Attachment Theory (Bowlby) — Most extensively represented

Behavioral systems, internal working models, secure base, goal-corrected partnerships. Brain's attachment system as an evolved survival mechanism.

* Bowlby (1969/1982) — Attachment and Loss, Vol. 1 (entries \#631–635, 5 parts)  
* Bowlby (1973) — Separation, Vol. 2 (entries \#636–640)  
* Bowlby (1980) — Loss, Vol. 3 (entries \#641–645)  
* Hart, Shaver & Goldenberg (2005) — attachment \+ terror management integration (\#617)

### 2\. Allostasis / Predictive Bodily Regulation — Second most represented

The brain's primary function is predictive energy regulation (allostasis), not cognition per se. Interoception at the core.

* Theriault, Katsumi, Reimann et al. (2025) — "It's not the thought that counts" (\#533) — THE allostasis-first paper  
* Katsumi, Theriault, Quigley & Barrett (2022) — hierarchical cortical gradients serve allostasis  
* Barrett (2015) — EPIC model, interoception, predictive coding  
* Barrett (2017) — constructed emotion theory; brain's primary function is allostasis (\#26)  
* Juster, McEwen & Lupien (2010) — allostatic load review, HPA/SAM axes  
* Mehrhof, Fleming & Nord (2025) — interoceptive energy allostasis (\#356)  
* Booth et al. (2015) — allostatic load and brain structure (\#78)  
* Feng et al. (2025) — allostatic load and white matter aging (\#172)  
* Finlay et al. (2026) — meta-analysis of allostatic load in psychiatric disorders (\#177)  
* McEwen (2007/2022) — life-cycle stress model

### 3\. Panksepp's Primary Emotional Systems

Seven (later nine) subcortical emotional circuits as the fundamental affective operating systems of all mammals: SEEKING, RAGE, FEAR, LUST, CARE, GRIEF/PANIC, PLAY (+ DISGUST, POWER/dominance).

* Panksepp (2010) — "Affective neuroscience of the emotional BrainMind" (\#653)  
* Entry \#654 — extension to nine systems \+ Affective Neuronal Selection  
* Kernberg (2016) — integrates Panksepp's systems as temperamental substrate (\#623)

### 4\. Cybernetic Big Five Theory (DeYoung)

Each Big Five domain \= variation in parameters of evolved cybernetic (goal-directed feedback) mechanisms. E=reward sensitivity, N=defensive responses, O/I=cognitive exploration, C=protection of non-immediate goals, A=coordination of goals.

* DeYoung (2015) — "Cybernetic Big Five Theory" (\#142) — THE foundational paper  
* Quirin, Robinson, Rauthmann, Kuhl, Read, Tops & DeYoung (2020) — Dynamics of Personality Approach, integrates CB5T with PSI theory (\#425)

### 5\. Evolved Social Motives (Kenrick / evolutionary psychology)

Seven evolved social motives: self-protection, disease avoidance, affiliation, status, mate acquisition, mate retention, kin care.

* Ko et al. (2020) — "Fundamental social motives" across 27 countries (\#287)  
* Pick et al. (2022) — FSMI instrument, 42 cultures, N=15,915 (\#418)  
* Michalski & Shackelford (2010) — personality as output of domain-specific evolved mechanisms (\#359)  
* Figueredo et al. (2005) — four evolutionary theories of personality variation (\#176)

### 6\. Approach-Avoidance Motivation

The most ancient motivational axis — bilateral organisms steering toward reward and away from threat — as the evolutionary root of extraversion/neuroticism.

* Bennett (2023), Ch. 2 — "The origin of good and bad" (\#38)  
* Read, Brown, Wang & Miller (2021) — Big Five emerges from hierarchical approach/avoidance (\#429)  
* Read & Miller (2026) — Virtual Personality Model (\#430)

### 7\. Life History Theory (Fast-Slow Continuum)

Organisms allocate finite energy between current reproduction vs. future survival; personality maps onto fast-slow life history strategies.

* Ellis, Figueredo, Brumbach & Schlomer (2009) — "Fundamental dimensions of environmental risk" (\#162) — THE foundational paper  
* Figueredo et al. (2005) — life history alongside frequency-dependent selection (\#176)  
* Brans & Johnstone (2013) — pace-of-life syndrome in dog breeds  
* Biro & Stamps (2010) — metabolism-personality links, energy budget models

### 8\. Reinforcement Sensitivity Theory / BIS-BAS (Gray)

Behavioral Inhibition System (anxiety/avoidance) and Behavioral Activation System (reward approach) as fundamental dimensions of temperament.

* Johnson, Edge, Holmes & Carver (2012) — BAS hypersensitivity in mania (\#264)  
* Tops, Boksem, Luu & Tucker (2010) — four neuromodulator systems mapped to personality (\#542)

### 9\. Incentive Salience / Wanting vs. Liking (Berridge)

Dopamine encodes wanting (incentive salience), not liking (hedonic pleasure). Fundamental dissociation between motivation and reward.

* Bennett (2023), Ch. 3 — Berridge's rat experiments (\#39)  
* Bennett (2023), Ch. 5 — TD learning, dopamine prediction error (\#41)  
* Depue & Morrone-Strupinsky (2005) — dopamine \= incentive salience of affiliative stimuli, mu-opiates \= consummatory pleasure (\#139)  
* Tops et al. (2010) — reactive "wanting" (dopamine) vs. proactive "broadening" (norepinephrine) (\#542)

### 10\. Agency & Communion (Bakan's "Big Two")

Two meta-dimensions of human social existence: getting ahead (agency/dominance) vs. getting along (communion/affiliation).

* Fiske, Cuddy & Glick — warmth x competence model across 19 nations  
* Hopwood, Wright, Ansell & Pincus (2013) — interpersonal circumplex, DSM-5 AMPD (\#238)  
* Pincus (2018) — agency/communion as meta-constructs for personality pathology  
* Thalmayer, Saucier & Ole-Kotikash — cross-cultural Big Two assessment

### 11\. Terror Management Theory

Awareness of mortality as a fundamental motivational engine; self-esteem and cultural worldviews serve as anxiety buffers against death awareness.

* Hart, Shaver & Goldenberg (2005) — tripartite security system model (\#617)  
* Hart (2014) — integrative theory of psychological defense (\#618) — integrates TMT with attachment, self-affirmation, meaning maintenance

### 12\. Social Brain / Machiavellian Intelligence Hypothesis

Social complexity drove primate brain expansion; neocortex ratio correlates with group size.

* Bennett (2023), Ch. 10 — Dunbar's social brain hypothesis  
* Byrne (1996) — "Machiavellian intelligence" (\#647)  
* Reader & Laland (2002) — innovation \+ social learning \+ brain size (\#431)  
* Andrews, Ahmed & Blakemore (2021) — social brain development in adolescence (\#14)  
* MacLean et al. (2014) — self-control across 36 species; brain size, not group size, predicts self-control

### 13\. Exploration-Exploitation Tradeoff

Curiosity as intrinsic reinforcement; organisms must balance exploiting known resources vs. exploring for new ones.

* Bennett (2023), Ch. 8 — "Why life got curious" (\#44)  
* Blanco & Sloutsky (2021) — exploration-exploitation in cognitive development  
* Wolf, van Doorn & Weissing — evolutionary game theory maintaining personality polymorphism via exploration-exploitation

### 14\. Constructed Emotion Theory (Barrett)

Emotions are not biologically hardwired categories but constructed via predictive coding and categorization — a direct alternative to Panksepp/Ekman.

* Barrett (2017) — "Theory of constructed emotion" (\#26)  
* Barrett (2015) — EPIC model

### 15\. Self-Domestication Hypothesis

Homo sapiens evolved via selection against reactive aggression, reshaping the entire cognitive-social-morphological phenotype toward prosociality.

* Hare, Wobber & Wrangham (2012) — self-domestication hypothesis (\#221)  
* Hare (2017) — "Survival of the friendliest" (\#222)

### 16\. Epistemic Motivation / Need for Cognition

Stable individual differences in intrinsic motivation toward effortful thought; the "reflective mind" as a thinking disposition.

* Stanovich (2012) — tripartite model, need for cognition as reflective mind (\#513)  
* Stanovich, West & Toplak (2013) — thinking dispositions predict bias independently of IQ (\#514)

### 17\. Default Mode / Triple Network Model

Salience Network dynamically switches between Central Executive and Default Mode; DMN serves predictive simulation and allostatic regulation.

* Menon (2011) — triple network model (SN/CEN/DMN)  
* Tops et al. (2014) — PARCS theory, DMN as predictive control (\#543)

### 18\. Drive Theory (Hull/Freud) — Present only as a foil

Secondary drive theory ("cupboard love") appears only in Bowlby's critique.

* Bowlby (1969/1982) — demolishes Hull-Dollard-Miller and Freudian drive theory using Harlow's experiments (\#631–632)

### 19\. CAPS — Cognitive-Affective Personality System

Personality as a network of if...then cognitive-affective units activated by situational features.

* Mischel & Shoda (1995) — foundational CAPS paper (\#367)
