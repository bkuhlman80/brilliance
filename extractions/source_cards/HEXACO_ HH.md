# HEXACO: HH

## Honesty-Humility, Authenticity, Deception & Related Constructs: What We Have

### HEXACO HONESTY-HUMILITY (H FACTOR)

Ashton, Lee & Goldberg (2004) — Entry \#19 | Quality: Structural M (N=310, small for 1,710 adjectives)  
Seminal lexical study first recovering Honesty-Humility as a sixth factor in English. Six-factor solution produces an "unpretentious vs. sly" dimension paralleling findings in seven other languages. Historically foundational for the HEXACO model.  
Ashton & Lee (2007) — Entry \#20 | Quality: Publication prestige XL (PSPR)  
Reviews empirical, theoretical, and practical advantages of HEXACO over Big Five. Key finding: HEXACO better predicts workplace delinquency, sexual harassment, and Dark Triad traits than Big Five. Evolutionary framework distinguishing kin-altruism from reciprocal-altruism tendencies — H, A, and E are the three factors that differ most from Big Five.  
Ashton, Lee & de Vries (2014) — Entry \#22 | Quality: Publication prestige XL (PSPR)  
H-H is the single strongest personality predictor of unethical behavior and dark personality traits. Big Five Agreeableness and Neuroticism are 45-degree rotations of HEXACO H, A, and E — collapsing distinct constructs. Robust sex differences in Emotionality support the evolutionary interpretation.  
Ashton & Lee (2016) — Entry \#24 | Quality: N \~97,583 cross-sectional  
Massive internet sample provides facet-level age trends for all 25 HEXACO-PI-R facets. H-H facets show developmental trajectories that don't map onto NEO patterns.  
Lewis & Bates (2014) — Entry \#310 | Quality: Heritability L (TwinsUK twin study)  
Genetic architecture of HEXACO dimensions. For most dimensions including H-H, genetic covariation among facets is explained by a single common genetic factor — supporting domains as coherent biological systems. Conscientiousness is the exception.  
Ludeke et al. (2019) — Entry \#323 | Quality: Structural L (4 samples, meta-analytic)  
BFAS-to-HEXACO translation. Politeness (BFAS) maps strongly to H-H (r \~ .51); Compassion maps to HEXACO-A (r \~ .58). When both predict H-H simultaneously, Compassion β drops to .07 while Politeness β \= .48. The 10-aspect framework clarifies where Big Five and HEXACO genuinely differ.  
Romano et al. (2023) — Entry \#452 | Quality: Structural L (3 studies, CFA/bifactor)  
HEXACO Adjective Scales (60 items). Self-peer convergence notably lower for Honesty-Humility (r \= .27) compared to other domains — suggesting H-H is especially hard for others to accurately assess, or that people manage impressions on this dimension.  
Misiuro et al. (2022) — Entry \#368 | Quality: Structural M (Polish adaptation)  
Polish HEXACO observer report shows acceptable reliability but PCA yields 7 components rather than 6\. Attitude toward the target person substantially affects recovered factor structure — relevant to whether H-H can be validly observed.  
Ørnfjord (2018) — Entry \#610 | Quality: Structural S (N=480)  
Norwegian HEXACO-PI-R validation. Big Five predicted only 20% of Honesty-Humility variance, confirming it captures genuinely novel content.  
De Raad et al. (2014) — Entry \#121 | Quality: Structural L (11 lexical studies, N=7,104)  
Cross-linguistic mega-analysis: only three factors replicate with high congruence across all 14 languages (Dynamism, Affiliation, Order). H-H does NOT consistently replicate cross-culturally — it appears at the 6-factor level but drops below .80 congruence in several languages.  
Centrality analysis (from extractions/personality/) — H-H has no truly peripheral facets (lowest centrality ratio \= .80). Its facets consistently cross-load on Agreeableness and Conscientiousness, suggesting structural interweaving across domains. H-H facets (Sincerity, Greediness) have weak links to all Big Five Aspect Scales, representing content genuinely beyond Big Five space.

### AUTHENTICITY & REALNESS

Hopwood et al. (2021) — Entry \#233 | Quality: Structural L (9 studies, N \> 6,000)  
Across nine studies, realness is the core unidimensional feature of authenticity. 12-item Realness Scale. Notably unrelated to Agreeableness — authenticity is not niceness. Correlated with lower Neuroticism, higher Extraversion, Openness, Conscientiousness. Test-retest r \= .74 over 5 months.  
Hopwood, Nissen & Bleidorn (2024) — Entry \#234 | Quality: Developmental M (N=412, 18-month longitudinal)  
Realness is highly stable over 18 months (nonsignificant slope). Replicated Big Five correlates. Associated with lower stress and higher social support. Suggests authenticity/realness is trait-like.  
Kernis & Goldman (2006) — Entry \#281 | Quality: Publication prestige L (Advances in Experimental Social Psychology chapter)  
Authenticity \= awareness \+ unbiased processing \+ behavioral authenticity \+ relational orientation. CFA supports hierarchical four-component model (N=294). Predicts lower verbal defensiveness, higher mindfulness, adaptive coping, well-being, relationship satisfaction. Parental authoritativeness (not authoritarianism) predicts adult authenticity. Authenticity predicts well-being independently of self-esteem level.  
Womick, Foltz & King (2019) — Entry \#597 | Quality: Structural M (2 MTurk samples, N=404 \+ 415\)  
Dark Tetrad moderates the authenticity-well-being link: among those high on dark traits, authenticity is less strongly related to well-being. At low authenticity, dark traits are associated with higher well-being — inauthenticity may be adaptive for those with callous/manipulative personalities. Narcissism positively associated with authenticity; psychopathy and sadism negatively. Challenges universal humanistic assumptions.  
Miller et al. (narcissism trifurcated model) — Entry \#355 | Quality: Structural M (N=273)  
Grandiose narcissism unexpectedly correlated with inauthenticity measures despite positive well-being associations. Antagonism and Neuroticism together account for most associations with inauthenticity and reduced well-being.

### DARK TRIAD / DARK TETRAD

Hudson (2023) — Entry \#246 | Quality: Experimental L (16-wave weekly design, N=467)  
Agreeableness interventions produced collateral reductions in all three Dark Triad traits (Machiavellianism, narcissism, psychopathy). Demonstrates Dark Triad is causally linked to low Agreeableness and that dark traits are malleable through personality intervention.  
Martindale et al. (2025) — Entry \#342 | Quality: Structural L (N=870 \+ N=201, test-retest \>390 days)  
Faceted Dark Triad (FDT) Scale: 27 non-redundant facets consolidated from three FFM-based measures. Explains 37% more variance in antisocial outcomes than Short Dark Triad. All test-retest stabilities \> .77. Provides facet-level granularity.  
Kowalski et al. (Dark Triad measurement) — Entry \#295 | Quality: Structural M  
Evaluates psychometric limitations of Dirty Dozen and Short Dark Triad measures. Argues Machiavellianism and psychopathy share an "antagonism core."

### DECEPTION & MANIPULATION (ANIMAL BEHAVIOR)

Bennett (2023) — Ch. 4 (Mentalizing) — Entry in book chapters | Quality: Publication prestige L  
Menzel's experiments with chimpanzees Belle and Rock: Belle learned to suppress signals about hidden food when Rock was present and exploiting her knowledge — strategic deception providing evidence for sophisticated social cognition in apes. Framed within Dunbar's social brain hypothesis.  
Bjorkqvist, Lagerspetz & Kaukiainen (1992) — Entry \#62 | Quality: Structural M  
Developmental trends in direct vs. indirect (social manipulation) aggression. Girls use indirect/manipulative aggression more; boys use direct physical aggression. Indirect aggression requires social intelligence.

### ANIMAL PERSONALITY STUDIES

No animal personality studies in the collection directly incorporate Honesty-Humility, trustworthiness, or authenticity as measured variables. The animal personality papers (macaques, great apes, dogs, etc.) use the HPQ (Hominoid Personality Questionnaire) or Réale et al. (2007) framework, which captures Boldness, Exploration, Activity, Sociability, and Aggressiveness — but nothing mapping to H-H. The closest connection is:

* Gosling & John (cited in multiple entries): comparative personality reviews note that the Big Five map reasonably onto primate personality dimensions (Friendliness, Assertiveness, Openness, Neuroticism), but a sixth factor analogous to H-H has not been recovered in animal studies  
* Ashton & Lee (2007) explicitly ground H-H in reciprocal altruism theory and contrast it with kin-altruism (Emotionality) — the evolutionary argument implies H-H should exist wherever reciprocal cooperation is important, but empirical animal personality work hasn't measured it  
* Strategic deception in primates (Menzel's Belle/Rock; Machiavellian intelligence hypothesis) is documented behaviorally but not linked to individual-difference personality dimensions

### KEY GAPS

1. No direct animal personality measurement of H-H analogs — this is a genuine gap. Reciprocal altruism is documented in many species, but individual differences in "honesty" or "trustworthiness" haven't been systematically assessed as personality traits in non-humans.  
2. Cross-cultural universality is questionable — De Raad et al. (2014) shows H-H doesn't replicate robustly across all languages at the lexical level, suggesting it may be culturally shaped rather than biologically universal.  
3. Low observer agreement — Romano et al. (2023) finds self-peer convergence for H-H is only r \= .27, the lowest of any HEXACO domain. This raises questions about whether H-H reflects internal dispositions vs. strategic self-presentation.
