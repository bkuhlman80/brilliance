*Structural note: Stanovich's tripartite model (Autonomous / Algorithmic / Reflective) is the closest existing cognitive-architecture framework to HPAM's Go/Turn/V structure. The Autonomous Mind maps to Turn (heterogeneous, no general factor, includes both innate and compiled routines). The Algorithmic Mind maps to Go (computational capacity, fluid intelligence). The Reflective Mind maps to the V dimension of SALMON measurement — evaluative governance of one's own defaults. Stanovich does not know about HPAM; these mappings are Brian's synthesis moves.*

**COMP-STA-TPT-01 `[MECHANISM] [FRAMING MOVE]`**

**Claim:** Stanovich's tripartite model splits Type 2 processing into two separable levels: the Algorithmic Mind (defined by computational efficiency — the capacity for cognitive decoupling and sustained secondary representation) and the Reflective Mind (defined by intentional-level functioning — goal management, epistemic self-regulation, and the decision to initiate an override). The Autonomous Mind (TASS) retains the defining feature of autonomy — mandatory execution upon encountering triggering stimuli, independent of high-level control. Each level has a unique capability the others cannot perform: TASS does parallel processing, the Algorithmic Mind does quarantined simulation, and the Reflective Mind sends the "instructional call" that initiates override. The three interact in a default-interventionist sequence: autonomous default → reflective detection → instructional call → algorithmic execution.

**Relevant to:** Go/Turn architecture, scaling relationship, override mechanism, Vitality gestalt

## **(MASK) Greene, *Moral Tribes***
