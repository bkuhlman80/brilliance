## Triple audit findings

Triple form convention applies. Per `Label-Change_Rubric.md` Section B, Test 8 (Triple labeling: form convention), triple labels default to noun (construct-name) form; adjectival forms admissible only when discipline-anchored as a measured bipolar dimension with both poles named in the literature. All four current Bilaterians triple labels are character-type adjectives (Hungry, Dominant, Stress Resistant) or adjective-phrase (Aimless) without bipolar-dimension anchors in the literature; all four fail Test 8. The Bilaterians triple table also has no Notes column, an automatic Test 9 fail.
