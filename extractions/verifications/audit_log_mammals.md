The vocabulary-surfacing pass at Mammals produced one partial confirmation and three replacement-candidate signals among the four pair labels. M-1 (Conscientious-Habitual) shows zero surfacing of either label half at the mammal level, with three-field convergence on "behavioral repeatability" — the canonical animal-personality measurement construct from Réale and colleagues. M-2 (Agreeable-Cooperative) shows zero surfacing of either label half, with three-field convergence on "social learning" and a visible split into three sub-traditions corresponding to the persona disciplines: P1 developmental-attachment, P2 social-information-use, P3 Laland's social-learning-strategies framework. M-3 (Playful) shows zero surfacing of the existing label including from P1 (affective neuroscience), the persona most likely to produce Panksepp vocabulary; P1 reaches for Panksepp's PANIC/GRIEF system instead, suggesting a possible function-anchor mismatch — the function "Affiliation informs Excitability" maps more naturally to social regulation of arousal than to play behavior. The Mammals pair tables in `Inheritance_Regimes_v2_5.md` have Notes columns for both pairs born here (Affiliation × Mastery, Affiliation × Excitability), so the auto-Test-9 failure does not apply at the pair level. One structural finding worth explicit flag: across M-2, M-3, M-5, and M-6, attachment-derived vocabulary (secure base, attachment-based regulation, Bowlby/Ainsworth lineage) appears with consistent borrowed-flag density, indicating that the mammalian psychology field's native vocabulary in the affiliation-related cells is genuinely thin and leans heavily on human-developmental constructs.

## Triple audit findings

Triple form convention applies. Per `Label-Change_Rubric.md` Section B, Test 8 (Triple labeling: form convention), triple labels default to noun (construct-name) form; adjectival forms admissible only when discipline-anchored as a measured bipolar dimension with both poles named in the literature. All four current Mammals triple labels are character-type adjectives (Alloparental, Assertive, Aggressive, Neurotic); all four fail Test 8. The Born-at-Mammals triple table at line 384 of `Inheritance_Regimes_v2_5.md` also has no Notes column, an automatic Test 9 fail across the three triples born here (Assertive, Neurotic, Alloparental). The Aggressive triple is a persistence cell that shares the form-convention failure but whose anchor question is governed at the Vertebrates entry where it was introduced. Recommended cleanup independent of label decisions: add a Notes column to the Born-at-Mammals triple table.
