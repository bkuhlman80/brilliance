## Pair audit findings

### Primates pairs

The vocabulary-surfacing pass at Primates produced one partial confirmation and three replacement-candidate signals among the four pair labels. All four current pair labels are Big Five aspect names (Conscientious-Habitual, Agreeable-Cooperative, Extraverted-Sociable, Open-Curious), each with "Big Five" as the entire content of the Notes column. P-1 (Conscientious-Habitual) shows zero surfacing of "Conscientious" at this cell with three-field convergence on coping style (proactive end) — Koolhaas-anchored, the same proactive-coping cluster that decided M-1 in the Mammals audit. P-2 (Agreeable-Cooperative) shows zero surfacing of either label half, with three-field convergence on triadic awareness and on the social-intelligence cluster, plus a visible split: P2 (EP) frames the cell as social learning / skill acquisition (Tomasello/Herrmann cultural-intelligence lineage), while P1 (BE) and P3 (B&C) frame it as Machiavellian-intelligence / social-competence deployment. The split is not noise — the function description ("Affiliation informs Mastery") is genuinely ambiguous between acquisition and deployment readings. P-3 (Extraverted-Sociable) shows zero surfacing of either label half with the strongest replacement-candidate signal in the pair set — three-field top-2 convergence on partner choice (flexibility) and coalition fluidity, vocabulary that names *revising* affiliation rather than *being toward* others; the existing trait-static label misses the cell's dynamic content (Autonomy revises Affiliation). P-4 (Open-Curious) is partially confirmed; "Openness" surfaces as an explicit primate-Big-Five term in P2 (EP) with King & Figueredo 1997 chimp anchor, and the wider innovation/exploration/neophilia cluster has three-field support, though only P2 bridges to the trait-aggregate label. The Primates pair tables in `Inheritance_Regimes_v2_5.md` have Notes columns for both pairs born here (Autonomy × Affiliation, Autonomy × Boldness), so the auto-Test-9 failure does not apply at the pair level — but the existing Notes content "Big Five" for both pairs is a Test-9 marginal case (the Big Five anchor is real and primary at the human level, but uses the term as a factor-aggregate rather than as the cell-level dimensional construct the framework needs; the operationalization phrase that would close the discipline-native reader's loop is not in the column). One structural finding worth explicit flag: across all four pairs, the personas surface *cell-level behavioral dimensions* in field-native primate-ethology / EP / cognitive vocabulary, while the existing labels speak *aggregate human-personality measurement* — convergence on the existing labels was structurally unlikely under this experimental design. The persona pass is generating a different kind of evidence than direct confirmation/replacement signal at this level.

## Cross-cell anomaly: Conscientiousness migration (P-1 ↔ P-7)

The strongest cross-cell signal in the Primates pass is a structural mismatch between where the framework places "Conscientious" and where one persona's primate-personality vocabulary places it.

The framework places "Conscientious" at P-1 (Mastery × Boldness pair, currently labeled Conscientious-Habitual). The function description for P-1 is "Mastery stabilizes Boldness across instances" — skill stabilizing risk-taking.

P2 (EP) places "Conscientiousness" at P-7 (Autonomy → Mastery → Boldness chain, Reactor cell with Affiliation absent, currently labeled Callous). At P-7, P2 cites King & Figueredo 1997 as the canonical primate-Big-Five anchor and lists it alongside persistence, mastery motivation, effortful control, industriousness, and grit — the cluster that converges on goal-directed self-directed effort yielding stable competence under risk.

At P-1, P2 names different terms — repeatability, cross-situational consistency, predictability/low rIIV, proactive coping style, behavioral syndrome integration, behavioral canalization, self-efficacy. None of these are Conscientiousness. The dimension P2 names at P-1 is measurement-stable individual difference in coupled risk-taking-and-skill expression; the dimension P2 names at P-7 is goal-driven self-directed effort that produces stable competence.

These are two different constructs in P2's vocabulary, and the P-7 construct is the one P2 names "Conscientiousness."

The framework currently treats them as related — Conscientious-Habitual at P-1, with the P-7 cell labeled Callous (a structural-absence-derived label, not a content-derived label). If P2's reading is taken seriously, the question is whether King & Figueredo's chimp Conscientiousness factor is empirically picking up the chain (autonomy → mastery → boldness, with Affiliation absent) rather than the pair (mastery × boldness). The chain content fits the EP cluster (persistence, effortful control, industriousness, grit — all about self-directed sustained effort) more cleanly than the pair content does.

Three possible resolutions, listed without endorsement:

1. **Migration accepted.** "Conscientious" moves from P-1 to P-7 and the P-1 label is replaced (proactive coping style is the current strongest candidate from P-1 surfacing). The P-7 label is replaced with Conscientiousness or persistence. This is the resolution P2's reading most directly suggests.

2. **Migration rejected.** "Conscientious" stays at P-1; P2's P-7 reading is treated as a primate-Big-Five-aggregation artifact rather than a structural correction (King & Figueredo's factor structure does not perfectly partition the framework's pair-vs-triple distinction). The current P-1 and P-7 labels both stand on framework-internal grounds.

3. **Both cells keep "Conscientious"-family content.** The framework recognizes that "Conscientious" at P-1 (the pair-level stable competence) and "Conscientious" at P-7 (the chain-level autonomous goal-driven competence) are related constructs at different levels of organization within Primates, and the labels reflect the relationship explicitly. This requires renaming both cells in a coordinated way.

The empirical question is whether the King & Figueredo 1997 chimp factor structure separates these two constructs or aggregates them. That would require going back to the original factor analyses and seeing what loads on Conscientiousness. This is out of scope for the surfacing pass but is the question that should drive the rubric-pass decision at both cells.
