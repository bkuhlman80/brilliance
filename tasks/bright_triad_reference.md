# Bright Triad Model — Reference Document

## The Model

The Bright Triad claims the Big Five personality domains are not five equal primaries. The real primary structure is three factors of the mental phenotype:

1. **Intelligence** (the full cognitive stack: reinforcement learning, simulation/model-building, mentalizing, symbolic language, rationality)
2. **Emotional Stability** (reversed Neuroticism)
3. **Extraversion**

The other three Big Five domains are pairwise intersections:

- **Conscientiousness** = Intelligence ∩ Emotional Stability
- **Openness** = Intelligence ∩ Extraversion
- **Agreeableness** = Emotional Stability ∩ Extraversion

The triple intersection is **Brilliance** — a directly measurable construct anchored by facets that load on multiple domains simultaneously.
