# Task H5: Facets Co-Develop More Tightly Within Bright Triad Zones Than Within Big Five Domains

## Reference
See `bright_triad_reference.md` for the model description and zone assignments.

## Hypothesis
When the Bright Triad reassigns a facet to a different zone than its Big Five domain, that facet's developmental trajectory should more closely resemble its Bright Triad zone-mates than its Big Five domain-mates.

## Key test cases

**Test case 1: N5 Impulsiveness**
- Big Five domain: Neuroticism
- Bright Triad zone: I∩ES (Conscientiousness zone, reversed)
- Prediction: N5's developmental trajectory should resemble C facets (especially C6 Deliberation) more than N facets (N1, N3, N6).

**Test case 2: E4 Activity**
- Big Five domain: Extraversion
- Bright Triad zone: Center triangle (Brilliance), leaning toward I∩ES
- Prediction: E4's developmental trajectory should resemble C facets more than E facets (E2, E6).

**Test case 3: N2 Angry Hostility**
- Big Five domain: Neuroticism
- Bright Triad zone: ES∩E (Agreeableness zone)
- Prediction: N2's developmental trajectory should resemble A facets (A2, A4) more than N facets (N1, N3).

**Additional test cases (if data available):**
- A1 Trust (Big Five: A; Bright Triad: ES core) — should develop more like N facets than A facets
- E1 Warmth (Big Five: E; Bright Triad: ES∩E / A zone) — should develop more like A facets than E facets

## Data needed
Developmental trajectory descriptions for NEO-PI-R facets from longitudinal studies. Look for:
- Direction of mean-level change across adulthood (increase, decrease, stable, curvilinear)
- Timing of change (which decades show the most change)
- Slope estimates (T-score change per decade or similar)
- Whether the trajectory is monotonic or shows reversals

Collect trajectory information for all facets involved in the comparisons, not just the reassigned facets.

## Analysis
For each test case:
1. Describe the reassigned facet's trajectory.
2. Describe the trajectories of its Big Five domain-mates.
3. Describe the trajectories of its Bright Triad zone-mates.
4. Judge: which set of trajectories does the reassigned facet more closely resemble?

This is primarily a qualitative comparison (trajectory shape similarity), not a statistical test. If slope estimates are available, quantitative comparison is better.

## Output
- Per-test-case table: reassigned facet trajectory vs. Big Five domain-mate trajectories vs. Bright Triad zone-mate trajectories
- Judgment per test case: resembles zone-mates / resembles domain-mates / ambiguous
- Overall verdict: Supported (all test cases favor zone-mates) / Partially supported (2 of 3) / Not supported (≤1 of 3)
- Note any test cases where the comparison is ambiguous or data is insufficient
