# Task H7: The Brilliance Composite Correlates with Intelligence More Strongly Than Any Single Big Five Domain

## Reference
See `bright_triad_reference.md` for the model description and zone assignments.

## Hypothesis
A composite of center-triangle facets (E3 Assertiveness, E4 Activity, E6 Positive Emotions, A3 Altruism, C3 Dutifulness, A6 Tender-Mindedness) should correlate with general intelligence more strongly than any individual Big Five domain — in particular, more strongly than Openness, which has the highest domain-level intelligence correlation.

## Prediction
Estimated Brilliance composite–intelligence correlation > Openness–intelligence correlation

## Logic
Each Big Five domain captures only one slice of the Intelligence dimension (in the Bright Triad model). The center-triangle facets sit where all three primaries converge, so they should collectively capture more Intelligence variance than any single domain can. Since the center-triangle facets come from multiple domains (E, A, C), their composite should integrate intelligence-related variance that is invisible at any single domain level.

## Data needed
Correlations between intelligence (general cognitive ability, g, IQ) and personality at the facet or aspect level. Look for:
- Meta-analytic personality–intelligence correlations at the facet level
- Meta-analytic personality–intelligence correlations at the aspect level (BFAS aspects)
- Domain-level personality–intelligence correlations as benchmarks
- Any distinction between fluid vs. crystallized intelligence correlations

For the center-triangle facets specifically (E3, E4, E6, A3, C3, A6), collect whatever intelligence-correlation estimates are available.

Also collect the Openness–intelligence domain-level correlation as the benchmark to beat.

## Analysis
1. Report all available facet/aspect–intelligence correlations for center-triangle content.
2. Report the Openness–intelligence domain correlation as benchmark.
3. If individual facet–intelligence correlations are available for most center-triangle facets, estimate the composite correlation. Note: a proper composite calculation requires inter-facet correlations. If those aren't available, report the simple average of facet–intelligence correlations and flag this as approximate.
4. Compare the composite estimate to the Openness benchmark.

## Output
- Table of available intelligence correlations for center-triangle facets
- Openness–intelligence benchmark
- Composite estimate (if computable) with method notes
- Verdict: Supported / Partially supported / Not supported / Untestable
- Data gap assessment: which center-triangle facets are missing intelligence correlations?
