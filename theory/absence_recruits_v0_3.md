# Absence Recruits: The Motive Force of the Cybernetic Cycle

---

## The principle: constitutive absence

A phenomenon can be organized around something that is not present in it. Terrence Deacon calls this a constitutive absence: a particular and precise missing something that is a defining attribute of a function, a purpose, or an adaptation. The absence is not a gap in the description. The absence is what the thing is for.

Hemoglobin is the cleanest biological case. The molecule that carries oxygen spends much of its cycle holding no oxygen at all. Its tense, deoxygenated conformation is the shape it takes in oxygen's absence, and its whole allosteric machinery, the cooperative binding, the sigmoid affinity curve, the release at the tissues, is organized around a gas that is not part of the molecule. The function of hemoglobin is a relationship to something absent from hemoglobin. Read the molecule alone and you cannot see what it does. Read it against the oxygen it is shaped to want and release, and the function appears.

This is the move the framework already makes at its center. The four capacities are each defined by the one cybernetic function they lack. The Planner is the constellation without a live Effector. The Pursuer is the constellation without a live Regulator. The Reactor is the constellation without a live Modeler. The Conservator is the constellation without a live Reviser. Each capacity is named by its absence, the way hemoglobin is shaped by the oxygen it does not contain. Absence is already doing definitional work in the framework.

This draft claims that absence does a second kind of work. It defines the capacities, and it also drives the cycle that builds them.

*Epistemic status: constitutive absence is Deacon's, verified. The specific hemoglobin illustration is offered here as an instance of the principle; the attribution of that example to Deacon himself is unconfirmed and marked below. The absent-function definition of the four capacities is existing framework canon.*

---

## The claim: absence recruits

Each of the four cybernetic functions is complete at what it does and incomplete on its own. Each carries a specific lack. The lack is not a flaw. The lack is what pulls the next function into being.

An Effector acts, and open action cannot see its own result. A Regulator supplies the missing comparison. A Regulator holds a reference, and a fixed reference cannot look ahead. A Modeler supplies the missing anticipation. A Modeler predicts, and a model fitted to the past goes stale. A Reviser supplies the missing update. A Reviser rewrites the model, and a rewritten model changes nothing in the world. Action supplies the missing test, and the cycle returns to the Effector.

The slogan is: absence recruits. A function's constitutive absence is the demand that the next function answers. The four adjacent-pair loops already named in the framework are the four answers. Controller resolves the Effector's absence. Anticipator resolves the Regulator's absence. Adapter resolves the Modeler's absence. Explorer resolves the Reviser's absence and hands the cycle back to action. The ring of four loops is the ring of four recruitments.

This does not mint a new construct. It is the framework's own absent-function principle, applied to the transitions between functions rather than to the capacities that the functions compose. Absence defines the capacities. Absence recruits across the cycle. One principle, two applications.

## The four recruitments

Each recruitment is stated the same way: the function, its constitutive absence, the function that answers, the loop that closes, and the prior art that already owns this link. No single thinker owns the whole ring. Every edge of the ring is already attested.

### Effector to Regulator: the Controller

An Effector produces action. Action alone is blind to whether it landed. What is absent is a comparison of result against reference.

A Regulator supplies the comparison. It measures the gap between where the unit is and where it should be, and it drives action to close the gap. Effector and Regulator together form the Controller, the textbook closed feedback loop.

This is the oldest and most attested link. Negative feedback is the founding object of cybernetics after Wiener. The canonical psychology version is the TOTE unit of Miller, Galanter, and Pribram. In their account the sensing mechanism tests for an incongruity and the effecting mechanism operates to reduce it, and this test-operate cycle, not the reflex arc, is the basic unit of controlled behavior. Carver and Scheier built the psychology of self-regulation on the same discrepancy-reducing loop. Incongruity, a lack, recruits operation. The base of the cycle is a century of settled cybernetics.

*Epistemic status: verified. TOTE and Carver-Scheier cover the Controller edge only, not the full ring.*

### Regulator to Modeler: the Anticipator

A Regulator defends a stored reference. A pure regulator can only respond to error after the error has occurred. What is absent is foresight, a way to act on predicted error rather than measured error.

A Modeler supplies the prediction. It carries an internal account of how the world behaves, so regulation can look ahead. Regulator and Modeler together form the Anticipator, model-informed regulation, the predictive-control and state-estimation loop.

This link is nearly a theorem. Conant and Ashby proved that every good regulator of a system must be a model of that system: a regulator that tracks a system well is, of necessity, isomorphic to a model of it. Robert Rosen made the same point from the other side with anticipatory systems, systems that contain a predictive model of themselves and their world and act on its forecasts. The Modeler is not an optional upgrade to the Regulator. Good regulation already contains a model, so the Regulator's absence is filled by definition once regulation is done well.

*Epistemic status: good regulator theorem is framework canon and near-theorem strength. Rosen's anticipatory systems pending Smithers ingest.*

### Modeler to Reviser: the Adapter

A Modeler holds a representation. A model fitted to past experience drifts out of register as the world changes, and it cannot correct itself from inside. What is absent is a way to update the model against surprise.

A Reviser supplies the update. It weights what has not been seen, treats prediction error as a signal, and rewrites the references the Modeler stabilizes against. Modeler and Reviser together form the Adapter, the self-revising model, which is what learning means in its most fundamental sense.

This link is owned by three traditions at once. Piaget's equilibration: when assimilation fails, the mismatch recruits accommodation, a revision of the schema. Powers' Perceptual Control Theory: persistent, uncorrectable error recruits reorganization, a restructuring of the control hierarchy itself. Friston's free-energy account: prediction error recruits model update as one of the two ways a system can reduce surprise. The model's staleness is its constitutive absence, and revision is the answer.

*Epistemic status: Friston is in the framework's citation set. Piaget's equilibration and Powers' reorganization pending Smithers ingest.*

### Reviser to Effector: the Explorer

A Reviser rewrites the model. Revision is epistemic. A rewritten model, held in the head, changes nothing in the world until something acts on it. What is absent is a test, and the only test is action.

The Effector supplies the test. Exploratory action puts the revised model into contact with the world and lets the world answer. Reviser and Effector together form the Explorer, the explore side of the explore-exploit trade, and the cycle returns to where it began.

This is the closing link, and it is the deepest. Peirce's pragmatism gives the within-lifetime version: doubt is an irritation, a lack, that recruits inquiry; inquiry settles into belief; belief is a habit for action. Revision that never issues in a habit for action has done nothing. Friston's account gives the same closure formally: a system reduces surprise either by revising the model or by acting on the world, and action is the second horn. Enactivism, after Varela and Thompson, states the standing commitment that cognition exists for action.

Evolution gives the across-lifetime version, and it is decisive for the framework's slow loop. West-Eberhard's principle is that genes are followers, not leaders: adaptive innovation begins with developmental and behavioral plasticity, and genetic change follows as an accommodation of what plasticity already found. The Baldwin effect and Waddington's genetic assimilation name the same sequence. Exploration leads. Structure consolidates behind it. This is exactly the framework's claim that the Reviser runs hot at a prediction-error premium and that the lineage advances when the slow loop has consolidated the explorer's work into stored structure.

*Epistemic status: West-Eberhard verified. Peirce, enactivism pending Smithers ingest. The tie to the framework's Reviser risk premium and slow-loop consolidation is a framework theoretical commitment.*

## Why the order is forced

The recruitments run in one direction. Effector recruits Regulator, Regulator recruits Modeler, Modeler recruits Reviser, Reviser recruits Effector. The framework does not claim Effector recruits Modeler, and it does not claim Effector recruits Reviser. The cycle has a direction and it skips no steps. Three separate forces produce that directionality, and they are not the same force, so they are kept apart here.

### Build-order dependency: the hard constraint

Each function is constituted by the one before it, so it cannot be recruited until its predecessor exists to be completed. This is the strongest of the three forces, and it is a straight lift of Deacon's own architecture, in which each higher-order absence is generated by relationships among lower-order absences: teleodynamic processes are built on morphodynamic ones, which are built on homeodynamic ones, in a fixed directional chain.

Apply it to the ring. A Modeler is a model of a regulation problem; the good regulator theorem defines the model's job as serving regulation. So an Effector cannot recruit a Modeler directly, because with no Regulator there is no regulation problem yet, and a model of nothing is idle. A Reviser revises a model. So an Effector cannot recruit a Reviser directly, because with no Modeler there is no model yet, and revision of nothing is empty. The skips are ruled out because the skipped-to function has no substrate to attach to. Absence recruits, but only the absence whose answer can bind to what is already built. Adjacency is forced.

### Control flows the other way: Powers and Carver-Scheier

The stack is assembled from the bottom and run from the top. In the hierarchy of control that Powers formalized and Carver and Scheier carried into psychology, the output of a higher loop is the setting of the reference value for the loop directly below it. Revision sets the model's targets. The model sets the regulator's setpoints. The regulator commands the effector.

So the framework's fixed order is two orderings at once. Build order runs Effector, Regulator, Modeler, Reviser, from the bottom up. Control order runs the reverse, from the top down. This is why the structure is a ring with a direction rather than a heap of four parts. Assembly climbs; control descends; the two directions meet.

### Why the ring closes back to action

The top of the epistemic stack answers to no higher loop, so the closure at Reviser to Effector needs its own account. Two forces supply it, and both are native to the framework rather than imported.

The first force is the structure of Darwinian selection. The Reviser generates variation, and the Effector is the selection-outcome, what propagates when variation meets selection. Blind variation is half of Darwinism. Variation that is never expressed as an acting, propagating unit cannot be selected, and variation that is not selected is not inherited; it dissipates as noise. So a Reviser cannot finish its own work without an Effector to express its variation into the world where selection can reach it. The framework's scope is a cybernetic loop subject to inheritance. A loop that generates variation and never closes to a selected outcome carries no inheritance, so it is not the kind of loop the framework is about. The closure is forced by what the framework is a theory of. This is Campbell's universal Darwinism read at the scale of the ring: the Reviser is blind variation, Darwin at the center is selective retention, and the Effector is where retention lands.

At the line boundary this closing edge is the transformation of one goo into the next. The Reviser cell of a line runs that line's achievement in blind, unbounded mode and throws off a residue. The founding Effector of the next line is the little of that residue which selection binds into a working unit. The framework's anchor is the fertilized egg: recombination is blind variation producing an unbound unit, and implantation is the binding that turns open-ended potential into a controlled program. A line's culmination becomes the next line's infancy across this one edge.

The second force is the epistemics of the untried. The Reviser's variation is exploration, the generation of options not yet tried, and epistemic value sits at the Reviser. The payoff of an untried action is unobservable except by taking it; no amount of modeling recovers the consequence of an act never performed. Revising the model against known options is already the Adapter edge's work. The Explorer edge exists for the options observation cannot reach, and those can be evaluated only by acting. So blind variation is inert not as a manner of speaking but as an information fact, and the Reviser depends on the Effector to sample the world it cannot otherwise see.

The two forces name the same edge from two sides. Selection needs the variation expressed. Exploration needs the untried sampled. Both are cashed out in action. This is also the joint where the strange loop lives, in Hofstadter's sense, a level that cannot complete itself from within passing out of itself and re-entering, and Peirce's pragmatism states the within-lifetime version, doubt settled only by inquiry that ends in a habit for action. One thing to keep straight: self-production and autopoiesis, after Rosen and after Maturana and Varela, name what the Reviser is, the self-producing work that keeps a line far from equilibrium. They are not the closure mechanism. The closure is carried by selection and by exploration.

The argument so far is sharpest at the clade scale, where the selector is natural selection and the closing edge is the egg. The ring also turns within a single lifetime, and there the closure is forced on its own terms rather than borrowed from the clade scale. The formalism is active inference. A system minimizes free energy in two ways only. It changes the model to match the world, which is perception and revision, the Reviser's horn. Or it changes the world to match the model, which is action, the Effector's horn. The word active in active inference names the Effector. The model horn cannot change the world, so the Reviser's horn is incomplete in exactly the way that recruits the Effector's, and this is absence-recruits stated in the free-energy formalism at the within-lifetime scale with no loan from the slow loop.

The two legs of the closure are the two parts of the quantity a within-lifetime policy minimizes. Expected free energy decomposes into pragmatic value, reaching preferred outcomes, and epistemic value, gaining information about the uncertain. Pragmatic value is the selection leg at the scale of one agent, because a preferred outcome is a change in the world and can be reached only by acting. Epistemic value is the exploration leg made exact, because information about an unsampled option is available only by sampling it, and sampling is action. Epistemic value already sits at the Reviser in the framework, so the Reviser owns the exploratory demand and the Effector is its only discharge. Both parts of expected free energy require the Effector.

So the closure runs on one engine at both scales. Campbell's blind variation and selective retention is the scale-general umbrella. Natural selection is the selector across the slow loop. Free-energy minimization, with reward as prior preference and attention as precision, is the selector across the fast loop, and Edelman's neural Darwinism is the same contest one level down in the tissue. The fast-loop and slow-loop closures are then one closure with two selectors, which is why the identity of the two loops is a consequence here and not an assumption the argument leans on.

*Scope note: active inference is formally exact at the neuronal line, because the neuronal goo is Friston goo. At the genetic and symbolic lines the same closure runs through the substrate-independent free-energy principle in each line's own machinery, not through mammalian active inference taken literally. The within-lifetime closure is sharpest at the neuronal line and generalized elsewhere, and the draft claims it that way.*

*Metaphor mapping, stated so the metaphor can carry weight. What transfers from the strange loop: a level cannot fully ground itself from inside, so it exits and returns. What does not transfer: Godel's incompleteness is a precise result about provability in formal arithmetic, and the cybernetic ring is not a proof system, so the theorem does not prove the closure. Hofstadter's strange-loop architecture and Peirce's pragmatism carry the closure. Godel-the-theorem is illumination, not evidence, and is kept out of the load-bearing wall.*

## What is forced and by which kind of force

The joints of the ring do not all hold at the same strength, and the draft names the difference rather than smoothing it. With the closing edge accounted for, the ring is forced the whole way around, but not by one kind of argument, and the kinds are worth stating.

Effector to Regulator to Modeler is forced at close to theorem strength. The Effector to Regulator joint is settled cybernetics. The Regulator to Modeler joint is carried by the good regulator theorem, which is a formal result rather than an empirical generalization.

Modeler to Reviser is forced by convergence rather than by a single theorem. Piaget's equilibration, Powers' reorganization, and Friston's free energy each derive the same recruitment, that a model which cannot correct itself against surprise must recruit a reviser. Agreement from three independent traditions is strong support, though it is not a proof in the way the good regulator theorem is.

Reviser to Effector is forced by the structure of inheritance, conditional on scope. The Reviser generates variation. Variation never expressed as an acting, selected unit is not inherited. The framework's scope is a cybernetic loop subject to inheritance. So any loop the framework is about must close this edge, or it is not the kind of loop the framework is about. The epistemics of the untried give the same result from the other side. This edge is as forced as the others, with one honest qualification: it is forced for inheritance-bearing loops, which is the framework's domain, not for arbitrary control systems outside it.

The framework's recommended stance: assert absence-recruits and the forced adjacency as architecture, present the full ring as forced, and name for each joint which kind of force holds it, theorem, convergence, or inheritance-plus-scope. State the scope condition on the closing edge in the open. The condition is not a weakness. It is the correct boundary of the claim, and the draft that names it first cannot be caught at it.

*Epistemic status: the directionality argument is a framework theoretical commitment built from standard control-theoretic, developmental, and evolutionary parts. Every joint is forced. The closing joint is forced conditional on the unit being an inheritance-bearing loop, which is the framework's scope. The within-lifetime edge is forced on its own terms by active inference, in which the Effector is the active term, so the earlier identity of the fast-loop and slow-loop closures is now a consequence of one shared engine rather than an assumption the argument leans on.*

## The cycle is a helix, not a circle

The ring returns to its start, and it does not return to the same place. This is the reconciliation of the cyclical picture with the framework's inheritance structure.

The same four functions run three times, once per inheritance line. The genetic line runs Effector to Reviser across its four cells. The neuronal line runs the same four functions again across its four cells. The symbolic line runs them a third time. The cycle is self-similar across the three lines, which is the framework's fractal character read off the cross-line architecture.

It is also self-similar across timescales. The within-unit fast loop runs the four functions in a single lifetime. The cross-line slow loop runs the same exploration-into-structure dynamic across deep time. The two are one dynamic at two scales, which is what makes the structure fractal rather than merely repetitive.

And the turns are stacked, not concentric. Each line's groundwork is the prior line's mature output, so the cycle that closes at the end of one line opens the next line one level up. West-Eberhard's sequence is the mechanism of that lift: the explorer's plastic innovation at the top of one turn is consolidated into the inherited structure that the next turn stands on.

*Metaphor mapping. What transfers from the ouroboros, the snake that eats its tail: the cycle closes, and the output re-grounds the input. What does not transfer: return to an identical origin. Inheritance ratchets, so each turn opens on a higher line, and nothing comes back to exactly where it started. The ouroboros is the two-dimensional shadow of a three-dimensional helix, the image you get by collapsing the inheritance axis and standing directly beneath the climb. Useful as a picture, wrong as the geometry.*

## Absence again, in real time: eclipsing

Absence defines the capacities and drives the cycle across deep time. It also does a third job inside a single unit in real time, and this is already framework canon under the name eclipsing.

A tendency's low pole is not a smaller amount of the tendency. It is the tendency switched off, with the older architecture showing through. At the low setting the newest layer goes dormant and the unit falls through to the layer beneath, running as it ran before that layer was added. The low pole is a layer made absent, and what the absence exposes is the ancestor.

So absence operates at every scale the framework describes. It defines a capacity by the function that capacity lacks. It recruits the next function across the cycle by the lack the current function carries. It exposes an ancestor in real time when a tendency is dialed to its dormant pole. Three applications, one principle. The framework is, in this precise sense, a theory of what specific absences do.

*Epistemic status: eclipsing is existing framework canon. Its unification with the absence-recruits principle under a single account of constitutive absence is the claim this draft adds.*

## What the framework borrows and what it assembles

Honesty about originality, because the claim is stronger when it is exact.

The framework does not originate the idea that absence recruits. Negative feedback is that idea, and it is the founding move of cybernetics. Deacon owns the name, constitutive absence. Lewin and the Zeigarnik effect are the same idea in the psychology of incompleteness, where an unfinished task recruits memory and attention.

The framework does not originate any single edge of the ring. The Controller edge is TOTE and Carver-Scheier. The Anticipator edge is Conant-Ashby and Rosen. The Adapter edge is Piaget, Powers, and Friston. The Explorer edge is Peirce, Friston, and West-Eberhard. Every recruitment is already attested somewhere.

What the framework assembles is the whole ring as one object, and no source in this survey holds that object. The four functions named as a closed four-cycle, the same cycle recurring self-similarly across three inheritance lines, and the low pole of each function reading as the prior layer showing through. The giants own the edges. The closed ring, the fractal recurrence, and the eclipsing are the assembly.

The recommended framing for the book follows from this. Present the cycle as an architecture built from standard, citable parts, not as a new law of nature. It is more defensible and more honest, and it lets the book stand on Wiener, Ashby, Deacon, Powers, Piaget, Peirce, Friston, and West-Eberhard by name, rather than claim ground it would have to defend from scratch. Absence recruits survives as the connective principle that threads borrowed parts into a single ring.
