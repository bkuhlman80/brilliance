## Terminology normalizations

**inheritance line / lineage / column.** The source collapsed three distinct things into interchangeable words — "genetic line" and "genetic column" for the same referent, "code lineages" and "the line" in one paragraph. Now enforced throughout: **inheritance line** (short form *line*) is the modality; **column** is a grid coordinate and nothing else; **lineage** is a concrete descent series of individuals. S-10 was the worst offender in the source and has been fully converted.

**row / stage / rung.** Three words for one axis. Normalized to **row**; *rung* survives only inside the relay metaphor in S-07, which is where it belongs.

**Affiliation, not Autonomy** *(your ruling at the gate).* P-001-066's table named the symbolic line's Reviser goo-ratchet **Affiliation**; P-001-067, the next paragraph, called the same cell **Autonomy** and glossed it as "self-revising symbolic constraint." The table stands. The gloss was rewritten to "exchange-based symbolic constraint: the trading and reciprocal marking of signs that lets a symbol system hold together as a shared code" — the old gloss described Autonomy and would have been incoherent attached to Affiliation.

**Bramble / the bramble — deliberately preserved.** I flagged this as a collision during the blind phase and withdrew it once I could see the prose. "As the bramble grows up the trellis": the character is named for the structure. Both usages are intact and neither was renamed.

**Also normalized:** the Trellis Framework (first use) → the framework (after), never "the trellis"; the Good Regulator theorem; the Law of Requisite Variety → requisite variety; structural risk premium (not "doubled heat"); cortical stacking (not "neural-lineage stacking"); the 4-sphere active window (not "the 4×4"); Major Evolutionary Transitions (MET) expanded on first use; Effector/Regulator/Modeler/Reviser capitalized as framework roles, lowercase only inside the thermostat example.

**Two naming schemes reconciled.** P-003-012 named the four rows Effector/Regulator/Modeler/Reviser; P-003-016 separately listed "Row 1 founding, Row 2 regulation, Row 3 interiority, Row 4 differentiation," with the correspondence implied by list order and never stated. The second scheme was dropped in favour of the S-03 role names.
