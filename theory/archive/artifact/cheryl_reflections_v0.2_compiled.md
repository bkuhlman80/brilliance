## S-03: The Trellis Framework — Core Architecture and Vocabulary

The Trellis Framework is the architecture this document uses to describe how information persists and changes across time. It claims that four cybernetic functions — Effector, Regulator, Modeler, and Reviser — recur in the same arrangement across each of several inheritance lines, so that the framework can be drawn as a grid: each line forms a column, each function forms a row, and the same four-row architecture repeats once per column. The framework applies only to units that bear inheritance — units that transmit information forward across time and modify it in the transmitting. That is what licenses trait talk about them in the first place; what makes trait talk apply to some units and not others is argued in full in the section that follows. Here the vocabulary is fixed so that argument has terms to work with.

**Three inheritance lines** are distinguished by three commitments: the inheritance modality each runs on, the kind of goo it ratchets, and the variation-selection cycle that runs on that goo. Each line is a distinct inheritance modality — a way of moving information across time that wasn't available before it existed:

- **Genetic line** stores information in nucleic acid, transmits it via reproduction, and modifies it through mutation and selection. It ratchets Boltzmann goo: life maintains itself far from thermodynamic equilibrium, importing low-entropy energy and exporting high-entropy waste, and selection preserves the constellations — cells, organisms, lineages — that ratchet best.
- **Neuronal line** stores information in neural patterns, transmits it via behavior and learning, and modifies it through experience. It ratchets affective goo: sensorimotor reentrant signaling produces affective and motivational structure, and associative learning shapes that raw material into the motivational architecture that coordinates behavior — the trait-level dispositional structures documented empirically from vertebrates upward.
- **Symbolic line** stores information in network-defined signs, transmits it via teaching and copying, and modifies it through cultural innovation. It ratchets Shannon goo: symbols are constraints on signal-space, and lexicons, grammars, conventions, and pragmatic norms each reduce the space of legitimate messages by large factors; cultural transmission preserves the constraints that work for coordinating groups.

*Epistemic status: the genetic line's Boltzmann-goo claim follows from Deacon's Shannon-Boltzmann-Darwin framework (2007a, 2007b, 2012). The neuronal line's affective-goo claim is supported by active inference (Friston) at the formal-mechanism level and by the behavioral-syndrome literature (Sih, Bell & Johnson 2004; Koolhaas) at the empirical-trait level. The symbolic claim is a framework-native application of the same principle.*

**Rows, columns, and cells** are the grid's coordinate vocabulary, needed before rows and columns can do any work. A **row** is one of the four cybernetic functions — Effector, Regulator, Modeler, Reviser — read across every line. A **column** is a grid coordinate only: the position of one inheritance line within the grid, nothing more. A **cell** is a row-column intersection: one function as it is instantiated in one line. Two further distinctions matter and are easy to blur: a column names a coordinate, not the modality that occupies it — that modality is the **inheritance line** (short form: line) — and a **lineage** names something narrower still, a concrete descent series of individuals running within a line, not the line itself. This document holds all three apart from here forward.

Each cell name is shorthand for a clade, a category of units sharing the cell's architectural signature: Eukaryote names the clade of organized-interior cells, City names the clade of city-states, urban-centered kingdoms, and other polities organized around an urban administrative core, Empire names the clade of territorial, maritime, and ideological empires.

**The four-row architecture runs as a cycle** at every line, instantiated in line-specific machinery; the machinery differs because each line ratchets a different kind of goo. The Effector-Regulator-Modeler-Reviser decomposition is not just four cybernetic functions held alongside each other. It is the variation-selection cycle made architectural:

- **Reviser is variation, the goo-ratchet.** The active work that keeps the line far from equilibrium.
- **Modeler is modeling.** Internal representation of what the ratchet produces.
- **Regulator is stabilization.** Regulated control against stored references.
- **Effector is the selection-outcome.** What propagates when the ratchet meets Darwin — selection, used here as shorthand.

Each line runs the same four-part cycle on its goo: Boltzmann thermodynamic chaos becomes variation through self-production, modeling through interiority, regulation through coordination, and selection of fitness.

| Line | Reviser goo-ratchet | Darwin-on-goo | Effector outcome |
|--------|--------------------|---------------------|------------|
| Genetic | Self-Production (Boltzmann) | thermodynamic selection | Persistence |
| Neuronal | Excitability (FEP) | prediction-error selection | Boldness |
| Symbolic | Affiliation (Shannon) | exchange-based selection | Reciprocity |

The mapping at genetic is cleanest: Self-Production directly names Deacon's teleodynamic goo-ratchet work. The mappings at neuronal and symbolic require more reading-in — Excitability as FEP-flavored active engagement, Affiliation as exchange-based symbolic constraint: the trading and reciprocal marking of signs that lets a symbol system hold together as a shared code. These require interpretation, but they are not strained. Framework concepts often unify multiple aspects of a phenomenon.

Bateson (1967, 1972, 1979) gives the earliest sharp formulation of the cybernetic view of inheritance: information as restraint, surviving lineages as the residue of a process that ruthlessly enumerated and excluded alternatives. Uexküll's umwelt theory (1934) supplies the environment-construction piece that requisite variety leaves implicit — the Regulator regulates against the organism-relative perceptual-effector world, not against objective physical reality. Maturana and Varela's autopoiesis is structural to the framework's scope criterion: self-maintaining environmental coupling. Pattee's work on semiotic closure treats biology as hierarchical self-modeling. Jablonka and Lamb (2005) extend multi-track inheritance — genetic, epigenetic, behavioral, symbolic — to the symbolic line. Deacon (*Incomplete Nature*, 2012; Shannon-Boltzmann-Darwin, 2007) supplies the entropy-reduction framing the scope criterion follows.

## S-04: Personality as Biographical Signature

What makes trait talk apply to bacteria but not to thermostats?

Both have the cybernetic components. A thermostat has a sensor (the thermometer), a comparator (the rule "above/below setpoint"), an effector (the heater), and a goal (the setpoint). A bacterium has the same four functions in different machinery. By the components alone, both should support trait description; only bacteria do. Bacteriologists describe strains as bold, sluggish, sociable, cooperative, or exploitative, and the descriptions earn their keep.

The difference is inheritance.

Trait adjectives are distributional summaries. Saying a system is "bold" is a probabilistic claim about how it tends to weight action under uncertainty across a class of situations. A distributional summary presupposes multiple situations where the parameter expresses, probabilistic rather than deterministic response, consistency in the pattern, and a reference distribution against which "tends to" is meaningful. The reference distribution is what makes the trait reading possible.

Inheritance creates that reference distribution. It propagates parameters across instances so variation persists; subjects parameter variation to selection so the distribution becomes structured rather than drifting to noise; preserves successful constellations so they appear repeatedly in the lineage; and generates novelty so the distribution stays alive. Without inheritance, the lineage has no parameter distribution; without a parameter distribution, individual readings have nothing to be a position within.

A thermostat lineage does not exist. There is no thermostat-to-thermostat parameter flow contingent on function; variation across thermostats reflects designer choice, not selection. The cybernetic loop is present; the biographical structure is not. Trait adjectives describe systems with histories; thermostats do not have biographies.

The biographical signature is what every line has evolved a genre to compress. Humans get obituaries, bacterial strains get strain reports, and agents get deployment retrospectives — different names, same form: a small set of adjectives summarizing how the unit tended across the situations it occupied. A personality assessment is a prospective bet on which adjectives the retrospective compression will land on. Things without biographies get event descriptions instead — hurricanes get meteorological summaries, thermostats get spec sheets.

The same argument is offered, more speculatively, to extend the Trellis Framework beyond biology. Code, trained-model weights, and agent behavior each support their own lineages — forks and version control for code, fine-tuning chains for weights, prompt and tool inheritance for agents. Each such line is engineered at origin rather than evolved, but parameter flow contingent on what works is present regardless. If that holds, personality applies not by metaphorical extension from the bacterial case but by the same structural argument — though the examples are all the support on offer.

**Personality is the biographical signature of a cybernetic loop subject to inheritance.** Wherever both conditions hold, traits apply. Where either fails, they do not.

This is why the framework's scope criterion takes the shape it does. The framework applies to inheritance-bearing units: systems that perpetuate themselves through environmental coupling, transmitting pattern across time. The criterion excludes thermostats, hurricanes, and rocks not intuitively but on principled grounds — they do not transmit pattern to successors, so there is no lineage for selection to write onto, so there is no biographical signature to summarize.

## S-05: Cybernetic-Inheritance Foundations

The framework applies to any unit that bears inheritance because inheritance creates the reference distribution trait talk needs — a claim resting on foundations worth stating plainly. Those foundations are distributed across several anchors; no single paper underwrites the entire cross-line claim, and that is honest: the substrate-independence claim benefits from convergence across multiple traditions rather than dependence on any one.

The Good Regulator theorem is the first anchor. Conant and Ashby (1970) showed that every good regulator of a system must be a model of that system — a formal result, not an empirical generalization. Applied at the lineage scale: a surviving lineage is a regulator whose continued existence demonstrates successful tracking of its environment, so by the theorem its accumulated parameters constitute a model of that environment. That model is not represented explicitly; it is distributed across the lineage's inherited architecture, the way a neural network's training is distributed across its weights — structurally present, reflected in behavior. This is the substrate-independent foundation for the framework's cross-line claim: whatever the substrate — DNA, neural pattern, cultural transmission, code — surviving lineages are good regulators by the same theorem. The architecture is cybernetic at every line by entailment, not by analogy.

*Epistemic status: Conant-Ashby is a theorem with formal standing. Its application to inheritance-bearing lineages is the move the framework makes. The application is consistent with cybernetic-inheritance literature (Bateson, Maturana, Friston), but the lineage from Conant-Ashby specifically to that literature is convergent rather than direct — most contemporary cyberneticists arrive at the result through their own reasoning rather than citing the 1970 paper.*

Ashby's second theorem, from *Introduction to Cybernetics* (1956), is the Law of Requisite Variety: only variety can destroy variety. A regulator must have at least as many internal states as the perturbations it can encounter — holding course against a complex environment requires a regulator as complex as the environment's disturbance-space. This explains evolutionary complexity at the structural level: lineages do not evolve complexity because complexity is intrinsically valuable, but because regulating against complex environments requires complex regulators. As environments get more variable, regulators must add variety to stay in the game.

The Cambrian explosion is a clean illustration: predator-prey coevolution drives an arms race in which each side's variety pressures the other's. The vertebrate Regulator introduction — basal ganglia plus operant conditioning — emerges from this arms race, because animals running model-free reinforcement learning carry more requisite variety than animals running pure reflex circuits. The pattern runs across lines: eukaryote scale-and-size emerges because multi-component cellular machinery requires more variety than prokaryote regulation can produce; cities emerge because multi-settlement territory requires more than settlements alone can produce; empires emerge because multi-city populations require more than cities alone can produce. Each line-transition is a variety-jump: the regulated environment outgrew what the prior line could match. The Good Regulator theorem and the Law of Requisite Variety are two halves of the same architectural claim: the first says regulating requires modeling, the second says modeling must be at least as varied as what it models.

Mammalian Modeler did not evolve because mammals wanted to think; it evolved because some pre-mammalian lineages faced environments that outran vertebrate Regulator plus Reviser, and the lineages that built the PFC, limbic system, and hippocampus regulated those environments — the lineages that did not build the Modeler went extinct or stayed off-thread. The "must" in "every good regulator must have a model" is this gravitational pull, not a logical tautology: any unit whose environment exceeds the Regulator's variety is pulled toward developing the Modeler, because the Modeler is the only path to matching that variety — resisting the pull means dying out. The same pull operated earlier for bilaterians, where predator-prey arms races demanded the vertebrate Regulator, and later for symbolic-line units, where settlements introduce Regulator-as-Stability, cities introduce Modeler-as-Representation, and empires introduce Reviser-as-Universality.

A second, independent line of grounding treats evolution itself as a form of learning. Watson and Szathmáry (2016) show that evolutionary and learning dynamics are different instantiations of the same algorithmic principles, carried by two formal equivalences: Bayesian updating is mathematically equivalent to soft selection on genotypes in asexual populations (Harper; Shalizi), and the multiplicative weights update algorithm is mathematically equivalent to selection on individual alleles at linkage equilibrium in sexual populations (Chastain et al.). Both equivalences are conditional: the Bayesian-asexual equivalence requires that the population be asexual, with genotypes that cannot be decomposed into independently fit components, and the multiplicative-weights-sexual equivalence requires linkage equilibrium, absent epistasis. The conditions matter; the equivalences are not unrestricted.

The free energy principle gives a substrate-independent formulation: any system that maintains itself against entropy must minimize a quantity equivalent to free energy, decomposable into pragmatic value (preferred outcomes) and epistemic value (information gain). Safron and DeYoung (2021) map this onto the metatraits of CB5T (Cybernetic Big Five Theory — DeYoung's cybernetic reformulation of the Big Five personality dimensions): Stability tracks pragmatic value (precision over preferences, the C matrix); Plasticity tracks epistemic value (precision over policies). For the framework, the principle is substrate-independent, compatible with cybernetic-inheritance at every level. Active inference is the specific mammalian instantiation; other lines run other line-native machinery — ratcheting at protocell, state-dependent gating at prokaryote, associative learning at bilaterian, reinforcement learning at vertebrate, gossip-tracked social debt at band, code-tracked obligation at city.

Safron and DeYoung are the strongest existing precedent for grounding CB5T's metatraits cybernetically: they did that work with detailed neurobiological grounding and proposed a methodology (P-structures) for operationalizing it, which the framework inherits, cites heavily, and does not duplicate. Where it departs is placement: the metatraits sit at single-primary positions in the unified Venn rather than at row functions across the framework. Stability sits at the Effector, adjacent to the Conscientiousness and Neuroticism pair-regions, which explains why confirmatory factor analysis recovers it as a metatrait; Plasticity sits at the Modeler, adjacent to Extraversion and Agreeableness, for the same reason. This specific placement is the framework's own extrapolation, not Safron and DeYoung's, and it is not independently evidenced beyond the argument just given.

A related demonstration explains how this modeling accumulates: gene-regulatory architecture internalizes the correlation structure of selective environments, the way a neural net stores training-set statistics in its weights. Watson et al. (2014) demonstrated, in simulation, that evolved gene-regulatory networks form a distributed developmental memory of phenotypes selected in the past, spontaneously recreating those phenotypes as developmental attractors, and generalizing to novel combinations of phenotypic modules fit for environments never previously selected for.

*Epistemic status: the formal equivalences are stated by Watson and Szathmáry as established mathematical results, attributed to prior work. The developmental-memory and generalization claims are demonstrated in simulation; the authors flag the extension to biological generality as an outstanding question.*

A third strand extends the grounding to cognition itself. Levin and Watson (2025) argue for cognition all the way down: agential competency is present, by degrees, at many interdependent organizational levels of biology. The central claim: there are no dumb parts in biology, because cognition is pervasive and scale-agnostic, and newer levels harness the autonomy of older levels rather than overwriting them. Substrate-independence is a central move: connectionist learning principles are implemented in many physical systems, from gene networks to protein networks to ecosystems, and Hebbian learning appears in non-neural and non-biological systems; the scope as the authors define it is biological-organizational levels plus computational hardware. For the framework's purposes, the paper underwrites scale-free competency from the Genetic line through the Neuronal line, plus silicon. It does not address the Symbolic line or cultural inheritance directly, and extending the cybernetic-inheritance claim into the Symbolic line requires additional anchors.

## S-06: How Each Line Instantiates the Architecture

Deacon's pre-life dynamics — homeodynamic, morphodynamic, teleodynamic processes — are the pre-line groundwork of the Genetic line, preparing chemistry for life. The pattern generalizes: each line has its own pre-line groundwork, work that ratcheted the line's goo and prepared it for the line's emergence. Each line's pre-line groundwork is the prior line's mature output, which closes the loop across lines.

- **Genetic:** Deacon's homeodynamic, morphodynamic, and teleodynamic processes — ratchets that prepared chemistry for life.
- **Neuronal:** pre-neural sensory and behavioral coordination — affective ratchets that prepared bodies for brains.
- **Symbolic:** pre-symbolic animal signaling, indexical and iconic reference — Shannon ratchets that prepared cognition for symbols.

**The domain shift.** Each line's primary domain of action is the goo of the line one level back, even while it is built from that goo. Genetic units act on chemistry, neuronal units act on biology — navigating ecological niches, finding food — and symbolic units (bands, cities, empires) act on cognition, shaping member minds through stories, norms, codes, and institutions.

The two patterns run together because a new line emerges precisely when the prior line's goo grows complex enough that a specialized control layer is worth its overhead. Neuronal systems emerged when bodies became complex enough that distributed neural coordination paid off relative to its metabolic cost; symbolic systems emerged when groups became complex enough that culture-based coordination outpaced direct social learning.

The Trellis Framework's flora-and-fauna picture encodes this same pattern. The body is Genetic-line flora; once the brain emerges, the body is what the brain rides on and operates on. Cognition is Neuronal-line fauna; once symbolic systems emerge, personality is Symbolic-line flora. Each line's fauna becomes the next line's flora. Two senses of "built on the prior line" run in parallel — one genealogical (the bridge-entity pattern, parent-of-next-line), one functional (each line acts on the prior line's goo) — and they are separable.

**Genetic line.** Gene-regulatory architecture internalizes the correlation structure of selective environments (Watson & Szathmáry). Selection acts on heritable variation; surviving constellations encode environmental regularities in the developmental machinery itself, on cross-generation timescales. The Reviser is mutation and recombination; the Regulator is the gene-regulatory network's stored architecture; the Modeler is the lineage's internalized environmental correlations; the Effector is the developing organism.

**Neuronal line.** Active inference is the line-native machinery at mammals, operating on within-lifetime timescales. The Reviser is exploratory action under epistemic uncertainty; the Regulator is the precision-weighted prior structure; the Modeler is the generative model that predicts incoming sensory data; the Effector is the motor system. Other levels of the line run other line-native machinery — state-dependent gating, associative learning, reinforcement learning — each implementing the same cybernetic dynamic in the form available to the level.

**Symbolic line.** Cultural transmission is the goo: stories, norms, prices, gossip, and ritual carry the inheritance. Selection acts on cultural variants; constellations persist by regulating something successfully in the cultural environment, on multi-generational timescales for the slowest levels (foundational myths, language structure) and within-lifetime to multi-decadal timescales for the faster ones (norms, prices, fashion). The Reviser is exploratory cultural variation (Boyd & Richerson's guided variation); the Regulator is institutionalized norm; the Modeler is the shared symbolic representation; the Effector is collective action.

## S-07: Bramble Growth and the Relay

As the bramble grows up the trellis, four parallel relay teams advance simultaneously, one per cybernetic function — Effector, Regulator, Modeler, Reviser. At any rung, four motivations are active, each carrying its team's flag. Each rung is a handoff for exactly one team: a new motivation takes the flag from its same-color predecessor, and the predecessor retires into consolidated memory. The other three teams keep their current flag-carriers.

The handoff order is fixed: Regulator → Modeler → Reviser → Effector, repeating across the matrix. Across the twelve cells from Protocell through Empires, the cycle runs three full rotations. The Effector handoff is a line boundary — Persistence to Boldness, Boldness to Reciprocity — while at every other handoff the line persists.

This relay is what differentiates the Trellis Framework from adjacent multi-scale-cybernetics work. Levin and Watson supply multi-scale competency at any moment in time; Maynard Smith and Szathmáry supply the diachronic story at coarse grain, transitions in individuality. Neither does the work of how the bramble grows. That work is the claim that the bramble at any rung operates with exactly four flag-carriers, one per team, with handoffs between rungs the mechanism by which the lineage's cybernetic architecture accumulates.

Two timescales operate together. The fast loop, within-bramble, is the standard cybernetic loop running at within-lifetime speed: the current Reviser explores, the Regulator stabilizes, the Modeler models, the Effector acts. The bramble learns, in its line's native sense of learning. The slow loop, cross-rung, governs the handoffs themselves. A handoff happens when the slow loop has consolidated the prior flag-carrier's exploratory work into the next cycle's stored structure. A motivation that enters as a flag-carrier doing exploratory work at one rung, when retired, becomes part of the consolidated infrastructure on which higher rungs run. The relay advances because consolidation has finished, not on a clock.

Retirement is not deletion. Interiority still operates in primates; coordination still operates in vertebrates; persistence still operates in everything alive. Retired motivations get backgrounded into infrastructure that newer attractors run on, becoming difficult for observers to discriminate against the signal of more recent attractors. The framework is measurement-theoretic here, not architectural-deletion.

The Reviser carries a structural risk premium across lines — the constitutive prediction-error premium of running the explorer position. At line-completing rungs (Eumetazoa, Primates, Empires), this compounds: the newest line-internal motivation occupies the Reviser position and the Reviser carries the structural risk at once. That doubling is what makes the bet hot.

The fixed handoff order — Regulator → Modeler → Reviser → Effector — also tracks the developmental arc of the hero's journey, a narrative parallel the framework does not develop further here.

The motivations on the stack are not equally consolidated: this is the maturity gradient. It runs from exploratory — most recent, under selection pressure, parameters still being shaped — to consolidated: deepest, internalized into inherited infrastructure, parameters stable enough to serve as foundation for everything above.

The mechanism is Watson and Szathmáry's developmental-memory result, generalized across lines. Selection operating across generations internalizes the correlation structure of the selective environment into the lineage's inherited architecture — gene-regulatory at the genetic line, neural-architectural at the neuronal line, cultural-institutional at the symbolic line. What enters as exploratory variation under active selection becomes, given enough generations of consistent selection, stored architecture that newer exploration runs on top of.

The maturity gradient is continuous, not binary. The four flag-carriers are most exploratory and individually discriminable in unit-level behavior; older motivations drop below into backgrounded infrastructure, where consolidation continues but unit-level behavioral discriminability has been lost.

That mechanism is high confidence at the genetic level, where Watson and Szathmáry's result is simulated directly. The generalization across lines is medium confidence: the cybernetic-inheritance literature supports the same dynamic in principle at the neuronal and symbolic scales but has not demonstrated it in detail, particularly at the symbolic scale.

## S-08: Ecology Across Lines

The unified Venn at each cell applies to a specific unit, and that unit changes as you move through the grid. At genetic-line cells, the unit is the cell or organism: Protocell-as-unit, Prokaryote-as-unit, Eukaryote-as-unit, Eumetazoa-as-unit. At neuronal-line cells, it is the brain-bearing organism: Bilaterian-as-unit, Vertebrate-as-unit, Mammal-as-unit, Primate-as-unit. At symbolic-line cells, the unit is the constituent human, parameterized by collective context: Band-Human, Settlement-Human, City-Human, Empire-Human. The collective itself remains the group; the Venn at the symbolic cell tracks the human within it.

That parameterization is not four different people. The systematic-analogy rule lets motivations transfer up the stack by structural analogy, not loose metaphor, and at symbolic cells the motivations are held throughout by one organism. What shifts across cells is which primaries carry flags given collective context. A Band-Human's Reciprocity is the band-context-conditioned attractor in the same organism that, in a different context, would activate a different motivation. One unit occupies several ecological positions at once — not sequentially, not as separate individuals, but as the same personality read against different collective surrounds.

Each line contains two ecological roles that make sense of this. Flora are sessile, persistent, and environment-shaping — slow on the line's timescale, generating the environment fauna consume. Fauna are motile, predatory, and behaviorally flexible relative to the flora they inhabit — fast on the line's timescale. Humans are to culture as plants are to animals: we are flora. The relationship is loopy, not simple. Animals forage on plants, and plants coevolve with animals, shaping them in turn; memes forage on human brains, and personalities coevolve with culture, shaping it in turn.

The roles populate every line:

| Line | Flora | Fauna |
|--------|-------|-------|
| Genetic | Plants and their predecessors | Animals |
| Neuronal | Bodies as organism | Brains and nervous systems |
| Symbolic | Personalities | Memes and meme-complexes |

At the symbolic line, the flora is the constituent human's personality structure — the trait-level structure personality psychology measures. The fauna is the full spectrum of memes, from small ideas to large meme-complexes. Superorganisms are large meme-complexes operating as fauna: a band, a settlement, a city, and an empire, each taken as a whole, are all superorganisms. They are not parallel units of personality alongside the constituent human. They are fauna inhabiting the human-flora personality — which is how the same person carries Band-Human, Settlement-Human, City-Human, and Empire-Human positions at once: one flora, several fauna feeding on it simultaneously.

Flora are not passive in any line. Plants manipulate pollinators, shape soil chemistry, and structure ecosystems on long timescales. Bodies grow brains, regulate metabolic resources, and determine the niches brains operate in. Personalities generate the symbolic content memes feed on — every utterance, every artifact, every child raised. The flora's mode of action runs longer and broader than the fauna's; the fauna's runs faster and more locally focal. Both are active.

**Coevolution across line transitions.** Each line emergence is a coevolutionary event. The new fauna develops alongside what becomes its new flora; it does not replace the prior line but enters into stable interdependence with the prior line's mature output. The pattern recurs across the framework's structural history: vertebrate brains coevolving with bilaterian body plans in the Cambrian explosion (~540 MYA); land tetrapods coevolving with forests in the tetrapod radiation (~360 MYA); mammals coevolving with dinosaurs across the Mesozoic (~200 to 66 MYA); and, from roughly 100 KYA onward, human personality coevolving with human culture.

Coevolution is the ecological story that runs alongside a genealogical one. The two are not in tension: which entity opens each new line is a separate question, taken up next as the bridge-entity pattern; coevolution describes how the line fills in once opened.

*Epistemic status: the deep-time coevolutionary events are well-documented in evolutionary biology and paleontology. The reading of symbolic-line emergence as a coevolution of human personality with human culture is a framework extension of the same pattern.*

## S-09: Line Transitions — Symbiosis, Elaboration, and the Thread

If lines coevolve rather than replace one another, most large-scale innovation happens inside a line, not between lines, so telling the two apart takes a sharp test: a new line requires a new modality of pattern transmission that can carry inheritance, the way genetic, neuronal, and symbolic each were. A 5th line would be identifiable the same way — selection-mediated agent-to-agent inheritance, a new goo of pattern propagation — though the framework does not claim one is forming.

This is the goo test, and it rules out most large-scale innovation. Substantial complexity often appears within a line without crossing the threshold: coral reefs at the eumetazoan level, corporations and markets at the symbolic line. Neither introduces a new inheritance modality — most innovation is elaboration, and lines are rare.

Coevolution within a line operates through two modes: symbiosis, the merger mode, and elaboration, the non-merger mode. Symbiosis commits previously independent parts as components of a new higher-level structure; elaboration develops a unit continuously without committing separable parts. Modeler and Reviser transitions are symbiosis-prone at every line; Effector and Regulator transitions are elaboration. The line determines what symbiosis looks like:

- **Genetic Modeler endosymbiosis:** free-living prokaryotes commit as mitochondria, and as chloroplasts in plants, inside a host cell.
- **Genetic Reviser multicellularity:** free-living eukaryotic cells commit as differentiated tissues inside a body.
- **Neuronal Modeler cortical stacking:** mammals introduce agranular cortex, a new neural lineage layered over the subcortical control structures (basal ganglia, limbic system) to model them; the two persist in obligate interdependence.
- **Neuronal Reviser cortical stacking:** primates introduce granular cortex as a meta-modeling layer over the agranular cortex — the same signature, one level up.
- **Symbolic Modeler city formation:** settlements commit as components of a city; trade networks are the connective tissue letting it aggregate goods and information beyond any single settlement.
- **Symbolic Reviser empire formation:** cities and hinterlands commit as provinces of an empire, which often adds colonies beyond contiguous territory.

Elaboration, the non-merger mode, looks different at the same four rows:

- **Genetic Regulator elaboration:** prokaryotes emerge from protocell logic through stored regulation.
- **Neuronal Effector elaboration:** bilaterian brains emerge from genetic-Reviser neural nets through elaboration of a control architecture for reflexes — steering and affect.
- **Symbolic Effector elaboration:** bands emerge from primate troop dynamics through language-mediated extension.

The neuronal version of symbiosis runs through cortical stacking, since the brain is body-internal and no free-living state exists from which neural lineages could merge. What is available is the pattern where a newer lineage develops the capacity to model the older one it is built on. Bennett's reinterpretation of MacLean's triune brain (*A Brief History of Intelligence*, 2023) supplies the mechanism: MacLean's stacking intuition was partly right; the lineage details were wrong, not the layered architecture.

That equivalence needs a caveat, not a footnote. The Modeler-and-Reviser symbiosis pattern holds across all three lines, with two confirmed cases at each row in the genetic and symbolic lines. The neuronal-line version, instantiated through cortical stacking, is well-documented at the structural-anatomical level. But its claim to carry the same architectural signature as endosymbiosis and polity merger is a framework theoretical commitment — not an established equivalence.

Elaboration, precisely because it doesn't commit independent parts into a new whole, can still produce enormous complexity without crossing a line. Coral reefs are a clean example at the eumetazoan level: multi-species ecosystems built around cnidarian colonies, with fish, invertebrates, symbiotic algae, and ecological productivity rivaling tropical rainforests. They are not a new phylum; the cnidarian biology that builds them stays within the eumetazoan-Reviser logic.

Corporations, modern markets, and exchanges are the parallel case at the symbolic line: multi-institutional structures built around late-empire elaboration — legal frameworks, financial instruments, supply chains, currencies — with complexity rivaling any historical empire. They are not a new line; the logic that builds them stays within empire-Reviser elaboration.

Both are platform elaborations — structures that create conditions under which other elaborations flourish. The reef anchors its ecosystem; the market anchors its institutional ecology. Neither breaks the line. Lines are rare.

Lines being rare raises a sharper question: what connects the old line to the new when a transition happens? A pattern runs across both confirmed transitions: the entity at Row 4 of column N parents the entity at Row 1 of column N+1 — Eumetazoa (Row 4, genetic) parents Bilaterians (Row 1, neuronal); Primates (Row 4, neuronal) parents Bands (Row 1, symbolic). This is the bridge-entity pattern. The Reviser motivation at the parent carries as Reviser of the child: Excitability, at Eumetazoa, is Reviser of Bilaterians; Affiliation, at Primates, is Reviser of Bands.

This is why Reviser motivations introduce at the end of the prior line and carry as Reviser of the new line: they are the goo the new line selects on. It is also why Effector and Reviser sit at opposite ends of the variation-selection cycle — Reviser generates variation, Effector is the selection-outcome.

Sample size is 2. Two confirmed bridges is enough to name the pattern but not enough to treat it as architectural law.

Row 4 entities are defined by the riskiness of the structural bet they represent. Three fates are possible, distinguishable only retroactively: the bet fails; it succeeds but stays off-thread, a real innovation opening no next line, the way fungi and plants are triumphs that parented nothing further; or it bridges, parenting the Row 1 founding form of a next line. Empire-Human is the framework's current Row 4. The framework does not claim which fate is in progress.

Our framework traces one phylogenetic thread — the thread — the lineage from protocell through bilaterians, vertebrates, mammals, primates, into bands, settlements, cities, and empires. It produced all four line transitions observed so far. Off-thread lineages — plants, fungi, slime molds, birds, bees, octopi — have their own stacks built from the same architecture, with different motivations filling the slots above their branching point. Bees branched at bilaterians, so their stack shares Persistence, Coordination, Interiority, Excitability, and Boldness with the human thread; above that, bees develop eusocial-arthropod motivations, and the lineage stops there — the bee thread never produced a symbolic line.

Octopi make the same point differently: parallel evolution becomes visible without forcing convergence. They branched at bilaterians and developed sophisticated cognition through a different neural architecture entirely. Their stack resembles the vertebrate stack at shared levels but with different motivations above the branching point — similarity where ancestry is shared, divergence where it isn't.

That converts an apparent limitation into a feature. Any organism's personality can be analyzed as a 4-sphere active window over its own evolutionary stack, wherever that stack sits. The thread that runs to symbols is one case among these, not a privileged one — the architecture applies to any thread of life.

## S-10: Acceleration and Pacing

Row intervals shrink at very different rates across the three lines.

| Line | Row intervals (bottom to top) |
|---|---|
| Genetic | ~500 MY, ~1.5 BY, ~1.2 BY (variable) |
| Neuronal | ~130 MY, ~220 MY, ~134 MY (flat, ~161 MY mean) |
| Symbolic | ~90 KY, ~5 KY, ~2 KY (accelerating) |

The genetic and neuronal lines do not accelerate within themselves. Only the symbolic line accelerates, and the mechanism is specific. Symbols mean by network position, which enables unbounded reflexivity: each generation can take the previous generation's self-understanding as an object and operate on it, with no architectural ceiling. Genes cannot be about genes. Neurons model lower neural layers but cannot operate on the modeling relation itself. Symbols can take their own operations as objects, indefinitely. The symbolic line accelerates because its goo removes the architectural limit on reflexive iteration that constrains the other two.

The same divergence shows up in generation rates: the slow loop — carrier instantiation, selection, deprecation — runs at vastly different speeds across lines, from genetic units turning over across millions of years to symbolic institutional carriers turning over across decades. Same dynamic, different rates.

The acceleration claim needs a caveat. A log-linear trend fits the symbolic line's four row-emergence dates reasonably well, but the inter-row ratios bounce between roughly 2.5x and 18x: the acceleration is real, the smoothness is not. Perreault (2012) found that rates of cultural evolution are inversely correlated with measurement interval, similar to biological rates — deep history is chunked into fewer rows than recent history, which manufactures some of the apparent smoothness. The phase change at band-emergence is real; the smooth within-line acceleration is partly real and partly a chunking artifact.

New lines consistently emerge well before their parent line would have produced its next row. Bilaterians, founding the neuronal line, emerge ~250 MY after Eumetazoa, against genetic row intervals of ~500 MY to ~1.5 BY. Bands, founding the symbolic line, emerge ~66 MY after Primates, about 41% into the ~161 MY mean neuronal row interval. But the sample is two transitions. The qualitative pattern holds across both — a new line emerges before its parent reaches its next row — though a single percentage cannot summarize it, since genetic row intervals are variable. Suggestive, not architecturally established.

Does the symbolic line's compression have a floor? Row lifespan presumably cannot compress below the cycle-times of whatever maintains the symbolic goo — though no direct evidence supports this beyond the reasoning itself. Three versions, descending in confidence. Strong: rows compress until they fall below line-renewal cycles, breaking the inheritance loop — probably false, since cycles likely rescale rather than break. Moderate: rows compress until they fall below the human developmental cycle (~20 years), forcing symbolic transmission to bypass biological humans — defensible but exposed. Weak, and recommended: saturation is the emergence question restated. When row compression approaches biological floors, the line exits through new-line emergence rather than continuing to add rows — consistent with the emergence pattern above, and it does not require the inheritance loop to break.

## S-11: What the Trellis Framework Adds

The Trellis Framework belongs to the same family as Maynard Smith and Szathmáry's Major Evolutionary Transitions (MET, 1995), Waring and Wood's work on evolutionary transitions in inheritance and individuality (2021), and the 2023 Royal Society B special issue on socio-cultural evolution. That cultural evolution constitutes a new inheritance line layered on the genetic is an established claim; dual inheritance theory has long explained why cultural transmission runs faster than genetic transmission.

Within that family, the framework's distinctive apparatus is the four-row architecture — Effector, Regulator, Modeler, Reviser — instantiated as line-specific machinery at every level and configured into fifteen discriminable Venn regions whose adjacent-pair loops, triple constellations, and opposite-pair signatures follow from the row functions. The same architecture organizes the four-team relay (flag-carriers handing off at every rung, the fast loop and the slow loop running the same exploration-consolidation dynamic at different timescales) and the maturity gradient (continuous consolidation from exploratory to inherited-infrastructural). It extends across every line and every goo type because surviving lineages are good regulators isomorphic to internalized environmental models, and the cybernetic minimum is substrate-independent.

This is what makes the framework a theory of personality across lines rather than a theory of human personality with metaphorical extensions: personality falls out of the architecture when the unit is an individual primate, and the architecture applies wherever a cybernetic loop is subject to inheritance.

Against the MET and dual-inheritance literature specifically, what the framework claims to add: mechanism specificity for the symbolic phase change, where standard accounts are demographic or fidelity-based and this one argues symbols mean by network position, enabling unbounded reflexivity; a parallel-row structure across lines, where MET frameworks list transitions without arguing that genetic, neuronal, and symbolic lines share the same internal Effector–Regulator–Modeler–Reviser structure; integration with Bennett's neural stack, giving the neuronal line its own row structure rather than treating neural inheritance as a single transition; a saturation and column-emergence prediction, where standard cultural-evolution models predict acceleration without predicting a transition condition at all; and a bridge-entity formalization that makes explicit, from two confirmed line transitions, a parent-of-next-line pattern only implicit in MET.

These five points are framework-native positioning, not established results: each carries no external evidence beyond the argument's own construction. Establishing them against the MET and dual-inheritance literature, rather than asserting them against it, remains outstanding work; readers should weigh them accordingly.
