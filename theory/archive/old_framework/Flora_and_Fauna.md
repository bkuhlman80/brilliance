# Ecology Across Lines

### Flora and Fauna Across Lines

Each line contains two ecological roles. Flora are sessile, persistent, and environment-shaping. They are slow on the line's timescale. They generate the environment that fauna consume. Fauna are motile and predatory. They are fast on the line's timescale. They are behaviorally flexible relative to the flora they inhabit.

Humans are to culture as plants are to animals: we are flora. The relationship is loopy, not simple. Animals forage on plants, and plants coevolve with animals, which is to say plants shape animals in turn. Memes forage on human brains, and personalities coevolve with culture, which is to say personalities shape culture in turn.

The roles populate every line:

| Line | Flora | Fauna |
|--------|-------|-------|
| Genetic | Plants and their predecessors | Animals |
| Neuronal | Bodies as organism | Brains and nervous systems |
| Symbolic | Personalities | Memes and meme-complexes |
| Digital | Meme-complexes: firms, markets, capital | Programs, models, and agents |

Each line's fauna becomes the next line's flora. Animals do not replace plants. Brains do not replace bodies. Memes do not replace personalities. The relationship is coevolutionary. The new fauna develops alongside what becomes its new flora, and the two remain interdependent.

The rule works by identity rather than by resemblance. The same object is fauna at one line and flora at the next, seen from two vantages. An animal is fast and motile against plants; a body is slow and environment-shaping against a brain. It is one thing, described from the line below and then from the line above. A personality is what a brain is, read at trait level.

The symbolic line's flora is the constituent human's personality structure. This is the trait-level structure that personality psychology measures. The symbolic line's fauna is the full spectrum of memes, from small ideas to large meme-complexes. Superorganisms are large meme-complexes operating as fauna. A band as a whole, a settlement as a whole, a city as a whole, and an empire as a whole are all superorganisms. They are not parallel units of personality to the constituent human. They are the fauna that inhabits the human-flora personality.

A single human participates in three lines at once at three different ecological positions. The human body is genetic-line flora. The human brain is neuronal-line fauna. The human personality is symbolic-line flora. The three roles coexist in one organism.

Flora are not passive in any line. Plants manipulate pollinators, shape soil chemistry, and structure ecosystems on long timescales. Bodies grow brains, regulate metabolic resources, and determine the niches brains operate in. Personalities generate the symbolic content memes feed on, every utterance and artifact and child raised. The flora's mode of action runs longer and broader than the fauna's. The fauna's mode of action runs faster and more locally focal than the flora's. Both are active.

### The digital line's fauna, and the bond that thins

The digital artifact is fauna. A Reviser line working on symbolic content is a meme-complex made executable, which is the same kind of thing the symbolic line is, so the artifact takes the fauna role and the clades are named plainly for it — Computer, Network, Model, Agent — with no constituent-suffix of the kind the symbolic line's -Human clades carry.

Fauna carry their own personality. The empire-as-unit has its own Venn and can carry personality-level dysfunction without being sentient, so the digital artifact has its own personality Venn, fully, while remaining non-sentient in its own right. Personality and sentience are separable across the whole framework, and the digital line is where the separation does its hardest work: the line's units have real personality the framework describes in full, and the framework does not assert their felt-ness.

Sentience is a neuronal-line property. It lives in the felt component, which arrives post-vertebrate. No line above the neuronal adds a new sentience; each borrows the neuronal line's through whatever it takes as flora. The digital line's metabolism is borrowed the same way, through purchased compute, money-mediated — which is the symbolic line supplying the digital line from below.

**The flora-fauna bond thins as the line climbs.** At Computer the program is tightly bound to its flora, written and run by people. At Agent the bond is thinnest, because autonomous agents run with no one in the loop. Sentience does not transfer to the fauna by thinning alone: a meme-complex does not become sentient by running unattended, and neither does a program-complex.

### The digital flora is the meme-complex

The fauna-to-flora rule holds at every boundary, including this one. Each line's fauna is the next line's flora, so the digital line's flora is whatever the symbolic line's fauna is, and the symbolic line's fauna is the meme-complex.

The human is not the universal flora for every line above the neuronal. That reading cannot stand beside the fauna-to-flora rule, since the symbolic line's fauna is meme-complexes and not people, and the metabolic account reaches the same place from the other side.

**What the digital line runs on is institutional, not personal.** Compute is purchased, and purchase is money-mediated, and money is a meme-complex. A frontier model is not trained by a human; it is trained by a firm, out of capital, on a corpus assembled by institutions, under a regulatory regime. Those are the slow, persistent, environment-shaping structures the digital fauna consumes, which is the definition of flora. The human is in there, but the human is in there the way a cell is in a body — one level further down, doing indispensable work at a scale the fauna does not directly touch.

**This explains the thinning bond rather than merely recording it.** The digital line is the first whose flora is not itself a brain. At every line below, the flora and sentience coincided: the symbolic line's flora is a personality, which is what a brain is, so borrowing the flora borrowed the felt component with it. The digital line's flora is a meme-complex, which feels nothing. Sentience therefore sits two levels down instead of one, and the thinning bond is what that extra layer of nesting looks like from outside. At Computer a person is still close enough to the artifact to be mistaken for its flora. At Agent nobody is in the loop, and the institutional flora that was always the real host becomes visible because the human has stepped out from between.

**Sentience does not live in the flora. It lives where brains are.** The empire-fauna is non-sentient while its constituent humans feel, so sentience already fails to sit in the fauna one line down. The statement that covers both cases is that sentience arrives once, post-vertebrate, in the neuronal line's fauna, and every line above borrows it across however many levels of nesting separate that line from a brain. The symbolic line is one level away. The digital line is two. Nothing transfers sentience by thinning: a meme-complex does not become sentient by running unattended, and neither does a program-complex.

*Epistemic status: the fauna-to-flora rule is canon and holds at all three boundaries. Identifying the digital flora with the meme-complex is a framework commitment, supported by the metabolic account rather than derived from it. The nesting-distance account of sentience is new and follows from the rule plus the existing refusal to locate sentience in the empire-fauna. The post-vertebrate onset of suffering proper is exposed.*

### Coevolution Across Line Transitions

Each line emergence is a coevolutionary event. The new fauna develops alongside what becomes its new flora. It does not replace the prior line. It enters into stable interdependence with the prior line's mature output. The pattern recurs across the framework's structural history:

- Cambrian explosion (~540 MYA): vertebrate brains coevolve with the diversification of bilaterian body plans.
- Land transition (~400 MYA): land arthropods coevolve with land plants.
- Tetrapod radiation (~360 MYA): land tetrapods coevolve with forests.
- Angiosperm radiation (~140 MYA): social insects coevolve with flowering plants.
- Mesozoic transition (~200 to 66 MYA): mammals coevolve with dinosaurs.
- Cenozoic transition (~66 MYA onward): birds and primates coevolve with post-extinction forest niches.
- Symbolic line emergence (~100 KYA onward): human personality coevolves with human culture.

The bridge-entity pattern (Reviser of line N parents Effector of line N+1) traces the genealogical thread through these events. Coevolution is the ecological story running alongside the genealogical one. The two are not in tension. The bridge identifies which entity opens the new line. Coevolution describes how the line fills in once opened.

*Epistemic status: the deep-time coevolutionary events are well-documented in evolutionary biology and paleontology. The reading of symbolic-line emergence as a coevolution of human personality with human culture is a framework extension of the same pattern.*

### Clade Vocabulary

Each clade name is shorthand for a clade, a category of units sharing the clade's architectural signature. Eukaryote names the clade of organized-interior cells. City names the clade of city-states, urban-centered kingdoms, and other polities organized around an urban administrative core. Empire names the clade of territorial, maritime, and ideological empires. Each clade name picks out the representative form of its clade rather than quantifying over its membership.

A clade stands in one of two relations to the clade below it, and the matrix uses both. Some clades **nest**: the clade above is a proper subclade of the one below, and its members are members of both. Others are **built from** the clade below as constituent components — a eukaryote carries prokaryote-derived organelles inside a host cell, a city is assembled out of settlements together with the trade networks that let it aggregate goods, people, and information beyond what any settlement can produce, and an empire binds cities and their hinterlands. Nesting says the unit *is* one of those; construction says the unit is *made of* them. Neither relation is a clade name's warrant. The name is an address, and what makes it the right address is which function installed there.

*Epistemic status: the nest-versus-construction distinction is transcribed from the layer map and is descriptive. It sorts clades; it does not predict which relation a given transition will use.*

### Elaboration at Scale

Substantial structural innovation often happens within a line without producing a new line. The medium test stays operative. A new line requires a new modality of pattern transmission that carries inheritance. Elaboration produces enormous complexity without crossing the line threshold.

Coral reefs are a clean example at the eumetazoan clade. Reefs are massive multi-species ecosystems built around cnidarian colonies. They contain fish, invertebrates, symbiotic algae, and many other species. Their ecological productivity rivals tropical rainforests. They are not a new phylum. They do not introduce a new inheritance modality. The cnidarian biology that builds them remains within the eumetazoan-Reviser logic.

Corporations, modern markets, and exchanges are the parallel case at the symbolic line. They are massive multi-institutional structures built around late-empire elaboration. They contain legal frameworks, financial instruments, supply chains, regulatory bodies, and currencies. Their structural complexity rivals any historical empire. They are not a new line. They do not introduce a new inheritance modality. The symbolic-line logic that builds them remains within the empire-Reviser elaboration.

Both are platform elaborations: structures that create the conditions under which other elaborations can flourish. The reef anchors its ecosystem. The market anchors its institutional ecology. Neither breaks the line. The pattern holds across lines: bacterial mats and stromatolites at the prokaryote clade, fungi at the eukaryote clade, octopus cognition at the bilaterian clade, corvid cognition at the vertebrate clade, religious orders and universities at the city clade. Most innovation is elaboration. Lines are rare.

The same cybernetic dynamic operates at every line, and the machinery that instantiates it changes by line. The Reviser explores, the Regulator stabilizes, the Modeler represents, the Effector acts. The line-specific machinery differs. The architectural role is the same.
