# Positioning

*Where the framework sits relative to the fields it borrows from. Not a cut through the architecture — a statement of what kind of thing this is and what work it is claiming to do.*

---

## What kind of theory this is

The framework is structural-explanatory first. Its lead move is retrodictive: explaining why the Big Five, the Dark Triad, HiTOP, and the p-factor took the structural shapes they did, given an architecture that was not built to produce them. Its forward-looking move is deliberately narrow — predicting the structure of a mechanistic-trait taxonomy as that program develops — because that is where it has traction and the rest would be overreach.

Specific testable predictions are extracted downstream rather than enumerated here. A framework that lists its predictions in its own overview is usually listing the ones it already knows the answers to.

## Upstream of the theory crisis

The cleanest position in the contemporary methodological literature is *upstream* of four papers in personality psychology and psychopathology that converge on one argument when read together.

- **Eronen and Bringmann (2021)** diagnose the field's theory crisis: too few robust phenomena, weak construct validity, fat-handed interventions that move many things at once.
- **Bleidorn (2024)** applies that diagnosis to lifespan personality development and finds the field rich on phenomena and weak on causes.
- **Bareinboim et al. (2020)** supply the causal-inference apparatus distinguishing observational, interventional, and counterfactual claims.
- **Ringwald (2025)** calls for mechanistic traits — stable person-environment dynamics measured through intensive longitudinal designs, rather than actuarial risk prediction.

The framework is not in dialogue with these four. It is the architectural-explanatory account their field-level diagnoses imply is missing. Each of them says, in its own register, that the field has descriptions and no mechanism. The framework's claim is that the mechanism is architectural rather than behavioral, and that supplying it is a different job from collecting more phenomena.

That position carries an obligation worth stating plainly: a framework claiming to be upstream of a diagnosis has to be falsifiable by something other than the diagnosis. What would count against it lives in the counts — a layer grid that fails an audit, a slot that carries when the layer rule says it must break.

## Three camps in philosophy of biology, and the framework splits across all three

The field is usually described as a two-sided argument and it is a triangle, with each pair allied against the third.

- **Functionalism.** Form is explained by selection and optimization. Dawkins, Maynard Smith, Dennett.
- **Structuralism, or laws of form.** Form is explained by generative or physical constraint, and selection filters what constraint allows. D'Arcy Thompson, Goodwin, Kauffman, and the modern complexity-science revival.
- **Historicism, or contingency.** Form is explained by frozen accident and path dependence, and there are no biological laws, only very old outcomes. Gould, Beatty.

Gould is not simply the pole opposite the structuralists. The spandrels argument is a constraint argument, which places him partly with them against the adaptationists.

The framework splits explanatory labor across all three, and this is its actual position:

- **Structure is structuralist.** The square, the Hamming geometry, the four-line cap. What slots exist, and why there are exactly these.
- **Aim is functionalist.** Selection supplies every aim. This is the explicit refusal of the structuralist temptation, and it is refused by making no claim that anything is driven toward order rather than by arguing against one.
- **Occupancy is historicist.** Which clades filled which slots, and when. This is inference and is marked as such wherever it is claimed.

The canonical home for the disagreement is Beatty's contingency thesis: all distinctively biological generalizations are either mathematical truths or contingent outcomes, and therefore not laws. Beatty later separates two senses of contingency, causal dependence and unpredictability. The framework asserts strong causal dependence — there is no neuronal line without a genetic one, because brains need bodies — together with low unpredictability, because the order is forced. That combination is coherent, and it is underoccupied.

The nearest third way already published is Mitchell's integrative pluralism, which grades generalizations on a stability continuum rather than sorting them into laws and accidents.

## The bio-psycho-social model, made structural

The three biological lines instantiate the model medicine, psychology, and sociology converged on across the twentieth century. Bio is the genetic line, psycho the neuronal, social the symbolic.

This is a descriptive mapping rather than a relabeling, and the direction of the debt matters. Bio-psycho-social is the older and better-attested framing; what it leaves underspecified is what the line-specific machinery actually is at each level and why there are three rather than two or five. The framework supplies that detail, and it also answers the question the older model does not ask, which is what comes after social.

*Epistemic status: the four-paper reading is a characterization of published positions and is the framework's own synthesis of them, not a claim any of the four authors makes. The upstream placement is an authorial claim about where the contribution sits and is not established by anything outside the framework. The three-camp description of philosophy of biology is a reading of a live disagreement, and the placement of any named figure in it is contestable; Beatty's contingency thesis and his two senses of contingency are his own, and the framework's claim to hold strong dependence with low unpredictability is an authorial position rather than a result. The bio-psycho-social mapping is descriptive and carries no derivation.*

*A standing rule for anyone defending this position: every school earns the mud its rivals throw, and a position with no mud on it is a hedge wearing a position's clothes. Where a critique lands, it is paid or it is named as unpaid. A critique of this framework is never answered by observing that rivals have it worse.*
