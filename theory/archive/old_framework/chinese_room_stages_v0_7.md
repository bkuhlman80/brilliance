# The Three Stages and the Line Table

*Tier 4. Pre-canon. Not a compliance baseline.*

*Scope: this draft covers the device, the three stages, and the per-line table. It does not
cover the derivation of the four capacities, the psycholinguistics camps, or the survey
results, which stay where they are.*

---

## The Device

Four analysts sit in a sealed room. Each runs one of the four cybernetic functions.
Candidates enter through one wall. Reports leave through the other.

The analysts do not change. A Regulator applies blocking to whatever arrives and emits a
redundancy number, and it does this identically whether the room sits inside a cell, a
brain, a conversation, or a compute cluster. The function is line-invariant.

If the analysts are held fixed, then everything that differs across the lines sits at the
walls or at the host. The room has two walls. Three questions follow, one per stage, and
they are the only three the device permits.

1. What builds a candidate able to enter the room at all, and what governs which ones can
   be built?
2. What happens to the candidates once they are inside?
3. What happens to a report once it leaves, and what governs which ones survive?

The first question is collection, and it is the input wall. The second is selection, and it
is the room. The third is retention, and it is the output wall.

The stages are an input, a process, and an output, in that order. Nothing is selected that
was not built. Nothing is retained that did not win the room.

### Why Line-Invariance Survives

The four biases are the same at every line. What differs is the host. A cell does not judge
the way a conversation judges, because a cell is not a conversation, and the host supplies
what a winner is scored against.

The scoring therefore happens inside the room. A criterion in the room is not a fifth
analyst. It is what the four analysts' outputs resolve into, and the resolution of four is
not a fifth. Running the contest and scoring the contest are one event.

*Epistemic status: the four line-invariant analysts and the host-supplied criterion are
framework commitments. The claim that scoring is internal to the contest follows from the
canonical statement that a function's bias is the function seen from the selection side.*

## Selection Is an Event

Selection happens once per event, and the event is small. Natural selection happens at every
birth and every death. Attention happens every time a body performs an action. Conversation
happens every time a band shares information. Execution happens every time a program runs.

A criterion is therefore a property of the individual competitor at the moment of the event.
It is not a statistic over a population. A trend across many events can be measured, and
measuring one is useful, but the measurement is downstream of the criterion rather than
identical with it.

This is why every entry in the two criterion columns names a disposition of the competitor
rather than an outcome or an aggregate.

---

## Collection

Collection is the construction of a competitor. It is not the intake of a unit, and reading
it as intake is the error that made this stage hard to describe.

Development builds a phenotype that can compete. Perception builds a percept a candidate
behavior can be conditioned on. Ritualized language learning builds a token that can carry a
role. The build turns source into an executable. In every case the channel takes a stored
recipe and produces something able to enter a contest, and the criterion governs which of
those can be built at all.

No line's collection channel takes in units. Development does not collect genes. Perception
does not collect nemes. The channel is upstream of the unit's expression, not upstream of
the unit's arrival.

The stage is a funnel. What transfers from that word is the narrowing: many possible builds
go in, fewer buildable ones come out, and the criterion is the narrowing. What does not
transfer is pouring. Nothing is being collected.

A channel names a process and the space it runs in, because neither half specifies the
channel alone. Development is not morphospace and morphospace is not a process. The space is
the domain the criterion is defined over, not the subset the criterion picks out.

**Every collection criterion asks whether the thing can be produced.** Accessibility asks
whether development can build the form. Perceptibility asks whether the umwelt can deliver
the signal. Pronounceability asks whether the vocal tract can make the sound. Compilation
asks whether the build succeeds. Four instances of one question.

*Epistemic status: accessibility is established in the morphospace literature.
Perceptibility follows from the umwelt account and is uncontested at the founding cell.
Pronounceability is established in phonology. The reading of all four as one question is a
framework commitment.*

## Selection

Selection is what the four biases do. It is the contest among candidates that were built,
run over something scarce, scored by a criterion the host supplies.

The room is that contest. What transfers from the room device is the sealed wall, the
interior specialists who cannot read each other, and the single output. What does not
transfer is a human operator following a rulebook. The room is hosted rather than free
standing, and the host is what varies across the lines.

**Every selection criterion asks whether the produced thing conforms.** Reproducibility asks
whether the organism meets the demands of living. Associability asks whether the cue predicts
what it is taken to predict. Grammaticality asks whether the string fits the grammar.
Correctness asks whether the program meets the spec. Grammaticality and correctness are the
same question in two media.

### The Two-Sided Pressure at the Symbolic Line

Pronounceability names the speaker's half of a pressure that has two halves. If ease of
articulation ran unopposed, every form would erode toward a single reduced vowel. What stops
that is perceptual distinctiveness, which is the standard account of why phoneme inventories
are shaped the way they are. Speaker effort pulls forms toward simplification. Hearer
recovery pushes them back apart. Surviving forms sit in the trade-off.

The two halves sit at different stages. Pronounceability gates what can be built.
Grammaticality scores what conforms once built.

*Epistemic status: the erosion pressure is established (the shortening of frequent forms,
lenition, and the convergence of transmission chains on more learnable systems). Adaptive
dispersion as the counter-pressure is established. Placing the two halves at two stages is a
framework commitment.*

## Retention

Retention is whether a winner is written down.

Winning and being kept are different events, separated in time, and the gap between them has
its own criterion. Three lines make the gap unmistakable.

At the neuronal line a behavior can win the room, be rewarded, and still fail to be retained,
because consolidation is separately gated. The separation is measured in hours, and
consolidation runs offline during sleep.

At the symbolic line an utterance can be understood and never repeated. Threat-related
content wins recall chains and loses when people merely choose whether to share. Falsehood
was found seventy percent more likely to be retweeted than truth, with onward transmission
tracking novelty and emotional response rather than accuracy.

At the digital line a defect report can be correct, survive every analysis that produced it,
and be recorded as a false positive because the engineer reading it did not understand it.
The report won and was not kept, and nothing about its correctness was at issue in the
refusal.

The retention channel is the pool a winner is written into, and the four-level stack already
names all four. The channel is the pool and not the trace that inscribes a single unit. At
the neuronal line the learned repertoire is the pool and the engram is the trace, the same
distinction that separates the gene pool from a chromosome.

*Epistemic status: the neuronal gap is established and measured. The symbolic and digital
gaps are established from survey and field data. The genetic row is open, below.*

## The Table

| Line | Collection channel | Collection criterion | Room | Selection criterion | Pool | Retention criterion |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Genetic | development in morphospace | accessibility | a cell | reproducibility | gene pool | heritability |
| Neuronal | perception in umwelt | perceptibility | a brain | associability | learned repertoire | consolidation |
| Symbolic | ritualized language learning | pronounceability | a conversation | grammaticality | culture | re-transmission |
| Digital | the build in a type system | compilation | a compute cluster | correctness | repo | the commit |

The pool column is the four-level stack's pool row and adds nothing to it.

### What Is Not a Column

**The selector.** Each line's selector is its founding-cell breakthrough, and the per-line
label libraries own breakthroughs. Natural Selection, Attention, Conversation, Execution. A
column here would restate material that has an owner.

**The prize.** The prize is the quantized unit of the room, so one relation generates every
row rather than a column. A brain has one action to give. A conversation has one turn. A
compute cluster has one invocation. The genetic row is unresolved, below.

### Adjacent Vocabulary Worth Keeping in View

The digital collection channel has field-native neighbors. Practitioners speak of a sandbox,
and of development environments as against production environments. Both name a space where
a candidate is assembled and exercised before it is allowed to compete for anything real.

Programs have syntax, and syntax errors are caught at compilation. Grammaticality at symbolic
selection and compilation at digital collection are therefore adjacent across a stage
boundary. They are not the same slot and the adjacency is worth stating rather than hiding.

Wordlikeness is gradient phonological well-formedness, measured as phonotactic probability
and neighborhood density. It belongs under grammaticality rather than in a cell of its own.
It is the standard instrument for measuring how sayable a form is, which makes it an
instrument rather than a construct.

## Open

**The genetic retention row.** A birth is simultaneously the selection outcome and entry into
the pool, so the genetic line may have no gap between winning and being written down. If it
does not, heritability is a constraint on what can enter the contest rather than a retention
criterion, and the cell reads collapsed rather than filled. The three other lines have
measured gaps and are unaffected either way.

**The genetic prize.** The prize relation holds for the upper three hosts and does not yet
have a genetic entry.

**Correctness against the transfer test.** A criterion statable at another line is not that
line's criterion. Reproducibility, perceptibility, associability, and pronounceability all
pass. Correctness does not, because utterances and phenotypes can also be correct. The
digital sense is formal verification against a spec, which is precise, but the word is
generic.

**One word across two registers.** The selection criteria are dispositions of a competitor.
The retention criteria are events that happen afterward. Both columns are called criterion.

**The symbolic host and the symbolic breakthrough.** The room at the symbolic line is a
conversation, and Conversation is also the Band-Human breakthrough. The genetic line has no
such coincidence, where the breakthrough is Natural Selection and the host is a cell. Whether
the symbolic coincidence is a fact about the line or a naming collision is unsettled.

**The room as a named category.** Unit, room, and pool are three levels: the gene and the
cell and the gene pool, the neme and the brain and the learned repertoire, the program and
the compute cluster and the repo. The symbolic entry is a human mind, which the neuronal line
already claims. The collision is structural, because the human is the host for every line
above the neuronal.
