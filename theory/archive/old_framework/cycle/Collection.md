*One of the four stages of the selection cycle. The cross-stage material — the cycle, the four sites, why there are four, and the per-line operations grid — belongs to the cycle as a whole and is not argued here.*

## What belongs here

**Collection faces two directions, and this is the stage's live problem.** It asks what is available now, and that question has two halves: what is in my store, and what has arrived at my boundary. Defense is the second half, and this is why the theme cannot sit inside a closed four-stage loop: a closed loop has no boundary events to adjudicate. Real lineages leak, and Collection is where the leak is adjudicated, because Collection is the only sighted-unbounded operation in the cycle.

**The genetic clade reads thin, and two accounts compete.** The Venn of Lines predicts it: the genetic line is the Effector line, so its Modeler-stage entry should be weakest. The second account is that the inside half of Collection — what is in the store — is the only half a genetic unit can consult cheaply, while the boundary half is the half its line is blind to. Both can hold.

**The named failure mode is salience decay.** An item's contents stay exact while its odds of being picked fall, because the context that made it potent has dispersed. This is structurally the same as original antigenic sin, where a response to a novel influenza strain is dominated by memory of the first strain met.

## Upkeep and Decay

Every stage transition is paid for, and every site also holds its contents between visits at a cost. The payer is the same in both cases and the line items are different. An item can sit through many turns of the cycle before the cycle reaches it again, and what it is worth when it is reached is not what it was worth when it was written.

**This is not a third class of residue.** Residue divides into two classes at the understory, where binding flips, and both are produced at the moment a stage narrows: something offered and never funded, or something funded and then not funded further. What decays at a site is neither. It is a retained item, one that no stage discarded, losing ground while it is held. Same payer, different line item.

**Two things drive it.** An item is potent only relative to the context that makes it potent, and the context disperses even when the item does not, so the cost of reconstructing what the item meant rises while its contents stay exact. The condition recedes; the item does not move. And the site keeps changing around the item, because Retention writes every turn, so the store that will be narrowed is never the store the item was written into.

Both drivers land at this stage and nowhere else. Collection weights potency, and an item losing salience is an item losing at Collection. An archive can go dark while its medium is perfectly preserved.

**Salience decay is a rate and it can run negative.** A recovered index, a released corpus, or a returning reader lowers the reconstruction cost, and the item's odds rise again. Rediscovery is the same quantity running the other way rather than an exception to it.

**What is held is a pair at every line.** There is the item, and there is the condition that makes the item usable. Upkeep pays for both, and decay takes the second one first.

| Line | Unit | What is held | Upkeep | An item held and losing salience |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Genetic | gene | the sequence and the regulatory context that expresses it | repair and replication fidelity, and the homeostasis that keeps the cell able to run either | the sequence is intact and the regulatory context that once expressed it is gone |
| Neuronal | neme | the trace and the cue structure that reaches it | consolidation, and the rehearsal that keeps a cue live | the trace is intact and no cue reaches it |
| Symbolic | meme | the text and the shared context that makes it readable | retelling and teaching, the transmission that keeps a reading community in place | the text is preserved and the readers who could read it have dispersed |
| Digital | program | the file and the reader that can open it | format migration and dependency upkeep | the ticket is filed and never worked; the file is kept and the reader for its format is gone |

The table has no column naming who maintains. The unit that is selected is the unit that pays, so there is no second agent to name.

**This is the framework's most exposed quantity.** It is the one that names an observable, gives it a direction, and predicts its sign reversal. Citation half-lives, dependency rot, language death, and the drift of a regulatory context away from a conserved sequence are all measured elsewhere. The prediction the framework owns is that restoring the condition raises retrieval for items whose content never changed, and that is checkable against any of them.

## Open

- Whether this stage's event-form and standing-form come apart the way Selection's canopy contest and accumulated trend do. The split is claimed for all four themes and worked for one.

*Epistemic status: the stage, its site movement, its bias, and its bits are canon. The operations grid is transcribed, not original here. The lineage-facing theme is a Tier-4 reading and is not canon. Retrieval failure without loss of the trace is established, on cue-dependent forgetting after Tulving and Pearlstone; DNA repair and consolidation are established. Reading what is held as a pair of an item and its condition of use is a framework commitment, as is naming this stage's bias as the quantity that decays. The upkeep cells are framework inference rather than a survey, and the symbolic cell is the weakest, matching the weakness already noted for live memes. Rates are unmeasured here, including any claim that high-throughput environments accelerate the decay, and should be checked before a number is given. That the quantity can run negative is a framework prediction and has not been tested.*
