## What belongs here

**Retention is where the cycle accumulates.** Every stage narrows, but narrowing inside a turn does not accumulate, because the next turn draws fresh from the store. Only Retention writes to the store, and running one way is a between-turn property. This is what makes the four stages one cycle rather than four operations standing next to each other.

**The gate has two phases rather than one catch.** A tendril coils before it commits: the first hold is carried by turgor and comes undone when the plant dries, and only later does the core lignify into a hold that no longer depends on water. A gate that can decline needs the provisional phase, and the transition between the two is what costs. That is why the stage is a gate and not a funnel — a funnel admits everything it receives, and declining requires having held something without yet having kept it.

**Being expressed and being kept are separately gated.** A somatically excellent organism can transmit none of it, at Weismann's barrier. A rewarded behavior can fail consolidation. An understood utterance can go unrepeated. A correct defect report can be filed as a false positive. Four breaks at one boundary.

**Grammaticality and correctness are the same question in two media.**

*Epistemic status: the stage, its site movement, its bias, and its bits are canon. The operations grid is transcribed, not original here. The lineage-facing theme is a Tier-4 reading and is not canon. That accumulation is a between-turn property carried by this stage alone follows from this being the only stage that writes to the store. The tendril's two phases are established botany (Klimm, Speck and Thielen, Advanced Science, 2023): in the free coiling phase the spring's tension and its form still depend on turgor pressure, and in the stabilization phase the center lignifies and the form holds independent of hydration. Reading the provisional phase as what lets a gate decline is framework inference. Everything under Open is unwritten.*
