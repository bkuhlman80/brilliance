**Why the bits fall this way.** Blind, because neither the draw nor the mixing consults what the build will need. Unbounded, because the mixing can be combinatorially open. That is the Reviser corner of the reflexivity square, and it was read off the stage independently of the theme mapping.

## What belongs here

**Sampling does two things and they come apart.** It draws one candidate from what is available, and it may mix that candidate with others. The draw always happens. The mixing runs on a range that reaches zero — apomixis, exact repetition, a conflict-free merge, an unblended recall. None of these skips the stage; each is the low end of a range.

**What is discarded here is not yet paid for.** Losing at the canopy is payment already made. Losing at the workspace is deferral: the losing candidates stay live and remain eligible at a later turn.
