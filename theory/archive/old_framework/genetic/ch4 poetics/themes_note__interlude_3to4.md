**To:** framework-Claude composing the After-Ch-3 (interlude 3→4) poetic, when that packet is built.
**Status:** thematic charge, not a full spec. The committed spine is unchanged; the notes below say what to lean into within it.

**Spine (unchanged, committed):** "Symbiosis as a mode — what it is for two lineages to commit to one unit." *Source: 'Symbiosis and Elaboration'* (`trellis_framework`, line 18 / §Symbiosis and Elaboration). Everything below deepens this spine; none of it replaces it.

**Lean into:**

- **The love reading.** Read one way, the Eukaryote cell's inventions — being host to another, and mating — are the biology of partnership. The commitment in "symbiosis as a mode" is a commitment of the kind we call love. This is the interlude where that reading is allowed to surface.

- **Cheryl's personal layer.** Cheryl discloses to Marge that her interest in all of this grew out of grieving her former partner. The deep-time material and her grief rhyme. *(Character/content commitment: Cheryl's backstory. The placement of the disclosure — woven into the interlude's narration vs. a Marge scene in the chapter or a lunch — is the packet composer's / voice's call. This backstory should also be recorded in `book_inventions` so framework and voice stay in sync.)*

- **Lane as Cheryl's guide, eyes open.** Cheryl gives Lane a full-throated endorsement — *Power, Sex, Suicide* (the mitochondria–sex–death–love braid; `clade_reference` 326). She names that the careful consensus disputes the energetics and overrides it as her conviction. The contestedness isn't hidden; she just overrides it, eyes open.

- **The partner face of the mitochondrion.** This interlude is where the organelle's *partner* face lands — the being taken inside that the unit can't live without, the one it would grieve. (The *engine* face was spent at After-Ch-2 / bigness.) Same organelle, two faces, Lane across both.

**Available enrichment (optional, not mandated):**

- **Symbiosis × mating, the two mergers.** The eukaryote invents two ways to make one out of two: symbiosis merges *unlike* lineages, permanently, into one unit; sex merges *like* genomes, recurrently, within one lineage. The interaction is the gold — the symbiont reaches forward and writes the rules of mating: the mitochondrion is inherited from one parent, and the cell sorts itself into mating types to enforce it. This lands on the committed **Foraging Type / Anticipator** Bramblogue (Regulator × Modeler) at Eukaryote (`Inheritance_Lines` 209, 222). Use to enrich the love throughline; the spine stays symbiosis-as-a-mode.

**Epistemic discipline (with the Cheryl exception):** the contested causal stories — bioenergetics as prime mover of complexity; mating types evolving *in order to* suppress mitochondrial conflict (Hurst & Hamilton; Lane's synthesis) — are leading but contested. The framework register marks them contested. Cheryl, in her own voice, may override eyes open. The two are reconciled by Cheryl *naming* the dispute and choosing her belief, not by laundering it as settled.
