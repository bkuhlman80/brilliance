# Resource Index — ABHOP project doc map

The living project is ten documents. This index maps which document owns which
material, in four tiers. Ownership is single: each topic has exactly one owner.
When two documents carry the same material and disagree, the owner wins and the
other is the derivative to fix. When this index disagrees with a document it points
to, the document wins and this index is updated.

Tier 1 — Canon: geometry and deep-time theory. `trellis_framework`.
- Part I owns the deep-time evolutionary story: personality as biographical
  signature, the cybernetic-inheritance foundations, what lines are made of, the
  goo Venn, how the bramble grows, ecology across lines, genealogy and the thread,
  and what ABHOP adds.
- Part III owns the Venn architecture: the four cybernetic functions, the
  adjacent-pair loops, the absence-recruits principle, the triples and the four
  capacities, the opposite-pair signatures, and the disease/disorder/
  disorganization taxonomy.
- Part I additionally owns the three realized kernels (the blind, unbounded peak
  within each of the genetic, neuronal, and symbolic lines), the full development
  of the neme and the four-level stack across all lines, the developed account of
  attention as selector, and the fourth line's characterization as the Reviser of
  the tree of life.
- Part IV owns the ethics and aesthetics of maintenance: the visible tax, the
  disease/disorder/disorganization failure taxonomy read as maintenance failure,
  gate-slipping as the Pursuer's unifying act, and the art of maintenance
  (the maintainer's grace, beauty as maintenance's dividend). Room for growth as
  the planned disorder book develops. The mechanics half (the seam, the two modes, the origami identity, the
  three Darwinisms) remains in `visible_tax_draft`, Tier 4.
- Part II is a stub stating the two-mind reading and pointing to bramble_specs
  Part I.

Tier 2 — Canon: Bramble engineering. `bramble_specs`.
- Part I owns cognitive architecture: the Evolutionary Algorithm, the two minds,
  Systems 0/1/2, clade-locking, the growth map, consolidation and sleep, motor
  atonia, the attachment system, self-localization, and the per-cell software
  constraints.
- Part II owns energy and thermal regulation. Part III owns the twelve knobs.
  Part IV owns the face-screen readout.

Tier 3 — Canon: per-line label libraries and clade reference.
- `Inheritance_Lines_genetic`, `_neuronal`, `_symbolic`, `_digital` own the
  per-cell label libraries: motivation names, capacity names, signature constructs,
  and breakthroughs at each cell. Line-split. When a label here and a label
  elsewhere disagree, the Inheritance_Lines file wins. The digital line is canon on
  the same footing as the other three; it is labeled for fewer clades, a
  development gap, not a lower epistemic standing.
- `trellis_clade_by_clade` owns the cell-by-cell empirical and structural
  reference.

Tier 4 — Pre-canon memos (not a compliance baseline). Not checked against for
compliance; not canon.
- `visible_tax_draft` develops the Regulator's maintenance work at the line scale,
  mirror to the Reviser's variation. Earlier-stage; for scrutiny.

Compliance rule. Compliance-checking runs against Tiers 1 through 3 only. Tier 4 is
excluded. Prose is checked for contradiction against the owning document.
