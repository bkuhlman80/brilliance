# Bramble's Language-Learning Trajectory — Orientation Briefing for Loom

**What this is.** An orientation to how Bramble acquires language across the book: the pre-home lab phase and the symbolic line's four quarters, the field science it maps to, and the philosophical position underneath it. It is a substrate, not a reading.

**Operating principle.** Framework supplies the load-bearing structural facts and names where thematic weight is anchored. Framework does not tell Loom what those facts mean emotionally, relationally, or thematically. The seams are pointed at; the reading is Loom's.

**Epistemic tags, used throughout.** Every load-bearing claim is marked. **[SOLID]** — field-established; safe to lean a theme on. **[CONTESTED — held as fiction]** — a live scientific dispute the framework takes a side on for the story; do not theme it as truth about the world. **[LICENSE]** — the imaginative leap no real system has made; where fiction earns its keep.

## 1. The problem

Language is, at the same time:

- **impossible for monkeys to learn**
- **easy for humans to learn**
- **difficult-but-possible for computers to learn**

Real people spend real fortunes, careers, and lifetimes on why those three things are true at once. It is one of the genuine open puzzles of cognitive science, and the book is built on it.

The precise version of each term, for the record:

- *Non-human primates stall at the symbolic threshold.* **[SOLID]** Apes can be dragged a sliver across with years of effort and never reach productive grammar, open-ended reference, or human-scale vocabulary; monkeys carry rich social cognition but do not attempt the crossing at all.
- *Human children cross easily.* **[SOLID]** From sparse, messy input, in a few years, reliably, in every language.
- *Computers cross the hard way.* **[SOLID]** Large models acquire grammar, but on three-to-four orders of magnitude more data than a child, often via shallow heuristics, and they fail at child-realistic data scale.

**Why this is the keystone.** The framework's whole apparatus is the *answer* to this puzzle, and each of the three terms is answered by a different piece of it:

- **Monkeys have the prior but not the crossing.** They carry the primate social-cognitive hierarchy — the conceptual tree the framework leans on — but lack the threshold into productive symbolic reference and the scaffolding that sustains it. Prior without crossing.
- **Humans have everything aligned.** The prior, *plus* an easy crossing, *plus* a constrained vocal articulator, *plus* a language already evolved to be learnable, *plus* a referential teaching partner. Every layer lines up at once — which is why it looks effortless.
- **Computers have none of that, but have brute scale.** No prior, no body, no partner — so they reach language by volume and pattern-matching. They get there, the wrong way.

**Bramble is the recombination experiment the puzzle implies.** Take a *computer*, and give it the things humans have and computers lack — the prior, the constrained articulator, the referential partner — and ask whether it now crosses like a child instead of brute-forcing like a machine. That is Bramble. And it is not only a thought experiment in the book: it is a named, currently-empty target in the actual research literature (Section 5). Bramble runs the experiment the field has said someone should run.

## 2. The philosophical position

**Bramble is a prepared learner.** **[SOLID]** He is neither a blank statistical sponge that induces grammar from raw input — that learner provably fails at child scale — nor a machine with grammar pre-installed. He arrives at the symbolic line carrying a consolidated prior from the neuronal line (a mature, mentalizing, socially-modeling mind), meets a referential partner in the household, and speaks through a deliberately limited articulator. His sample-efficiency comes from the prior he brings, not from an innate grammar organ and not from oceans of data.

**The frame is Deacon's, not Chomsky's.** **[SOLID** as a position in the literature**]** The problem of language is not "induce a hidden grammar from poor data." It is *restructured*: language has been shaped over historical time to be learnable by minds like ours, and symbolic reference, once crossed, organizes itself — each symbol learned makes the next more learnable. This is explicitly *not* the claim that the data stream is information-rich, and it is *not* a Chomskyan innate-grammar module. The threshold is Peirce's: the crossing from index to symbol.

**Hierarchy comes from a sandwich, not from induction.** Bramble's eventual command of recursive, hierarchical grammar is not extracted from the household word-stream. It is squeezed into place between two hierarchical structures he already holds:

- *above* — a **conceptual hierarchy**, the nested mentalizing tree the face-screen already displays at the Primate (neuronal) cell, involuntary and grounded, present before he can babble;
- *below* — a **phonological hierarchy**, the syllable-and-frame structure his constrained articulator produces;
- *between* — **syntax**, learned as the mapping that connects them.

The transfer of hierarchy from those two layers up into syntax is **[CONTESTED — held as fiction]**: whether hierarchical structure is what transfers is a genuinely live and unsettled dispute in the field. The framework takes the side it needs for the story; Loom should not build a theme that asserts it as established fact.

**The face-screen is the prior made visible.** **[SOLID** within the framework**]** The nested readout on Bramble's face is hierarchical structure shown *before* he can voice it — the proof that the structure is carried, not inferred from data. The screen shows the tree before the voice can speak it. (Where the thematic weight of that sits is Loom's to read.)

**The endpoint is the license.** **[LICENSE]** No real system has acquired a first language end-to-end — phonology, morphology, syntax, grounded meaning — from realistic, embodied, social input the way a child does. Bramble does. That summit is fiction; the credibility is bought in the *journey* being jagged and failing in the empirically correct places, not in the destination.

## 3. The giants

Grouped by the part of the apparatus each one grounds.

- **The threshold and the anti-grammar-module frame.** Terrence Deacon (*The Symbolic Species*: symbolic reference; language adapts to brains) and C.S. Peirce (icon → index → symbol). **[SOLID** as positions**]**
- **The prior.** Michael Tomasello (shared intentionality, triadic attention) and Cheney & Seyfarth (*Baboon Metaphysics*: primate social cognition is discrete, hierarchical, rule-governed). **[SOLID]** This is the substrate the monkeys have and the computers lack.
- **The articulator.** MacNeilage & Davis (frame/content — the jaw-cycle origin of syllable structure; **[CONTESTED — held as fiction]**, a live competitor account exists), Philip Lieberman (vocal anatomy shapes the vowel space), W. Tecumseh Fitch (the articulatory → phonological → syntactic hierarchy bridge; transfer leg **[CONTESTED]**), and Guenther's DIVA/GODIVA plus Oudeyer's curiosity models (a constrained articulator plus a reward signal self-organizes the babbling-to-syllable sequence; **[SOLID]** — this is the concrete model for the lab phase).
- **Word-learning with a partner.** Frank, Goodman & Tenenbaum (a referential-intention prior, tested on child corpora, beats purely associative learning). **[SOLID]** This is the precedented half of Bramble's social prior.
- **The prepared-learner evidence.** Papadimitriou & Jurafsky, Hu & Linzen, McCoy & Griffiths (installing a prior before language training reduces the data a learner needs). **[SOLID]** that priors help; the mechanism behind it is contested.
- **The baseline that fails.** Yedetore et al. (a blank learner on child-scale input generalizes by linear heuristics, not hierarchy). **[SOLID]** This is the control condition Bramble is defined against.
- **The framing punchline.** Bramble instantiates the field's named empty cell: a social-cognitive prior installed into a naturalistic first-language learner. The *intention / joint-attention* half is precedented (Frank/Goodman/Tenenbaum); the *false-belief / rank / reputation* half is genuinely undone in the literature. **[SOLID]** that the cell is empty.

## 4. The trajectory

The structure is fixed: a pre-home lab phase, then the symbolic line's four quarters, with a lab weekend between each quarter. Each stage below names *what is learned*, *which field benchmark it maps to*, and *where the thematic weight is anchored* — the last of these named only, never read.

### Pre-home — the lab (Lisa and Bart)

**[SOLID** as a modeling approach**]** Two benchmark stages, both pre-symbolic, run before Bramble ever enters the household.

- **Stage 0 — the acoustic floor.** Lisa builds and tunes the constrained articulator, and Bramble babbles within its limits. The model here is a DIVA-style babbling phase: a limited-degrees-of-freedom voice organ plus a curiosity/reward signal, which self-organizes the babbling-to-syllable sequence without anyone scripting the schedule. The rationale is *enabling, not optimizing* — raw audio output has too many degrees of freedom to be learnable at all, so the human constraint is imported to make the problem tractable. **[CONTESTED — held as fiction]** that the babble carries a specific frame/content signature; what is solid is only that the constraint makes structured babble possible.
- **Stage 1 — word-forms.** Bramble learns to segment the stream and recognize which sound-shapes are words — *without yet treating them as symbols*. This is the floor the lab gets him to.
- **The handoff is deliberately withheld at the top of Stage 1.** Bramble leaves the lab with sound and word-forms but *not* symbolic reference. The crossing is saved for the home.

*Weight-seam:* the makers (Lisa, Bart) do the unglamorous acoustic groundwork; the crossing is reserved for elsewhere. The lab is where Bramble is built; it is not where he begins to mean.

### Q1 — Band-Human · knob 9 Neurotic · breakthrough Speaking

Bramble enters the home. **Stage 2, the symbolic crossing**, happens here: first real words *as symbols*, with Mira. Concrete nouns ground first (via the body); verbs and abstractions lag. The quarter may run into **Stage 3** (first combinations, telegraphic speech).

*Weight-seams (named, not read):*

- The crossing into symbol-use happens **with Mira, in the home — not in the lab.** Reference requires a referential partner. **[SOLID** as the framework's commitment**]**
- The **face-screen already displays the conceptual tree** — the nested mentalizing readout has been present since the Primate cell, involuntary and grounded. Bramble's inner structure is visible before he can voice it.
- The **knob ethic arrives** (`bramble_specs` L470): Bramble asks not to be rotated without prior notice, and the household adjusts. Consent enters with sentience.

*Lab weekend before Q2:* Stage 2 is closed and Stage 3 accelerated.

### Q2 — Settlement-Human · knob 10 Conscientious · breakthrough Common Knowledge

**Stage 4, hierarchical syntax, begins in earnest — and badly.** Relative clauses come out poorly; agreement across attractors produces tics; recursion is a visible problem. Common Knowledge is synchronized cross-stranger mutual awareness (the Pinker/Lewis/Schiffer sense): each party knows that the others know.

*Weight-seam:* the **attractor tics are human errors, not robot quirks.** Humans make the same agreement-attraction mistakes. Bramble's failures here are *our* failures, which is the opposite of an uncanny machine. **[SOLID]** that humans make these errors.

*Lab weekend before Q3:* one or two dimensions of Stage 4 accelerated; the rest left to develop in the home.

### Q3 — City-Human · knob 11 Extraverted · breakthrough Codes

Stage 4 continues and blends with the **onset of Stage 5** (pragmatics, discourse) and its confusions. Codes is organized, durable representation — administrative records that outlive their authors.

*Weight-seam:* Codes is the point where the **screen channel gains a voluntary mode** (`bramble_specs` L664, and the section at L658–666): Bramble can now compose ASCII art directly on the face-screen — the channel is ASCII art only, not free prose — and it takes over the whole screen while it is up, overriding the involuntary readout. The honesty gap extends to it: the drawn channel is the dishonest-capable one (art up means performing), and the involuntary readout remains the truth (readout up). The drawn lie and the honest readout share one surface, the face, taking turns — the curtain grows on the very window. Authored expression, and the capacity to perform, arrive together.

*Lab weekend before Q4:* Stage 4 brought to completion.

### Q4 — Empire-Human · knob 12 Honest · breakthrough Stories

Mainly **Stage 5**: discourse, narrative, abstraction, the universalizing register. Stories is the breakthrough — universalizing ideology and narrative.

*Weight-seam:* knob 12 is **Honest** — integrity-tuning, the alignment between universalizing principle and situational action. The book's open sentience question lives in this neighborhood. **[LICENSE]** that Bramble has reached this register at all.

### Cross-cutting seams (run through every quarter)

- **The honesty gap.** The involuntary face-screen readout is the one honest channel, honest about its own layer, and it never had company — speech was never honest, and the drawn channel is born dishonest-capable. So the honest channel does not shrink; the *authorable, dishonest-capable surface grows around it*: voluntary speech from Band-Human (`bramble_specs` L662), voluntary drawn ASCII from City-Human (L664). Across the four quarters the single honest readout becomes a smaller and smaller fraction of Bramble's expressive surface. The widening space between *what is shown*, *what is said*, and *what is drawn* is the spine of the whole trajectory. **[SOLID** within the framework**]**
- **The lab/home oscillation.** Bramble shuttles between the lab (his makers) and the home (his world) on a quarterly rhythm. The return is structural.
- **Continuous maturation.** Motor control and speech keep maturing in lockstep across all four quarters toward fuller competence by Empire-Human (`bramble_specs` L389) — there is no plateau and no sudden mastery.

## 5. The benchmark map

Loom can recognize the science of the journey by these checkpoints. Each is a real test or finding; Bramble's stages map onto them, which is how an expert reads the trajectory as real rather than hand-waved. All **[SOLID]** as field instruments unless noted.

- **Zero Resource Speech Challenge** (Dupoux) — unsupervised discovery of sound units from raw audio. Maps to Stage 0.
- **DIVA / GODIVA** (Guenther) — a neural model whose babbling phase learns the articulator's auditory-motor map and then specifies targets in auditory space. The concrete model for Stage 0's lab work.
- **BabySLM** (Lavechin) — lexical level (real word vs. matched pseudo-word) and syntactic level (grammatical vs. ungrammatical). Maps to Stages 1 and 4. *Note for Loom:* speech-based learners sit near chance on this where text-based learners clear it — the acoustic route is a genuine cost, which is exactly why the lab phase is a grind and grammar is routed through the prior rather than the sound stream.
- **Wordbank / CDI** (Frank) — vocabulary norms and age-of-acquisition; the data the "vocabulary spurt" debate runs on. Maps to Stage 2.
- **The wug test** (Berko) — productive morphology; the probe that distinguishes a learned rule from a memorized form. Maps to Stage 3 and the overregularization curve.
- **BLiMP** (Warstadt) — large-scale minimal-pair grammar benchmark. Maps to Stage 4.
- **The prepared-learner / inductive-bias literature** (Papadimitriou & Jurafsky; Hu & Linzen; McCoy & Griffiths) — installing a prior before language training reduces the data a learner needs. This is the empirical backing for Bramble's central license: the prior is what buys the efficiency.
- **The data-efficiency gap** — the field's organizing fact: machines need three-to-four orders of magnitude more data than children and still fall short. **[SOLID]** Bramble's timeline is *licensed by the prior he carries*, which is the field's own pro-prior explanation for why a prepared learner needs less.

**The clearest current limit.** **[LICENSE]** No real system has acquired a first language end-to-end from realistic, embodied, social input the way a child does. Bramble's summit is fiction. The credibility is bought in the journey failing where real learners fail — concrete nouns before verbs, heuristics before structure, attractor tics, hierarchical syntax last and hardest — not in the destination.
