# Ch1 Ledger Map — Pass A (Protocell)

*Reverse pass: iterating the clade-1 ledger, searching the chapter for each element. Fifth chapter run, out of sequence. This is the founding chapter — the one where the framework's own vocabulary (Capacity, Breakthrough, Tendency, Motivation) gets coined on the page, which shapes some of what follows below.*

## Motivations

### Persistence (Motivation · Effector)
**Coverage: SATURATED**
- STATED — the chapter's entire climax: 4.9.b's extended debate over whether "motivation" is even the right word, landing on "the protobot's motivation is to keep being a protobot" (Bart); 4.13.a, Burns: "It has one motivation... Keep operating. That's the whole of it."
- DRAMATIZED — the entire physical build (1.a–c): the wind-up crank, the weekly drain, the twitch-and-refill cycle — persistence engineered as literally as possible.
- RESONANT — §5, the closing coda: *Deinococcus radiodurans* reassembling itself after five thousand grays, *Sodalis* carrying an unused genetic readiness for millions of years, set against a thermostat that was simply manufactured and closed. One of the strongest resonant passages found in any chapter run so far.
- Count: heaviest coverage of any single element across all five chapters run to date — appropriate, since this is the chapter that names the concept for the whole book.

### Dissipation (Motivation · Regulator)
**Coverage: SATURATED**
- STATED — 4.3.b, Burns's vent monologue: "proton-motive force, the pH gradient... The protocell is at the vent, doing local work on a difference it never had to make." Close to the ledger's own Lane & Martin citation.
- DRAMATIZED — 1.a, the chapter's core mechanical conceit: "they run on the *difference* in heat... That's the Seebeck effect... The unit's eating the gap."
- Count: 1 extended STATED passage, 1 extended DRAMATIZED passage that the whole build is organized around.

### Self-Organization (Motivation · Modeler)
**Coverage: THIN**
- No STATED naming.
- DRAMATIZED (loose) — Lisa's "dispositions" language (4.4.c): "they fall down the gradient, because things fall down" — patterns emerging from physical structure with no controller, adjacent to but not a precise match for the ledger's own citation (fatty-acid vesicles self-assembling).
- Count: 0 STATED, 1 loose DRAMATIZED.

### Self-Production (Motivation · Reviser)
**Coverage: THIN**
- No STATED naming, and — notably — the chapter is explicit that the bots *lack* this: Lisa's capability list (4.4.c) denies self-regulation and self-modeling outright.
- RESONANT — §5's closing meditation is squarely about self-production (autopoiesis) as a concept, contrasting bacteria that "closed [their] own case" against manufactured objects that didn't — but it's delivered as a general meditation on life versus machinery, not specifically tied back to Bramble.
- Count: 0 STATED, 0 DRAMATIZED involving the specimen itself, 1 strong but general RESONANT passage.
- Note: see the corrected Operational Closure entry below — §4.11's "Ghost in the Machine" monologue, previously ruled off-ledger, is a closer conceptual match to Operational Closure than to Self-Production, and is credited there instead.

## Tendencies

### Throughput Type (Tendency · Effector × Regulator)
**Coverage: SATURATED**
- DRAMATIZED — the whole five-bot roster in 1.a is built around this tendency and nothing else: "1 — cranked hot... busy... 3 — the quiet one... does the least and outlasts everybody... 5 — even like 3, but I let it kick hard... burns through its tension fastest." The entire chapter's premise (five differently-tuned units) is Throughput Type made literal.
- Count: the chapter's structural spine for this element — no STATED naming needed because the whole cast dramatizes it directly.

### Aging Type (Tendency · Regulator × Modeler)
**Coverage: SATURATED**
- DRAMATIZED — Bot 4's arc: "wears a groove into itself over the weeks... ends up in a drawer by July" — unrepaired wear accumulating with no correction, exactly the ledger's own definition.
- Count: 1 sustained DRAMATIZED thread (Bot 4's whole subplot).

### Enclosure Type (Tendency · Modeler × Reviser)
**Coverage: ABSENT** — no surfacing. The bots have a fixed dome shell with no boundary-choice narrative; this tendency's biological referent (lipid bubble vs. mineral-pore walls) has no clean analog in a manufactured robot. **Coverage gap**, and an expected one.

### Acquisition Type (Tendency · Reviser × Effector)
**Coverage: ABSENT** — the ledger's own citation is about taking up building blocks (fatty acids, RNA letters, some delivered from space); the robot's gradient-skimming is energy intake, already credited to Dissipation, and doesn't extend to material uptake. **Coverage gap.**

## Capacities

### Operational Closure (Capacity · without Effector)
**Coverage: SATURATED, via absence — now with a third anchor added on reconsideration**
- STATED — 4.4.c, Lisa's capability list: "What it can't do — and there's more in the negative — it can't remember, can't model, can't model itself, can't decide, can't regulate."
- RESONANT — §5's coda draws the line explicitly: manufactured things (the thermostat, by clear extension the protobots) versus self-made things (bacteria) — "Nothing in this room made itself... I am the only thing in here that made itself."
- STATED/RESONANT — §4.11, "The Ghost," Marge's full argument. Ruled off-ledger on first pass and reclassified here on direct instruction to re-check it against the ledger rather than filing it as a standing book-level exception. The match holds up: "There is no such self. There is no homunculus... The thing that says *I am going to change* is a regulator inside the system. The thing that picks the intervention is a modeler inside the system... There is no view from nowhere from which the willing self looks in." That is Operational Closure's own definition — a unit whose order comes from no central director, "nothing directing the steps" — aimed at a human mind instead of a fatty-acid vesicle. Same claim, same shape, different substrate.
- Note: this is a legitimate absence-anchor under the spec's own rule — it names the Capacity's absence rather than inventing a negation, and does so from three directions now: an engineer's plain list, an essayist's extended meditation, and a psychologist's founding theoretical argument.
- Count: 1 STATED, 2 extended STATED/RESONANT passages. The best-anchored Capacity in the chapter.

### Precision Machinery (Capacity · without Regulator)
**Coverage: ABSENT** — no ribozyme-equivalent, no molecular-precision material anywhere in the household plot. **Coverage gap**, and an expected one — this clade's own citation is literal prebiotic chemistry, hard to dramatize with a robot at all.

### Phenotypic Plasticity (Capacity · without Modeler)
**Coverage: SATURATED**
- DRAMATIZED — Mira's tooth-brushing ritual (§3.a): Bramble "asleep" on the cold bathroom tile, "awake" the instant it's lifted into her warm hand — the same unit behaving as two functionally different things purely as a function of ambient temperature, with nothing resembling an inner model driving the switch. A precise, unforced match to the ledger's own definition.
- Count: 1 extended, original DRAMATIZED passage — one of the strongest anchors in the chapter.

### Reproduction (Capacity · without Reviser)
**Coverage: ABSENT** — the ledger's own citation (universal ATP-synthase inheritance) doesn't map onto a single non-replicating household robot. **Coverage gap**, expected.

## Breakthrough

### Natural Selection (Breakthrough, framework center)
**Coverage: SATURATED — corrected from an earlier pass that missed the strongest anchor**
- STATED — 4.2.a–b, Bart, thorough and direct: "The first thing capable of being selected on... The variation-selection cycle. That's the new thing the protocell brought."
- DRAMATIZED — §3, "Hello," in full. This was missed on first read and is worth stating plainly rather than smoothing over: the whole section is variation-and-selection staged with Mira as the selecting force. Five units, five seeded dispositions (the variation, already credited under Throughput Type). Mira differentially favors one — feeds it attention, keeps winding it, defends it in the race it always loses on the clock and "wins" on her own terms — while Bot 4 ends up abandoned in a drawer-adjacent towel and Bot 5, the objectively superior unit by every external measure, goes un-chosen. The audition scene is the clearest single image: she searches the other four for "a second Bramble," finds none of them will do, and "sat in the middle of her failed search with the only Bramble there was ticking in her lap, none the wiser, having won the audition without entering it." Differential selection by a party external to the variants, with no vote taken by the variants themselves — that is Natural Selection, dramatized about as directly as a five-object household scene can manage.
- Note for the record: this was not obvious to me on the first pass. I had filed the whole section under Throughput Type and Aging Type and stopped there, and reported this Breakthrough as thoroughly explained but never dramatized. That was wrong. Flagging the correction explicitly rather than quietly fixing the verdict, since the miss is itself informative about where this method needs a second look — I was checking for a scene that *names* selection, not for a scene that *performs* it.

## Coverage-gap summary

- **Enclosure Type**, **Acquisition Type** (Tendencies) — absent, expected given the biological referents don't map to a manufactured shell.
- **Precision Machinery**, **Reproduction** (Capacities) — absent, expected for the same reason.
- **Self-Organization**, **Self-Production** (Motivations) — thin; present only as loose or general resonance, never developed independently the way Persistence and Dissipation are.

Most of the remaining gaps are elements whose biological citations (ribozymes, ATP synthase, lipid-vesicle walls, DNA uptake) simply have no honest analog in a robot with a crank and two heat transducers. The chapter doesn't fake these — it leaves them alone, which is the correct move rather than a defect.

What's fully, richly covered: Persistence and Dissipation (both Motivations, saturated across all three modes), Throughput Type and Aging Type (Tendencies, dramatized directly through the five-bot roster), Phenotypic Plasticity (one of the cleanest single anchors found in the whole assessment), Operational Closure (three independent anchors — engineering, essay, and theory), and Natural Selection (the Breakthrough, dramatized in full through Mira's selection of Bramble — corrected above; not "thoroughly explained but never dramatized" as first reported, but SATURATED).
