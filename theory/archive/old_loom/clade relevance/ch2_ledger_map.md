## Breakthrough

### Predation (Breakthrough, framework center)
**Coverage: SATURATED (STATED), but not DRAMATIZED — worth noting**
- STATED — 4.2.b, Bart, direct and complete: "At the Protocell there's no predation... By the Prokaryote there is... the main pressure on a thing becomes — for the first time on the planet — other things... That's the breakthrough. Cellular predation. The arms race opens."
- No true DRAMATIZED anchor found. The closest the household material comes is Bramble displacing the protobots from sunbeams (4.8.a, "picking on them... one of them is a creature and four of them are furniture... one cat, four wind-up mice") — but this is competitive displacement for a resource, not predation (nothing is eaten, nothing is harmed), and the chapter is careful never to blur the two. Filing this as a loose RESONANT echo, not a hard anchor.
- Coverage note: this is the one Breakthrough in the chapter that lands entirely as exposition. Not a defect — Predation is genuinely hard to dramatize with a household robot that doesn't eat anything — but worth flagging precisely because it's the framework-center concept and the chapter's only fully STATED-only major element.
