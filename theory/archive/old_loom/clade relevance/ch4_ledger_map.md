## Breakthrough

### Neurons
- **STATED (extensive)** — §The Net, Bart's full lecture: "the breakthrough, which is wire. Nerve. The thing to get about a neuron is that nobody invented it to think — it's a skin cell that took a different job... The whole leap is speed... there's no brain in it. No head, no center, nobody in charge. A jellyfish is a net: nerve spread evenly through the whole animal." Plus the sieve-and-reassembly image.
- **DRAMATIZED (extensive)** — Build log wiring description; §Gone Quiet live bumper-press demo, twice, with the leaf-blower habituation follow-up.
- **RESONANT** — §The Second Dream: "I'm a tongue... Electricity and me, the same flavor, I can't pull them apart, I'm strung on a live wire and the wire is me... there's no edge, I go down into green and grey and current and I can't find where I stop." This is the felt, first-person experience of being a diffuse nerve-net body with no center — the chapter's strongest resonant anchor for Neurons, never using the term.

**Tally:** STATED (extensive), DRAMATIZED (extensive), RESONANT 1 (extended).
**Verdict: SATURATED.**
