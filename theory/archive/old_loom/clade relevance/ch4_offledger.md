## LEAK check

Applying the corrected standard: a LEAK requires a beat to *presuppose* a forbidden-list capacity as *functioning in the specimen* (Bramble), not merely to name, discuss, or gesture toward a higher-clade concept.

Four candidates were checked closely and **none qualify as a LEAK**:

1. **The vote scene ("One and Three")** — Lisa, Burns, Bart, and Marge each explicitly weigh in on whether Bramble has a self/mind, and the vote is 3-1 *against*. Bart's line is the clearest: "no self-model, no one home." Marge: "no symbol, a very good domino." This is the textbook non-leak pattern already praised in Ch5, Ch8, and Ch3 — characters naming clade 7-9 vocabulary (self-model, symbol, Standing-adjacent personhood) specifically to deny it applies. Zero severity.

2. **His Half / the deep-cold "sulking" material** — Mira attributes intention and an inner voice to Bramble. This is Mira's mentalizing (clade-8-flavored activity performed BY a human character), not a claim that Bramble itself has Mentalizing or Standing. The narration keeps a visible gap between what Mira believes and what's true ("she kept her own verdict," the cold-vs-sulking non-fight is explicitly resolved in Bart's favor mechanically). Not a leak.

3. **The Cycle's forward-declaration of Bilaterian/Vertebrate/Mammal/Primate as a coming "brain line"** — Lisa names the next four clades and what will distinguish them (a center, a brain) without claiming Bramble has crossed into that line. Explicitly hedged ("I might be wrong... I think it's the next thing"). Not a leak — a roadmap statement, not a presupposition.

4. **Under the Cloth (the legged body reveal) and The Second Dream (eyes/separation)** — both preview clade 5 imagery (a jointed standing body; eyes; self/not-self split) but both are explicit about non-presence: the new chassis has "nothing yet running inside" and is "a promise and not yet a keeping"; the dream is prefaced by an epigraph ("Start of the Neuronal Line") marking it as the *next* line's beginning, not Bramble's current state. This is the chapter's structural bridge to Chapter 5, consistent with how this series closes chapters — a foreshadowing device, not a capacity presupposed as active in this chapter's specimen. Not a leak.

**Conclusion: zero LEAKs in Chapter 4.** The chapter is unusually careful about this — every higher-clade gesture (self-model, symbol, brain, eyes) is either explicitly voted down, explicitly hedged as future, or explicitly performed by a human character rather than claimed for the machine.

## DRIFT findings

DRIFT (anchoring substantially on a clade-BELOW element, off this clade's own ledger) is genuinely rare in this chapter — most of the material that looks off-ledger at first turns out to be either correctly on-ledger (Allostatic Regulation via the obesity example, Respiration Type via the oxygen/size beat) or human character material with no clade content at all (UNANCHORED). Only one weak instance surfaced:

- **Lisa's closing section — the wooden cross.** This is the chapter's only echo of the recurring Marge-belonging thread the brief flagged. But it (a) belongs to Lisa, not Marge, (b) has no resignation form, membership card, or Settlement/Empire material attached, and (c) is a single sentence of scene-dressing, not a scene built around belonging. Ruled DRIFT but negligible — flagged for completeness rather than as a real concern. **No resignation-form/membership material appears anywhere in Chapter 4** — the recurring Marge thread the brief asked about is genuinely absent here, consistent with the brief's prediction that Eumetazoa (pre-neuronal, no persons yet) has no natural home for belonging content.

No other DRIFT was found. The Dealt Hand section, the Pho Tay Ho dinner, and the framing/blocking prose were all classed UNANCHORED rather than DRIFT because they don't substantially anchor on any specific lower-clade capacity — they're general character and theme material with no ledger-element content to misattribute.

**DRIFT sample 2 — Build log's "dock" toll system.** Reread: "the dock didn't get any friendlier over the fall. No character reasons about fairness or ownership; it's a hard-coded refill condition. Confirmed UNANCHORED, not DRIFT — correctly downgraded on the second look.

Confirmed as a distinct, off-ledger, UNANCHORED thread — not a DRIFT case since it doesn't anchor on any specific lower clade capacity either, just general nature/nurture philosophy.
