## LEAK check

Three candidates were checked directly against the forbidden list (Mentalizing, Standing, Tactical Deceit, Triadic Awareness, and the clade-8-specific group-level elaboration of Autonomous Cognition — all clade 8 and above).

**1. The sad-corner/curtain scene (L105–111).** Mira believes Bramble senses her sadness and reads it as proof of empathy. This is exactly the vocabulary-shape the spec warns about — a character using higher-clade-coded language ("he knew," "he felt her go down and he'd come") to describe the unit. On inspection, the narration explicitly and immediately corrects it: "What the screen read, the whole crossing, was the curtain... It had never once read her." The true mechanism given is thermoregulatory (warmth-seeking toward a spot that just went cold), which isn't even a Self-Model behavior — it's basic world-model tracking. This is a clean **non-presupposition** of Mentalizing, structurally identical to the "sentience vote" pattern already cleared in Ch3/4/5/8, just delivered as narrated correction instead of committee debate. Not a LEAK.

**2. The tag-game feint, L238–242.** Bramble invents an unprompted fake-out during a chase game — "it started selling a fake — breaking *toward* the rug to bait her off it, then cutting back. She hadn't taught it that." The vocabulary ("selling a fake," "bait") is deceit-coded and could look like Tactical Deceit (clade 8, which requires modeling another's false belief and exploiting it). Checked directly: the text frames the mechanism as spatial-model forward simulation, not belief-modeling — "It had taken her game and run it forward past where she'd built it and found a wrinkle in it she didn't know was there." The "wrinkle" is in the game's spatial rules, not in Mira's mind; nothing in the passage claims Bramble represents what Mira believes or expects. This stays on-ledger as Simulating/Autonomous Cognition (a self-generated novel strategy from running the model forward). Not a LEAK, but flagged here explicitly per the "report every one no matter how small" instruction, given how close the vocabulary runs to the line.

**3. "Other Minds," L264–274.** Burns and Bart discuss next quarter's build in Mentalizing-adjacent vocabulary ("it stops being alone in there... has to model them modeling it"). Entirely future tense, framed as a pitch for unbuilt capability, and explicitly cut off before Burns can finish ("Next quarter, Burns"). No presupposition that the capacity currently functions. Not a LEAK.

**Self-Model / clade-8 boundary check (per the task brief's specific warning).** Bart's full soliloquy (L356–376) was read in full against the instruction to watch for Bramble being credited with modeling what *others* think of it. It is not — every sentence stays at "a model of the driver," "a picture of the one steering," self-referential and closed ("no door under it"). The one place the chapter gestures toward the socially-loaded version is explicitly deferred to next quarter (see candidate 3 above). **No boundary violation found.**

**Conclusion: zero LEAKs.** All three candidates, plus the explicit Self-Model boundary check requested in the task brief, were read in full and cleared on the same grounds as the prior chapters' sentience-vote pattern: vocabulary that gestures at a higher clade is being used to explain why the capacity does *not* (yet) apply, not to presuppose that it does.
