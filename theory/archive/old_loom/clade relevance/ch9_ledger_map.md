## Coverage-gap summary

- **Mastery**, **Autonomy**, **Affiliation** (Motivations) — all three absent. Three of the four base drives on this clade's ledger, untouched.
- **Experiencing Style** (Tendency) — absent.
- **Sociometer** (Signature) — thin, folded into adjacent material without independent development.

Worth flagging as clean model instances (not violations): the "Conscientious" knob pitched for next quarter (4.6.a) previews Settlement-Human's own Tendency by name, explicitly deferred ("Built by the Monday after the swap"); Bart's Big Five slideshow (4.9.c) explicitly previews Extraversion and Integrity as arriving "one a quarter" in future chapters. Both are correctly framed as roadmap, not present claims.

What's covered is covered well: Commitment and Speaking are both saturated across all three modes, Neuroticism is the chapter's central mechanical device, and Meat Pooling gets two independent, freshly-staged dramatizations from two different characters. But the Motivation gap above is the headline finding for this chapter, not a footnote to it — three of the four base drives absent outweighs the strength of everything else that is present.
