# Prediction for Ch 13

The voice has spent 12 chapters building toward this moment. The work is done. The machine is finished.

## Prediction

Ch 13 will be **absence**, not presence. The installment will be **very short** — brief enough to register as a signature break from the logs' typical length. It will not contain a build log. The filing date will mark a gap (3-6 months forward), not the usual quarterly interval.

**Opening**: The voice will either:
- Not file a log at all, but speak in a different register (a note, a reflection, a conversation recalled)
- Or open with a line that signals the absence of work: "Nothing to file," or a date with no log number, or a statement about the bench remaining empty

**Duration**: 200-400 words max — roughly half the length of a typical log or shorter.

**Register**: The crisp, itemized clarity will fracture or soften. Not expansive — the voice doesn't become expressive — but less clipped. More silence. More breathing room.

**Concreteness**: The focus will shift from objects (knobs, boards, wires) to the absence of them, or to the person (the kid, the house) now in possession of what was built. Physical detail will thin. What was there will be gone.

**Interiority**: This voice has stayed exterior throughout — action, hardware, what the hand did. Ch 13 may be the first time it names a feeling, or it may name nothing at all. If a feeling appears, it will be unnamed at first, arriving as silence or a withheld sentence.

**Attention**: The voice will linger on whatever it does not say about what happens next. The kid has the unit. The bench is clear. What comes after the work is over — whether the voice returns, whether there is another knob to build, whether the hand stays idle — will be the thing it dwells on by refusing to dwell.

**Signature**: The voice will use fewer numbers, fewer technical terms. The sentences may stay short, but they will not be followed by explanation. "That's all" or "That was it" or simply a date and then nothing — a grammar of closure.

**Confidence: High**

The 12-chapter rhythm is exact. The voice has built toward this endpoint without wavering. The final log explicitly marks the completion and the cleared bench. The next move is either silence or a new register entirely — and silence (or its near cousin, a voice that speaks without building) is what the pattern predicts.
