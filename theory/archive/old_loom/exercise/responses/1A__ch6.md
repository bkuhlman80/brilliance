# Prediction for Chapter 7

## Duration & Expansion
Expect a build log of moderate length, likely in the 600-900 word range. Bart's logs have stabilized around substantive single-topic explanations with some methodical expansion on one or two mechanisms. Not verbose, but not clipped either. The pacing accommodates both the technical detail work and his reflective asides on implications.

## Opening
Will likely open with a date stamp and attribution line ("Bart, hardware. Filed [month/year], for the team.") establishing the institutional formality. Then a direct statement of what was accomplished or tested this quarter — not a summary, but a clean first sentence that names the thing.

## Register  
Clipped and plainly stated at the sentence level, but the overall register expands when Bart moves into explanation. He uses short declarations ("That's the knob." "That's my whole stake in it.") as anchors, then elaborates outward from them. Expects the reader to follow precision without handholding. No colloquialism—his diction is exact, slightly formal. This quarter's language should carry that same measured tone.

## Concreteness
High. Bart stays object-level: knobs, boards, wires, screens, movement. When he reaches for abstraction (nerves, thinking, self) he immediately pulls back to what can be measured or observed. Expect descriptions of something tangible the unit can now do, with physical specifics about where things sit or what has moved.

## Interiority  
Sparse. Bart names his own concerns and uncertainties explicitly ("I'm not betting a nickel," "I've been bracing for"), but he does not narrate inner states of the unit or the team. The unit's interior states remain behavioral—what the board decides, what the gauge calls for, what the knob sets. No emotional inference; only action and mechanism.

## Attention & Silence
Bart dwells on the architectural problem (board space, slot allocation) and on what the unit can now *do* in measurable terms. He passes over the team's conversations lightly, usually just noting who said what. He is silent on: whether any of this matters, whether the design is beautiful or ugly, whether the team's relationship is strained or easy. He notices Burns's palm-flat gesture and records it, but doesn't interpret it. Silence is his refusal to say what something *means*.

## Signature Moves
- Opens with problem or mechanism, restates it as a specific fact ("The knob sets..."), then moves to implication  
- Uses short parallel sentences to establish baseline, then longer ones to explain variation ("Aerobic or anaerobic." "Warm-or-busy." Then the expansion.)
- Parenthetical asides for his own commentary ("Lisa's word," "I know for a fact, because...")
- Signs off with a forward-facing statement: what he'll watch this quarter, what the team should know, what remains unsolved  
- Never concludes with resolution; always leaves the knob still turning
