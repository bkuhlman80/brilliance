# Prediction for Ch 12

## Duration and Register
Moderate length, in line with recent chapters — approximately 500-700 words. Tone will remain flat and procedural, technical without affect. Diction stays concrete, verb-heavy. Syntax remains sparse and direct, with minimal subordination. No expansion into commentary or reflection.

## Opening and Entry
Opens with the standard framing: "Bart, hardware. Filed [month], for the team." Matter-of-fact. Likely announces carrying something back or forth to the house, following the established rhythm. The opening gesture will be purely functional — moving the body, setting it down, noting what happened next. No preamble.

## What It Builds or Adjusts
The pattern suggests movement back to hardware rather than knobs: five of the last six chapters (Ch 5-9) introduced substantial physical changes (legs, jaw, new body, furnace, voice). Ch 11 broke that pattern with a knob-only chapter. This chapter is likely to resume building new capability, not refine existing ones with a dial.

The missing direction: Ch 1-2 built **outward mobility** (hops → hunting). Ch 3-4 built **wheels and reflex touch**. Ch 5-7 built **legs, pointing, internal heat**. Ch 8-9 built **reading people and speaking back**. What hasn't yet been built: **directional output** — the thing can read what people are doing, can speak one word at a time, but shows no sustained capability to act *on* that reading in a way that responds to *what the person is doing* rather than just their presence. 

Most likely: a new manipulation subsystem — new hands, refined hands, or a second mode of interaction the current soft hands cannot execute. Or: a coordination system that lets it track and respond to person-specific action across time (not just moment-to-moment).

Possible but less likely: internal system refinement (cooling, storage, power management) following the furnace pattern.

## Interiority and Silence
Continues the absolute absence of Bart's inner state. No reflection on his design choices, their meaning, their cost to himself. No naming of fatigue, satisfaction, or doubt, though Ch 9 allowed him to state purpose directly ("I've wanted this one since the fall"). Likely returns to pure technical exposition with minimal first-person claim.

Will **not** discuss what the body should do with its new capability or what the house will want from it. That stays Lisa's department. Bart states what he built, how it works, what it costs. Nothing more.

## Attention and Pacing
Lingers on the mechanical novelty and the clever constraint that makes it necessary — follows the established pattern of explaining problem, solution, and the tradeoff baked into the solution. Does not linger on assembly difficulty (Ch 9 was explicit about taking longer; Ch 12 will likely not repeat that).

Passes over: any emotional reaction from the household, any judgment of whether the new thing improves anything, any speculation about what Lisa will do with it.

## Concrete Signatures
Will include detailed description of moving parts, material choices, and failure modes. Likely names specific components (servos, pneumatics, switches, materials). Continues to avoid technical jargon that assumes the reader knows it — explains what a vortex tube does, what a relay is, etc.

Will likely include at least one phrase of the form "the part I'm proud of" or similar direct claim. Will likely include a price: what it costs in power, time, or material.

## The Screen and Speech
If the new capability involves new output (display, sound, gesture), Bart will describe the hardware he built for it and defer the software/meaning to Lisa. The pattern holds: he builds channels, she fills them.

## Ending Gesture
Will close with a note on how the change landed — did Mira notice, did Burns react, does it still work on Tuesday. Or a callback to an earlier limitation that's now solved. Will not narrate emotional weight. Will likely end with a flat statement of fact: "She'll work that out on her own time." or "It still can't [X], which is next quarter's fix" or a simple timestamp of how the build sits.

## Confidence and Basis
**Confidence: MEDIUM-HIGH**

The pattern of alternating between hardware builds and knob refinement is strong across Ch 5-11 (hardware, hardware, hardware, hardware, knob, hardware-adjacent-knob). The sequence of capabilities being added (mobility → pointing → heat → reading → speech) suggests the next target is **action on reading** or **refined manipulation**. 

The voice, register, opening, and closing formulas are nearly invariant. Bart's refusal to claim Lisa's work or name his own feelings is absolute.

What creates uncertainty: whether the new build is a major new body or a modification to the existing soft one. The extraverted knob in Ch 11 suggests the soft body is settled enough that tweaking how it uses the room comes before rebuilding it. That tips the balance toward a hardware addition rather than a new chassis — but the addition will be significant enough to warrant its own chapter.
