# Prediction for Ch 10

## Duration
Medium length. Not expansive—the pattern holds to 1500–2000 words. The entry will dwell on one central build or upgrade, taking time to explain a mechanical principle or the reasoning behind a design choice, then move through downstream effects or complications. Pacing consistent with prior entries: deliberate exposition followed by a brief reflection or note on what wasn't built.

## Register
Clipped and concrete, with occasional expansive phrases when describing something Bart is proud of. Sentences favor short clauses connected by conjunctions or dashes; technical language sits beside plain vernacular without affectation. Tone remains level—neither celebratory nor regretful, but attentive to consequence.

## Concreteness
Physical and object-level throughout. Any abstraction (like capability or limitation) is always grounded in mechanism: a missing part, a wire, a threshold. The voice does not philosophize; it describes what was built, how it moves, what it costs.

## Interiority
Sparse. Bart reports what things do and what happened; he names feelings only when they attach to a design decision ("I'm proud of," "I miss"). What he doesn't say—what he keeps back—matters as much as what appears. He will not narrate the household's emotional response to what he builds, though the chapter may hint at it.

## Attention
The voice lingers on:
- How a piece of the previous build was repurposed or carries forward
- The mechanism that makes something possible and what it costs
- Moments where he had to choose between two engineering paths and why he chose one
- A limitation that remains unfixed (the thing the next build will address)

It passes quickly over:
- Who suggested a change (credit goes to Lisa, Burns, Mira, but never explained)
- The household's daily use of what was built
- Anything that sounds like narrative or metaphor

## Silence
Bart will not name Mira's emotional state or intention. He will not describe Burns's reasons or Jasmine's reaction as anything but an observation (she won't notice, she'll bend down, she'll put her palm). He will not say whether the builds are succeeding at their purpose. He will not speculate about what comes next.

## Signature
- Opens with a single-sentence version of delivery ("Lisa's running it back / Carried it in")
- Uses present tense for mechanism, past tense for the build work
- Repeats a phrase across quarters to mark time or progression (the dock, the noise, feeding)
- Uses "this one" or "it" rather than a name when the machine has not yet earned one
- Ends with either a limitation acknowledged, a cost stated plainly, or a hand-off to Lisa
- Numbers and durations (six-degree gap, a few thousandths of a watt, three crude little arms) appear without apology

## Expected Pattern for Ch 10
The new chapter will open with a delivery sentence and a date in late 2032 or early 2033. It will address a capability that the soft body (Ch 9) is missing or that the household has begun to need. The build will involve either:
- Adding to the soft body a piece that allows it to do something it couldn't before
- Repairing or upgrading one of the older machines (the vacuum, the four-legged one) because the soft body's presence has changed how the household works
- Building a small rig or tool that one of the existing bodies needs

Bart will describe the mechanism clearly. He will note what it costs—in charge, in noise, in the complexity it adds. He may report a side effect (water pooling, a noise returning, a slowness in winter). He will hand off to Lisa what belongs to her side. He will name one thing that still remains to be built, either explicitly or by showing what the body still cannot do.

The chapter will not be long. The voice will stay level. There will be no judgment of whether the work succeeded, only the facts of what it does and what it takes.

## Confidence
**Medium-High.** The pattern has held stable across eight quarters. Bart's voice, the structural components of each entry, and the progression of builds (each one adding capability, each one revealing a new dependency) are consistent enough to predict the form with confidence. The substance remains harder—which of the three machines gets the upgrade, whether the soft body's limitations are addressed or left to generate next quarter's work. But the manner of the telling is predictable. Bart will show; he will not judge; he will move on.
