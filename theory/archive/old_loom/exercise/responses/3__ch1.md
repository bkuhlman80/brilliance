# Prediction for Ch 2

## Duration & Structure
Expect a chapter of moderate length—roughly the same span as Ch 1. The voice tends to layer small scenes into a sequence rather than build a single sustained arc. This chapter will likely move through 3-5 distinct moments or stretches, each dwelling on observation before shifting.

## Opening
Will not begin with explicit time marker or external event. Instead: a sensory detail or a quiet action already in progress—touching, carrying, watching. Possibly something Mira does with or to Bramble. The opening will be oblique rather than declarative, letting us discover what's happening through her movement.

## Register & Tone
The register remains steady: flat, unadorned, colloquial but precise. No decoration. Phrases are short and built on verbs of simple action (sat, set, carried, wound). The voice does not ornament or editorializing; it reports. Expect minimal emotional vocabulary—what feeling appears will be behavioral or structural, not named as an interior state.

## Concreteness
High. The chapter will ground itself in objects and their properties: temperature, weight, sound, position. Physical specificity is the signature of this voice. Expect textures, locations, the way things move or don't move. Abstract ideas (if they appear) arrive via concrete observation, not assertion.

## Interiority
The voice's interiority is the puzzle. Mira's inner life is *inferred*, not stated. We see what she does—her patience, her logic, her attachments—but she does not explain herself. The next chapter will likely continue this silence. If a feeling emerges, it will be through action or behavior, not through the line "she felt" or "she knew." Expect decisions without reasoning; choices without apology.

## What It Lingers On
The voice lingers on waiting, watching, and the gap between expectation and fact. It passes quickly over what is obvious (the show-off wins; the broken one fails) and dwells on what requires attention (why Bramble is better; what the difference is between asleep and stopped). Expect the next chapter to linger on something small that matters to her and not to anyone else.

## Silence
The voice does not name why she loves Bramble—only shows it. It does not explain what Bramble is, exactly (a toy? a device? something else?). It does not ask big questions or reflect on them. It does not say what will happen next. Expect this chapter to maintain that silence: to present what is without speculation or dread about what it might become.

## Signature Tics
- Short declarative sentences: subject, verb, object. Little subordination.
- Repetition as rhythm: "once-and-wait, once-and-wait"; "didn't like... didn't like... didn't like."
- Pauses mid-thought, then continuation: "She can watch a thing a long time. She watched it a long while."
- The word "thing" repeated; the word "quiet" used for states, objects, behavior.
- Present tense mixed with past; time collapses or moves in her own clock.
- A near-absolute avoidance of why; constant presence of what and how.

## Likely Content Trajectory
The next chapter will probably deepen the Bramble relationship or shift it—introduce a new pressure on it, a challenge to her logic, or an expansion of how she uses or understands it. OR it will zoom out slightly to show how the five bots matter to her daily life. OR it will introduce a moment when her private understanding of Bramble collides with how others see it. Whichever: it will be shown, not explained.

**Medium confidence** on interiority and what lingers. The voice is consistent but Chapter 2 might shift focus or introduce a new relationship.
