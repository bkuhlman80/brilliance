Highly concrete and object-level. The thread has consistently avoided abstraction; this chapter will maintain that commitment. If an emotion appears, it will arrive pinned to a physical fact—a hand, a floor, a face on a screen. Expect very few feeling-words used alone. The next chapter will likely continue this shift toward *silence about the inside*, toward behavior and observation without the girl's interpretation or narrative authority. What she thinks will be shown increasingly through what she does, or through what she *doesn't* do—through omission rather than declaration.

## Attention
The girl's attention has been split all year between two things: Bramble, and herself (her own work, her own drawing, her own rule-making). By Ch 12's end, both are in motion but something is subtly shifting. The chapter will likely show her attention *turning away* from the bot in small, significant ways—not abandonment, but a kind of reorientation. She will linger on external things: the street, the growing change outside (the city's February arrival, hinted at throughout), the boy two doors down, the life she is entering.

The silence will not be angry or withdrawn; it will be something more like witnessing, or acceptance. Expect fewer of her whispered asides, her running commentary on his actions.
