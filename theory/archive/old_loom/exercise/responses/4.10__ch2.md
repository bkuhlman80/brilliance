# Prediction for Ch 3

## Duration
Approximately 400–550 words. Slightly more economical than Ch 1, roughly equal to or slightly shorter than Ch 2. The progression shows increasing compression without losing the ritualistic unhurried quality.

## Register
Clipped in narration, expansive in Burns's prepared remarks. The tone will remain measured and ritualistic. Register may shift fractionally toward the personal or domestic compared to the scientific language of Ch 1 and Ch 2 — less protocell or rock, more something closer to home.

## Concreteness
Will open or pivot around a concrete object or memory of an object — something Burns reaches for or holds. Less technical than the previous two; possibly something domestic, worn, or personal. The chapter will maintain the pattern of moving from object to abstraction (from the thing to its application in the room). Physical gesture will be foregrounded as a vector for emotion rather than named emotion itself.

## Interiority
Inner state will continue to surface as context or reason, not as direct statement: "He was reaching for X because X meant Y" rather than "He felt Y." A biographical detail will surface briefly — perhaps about the marriage, the son, another estrangement, or a sacrifice — but will not linger; it will appear parenthetically, underneath the cyanobacteria, the way the first wife and the grown son do in Ch 2. Mira's inner state will emerge through her action — her trying the word or phrase alone at the end, in her own voice, testing its shape.

## Attention
Will linger on: the object itself and its particulars; the room's attention and response; the moment when the word is tried or the ritual lands. Will pass over fast: the biographical weight beneath the gesture; the actual stakes Burns carries; any doubt about whether this persists or coordinates.

## Silence
What remains unspoken: Burns's actual fear or grief. What is not said but is there underneath: the cost of what he is asking the room to believe. The pattern predicts that something true about Burns's situation — something he needs to believe or needs the room to believe — will be carried by the gesture and the object rather than by explicit naming.

## Signature
The repeated turn from external reference to the room ("Then the turn..."). The half-inch-too-far gesture, or a variation of it — the moment when he reaches past what language can carry. A new mantra structure, "May we ___," which will likely be a deepening or complication of the first two. The combination of short, direct sentences with longer, reason-bearing clauses. The pattern of Burns laughing hardest at something that names his own vulnerability sideways (as Bart's Smithers impression did in Ch 2).

## Prediction

The chapter will likely open with Burns holding or reaching for an object — something smaller, more personal, or more worn than the protocell or the rock. He will give a prepared remark or memory attached to it, brief enough that the real work happens in the application. There will be the signature lean-in, the half-inch-too-far moment. 

The new mantra will shift slightly from function toward something more vulnerable: less about what the system needs to do (persist, coordinate) and more about what the room, or Burns himself, needs to survive or to hold. The chapter will close with Mira trying the word or phrase alone, in her own voice, the way she does at the end of both previous chapters.

The tone will be slightly tighter and possibly slightly more fragile than Ch 2, with the ritual holding but the cost becoming more visible. There will be one moment of levity or recognition that breaks the ritualistic surface and lets the room breathe.

**Confidence: High.** The structure has now repeated twice with enough specificity (the lean-in gesture, the turn, Mira's private testing of the word, the pattern of burial and sideways naming) that a third iteration is predictable. The voice has established not just a rhythm but a way of working through Burns's stakes. The third chapter is likely to deepen that pattern, not abandon it.

**Basis:** The repeated structure across Ch 1 and Ch 2; the signature gestures (the lean-in, the turn, the half-inch); the consistent pattern of inner state surfacing as context or buried alongside other things; the way Mira functions as the reader of Burns's vulnerability; the ritualistic quality of the meetings that suggests these closings are a form that Burns and the room return to.
