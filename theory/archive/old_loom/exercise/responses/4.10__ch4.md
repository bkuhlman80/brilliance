Confidence: HIGH. Register shift is the signature move; it repeats identically across all four chapters.

Will introduce a new biological or geological fact—something living, old, or strange. The pattern predicts something smaller or more common than previous items (protocell → rock → mitochondrion → jellyfish). Expect an organism or process that embodies a quality absent from the prior three: not persistence, coordination, or interiority, but something that names a fourth dimension of the lab's work or life. Candidates from the pattern's logic: multiplicity, memory, or change. Will likely invoke sleep, rhythm, seasonal motion, or reproduction.

Confidence: MED-HIGH. The biological anchors are consistent; the fourth theme is less certain.

## The Turn
The turn (from thing to room) will occur in the expected place—after the image settles—and will land on a different family. The pattern predicts a direct address to someone at the table, but not Mira this time. Previous chapters have addressed the team's collective survival, work ethic, and internal life; this chapter may name growth, generational shift, or the lab's future beyond the current four quarters.

Confidence: MED. The turn always happens; its target varies.

## The Verb Problem
Burns will hunt for a word that won't conjugate—this is now signature. He will stall, others will wait, he will find it a half-inch too far. The new verb will be in the same family as persist/coordinate/hold/quicken: a word that bridges action and state, doing and being. Likely candidates: *grow, shift, yield, inherit, carry, hand forward*. The verb will surprise him as much as it surprises the room.

Confidence: HIGH. This is the structural spine; it repeats.
