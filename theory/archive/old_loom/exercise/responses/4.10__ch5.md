# Ch 6 Prediction

## Opening
The chapter opens with "Burns took the close" or a near-identical formula ("Burns stood up" + action with beer/glass), naming Lisa's agenda slot one more time or letting it pass without remark. The setup is under 30 words. The verb is simple: took, stood, set down. The physical world is exact.

## Duration
Approximately 550-700 words. The pattern has settled into a zone narrower than Ch 3's amplitude. Not a shortest chapter (those were Ch 2 at ~450), not at the edge (Ch 3 at ~750). Sits in the middle-to-comfortable range where the apparatus can unfold without rushing or lingering past appetite.

## The Creature & Its Story
A new organism. This is the highest-confidence element across the thread. Ch 1: protocell. Ch 2: cyanobacterial mat. Ch 3: endosymbiotic cell. Ch 4: jellyfish. Ch 5: Cambrian larva. The pattern of descent into evolutionary time (4B years, 3.5B, 2B, recent, 500M) does not continue monotonically. Ch 6 may return upward or hold at a recent scale. The creature is chosen because it answers something the narrator wants the room to feel about the work, the team, or the condition of having to go forward. The story is not long; it takes 100-150 words maximum. It contains one arresting fact: no brain, simultaneous decision, a boundary crossed, a moment of venture. The tone is neither celebratory nor mournful—precise, almost archaeological.

## The Turn
Explicit. From the creature's behavior or condition to the room's condition, the team's situation, or the child's quiet watching. The half-inch too far over the table is likely present, though it could be abbreviated or replaced by another inch of leaning—into belief, into the request. Eyes leave the room and come back. The sentence that makes the turn is the hinge. It states a parallel without softening it.

## The Verb Hunt
Burns reaches for the word he needs and it will not sit in the place the sentence made for it. He stops. He says something like "you can't—" or "there's a word in here." The hunting is not rushed. The room lets him hunt. This is the ritual moment, the one Marge and Bart know now: the Best Part. The verb he finds has not been used before in the five previous closings. It is not a simple synonym for persist, coordinate, hold, quicken, or seize. It extends the architecture sideways or down. It contains difficulty. It is the size of what the team is doing. It is a half-step too grand and also exactly right.

## The Toast & The Response
He raises his glass. "May we [VERB]." The team repeats it—Lisa first or early, Marge, Jasmine in their usual order. Bart delivers the Mr. Burns voice version, the plutocrat over the father, pitch-perfect. Burns laughs the chest-laugh: early, loud, because the impression landed and it is eight o'clock and the night has been a good one.

Mira goes last. She has been quiet through the hunt. She may be writing something, or watching something off-frame, or simply in the private work of considering. When she speaks, she uses the word—not the full toast necessarily, maybe just the verb, said in her own voice, the word weighed the way she weighs a color before the page takes it. No explanation. No performance. The word kept.

## What Is Likely Silent
The full weight of what Burns is asking the team to become will not be named. The shape of it lives in the creature and the metaphor and the turn. The son is not mentioned (the pattern holds: brief appearances, submerged). The first wife is not returned to. Whatever the team has lost or is losing to get here stays below the surface, visible in Burns's leaning and his need for the word to be the right word.

## Register & Signature
The language is a mixture: the scientific cool of the creature (its genus, its behavior, the time it dates from) and then the heat of the turn—the word-choice becoming visible, the half-sentence shape of need. Short sentences during the hunt. Longer sentences when the verb is found and held. Parenthetical remarks from the narrator about what Burns is or isn't thinking, or about the room's knowledge. The phrase "a half-inch" or an equivalent measure of leaning. The word "the" used as a article of faith, not possession: not his glass, but *the* beer; not their team, but *the* room. The body of the son, if present, is mentioned with the same exact distance Burns uses when he looked at it in Ch 5—once, and then back to the room.

## Concreteness & Interiority
Details are held at the physical level: the glass, the hands, the gap in Mira's mouth (if she still has it), the tape running or not running. Inner states are embedded in action or in the narrator's aside. Burns does not say what he is afraid of or why the word matters so much. The room does not discuss why they repeat the toasts. Mira does not explain the weighing. The interiority is there in the silence—in what takes time, what takes a beat, what takes a half-inch of leaning to mean.
