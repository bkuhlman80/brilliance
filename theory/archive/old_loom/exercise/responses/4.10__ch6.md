# Unit: 4.10__ch6  (category 4.10 · chapter 6 · predicts Ch 7)

# Prediction: Ch 7

**Confidence: Med-High**  
**Basis: Structural pattern across chapters, attention topology, signature silence, and the trajectory of Burns' intoxication**

---

## Duration & Pacing
The installment will run roughly the length of Ch 5 or Ch 6 (substantially longer than Ch 1-2, shorter than Ch 3). Burns will dwell longest on the image or animal, moderate dwelling on the moral turn, then compress the toast and aftermath. The speech will feel less built-up than Ch 5 and less unguarded than Ch 6 — somewhere between the two in sobriety.

## Register & Diction
The voice will remain expansive, hands-moving, eyes-leaving-and-returning. But the *cadence* will tighten fractionally. Ch 6 broke syntax ("That perfect makes practice") as a marker of intoxication; Ch 7 will maintain grammar while the *density* of the thought will shift. Less philosophical architecture, more immediate sensation. Diction will stay concrete and grounded — no abstraction unless it's embedded in a creature or a scene. The trademark half-inch-too-far reach will appear, but the landing will be smaller in scope than previous chapters.

## Concreteness & Imagery
An animal or organism will appear — the pattern is unbroken. It will be smaller-scale than the jellyfish or Cambrian larva. The image will be *behavioral* rather than evolutionary — something about what the thing *does* repeatedly, or what it fails to do. The concrete level will hold throughout; no summary or paraphrase. The hand-movement and body-language notes will continue as the mode of describing his delivery.

## Interiority
The first *explicit naming* of a feeling in Burns' own interior (not displaced into an animal or premise) will appear — probably something like weariness, or the weight of the previous toasts, or the recognition of repetition itself. This will be *brief* and not lingered on. It will surface in the body before it surfaces in language. It will be undercut by someone else (likely Bart) before it can deepen.

## Attention & Silence
Burns will *not* look at Mira this chapter (or will look away from her more deliberately). The thing on the floor will go unremarked or remarked in a different register — less belief, more fact. The transcript will note that one person in the room *wanted* to speak but didn't. The toast will be the one that breaks from the formula — either in phrasing or in what happens after it. The goodbye sequence (if one comes) will be more uncertain, less ceremonial.

## Signature Marks
- Partial sentence hunts ("...the word he couldn't..."; "...there was a verb but") will appear, but resolved faster than in previous chapters
- A single precise name of a process or mechanism will arrive mid-sentence and reset the trajectory
- The hand-language will read as tireder: "hands open at" constructions may give way to "hands fell" or "hands stayed"
- One compound negative: "could not [verb] and could not [verb]" — a doubled refusal or double bind
- The Mr. Burns/Smithers callback will either not happen, or will misfire (Bart attempts it; Burns doesn't laugh the chest-laugh)
- The toast itself will be *easier* to say than in previous chapters — less hunting, less labor — which will make it feel thinner

## Predicted Shape
- Opens with the ritual of taking the close, but Lisa's objection (or acceptance) will feel different — weariness on one side or the other
- A smaller creature appears (insect, small fish, a single-celled thing with a history); Burns describes one specific, repeated behavior
- The moral turn arrives faster than expected
- The verb-hunt either vanishes or compresses to a handful of seconds
- The toast comes out clean and quick — *too* clean, in a way that registers as a loss rather than a landing
- Aftermath is brief: no Mira moment, or a Mira moment that's different in quality (she says the word but doesn't weigh it; she's already asleep; she corrects him)
- The red light stays on, but the room is quieter when it stops

## Register Shift Signature
The breath-pattern will change. Previous chapters breathe between the animal and the moral; Ch 7 will breathe *within* the animal description, as if Burns is uncertain whether the story he's telling is the story he meant to tell. This hesitation will *not* be marked with explicit doubt; it will show in syntax — a clause that starts and restarts, a simile that cancels itself.

---

**What it declines to say:** Burns will not name fatigue. He will not revisit any previous animal or building block. He will not say anything about whether the project (the lab, the thing on the floor, the family gathered) is working. One person will leave the table or stay at the table in a way that clarifies nothing.
