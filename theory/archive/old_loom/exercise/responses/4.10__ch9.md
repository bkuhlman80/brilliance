# Chapter 10 Prediction

## Duration & Pacing
The chapter will be approximately **medium length** — shorter than Ch 9 (which is the longest and most introspective), approximately the length of Ch 8. It will dwell longest on the **moment of the turn/lean itself** — the physical gesture of reaching past what the sentence can hold, which has become Burns' signature move and the place where the narrator lingers most intensely. The ending will move quickly once the toast lands, following the established rhythm of the final beats.

## Register
**Deeply colored and expansive**, especially at the moment of reach. The language will be warm and textured — not the austere economy of Ch 1-2, but the densely layered, almost incantatory register of Ch 8-9. Unlike Ch 6's tipsy looseness, this will be sober and precise in its expansion. The tone will be reflective but not yet elegiac; there is stillness in it rather than movement.

## Concreteness
High level of physical detail. The narrator will ground the abstract concept in **specific bodies and spatial relationships** — what the table looks like, what the room contains, what the machine does in the literal present tense of the meeting. The abstraction (whatever concept Burns reaches for) will be embedded in or generated from concrete observation, not stated first and then illustrated.

## The Argument/Concept
Burns will address **something to do with time, recursion, or inheritance** — the way the thing they built now teaches them what they built it to teach. OR: the way the present moment contains all the previous moments (persistence, coordination, interiority, quickening, seizing, practicing, going, keeping). He will reach past argument into something about **witnessing** or **being witnessed** — there's an untouched seam there. The concept will likely not have a clean verb form (following the pattern of interiority→hold, autonomy→go, affiliation→keep), forcing him to hunt once more.

## The Verb Hunt
Burns will **struggle visibly to conjugate or name** the concept. He will try a wrong word, hear its wrongness immediately, and reach for something simpler and more direct. The final verb will be **mundane and plain** — not grand like "seize" or "quicken," but ordinary enough that it lands like a stone in still water. Possibilities: *watch, see, tend, mind, witness, mark, know, show.*

## Interiority & Silence
The chapter will name something about **looking/being seen** — Burns' eyes will move around the table in a particular pattern that the narrator will track. There will be a moment where Burns does NOT look at someone (or does look, and the narrator notes it as a choice). What the chapter declines to say directly: whether the bare body on the floor is truly autonomous, or whether it is still fundamentally dependent (this remains unresolved and the chapter will skirt it).

Lisa will cut the tape after a **meaningful silence** (not immediately, but held for a breath that contains something). The last image will NOT be of the machine; it will be of **the people around the table in relation to one another** — their distances, their agreements, their separatenesses all visible in one frame.

## Confidence & Basis
**Medium confidence (65%)**

The pattern is clear and repetitive: Burns reaches for something unsayable at the edge of his argument; the concept emerges from the lab's work or from observation of Mira or the machine; the verb doesn't conjugate; he finds a simpler word; they drink it; Mira speaks. This is Burns' liturgy. What's less certain: whether this is the chapter where the pattern **breaks** or **deepens**, and whether the concept will be about the **future** (something new they're reaching toward) or the **past** (something they're recognizing in what they've already done). The structure predicts the former, but the emotional weight suggests the latter.

The basis: nine chapters of nearly identical ritual structure, with concepts building in a logical sequence (from passive survival to active choice to active care to implicit belonging), and a metadata hint that there are "three still to come" — suggesting this is not the final chapter, leaving room for rupture.
