# Prediction for Ch 10

## Duration
Moderate length, dwelling longest on the shape itself and its mechanics. Shorter on reaction. The voice does not linger on feeling but holds time on system. Expect 800-1200 words — longer than the worm-band movement took to deliver, because a second shape requires its own parallel structure of builds and rungs.

## Register
Clipped, functional, technically precise. The register stays flat through the shape's exposition, colored only by the cartoons and their economies. Diction remains fastened to action and mechanism: *acts, moves, holds, takes, reaches.* No elaboration. The voice does not inflate.

## Concreteness
Physical and observational — a new animal or scene on each slide, seen at the level of what it does and how it's built for doing it. The shape lives in its behavioral signature, not its interior. The cartoons ground it. Expect another rung-by-rung walk from simple to complex, each rung holding a body that does something.

## Interiority
Minimal, possibly absent. The first shape (Reactor) got none — the primate on the slide *doesn't run the picture of how the other kid's going to take it*, which is a negation, not a naming. Interiority is described as switched-off, as mechanical absence, not as felt or known. The voice does not locate the inner state of the shape. Expect the same: shapes are known by what they don't do inward, not by what they feel.

## Attention
The attention splits between the mechanics of the shape and the people watching it turn through its rungs. In Ch 9, Marge watches the four people who will see themselves; the narrator watches Bart run his own shape fastest and cleanest. The next chapter will likely balance the shape's exposition with side-glances at how it lands on the listeners — not dwelling there, but registering the landing. The shape itself gets the main beam.

## Silence
What Bart will not say about the shapes beyond their recursion. He named the first one and promised three more, one a quarter, one running the whole tree — but he declines to name what each quarter holds, or what the tree is for, or what the final shape resolves. The silence is structural, not emotional. Expect the same avoidance in Ch 10: the voice will show the shape moving through its rungs but will not step back to say what it means in the larger frame. It will decline the explanation.

## Signature
"Worm to X" — the voice builds its prediction on increments, a rung to a clade, a slide showing the same cartoon on a new body. The syntax is coordinated: subject, verb, object, then a note on what the verb excludes: *doesn't poll, doesn't ask, doesn't wonder, doesn't need.* These are negations that define. The voice uses rhythm to hold attention in exposition — *Fast, pointing. Brilliant at it. Best thing on the mountain.* Short and sure. Parenthetical notes that set the pace: *(that's not the joke — that's the deal).* The narration waits for Bart to pause, then comments from outside, never interrupting the show itself.
