# Prediction for Ch 3

## Duration & Pace
Medium length, comparable to Ch 2. Will dwell at length on one moment — likely a specific interaction or object-inspection, following the pattern of dwelling on Mira's examination of the bramble (twice now: first the drink, then the bot, the duplicate naming). The retrospective frame will add framing sentences but will spend measurable time inside a single scene.

## Register
Maintains the same flat-to-slightly-colored register: factual reporting of action and speech, with occasional color notes (the August light, the beer-and-kettle evening decision). Will not become expansive or lyrical. Diction remains precise, direct, deliberate — minimal abstraction. Will include a detail about sight or touch that reads as mundane but carries weight.

## Interiority
Inner states will remain mostly unnarrated. What people feel will be shown through: hesitation, silence, where hands go, what they carry or set down. The exception is the narrative voice in retrospective framing, which may briefly name intention or significance (as it did with "he had a thing to carry into the room that he had not had in the spring"). Mira's interior state will not be explained; she will simply do things or look at things.

## Concreteness
Highly concrete and physical. The scene will be anchored in objects (the bot, something Marge makes or sets down, possibly Burns carrying something into the room, possibly the camcorder in Lisa's hands). Will include specific sensory detail about temperature, light, weight, or texture. Abstraction will appear only when a character speaks it aloud or when the retrospective voice frames significance.

## Attention
Will follow the established pattern of lingering on: small transactions between people (handoffs, greetings, how a child moves through space), acts of noticing (who watches whom), and objects as they move from hand to hand or are named. Will pass quickly over: dialogue that is purely procedural, explanation of technical matters, anything that requires abstraction. Silence matters: what goes unsaid, what someone notices but does not voice.

## Signature
- Opening will be a time marker and weather/light description (the establishing pattern from both prior chapters)
- A named task or agenda item will structure the meeting
- Mira will do something that creates a small moment of recognition or significance in the room
- Speech is spare, direct; names are used as greetings and calls; people explain things to Mira using simple repeated language
- The retrospective voice interrupts the scene with framing (as it did in Ch 1 with the note about Cheryl reconstructing the bramble-naming)
- The camcorder is present and running; what gets recorded matters

## What It Opens On
The chapter will open with arrival — Jasmine and Mira coming into the warehouse, or Burns and the team already there waiting. The light will be described: the long August light, or a transition into evening. The description will be paced and attentive (not rushed).

## What It Will Do in the Middle
A moment of work or discussion will happen. Lisa, Burns, or Marge will address something that requires the team to attend. Bart's hands will be visible (he sets them down, moves them, or keeps them still — his hands mark his state). Mira will be present, and will notice or name or do something that creates a pause in the adult conversation.

## What It Will End On
One of these: (1) A decision made, followed by a brief image of the room settling into a new configuration (like the lamplight decision at the end of Ch 2); (2) A moment of attention shared between an adult and Mira, or between two adults watching Bramble; (3) The camcorder running, capturing something small. The final image will be physical and still — a table, a hand, a machine, the light, the absence of speech.

## What Will Not Appear
- Explanation of why the work matters or what the bot is for
- Internal monologue or named emotion
- Dialogue longer than a few exchanges
- Judgment language about the team or their dynamics
- A scene that breaks the warehouse as location

## Confidence
High, based on the consistency of structure, the repeated patterns of attention, and the signature of the voice. Medium-high on the specific content (which character drives the moment, which object centers), because the voice is consistent but the situation appears to be unfolding — we cannot know what Burns carries into the room or what will need to be addressed in this quarter.

## Basis
Both chapters open on arrival and light; both establish an agenda; both include an object (the bot, the drink) that gets examined or named; both include Mira noticing and marking something; both end on a moment of settled attention or silence; both use the retrospective frame to note what has been reconstructed or what was recorded; both treat speech as sparse and hands/movement as meaningful.
