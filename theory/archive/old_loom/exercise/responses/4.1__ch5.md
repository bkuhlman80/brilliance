# Prediction for Ch 6

## Duration
Moderate to long, dwelling in the territory Lisa opens — the meeting space itself, the texture of assembly, the small rituals that hold the team. Will not rush through the meeting mechanics, but will track more interior states than previous chapters. Likely 3,500–4,500 words.

## Register
Clipped and exact, as this voice maintains. Precise naming of objects and actions. Sparse emotional language; feelings tracked through behavior and arrangement, not declaration. The formality of the retrospective framing will be present but the narration will be the same measured register, neither elevated nor reduced.

## Concreteness
High. The voice tracks the bot's physical presence obsessively across every chapter. Ch 5 ends with the bot gone still on the floor. Ch 6 will almost certainly return to its motion, its state, what it does when the light comes on. The trellis/bramble metaphor introduced in Ch 2 and embodied in Ch 5 will likely be carried further through concrete acts — Jasmine's hands, the growth or stillness of what she planted, the parallel between what climbs a trellis and what the team builds.

## Interiority
Minimal direct naming. Burns's unannounced pacing in early chapters, Marge's "worried anyway" mouth, Bart's hands laid flat when his worry is acknowledged — the voice shows inner state through the body and what the body refuses to do. Any interior feeling in Ch 6 will arrive the same way: through pauses, what is held, what someone's hands are doing.

## Attention
The voice has tracked, with near-religious consistency:
- What Mira does, holds, names, points at
- The state of Bramble (warm, clicking, dome, screen)
- Who arranges the room and how
- The red light of the camcorder as threshold
- Objects that mean something: the drink, the tetrapod, the trellis, the wrapped shape under the drop-cloth

Ch 6 will linger on these categories. New attention will likely go to: the wrapped thing (revealed or discussed), Bramble's behavior in the quarter's work, and Jasmine's interior posture now that she has planted something with her own hands that is not the bot.

## Silence
The voice is consistent about what it refuses: plot summary, editorial judgment, psychological depth beyond behavior. It will not explain why Cheryl's note about Bart mattered, only that it changed how Burns listened. It will not evaluate the trellis as a metaphor; it will show it.

Silence will likely deepen around: Marge's unexplained standing-still at the bench in Ch 5, the nature of the wrapped shape's purpose, any interior experience of grief or failure (if such exists in the team's quarter), and Jasmine's emotional state regarding her work outside the lab.

## Signature
- Parallel construction ("The welcome they had agreed on," "the kind Lisa was built for," "the version of hello")
- Repetition of a word or phrase to track its becoming: "Bramble," "Strelley Pool gets two," "the trellis"
- Naming of absence ("It is one of the only things in the book about which there is no surviving footage")
- The camera as epistemic boundary: what is recorded vs. what Cheryl reconstructed, what the tape can hold
- Flat declaration followed by specificity: "She did not watch it go. She came in the thin grey zip-up and the Garmin, alone and entirely from the outside."
- Ownership and proximity: "her daughter," "her chair," "her house," "his dome"
- The conditional: "It would either take the whole fence or die inside a month, she couldn't tell which"

## The Prediction

Ch 6 will open on a specific threshold — likely Lisa arriving early again, or the camcorder being set up, or the moment before the light goes on. It will establish the quarter's time (probably August 2032 or November 2032, given the seasonal clock). The team will assemble in the configuration that has become automatic.

The chapter will turn on the wrapped thing revealed (or confirmed as revealed). This will not be described as triumph or problem — the voice has never used those words. It will be shown: the shape uncovered, the team's arrangement around it, what Bramble does in its presence, what Mira asks or does not ask.

Bramble's physical state will be tracked (warm, still, clicking, what its screen reads). The tetrapod will either appear again or be noticeably absent. Jasmine will either report on the bramble she planted or the conversation will turn to it obliquely, through Lisa's reading of her posture or Burns's refusal to ask her something.

The chapter will dwell on how the meeting moves — what Lisa allows in, what gets cut for time (the deep-time adjustment introduced in Ch 2), what Bart brings to the table, what Marge holds back. It may include a moment where one of them does something that changes the room's arrangement or tempo, and that will be shown through the room's response, not explained.

The chapter will likely end on a held moment — someone's hand on the bot, the light on the trellis, the red eye of the camcorder coming to life, or the room assembled and waiting for Lisa to begin. It will not close the retrospective; it will show the threshold of it.

Silence: The chapter will almost certainly not explain what the wrapped thing is meant to do, what the quarter's success or failure has been in measurable terms, or what any team member thinks about their own work. It may include a moment of Marge's continued stillness or unexplained drifting, but will not account for it.

## Confidence: High
**Basis:** Every chapter has maintained the same structural frame (arrival → assembly → threshold of meeting), the same register (clipped, object-focused, behavior-based), the same attention (the child, the bot, the room's arrangement). The introduction of the trellis as a living parallel, the careful naming of what the camera does and doesn't capture, and the consistency of treating Jasmine as the person who knows "the house the thing actually lives in" all predict a continuation in this register. The wrapped shape suggests a revelation is coming, but the voice has shown it will not be delivered as celebration or crisis — simply as an object in the room, to be accounted for like the bramble, the tetrapod, or the kettle.
