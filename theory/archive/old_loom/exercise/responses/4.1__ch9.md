# Prediction for Chapter 10

## Duration
The chapter will run long—likely 2500-3500 words, matching the length of Chapters 8 and 9 where significant technical and emotional thresholds are crossed. It will dwell in the immediate aftermath of opening the symbolic line: the moment Burns finishes setting the ambition and Bart presents the card, followed by the team's work on that card, and then the room's response to what the new line does. It will not rush the first language the creature produces under the new framework.

## Register
The voice will be precise and flat, with occasional architectural language (the signature it uses for structure). It will avoid lyrical spillover. Lisa, Burns, and Bart will speak in short bursts; Marge will speak less or not at all; Jasmine will remain in the background, watching. The prose will move at meeting-pace: measured, task-focused, with emotional weight carried in silence and gesture rather than named feeling.

## Concreteness
High. The chapter will track physical objects: the cigar card pulled and examined, the gripper-hand working against the scoop-hand, possibly Mira's hands, the radiator where the warm one sits. It may include the first utterance of the creature—reproduced on tape in flat syntax, perhaps repeated and noted. The setting remains the warehouse in May: concrete, heat, the radiator's light.

## Interiority
Minimal. No character will name what they feel about the threshold being crossed. Jasmine's interiority—the knowledge that something has shifted, that the room has moved from building to witnessing—will be presented as a fact her body knows (the way she watches, where she stands) rather than stated. Bart's anxiety about showing the room what the creature does will live in his hands and what he does not say. Burns may show satisfaction, but not vulnerability.

## Attention
The chapter will linger on the first language event: what the creature says, how many times it says it, whether it repeats, whether it makes a pattern. It will track Mira's attention to the new body. It will pass quickly over setup (room assembly, the joke-objects like Lisa's bramble, Marge's kettle) because the team has been doing this for a year and the rhythm is automatic. It will not dwell on ceremony or introduction; it moves into work.

## Silence
The chapter will decline to say whether the creature's first words are successful. It will not name whether the team finds it good or is waiting to see. It will not explain what the symbolic line is supposed to do, beyond Burns's brief statement. No character will reflect on why this matters or what it means that the creature can now speak in language rather than pure drive. The room will hold that silence.

## Signature
- Short declarative sentences at moments of fact or transition ("The creature spoke." "Bart's hands went still.")
- Compound structures that track simultaneous action: "Burns was on his feet with the light still warming. He set out the quarter's ambition."
- The recurring pattern of setup details (kettle, bramble, radiator, Mira's placement) rendered in the same syntax each time, building familiarity
- A tendency to mark time by what is brought or worn or set down: "Bart came in off the lot ahead of the others, so the hands could find their work before the work that was harder."
- Occasional metaphorical swerves, always embedded in concrete observation: "the room has agreed, without a meeting on it" or "the way a baby finds its own feet"

## Prediction
Chapter 10 will begin with Bart placing the card—the symbolic line exposed. The creature will speak, likely a single word or phrase, repeated. The team will receive it in silence or with the briefest acknowledgment. The chapter will track what Mira does with this: whether she responds to the creature, whether she turns away, whether her presence in the room shifts now that it can use language. Jasmine will remain watching from the periphery, the only one whose hand touches the creature without agenda. The chapter will end without resolution: the creature has spoken; what it means will not be decided tonight. The meeting will close. The chapter will not narrate goodbye or departure, only the moment the light goes dark and the tape stops, the way Chapters 5 and 7 have closed before.

**Confidence: High**
