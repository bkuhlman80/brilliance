# Prediction for Ch 4

## Duration
The next chapter will run to approximately the length of Ch 3 (950–1100 words). It will dwell longest on whatever physical capacity is being introduced — the speaker will pause over mechanisms, will make one or two tangential observations that he then reins back in, will use repetition to circle a point rather than move linearly through it.

## Register
The register will remain clipped and matter-of-fact, with occasional eruptions into the cereal-commercial showman voice. The tone will be affectionate toward the audience without ever becoming warm; the speaker will remain slightly detached from his own enthusiasm. Where interior states appear, they will be brief and parenthetical — "he could not help himself" — not dwelt upon.

## Opening
The chapter will open with a card being held up: "Bart took the [X] card from the box" or "Bart held up the [X] card so the camera could see it." The front of the card will have a drawing and a title to be read aloud in the cereal-commercial voice. The title will be another evolutionary stage (Multicellular, Differentiation, Neural Net, or Vertebrate are likely candidates). The speaker will then name the stage and explain what it is using a sentence or two of plain statement.

## Signature moves
- Opening with a physical object (card) held up
- A title announced in a different register (cereal-commercial voice)
- Defining the stage by contrast with what came before
- Introducing a named capacity (four or five, named and elaborated)
- At least one extended physical demonstration or metaphor
- A brief interruption or comment from another person
- A sentence or two that names or hints at what happens next, usually phrased as a trajectory or a "breakthrough"
- Closing with the card set down and/or a pause after a named concept

## Concreteness
The chapter will maintain high concreteness. Mechanisms will be described at the level of physical action: how things move, how they attach, how they stack. Abstract language will be minimal; even concepts like "interiority" get fleshed out as actual rooms, actual tenants. But the speaker will occasionally acknowledge that these are metaphors borrowed from human experience — "near enough" for "two feet," "actual legs, actual walking, actual tracks."

## Interiority
Inner states will appear only as fleeting annotations: the speaker noting his own tone, his awareness of an audience reaction, his moment of looking past the wall. Mira and the other listeners will have more interiority on the page than Bart does. His emotional or cognitive responses will be sidelined in favor of what he is explaining.

## Attention
The chapter will linger on the four named capacities in turn, spending 50–150 words on each. It will pass quickly over definitions, slower over mechanisms and demonstrations. It will devote sustained attention to whichever capacity draws a physical demonstration or a tangential thought. It will move very quickly over objections or corrections from the audience, then move on.

## Silence
The chapter will not name doubt about the evolutionary sequence or the validity of the metaphors, except in the brief, resolved form it already has (Lisa raises the energy story; it's acknowledged and overruled by Burns with an easy confidence). The speaker will not reflect on why he is teaching this, or what it means to the listeners, or what happens if they don't understand. He will not name fatigue or boredom. He will not look back.

## What it does at the end
The chapter will end with a card being set down or picked up again, followed by a brief statement naming a concept or a trajectory. That statement may be incomplete (trailing off mid-thought, as "Phagocytosis is..." does at the end of Ch 3). The chapter will close without resolution of the idea it names — the idea will be suspended for later, or simply left hanging at the point where Bart has spoken it.

## Confidence: HIGH
This pattern (opening with card, naming stage, listing capacities, at least one demonstration, closing with concept suspended) is present in all three chapters with only minor variation. The speaker's voice — the ratio of concrete mechanism to abstract concept, the distance he maintains from his own teaching, the register shifts, the brief interruptions he tolerates or dismisses — is consistent and distinctive. The chapter-by-chapter progression (introducing a stage, elaborating its capacities, occasionally hinting at what comes next) follows a clear structural template that shows no sign of breaking.

Ch 2 repeats the structure with added demonstrations (Bart standing up to model run/tumble) and a more complex interruption pattern. Ch 3 adds Burns's voice as a second explainer but keeps the core template intact: card, title, contrast with prior stage, four capacities named and elaborated. The speaker's refusal to dwell on his own interiority, his focus on mechanism, his control of the register, and his tolerance for interruption are consistent across all three. The chapter appears to be part of a structured teaching sequence where each chapter introduces one clade and its capacities, likely building toward multicellularity next.
