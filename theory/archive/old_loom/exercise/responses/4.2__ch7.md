# Unit: 4.2__ch7  (category 4.2 · chapter 7 · predicts Ch 8)

# Prediction for Ch 8

## Opening & Register
Ch 8 will open with Lisa calling for the next clade card (continuing the pattern "Clade card, Lisa said"). Bart will have the card ready, suggesting advance preparation. He will read the title in the cereal-commercial voice—a new clade name, likely one syllable or two strong beats. The voice will shift immediately to plain exposition. No extended preamble; the pattern favors swift entry.

## Expected Length & Pacing
Moderate-to-long chapter. The material will dwells on mechanism (how the brain actually works, not why), with at least one concrete example run at length. Bart will set down and pick up the card once, possibly twice. He will pause before naming the new breakthrough—a moment where the room waits. The chapter will end with summary or handoff, not open-ended speculation.

## Content Direction
Given Ch 7's setup (world-model vs. self-model, the cortex structure, "what lets the rat leave the wall"), Ch 8 likely introduces the next evolutionary leap or deepens primate cognition. The breakthrough will be named plainly and will concern either:
- **Attention** as a directed faculty (not just the knob from Ch 7, but intention itself), OR  
- **Social modeling** (the ability to hold other minds in the self-model), OR  
- **Planning across time** (the self-model extended into future/past)

The four capacities will include at least one mechanism so precise it can be written on the card in pencil or ink.

## Interiority & Silence
Bart's interior state will remain mostly hidden. He may show pleasure ("delighted," "lit") in the fact itself, not the telling. He will not explain why this clade matters to him. He will decline to speculate about human behavior or consciousness beyond the bare mechanism. Feelings named on the page (if any) will be brief and controlled—a single phrase, not dwelling.

## Signature Moves
- Title read in cereal-commercial voice  
- At least one physical gesture or thought-experiment (may be scaled smaller than Ch 3's Frankenstein lurch—a hand movement, a glance, a shift in posture)
- One handoff to Burns or Lisa at a critical juncture  
- The word "That's" used flatly to close a section ("That's the four")  
- One moment of bracketing doubt or precision (e.g., "I'm choosing not to hear that," or a clarification from Marge/Lisa that Bart accepts without defending)  
- Card set face-down at the end, or held up once more to show the face

## Diction & Syntax
Short sentences for facts, longer ones for mechanisms. Repetition of key words (the breakthrough, the capacities). Few adjectives; those that appear are objective (fast, slow, high, low, old, new). No metaphor beyond what the biology itself provides. Dialogue brief and functional. When Bart speaks in "his own voice" (not cereal-commercial), it is steady and unhurried.

## Confidence: Medium-High (75%)
The pattern is consistent across seven chapters. Variability exists in which clade appears next and which breakthrough gets named, but the voice—the pacing, the handoff, the flatness, the refusal of editorializing—is locked in. The risk is misjudging which biological level comes next or whether the chapter deepens rather than expands into a new clade. The tone and structure should hold.

## Basis
- Consistent opening ("Clade card, Lisa said")
- Consistent card ritual (held up, title read, set down)
- Consistent breakthrough announcement and 4-capacity breakdown
- Consistent refusal to explain significance  
- Consistent use of concrete examples over abstraction
- Consistent handoff pattern (Lisa leads, Bart carries mechanism, Burns or others handle implications)
