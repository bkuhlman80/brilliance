Clipped, propulsive, and direct. Short sentences driving forward. Likely to include one of his characteristic self-interruptions (like "the ice doing something to the chemistry I will spare you" or catching himself spiraling and being stopped by the room). The team knows his dial and he knows they know it.

## Attention and What It Dwells On
Burns dwells on the *mechanism* of scaling—how something small replicates, amplifies, feeds back on itself. He lingers over the object that does work without being understood: the cup, the ledger, the thing that outlasts intention. He passes quickly over dates and controversies (hedging with "near enough," "give or take") but slows on causality and consequence.

Given Ch 11 ended on writing, writing-as-tool, and the flywheel of recording → taxing → scribing → recording, this chapter probably lingers on what happens when the flywheel accelerates—probably either the emergence of hierarchy (kings, priests, bureaucracy), or the first collision with limits (famine, war, soil failure). Either way: mechanism and cascade, not just events.

## Interiority
Minimal. Burns names almost no inner state. He describes what was done, what the physics required, what the structure incentivized—not what anyone felt or intended. The exception: brief moments of recognition when the pattern mirrors another pattern (the cell swallowing an engine; the two lineages arriving at the same answer). When he opens his eyes, it's usually to signal a shift in register or be pulled back by the room.

This chapter will likely maintain that stance: the city-dwellers *wanted* what the system incentivized, or they didn't, but that's not the point. The point is the structure itself.

## Silence and Refusal
Burns avoids moral judgment. He will not say "the city was exploitative" or "the collapse was tragic"—he states what happened and what the shape of it was. He refuses grand narrative simplicity; he hedges when the record is contested.

In this chapter, expect silence on: individual ambition or cruelty, metaphysical meaning, whether what happened was "progress." He may decline to name a feeling—his own or the ancient peoples'—that the shape of the argument tempts toward. There will be no judgment on the soil-poisoning or the resource depletion; he will state it as fact and let the listeners sit with it.
