# Prediction for Ch 3

## Duration & Structure
Ch 3 will run substantially longer than its allocated time frame (parallel to Strelley Pool's two minutes becoming twenty-one minutes). Expected length: 1,800–2,200 words. The chapter will open with a timed setup or constraint, which Burns will exceed. Dwelling: extended immersion in a specific deep-time interval or transformative event; the narrative will resist rushing through it.

## Register
Predominantly clipped, declarative sentences clustered around observations and mechanism. Limited color—the palette stays instrumental. Moments of expansiveness appear where Burns moves between scales (from mechanism to consequence, from local action to planetary effect). Syntax remains relatively flat through technical explanation, then unfolds slightly when reaching toward implication.

## Concreteness
High physical/object specificity. The voice pins itself to rock, bacteria, chemistry, observable remains. Abstract concepts (like "life," "evolution," "selection") arrive only after grounding in what can be held, pointed at, or reconstructed from chemistry. The register keeps sensation and feeling downstream of mechanism; little sensory colorization.

## Interiority
Minimal explicit naming of inner state. Burns's attention, fascination, and conviction show through *what he lingers on and how*, not through introspection. His amusement at himself appears only in action: the laugh, the hand left open without the word. The voice does not narrate feeling; it narrates discovery and lets feeling trail behind as shadow.

## Attention
Lingers on: the discontinuities between geological epochs (the threshold from chemistry to evolution; the shift from iron-dark to oxygen-rich planet); the scale-bridging leap (how one small cell repeated across time remakes a planet); the object-lesson echoes (Templo Mayor, stromatolites). Passes over quickly: speculation without fossils; the "why" of origins in favor of the mechanism and consequence.

## Silence
The voice does not say what these discoveries *mean* to Burns, only what they *are*. No reflection on how deep time changes perspective or humbles the human moment. No sentimentalizing of bacteria or the protocell. No acknowledgment of awe as feeling—awe appears only as the shape of the argument. Avoids teleology; does not frame bacterial oxygen-production as "leading to" animal life or humans, only as a fact with a consequence.

## Signature
- Cadence: short, load-bearing sentences followed by clarifying clauses. "The thing it brought to bear." "That is the clade."
- Repetition as structural tool: "four billion years," "the chemistry," "the thing," "one small thing." Repetition marks emphasis and creates rhythm rather than eloquence.
- Quoted phrases from others held at arm's length or turned over ("framework word," "land the plane").
- Parenthetical asides and qualifying clauses that complicate without undermining: "(or may not have happened)," "we don't know, and everyone has a favorite."
- The hand gesture as a linguistic placeholder: when words run out, the gesture holds the gap.
