# Prediction for Ch 4

## Duration
Approximately 18-22 minutes of delivered content, likely drifting past a stated time limit again. Burns will open with a clear scope or deadline, likely framed with Lisa, that he immediately or eventually exceeds by 2-5 minutes. The rhythm of "twenty minutes gets two" / "two minutes that become twenty-one" / "nineteen minutes when twenty was promised" shows this as a signature pattern now — not a failure but a structure the room expects and measures.

## Register
Clipped at the frame (the setup, the anchor point to the room) but expansive in the deep part. Will open with his eyes still closed or closing before speaking. Flat, businesslike statement of the topic and constraint, then shift into the moving-hands, embodied register where he makes the planet or the process visible in his gestures. Mix of technical language (genus-level) and colloquial anchor ("Oh my heck," "chest-laugh"). 

## Concreteness
High. He will use a real place, probably geologically grounded (Strelley Pool, Templo Mayor, banded iron). The next chapter is likely to anchor to a visible object or location and move outward from there. Hands will be in the air making shapes of time, process, or structure. Will avoid abstract naming ("evolution," "life") in favor of the small action that creates it ("bacteria pinning down sediment," "splitting water").

## Interiority
Virtually none. No narration of feeling, motivation, thought-state. Inner state emerges only through what Burns *does* — closes his eyes, reaches for a word and doesn't take it, laughs at his own circling, sighs when done. His wife reads him. The team reads him. Silence substitutes for interiority; the word he won't say because it's "a framework word" is more present than any introspection would be.

## Attention
Lingers on: the layering of time (stromatolites, rebuilt ground, banded iron); the small action repeated at scale (bacteria, five hundred years, a billion years); the byproduct that remakes the world (oxygen, rust, the green color). Passes quickly over: formal definitions (LUCA gets bracketed and explained, then abandoned); justification for method (his eyes closed — acknowledged, never defended); emotional stakes or meaning-making (the bacteria "did not mean the world they made. They made it anyway" is the only philosophical reach, and it's presented flat, without landing on what that means for the listener).

## Silence
Will not explain *why* deep time matters or what it does to a person to hold it. Will not defend his eyes being closed. Will not linger on mystery for its own sake — the "honest answer is we don't know" is said matter-of-factly, not with awe. Will not name beauty, sublimity, or terror, even when the content touches those edges. 

The next chapter will likely omit the internal debate or wonder that the *subject* (life, geology, deep time) might naturally provoke. Burns stays on the outside of the feeling, narrating only the shape of the thing and what it does.

## Signature
- "He closed his eyes" / "He opened his eyes" as punctuation marks, the rhythm of in/out, in/out
- One concrete comparison per chapter (alkaline vents → pH gradients; bacteria → Aztecs; sex and trading → new arrivals)

- Hand gestures described as the action itself ("the hands made the planet," "leaving his hand open in the air where the word should go")

He will anchor to a real location or object — possibly something smaller or more recent than previous chapters, or something he will hold in his hands, or something the team can see around them.
