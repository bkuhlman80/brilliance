## Register
The voice maintains its characteristic mix of technical precision and vernacular ease. The diction will remain concrete and grounded despite abstract subject matter. ## Concreteness
Burns roots every abstraction in physical, observable fact. He will reference a specific geological site, a particular animal, or a piece he has handled. The chapter likely opens with a place-name and a time scale, then moves into behavior or mechanism. Hands will gesture; eyes will close. Objects in the room (the warehouse itself, tools, a piece of vertebra or bone) will anchor the metaphysical.

## Interiority
Inner states are almost never named on this thread. No mention of feeling, doubt, desire, or fear. What Burns knows about his own obsession comes through behavior: the hands, the closed eyes, the insistence on detail, the delight in being caught mid-digression, the laugh. The listener—especially Jasmine—exists in the text chiefly as a signal to redirect; her knowledge or memory is confirmed but not explored. Consciousness is treated as mechanism, not sentiment.

He passes quickly over geological time-spans, names of authors, competing theories (Lane vs. Mulkidjanian). He speeds through the catastrophes (the snowball earth, the asteroid) and slows for the physiological or neurological innovations. Ch 7's long turn on the mammalian brain suggests the next chapter may follow a similar logic: a quick sweep of deep time, then sustained attention on one capability or transition.

## Silence

What the warehouse is actually for. Burns will not explore his own investment in these narratives. He also avoids speculation about consciousness, purpose, or teleology—the universe is not building toward anything, it is simply making complicated things. The next chapter likely continues this silence, withholding judgment or meaning-making from the mechanisms described.
