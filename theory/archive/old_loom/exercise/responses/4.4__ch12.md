# Prediction for Ch 13

## Voice Model Summary

Bart's voice across 12 chapters has developed a stable signature:

**Register & Diction:** Conversational and casual, with embedded technical/engineering vocabulary used without self-consciousness. Ideas build through negation ("No battery. Where's the chip."), parallel structures, and rhythm. Shifts between colloquial interjection and precision, often deflating emotional weight with understatement or humor.

**Duration & Pacing:** Long, unhurried sentences with embedded clauses. Dwells heavily on physical demonstrations and sensory detail. Passes over emotional declarations quickly, leaving them unspoken. The quarterly demos follow a stable rhythm: technical presentation, then the humanistic "point" that wasn't on the build sheet.

**Concreteness:** Heavily grounded in objects and physical action—bots, knobs, dials, warm spots, screens. Builds ideas through what things *do*, never through abstraction. Uses workshop language (drives, installs, turns) and shows rather than tells.

**Interiority:** Rarely names his own feelings. Shows interior state through what he doesn't say, what he looks at or away from, and small physical tells (hands going still, eyes avoiding Mira's). Uses Owen as a foil to externalize his own thinking about the work.

**Attention:** Lingers on unintended consequences (the water dribble, the bot's neglect of Mira mirrored in the Conscientious knob breaking her heart on a kitchen floor). Passes quickly over raw feeling but dwells on the mechanism that produced it. Returns obsessively to: the dumb disc/vacuum, Mira's neglect, Owen's judgment, the gap between what the screen displays and what's true.

**Silence:** Never directly states his guilt about Mira. Doesn't explain why the builds take their shape. Keeps Owen's responses ambiguous. Often the narrative voice (Lisa, Marge) fills what Bart leaves unspoken. He is silent about what he fears.

**Signature Moves:**
- Opens by introducing a voice/perspective (Owen in Ch 1, the clade block in later chapters, followed by "Bramble block. Bart.")
- Builds demo around one core innovation, demonstrated physically
- Uses the opening formula "Here's what got to him" or "Here's the part I didn't say"
- Explains through negation: "Didn't do X, only did Y"
- Ends on unresolved tension or an unexpected emergence the bot has begun to do
- Final lines land either flat/technical or as a phrase that carries the quarter's weight without saying it aloud

**Basis:** This register is now stable and is part of the read—the plainness is part of what makes the unspoken emotional content hit harder.

**Prediction: A new knob or capability related to the bot's ability to choose what to show, or a consequence of the identity/honesty knob work from Ch 12.** Likely candidates:

**Basis:** Ch 11 ended with the bot choosing the clown face—a moment where it gained the ability to choose its own display. Ch 13 will likely show it using that ability in a way that troubles the "honesty is always good" premise.
