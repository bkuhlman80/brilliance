Ch 6 will likely continue this — showing not telling, behavior not belief.

The chapters linger on what the bot *doesn't* have or *can't* do, then sit with why that matters. Quiet attention to the room's reactions — Marge's wordlessness, Burns's thumb-down, Lisa's ready hand. Ch 6 should attend to either another failure moment or someone's response to the one just witnessed.

The thread stays mute on the larger purpose — why build this way, what does it eventually become, does it suffer. Bart will not answer Big O's unstated disapproval; he just marks it *won't fix* and moves on. Ch 6 should preserve this silence — describe the problem, skip the defense.
