# Prediction for Ch 12

## Prediction Summary

**Duration:** Medium-length, 800-1200 words. The chapter will dwell heavily on one Mira exchange (the core Call), move through two shorter ones briskly, then expand into observational silence at the close.

**Register:** Flat declarative opening, warming slightly through the Mira testimony, then hardening back to flat at the end. No editorializing. The prose itself does the emotional work; Mira's voice carries the color.

**Concreteness:** High. Physical behavior and objects dominate. A knob setting, a room state, an action repeated. No abstraction; everything stays at the level of what happens and what is watched.

**Interiority:** Named only in Mira's voice, never Bart's internal state. Mira reports what she sees; the reader infers what the machine is doing from her testimony. One unnamed question may linger at the end.

**Attention:** The chapter lingers on whatever Mira tests—the one Call that matters most—and passes over the dare quickly unless the dare itself is what's being tested. The closing always pivots away from the immediate into the room's atmosphere or an unspoken tension.

**Silence:** Bart does not explain what a Call means. The room does not speak after Mira finishes unless Lisa needs to correct the record. Anything Mira leaves unsaid is sacred ground.

**Signature:** Repetition of the interrogation frame ("Yes or no, no speeches." "One — ... Two — ... Three —"). Short declarative sentences from Mira. Bart's voice: "Taking the yes," "Noted," "Last thing," "Dare holds." The log. The page. Anger buried under procedure. Tenderness buried under Mira's flatness.

**Opening:** Bart with the log face on, a quality-control setup. Likely three Calls, possibly on a new knob or a familiar one in a new quarter (spring is coming; these sessions seem seasonal). He will read them off.

**Middle:** Mira answers yes or no with one-sentence evidence. At least one answer carries a weight the table will feel. She will report something the machine did that Bart did not build or predict—a small kindness, a failure, an unexpected complexity.

**Closing:** Not Mira's voice, but the room's silence or the machine's unbidden action. Bart will not name what it means. The dare will stand or fall. Something will be kept.

## Confidence: Medium-High

**Basis:** The pattern holds across all eleven chapters. The frame is invariant: Bart opens, Mira testifies, the machine does something unscripted in the silence. The knobs change (Bramble, Explore, Coping, Cat-watching, Agreeable, Conscientious, Extraverted), but the shape—interrogation, evidence, the room learns something about the boundary between build and growth—never varies. The dares accumulate without being spent. Mira grows older in her answers; the machine grows toward something Bart cannot name.

The next chapter may ask her something that touches the machine's own emergence—the thing Bart called "the one I couldn't build" in Ch 9. If so, expect her to answer plainly, and the room to go still.
