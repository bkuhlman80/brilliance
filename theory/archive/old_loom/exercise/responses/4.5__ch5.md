# Prediction for Ch 6

## Duration
The chapter will likely run medium length — not the expansive, multi-turn dialogue of Ch 4 or 5, but substantial enough to contain at least one substantive observation. Expect 300-500 words. The pattern shows Bart pacing out reflection across page-readings, so a single focused sequence with 1-2 major turns.

## Register
Clipped and direct, with that familiar flattening of affect. Bart's syntax stays spare: simple declaratives, short questions. But expect one moment where the register deepens slightly — a beat where he names something unguarded, then yanks back. The opening will be matter-of-fact; the middle may soften; the close will lock down.

## Concreteness
Heavy on physical detail, as always. The voice dwells on what the body does — position, motion, time-bound action. The new unit (or refinement) will be described through its literal behavior: what it reaches for, where it sits, what happens when it fails. Abstract assessment will be avoided; judgment stays embedded in observed fact.

## Interiority
Minimal named inner state. Mira or Jasmine will do the emotional naming if it happens at all — Bart will read the evidence and let the reader infer. But expect one implicit admission: a line where what he *didn't* say becomes audible, the way it did in Ch 3 when he folded that page and kept it. The silence around something — what he's not taking credit for, what he's afraid to claim — will be the chapter's quiet center.

## Attention
Lingers on: specificity of measurement, what changed this quarter, what the body chose. Moves fast over: abstract stakes, emotional weight, his own intentions. Will sit longest on one unexpected detail — a behavior that contradicts what he'd filed, or a success he's reluctant to own. Passes over quickly: anything about the larger project's purpose.

## Silence
What stays off the tape: admission of luck, or of watching something he didn't design. The page-reading will probably contain a call he refuses to speak aloud, the way he nearly didn't say the thing about Mira's observation in Ch 3. One answer to his own question will arrive without words — just Mira or Jasmine naming it, Bart accepting it.

## Signature
- "Yes or no, no editorializing" (control of the frame)
- Pauses mid-sentence to recalculate ("A small recalculation crossed his face")
- Uses the table/room as witness ("to a table that was not betting against him")
- Sets objects down as though laying confession: beer on a page, a page face-up
- Reads from the log without flinching, but flinching *is* the flinch
- Ends with a clean tally ("Three for three")

## Prediction
Ch 6 will open with another page — another quarter's log. The question will be framed as simple quality control or measurement, but it will concern something that shifted in unpredictable ways: either the new unit's integration with the old ones (picking up on Mira's observation about the circle from Ch 3), or a behavioral refinement in one of the originals. The final call will be the one he didn't write; it will be Jasmine's or Mira's or Lisa's claim about what the unit did that he couldn't have scripted. He will acknowledge it without quite naming it — a gesture, a slight nod, or a line that folds irony back on itself. The chapter will end not with triumph but with precision: something measured, something that fits, something he can trust enough to stop checking.

The whole thing will close on a small fact, a number, or a repetition — the signature move. Clean. Sufficient. Left to stand.
