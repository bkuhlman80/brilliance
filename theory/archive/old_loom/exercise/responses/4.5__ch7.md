The feeling (if it surfaces) will be about surprise at being understood correctly by her. The last line will either be Bart's dry deflection ("That's the one") or a very small note of something he lets stand without hammering it—a pause instead of a quip. He might preserve something odd or tender on the tape instead of erasing it. Confidence: **medium-high** — the ritual form is locked in; the surprise will come in how Mira's answer reshapes what he thought he'd written.
