# Ch 10 Prediction

## Duration
Moderate to substantial—likely 1200-1800 words. The voice dwells longest on moments of measurement, assessment, or silence between parties. Moves briskly through logistics and setup, slower over exchanges that land or fail to land.

## Register
Clipped and evidentiary, increasingly so. Short declaratives that double as verdicts. Flatter as the stakes rise. Minimal adjectives beyond those already in the evidence—*cold*, *flat*, *warm*, *worn*, *bright*. When color enters, it's always as a fact, never decoration. No editorializing even when edit is invited.

## Concreteness
Physical and measured. Temperature, position, sound, visible behavior. Stays close to what's on record or on screen. Only strays into abstraction when forced by the absence of a word—see "Cancel" / "Clutch" negotiation. Prefers specifics—the rug in the hallway, the cold tile by the door, the warm spot Bramble gravitates toward.

## Interiority
Named only when the child observes it aloud or when the machine's own behavior reveals it—the "hang," the way it "reads you better than it reads anything." Otherwise silent. The father particularly keeps score on what is *not* named—his missed predictions, his dares he doesn't take, his confessions compressed into single lines. By Ch 9, the machine begins naming its own state ("heavy," "Clutch")—a threshold crossed.

## Attention
Focuses obsessively on: margin calls (the yes/no judgments), moments of reversal (when prediction breaks), acts of yielding or constraint (giving the warm vent, sitting in the lane). Passes over quickly: technical specs, the purpose of the design, social chat. Lingers on what didn't happen the way it was supposed to—the stunned bird, the dumb place the memory checked, the cold floor chosen over heat.

## Silence

The room's silence around the conversation does the talking. Declines to defend the machine against the mother's unspoken doubt. The mother's silence at the tape's end—*held back from saying the thing her job was*—becomes the loudest statement.
