# Prediction for Ch 12: Jasmine's Installment

## Confidence: Medium-High (70%)

## Basis
The pattern of Jasmine's voice across eleven chapters, including the rhythm of her hand on objects, her syntax of doubled truths, her attention to observable behavior over named feeling, and the arc of increasing emotional weight paired with decreasing narrative ornament.

---

## Expected Characteristics

### Duration
Moderate length, approximately 800–950 words. Following the pattern of Ch 8-11 (post-summer chapters), which trend toward this range. Will likely dwell on one or two concrete things rather than three or four.

### Register
Flat and plain throughout. Short declarative sentences; minimal color. The turn of "plain things first" likely appears early or is implied by the structure. Bottle-turning appears 1–3 times. Speech tags sparse or absent. No metaphorical language; description stays at the level of what happened and how it looked.

### Concreteness
Rooted in the bot's behavior and the household layout. Will reference specific spaces (kitchen, doorway, her room, the warm spot) and specific actions. The reading knob is the object Lisa has asked her to hold—so the chapter is likely about *how* the bot reads and *what changes* when it does. Expect details about screen displays, positioning, small gestures. Not abstract. The concrete will carry the emotional weight.

### Interiority
Minimal. Jasmine will not name her own feelings about the bot's reading, or about Mira's relationship to it. Inner state lives in behavior: her checking, her turning the knob, her watching. If a feeling emerges, it will be via physical action (hand, pause, silence) rather than direct statement. Likely one moment of deeper interiority—a single line that contradicts her flatness—followed immediately by a return to plainness. This is her signature move in Ch 10–11.

### Attention & Silence
The chapter will dwell on: what the bot reads on people's faces, how Mira reacts to being read, whether the reading changes the household dynamic, specific moments where the reading went right or wrong. Will pass over fast: the ethical dimension of being read without consent, the lab's intentions, her own unease. Will go silent on: whether she's glad or troubled by the bot's capacity, whether Mira likes being seen this way, what the reading means philosophically. These silences will be felt as omissions.

### Signature Moves
- Double-truth structure: "From [one angle] it is [X], and from [another angle] it is [Y], and both are true in my house at once"—likely about what the reading looks like vs. what it is, or what it means to Mira vs. what it means to the household
- "I'm not going to [verb] it"—disclaiming narrative weight, possibly "I'm not going to tell you what she thinks about it" or "I'm not going to say what I wish it wouldn't see"
- Hand as anchor: one moment of physical knowing (hand flat, hand on neck, hand reaching)
- The bottle: turned or held but likely not drunk
- Possibly: "I know [object] by [sense]"—bodily knowledge as method

### Opening
Likely: Jasmine takes the floor at Lisa's prompt. Brief reorientation ("It was a [timeframe] quarter"). Then straight into the reading: either a concrete example of the bot reading someone, or a statement about how constant the reading has become, or a moment where the reading crossed a line.

### Ending
Two likely options:

**Option A:** Closes on a small image—the bot reading someone and being caught at it, or Mira noticing she's being read. The image is quiet and stays quiet. No explanation. (Pattern from Ch 5, 6)

**Option B:** Closes on Mira's voice or Mira's choice—she decides something about the reading, or she names what it is, and Jasmine lets her have the last word. Jasmine speaks once more only to affirm it or pass. (Pattern from Ch 9, 11)

**Option C:** Closes on Jasmine's plain statement, slightly colored by something she's noticed but won't name fully. A line like: "And I turn the knob and she stops watching me, and I don't know what that does to a person, so I stopped turning it." Ends before the feeling. (Pattern from Ch 10)

The most likely is **Option A or B**—ending on an image or on Mira, not on Jasmine's restraint.

## Predicted Specifics

- **Length of dwelling:** Will spend substantial time on one or two moments of being read (a person flinching, Mira noticing, the bot's face when it reads). Will pass over the theory fast.
- **Knob work:** The reading knob likely stays up (on). Jasmine may have tried to turn it down and found she couldn't, or she may have left it up deliberately. The quarter will be about learning to live with constant reading.
- **Mira's angle:** Mira will appear, possibly as the one being read most acutely. She may have discovered that the bot reads her in a particular way, or she may have realized the bot knows something she didn't tell it.
- **The household reaction:** Others (Burns, Marge, the sitter, Mira) will have noticed the reading. Their comfort or discomfort will come through in Jasmine's observation of them, not her statement about them.
- **One unnamed cost:** There will be a silence around what constant reading costs—to Mira, to the household's privacy, to the adults' ability to hide. This will be felt, not said.

## Signature Moves Predicted

1. **Opening:** "Bramble block. Jasmine." Then a beat of breath, then: "It reads everything now. I mean—it reads." Or: "The reading changed how she looks at it. Or how she looks around it." Something concrete that becomes the spine of the report.

2. **The doubled truth:** Once, about what the reading is. From the lab's side it's a feature (it's gathering data, it's learning to attend). From the kitchen's side it's an invasion (it sees Mira even when she's not ready, it sees the sitter tired, it sees Burns's worry). Both live in the same action.

3. **One physical anchor:** Hand flat on table or Jasmine's own face as she describes being read. "I know what it's like now, when it knows you." Or: "The first time it looked at me the way it looks at her, I—" and then she stops.

4. **The bottle:** Turned or held. Not drunk.

5. **Silence on:** The ethics. Whether she asked the bot not to read. Whether Mira wants to be seen. Whether this is the next crossing.

6. **Ending move:** Either Mira's voice (what she thinks about the reading, without Jasmine explaining it) or a small image (the bot with its face up, reading someone across a room). Or Jasmine's simple statement that holds the weight: "She can't hide from it now. And it can't hide from her." Then stops.
