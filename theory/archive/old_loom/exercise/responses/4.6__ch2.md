# Prediction for Ch 3

**Duration & Register:** Expect a shorter chapter, pulled toward the terse. Both chapters have moved through expansion (the first sprawling across household practice, the second methodically traversing sunbeam routes), but the register is sharpening. Ch 2 ends on a physical gesture—the hand as a landing place—held in near-silence. Ch 3 will likely move back toward Jasmine's voice, but clipped. Sentences will contract. The expansive explanations of how Bramble moves will compress into observation.

**Concreteness:** Highly concrete throughout both chapters, but the movement is *toward* smaller scales. Ch 1 operates in whole rooms; Ch 2 narrows to bot trajectories and inches of floor temperature. Ch 3 will dwell at an even tighter scale—the texture of a hand, the weight of Bramble on skin, the precise mechanics of a moment. Less architecture, more haptic.

**Interiority:** This voice names feelings rarely. Ch 1 has Jasmine's smile; Ch 2 has her pause. No interior state is stated. The voice is almost entirely exterior—what she sees, hears, feels with her hand, can measure. This will hold in Ch 3. If feeling emerges, it will be through the body, through silence, through what she *does* with her hands. It will not be named.

**Attention:** Both chapters linger on the tactile and the iterative. Jasmine watches the same loop of bots day after day and reports back not about dramatic change but about pattern—the cadence, the clicks, the cooling. She attends to texture (cold stone, warm floor, the slide of a bot). Ch 3 will attend to a similarly narrow, deep focus. It will not veer outward to plot or to the larger shape of the project. It will stay in the house, in one moment or a series of closely held moments.

**Signature:** The voice is characteristic in three ways: (1) It explains through hand knowledge—"I didn't have it till I had my hand on the cold." (2) It refuses dramatization—bots are bots, they do what they do, the cooling is just cooling. (3) It builds legitimacy through exhaustive, patient notation. When Jasmine tells you something, she has been watching it for weeks. This voice will not change in Ch 3. Expect the same steady reportage, the same investment in what can be felt or seen, the same deflection of bigger claims.

**Silence:** What the voice declines to say: the *meaning* of anything. Both chapters end with implication—the heated floor might matter; the bumping looks like unfairness—but Jasmine does not resolve these. She says what is, not what it means. She will continue this. In Ch 3, something will be left hanging, and the chapter will end before it is named.
