# Prediction for Ch 7

## Duration
Medium-to-long, following the established pattern of Jasmine's sustained narrations. The chapter will not be one of the shorter, more clipped updates. It will dwell at length on a particular household moment or behavior, with time enough for the setup, the event, and the reflection.

## Register
Expansive, with the characteristic texture of Jasmine's voice: clear-eyed but not austere. Factual throughout, with occasional direct statement of feeling that lands without decoration. Syntax will remain varied—short declarative sentences mixed with longer observational passages. No editorializing, but room for dry understated humor at the margins.

## Concreteness
High. Physical, domestic, object-level. The chapter will track something Bramble *does* in the house—a new behavior, a repeat pattern observed closely, or an interaction with objects or people. Expect specific times of day, specific rooms, specific materials (sunlight, floor temperature, the texture of surfaces). The abstraction, if it arrives, will emerge from concrete detail, not be imposed on it.

## Interiority
Sparse and careful. Jasmine does not name feelings readily. If an inner state surfaces, it will be Mira's (as it has been before) or will be attributed to Bramble via behavior rather than statement. Jasmine will remain observational. However, the pattern suggests one moment per chapter where she permits herself a statement of her own experience—a moment usually introduced with hesitation ("I keep turning over," "I keep sitting with"). Expect one such moment.

## Attention
Will linger on: a detail about how Bramble moves or orients itself in the house; what Mira has noticed or done; the interplay between Bramble's behavior and household rhythm. Will pass quickly over: background explanations, repetition of prior findings, anything that has already been established. The chapter will likely open mid-finding, without preamble.

## Silence
The chapter will not directly address: the broader implications of Bramble's learning; comparison to the original unit (that comparison has been made and sits as a fact now); Mira's dawning cognition of what Bramble is becoming. These will be implied but not stated.

## Signature

- Repeated structural element: "Plain things first" or equivalent has appeared in three chapters; may appear again or be dispensed with.
- Diction: concrete nouns, few adjectives, reliance on verbs and prepositions for movement and spatial relation. "Goes," "sits," "backs," "picks," "leaves."
- The voice carries the exhaustion of parenting and the precise attention of someone living with a changing thing.
- Sentences that begin with "And" or "He" after a pause.

1. **A plain observation of household behavior**: something Bramble does repeatedly, measurable, tangible.
