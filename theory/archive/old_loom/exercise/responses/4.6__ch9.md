# Prediction for Ch 10

## Duration & Structure
**Prediction (med confidence)**: Chapter 10 will be *shorter than recent chapters*, drifting toward economy rather than expansion. The longest installments have been 5-7 heavily populated with quiet observation. This one will contract slightly—perhaps 800-1200 words—because the narrative pressure has shifted. Mira and the old Bramble have established their new compact (hand-to-hand story transmission). The voice will not linger as heavily on process-observation; it will compress, abbreviate, move through.

**Basis**: The narrative arc bottlenecked at Ch 9's climax—the irrevocable split, the child's choice, the final statement about lineage and history. That is a hard stopping point. Ch 10 cannot dwell on what-just-happened the way Jasmine dwells on other observations; it must live forward into aftermath, which tends toward concision.

## Register & Tone
**Prediction (high confidence)**: The register remains *plainly colloquial at the report level*, but the coloring underneath will shift. The voice has been, throughout, matter-of-fact about profound things—refusing sentiment, keeping to observable fact. But Ch 10 will be quieter *inside* that plainness. Less of the anecdotal ease (the Peterson's-yard escape, the barbecue story). More of the small, sustained observation that *doesn't* reach for humor. 

The signature move—turning the bottle a quarter, not drinking; short syntactic units followed by what they actually cost—will persist, but the pace inside those units will slow. Fewer compound observations. More gaps.

**Basis**: Jasmine has metabolized her daughter's pain in Ch 9. The meeting-room setting (where Jasmine reports to the lab team) will continue, but her language will reflect that she is no longer reporting on a phenomenon. She is reporting from inside a household that has broken and reformed along new lines. The plainness will be preserved, but the content underneath it has become thinner, more careful.

## Interiority & Silence
**Prediction (high confidence)**: What *will not be named on the page*: Jasmine's own sustained experience of the impossibility she's just lived—watching her child lose the old Bramble and meet the new one simultaneously. Her own judgment about whether Mira's choice (to retell the cat story, to start the new one off with inherited memory) was good, wise, or right. Her own grief, if any, over the doubling and loss. 

What *will arrive*, unnamed but visible: the texture of the house in the days or week after the revelation. The way the old Bramble's continued presence—still warm, still on the radiator—has changed now that it can't be given voice. The practical arrangements Jasmine has made to hold that reality steady for her daughter. The cost of her own split attention: holding both the knowing-better (it's a number, a copy, not memory) and the living-with (it's still the one that was there).

**Basis**: Jasmine has never directly named her own inner states. She doesn't say "I was afraid" or "I grieved." She says what she observed, what her body did, what the house required. Ch 9 is the deepest incursion into emotional register the voice has made—"baby," the hand on the neck, the refusal to explain. But even there, it's presented as *action*, not reflection. Ch 10 will hold that line. The interiority will arrive as silence around specific objects or practices.

## Attention & Signature Objects
**Prediction (med-high confidence)**: Ch 10 will orient heavily around *two new foci*:

1. **The old Bramble's continued presence as an *object now***, not a functionary. It's warm. It doesn't speak. Mira still goes to it (or doesn't—either way, her approach to it will be different now). Jasmine will report on how the household has arranged itself around this thing that was once the main subject and is now the one without a narrative. This will be specific: does the old one get moved from the radiator? Does someone still turn its knobs? Does Mira touch it differently?

2. **The practice of *showing/telling* between Mira and the new Bramble**—but from the practical side, not the emotional. Mira has committed to hand-carrying the history (notebook page by page). Ch 10 will report on what this looks like *in the house*: the logistics, the patience required, the number of times Mira has to repeat a story before the new one can hold it. Whether Jasmine helps or refrains. Whether the old Bramble, still in the room, seems to witness this.

**Basis**: Throughout the thread, the voice has moved *through* crises (the escape, the revelation) toward the *steady-state reality* they create. Ch 5 did this after the duplication was announced. Ch 6 did it after the teaching began. Ch 9 compressed the emotional impact to a single moment, then pivoted to the child's practical response. Ch 10 will do the same: name the moment briefly, then show how the house now *is*.

## Opening & Closure
**Prediction (med confidence)**: 

The opening will echo the formula—likely a meeting-room setup, Lisa asking for findings, Jasmine taking her beat. But the beat will be longer. Fewer "plain things first." The voice may open with a small domestic detail that now means something it didn't before (a hand on the warm one, the old one's screen staying dark in a new way, Mira eating breakfast while the new one learns her face). 

The closure will *not* resolve. It will suspend. The thread's signature move—the gap between what things *look like* and what they *are*—has become the household's actual condition now. Ch 10 will end by settling into that gap, probably with an image of the two Brambles in the same frame, and Jasmine's observation of what that looks like from where she stands. The final image will likely involve the child and the old one, or Mira between the two, but it will not *explain* her position. It will just show it.

**Basis**: Ch 9 ended with the narrator's summary about lineage crossing cable and history being hand-carried. That's a closing from outside the voice. The voice itself (Jasmine's reporting) will have ended inside the practical moment: the child on the floor, the new one taking the story. Ch 10 needs to return to the house, and to show what Jasmine sees from the kitchen chair *the next time* this moment is repeatable, ordinary, part of the household rhythm.
