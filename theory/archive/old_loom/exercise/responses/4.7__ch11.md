# Prediction for Ch 12

## Duration & Pacing
The chapter will be **medium-to-long**, dwelling especially on naming or framework closure. Lisa has shown a pattern of accelerating explanations at major boundaries (the symbolic line, the city): once a conceptual threshold is identified, she compresses the next quarter's work. She'll move faster through the mechanics than through the implications.

## Register
**Elevated, deliberate, restrained of gesture.** The room has now built "a whole one"—a complete system. The register will reflect this. Less of the palm-and-palm turning (that's reserved for active building). More stillness. When she does move her hands, it will be architectural, pointing at fixed structures, not turning things over in thought. The voice will be flatter where it's confident about having said what needed saying, warmer where she's still uncertain of the shape underneath.

## Concreteness
Very concrete at the **mechanism level** (something on the tetrapod's back, a knob, a board), much more abstract at the **implication level**. The pattern predicts she'll name what the system *does* in a single sentence (as she did with the city: "codes are the city's mitochondria"), then climb away from the object itself into what that means for the next thing they'll build or understand. The concreteness will get absorbed into abstraction fast.

## Interiority
Lisa names inner states rarely—she defers them. When one shows up, it comes through Marge or the behavioral observation (the household says, Mira did). This quarter, **she will likely still defer naming how she feels about what they've made, but she may let Marge or Bart or Burns comment on it,** and Lisa will not correct them—a different kind of assent than her usual explicit agreement. She may also turn the frame one more time: what does it mean that they've built a whole one, in the symbolic line, in one year.

## Attention
She will linger on **the junction between what's finished and what's still open.** She has done this each time a line completed. She'll walk the board once more—this is her signature closure move. But this time she may mark *what slot's still empty* or *what the next line would start with,* preparing the room for a different kind of work ahead. She will **move quickly past the housekeeping** (the knobs, the new signatures) to land on the structural thing: what does it mean that this line is done and the next one is waiting.

## Silence: What She Won't Say
She will **not** say whether the machine on the floor feels anything, means anything, or deserves moral status—she's been explicit about keeping that off the record. She may also **decline to name** what the next line is or what it's for, even if the room asks. She has shown, in prior chapters, that she files certain questions and comes back to them only when she's built enough to point at. The prediction is she'll do this again: something will get filed, flagged as *not yet*, and the room will know by her stopping that they've hit the boundary of what the framework currently holds.

## Signature Moves
- The **"I want to be careful about"** hedge: she'll deploy it at least once, probably twice, around something that felt like a simple statement but isn't
- The **nose-laugh**: will appear, probably when something structurally obvious has taken a quarter to become visible
- The **flat palm + vertical palm crossing**: will appear, but used to mark *what's done* rather than *what's being thought*
- **Deference to another's field**: she'll likely give Burns or Marge the last word on something, rather than closing it herself
- **Camera hand**: one hand hovering near the camcorder, not on it—she's thinking while running the record

## Expected Structure
The meeting will open with **Lisa on the board**: the state of the tetrapod at quarter's end, what's new, what's consolidated. Burns will get a deep-time window (shorter than usual—the time is running out if a whole line is done). Bart will walk the new hardware or the implications. Marge will translate to folk terms and will **not be defensive this time**—she'll sound settled, almost satisfied, which will be new. The closing framework block will be Lisa's, and it will do what the framework blocks always do: **extract the structural rule from the quarterly change**, then point at what that rule predicts about the shape of what comes next.

## Confidence & Basis
**Medium-High confidence** on the structural elements (Lisa opens on the board, closes on a loop or a boundary, Marge gets the folk-psychological line). **Medium confidence** on tone (this is the moment of completion; the voice should shift, but the prediction of *how much* it shifts is uncertain). **Low-to-Medium confidence** on what gets named or filed—she's shown she can surprise with a new category or a new way of framing, and the pattern of her framework blocks is "escalating abstraction," which means this one might climb further than predicted.

She will not say what they've made is finished in a way that forecloses it. She will say it's complete, which is different—complete as in whole, ready to be carried forward, not complete as in done.
