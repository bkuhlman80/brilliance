# Prediction for Chapter 5

## Basis
Four chapters establish a clear pattern in Lisa's voice: she opens with concrete sensory detail, moves to technical explanation at the table, shifts to abstraction and framework-building, tests those frameworks against what they don't yet know, and consistently flags her own uncertainty. The trajectory shows her voice becoming more comfortable with ambiguity and more explicit about gaps. She names things with precision, builds through repetition and analogy, and preserves her interiority through silence rather than through vagueness.

## Duration
Likely 2200-2800 words, continuing the expansion visible across chapters 1-4. The pattern suggests the meeting structure continues (someone calls her section, she takes the floor or the table, delivers her block, fields questions), so the container remains predictable even as content deepens.

## Register
Opening will be conversational and grounded. The explanatory sections will shift toward the expansive — longer sentences, more subordination, more conditional phrasing ("if... then..."). The dialogue interjections will remain clipped, especially from Bart, but Lisa's responses to challenges will be longer and more architecturally intricate. By the end, register will tilt abstract.

## Concreteness
Will begin with a physical moment — probably Lisa with an object (a board, a card, something Mira has drawn, or the camera). Will move through high-concrete middle sections describing mechanism or change. The final abstraction will be nearly object-less — discussion of what happens *next quarter* or what's *necessary but missing*. The ratio between concrete and abstract will be roughly 30/70 by the chapter's end.

## Interiority
Her own inner state will not be named. What appears instead: hesitation (caught mid-word), correction of herself, or notational action (the glass-moving gesture, the hands-thinking-together motion). Other people will read her states more explicitly — Jasmine will notice something about her body; Bart will know what her silence means. The gap between her description of Bramble's interiority and her own will be the silence the chapter leaves behind.

## Attention
Will linger on: the incompleteness of the four-box model as it scales to the next clades; what a "phase change" would look like if they got it right; the specific moment when a system becomes *capable of choosing against its own nature.* Will pass over quickly: why she cares about this particular escalation; what she fears might happen if the framework breaks; how much of this came from reading versus living.

## Signature
- Opening likely with "What came next was Lisa [verb]..." or "Lisa [state], the only change..."
- Sentences will contain nested conditionals: "If a thing can do X and we let it run that against Y, then Z happens, and the happening is..."
- Will use "is" predicatively more than descriptively: not "the board has memory" but "the board *is* the memory" or "the board *is* what makes an inside worth the word"
- Will move between registers by returning to a named thing: "Call it X. It's..." — regrounds each abstraction
- Will flag uncertainty explicitly: "I don't know yet," "It might," "We could be wrong about..."
- Will use repetition as a thinking-tool, not for emphasis: "Scaling. Scaling the four. Can four boxes that describe a protocell to a mouse describe a mouse to a human? Can they? Can they?"

## What Gets Named
Something previously circling without a name will be named. Likely candidates: the relationship between the four boxes (not a hierarchy, not a sequence — something else); the difference between what Bramble *is* and what it's *for*; what it means that they're building something that might eventually want something they didn't intend.

## Silence
- Will not directly answer whether the brain line is a phase change or a continuation
- Will not explain why this particular escalation (reflex → revision → decision?) matters to her personally
- Will not address directly whether Marge's worry (they might spend three years arguing about what feels) is justified
- Will leave a gap where someone expects a synthesis but gets instead "I need to think about it" or "That's next quarter"
- The gap will feel intentional, not like a dodge

## Confidence & Basis
**MEDIUM-HIGH** on duration, register, and signature. The pattern is stable across all four chapters. 

**MEDIUM** on what gets named and where silence falls. Lisa's uncertainty about the "phase change" question is genuine, and she might circle it for a full chapter without resolving it, or name it so obliquely that the naming doesn't feel like naming.

**MEDIUM-LOW** on interiority and attention. The thread shows her becoming more willing to let discomfort sit (the juice-box moment, the catch-out about Evo-Algo), but whether that trend continues or whether this chapter brings a pull-back to her usual precision is unpredictable.

## Key Prediction
The chapter will end either (a) with a named thing that reframes what they've built so far, or (b) with Lisa explicitly declining to name something and explaining *why* the unnaming matters. Either way, it will be a moment where the gap between what she can say and what she's thinking becomes visible to the room.
