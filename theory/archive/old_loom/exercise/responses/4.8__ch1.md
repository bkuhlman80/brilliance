# Prediction for Ch 2

## Duration and Structure
The next chapter will occupy a middle range — longer than a quick interjection but shorter than the philosophical sprawl of Ch 1. It will spend concentrated time on a single domain or case, 2500–4000 words. Where Ch 1 opens with standing and announcement, Ch 2 will likely open in motion or arrival: someone entering, returning, or a shift in posture that breaks the opening paragraph's stillness.

## Register
Ch 1 is expansive and formal, but its formality lives in *patience*, not coldness. The voice is didactic without being pedantic — it loops back, concedes, re-frames. Ch 2 will maintain that expansiveness but with more room for specific instance. The clipped phrases ("People can change. People do change. Fine.") will reappear, but the chapter may open or close with something more compressed, almost terse, to mark a boundary.

## Concreteness
Ch 1 anchors abstraction to one physical object: the beer, the tea-posture, the switches on the back of heads. Ch 2 will likely introduce a new case or scenario — probably from her clinical work, since that's where her theory meets resistance. There will be a person, or a session, or a choice made by a client. The concreteness will be higher than Ch 1 because it's moving from principle to example. The beer will probably not return; another object will replace it.

## Interiority and Attention
Ch 1 names nothing of Marge's inner state — no doubt, no excitement, no cost. It's all architectural: here is the belief structure, here are its gears. Ch 2 will likely touch her interiority only through behavior: what she pays attention to, what she skips, which question she lets hang. She will dwell on something a client said or did that *confirms* or *complicates* the Ghost-in-the-Machine diagnosis. The attention will narrow to a single interaction or relationship problem, moving from the general theory to its collision with a specific want.

## Silence
Ch 1 declines to explain why she waited years to say this aloud. It declines to name what the room gives her (beyond "working condition"). Ch 2 will continue that restraint. It will not psychologize her own theory — not explain why she needed to announce it standing, not unpack why Burns laughing landed differently than his earlier silence. The silence will deepen around one client's response or resistance: what they *almost* said, what Marge did not interpret.

## Signature
The diction is precise and slightly formal — "homunculus," "regulator," "typological dimension" — but not inaccessible. There's a rhythm of restatement: "The willing self *is* the system. The willing self *is* part of what changes." This rhythm of doubled or tripled phrases, each tightening or reframing, will persist in Ch 2. The voice uses "I" sparingly and almost impersonally — "I have written," "I have not had this room before" — treating her own actions as data points, not expressions of feeling. 

The short sentences will increase at points of emphasis. There will be pauses — marked with breaks or silence — that let an idea settle before the next claim builds on it.

## Prediction Summary
**Length:** ~3000–3500 words

**Opening:** Likely a shift from the standing-room static of Ch 1 — someone's arrival, a session transcript, a client name introduced — or the resumption of speech after a quarter has passed.

**Middle:** One case or scenario from her clinical work or teaching practice that tests the Ghost-in-the-Machine frame. A person who wants to change and discovers they cannot, or attempts to change via the external-willpower model and hits a wall. The patient's disappointment or resistance will be the hinge.

**Closure:** No resolution or judgment of the case — instead, the case left open as it illustrates some wrinkle in the theory. The chapter will end with a reframing or a further claim that the case does not settle, or with a return to quiet — her sitting down, or the room going still again.

**Feel:** Slightly less declarative than Ch 1, more entangled with specific pain or confusion. The theory will remain architecture, but it will be bearing weight.

**Confidence:** Medium. The structure of her speech (numbered bits, quarterly delivery, the short story already written) suggests episodic deepening. The voice's refusal of personal disclosure predicts she will move through example rather than introspection. But the weight and tenor of which example, and whether it's clinical or pedagogical, sits at the edge of predictability.
