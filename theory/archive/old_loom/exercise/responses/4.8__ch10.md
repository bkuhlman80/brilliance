## Interiority
This is the domain where Marge labors most. She does not name feelings on the page; she names processes instead. She will not say "I felt afraid" or "I wanted." She will describe what it looks like when a person cannot set something down, and in that description, interiority will be present as an absence — the thing the reader fills in themselves, the way she does for her clients.

Chapter 11 will linger on a place where what someone says does not match what someone does, and will leave that gap open longer than comfortable.

And a Dealt-Hand believer who isn't keeping." She builds by doubling back. She has a habit of correcting herself midstream — "Not proven. A thing I keep failing to disprove" — which gives her voice a quality of intellectual rigor that is also honesty. Chapter 11 will maintain this: short bones of sentences, built on repetition and self-correction, pauses that do the work of punctuation.
