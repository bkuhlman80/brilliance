# Prediction for Chapter 10

## Duration and Pacing
Expect a chapter of moderate length—not the expansive philosophical dissertations of earlier installments (Ch 1-5), but longer than the recent technical interludes (Ch 7-8). Marge has filed the pieces and reached for her beer; the next movement will likely dwell on something concrete—an instance, a moment of practice, a specific shape running into its mistake—before pulling back to connection. The narrator's hand will be present but restrained.

## Register and Tone
Marge will speak more quietly than in the thesis-bearing quarters. The clinical register will be there but worn lightly, almost in the background. There will be moments of color—physical description, image—but the dominant mode will be *plain*, the way she says things she has settled long before. The "careful sentence" will have different weights in it: some parts matter to say, other parts she will move through quickly. The room will hold its breath in the quiet places.

## Concreteness
The chapter will be anchored in an instance or a small set of instances, the way Ch 2 had the twelve-year-old girl and the podcast, the way Ch 3 opened with Iron John. Marge doesn't philosophize in a void; she runs philosophy against the shape of a life. Expect a person—likely from her practice, anonymized, sitting in front of her in the past—whose particular shape will carry the argument forward. The physical world will be there: chairs, tea, the things bodies do when they are running a program under load.

## Interiority
This is where the register shifts. In earlier chapters, Marge named inner states on the page, sometimes with visible resistance (the wavering in Ch 3, the clinical voice cracking). In recent chapters, she has been tracking what is *not* inner—the vote, the field, the ancient circuitry that runs before anyone is home. The next chapter will likely hold both: the shape of what a person feels, and the mechanism underneath it that generates the feeling without requiring a feeling-er. The distinction will matter. The interiority will be there, but it will be *accounted for*—shown not as the evidence of a self but as a signal in a system.

## Attention and Silence
Marge will linger on the crux—the moment where a shape meets the world and makes its characteristic mistake. She will pass quickly over the diagnosis itself (the room has seen four of these now) and rest on something harder to say: what it costs to live in that shape, or what it costs *her* to watch someone live in it. The silence will be in what she doesn't name—the analogy between what she is watching and what she has learned to watch in herself. She will decline to close that gap, but it will be visible to anyone who knows to look.

## Signature
The diction will stay in Marge's register: plain verbs, sentences that don't elaborate, occasional hand gestures that the camera catches without her sending them. Syntax will be mostly parataxis and simple compounds—not baroque, not complex embedding. When the subordination comes, it will be *because*-driven, causal chains. There will be one moment where she corrects herself or checks herself mid-sentence, the way she did with "spitting image," the way she has done when she catches herself going further than she meant.

## Structural Prediction
The chapter will open with Lisa's cue or a beat of room-settling (the kettle, the camera, someone shifting). Marge will not stand. She will offer a case, a shape, a groove. She will run it against the old wiring—the flatworm, the lamprey, whatever the oldest version is that lacks this particular error. She will name the mistake the shape makes. Then—and this is the turn—she will go one beat further than the argument requires, into something that sounds like it's about the *person* in front of her, or in her past, and then she will stop herself or the room will interrupt or she will simply set the beer down and let the moment settle. The machine is coming, the narrator says. This chapter will be the last pure note before the technical drawing starts.

## Confidence and Basis
**Confidence: High**

The basis: (1) the pattern of escalation—each chapter has added a layer of mechanism and taken a layer of human agency away, and the inverse is now in motion: the room is moving toward seeing the person and the mechanism at once; (2) the narrator's increasing presence and explicit attention to what Marge is *not* saying, and the next chapter will give the narrator more to hold back; (3) the arc across chapters—the four fallacies established, the four mechanisms paired against them, the joint between them laid a hand's width apart, and now the chapter where that gap closes, not in theory but in a particular instance; (4) the register shift already underway in Ch 8-9, where the tone grew simpler and the attention turned inward, toward the practice itself rather than toward defending the theory; (5) Marge's visible resistance in recent chapters to the moment when the apparatus starts to resolve into a shape—the "careful" that came out of her when Burns named Experiencing Style, the beer held still while she corrects herself about the Dealt Hand—all of this suggests the next chapter is *hers* in a way the recent technical ones have not been.
