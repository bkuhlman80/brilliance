# Unit: 4.9__ch11  (category 4.9 · chapter 11 · predicts Ch 12)

# Prediction for Ch 12

## Expected characteristics

**Duration**: Shorter than Ch 10-11. The novel has moved through escalation (opening chapters 3-7 medium-long, peak at Ch 10, slight contraction at Ch 11). Expect a brief concentrated passage, 500-800 words — a step back, not another expansion.

**Register**: Restrained, internal. The voice has grown more sparse over chapters 7-11, stripping away decoration. Expect fewer verbs of feeling, more verbs of noticing. Flat, carrying weight underneath.

**Concreteness**: High. Will not explain or resolve in abstract terms. If someone leaves the room, we see the leaving. If a thought occurs, we see the hand that carries it. No general statements. Physical anchoring.

**Interiority**: Not named. The narrator observes what people carry from the meeting but does not say they carry it. Shows it in postponed motion, or in what they do not say to each other afterward.

**Attention**: Will linger on someone moving away from the table or toward it. Likely Jasmine, or one of the four lingering in their private reckoning after the unanimous vote. Not on the vote itself — that is done. On what it cost to reach it, registered in the body of whoever has to know the cost most clearly.

**Silence**: Will decline to explain why that person is doing what they're doing. Will not name the feeling. Will not reconcile the household count (Jasmine/Mira) with the lab count. Will hold both open.

**Signature**: A moment observed after the others have moved. Likely a parenthetical aside about what the tape does or does not capture. The voice's characteristic move: standing outside a person's privacy and not breaching it, but marking that it's there. "The narrator noted, and did not say" or its equivalent.

## Predicted opening and shape

Expect it to open *after* the meeting ends or as people are leaving — not the vote itself, which has been given four-to-nothing finality at Ch 11. 

Likely opening: Someone doing a small physical thing that carries what the vote cost. Marge picking her beer up again. Burns standing longer than he should. Jasmine gathering something. One of them alone in the room.

**Predicted ending**: Not a resolution. A silence held, or a gap widened. A moment where what the narrator is *not* saying becomes the actual content — the shape of something nobody can make fit into the vote's clean line.

**Confidence**: Medium. The pattern of the thread is strong (escalation followed by deepening disagreement; votes that bury rather than resolve; narrator's repeated move of marking what is not said). But Ch 12 could pivot toward a different voice or moment entirely — the thread has shown it can shift scope (Ch 9-10 bring in Jasmine and Mira). It could follow one of them instead of the narrator's observational remove.

**Basis**: The thread's repeated structure: vote happens, then narrator steps outside it to show what the vote did not contain. The escalation of this move in Ch 10-11 (Marge calling for other votes, the household count against the lab count) sets up a final moment where someone has to carry the fact that consensus was reached without agreement. That someone is most likely the one the narrator has been watching keep silence: Jasmine, or the narrator herself reflecting on what her own counting has been.
