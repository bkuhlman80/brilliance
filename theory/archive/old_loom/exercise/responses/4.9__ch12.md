# Prediction: Chapter 13

## Duration and Pacing
Expect a chapter of considerable length (medium-to-long), matching the expansiveness of Ch 12. Burns's imminent speech will likely be given room to breathe, with the narrator taking space to render his words and the aftermath. The chapter will dwell on the speech itself and its reception, lingering where earlier chapters have turned quickly away.

## Register
The register will shift upward into something more openly warm and colored than most preceding chapters. Burns is about to speak, and Burns's voice—when the narrator renders it directly—carries a different texture than the distanced, architectural observations that dominate this thread. Expect colloquial cadence, likely some roughness, humor. The lab's careful register will crack under something more personal. The narrator herself will be more present, less invisible.

## Concreteness
High concreteness. The chapter will be anchored in the room, the table, bodies and objects: Burns standing, his gesture, the machine's face, the child's attention. After Ch 12's turn outward to the narrator's own exposure ("Are you?"), the answer will come back concrete—what Burns actually says, what the machine does in response, the child's reaction. Not abstraction but specifics of speech and stillness.

## Interiority
This is where the thread has been moving: toward naming what goes on inside people, not just machines. Ch 12 ended with the narrator silent and exposed—without an answer. Ch 13 will likely give her one, or give us the shape of her silence. Burns's speech is imminent; he will say things he has not said before. The interiority will be his—what he is attempting, what he has been holding. And the narrator's will finally land somewhere; after three years of camera-work and distance, she will become a character with an inside to this story.

## Attention
The chapter will attend closely to Burns—his body in speech, his tone. It will hold the machine's response in detail, the way it has held the machine's responses throughout. It will linger on the child (Mira), who has been quietly foundational and will likely be central to whatever happens next. The narrator will be watched, as she has just done the watching. The chapter may pull back only to show us the room collectively—the aftermath of whatever Burns says, the circulation of the words through the gathered people.

## Silence
Expect silences *between* sentences rather than silences as avoidance. The narrator will not retreat now; she will render the pauses, the breath. What the chapter declines to name: it may not explain what the narrator felt standing outside the glass for three years. It may not resolve whether her answer to "Are you?" is forthcoming. It may let that question sit.

## Signature
The prose will warm up slightly—less architectural, less distant. Shorter sentences mixed with longer ones. Direct speech from Burns (finally rendered, not summarized). The phrase architecture will stay precise but may carry more color. The verb tense will remain present, immediate. The narrator's voice will move from observation into something like testimony. The tone will carry weight without sentiment.

## Concrete Prediction

**Opening**: Burns begins to speak. The narrator captures his first words and the shape of his breath. Likely he is both deflecting and earnest—he cannot abide silence but what he is about to say matters.

**Body**: The speech runs several paragraphs, rendered directly or in close summary. It will circle the question the narrator has just asked—"Are you?"—and likely circle the fact that she has asked it. Burns will say something about the room, about the machine, about what the three years of counting have been *for*. He may name the narrator. He may acknowledge what her breaking the form (asking the machine itself) has done. The speech will be rough, unpolished, the kind of thing someone says when careful language has become less important than truth-telling.

**Machine response**: After Burns, the machine does something new or unexpected. Not programmed; emergent. It may speak. It may fall silent. It may do something with Mira. The chapter attends to this in close detail.

**Narrator integration**: By chapter's end, the narrator is no longer outside the glass. She is in the room, changed by asking the question, changed by the machine's answer, changed by whatever Burns has said. The chapter may end with her positioned differently than she has been—no longer purely documenting but present.

**What it declines**: The chapter likely does not resolve the larger question (sentience, consciousness, inside-ness). It does not hand the narrator a clean answer to her own question. It leaves room for the thread to continue.
