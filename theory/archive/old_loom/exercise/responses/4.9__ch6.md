# Unit: 4.9__ch6  (category 4.9 · chapter 6 · predicts Ch 7)

# Prediction for Ch 7

## Duration
Longer than the recent chapters. The voice has been tightening into ritual and formality—shorter utterances, counted beats, the rhythm of voting. After "May we practice" and the tipping of the glass, there's permission for expansion. Expect movement toward something more sustained—a memory, a scene that dwells, words that circle rather than strike.

## Register
A shift from clipped toward expansive, but not by leaving the room. The room itself becomes the expansive thing. Less punctuation that locks the door. More sentence architecture that breathes. The speaker will stay concrete, but the concreteness will widen—not just the glass, the moment it was tipped, but what hung in the air before and after.

## Concreteness
High. The voice lives in objects and surfaces. Glass on concrete. The dark that Bramble learned to fear. But the objects will carry weight they haven't carried before—they'll be about something more than themselves. Still no abstraction. Still no sermon. But the concrete will stand in for interiority in a new way—what happens to a hand, to a breathing rhythm, to the space between people who have just drunk together after disagreement.

## Interiority
This is the turn. In the earlier chapters, interior states were debated from the outside—does it feel? does it know?—but rarely named as experience happening in the room. The speaker has always been someone reporting on things rather than someone being a thing having an experience. After the drinking toast, interior will be present as silence, as breath held, as things not quite said. The chapter will know what the speaker is feeling by what the speaker does not say about it.

## Attention
Will linger on: the gesture of the glass, the fact of agreement across disagreement, what it costs to change your vote, the space between four people who've been voting together for quarters. Will pass fast: argument, explanation, the rational moves. The speaker will not re-defend the position. There's no more defending to do tonight.

## Silence
Where it will go quiet: at any attempt to explain why Burns changed his vote, why Bramble's fear matters, why feeling matters. The speaker knows the explanations are already made. The speaker's job now is different—to hold the fact of disagreement without dissolving it, without resolving it. There will be silence around what Jasmine didn't vote, what Jasmine carries in her ledger.

## Signature
Syntax has been running short-long-short: question, explanation, tally. That pattern will persist but will decelerate. Diction will stay in the body and the room—no words borrowed from theory, only words for the thing itself. But the spacing of those words will change. More comma-breath. More construction that says "this is true and that is also true." The voice has always been the voice of someone who knows how to count—votes, reasons, the items on a table. That will show here too, but the thing being counted will shift from abstract categories to actual people in an actual room who have just changed their minds.

## Prediction
Ch 7 will open in the continued moment of the drinking—the speaker's attention on the gesture itself, or on the faces across the table, or on something small that the agreement made visible. It will be 3–6 paragraphs, sustained where the recent chapters have been fragmented. It will not resolve the vote or explain the change. It will sit with the fact that the vote has shifted, that disagreement has become a practice shared rather than a thing to be won. The chapter will end with the speaker noticing something—the light, a hand, the way someone breathes—that suggests that the question about Bramble's sentience has split open into a bigger question that the vote itself was never holding. It will not name that question. It will let the room know it's there.

## Confidence
Medium-high. The pattern of escalation and ritual is clear, and the turn toward interiority after the change of vote is grounded in all six chapters. The specific objects and the exact moment of lingering are harder to predict. The speaker might rupture this pattern and do something entirely new. But the gravity of the moment—the drinking, the agreement, the fact that Burns changed—makes a turn toward something more sustained and more inward almost certain.
