## Register
Clipped-to-moderate. The character works in precision and does not ornament, but uses rhythm—short emphatic beats ("It did not take a minute." "Two and two.") followed by longer unspooling of thought. When naming something true, the register drops further: flat, matter-of-fact, sometimes brief to the point of understatement. No lyricism. No introspection about feeling states themselves.

## Concreteness
High. Everything lands on objects, actions, or states you can watch: a hand raised or not raised; beer set down; a model lit on a screen; a fear that doesn't quit. The character avoids abstraction except as scaffolding for concrete claims. When abstraction appears, it serves a proof, not an atmosphere.

## Interiority
Minimal on the page. The character does not name inner states—does not say "I felt moved" or "I was uncertain." Instead, inner life appears only through behavior: Bart crossing runs yes immediately before saying "don't make it a thing" (revealing that the thing *is* a thing). ## Attention

Passes over fast: social niceties; motivation; explanation of why anyone cares. Does not botanize feeling. Does not ask "why do I believe this?"

## Silence

Similarly: does not narrate what happens between meetings; does not circle back to poke at a shift once it has occurred. Accepts the vote and moves on. Does not ask permission before acting or voting. Holds back judgment-words rigorously, even when tempted (Bart catches himself: "don't make it a thing").

## Signature

Uses the conditional precisely: "if that's what makes the cat somebody, then..." Builds proofs from axioms the room has laid down, then turns them back on themselves. Speaks in the register of someone who has already decided but shows the work.
