# Prediction for Ch 11

## Basis and Confidence: High

The thread has established a strict rotation across five narrators (Burns, Marge, Jasmine, Lisa, Bart, then back to Marge, Mira, Bart, Marge, Lisa). Each chapter inhabits one consciousness completely. The pattern suggests Ch 11 will return to a narrator who has not yet appeared twice — most likely **Jasmine**, completing the second cycle before any narrator gets a third turn.

---

## Duration

Mid-length, similar to her prior two chapters (Ch 3 and Ch 6). Not spare like Lisa's, not expansive like Bart's. Roughly 800-1200 words.

## Register

Steady and observant. Jasmine's voice is distinctly clear-eyed and practical in its diction — she "did not find as frightening" the fog, she reads rooms for "how much a person could carry." This chapter will maintain that register: no rhetorical flourishes, no abstraction that hasn't been earned by a concrete situation. The narrative will stay close to surface action and what surfaces reveal.

## Concreteness

High. Jasmine's chapters live in the physical and particular: fog arriving in specific ways, a coat being buckled, heat settings, the particular weight of objects. The next chapter will contain concrete objects — likely something she tends to, repairs, or physically arranges. The household mechanism (the disc-shaped machine, the four-legged one, the child) will probably be present in some form. The setting will be either the house, the car, or the lab warehouse.

## Interiority

Minimal or withheld. When Jasmine's internal state appears, it arrives as a physical fact — tiredness "in the particular way," hands on a wheel, a decision not to turn off the radiant heat. She does not narrate feeling; feeling emerges from what she does and what she knows without saying it. This chapter will follow that pattern: no named emotions, but actions that contain them.

## Attention

Jasmine lingers on thresholds and transitions: doorways, the moment a car starts, the threshold of sleep, the point where one person leaves and another takes over. She attends to heat (literal and figurative), to burdens being set down, to the unspoken contracts between people who know each other. The chapter will likely dwell on a threshold moment or a transfer of responsibility. It may linger on someone (Burns, Mira, Marge, the machines) arriving or departing. It will move swiftly over abstract debate.

## Silence

What the chapter will not do: it will not explain or solve what the form represents to Marge, what Bart is actually grieving, why Lisa's running matters. Jasmine has access to fragments of these truths (she reads Marge's shape of "a thing a person cannot set down," she witnesses Bart's hands) but will not systematize them. The chapter will decline interpretation and offer only the shape of what she observes. There may be a moment where she could intrude on Marge or Bart's solitary work, but she will not.

## Signature

- Short, clear sentences often built on a single strong verb: "The house took them in," "She did not stay long."
- Phrases that turn on the distinction between what is and what appears: "the way it was every night," "having stood in too many rooms," "as though it had."
- Repetition of structure across sentences to build a cadence rather than emphasis: parallel actions laid side by side without subordination.
- A tendency to follow a concrete action with a brief reasoning that names a human fact without sentiment: "The house did not need breaking down, and Bart would have done it..." / "She put her hand flat on his dome for a second, the way Mira did, not to do anything to him, just to have her hand on him."
- The phrase "as if" appears at moments where outer and inner nearly touch.

## What the Chapter Will Likely Contain

The setting will probably be either the house during daytime (a domestic arrangement or repair) or the car/warehouse at an in-between moment. Jasmine is the one who drives people, carries sleeping children, manages the household's logistics and its unspoken agreements. The chapter may show her: settling the machines after a lab session; managing Mira's arrival or departure; possibly confronting Burns about the unfinished feeling between them (she has "not let herself know" that his tenderness is owed elsewhere); or being present when one of the other characters (Marge, perhaps) appears at the threshold of the house.

The chapter will open with a concrete arrival or departure, or with her at work in the house. It will close with her at a threshold again — someone leaving, a decision made silently, a light turned off, a door held open — because that is what Jasmine does: she tends the passages between states. The last line will likely leave her standing still, observing, reading the room or the person, the way she does.

There will be no summoning of feeling into words. There will be the weight of understanding what others do not speak.

## Unlikely Elements

- First-person direct address or meta-commentary (that is Lisa's and the narrator's voice)
- Extended soliloquy or reasoning (that is Marge and Bart)
- A shift in Jasmine's fundamental stance toward the household or its machines
- Explanation of what any of the other threads mean or why they matter
