# Prediction for Ch 12

**Confidence: Medium-High**

**Basis: Narrative rotation pattern and voice consistency across the 11-chapter cycle**

## Duration & Structure
Expect moderate length (700-900 words), consistent with Burns's first chapter and the paired patterns established across the thread. The chapter will dwell longest on a single moment or object—the thread's signature move—rather than spanning action over time.

## Register & Diction
The prose will maintain the thread's characteristic compression: clipped syntax ("He had given the speech"), repeated constructions ("the way he..."), and careful parataxis joining clauses without subordination. The register will be measured and concrete, avoiding ornament or metaphor, though metaphor will arrive as observation rather than embellishment. Expect the voice to stay close to sensory fact and behavioral description.

## Concreteness
High specificity required. Objects will ground the chapter: a particular thing (a hand, a rock, a map, the truck's heater, something that needs fixing or breaking down). The narrative will not abstract into reflection but will let the thing itself carry the weight of interiority—the way a man holding an object reveals what he cannot say aloud.

## Interiority
Inner state will be withheld or named only through action. The chapter will not tell the reader what Burns feels; it will show the *shape* of feeling through his hands, his stillness, his choice to stay late or to leave early. One gesture will carry the whole pressure—a hand placed on something, or something set down not quite right. The speech haunting him will be present only as absence, the way it shadows Ch 11.

## Attention & Silence
The chapter will linger on a single detail: Bart's hands, the truck, the boy's drawing, the workshop bench, the desert (maybe), or something Burns is building or unminding. It will move quickly past dialogue. It will not name what is unresolved between Burns and Bart; instead, it will place them in the same room or thought-space and let the silence do the work. What gets named: the precise mechanics of something. What stays quiet: all apology, all confession, all direct addressing of harm.

## Signature Patterns
- Opens with a completed action or an hour after action stops (consistent with Burns's chapters 1 and 11)
- Contains a moment where Burns handles or observes a thing in his hands; the thing will become metaphor only in retrospect, never announced as such
- Ends with him in stillness or motion toward something not yet reached, standing at a threshold (doorway, window, point of decision) or setting something down
- Uses repetition of phrase structure: "the way he...," "the thing he...," "the one thing..."
- Includes an observation about what Jasmine or another character does in parallel, forming a quiet contrast (he cannot land; she lands immediately)
- No dialogue, or dialogue so spare it reads as absence

## Likely Tone & Shape
If the chapter follows Burns forward in time: a quiet reckoning. If it cycles back to the same night (as the thread has permitted before): a different angle on the warehouse moment or the drive home, or a scene between him and Bart that the earlier chapters gestured toward but never showed. Most likely: the workshop in Millcreek, or a moment in the truck, or a moment in a room with his son where the speech's failure becomes unavoidable because they are face-to-face and there is no work left to hide in.

The chapter will end with him suspended—not resolved, but held at the point where the next action has already been decided in his body, even though his mind has not finished admitting it.
