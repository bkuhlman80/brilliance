# Prediction for Ch 6

**Confidence: High** (the pattern is consistent across all five installments)

## Register & Duration
Medium length, likely 4-6 paragraphs. Clipped and precise in the opening gesture. Spare diction throughout, with one sustained moment of sensory detail. Pacing will slow mid-passage and accelerate again at the close, as is signature.

## Opening
Will begin with a concrete action — someone leaving, arriving, or preparing something. The sentence will be simple and direct, establishing location and physical fact. No internal state on page one; behavior first.

## Concreteness & Attention
Heavy on object-level detail: what someone touches, carries, sets down, or encounters. One environment will be rendered closely (kitchen, car, lab space, or home interior). Physical geography matters — the fog, the light, the specific rooms of these houses reappear because they are what the voice notices.

The voice lingers on displacement, on things held, on the small acts of adjustment and placement. It moves quickly through dialogue and emotion. It will skip over what it could have said about feeling and name instead what the body is doing.

## Interiority — What It Will Decline
The chapter will not name what the character is afraid of, hoping for, or regretting — not directly. Inner life arrives only through what persists in the body: a hand that won't move, breath held, the stomach registering what the mind won't say. Quiet avoidance of the word "felt" or "thought." Feelings present as fact, embedded in sensory observation.

## Signature
Short sentences interspersed with longer ones that build through accumulation ("the kettle on its ring, the small ceremony of the flame"). Repetition of words and phrases creates a kind of insistence. The voice circles back to the same preoccupations: being held, holding still, the space between what should happen and what does.

Construction often moves from outside to inside (arriving at a house, entering a room, standing at a threshold). Strong use of the dash. Preference for "did not" over negatives. The word "still" appears frequently and carries weight — both stillness and persistence.

## Likely Content
The chapter will focus on one of the three characters not yet in solo focus at greatest length, or will return to Burns or Lisa with a new specificity. Wherever it lands, it will involve: a threshold or transition, an object or ritual that matters more than the passage acknowledges, and an ending that leaves the character in a room, a car, or a held space—not resolved, but positioned. The closing line will not declare what happens next. It will close the door and leave the character inside it, held by the architecture of their own careful living.

## What Will Not Appear
No large revelations. No summary of why things are as they are. No dialogue that explains. No judgment of the characters' choices or silences. The voice does not editorialize about loneliness, duty, faith, or failure—it describes the posture of someone living inside one of these, and trusts the reader to see the shape.
