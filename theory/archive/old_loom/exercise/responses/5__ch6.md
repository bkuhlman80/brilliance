# Prediction for Ch 7

## Duration & Structure
The next chapter will be **medium length**, roughly 700-900 words — matching the expansive registral range and dwelling-time established across the thread. It will move through a single evening or contained scene rather than a full day arc, but will pause to hold certain moments rather than compress them into summary.

## Register & Signature
The voice will remain **clipped yet colored** — short declarative sentences punctuated by longer, subordinate constructions that fold in physical sensation and emotional architecture. The diction will stay anchored to objects and the body (hand, breath, the shape of holding something). The syntax will continue its habit of negation and exclusion: *not quite*, *without being asked*, *never moved again*, *the trouble not being the paper*.

## Opening
Will likely open with a **named character followed by a simple action** — in the pattern of Chs 1-6: *Burns did*, *Marge cleared*, *Jasmine carried*, *Lisa had*, *She left*, *Jasmine got*. The opening will be grounded in a room or a thing (a car, a kitchen, the lot, a house) before it dwells on interior state. It will be **concrete and low-register**.

## Interiority
Inner state will **remain largely unnamed and left behind behavior**. The character will be observed doing something — waiting, preparing, standing — and the cost or feeling of it will be suggested through what the body does (hands gripping the counter, stillness, the wrong kind of heat). Any feeling will emerge **sideways, through objects or physical symptom**, not declared directly. The pattern strongly predicts continued silence on direct emotion-naming.

## Attention & Silence
The chapter will **linger on a single location or repeated gesture** — the way Ch 1 lingered on Burns in the warehouse, Ch 2 on Marge's kettle, Ch 3 on Jasmine's fog drive. It will **pass over quickly** what doesn't touch the obsession: dialogue will be sparse or absent, other people will be minimized or move through the scene without stopping. The chapter will be **quiet about motivation** — will show a person doing something precise and disciplined without explaining why.

## Concrete Prediction
- **Character voice:** likely **Burns** (returning after two chapters away) or **Lisa** (unvisited since Ch 4) — the pattern alternates focus or tends to return to figures with rooms that hold particular kinds of absence
- **Setting:** a **home interior** — every chapter pivots on how a house holds or fails to hold; likely a room at evening or night, in silence
- **Core image/gesture:** will feature **repeated, small, physical precision** — the way Ch 4 had the measured drink, Ch 2 had the kettle ceremony, Ch 5 had the form on the counter; something done the same way each time, or something set down and worried over
- **What it declines to say:** **why** — the chapter will not explain motive or history; it will show only the shape of an action and the cost inscribed in the body
- **How it ends:** will likely **stop in the middle of an evening, with something held or set down**, the way Ch 6 ends with Jasmine driving three creatures home and Ch 5 ends with Marge standing in the dark below her family's photograph. A small, precise closure that is not resolution.
