# Prediction for Ch 8

## Duration & Structure
Medium-length chapter, similar to Ch 4 and comparable to the others. Will dwell primarily on one character's arrival/passage home or one contained moment of domestic space, consistent with the established rhythm of individual protagonist chapters. Likely 300-500 words, concentrated on a single thread rather than branching.

## Register
The voice will remain clipped and deliberate, resistant to sentimentality. Direct declarative sentences dominate, with occasional longer syntactic stretches built from parallel clauses. No exclamation or rhetorical flourish. Flat affect maintained even when describing moments of dislocation or quiet weight. The prose will continue its practice of stating what is rather than what is felt.

## Concreteness
High concrete specificity. The chapter will orient itself around physical objects, spatial relations, and material details—the signature move of this thread. Expect precise description of how something is set down, turned over, carried, or left behind. Abstract emotional states will be minimized or withheld entirely; instead, the inner life emerges through the selection and arrangement of physical facts.

## Interiority
Inner state will be largely *not named* on the page, following the dominant pattern. The exception is when interiority surfaces through action or bodily response—the stomach registering what the mind won't say (as in Ch 5), the hands revealing what the voice conceals. This chapter likely continues that discipline: feelings appear as effects, not declarations. A character's dislocation or longing will be visible in what they do with their hands, where they look, what they fail to do.

## Attention
The chapter will linger on threshold moments—entering/leaving a space—and on the arrangement of objects within that space. It will move slowly through a few specific details and pass over narrative summary quickly. Likely lingers: how something is set down, the quality of light or temperature in a room, an object that doesn't belong or has been avoided. Passes over quickly: anything that doesn't carry the weight of the larger emotional architecture.

## Signature Features
- Sentences that track the movement or settling of objects
- The phrase "the way [noun] does" as a structural and observational tic
- Verbs of placement: set, carry, hold, leave, stand
- A single photograph, cross, envelope, or inherited object as the moral hinge of the space
- The voice of someone alone in a room full of objects they have chosen or inherited but not made
- Declarative syntax that refuses softness: "There was [nothing/no one/no sign] in [space]"
- The body as a readable surface when the voice refuses interiority
