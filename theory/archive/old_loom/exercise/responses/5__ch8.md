# Prediction for Ch 9

## Voice Profile

This voice operates with precise constraint and refuses sentiment. It stays close to the physical—objects, temperature, the small actions bodies perform—while orbiting interiority without entering it. When inner state appears, it is named only through symptom or refusal: a stomach keeping a vote the mind cast, hands that will not mail a form, a man who stands because standing is what holds him up.

The register is formal and spare, almost liturgical in its cadence. Sentences move through long pauses and strange conjunctions. Time moves in two speeds: the swift, efficient reporting of action, then the slow dwell on what action costs. The voice has a signature precision about objects—a kettle "on loan," a hand "flat on his dome," a form "filled in down to the date"—and reserves its densest language for moments of restraint and holding.

Each chapter attends to one character's private correspondence with their home or dwelling place. The chapters track a pattern: meeting disperses, character moves through world, arrives at solitude, stands in a moment of interior pressure that does not resolve. The pressure appears as physical resistance—standing as what holds you up, stomach as body's revolt, hands that cannot move. The voice does not name the feelings these postures suggest; it only shows the posture and what the posture refuses.

The attention pattern is consistent: what lingers gets the dense, recursive language; what the character passes over fast gets reported in a beat. Silence is where the voice is most present—what Burns does not know he is doing in Mira's doorway, what the form will do if mailed, whether Marge will mail it. The chapters accumulate a shared ecology: the warehouse with its late light, the fog, the warm house, Bramble the bot, the family photograph on the wall, the kettle, the form unmailed.
