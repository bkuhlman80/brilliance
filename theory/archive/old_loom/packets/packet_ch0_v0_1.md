**Background — make-up and technology of the wind-up bots.** The voice editor does not have to render any of the following, but if they describe the bots in detail, they cannot contradict these facts.

- Each bot is roughly 10 cm across (about the size of a computer mouse), low-slung, with a domed top. A wind-up key sits on top of each bot, sticking up a few centimeters.
- Each bot has paired heat-transducer and movement-actuator components visible on the chassis surface, in different positions around the chassis.
- The bots look almost identical externally — same chassis, same dome, same wind-up key — but their internal pair seeds differ; the differing pair patterns produce different twitch behaviors when the bots are wound and placed on a warm floor.
- The bots run on the temperature gradient between the warm radiant-heated floor (in thermal contact with the bot's bottom) and the cooler room air (in contact with the bot's top); they cannot run on cold floor or in uniform ambient heat.
- The bots have no battery, no sensors, no finite-state machine, no central coordinator, no memory, no display, and no lexicon. The household reads each bot's condition off its body: twitching means wound and on the gradient; still means run down or off the gradient.
- Each bot has one wind-up crank, which engages an internal clockwork spring; the spring's tension decays over the week and the bot goes still until rewound, setting up a weekly winding rhythm for the household.

Specific temperature ranges (floor surface roughly 25–29°C, room air 20–22°C, sustained 5–10°C gradient), Seebeck-effect physics, and other deeper technology details are background, not required in the prose.

## Poetic — After Chapter 0 (Evolution as Learning)

*The chapter closes with its own poetic, attached to the bottom of Ch 0. Reading-order placement: end of Ch 0, before Interlude 0→1 (which is vignette-only — no lunch, and the poetic now closes Ch 0 rather than opening the interlude). Source pool: `trellis_framework doc` § "After Ch 0 — Evolution as learning," with substance in § "Evolution as Learning" under Cybernetic-Inheritance Foundations.*

- **Substance source:** `trellis_framework doc` § "Evolution as Learning"
- **Voice:** Brandy's reflective register, at slightly loosened distance from the Pitch. Reflections *about* what the team is going to discover. They don't have any of this yet. She does.
- **Stance:** Brandy has just walked away from the Pitch. She gave them the cards and the runway and no theory. She is thinking, privately, about what they are going to find when they start looking.

**Substance to lyricize.** The poetic carries the content below. Voice handles structure, rhythm, and ornament; what must land is the claim-set.

- MANDATORY: **The headline claim.** Evolution and learning are not analogous — they are formally equivalent. Different instantiations of the same algorithmic principles. Selection IS computation; lineages compute across generations the way learning systems compute across training cycles. (Watson & Szathmáry 2016.)

- MANDATORY: **The two formal equivalences, both conditional.**
  - *Bayesian–asexual.* In an asexual population — genotypes that can't be decomposed into independently-fit components — natural selection IS Bayesian updating. The parent generation is the prior; the environment is the likelihood; the next generation is the posterior. (Harper; Shalizi.)
  - *Multiplicative-weights–sexual.* In a sexual population at linkage equilibrium — no epistasis between loci — selection on individual alleles IS the multiplicative-weights update algorithm. The same algorithm used in online learning. (Chastain et al.)
  - The two regimes get two different algorithms because they ARE two different kinds of update rule.

- MANDATORY: **The accumulation mechanism.** The computation stores in gene-regulatory architecture, the way a neural net stores training statistics in its weights (Watson et al. 2014). Past phenotypes that survived become attractors of development — the lineage re-expresses them under appropriate conditions. Combinations of stored phenotypic modules produce novel phenotypes fit for environments the lineage hasn't yet faced. The memory generalizes.

- MANDATORY: **The unit that learns.** The lineage. Not the individual. The individual doesn't update — the lineage updates across generations. The genome is the substrate the memory writes onto.

- LOAD-BEARING: **The epistemic-status hedge.** The formal equivalences are established mathematical results. The developmental-memory and generalization claims are demonstrated in simulation; biological generality is an outstanding question. *(Brandy can carry the hedge openly or compress it; voice's call. If compressed, lose the conditional language rather than the qualifier on the developmental-memory claim — that one matters more.)*

**Background — why this poetic is the book's first.** The Evolution-as-Learning result is what licenses the framework's cross-substrate claim downstream. Wherever there is inheritance plus selection plus an accumulating memory, the same learning machinery runs in different goo. The team doesn't yet know the framework will reach into the neuronal and symbolic lines on the strength of this result. Brandy does. The poetic plants the seed; the team will find it later. Voice does not need to make this connection explicit on the page — the poetic carries the substance, not the framework's downstream use of it.
