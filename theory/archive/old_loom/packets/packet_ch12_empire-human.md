### Clade cards, breakthrough, capacities (Bart + Burns)

- MANDATORY: the Empire-Human clade card *(ethological — the "creature," not the robot)*. The Empire-Human is a human parameterized by imperial collective context: the imagined-community imperial subject. The empire-clade contains territorial, maritime, and ideological empires — all sharing the move of binding many cities and colonies under one universalizing framework (Rome, Han China, the Achaemenid Persians, the Caliphates, the British Empire). The empire forms by symbiosis: cities and their hinterlands supply the administrative base, colonies supply territorial extension. Its "body plan" is the universalizing frame itself — the thing that holds together populations with no shared kinship, language, or local history, supplying a common identity kinship cannot. Reinforcing cycle: universal religion, codified imperial law, monetary integration, and trade networks that cross city borders.
- MANDATORY: the breakthrough — **Trust**: universalizing frameworks that let peoples with no shared kinship rely on one another. Its narrative engine is the Stories (Planner) capacity — Harari's narrative collective identity — operationalized through cultural memory — Assmann's distinction between communicative memory (living conversation, roughly an 80-year horizon) and cultural memory (sacred texts, rituals, monuments, institutionally maintained across generations). Empire cohesion requires cultural memory because the empire outlasts any communicative chain.
- MANDATORY: the four capacities at Empire-Human *(illustrative mirrors here, fuller examples in Section D)*:
  - **Conquering** (Reactor, Modeler absent): daily-cycle imperial action — reciprocal in tribute, stable in territory, universalizing in extending the frame, run without engaging novel representation. The structural inversion of forager risk-pooling: where the band distributed calories to average risk, the empire concentrates everything at the center. Pooling becomes hoarding — the Mongols took roughly a quarter of the world's land in about 80 years.
  - **Standardizing** (Conservator, Reviser absent): the mature loop without meta-update, scaled empire-wide — enforced uniformity across populations who never meet, through enforcement infrastructure (inspectors, prefects, governors). Qin Shi Huang's unification of script, axle-widths, weights, and currency (221 BCE); Rome's standardized brick, road widths, and Latin. (The City standardizes within one urban core; the Empire enforces it across territories.)
  - **Stories** (Planner, Effector absent): strategic deliberation about how to be recognized at imperial scale — identity constructed and projected rather than inherited. Augustus's centrally directed image program (Zanker): one face on coins from Britain to Syria, busts in every forum. *(This is the capacity the brand-tee Bramblogue instantiates.)*
  - **Extracting** (Pursuer, Regulator absent): goal-pursuit plus representation plus revision without stabilization — systems built to pull resources out of conquered land and people. Potosí silver under the mita corvée (roughly 14,000 laborers a year for nearly three centuries).
- MANDATORY: Burns's deep-time context. The clade window runs from the Old Babylonian Empire (~1800 BCE), with Akkad (~2300 BCE) as imperial precursor, to the onset of the coal-and-steam Industrial Revolution (~1750 CE) — roughly 3,500 years, beginning in Mesopotamia and recurring independently across the Mediterranean, South and East Asia, Mesoamerica, the Andes, West Africa, and the Eurasian steppe. The anchor for Universality is the Axial Age (~800–200 BCE), when universalizing religious and philosophical traditions arrive.
