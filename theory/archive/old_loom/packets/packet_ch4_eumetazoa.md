## Clade block

### Clade cards, breakthrough, capacities (Bart + Burns)

- MANDATORY: **The clade.** Eumetazoa are the first animals with true differentiated tissues and a nervous system: cnidarians (jellyfish, sea anemones, corals, hydra) as the canonical exemplar, plus the bilaterian stem. The category excludes sponges (no neurons, no gut). The defining structural fact is differentiated tissue layers coordinated into a body. Source: `clade_reference_genetic doc` Cell 4.
- MANDATORY: **The breakthrough is Neurons.** A neuron is a modified epithelial cell specialized for electrical signaling. The milestone is not intelligence; it is the first architecture capable of behavioral flexibility at the millisecond timescale. The neurotransmitter vocabulary is ancient: cnidarian nerve nets already use glutamate, GABA, and neuropeptides, the same core molecular language as a human brain. What changed since is architecture, not the molecular hardware.
- MANDATORY: **The four capacities at Eumetazoa** (`Inheritance_Lines_genetic doc`):
  - Pursuer — **Ingestion.** The first animals to eat the way animals eat: coordinated prey-capture and digestion in a dedicated compartment (the hydra's harpoon-cells, tentacle-haul, gastric cavity). Mouths, guts, and the predator's gaze start here.
  - Planner — **Allostatic Regulation.** With nerve tissue, an animal can hold different "normal" body states for different situations rather than defending one fixed set-point. Brain-predicted, set-point-adjusting regulation. Allostasis arrives the moment nerve tissue does.
  - Reactor — **Non-Associative Learning.** Habituation and sensitization: a hydra poked gently and repeatedly stops reacting; shocked then touched, it over-reacts. Tweaks to nerve-net excitability, not stored associations. The simplest learning a nervous system can do.
  - Conservator — **Intracellular Transport.** The eukaryote's kinesin/dynein motors, now asked to move cargo down axons that can be meters long. Same machinery, new distances; no architectural revision.
- MANDATORY: **Burns's deep-time context** (`burns_deep_time_genetic doc`): shallow continental-shelf seas, soft sediment, microbial mats; the world recovering and warming after the Cryogenian Snowball Earths; oxygen rising through the Neoproterozoic toward thresholds animal metabolism needs. The Ediacaran biota first (Dickinsonia, Charnia at Mistaken Point and Ediacara), then the Cambrian explosion delivering nearly all modern animal phyla. Sites: Doushantuo, Chengjiang, Burgess Shale.

**The Levin beat (Bart, capped).** Bart owns this and runs hot.

- MANDATORY: The empirical core — multicellularity is *electrically coordinated between cells*. Membrane-voltage states and gap-junction coupling act as instructive signals that shape what tissue builds where (Levin's frog and planaria work). This is a pre-neural control layer; neurons are the fast, specialized version of an electrical coordination that already organizes the body. Present it as the layer the breakthrough (Neurons) specializes out of.
- MANDATORY: The vivid demo — **Anthrobots**: clusters of adult human cells that self-assemble into small motile constructs that move on their own cilia and can bridge neurons in a dish. Bart thinks this is the coolest thing in biology.
- MANDATORY: One skeptic line holds the cognitive overlay at arm's length. The contested part is the *interpretation* ("memory," "goals," "intelligence," cognition all the way down), not the findings. The clean handle: Levin's own framing goes all the way to cognition, which is Lisa's gradient pillar, not Bart's self-model position — so Bart's hero argues *Lisa's* side of the sentience question. Bart geeks out on the engineering while rejecting the philosophy attached to it. Voice can play the irony; keep it to a beat, no critic roll-call.

## Behavior cluster

- **A hydra poked gently and repeatedly stops reacting; shocked then lightly touched, it over-reacts** (Rushforth 1973; Kass-Simon & Pierobon 2007). — Habituation and sensitization, the simplest nervous-system learning. — [segments: capacities (Reactor); leaf-blower vignette echo]
- **A hydra fires harpoon-cells (cnidocytes) when prey brushes its tentacles, tethers it with toxin, and hauls it to the gut** (Beckmann & Özbek 2012; Holstein & Tardent 1984). — Prey-capture as a coordinated whole-body act; animal feeding begins. — [segments: capacities (Pursuer/Ingestion); clade card]
- **Cassiopea jellyfish — no central brain — show a clear sleep state at night**: pulsation drops ~⅓, response threshold rises, and they rebound after sleep deprivation (Nath, Bedbrook et al. 2017). — Sleep is a nerve-tissue phenomenon, not a brain phenomenon; it begins with neurons. — [segments: Arousal-Type tendency; capacities (Planner); breakthrough]
- **Box jellyfish do coordinated prey-capture with elaborated visual targeting.** — The predator's gaze starts at Eumetazoa. — [segments: capacities (Pursuer); clade card]
