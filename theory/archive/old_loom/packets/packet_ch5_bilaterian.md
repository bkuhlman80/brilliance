### Component 1 — Clade cards, breakthrough, capacities, CPG (Bart + Burns)

- MANDATORY — **The cell:** Bilaterian — bilateral body plan plus a centralized nervous system, first radiating in the Cambrian. Defining substrate: the monoamine system — **dopamine, serotonin, and the tyramine/octopamine group** (the invertebrate biogenic amines; not vertebrate norepinephrine) — setting affective state on a valence × arousal plane that biases arousal and movement toward or away from stimuli. Motivation introduced: **Impetus** (Effector). *(Framework-side note: which amine controls which affective state is lineage-specific, not a fixed code — the breakthrough cluster anchors the empirical story in the nematode and uses the leech and flatworm to show the lineage-specificity. The matching `clade_reference` edit — drop "norepinephrine," and drop any single-amine-to-quadrant assignment — is an outstanding CC task.)*
- MANDATORY — **The breakthrough, Attention:** within-lifetime selection — candidate movements compete and valence picks the winner. Its visible expression is **Steering** (the Pursuer capacity): the first time nervous tissue could orient the whole body toward a goal or away from a threat — directed, head-first locomotion with a front-end bias (cephalization). This is what the head and forward sensor suite are *for*.
- MANDATORY — **CPG (Bart's talking point):** locomotion runs on a central pattern generator — a circuit producing rhythmic gait without rhythmic input, the real bilaterian motor solution (invertebrate ganglia; the lamprey spinal cord). In Bramble it's the frozen reflex layer Bart built and froze — how the body walks without deliberating each step.
- MANDATORY — **The four capacities (the "cool tricks"), each tied to its shape:**
  - **Steering (Pursuer):** a neuromodulatory state biasing "go check it out." Menu anchor: *C. elegans* neuromodulatory foraging-and-exploration state (Stern et al. 2017); the literal go-toward image is *Platynereis* larval positive phototaxis (Jékely et al. 2008 ‡).
  - **Associative Learning (Reactor):** pairing two events so one predicts the other — with no model of *why*, just that it does. Menu anchor: *Aplysia* gill-withdrawal conditioning (Kandel; Hawkins et al. 1983). Founding-taxon companions: *C. elegans* salt-starvation conditioning (Saeki et al. 2001 ‡) and planarian familiarization memory surviving head regeneration (Shomrat & Levin 2013 ‡). This is the cell's signature capacity — the dark-fear demo and the Pavlov round both unpack it.
  - **Allostatic Regulation (Planner):** prediction-based regulation running several homeostatic axes at once. Menu anchor: *C. elegans* lethargus — a sleep-like state with reduced responsiveness, an elevated arousal threshold, and homeostatic rebound after deprivation (Raizen et al. 2008 ‡). Also grounds the settle-to-charge/consolidation beat.
  - **Inhibition (Conservator):** holding a default program back by context — ready to act, choosing not to. Menu anchor: *C. elegans* tyramine-mediated suppression of head oscillation during the touch-evoked escape — descending neuromodulatory inhibition of a default motor program (Pirri et al. 2009); alternative framing: the leech swim-vs-crawl decision distributed across competing neuron populations (Briggman et al. 2005 ‡).
- MANDATORY — **Burns's deep-time:** the Cambrian world where directed movement under affective valence first paid off — bilateral, centralized-nervous-system animals radiating into a newly navigable world. Burns's paleontology register; vignette options from Section D's deep-time cluster.

### Component 2 — The associative-learning demonstration (the dark-fear)

- MANDATORY — **The mechanism:** run in light-mode (solar harvest), darkness means no harvest = energy-starvation. Darkness repeatedly experienced during starvation acquires negative valence; Bramble then **steers** away from dark and toward light. It becomes afraid of the dark. - MANDATORY — **The channel:** no one trains this. The unit's own internal energy-state revalues a co-present perception, exactly as the worm's hunger revalues salt (Section D, *C. elegans* — Saeki et al. 2001). This is the **environmental** conditioning channel — distinct from the family/thumbs channel. Two routes now write valence: the family (thumbs → attended perception) and the world (internal state → co-present perception).
- MANDATORY — **Persistence across mode:** once learned in light-mode, the dark-avoidance **persists when Bramble is switched to heat-mode**, where darkness is energy-irrelevant. The consolidated negative valence sits on the *perception* of dark, not on the current harvest mode, so the fear outlives its functional basis. *(This is the fact Burns's sentience vote turns on — see Trellis.)*
- MANDATORY — **Persistence across sleep:** the dark-fear holds across the settle-to-charge sleep, because consolidation is on (since Eumetazoa).

### Component 3 — The Pavlov round

*Four labmates each explain one associative-learning heuristic. Mechanisms are MANDATORY (Voice has no framework KB). The assignment is locked. The heuristic→function structure stays backstage — not spoken.*

- MANDATORY — **Burns explains Latent Inhibition:** a cue seen over and over with nothing following gets flagged as background and filtered; when it later co-occurs with something that matters, it's already filtered and can't form the new association — novel cues keep full attention. (Pre-exposure lowers associability.)
- MANDATORY — **Lisa explains Overshadowing:** when several cues precede one event, the strongest cue takes the credit and the quieter (also-predictive) cues are overshadowed — competitive associative learning; you lose the weak signals but don't waste capacity on all of them. (Mackintosh, 1970s.)
- MANDATORY — **Bart explains Eligibility Trace:** the cue in the short window just before the event (sub-second to ~a second) gets the credit; a predictor several seconds back is "too cold" to connect, so long-horizon links are invisible. *Clade-accuracy note (already correct in the source):* this is cue-recency in **Pavlovian prediction** — which cue gets credit for predicting the outcome — **not** operant action-credit. The operant/temporal-credit sense is the Vertebrate breakthrough; keep it out.
- MANDATORY — **Marge explains Blocking:** once cue A reliably predicts event X, a new cue B co-occurring with A never gets learned as a predictor — A blocks B. Efficiency: don't rewrite what you already predict. (Kamin, 1960s; replicated across nervous systems — including the planarian, Prados et al. 2013 ‡.)
- MANDATORY — **Marge's first structural rhyme** (the Ch 5 fallacy-arc beat, delivered here, carried to her Trellis slot): right after Burns's Latent Inhibition, Marge observes it's the same shape as the **Blank Slate** — both make attention/behavior a function of contact-and-exposure history. She **files it, does not develop it.** *(This is the Ch 5 fallacy content — no separate fallacy lecture; the Blank Slate was named at Ch 2.)*

- MANDATORY — **The geometry:** the four circles are the four cybernetic functions — Effector, Regulator, Modeler, Reviser. Their overlaps generate fifteen regions, sorted by how many circles overlap (the more functions a region needs, the more specific and the rarer it is):

  - two adjacent circles (×4) → a **tendency** — the dispositional expression of two neighboring motivations;

  - three circles (×4) → a **capacity** — a synthesis of three motivations the unit can perform;
  - all four, the center (×1) → the **breakthrough** — the synthesis that needs all four.

### The Trellis framework, part 2 — the line boundary and the goo change (Lisa)

*The framework's hardest-to-grasp move, by its own description. This is what makes the Ch 5 retirement different in kind from the other three.*

- MANDATORY — **Distinguish this retirement from the within-line ones.** Persistence has retired and Impetus installs — but this is not the kind of swap that happens *inside* a line. The other neuronal retirements (Ch 6–8) are within-line elaboration. This one is a boundary, because Bilaterian is the clade that kicks off the neuronal line: the Effector knob in slot 1 swaps from a genetic to a neuronal knob, and that resets what the line is selected on.
- MANDATORY *(load-bearing — preserve the formulation)* — **Why an Effector swap is a line boundary:** the Effector motivation names what selection acts on, so when it changes, the line's selection target changes. The genetic line is selected on **differential reproductive success** — Boltzmann's bookkeeping. The neuronal line is selected on **forward action under affective valence**: *forward* (directed, head-first movement toward a goal or away from a threat — Steering, not drift) governed *under* a monoamine good/bad signal. Regulator, Modeler, and Reviser swaps leave the selection target intact, so they're elaboration within a line. **The new regime is layered on, not a replacement:** genetic/reproductive selection keeps running underneath (neuronal clades still differ genetically and are still selected on reproduction) — the neuronal line adds a second selection target on top of it.
- MANDATORY *(load-bearing — preserve the formulation)* — **The goo change:** the genetic line works on **Boltzmann goo** — thermodynamic disequilibrium. The neuronal line works on **affective goo** — valence-and-motivation structure produced by sensorimotor reentrant signaling and shaped by associative learning. ## Breakthrough material cluster (Attention)

*Substantiates the monoamine valence/arousal system and the mechanistic content of Steering. The organizing structure is the **valence × arousal affective-state plane** — the two axes Bramble's affect implements (the valence and arousal scalars on the build sheet). The plane's four states are real and documented; what is **not** conserved across the founding phyla is which amine controls which state — that is lineage-specific, and serotonin sits in opposite quadrants in different animals. The amine-control findings below (the negative-valence states) are Smithers-cleared and carry no ‡; origin/phylogenomics and the Steering-minimum anchor remain ‡.*

- **The valence × arousal affective-state plane** *(Bennett, A Brief History of Intelligence (2023), Figs 3.2 & 3.4 — theory-level; the framework's Steering source)*. Affect sits on two axes: **valence** (positive = facilitate feeding, digestion, reproduction; negative = inhibit them) and **arousal** (movement vs. none). Four states: *exploiting* (positive/high — slow movement, frequent turns, local search), *escaping* (negative/high — fast movement, infrequent turns, relocate), *satiation* and *chronic suppression* (low arousal). *Bennett also pins one neuromodulator per quadrant (dopamine / serotonin / adrenaline); treat that as his idealization, not a conserved code — the founding-taxa data below show the controlling amine is lineage-specific. The plane is the transferable claim, and it is what Bramble's two scalars implement.* *[segments: clade cards; signal/compute.]*
- **The nematode is the clean affective-amine anchor** *(Sawin, Ranganathan & Horvitz 2000, Neuron 26:619; Pirri, McPherson, Donnelly, Francis & Alkema 2009, Neuron 62:526; Dag et al. 2023, Cell)*. In *C. elegans* three amines occupy three states: **dopamine** drives on-food basal slowing / local search (positive valence — exploitation); **serotonin** drives enhanced slowing, dwelling, and feeding suppression (the satiation / low-arousal state); **tyramine**, released from the RIM neuron onto the LGC-55 channel, drives the touch-evoked escape — suppressing head oscillation and promoting backward locomotion. A coherent, fully-cited single-organism map. *(Tyramine is a tyramine/octopamine-family amine, so the worm's escape amine is consistent with the invertebrate adrenergic family — but the leech is not; next.)* *[segments: clade cards; signal/compute; capacities (Inhibition, Approach).]* **[nematode]**
- **Serotonin's role is not conserved — the polarity flip** *(leech: Willard 1981, J Neurosci 1:936; Nusbaum & Kristan 1986, J Exp Biol 122:277; O'Gara et al. 1991, J Neurosci 11:96; dopamine/crawl runner-up: Puhl & Mesce 2008, J Neurosci)*. In the leech, serotonin is the **escape** amine — it initiates and sustains the swim that carries the animal away from disturbance (dopamine drives the competing crawl and blocks swimming). So serotonin occupies the escape quadrant in the leech and the satiation quadrant in the worm — opposite corners of the same plane. *Load-bearing illustration: same amine, same plane, opposite role by lineage. Concrete biology behind the framework's choice to name functions by role, not by molecule — the molecule isn't the invariant. Available to Voice as a Trellis / Burns beat; not mandated.* *[segments: clade cards; signal/compute; Trellis tie-in.]* **[annelid]**
- **The flatworm's negative-valence states are non-aminergic** *(escape/scrunching: Cochet-Escartin, Mickolajczyk & Collins 2015, Phys Biol 12:056010; Sabry et al. 2019, PLoS One 14:e0226104; suppression: Buttarelli, Pontieri, Margotta & Palladini 2000, Comp Biochem Physiol C 125:225)*. In planaria the escape gait (scrunching) is triggered by TRPA1/TRPV channels and is muscle-driven; the documented hypokinesia is cholinergic. Planarian amines (serotonin, dopamine) are pro-locomotory, not suppressive. *The flatworm contributes no amine to either negative-valence state — the boundary case.* *[segments: clade cards.]* **[flatworm]**
- **The monoamine system is a bilaterian innovation** *(Feuda et al. 2023, Nat Commun 14:3284 ‡)*. The genes for producing, modulating, and detecting monoamines originate in the bilaterian stem group — the system is a bilaterian novelty, plausibly a driver of the Cambrian radiation. *Grounds the cell's defining substrate and its deep-time timing.* *[segments: clade cards; signal/compute; Burns deep-time.]*
- **The bilaterian biogenic amines, and the norepinephrine correction** *(Bauknecht & Jékely 2017, BMC Biol 15:6 ‡)*. Norepinephrine, octopamine, and tyramine receptor systems coexisted in the bilaterian common ancestor; the invertebrate adrenergic-family amines are octopamine/tyramine, not vertebrate norepinephrine. *Fixes the amine identity at Component 1; it does not assign any amine to a behavioral state — that control is lineage-specific (above).* *[segments: signal/compute; clade cards.]*
- **Steering at its founding minimum** *(Jékely et al. 2008, Nature 456:395 ‡)*. In the *Platynereis* annelid larva, light-sensor neurons synapse directly onto the locomotor cilia — no interneurons; illuminating one eyespot slows the cilia on that side and the larva rotates toward the light. Directed locomotion toward a goal in the simplest sensorimotor circuit known. *Cleanest illustration of Steering itself.* *[segments: clade cards / breakthrough; CPG tie-in; signal/compute.]* **[annelid]**
- **Cephalization / CNS centralization in bilaterians** *(Holland et al. 2013, EvoDevo 4:27 ‡)*.
