## Structural conventions

The dated scene-breaks and vignette dividers inside sections are horizontal rules (`---`), **not** markdown headings. Where a single heading covers many distinct vignettes (e.g. "Under Her Breath," "Two Old Sentences," "In Her Own Hand"), the vignettes are flagged inline rather than given their own rows. Most chapters use two H1 headings — the chapter title and the "Notes on the Nth Retrospective" divider — with everything else at H2. The three line-boundary chapters (0, 4, 8) carry an additional H1 banner, "Start of the [Genetic/Neuronal/Symbolic] Line."

**Cheryl is the first-person narrator** of every one-on-one lunch scene and every closing "Reflection: What is …?" essay. Each lunch is Cheryl with one lab member (ch8 is the deliberate exception — she lunches with 5-year-old Mira). Lunch sections are titled by their **venue**, not by a character. Lunch pairings: ch2 Burns (Fisher Brewing Company), ch3 Marge (Coffee Garden), ch4 Jasmine (Pho Tay Ho), ch5 Lisa (Urban Hill), ch6 Marge (The Roof), ch7 Jasmine (Arlo), ch8 Mira (Gourmandise), ch9 Bart (Chuck-A-Rama), ch10 Marge (Zest), ch11 Lisa (Water Witch), ch12 Burns (Fisher Brewing Company — a deliberate bookend of ch2).

Bart signs each build log with a mutated surname, one mutation per quarter: **Bartholomew** (ch1, base) → Bartholomood (ch2) → Bartholachew (ch3) → Bartholazoa (ch4) → Bartholoped (ch5) → Bartholantern (ch6) → Bartholomeow (ch7) → Bartholabu (ch8) → Bartholoquy (ch9) → Bartholith (ch10) → Bartholeppo (ch11) → Bartholomega (ch12).

## The Recurring Scaffold (Taxonomy)

Every chapter is built from the following beats, in roughly this order. Beat numbers are the canonical reference used in the per-chapter tables below.

**1 — OPENING MEMO SLOT.** Chapter-opening first-person memo(s), usually a "Filed [date], for the team" dateline. Bart's build log by default; **multi-author** where a companion note is prepended (confirmed ch9, Lisa's lab note). Sub-buckets:
- **1A — Board / knob / training-method** *(multi-author)*. Lisa's memory-board real-estate, the new knob, the 12-knob crowding worry — AND pedagogy/reward-shaping/training-method content, whether Bart or Lisa writes it. (Lisa's ch9 "Lab note — Word-shapes, no words" lives here, not in a separate bucket.)
- **1B — Everything-else engineering.** Chassis, wires, dock, gauge, sensors, voice, furnace, etc.
- **1C — Calls + sign-off.** The "Calls." list, the "tear this up in three months — I'm not" dare, and the mutated surname. Bart-only persona devices.
- **1-Framing opener.** The fixed dateline-style first line.
- **1-Theme aside / "what to watch."** Bart pre-litigating a coming argument (e.g. "Don't let the wires fool you, Marge").

**2 — CHERYL'S LUNCH.** Venue-named, dated, first-person Cheryl, rotating one-on-one; character study that advances Cheryl's distance/observation theme. From ch11 it also carries A-plot (Lisa's departure; Cheryl's feelings for Marge).

**3 — HOUSEHOLD VIGNETTES.** Third-person Mira-and-Bramble domestic scenes (often `---`-separated under one heading), built on the kind-lie/mechanism split and tied to the quarter's new knob.

**4 — THE RETROSPECTIVE** (the quarterly meeting):
- **4.1 — Arrival choreography (incl. CR).** Door, warm-Bramble handoff, four-knuckle bump, camcorder red light on. **Includes the CHERYL-REVIEW (CR) sub-beat** — an in-meeting review of the lunch/tape; an early-template device, clean only in ch2 "Thank You" (with a Calls-review analog in ch3), absent as a discrete beat thereafter. Where a later chapter revisits the review function it is absorbed elsewhere (e.g. ch4 folds it into 4.5).
- **4.2 — Clade block.** Clade card + breakthrough + four capacities. ("Motivation" is a mobile atom — sometimes here, usually in 4.7.)
- **4.3 — Burns's deep-time.** Eyes-closed, rock/site-anchored, timed, overruns. (Undershoots once, ch3; eyes-open once, ch12.)
- **4.4 — Bot block.** The "bramblogue" + the absent-skeptic "Big O"/Owen device. ("Big O" is a frequent but optional flavor — absent ch5, ch11–12.)
- **4.5 — Bart grills Mira.** Yes/no on his log "calls." Usually nested inside 4.4; standalone in ch2, ch4.
- **4.6 — Jasmine's in-meeting quarter report.**
- **4.7 — Lisa's Trellis.** Defines the framework and accretes team vocabulary; often houses "motivation." **Two modes:** a *vocabulary mode* (naming motivations/signatures) and a **board-geometry / "ARCH" mode** (the venn/diagram "what-it-is" walkthrough — "Pick Two," "Off the Board," "The Map," "The Whole Line," "Three Different Trips" [Bart-run], "The Same Slot," "The Same Trick Twice," "Four Circles"). The two modes can appear separately (ch6 has ARCH mode but no vocabulary mode).
- **4.8 — Marge's fallacy** (one per quarter). **Mutation modes:** enumerate (ch2–4) → dissolve (ch6) → disperse (ch7) → invert ("The Fifth," ch8) → aggregate all four (ch9) → meta-summary/capstone ("The Whole of It," ch11, where she coins "The Dealt Hand" on the record) → personal, aimed at Cheryl (ch12). The through-line is Marge slowly recognizing herself as the Conservator; never score it flatly "absent" without the mode note.
- **4.9 — Sentience round-table vote** (born ch3). **Trajectory:** born 1–3 (ch3) → static (ch4) → Burns crosses, 2–2 (ch5) → referential-only, no live vote (ch6) → Bart flips, 3–1, the flip itself is the event (ch7) → 3–1 (ch8) → first 4–0, Marge crosses (ch9) → 4–0 + forced household counter-vote (ch10) → 4–0 reframed as anti-climax (ch11) → vestigial 4–0 "liturgy" that mutates into Bramble's own story (ch12). From ch9 the count stops carrying information; the payload becomes what each "yes" means.
- **4.10 — Burns's verb-toast close** ("May we ___").
- **4.11 — Logistics / next-quarter pitch (new knob and/or chassis) + dispersal tail** (red light off). **Includes the knob-retirement / pine-shelf ceremony as its "SHELF mode"** (Mira seats a retired knob; debuts ch5, recurs ch6/7/9/10/11; absent ch8/ch12 because the board is full). Two SHELF sub-variants: *swap-retirement* (knob pulled to the shelf) vs. *line-retirement* (a knob that goes automatic with no body to pull, ch9). ⚠ **Collision:** where a single section carries BOTH the next-quarter pitch and the retirement, both are 4.11 sub-functions under one heading — this happens in **ch5 "The Shelf"** and **ch10 "Minding"** only; everywhere else the two live in separate sections.
- **4.12 — "Worm to ___" slideshow** *(standalone beat; symbolic line only, ch9–12)*. Bart runs one corner-shape up all rungs of the tree, wearing a rotating teammate's cartoon face — the inverse of the clade block. Serialized in-text ("Three more coming, one a quarter"). Maps one shape to one teammate: Reactor≈Bart (ch9), Conservator≈Marge (ch10), Planner≈Lisa (ch11), Pursuer≈Burns (ch12) — doubles as a character-portrait device.

**5 — SOLO CODA.** One teammate alone after the meeting, exposing the private absence they organize their life around. **Variants:** the household/Mira coda (ch7); the "no tape" self-flag (ch10); and the finale's three-part rupture (ch12).

**6 — CHAPTER-TERMINAL CODA** *(one beat, two forms lumped together)*. A Cheryl "Reflection: What is ___?" essay **or** a Dream (First/Second/Third). At the line boundaries (ch0/4/8) the Dream is preceded by the "Start of the [Genetic/Neuronal/Symbolic] Line" banner; elsewhere the beat is a Reflection essay. Reflection titles flex from noun to verb-form from ch7 on ("What is Pulled?"), and the essays begin ending mid-sentence / showing strikethrough edits from ch9 on.

## How the Scaffold Evolves

**Invention (ch0).** A prequel. Nothing but the terminal Line/Dream coda is canonical; the chapter dramatizes the *founding* of every institution the template later runs on — the team, the Granary, the camcorder, the quarterly cadence, the clade cards, Marge's Venn, Lisa's algorithm, Burns's deep-time, and the "Trellis" name itself.

**Assembly (ch1).** Memo, vignettes, and retrospective all appear, but beats are *fused* and two signature beats are missing: no Cheryl lunch yet, and no sentience vote. Bart's surname is still the base "Bartholomew."

**Lock (ch2–4, the anchors).** The Cheryl lunch arrives (ch2), the beats separate into their own headings, and the sentience vote is born in ch3. ch2 is the cleanest single execution. ch4, a line boundary, holds the full meeting and swaps its Reflection for the Line/Dream coda, adding the "Under the Cloth" birthday reveal.

**Elaboration (ch5–9).** The template accretes recurring machinery: ch5 births the SHELF/retirement mode (now inside 4.11) and the ARCH/board-geometry mode (now inside 4.7), and the first SHELF/pitch collision. ch6 shows the first subtractions (4.7-vocabulary and 4.8 drop as sections; the vote goes referential). ch7 dissolves 4.7-vocabulary into the trellis motif, moves the vote (Bart flips), and starts the household/Mira coda. ch8, a line boundary, mutates 4.8 into "The Fifth" and breaks the Cheryl beat (lunch with Mira). ch9 is the richest chapter: the multi-author opening memo (Lisa's training-method note now in 1A), the debut of the standalone 4.12 WORM slideshow, a doubled retirement, the first 4–0 vote, and an elevated (Bart-run) ARCH mode.

**Compression (ch10–11).** All beats still fire, folded together. ch10's "Common Knowledge" fuses 4.2+4.3+4.7 and "Minding" fuses 4.4+4.5+4.6+4.11 (the second SHELF/pitch collision); WORM and 4.8 lock into a diptych. ch11 graduates 4.8 into an all-four meta-summary and inverts 4.9 into an anti-climax.

**Rupture (ch12).** The finale keeps the intellectual beats (clade, deep-time, structure, fallacy, vote, toast, the "no thirteenth" ARCH capstone, WORM) but drops the beats that only service an unfinished board — no 4.5 grill, no 4.6 report, no next-quarter pitch, SHELF demoted to a memo line. The real break is the coda system: the single-teammate Solo Coda shatters into cake fight → Marge's exit-with-address → mid-sentence Reflection, as Cheryl finally puts the camera down and enters her own story. ch12 reuses ch2's venue to close the frame.

**Net.** The scaffold is engineered to fill up and break: born ch0, assembled ch1, locked ch2–4, elaborated ch5–9, compressed ch10–11, deliberately fractured at the finale. Every later chapter holds the ch2–4 template — every core beat remains identifiable — but each adds, fuses, or subtracts on purpose.
