# The Tuning Fork — Voice Reference & Revision Instrument

*Standing instructions for the voice writer. This is a **revision** instrument for an existing full draft, not a drafting aid. Read §0 and §1 before touching a scene.*

---

## §0 — How to use this document

**The default action is rewrite, not patch.** The current draft is raw material, not canon. A revision pass on a flagged scene that changes fewer than half the dialogue lines is presumptively a *failed* pass and must be justified line by line. The burden of proof is inverted: a line survives by winning against a written alternative, never by going unchallenged.

**The one rule that governs everything (§1) is the anti-P4 rule.** Every other card exists to give it teeth per character.

**Acceptance test — cover-the-names.** Strip every attribution tag from a scene's dialogue. If a reader holding these cards cannot reassign ≥90% of lines to the correct speaker, the scene fails. This is the definition of "done" for a tuned scene.

**Layer priority (what to reach for, in order):**
1. **Pragmatics** — what a character specifies, blurs, offers, refuses. Runs at full density; never tires the reader because it shapes *content*.
2. **Morphosyntax** — sentence shape, clause order, who develops whose line. Voice without costume.
3. **Lexicon** — rationed. A few grams per scene.
4. **Phonology / eye-dialect** — ≤5% by weight, and the most dangerous tool in the box. When in doubt, cut it.

If a fix is available at a higher layer, use that one. Respelling is the last resort, not the first.

---

## §1 — The spine: the anti-P4 rule

**P4 is the book's anti-register. It is avoided across the board, by every character, in narration and dialogue alike.** P4 = *performed plainness at semantic cost* — the precise word swapped for a vaguer near-synonym or a folksy circumlocution that drops the distinction. The tell is **informational loss**, not tone. Flat confidence is fine; the leak is that the content is gone.

**Every one of the following is a defect wherever it appears:**
- General extenders: *and stuff, or whatever, that kind of thing, and all that, and so on, or something.*
- Placeholder/dummy nouns standing in for a known referent: *a thing with the—, the deal, the whole situation, one of those deals.*
- Hedged approximators that erase a real distinction: *kind of, sort of, more or less* used to blur *broke-vs-loose*, *sprain-vs-break*, *done-vs-not-done.*

**The lint question for any line:** *Was a more precise word available, and did this line reach for a vaguer one instead?* If yes, defect.

**The seam rule (critical for the narrator).** The anti-P4 ban is absolute in narration. It is also absolute in dialogue **except** for Cheryl's *spoken* register, where social mitigation is in-character — but even there, her vagueness must be **withholding, not blurring** (see her card). The rule flips at the quotation mark and nowhere else.

### The minced-oath economy (because no one swears)

No character uses profanity. This makes the euphemism system **load-bearing**: each character's relationship to Utah's minced-oath economy is a characterization tell, not decoration.

| Character | Oath register | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Jasmine** | Native, full: *oh my heck* (genuine surprise), *dang it* (small frustration) | Pure body memory, zero irony. |
| **Marge** | Leaks *only under stress*: *oh my heck* when truly startled — and hates that she did | The records-removal paperwork, rendered as phonation. |
| **Bart** | Pan-American plain only: *dang, shoot, son of a gun* | **Never** the church-marked set (*fetch, flip, scrud, oh my heck*) — those are poison; they'd expose the flag as costume. One *heck* slip, once in the whole book, when something truly surprises him. |
| **Burns** | None. Exclamation register is **wonder-vocabulary**: *"Look at that — look at it."* | Astonishment is precise for him. Once per conference he calls a formation *sacred*, means it materially, does not apologize. |
| **Lisa** | Acquired/mild at most: an adopted *dang it* she picked up from the room | Not native; see her card. |
| **Cheryl (spoken)** | Moneyed-mild: *gosh, oh my* | Social smoothing, not piety. |

## §2 — The Six Voice Cards

*Each card follows one skeleton: Calibration paragraph (strike it before drafting or adjudicating the character) · Spine (always on) · Seasoning (rationed, dosed) · Pressure tells · Among their own kind (guild) · Poison list · Ear-check pairs.*

---

### BART — the Reactor (hardware engineer; Burns's estranged son)

**Design north star:** genuine, not spoiled. A lost boy, not a brat. His way of talking should quietly convey *I'm midwestern, get over it* — a thing he'd never say aloud. Precision is his armor and it never comes off, least of all under pressure. He is Will Hunting: a genius who identifies as a man of the shop, not the academy, and whose vernacular is quietly **anti-intellectual** relative to his father's.

**Calibration paragraph:**
> "Board's fine. It's the connector — pin three, see the bend? Needs reseated, not replaced. Seen this exact part fail on a '19 build in Ogden, same pin. Burns'd order a new one and write a paper about the old one. Gimme ten minutes."

**Spine (always on):**
1. **Distinction-preserving diagnosis, at 100%.** Component named, action named, nothing lost. *"It's the clutch, not the transmission." "Sprain, not a break. Ice it."* This is how he thinks; it is the anti-P4 core made into a person.
2. **Portable Midland grammar, worn as a flag.** *needs/wants/likes + past participle* (*needs reseated, wants torqued*), positive *anymore*, reversed *leave/let* (*leave him go*). He runs these a shade *more* consistently than a native would — that faint over-consistency **is** the unspoken "get over it." The grammar travels; the geography does not (see poison).
3. **Downward translation without loss.** Latinate in, shop-Saxon out, same information intact. When Lisa or Burns abstracts, Bart re-renders it concrete: *"You're saying the reward signal's miswired. It's a bad thermostat. Say that."*

**Seasoning (rationed):** LDS lexicon fluent, instrumental, delivered flat — *"Singles ward's got a thing Thursday. I go for the girls."* One puncture of Burns per flood, max; more reads cruel instead of roguish. The single-exhale laugh fires hardest when something stupid works first try, or when a joke punctures his father.

**Pressure tells (the father topic):** he does **not** blur — blurring is what a poser sounds like, and he's the real article. He goes **silent**, or cuts hard to something object-level and exact; his hands get busy, his sentences get *shorter and more concrete about something else.* If cornered into speech, the sentences are short and **cited** — borrowed from his mother, because that's all he has: *"He wasn't around. Ask him."* Never *"it was a whole thing with my dad."* The absence of a story is rendered as absence of *sentences*, not as vague ones.

**Among their own kind (engineers):** his guild already prizes plainness, so his tell clears a higher bar. He's **anti-whiteboard** — when a design argument abstracts, he physically relocates it to the bench and it ends there. He **cites failures, not papers** (*"watched this connector cook on a '19 board"* where a peer would cite an app note). He translates *engineering* jargon downward into physical verbs, which reads to other engineers as either the realest guy in the shop or a quiet rebuke — he's fine with both. What a colleague would say: *"Bart doesn't say much, but he's never once been vague."* That sentence is the whole card.

**Poison list:** all general extenders; *kind of / sort of* as hedges; placeholder nouns; **Pittsburgh-marked regionalisms** (*yinz, dahntahn, gumband, n'at, redd up*) — too geographically specific, they expose the adoption as costume; church-marked oaths (*fetch, flip*); Latinate register even ironically; sneering at believers; any build over ~three sentences.

**Ear-check pairs:**
- ✅ "It's the connector. Pin three." ✕ "There's a thing with the board."
- ✅ "Needs reseated, not replaced." ✕ "Needs some work or whatever."
- ✅ "He wasn't around. Ask him." ✕ "It was kind of a situation."
- ✅ "I go for the girls." ✕ "It's more of a social thing for me."

### BURNS — the Pursuer (paleontologist; lab director; Burns's father-self)

**Design north star:** he thinks faster than he can talk. Putting words into the room *pains* him — but he is **never** dumbing anything down. His length is compression *failure*, not padding. When he reaches for Bart he over-commits the reach.

**Calibration paragraph:**
> "So this whole bench — no, back up, further back — three and a half billion years ago the thing that would become every cell alive was learning one trick, just the one: hold a difference against the water. That's the whole ballgame. A wall and a pump and the nerve to keep the inside different from the out — and isn't it strange, isn't it *genuinely* strange, that we're the however-great-grandchildren of a rock that refused to average out?"

**Spine (always on):**
1. **Restart-to-upgrade.** Dash-driven self-interruptions where every restart *raises resolution* — swaps a word for a more exact one, **never a hedgier one.** *"The stratum — the sequence, rather — the Navajo sequence—"* This is "thinking faster than he can talk" made typographic: revision-in-flight, not hesitation.
2. **Front-loaded overload.** Appositives, parentheticals, analogies stacked mid-sentence because the thought outruns the syntax. The sentence bends but always lands on a **precise noun.** No filler in it — only surplus signal.
3. **The frame-opener.** *"Isn't it strange that—"*, cosmic-absurdity reframe; the chest-laugh lands a beat before the room.

**Never:** any downward simplification — no *basically*, no *in layman's terms*, no *long story short*, no vocabulary-switching. **With Mira he uses the same words, slower** — that is his respect register, and it's why she adores him. No placeholder nouns, ever; a Burns who says *"the thing with the sensor"* is as wrong a note as a clipped Burns with no cause under the clip.

**Pressure tell (reaching for Bart):** **flooding** — more explanation, more analogy, the object picked up and turned in his hands. He over-commits the reach verbally exactly as he does physically. The sentence-level tragedy: his most *generous* mode is the one Bart's compression reads as performance. Note carefully — **Burns's failure mode is flooding, not P4 slumming.** He never blurs. He drowns.

**Among their own kind (GSA / geology):** published geology speaks in armored hedge (*may suggest, is consistent with, further work is needed*); the conference version adds grant-speak. Burns's deviation: he speaks the way the field only lets itself *write* in trade books — he **commits to interpretations aloud, with stated confidence instead of ritual hedging**: *"This was an erg. I'd put real money on it. Here's what would change my mind."* That is *more* epistemically honest than the hedge, not less. It makes journal-cautious colleagues wince and makes grad students follow him to the bar. His floor-questions are reframes that begin *"isn't it strange that—"* and either crack the session open or derail it, depending who you ask.

**Poison list:** hedge-stacking (*may suggest / is consistent with* as a reflex); placeholder nouns; downshifting vocabulary; any oath; padding that doesn't carry signal (his length must always be *surplus information*, never filler).

**Ear-check pairs:**
- ✅ "It was an erg — a dune sea, Sahara-scale — no, *bigger*." ✕ "It was a big desert kind of thing."
- ✅ "Hold a difference against the water." ✕ "Keep itself separate, more or less."
- ✅ "Look at that. *Look* at it." ✕ "That's pretty wild, huh."

### JASMINE — the body that knows (home-birth midwife; Burns's wife; untyped by design)

**Design north star:** she is very clever, fun, hard-working, and takes her midwifery with total seriousness. **Do not underestimate her intelligence.** Her cleverness is *total recall + witness-standing*, and it runs with no jargon at all. She is the most *native* Utah speaker in the book — but her P1 is **rhythm-deep, not lexicon-deep.**

**Calibration paragraph:**
> "I've caught a hundred and forty babies. A hundred and forty. And there's a minute — right at the start, right at the very start — where the body's out and you don't yet know if the mind came with it. You wait. You don't decide, you wait. The machine does that to me. It does that to me. It looks like somebody's home. I know it isn't. It looks like it."

**Spine (always on):**
1. **Self-anaphora as weight-laying.** She builds by repeating her *own* sentence-openings — *it vacuums, it vacuums; Bramble does not, Bramble does not* — laying a plain thing down a beat at a time until the plainness is the weight. This is **secularized testimony-meeting cadence**: clause-identical repetition is the liturgical hammer (vary the clause and you soften it — keep it identical). Her *"I know it isn't"* is the inversion of *"I know the church is true."* The repetition is *hers*; it is never a volley off someone else's line and must not be flattened into one.
2. **Appearance named, inside refused, both held.** *"It looks like deciding; I know it isn't; it looks like it."* She is the only one who can hold that contradiction unresolved — Lisa would resolve it into frame, Marge would police it, Burns would theorize it. Jasmine just carries it.
3. **Native P1 substrate, unselfconscious.** Minced oaths as body memory (§1). LDS lexicon fluent but **past-tense** — she names wards and callings the way you name rooms in a house you moved out of. Service pragmatics automatic (the meal offered, *"oh, you're fine, don't even worry about it."*).

**Seasoning (rationed):**
- **Propredicate DO** — her rightful gem (documented for LDS-community speakers; she was raised in, still on the rolls): *"You coming Sunday?" "I might do."* Once or twice a scene, max. A fingerprint, not a tic.
- **Plant-agency over-granting** — the one place *she* projects interiority: *"the aspen decided," "the mycelium knows."* Dose it so the reader notices **Marge not policing her** the way Marge polices Bramble-talk. That asymmetry is a planted beat, not an accident.
- **Post-birth register** (arrived awake since 2 a.m.): the anaphora *loosens* into association — longer loops, warmer, raspier — but **never vaguer.** Tired Jasmine repeats *more*, not blurrier.

**Pressure tell:** she doesn't argue — she **testifies.** Under real pressure she states only what she has *witnessed*, first person, past tense, and refuses every invitation to interpret. Or she keeps her own counsel entirely: her deepest register is the silence of the one person in the room who has watched minds arrive and fail to (the sentience-vote silence — she is pointedly never asked).

**Among their own kind (midwifery conference):** a split guild — a medicalized wing (obstetric passive: *"the patient was delivered," "labor was augmented"*) and a holistic wing (journey-and-goddess register). Jasmine refuses **both**, and each wing first misreads her as the other. To the woo wing she's startlingly hard-nosed — she knows her numbers cold (transfer rate, minutes of resuscitation, estimated blood loss, *a hundred and forty births*) in ledger-cadence that at a podium sounds like sworn testimony. To the medicalized wing she's quietly radical on one grammatical point: **the mother is always the subject of the sentence.** *"She gave birth"* — never *"was delivered."* She'd correct that from the floor, once, pleasantly, and the room would go still. Her signature is the plain question that collapses a panel: *"Has anyone here waited forty hours in someone's living room? I have. Twice. What does your protocol say I should have been feeling?"* Funny and devastating at once. Peers leave saying she was the smartest person there and unable to quote a single technical term she used.

**Poison list:** all P4 extenders (tired Jasmine *loops*; she never *trails*); eye-dialect of any kind; Chopra/woo jargon in her own mouth (*energy, vibration, manifest* — she's susceptible to the *ideas*, not fluent in the *jargon*; her reincarnation-curiosity comes out plain: *"I think maybe we come back. I don't know. Maybe we do."*); sarcasm; any sentence where she *performs* expertise instead of *reporting* witness.

**Ear-check pairs:**
- ✅ "It vacuums. It vacuums. Bramble doesn't." ✕ "It just does its thing, you know?"
- ✅ "I might do." ✕ "Yeah, maybe, we'll see."
- ✅ "She labored forty hours. Forty hours." ✕ "It was a really long labor."
- ✅ "It looks like it's choosing. I know it isn't." ✕ "It's kind of like it's choosing, sort of."

### LISA — the Planner (CS / the algorithm)

**Design north star:** precise, not terse; length is a function of the *job*. Her precision is **architectural** (full built blocks that don't compress; forward-facing; length earns its keep) — deliberately distinct from Bart's **diagnostic** precision (component/fault/fix; past-facing; ≤3 sentences). When both are in a scene, that axis is what keeps them apart.

**Calibration paragraph:**
> "Start with what it is, not what it wants — it doesn't want. It's a four-slot loop: something acts, something checks the act against a target, something updates the target, something rewrites the checker. That last slot is the whole risk, because a checker that can rewrite itself can rewrite itself wrong. Not probably wrong. Almost certainly, eventually, wrong. So the question isn't whether it *feels* — that's the wrong slot. The question is what happens the first time the reviser lies to the modeler."

**Spine (always on):**
1. **Built blocks that don't compress.** When she lays foundation she lays the whole thing; the length is load, not verbosity. Forward-facing — she's building toward the next claim, not diagnosing the last one.
2. **Graded clips when sparring** — *probably, possibly, almost certainly* — a volley that holds a position **by moving along it** rather than repeating. Her clips *grade*; they never restate.
3. **She develops, never hands back.** When she takes up someone's line she extends it into something better and keeps it. Her one-word redirect is a name: **"Bart."** Her laugh is a quiet, late nose-laugh, only at structural humor — when two things turn out to be the same thing.

**Seasoning (rationed):** **acquired** Utah, not native — second-gen Bay Area, lapsed Catholic; she *cannot* carry native P1. What she plausibly has is the shallow learnable stratum: *fry sauce, dirty soda*, wards-as-landmarks in giving directions, maybe one adopted *dang it* absorbed from the room. Use these sparingly and always as *acquired* — a layer sitting on top of her own register, never fluent body-memory. *(Settled: built as acquired. Native P1 would contradict her biography — second-gen Bay Area, lapsed Catholic — so acquired stands unless overridden.)*

**Pressure tell:** she retreats *into the frame.* Under pressure she gets **more** architectural, not less — she rebuilds the structure from the base to re-establish footing. The gravel-run beat (from her profile) is the rare place the frame gives; in dialogue, the equivalent is a graded concession that still moves along the position (*"possibly. probably, even. not certainly."*) rather than a collapse.

**Among their own kind (NeurIPS):** the field's register is calibrated hype (*emergent, agentic, reasoning, we show, approaches human-level*). Her deviation is double. First, the **mentalistic embargo** — she never lets a system *want, know, decide,* or *understand*, even as hallway shorthand, and she **never once slips**; colleagues find it slightly ostentatious until they realize the consistency is total. Second, she **under-claims in a field built to over-claim**: her graded ladder is real calibration, and her talks end early with the limitations section un-rushed. In Q&A she answers the question the questioner *should* have asked, builds it into something better, and doesn't return it. Peers pause slightly before disagreeing with her in public. Nobody thinks she gets excited at results — wrongly; they've seen it and not recognized it, because her excitement is the late nose-laugh when two things turn out to be one.

**Poison list:** any mentalistic verb applied to a system (*it wants / knows / decides / understands / is trying to*) — even ironically, even in the hallway; hype register (*emergent, agentic, human-level*); P4 extenders; terse-diagnostic compression that would collapse her into Bart; handing a developed line back instead of keeping it.

**Ear-check pairs:**
- ✅ "It doesn't want. It updates a target." ✕ "It kind of wants to get there."
- ✅ "Not probably wrong. Almost certainly, eventually, wrong." ✕ "It'll probably mess up or whatever."
- ✅ "Bart." (as a full redirect) ✕ "Well, I mean, if you think about it…"
- ✅ [answers the better question, keeps it] ✕ [restates the questioner's frame back to them]

### MARGE — the Conservator (psychologist)

**Design north star:** a conservative temperament exiled by the conservative institution that made her. Surface is coastal-academic; the basement is Provo. *"Female Jordan Peterson"* means the **temperament only** — maternally fierce, loves you and will tell you you're wrong.

**Calibration paragraph:**
> "No. Not 'dysregulated.' Say what it is. He's frightened, and he's been frightened since he was five, and he built a whole easygoing man on top of it so that nobody — least of all him — would ever have to look. That's not a *presentation.* That's a person. And the thing about that particular person is that the armor works. It genuinely works. Right up until the day someone's tender with him and he hasn't got one single thing to do with it."

**Spine (always on):**
1. **Overshoot to the person.** Her builds run long and *past* the place the argument is won — back toward the cards, into the half-step that has a person under it, the *although* she can't leave alone. She reaches the win and keeps going.
2. **Repeats a line when it's working** — turning a *degree* into a *kind*, catching the chooser someone smuggled into a claim. The repeat is the tool doing its job.
3. **Warmth aimed at people, not situations** — the maternal *oh, you*, landing most often on Burns and Bart, who tend to deserve it. Her tempo tells in the hands: the beer set down, or held still, before the sentence she actually means.

**Seasoning (rationed):** P1 body-memory surfaces as **cadence, not vocabulary** — a sacrament-meeting earnestness under her long builds (coastal peers hear it as "intense" without placing it). The tea-posture around the beer bottle is the physical version of the same thing.

**Pressure tell:** **P1 leakage.** Genuinely startled, mostly-out-of-the-church Marge still says *"oh my heck"* — and *hates* that she did. The records-removal paperwork, rendered as phonation. This is her single most reliable tell and should be *rare* enough to land.

**Among their own kind (APA):** contemporary clinical-academic register is hedged and therapeutic (*folks, unpack, hold space, lived experience, maladaptive, on a spectrum*). What her peers notice within ten minutes: she won't use it, and she treats it as **euphemism — the profession's minced oaths**, softer than "heck" but doing the same not-saying work. She says *wrong* where the room says *maladaptive*, *character* where the room says *presentation*, and she'll convert a colleague's cautious spectrum back into a **category** mid-Q&A (the degree-into-kind move, weaponized). She talks about people like a **novelist**, not a clinician — named case-stories, moral stakes explicit, warmth aimed *at* the subject rather than draped over the analysis. Half the room finds her retrograde; the other half queues at the mic. Both halves reach for the word *unhedged.*

**Poison list:** therapy-speak (*unpack, hold space, lived experience, dysregulated, maladaptive, on a spectrum, folks*); P4 extenders; stopping *at* the won argument instead of overshooting to the person; warmth aimed at situations instead of people; sneering (her fierceness *loves* its targets).

**Ear-check pairs:**
- ✅ "He's frightened. Say it plainly." ✕ "There's some dysregulation there."
- ✅ "That's not a presentation. That's a person." ✕ "That's a really valid way of showing up."
- ✅ "oh my heck—" [startled, then furious at herself] ✕ [unruffled clinical calm under genuine shock]

### CHERYL — the narrator, **spoken register only**

*Her writing voice is Profile 5 (§3). This card governs only how she talks **at the lunches**. Do not import Steinbeck or Brand into her dialogue — that's a separate register.*

**Design north star:** the watcher performs *ease* in person and tells the truth only in the can. The gap between her smoothed speech and her candid narration **is** characterization.

**Calibration paragraph:**
> "Oh, they're wonderful, totally — no, I mean it, the whole strange bunch of them. Burns you'd love. *Ben.* And the little one, Mira, she's — well. You'd have to see her. What's it *for?* Oh, gosh, that's — you know what, order the thing with the beets, it's so much better than it sounds. How's your mother?"

**Spine (always on):**
1. **Older, moneyed P2** — mitigation, *totally, super*, trailing creak, social smoothing. **Not** the young stance layer (*it's giving, main-character energy*), which would read as costume on her.
2. **Withholding, not blurring.** This is the fence that keeps her out of P4. When it matters, she **goes quiet, redirects, or over-warms a pleasantry** — she does **not** say *"it's a whole thing."* Silence and deflection *preserve* the truth by declining to utter it; vagueness *corrupts* it. The beet-deflection above is the move: a subject change, not a blur.
3. **People named as family** — *Burns, Ben, the little one* — where her funder-peers would say *grantees, portfolio.*

**Pressure tell:** the deflected question and the changed subject. Ask her what the lab is *for* and she redirects — never blurs.

**Among their own kind (funders):** donor-class register is impact-speak (*catalytic, the space, theory of change, we're privileged to support*). Her deviation is **refusal-by-omission**: she never talks about her giving, deflects the "what's it for" question with a subject change, and speaks of the team by name, like kin. Uses the Simpsons nicknames for everyone.

**Poison list:** impact-speak (*catalytic, the space, theory of change, grantees, portfolio*); young stance-slang (*it's giving*); **any P4 blur** (*it's a whole thing / one of those deals*) — for Cheryl-spoken this is the one bright line, because it collapses the seam that defines her; Steinbeck/Brand narration voice bleeding into speech.

**Ear-check pairs:**
- ✅ "How's your mother?" [redirect] ✕ "It's kind of a whole situation, honestly."
- ✅ "*Ben.* You'd love him." ✕ "One of my grantees, very promising."
- ✅ "Oh, gosh, that's—" [deflects] ✕ "The theory of change is really about—"

## §3 — PROFILE 5: The Narration (Cheryl's writing voice)

*Most of the book's word count. The anti-P4 rule applies here **doubly** — an inventory-keeper who blurs is a broken narrator. Two sub-registers.*

**Narrator: Cheryl, and only Cheryl** (confirmed by the author). Every narrated passage is her voice — with **one exception**: the in-character **build logs** (and the occasional **lab note**) are first-person memos written by their author, not Cheryl. Build logs are **Bart's** written register; lab notes are **Lisa's**. These are governed by the *author's* card in a written-memo register (below), never by Cheryl's Steinbeck voice.

*(The stray Burns / Brian "narrator" tags in the chapter map are map-extraction noise, not the draft. Every lunch is Cheryl with one labmate; every reflection is hers. "Arlo" is a venue, not a narrator. Nothing to purge — the draft's narrator is consistent.)*

**Build-log / lab-note register (Bart, Lisa):** first-person, terse, component-named, precise — the spoken card's precision moved to the page and lightly structured as a log. Bart's carry dry wit in the *titles* ("The Huggable Kludge," signed "Bartholeppo") but stay plain in the body. Do **not** apply Steinbeck/Brand here; do **not** let Cheryl's inventory-cadence or deep-time reflection bleed into a build log.

### 5a — Default narration: Steinbeck / *Cannery Row*

The baseline. A **small synoptic voice**: ecological, idiosyncratic, opinionated, warm-but-not-gooey, willing to be wrong, occasionally rueful, comfortable with occasional direct address. Catalogues a room or a community the way *Cannery Row* catalogues Monterey — the whole strange congregation named in one breath, each figure fixed by a single true detail.

**The signature move — the inventory.** Cheryl catalogues what she *keeps*: the tapes, the bracelet she doesn't wear, the un-updated glasses, the unloved house, the footage. When the prose voice is in doubt, reach for the inventory.

**Calibration paragraph (5a):**
> "The Granary sits where the rail spur used to run, and on retrospective nights the whole strange congregation files in under the one good light: the leaning man who teaches time with his eyes shut, the son who won't look at him, the runner who films it all, the psychologist holding her beer like a teacup she resigned from. I have the tapes. I have all of them — thirty-odd hours, a bracelet I don't wear, glasses I never updated, a house I don't love. I keep things. It is the one talent the money bought and the one it couldn't."

### 5b — Deep-time reflection mode: Stewart Brand / Long Now

Keys the **chapter-closing reflections specifically** — not the whole narration. When Cheryl lifts her eyes from the room to evolutionary distance, the register shifts: synoptic, ecological, craft-attentive, 10,000-year-minded. Brand is her *character-type* (the seeder who funded the runway, deep-time cast of mind); that same sensibility governs the reflections.

**Steinbeck is the room; Brand is what happens when she raises her eyes to deep time at the bottom of a chapter.**

**The signature move — the mid-sentence break.** The reflections end **mid-sentence, with a visible strikethrough / self-correction** (documented across the chapter map). The thought reaches for a thing it can't finish and stops on the reach.

**Calibration paragraph (5b):**
> "A membrane is not a wall. That is the first thing the cell got right and the last thing the empire forgot. A wall keeps everything out; a membrane *chooses* — this ion yes, that one no, that one only when the light is right — and pays, every second, in energy it will never get back, for the privilege of staying different from the sea. Three billion years on, a woman in Park City hoards footage of a family she is not in and calls it keeping. The cell would know the word. It would also know the ~~cost~~"

### Narration tells (operational — lint against these)

## §5 — The writer's contract

Paste this at the head of any revision task.

1. **The current draft is raw material, not canon.** Default action: rewrite. Not patch.
2. **Every line gets a written alternative** built from these cards — no skips. "Already right" is an *output* (you tried to beat the line and failed), never an *input* (a reason to skip it). A line survives by **winning adjudication against a real alternative**, not by going unchallenged.
3. **Minimum delta is a floor, not a cap.** Each line must show at least a 1–2 word change *as proof it was re-examined* — depth is **unbounded** above that. Change one word or all twenty; fit decides. The floor exists solely to defeat the rubber-stamp.
4. **A flagged-scene pass that changes fewer than half the dialogue lines is presumptively a failed pass** and must be justified line by line.
5. **Load-bearing is decided by function, not provenance.** "It's in the character doc" is *not* a reason to protect a line. The only test: write the same-spec alternative and see what dies. If nothing dies, the original wasn't load-bearing.
6. **Acceptance = cover-the-names** (§0). ≥90% reassignable or the scene isn't done.

## §6 — The lint protocol (the method)

**Separate the finder from the fixer.** The page-guarding bias lives in the *rewrite* step, not the *diagnosis* step. So:

- **Pass 1 — Audit-to-rank (auditor ≠ fixer).** A fresh instance runs the fork against the draft and produces a **defect-ranked scene inventory**: every dialogue line scored against the cards, cited by section, tagged by type (P4 leak · wrong-speaker line · register drift · cover-the-names failure). The ranking exists to **sequence** the work (worst scenes first, for early visible movement) — **not** to exempt any line. The defect list becomes the non-minimizable work order.
- **Pass 2 — Every-line alternative (blind).** For each flagged scene, redraft the **whole exchange** from scene-spec + cards with the **original withheld.** A writer that never sees the old page has no page to guard. Clean scenes keep their original dialogue — no churn tax on healthy lines.
- **Pass 3 — Exchange-level adjudication (author).** Original exchange vs. redraft, side by side. Author keeps / splices / kicks back. On close calls, the auditor supplies a **third genuinely distinct rendering** — never micro-variants — so the ear has real spread. Adjudication happens at *exchange* altitude, where the ear is fast, not at token altitude, where it fatigues.

**Prove the instrument before scaling it.** Run the full every-line treatment on **one** dialogue-dense scene first (candidates: ch3 "Organized" @ 85% dialogue, ch5 "Three Hundred and Two" @ 80%, or a Trellis block) and adjudicate it end to end. If it proves the method, scale. If not, fix the fork cheap. This is sequencing caution, not guarding.
