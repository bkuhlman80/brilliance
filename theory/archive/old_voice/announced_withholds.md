# The Announced-Withhold Sweep

*Find-and-report across all twelve chapter drafts in `theory/chapters/sequenced/`. Nothing was changed. Every one of these is a small scene, not a word swap, and they are Brian's to fix.*

**The defect:** the narrator signals that she is not going to say a thing, instead of simply not saying it — turning to the reader to be admired for leaving a gap. The ellipsis is allowed; the announcement is not. The crime is never the gap. It is the narrator pointing at it.

**The discriminator, applied to every row:** *could the sentence be deleted, and the reader still feel the gap?* **Yes → the sentence exists only to point at the withhold; it is the defect (Deletable = Yes).** No → it does real work; reported anyway, marked Deletable = No, Brian rules.

## Part 3 — the two known cases: both already fixed

1. **The fart (Chs 7–9).** The prose now says *fart*, plainly and repeatedly: *"It farts, same as the four-legged one did"* ([ch_9:24](theory/chapters/sequenced/ch_9_v2_2.md#L24)); *"Farts on the floor, twice a day, wet and loud"* ([ch_8:288](theory/chapters/sequenced/ch_8_v2_2.md#L288)); Mira says it out loud and Bart quits correcting her ([ch_7:52](theory/chapters/sequenced/ch_7_v2_2.md#L52), [ch_7:290](theory/chapters/sequenced/ch_7_v2_2.md#L290)). **No evasion survives.** Bart's own soft word *"Spill"* ([ch_9:107](theory/chapters/sequenced/ch_9_v2_2.md#L107)) is explicitly framed as *his* private word against *"the same plain word by everyone but him"* — characterization, not an announced withhold. The old "I'm not writing the name down" gag does **not** appear anywhere in the narration.

2. **The Ch 12 door.** The narrator does **not** decline to name the knocker. [ch_12:433](theory/chapters/sequenced/ch_12_v2_2.md#L433): *"And then, face dripping, she blinked as I knocked at her door."* — Cheryl names herself. The terminal coda then has her put down the camera and *act* — *"drive to where the paper says"* — ending mid-sentence on *"and close the"* ([ch_12:456](theory/chapters/sequenced/ch_12_v2_2.md#L456)), an intentional cut, which is the invisible-withhold done right, not the announced one. **The defect is out of the climax.**

## The pattern (worth saying out loud)

The prediction held. **The clean, Deletable = Yes instances cluster almost entirely in Cheryl's first-person warm beats** — the lunch self-narration (2.c) and the interlude/coda material — and in the sibling first-person "narrator on the tape" asides that the third-person chapters reach for at the same emotional altitude (3.8.a, 9.4.2.c, 10.4.5.b, 11.4.8.c). That is exactly where the prose was reaching for texture and was least supervised, and it is where the tasteful reflex lives.

Two shapes recur and are worth naming so they can be hunted as families, not one at a time:

- **The lunch/coda "I was not going to put a word to it" move** — Cheryl declining to name her own feeling and telling the reader she is declining. Ch 6 [2.c], Ch 9 [2.c], Ch 12 [2.b / 4.2.c / 4.13.c]. This is the flagship family; five of the strongest hits.
- **The "narrator on the tape … did not reach in after it" move** — the third-person chapters importing the first-person discretion tic as a self-conscious aside about their own restraint. Ch 3 [4.8.a / 4.10.c / 4.11.c], Ch 9 [4.2.c / 4.10.a], Ch 10 [4.5.b / 4.10.c], Ch 11 [4.8.c]. Same reflex, third person; "on purpose" and "did not reach in after it" are the tells.

**Chapter-closers are a repeat offender.** Ch 6 [5.b] and Ch 7 [5.b] both end the chapter on a performed refusal to follow the closing image "the whole way down." These do characterization work and are the hardest calls on the sheet — but they are the same gesture twice, in the same slot, and a reader who has seen one will feel the second reaching.

One honest caution on the two book-scale cases — **Ch 12 [5.b]** (the wall of words around the unsaid verb) and **Ch 6/7 [5.b]** (the closers): these are formally the defect but structurally load-bearing. Ch 12 [5.b] in particular is the engine the whole ending exists to *break*. Cutting it as a tic would collapse the close. Reported so they are on the sheet; Brian rules on whether the announcement earns its keep or whether the gap alone would carry.
