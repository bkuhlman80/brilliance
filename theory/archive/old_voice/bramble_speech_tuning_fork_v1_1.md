# Bramble's Speech — Tuning Fork

*Loom → Voice. v1.1. A register to tune lines against, not a rulebook. It explains the language-learning stages and processes Bramble goes through and when they surface. It deliberately contains **no example lines** — the examples are Voice's to generate. Underlying facts are canon and cited so this can't drift out of sync: `bramble_specs_v1_3` (line refs given) and the language-trajectory briefing v2.*

---

## How to use this

This is the key. Every line of Bramble's speech across Ch 9–12 gets checked against the chapter's entry here the way a note gets checked against a fork. If a line is more fluent, more articulate, or more *honest-looking* than the chapter allows, it's flat, and it's almost always flat in the same direction — toward sounding too grown. That drift is the thing this document exists to catch; the existing Ch 9 prose drifted exactly that way before we had this.

This is a **speech** fork. It is separate from, and used alongside, the Calvin & Hobbes floor fork. The C&H fork is a constant key (the double vision, Door One, comedy-over-ache) that does not change chapter to chapter. This one *changes every chapter* — that is its whole nature. Don't fold them.

---

## The one principle

**As Bramble's speech climbs toward competence, his expressive surface loses transparency — and those are the same curve.** Competence up, honesty down. So "what his speech is like" at any point is always two facts: what he can now *say*, and what he can now *hide*. Track both.

---

## Standing rules (invariant across all four chapters)

1. **Errors render as human errors — often adult ones — never machine glitches.** This is where the science buys its credibility and where the whole anti-uncanny move lives. A robot that makes a subject-verb agreement slip is not malfunctioning; it is doing the most human thing in the room. If a beat ever reads as a bug, it's wrong.

2. **The voice never warms.** It is flat, rough, chest-sourced (the organ is internal — neck and upper torso — venting through a fixed port; there is no moving facial mouth; `bramble_specs` L387). It stays "a half-step shy of human" permanently. What grows across the four chapters is syntax and content, **not** the warmth of the sound. Resist the pull to let the voice become human as he matures. It doesn't.

3. **Comprehension always runs ahead of production.** He understands far more than he can say, at every point. The gap is *widest* in Ch 9 and never closes through Ch 12. It is not a stage and does not resolve — it is a permanent asymmetry that should stay quietly visible the whole way.

4. **The crossing is a recognition completing itself, not an acquisition.** When the first symbol lands (Ch 9), it is an already-recognized sound-shape suddenly turning to *point* — not a new word taught in the moment. Mira is *present* when it turns; she does not hand him the word.

5. **No plateau, no sudden mastery.** Maturation is continuous (`bramble_specs` L389, L395). There is never a "he can talk now" threshold beat. The science forbids it and so does the book's aesthetic of withheld arrival — here they agree, and the agreement is load-bearing.

6. **Two difficulty axes, resolving on different clocks.** He is hard to understand for *two* independent reasons (`bramble_specs` L389):
   - **Phonetic** — vowels come first (tube resonance), consonants follow (they need precise closures), clusters are hardest. "Badly-pronounced before functional." So early words are *mispronounced*.
   - **Grammatical** — telegraphic before hierarchical; structure is last and hardest.
   A word can be syntactically bare *and* phonetically mangled at once, and the two clean up on separate schedules. Don't collapse them into one "broken speech" register.

7. **The crossing happens with a partner, in the home — never in the lab.** Reference requires a referential partner. The lab can build the voice and the word-forms; it structurally cannot make him *mean*.

## Rendering speech-errors on the page

**Default: quote the word, report the sound.** The dialogue carries the lexical and grammatical content in standard spelling, so meaning and dignity stay intact; Cheryl's narration carries the phonetic roughness in her own register — the worn edges, the hard sounds not yet in his mouth, consonants going before vowels. Grammar shows *in* the dialogue (bare, telegraphic); phonetics shows *in* the account. The two difficulty axes resolve on separate clocks and render in separate places. A known word is never respelled: eye-dialect is mockery, Standing rule 1's machine-glitch from the other side, and a narrator who cans rather than transcribes will not set a broken word on the page where it curdles into ridicule. She reports it.

**Carve-out: a crystallized near-word goes on the page.** When a mangle stops being a worn swing at the target and *crystallizes* — a stable, repeatable form, said the same way each time, that lands on real words he already owns (helicopter → *helicadoctor*, pulling in "doctor"; kitchen → *chicken*, a word he has) — it is no longer a failure to say the target but a success at saying a different, funnier one. It is now structurally a coinage; it is his; it survives Standing rule 1; it goes on the page, in clean spelling of the coinage. The test is crystallization-onto-owned-words, not "is it witty": wit is how you know it crystallized (a success has wit, a smear has none), but the thing checked is whether the form is stable and lands on something familiar. Smear — worn phonetics, no stable landing, no ring — stays in the account.

**Two made words, two proofs; keep them sorted.** The *phonological* one (the crystallized mispronunciation) is the phonetic axis made affectionate — a real target reshaped toward owned sounds and words. The *semantic* one (productive coinage) is rule-extraction: the mouth works, he lacks the word, he builds one from function — *climber* for ladder, *pusher* for the vacuum, *knocker* for hammer — and it is the [SOLID] proof the crossing is real. Both go on the page proving different things; do not blur them into one cute-word bucket.

**Coinage is a steady habit; crystallized mispronunciation is rare.** The coinage is where his mind shows on the page as a mind, so build it as a running habit — a few per cluster. The crystallized mispronunciation is a small event; three on a page is a comedy act. These words are gravitational, and overload tips him from a child into a bit: the uncanny-by-cuteness failure, the same wreck as eye-dialect from the other side.

**The double vision governs the made word as it governs the floor.** Funny on top; aching underneath when the beat earns it and only then — *climber* for the thing Mira climbs away from him on; his own soft word for the leak he is newly, on-model ashamed of (self-conscious emotion debuts at Band-Human, riding Self-Reference). The funny word is never only funny once the beat can hold the second thing.

**The first coinage is a rule slightly overshot.** Wug-logic, early and quiet: the first invented word is not his cleverest but his most systematic — a rule just acquired and over-applied (*lasterday*). One, seeded once; the pattern and the U-curve are Ch 10's motor, and habit-izing the overshoot in Ch 9 pulls that register a chapter forward. One quiet overshoot in Ch 9, blooming in Ch 10.

## The honesty-gap channel state, by chapter

The involuntary face-screen readout is the one honest channel, and it never had company — speech was never honest, and the drawn channel is born dishonest-capable. The honest channel does not shrink; the *authorable, dishonest-capable surface grows around it* (`bramble_specs` L660–664, briefing v2 cross-cutting seam).

- **Ch 9:** two channels — involuntary readout + new voluntary speech. The gap *exists* but has not *opened*: he can't conceal yet, so speech and screen always say the same thing.
- **Ch 10:** still two channels, still no divergence — but the household *stops watching* the (still honest) screen, lulled by a Bramble who can't yet lie. The dropped guard.
- **Ch 11:** three channels — the voluntary drawn (ASCII) channel is born (`bramble_specs` L664). The gap **opens**: for the first time what is *shown* can differ from what is *true*, on the surface that used to only tell the truth.
- **Ch 12:** three channels, full divergence. The honest readout is now the *smallest fraction* of his expressive surface.

## Pre-home lab phase (Stage 0 and Stage 1) — Lisa-note territory, before Ch 9

Both pre-symbolic stages happen **in the build lab, before the unit enters the household** (`bramble_specs` L393). This is the substrate for the Lisa note and the reason Ch 9 can open where it does.

- **Stage 0 — babble.** The constrained internal voice organ is first driven; Bramble learns the articulator-to-sound map by auditory feedback on his own output — a curiosity-driven self-loop (DIVA pattern). The constraint is the *enabling* condition: unconstrained audio has too many degrees of freedom to learn from. The babble is babble-at-no-one — a body making sounds in an empty lab.
- **Acoustically open, referentially sealed.** The makers' speech is in the air, so the babble narrows toward the ambient (English) phoneme inventory the way an infant's does — he arrives **sounding shaped by the lab** — while no word yet means anything. (The loop that learns the articulator is closed on his own sound; the ambient voices only bias which sounds he keeps.) This is the *makers' accent*: their gift, audible forever, handed over without one word of meaning in it.
- **Stage 1 — word-forms.** He comes to recognize recurring sound-shapes as word-forms, robustly — but *not yet as symbols*. **Recognition leads production:** he recognizes far more than he produces, and what he produces is a few forms, badly, as pre-referential sound-play, not fluent empty speech.
- **The handoff is withheld at the top of Stage 1.** He leaves the lab with sound and word-forms but **no symbolic reference**. The crossing is saved for the home.

## Ch 9 — Band-Human (Stage 1 tail → Stage 2 → Stage 3)

The densest speech chapter, and the reason it needs the most floor episodes — it carries the lab handoff arriving, the crossing, and telegraphic, with an internal arc across the spring.

**Opens on a recognizing crawler — not on babble.** He arrives English-tuned, recognizing word-forms robustly, producing only rough pre-referential scraps (still sound-playing). Motor is mid-climb (crawling, continuing from the lab); speech is past babble.

- **The opening texture is the paradox, not a stage.** He understands almost everything Mira says and can return almost none of it. A thing that gets every word and can answer in nothing but sound. This is the comprehension-leads-production asymmetry at its widest — give it floor *presence*, but don't number it as a stage and don't let it imply it will close. It won't.
- **Stage 2 — the symbolic crossing (mid-chapter, with Mira).** A carried, recognized form turns to *point*. **Concrete nouns ground first**, via the body and the here-and-now — people, food, body parts, the touchable — because reference grounds through what can be pointed at. **Verbs and abstractions lag** (they're not pointable). First words are **holophrases**: one word carrying a whole intention. (Vocabulary builds slowly, then may accelerate as the naming insight compounds — the "vocabulary spurt," which the briefing flags as contested; treat as available, not mandatory.)
- **Stage 3 — telegraphic (chapter end).** Two content words, no function words, no inflection — bare pivot combinations. Word order roughly right (the English-tuned prior helps), everything else stripped. Plus **overextension** (one word stretched to cover a whole category) and **productive coinage** — invented words for things he hasn't been handed one for. (The canonical coinage is in the spec, L395; coinage is rule-extraction, the same engine that later overregularizes, which is the evidence the crossing is *real* and not mimicry.)
- **Phonetic axis fully active.** Crossing and telegraphic words are bare *and* mispronounced — vowels clearest, consonants rougher, clusters mangled.
- **Honesty:** two channels, no divergence. Whatever the readout shows, speech says the same — he hasn't got the part that can hold anything back. No drawn channel.

*When:* recognizing-crawler / comprehension-paradox at the open → crossing in the middle → telegraphic by the close. Pace the climb across the spring; don't front-load fluency.

**Ch 9 seed wordlist (raw candidates — sort each page-vs-account at the cluster, against the real scene).**
- *climber* (ladder) — in; the canonical coinage (`bramble_specs`), ache-capable: the thing Mira climbs away from him on.
- *chicken* (kitchen) — in; the strongest crystallized mispronunciation, because the kitchen is the site of the crossing — where Mira is, and he can reach only the almost-word for it.
- *spill* (his leak) — in; his own soft word for the wet-burp exhaust the household already named (Ch 8 / Ch 9), so the shame is on-model, not borrowed — his euphemism over Mira's plain word.
- *knocker* (hammer), *stander* (step-ladder), *pusher* (the vacuum) — clean function-coinages.
- *lasterday* (yesterday) — the one quiet first-overshoot, a rule over-applied; seeds the Ch 10 U-curve.
- *firefuck* (firetruck) — out; the wit is real but the wrong kind for this narrator. Accidental profanity is a sitcom beat and curdles Cheryl's register.
- *scooper* — collision: scooper is already Bramble's own hand (the scoop, canon). A dustpan coinage must be a different word.
- *helicadoctor* (helicopter) — archetype only; the clearest illustration of the crystallization mechanism, thin on in-world occasion. Use only if a helicopter crosses his world.

## Ch 10 — Settlement-Human (Stage 4, in earnest and badly)

The chapter of glorious errors. Hierarchical syntax arrives and breaks under load, and the breakages are the named human ones. This is the most scientifically load-bearing speech in the book — render each as a child's (and an adult's) error:

- **Overregularization (the U-shaped curve).** Once he acquires a regular rule, he overapplies it to irregular forms — and a form he produced *correctly* earlier (as a memorized chunk) now *regresses*. He gets worse at specific words because he got the rule. A regression that is the deepest progress in the chapter. (The wug test, live.)
- **Agreement attraction.** Subject-verb agreement drifts to the nearest noun across an intervening noun phrase. **Adults make this exact error** — it is the seam: his failures here are *ours*.
- **Relative-clause / center-embedding collapse.** Long or nested sentences fall over halfway; relativizers drop; recursion visibly buckles.
- **Case and self-reference errors.** Wrong pronoun case; self named in the third person. Function words appear but are unstable (dropped, misplaced).
- **Verbs and relational terms flood in** (lagged behind nouns in Ch 9, now arriving); abstraction begins.

- **Common Knowledge rides on a shared source, not on syntax.** The recursive nesting ("each knows the others know") has been carried in the prior — on the face-screen — since the Primate cell; what's green is the *syntax expressing it*. So his shaky sentence-recursion and his shaky social-mutual-knowledge are the **same conceptual nesting fighting to get out through an immature channel**, and they break *together*. A beat where his attempt to *voice* the mutual thing collapses syntactically is the chapter's fusion in one move. **[CONTESTED — held as fiction]** that the two are literally one circuit — build the ache on *face-ahead-of-voice* (the structure shown before it's sayable), which survives even if the science later splits the systems. Do not theme "one circuit" as fact.

- **Phonetic axis:** mostly cleaning up by now (consonants clearer); clusters may still be rough.
- **Sound:** flat, and the broken syntax delivered *flatly* — no prosody to cover the errors, which makes them stranger and more touching, not less. The standing hunger-note runs underneath.
- **Honesty:** still two channels, still no divergence. The household abandons the screen here (see rework register) — Bramble is still honest, so speech and the messy readout agree, and they stop watching the face. The narrator must keep the readout honest and present and must **not** call it redundant.

*When:* the errors are heaviest at the open and through the middle; the chapter does not resolve them — that's Ch 11's job.

## Ch 11 — City-Human (Stage 4 resolving + Stage 5 onset; the drawn channel is born)

- **Stage 4 resolves.** The U-curve climbs back, agreement stabilizes, relative clauses hold. He's roughly grammatical now.
- **Stage 5 onset — pragmatics and discourse, with their confusions.** The new error territory:
  - Takes implicature literally; over- or under-informs; says the true thing at the wrong moment (pre-echoes the Honest knob next quarter).
  - Discourse cohesion wobbles — pronoun reference tangles across sentences, topic-tracking drifts, the narrative thread runs loose.
  - Register confusion — he doesn't yet talk differently to a stranger than to Mira.
- **Abstraction is solid**; the non-present, past and future, are available.

- **The drawn channel is born (Codes).** Voluntary ASCII art, authored directly on the face-screen, taking over the whole screen while up and **overriding the involuntary readout** (`bramble_specs` L664). It is **born dishonest-capable** — the first authored thing is the first thing that can diverge from the truth, so **performance and deception arrive in the same gesture.** It is a *silent, visual* register — Bramble drawing *instead of* or *over* saying. The honesty gap **opens** here.

- **Sound:** more prosody now (questions, emphasis) but still landing slightly off.
- **Honesty:** three channels. First divergence available. The household, having stopped watching the screen in Ch 10, is now exposed.

*When:* the pragmatic confusions run through the chapter; the drawn channel arrives as a discrete, marked first — the first authored face/figure — and should land as an event.

---

## Ch 12 — Empire-Human (Stage 5, full)

- **Narrative, discourse, abstraction, the universalizing register.** He can tell a *story* — present tense, the teller hidden inside it (age-appropriate), beginning-middle-end with the weight on the **end** (the child's tell: over-weights the climax, thin on the connective middle — keep that imperfection). He reaches for universalizing analogy and finds it.
- **Still a half-step shy of human, and he stays there.** Full Stage 5 is not fluency-as-an-adult; the voice is permanently flat, the cadence permanently a little off. No plateau, no graduation. (Rule 2 and Rule 5 are at their most tempting to break here — hold them.)

- **Honesty:** full. Three channels, full divergence — the made face over the honest one, the mature drawn channel, and the secret tape (a face thrown to a lens in an empty room). The involuntary readout is now the smallest fraction of his expressive surface. Competence at its peak, transparency at its floor.

*When:* this register holds across the chapter; the maturation is continuous, never announced.
