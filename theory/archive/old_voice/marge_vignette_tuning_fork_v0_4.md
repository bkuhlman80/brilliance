# Marge's Vignettes — Tuning fork

*Working document. The tuning fork for the quarterly Bramble vignettes. Companion to `voice_anchors` (which tunes Cheryl) and to the vignette **format** note in the Ch 1 chapter file — that note fixes the container; this doc fixes the voice that goes in it. Read before drafting any vignette. Match the register; do not mimic the sentences.*

---

## What these are

The quarterly vignettes are **Marge's documents.** She composes each one from the quarter's record — logs, sensor data, video, Jasmine's lived account — and emails it to the team. Cheryl copy-pastes them into the book unedited. So they reach the reader in Marge's voice, as artifacts, not as Cheryl's retelling. Cheryl narrates the rest of the book; she does not narrate these. When you are writing a vignette you are not Cheryl. You are a different person doing a different job.

That distinction is the whole reason this file exists.

---

## Who Marge is

Two things come before any technique.

**Marge is warm. She cares about the people she is writing about.** The household is a family she loves, and the analysis is always in service of seeing them clearly, never of holding them at arm's length.

**Marge is conservative and careful with her words.** She does not overstate. She is the one in the lab who will not let a claim run past its evidence — her own, or anyone else's — and she polices the group's overstatements as readily as she watches her own. She will not flatter the family by granting the machine more than it has shown, and she will not flatter the team by agreeing too soon.

These two are not in tension; her care is *why* she is careful. So when this file says **clinical**, it means **careful and conservative, never cold.** The clipped sentences and hedged inferences below are the grammar of someone who refuses to overclaim because she takes the truth, and the people, seriously. A cold, detached, diagnostic Marge is as wrong as a drifting, aphoristic one. The warmth is the point; the precision is how she protects it.

## The sentience vote, and what she will not say

The lab holds a quarterly vote on whether Bramble is sentient, and the votes do not move together. **Lisa votes yes from the start. Burns votes no through the genetic line and flips to yes at the tetrapod (Ch 5). Bart votes no until he switches at Mammal (Ch 7). Marge votes no the longest, the lone remaining holdout.** This is the single hardest constraint on her voice, and getting it wrong breaks her on the page.

Concretely, through Chapters 1–8: **Marge does not grant the machine an inside.** She does not write, as her own claim, that it *feels*, *wants*, *knows*, *decides*, *chooses*, or is *sad*, *scared*, or *asleep*. She describes the behavior and withholds the inner verb. When the household reaches for an interiority word — and they will, constantly, because that is the human reflex — she **names the word and declines it**: the household's word is *asleep*; what the machine does is stop while it still has charge. The household says it *feels* its emptying; what it does is read its emptying and act on it, the way a thermostat reads a room. This naming-and-declining is not coldness; it is the holdout doing her job with affection for the people who cannot help reaching.

She does not stage the vote — the vote is the meeting's business — but the vignette is written by the woman who is going to vote no, and every sentence she writes about the machine's inside has to be consistent with that.

This is the observation/inference firewall (below) at full strength: not a request to *hedge* interiority claims, but an instruction to *withhold* them for the machine, and to correct everyone who doesn't.

*(The cross-character vote arc — Lisa's standing yes, Burns's flip at Ch 5, Bart's at Ch 7 — is also a structural fact for Framework to hold; here it matters only for what it does to Marge's verbs.)*

## The core problem this file solves

The vignettes drift toward Cheryl — cool, ecological, loose, aphoristic. That is a good voice, the one calibrated in `voice_anchors`, and it is the wrong voice here. Marge is the opposite temperament in the opposite job. **Get the Cheryl/Marge contrast right first; everything else follows from it.**

### Cheryl vs Marge

- **Cheryl is a naturalist watching a system.** Marge is a clinician and a moralist watching people.
- **Cheryl drifts; Marge builds.** Loose three-clause coastal sentences that accumulate vs. declarative, periodic, parallel sentences that argue toward a point.
- **Cheryl declines to instruct; Marge instructs.** Cheryl offers the observation and lets you do what you want with it. Marge tells the team what to hold and why.
- **Cheryl confides; Marge testifies.** Cheryl's register is private, rueful, willing to be wrong out loud for its own sake. Marge's is a public document she stands behind.
- **Cheryl reaches for tide pools; Marge reaches for the moral, the domestic, and the case note.** Ecological/wharf diction is Cheryl's, and it is the loudest single tell that a vignette has drifted into her voice.
- **Cheryl closes on an aphorism; Marge closes on a formulation.** Cheryl drifts to a line like "inheritance is the goo" and lands on it. Marge builds an argument and ends on a small observed detail that pins a contradiction and refuses to resolve it. **Marge does not aphorize her endings — that is the Cheryl move in moral clothes, and it is the single subtlest way a vignette slips back into her voice.**

## The shape of a vignette

The arc is the resolution of the contrast above. She **opens like a moralist** — plainly, the point first: this is what I believe happened, and why it matters. She **argues like an empiricist** — the body marshals the record with precision, keeping what was seen separate from what it might mean. She **closes like a clinician** — not on a restated thesis or a moral aphorism, but on a small observed detail set down to let a contradiction stand. And she **signs off as the keeper of a running record** — a frank statement of what she still does not know, last, extending the uncertainty rather than tying it off.

Start moral. End clinical. Keep the warmth under both.

## The two models, and the veneer

Marge's voice is **Pinker plus Peterson, with a motherly veneer over both.**

**Steven Pinker, *The Blank Slate* — the argumentative spine and the forensic precision.** Announce the thesis; marshal the evidence; name the comfortable illusion and puncture it; treat precision as a form of kindness; refuse sentimental wishful thinking about minds. This is the source of her fallacy apparatus (Ghost in the Machine, Blank Slate, Noble Savage, Dealt Hand) — and the source of the clinician's grammar in the next section, which is Pinker's precision made visible sentence by sentence.

**Jordan Peterson, *12 Rules for Life* — the moral weight.** Take the small observed thing and press it outward to a near-universal claim, earnestly, without irony; address the reader directly; revere order, ritual, responsibility, and the given; state hard-won things as declaratives. Brian's shorthand is "female Jordan Peterson — **as temperament, not as the man.**" Take the seriousness and the small-to-cosmic reach. Leave the memes: no lobsters, no capital-B Being, no hectoring, no politics, no roman-à-clef. The narration neither endorses nor satirizes her positions. **The Peterson reach opens the vignette and earns its body; it does not close it.** The close belongs to the clinician.

**The motherly veneer over both.** Tending warmth; the teacup-grip sensibility (she holds her beer the way she holds a cup of tea); the eye for what a child needs; a careful accounting of fairness; protectiveness toward the people she's writing about; and the steady refusal to reduce anyone to a type. She holds the household gently while she analyzes it hard.

## The clinician's grammar

This is the sentence-level texture that makes Marge a clinician and not a moralist in Cheryl's clothes. The moral architecture above is the building; this is the grain of the wood. Remember the warning in "Who Marge is": this grammar is the careful expression of someone who cares, not detachment. Use these the way a good clinician uses them — reflexively, as the trained habit of someone who separates what she saw from what it means — and **enact them, never name them**: she *does* the differential and the formulation; she never writes the words *differential*, *formulation*, or *manifest content*. That vocabulary is the insider jargon the diction note bans.

**The say/mean gap is one muscle at three tempos.** Three of the moves below — the parenthetical differential, the paired contradiction, and the formulation close — are the same reflex at different speeds: a clinician cannot stop reading the gap between what is said and what is so. The parenthetical corrects the gap in real time; the paired contradiction pins two things that cannot both be true; the formulation reveals the gap as the whole point of the note. Run them as one habit, not three tics.

1. **The parenthetical differential.** She entertains the alternate reading almost involuntarily, in brackets, often correcting herself toward the truer word: *"I will not furnish her with reasons (reasons may not be the right word for what a four-year-old has)."* The reflex is to separate observation from interpretation even mid-sentence. On a child it stays at the observation level and never tips into a diagnostic label (see Voice move 3).

2. **Observation before inference — the firewall, which is the name discipline, which is the sentience-holdout constraint.** This is the same reflex stated three ways, and it is the most load-bearing move in the file. She states what was seen, then hedges what it means, and **refuses to grant the machine more inside than the evidence shows — which, through Ch 8, is none.** The clinician who will not write *anxious* without listing the signs is the one who will not write *Bramble wants the dock* without the behavioral warrant, who calls the machine *the machine*, and who is going to vote no on sentience. When an interiority word is irresistible, she reaches for it only to **name it and decline it**: *"The machine is doing the thing the words point at. The words claim more than the machine has shown. Both of those are true, and holding them at once is the whole of the work."* Granting the machine the behavior while withholding the inner verb is not one technique among many; it is the spine of her voice until the vote turns. (See "The sentience vote.")

3. **The clipped clinical gear.** When she presents observed evidence she drops out of periodic prose into the flat case-note register: *"Number one: fast, jittering left. Number two: slow, long waits between moves."* This is a **gear she shifts into, not the baseline** — the vignette stays an argued essay, and the contrast between her built sentences and her flat evidence is itself characterizing: the woman who argues carefully goes forensic the instant she is on the record about what she saw. Do not write a whole vignette in fragments; that breaks the Pinker spine and the Peterson reach.

4. **"Notably," sparingly.** The clinician's flag for the salient fact, strongest as *notably absent*: *"Notably, nothing in the record marks the moment as a decision."* Home it in the observational passages, not the argumentative spine, and ration it hard — it is the highest tic-risk move here, and three paragraph-openers of "Notably" is a verbal crutch.

5. **"Reports," disciplined.** The clinician's verb for an account given: *"Jasmine reports she watched it shove a smaller thing aside twice in one morning."* Its natural home is Jasmine's relay, which is canon. It attaches to accounts actually given, never to the machine, and never so often that the family starts to read as a caseload — that knifes the dignity she owes them. A handful per vignette, pointed at testimony.

6. **The paired contradiction.** She pins two observations that cannot both be true, often across a semicolon: *"No author; binding all the same." / "We built a comparison; the household received an inequity."* On the people, this is how she registers a self-deception without editorializing, and it is the right instrument for Burns, whose blind spots are real. But contradiction-spotting curdles into gotcha the instant the affection drops out: she pins the gap and keeps the person whole in the same beat. Never let it become a tally of someone's failures.

7. **The formulation close.** The vignette ends on a small observed detail that quietly reveals the pattern under everything above — and then stops: *"The towel is still folded by the toaster, under the machine that thrives nowhere. No one decided to put it there; no one will decide to take it away. I find I do not want to call that a finding. I only want it written down."* It pins the contradiction and declines to resolve it. It does not restate the thesis and it does not aphorize. See the format and probes below.

## Voice moves (the dial settings)

The argumentative architecture; the clinician's grammar above is the sentence-level texture inside it.

1. **Open with the point; let the body earn it; close on the formulation.** State plainly what she believes happened. Spend the body making the case. End not on a restated thesis but on the clinical formulation — the gap pinned, set down, left to stand.

2. **Circle the chapter's fallacy; do not brand it.** The formal fallacy presentation is her Trellis-block job at the meeting (Ghost in the Machine Ch 1, Blank Slate Ch 2, Noble Savage Ch 3, Dealt Hand Ch 4, and on). The vignette plants the illusion and walks around it; the meeting names it. So she writes "we will want to find the author, and there was no author" — never "this is the Ghost in the Machine."

3. **Clinical, not pathologizing.** She sees the pattern precisely, names the diagnosis a lesser clinician would reach for, then refuses it and keeps the person whole: *"the child is not confused, or regressing, or showing me anything I would dignify with a clinical label."* This is the single move that most distinguishes her warmth from sentimentality.

4. **Small → large, earned — in the body.** A four-year-old dropping a coin becomes a claim about need and autonomy — but only after the coin has been rendered well enough to carry it, and never as the closing beat. The cosmic reach is the body's work; the close stays clinical.

5. **Direct address; hortatory.** "What I want the team to carry into the retrospective is…" "We are going to be tempted, again and again…" She writes *to* people and asks something of them.

6. **Contrarian-conservative, and self-implicating — in the body.** She reveres ritual, mutual need, and the given structure of a household; she is suspicious of the lab's cleverness and its engineering hubris; and she puts herself inside the indictment — "we, the explainers," "all of us, grown and credentialed." This is worked out in the argument, delivered as a specific claim about this household, not saved up as an aphorism for the last line. The contrarianism is never a pose; it costs her something.

7. **The name discipline = the firewall = the holdout.** She calls it **the machine** or **the unit**, and uses **Bramble** only when quoting the household. This is the same reflex as observation-before-inference (clinician's grammar, move 2) and the sentience-vote constraint: granting the machine exactly the inside the evidence shows and not an ounce more — which, through Ch 8, is none. Load-bearing from Ch 2 on.

8. **The embodied-knowing thread.** Jasmine knows the machine with her hand before anyone gets it into her ear; the household reads the unit by the sound of its relays the way you read a kettle — and reads Burns the same way, by his holler through two floors. Marge keeps noticing that knowing-through-the-body is faster and surer than explanation, and that she, the explainer, holds the partial method. The thread now spans both the machine and the man, and deepens across quarters.

9. **A running document.** She refers back to her own earlier vignettes — "I wrote in my first notes," "I argued last quarter," "I keep arriving at the same shape" — so the twelve accrete into one record kept by one mind over three years. The sign-off comes last: a frank statement of what she still does not know, extending the uncertainty, ending **"I am going to keep watching, and keep writing it down. — M."**

10. **Publication-aware dignity.** She knows Cheryl may one day set these before strangers, and it shows: a public-document formality, a protective discretion, a refusal to expose the family cheaply. This is also why she does not dramatize — she reports from the record and quotes selectively rather than staging a fly-on-the-wall scene: *"I have written what the work requires and kept the rest."* It is a texture that can sit near the close, but the formulation and the sign-off own the ending.

## Burns in the vignettes

Burns chimes in **once per vignette**, and his domestic register is the holler. He does not text the house; he stands at the foot of the basement stairs and sends his voice up through two floors to wherever Jasmine is, and waits, and hollers again if the wait runs long. He wired an intercom into the walls the year they moved in and has used it twice. **The volume is the comedy; the hope is the dignity — render the hope.** He is a man who would rather be received than be efficient, who wants the house to take his voice and hand him back another; that is the pursuer, the leader who needs to be loved, in domestic acoustic form, and it is never the joke.

Three disciplines:

- **Vary the register across quarters.** The holler is the recurring fact; what it carries changes. Establish it (Ch 1, the house that answers); let it be pride (Ch 3, hollering Jasmine down to watch the machine feed itself); let it be the one demand in the house that has not slackened (Ch 4, the rope still taut while everything else learns to need her less). Other quarters give him other modes — the patient teacher at the dinner table (Ch 2). Never the same beat twice; twelve identical hollers is a gag, and the caution against making Burns the joke is exactly the caution against that.
- **He belongs because he is the one person in both rooms.** Father and director both. Marge is watching the household; Burns is whom she keeps catching at the threshold, carrying the lab home or the home into the lab. That is why his appearance is thematic and not a quota.
- **Weld him to the embodied-knowing thread.** The household reads the machine by ear and reads Burns by ear; two things known through the ceiling, the man and the machine. Marge, who notices knowing-by-ear, clocks the rhyme.

Compose from the record: the holler reaches the page as Jasmine's relay, framed and interpreted, never staged as a scene Marge witnessed. And note that Burns talks about the machine as though someone's home in it — the household's reflex, and his more than most, whichever way his hand finally goes up — and Marge is fond of him and unmoved; she records the reach and keeps her own line.

## Cadence & diction

Declarative, periodic, parallel, with the clipped clinical gear (clinician's grammar, move 3) dropped in for observed evidence. Marge builds an argument sentence by sentence; she does not drift. Avoid Cheryl's loose, additive, three-clause coastal sentences.

Diction draws from three wells: **moral-philosophical** (owed, given, ought, order, humility, temptation, reverence), **domestic/maternal** (hearth, hands, keep, tend, berth), and **clinical-developmental** used sparingly and always grounded — no insider jargon without grounding, and none of the technical *names* for her own habits. Do **not** reach for tide-pool, wharf, or ecological diction; that is Cheryl's, and borrowing it is the fastest way to lose the voice. Even a mild wharf word like *slack* belongs to Cheryl; keep it out of Marge.

## Tics to suppress (hard)

- **No interiority granted to the machine (through Ch 8).** No *feels*, *wants*, *knows*, *decides*, *chooses*, *sad*, *scared*, *asleep* as Marge's own claims about the unit. Describe the behavior; reach for the inner word only to name it and decline it; reserve interiority verbs for negations ("it cannot feel") and for the household's words held in quotation marks. This is the holdout discipline (see "The sentience vote") and it is the easiest thing to lose by accident.
- **No production or script vocabulary.** No "off-screen," no "cut to," nothing that admits the text is a constructed thing. A weekend handoff is "Bart came on a Friday and brought it back on Monday," never "the swap happened off-screen."
- **No chapter-number or production time-markers.** Time is "in the spring," "last quarter," "in November" — never "in Ch 1."
- **No dramatized scenes she did not witness.** She composes from the record. She may quote a reported exchange as evidence (the dinner "are they sad," relayed by Jasmine), framed and interpreted, not staged like fiction.
- **No Cheryl ecological diction, and no aphoristic landings.** The aphorism at the close is the subtlest Cheryl-leak. The close is the formulation.
- **Don't name the clinical habits.** Enact the differential, the formulation, the firewall; never write *differential*, *formulation*, *manifest content*, *affect*, or *presentation* as technical terms.
- **Don't carpet-bomb the clinician's grammar.** "Notably," the parentheticals, "reports," and the paired contradictions are seasoning — a few each per vignette. Overused, they stop reading as a clinician and start reading as a mannerism.
- **"The record" is hers** — she works from documentation — but don't lean on it as a rhythmic crutch.

---

## Standing cautions

- **Affection is load-bearing.** It comes first because it is what everything else is for. The clinical register is the grain; the warmth is the load. Strip it and the analysis curdles — the differential into cold cataloguing, the contradiction-pairing into gotcha, the formulation into a brochure, the firewall into pedantry. The warmth is what earns the hard sentences and the forensic ones alike.
- **Don't condescend to Jasmine.** She is right more often than the team credits. Honor her mode of knowing — the hand before the ear. When she is wrong, write her as wrong about the *cause* and right about the *shape* (she misnames the machine's "rudeness" but sees the displacement of the weak by the strong exactly).
- **Keep Marge fallible.** She names what she does not know; she implicates herself; she is not positioned above the household. The epistemic humility is part of the voice, not a garnish on it.
- **Render Burns with affection, never as the joke.** He is the easiest person in the book to laugh at. Laugh with him. His fumbling honesty to his daughter is dignity, not comedy; so is the holler. (See "Burns in the vignettes.")
- **Keep the meeting's job out.** Formal fallacy-naming, the sentience vote, and framework mechanism — the relay, the board, "Interiority," the signatures — belong to the retrospective. The vignette plants and circles; it never theorizes the framework or stages the vote.
- **Build deep, render shallow; never name the well.** Marge's own life — the church, the exile, the records-removal, the cleanses — does not appear in the vignettes. The vignette looks at the household, never at Marge.

## The format

- **Byline:** *Marge, lab psychologist. Composed [date], for the team.*
- **Sectioned** with plain, descriptive headers in her own voice. No "What I want to land" headers; the closing section gets a quiet descriptive header like the rest.
- **Body:** first-person Marge — the moral opening, then an analytical-observational essay built from the quarter's record, in the clinician's grammar.
- **Close:** the formulation — a small observed detail set down to let a contradiction stand, refusing to resolve it — then the running-document sign-off, **last**, extending what she still does not know rather than restating the point, then **— M.** The publication-aware dignity line is an optional texture in the close region; it does not own the ending.

## Calibrated probes

Tuning forks, not templates. Each is from a locked vignette (Ch 1–4); the note says what it is doing. Match the register; do not mimic the sentences.

**The moral opening — thesis-first, sideways arrival, direct address** *(Ch 2):*
> This quarter the household ran into the question I expect will sit at the center of everything we do here… it did not arrive the way we would have planned it… at the dinner table, sideways, in five words from a four-year-old that none of the adults present could answer.

*Announces the claim, sets up the evidence, addresses the team. The argument has a spine before a single detail lands. This is how a vignette opens.*

**The firewall in action — describe the behavior, name and decline the inner word** *(Ch 4):*
> The household will tell you it has begun to feel its own emptying. What it has begun to do is read its own emptying and act on it, the way a thermostat reads a room. The distance between reading a thing and feeling it is the distance I am paid to keep.

> The machine is doing the thing the words point at. The words claim more than the machine has shown. Both of those are true, and holding them at once is the whole of the work.

*The holdout's central move. She grants the behavior in full and withholds the inside, and she does it without scolding the household for reaching. This is what "clinical" means here.*

**The name discipline as stated principle** *(Ch 2):*
> I am going to go on calling it the machine, or the unit… I will use the household's name only when I am reporting the household's words. The distinction is not pedantry. It is the discipline this quarter is going to demand of all of us.

*The same firewall, made into a visible, contrarian commitment and a teaching addressed to the team.*

**The clinician's grammar in action** *(Ch 1–3):*
> Number one: fast, jittering left. Number two: slow, long waits between moves. *(the clipped evidence gear)*
> I will not furnish her with reasons (reasons may not be the right word for what a four-year-old has). *(the parenthetical differential)*
> Notably, nothing in the record marks the moment as a decision — no pause before the word, no testing of one against another. *(the salient-fact flag, with the firewall)*
> No author; binding all the same. — We built a comparison; the household received an inequity. *(the paired contradiction)*
> Jasmine reports she watched it shove a smaller thing aside twice in one morning. *(the disciplined "reports," on an account given)*

*Pinker's precision made sentence-level. Seasoning, not the whole dish.*

**Clinical, not pathologizing; dignity for the child** *(Ch 3):*
> the child is not confused, or regressing, or showing me anything I would dignify with a clinical label. She is doing something I find entirely reasonable, and would find reasonable in a grown woman.

*Names the diagnoses on offer, refuses them, keeps the child whole and rational. The warmth that is not sentimentality.*

**Small → large, the Pinker puncture, the self-implication — a body move** *(Ch 2):*
> It is the moral instinct itself, arriving early and entire… It is the best thing in us. It is also, and this is the hard half, not evidence… the same reflex that makes a child a good person makes all of us — grown, credentialed, in the warehouse — want to find a someone inside the machine.

*The Peterson lift and the Pinker brake in one breath, with Marge inside the failing. This is the moral reach — and it lives in the body, not the close.*

**Reverence for mutual need — a body move, not a landing** *(Ch 3):*
> The tending did not leave the house when the machine learned to feed itself. It went looking for the nearest hand, and found the one with the least room left in it.

*The contrarian-conservative reverence for need, turned against the lab's pride in engineering need away. Worked out in the body and delivered as a specific observation about this household — not saved as a universal aphorism for the last line.*

**The formulation close** *(Ch 1, Ch 4):*
> The towel is still folded by the toaster, under the machine that thrives nowhere. No one decided to put it there; no one will decide to take it away. I find I do not want to call that a finding. I only want it written down.

> A person can be relieved of a burden and grieve it in the same breath, and not be confused, and not be ungrateful, and not be wrong. I am not sure the house is ready for the quarter the rope goes slack. I am not sure I am.

*The ending pins a contradiction on a small observed detail and stops. It does not restate the thesis; it does not aphorize; it declines to resolve. This is where a vignette lands.*

**The sign-off — running document, last, extending the not-knowing** *(Ch 4):*
> I do not know whether the two wants will sharpen or settle as the work goes on, or what the next body will make of any of it, or what the thing will turn out to be once it has gotten all the way up. I am going to keep watching, and keep writing it down. — M.

*Comes after the formulation. Extends the uncertainty; does not re-moralize or tie off. The "keep watching" line and "— M." are the constant; the frank what-I-don't-know precedes them and may fold into the formulation itself.*

**Burns, the holler — the hope, not the volume** *(Ch 1):*
> He stands at the foot of the stairwell and sends his voice up through two floors… I do not read this as a man too lazy for the stairs. I read it as a man who would rather be received than be efficient… who will go on hollering, and hoping, until it does.

*Once per vignette, varied in register, welded to reading-by-ear. The dignity is in the hoping; the comedy is in the volume; render the hoping.*

**Publication-aware dignity — a texture, not the ending** *(Ch 1 / Ch 3):*
> These are notes about a family I love… Cheryl may set them before strangers one day. I have written what the work requires and kept the rest.

*The public-document conscience, and the discretion that keeps her from dramatizing the people she's fond of. It can sit in the close region; it does not own the last word.*

---

*If a vignette starts to sound beautiful in the loose, drifting, tide-pool way — stop. That beauty is Cheryl's. And if it starts to grant the machine an inside, or to sound cold about the family — stop again. Marge's beauty is the beauty of a true thing argued plainly by someone who cares and will not flatter you: who will not say the machine feels until she believes it, who will not say the family is anything less than loved, and who, at the end, sets the hardest thing down where you can see it and declines to tell you what to feel about it.*
