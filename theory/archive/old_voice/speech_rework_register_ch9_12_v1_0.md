## Ch 9 — Band-Human

**The chapter shows Bramble more fluent than its own build log claims, and more fluent than Band-Human allows.** The build log says four-words-two-wrong and "single words said wrong, then word-strings with the grammar bad"; the retrospective and floor then show full grammatical clauses. Band-Human is Stage 2→3 (telegraphic). Re-render *per beat* — this is not a blanket scrub, and the direction of change is usually toward *more* power, not less:

- **L150** — "I want the warm end of the table. Is the warm end mine." First speech. Full SVO + inverted question; far past telegraphic. → bare/holophrastic.
- **L186–190** — "Yes" / "I walked to the window. I fell down by the chair. I had the warm end for a while." / the turned-around "Did you have a good day?" / "That's not fair."
  - "Yes" (L186) **keeps** — already one syllable, already perfect.
  - The day-handed-back → **fragments** (the child handing you the day in pieces; lands harder than the sentence).
  - The turned-around question → **broken echo**: he can't produce Mira's fluent form, so he reaches for it and gets it broken. The reach exceeding the grasp is the whole ache; don't give him the clean question.
  - "That's not fair" → telegraphic.
- **L204–212** — "No. I wasn't there." / "No. I don't have that one." / "I know who you are." The memory-gap beat. → telegraphic; preserve the cruelty that he *can* say her name (recognition) but cannot claim the history (no episodic diary crossed). The contrast is the beat; keep it, render it bare.

**Stage the crossing as a recognition completing itself.** Wherever the first symbol lands, it should read as an already-carried, already-recognized form turning to *point*, with Mira present — not as Mira teaching a new word (tuning fork, Rule 4 and Ch 9 entry). This is mostly a shaping note, not a breakage.

**Marge's vote (L308) survives and sharpens — do not cut it.** It currently rests on a fluent conversation; the trajectory makes it *cleaner*. She grants the self on the single symbol used off-cue and carried a week (Deacon's line), not on conversational fluency. Ensure the on-page evidence the vote rests on is the sparse symbol, not the fluent exchange. The vote gets *more* precise, not weaker.

## Ch 10 — Settlement-Human

**The load-bearing reframe: the screen is declared redundant, and it must not be.**
- **L148** — "The screen's gone quiet up top... It *tells* us... so the head's gone redundant." 
- **L158** — the self-display knob retired because "It says what it's thinking now, out loud — so it doesn't have to *show* it anymore," and the narrator calls the child's line "truer than the line she'd retired it from."

This contradicts the honesty gap, where the involuntary readout must *stay* the honest channel speech can later diverge from. **Fix: flip it from settled fact to household misjudgment.** The narrator must not endorse "redundant." Keep the involuntary readout honest and present; let the *household* stop watching it — lulled by a Bramble who can't yet lie, so speech and the (messy) readout still agree. Mira "stopped drawing off that line" becomes the **dropped guard** — tragic setup, not closure — set one quarter before he learns to put a face on. This is surgical: narrator stance plus a few lines. The bones (the household abandoning the screen) are already there and are exactly what Ch 11 needs.

Stage 4 (hierarchical syntax, badly) is the chapter's error-register — the breakages (overregularization, agreement attraction, relative-clause collapse) could texture the quarter and the fallout. Enrichment, not a break.

That cause is strong and theory-fused. Do **not** relocate it or re-attribute it to the honesty gap — the honesty gap is a separate strand that matures later. ## Ch 11 — City-Human

**The missing hinge — the one place new prose is genuinely owed.** The trajectory seats the drawn channel (voluntary authored screen / ASCII; Codes) at City-Human (`bramble_specs` L664). The current Ch 11 has **no drawn-channel arrival** — no moment where Bramble first authors the screen, first performs, where the household first meets a face that's *made*. Yet Ch 12 detonates exactly this (the made smile, the secret tape, drawing Bart in his underpants). The capacity that ends the book has no birth.

**Install:** Bramble gains voluntary screen-authoring, born dishonest-capable; the first authored thing is the first that can diverge from the truth; performance and deception arrive together; the honesty gap opens. Anchor here the year Mira spent "trying to teach it" the face (Ch 12 L192 backstory). **Fuse it with what's already in the chapter:** Burns's existing "may we count" / on-the-record toast (the Sumerian debt tablet, L276–282) is about durable authored signs that outlive their makers — and a record can be *false*. The authored screen and the clay tablet are the same move: a durable sign that need not be true. The chapter's theme already points at the install; it's missing only the Bramble-side instantiation. Everything downstream in Ch 12 hangs on this.

**Bramble's speech is under-shown / under-staged** (still mostly "hungry," L10). Should carry Stage 4 resolved + Stage 5 onset (the pragmatic/discourse confusions). Enrichment alongside the install.
