# Prediction for Chapter 9

## Duration & Attention
Expect a chapter of substantial length (somewhere in the 2000-2500 word range, consistent with Chs 3, 4, 5, 7, 8). The pacing will dwell longest on moments of perception or negotiation—moments where Mira reads a thing or decides something. Fast passes over setup; lingers on consequences realized. Will likely open with action already underway rather than establishing scene.

## Register
The voice maintains throughout: declarative, unsentimental, densely packed. Sentences build from observation to interpretation without hedging. No qualifiers softening the logic. Tight, matter-of-fact cadence broken by occasional longer declaratives when something shifts. The prose is clipped but not cold—the clipping comes from precision, not detachment. Minimal adjectives; the voice finds descriptors in verbs and structure.

## Concreteness
Highly concrete. The chapter will open with something physical—a location, an object, an action the body performs. Will stay rooted in objects and spatial positioning. Will not drift into abstraction. Any internal state will be shown through what is done or left undone, not named directly (with rare exceptions where the feeling has been earned through repeated pattern).

## Interiority
This thread does not name feelings. It shows them through action, positioning, choice, and through what gets narrated versus left silent. Mira's interior state emerges as the reader watches what she does, what she builds, what she abandons, what she guards. Expect the new chapter to follow this rule strictly—what she wants, needs, or feels will be demonstrated, not stated. Her mind will appear only through her decisions and their consequences.

## Signature Moves
- Opening with a concrete scene already in motion
- The "all-at-once" recognition (how Mira knows things suddenly, whole)
- Paired opposites: cold/warm, moving/still, bright/dark, given/withheld
- Shifting from "she" narration into moments where her voice bleeds in—direct statements that are plainly hers
- Extended observations of the bot's behavior or readouts, followed by her interpretation
- The pattern of testing something (usually through repetition or controlled variation), then taking the finding as certain
- A situation established, a problem implicit, then her solution offered without fanfare
- Quiet moments on the floor, often with the bot or alone, where something invisible shifts

## What This Voice Does Not Do
- It does not hesitate in its judgments, though it may revise them
- It does not describe beauty or ugliness—only what a thing is and what it does
- It does not explain why the adults allow things; it only notes that they do
- It does not apologize for Mira's logic or second-guess her readings
- It does not use sentiment language (never "sweet," "cruel," "beloved," etc.)
- It does not move backward; momentum is always forward into what comes next

## The Gap the Voice Holds
The narrator knows Mira entirely from the outside—what she does, what she says, what she builds, what she leaves. The narrator has never once told us Mira's feeling except as consequence (relief, satisfaction). There is an enormous interior to Mira that the voice declines to enter. This is consistent and intentional. Expect it to hold.

## Prediction for Chapter 9

**Confidence: High**

The chapter will likely open with Mira either discovering something new about how the bot reads her, or testing further the limits she found in Ch. 8 (the places she can hide from the glass, or the ways the glass misreads what matters). 

Alternatively, it will open with a shift in the bot itself—a new capability, a new setting, a new readout she has to learn to interpret. The structure would follow the pattern: observe the new thing, work out what it means, test it, take the finding as settled fact.

The chapter will be grounded in physical space—likely the kitchen or a specific corner of the house. Will dwell on a problem Mira has identified and her method of solving it. There may be a moment where an adult (Jasmine, Bart, or the sitter) does something that misses what Mira knows, and the voice will note the missing without comment.

There will be a moment of stillness—her alone or with the bot, or both of them waiting. The silence will hold meaning.

The register will be steady and cool throughout. No surprises in diction; the surprise will be in what Mira does, not in how it is told. The ending will not resolve a problem; it will position her at the threshold of something next.

**Basis**: Eight chapters of consistent voice that does not vary in tone or stance, though it deepens its focus. The voice has taught us to expect: Mira observes, Mira interprets, Mira acts, then the world shifts or doesn't. The next chapter will follow this shape. The thread has not yet shown us Mira failing at her own logic or being surprised by the bot in a way she cannot account for—those remain possible territories.
