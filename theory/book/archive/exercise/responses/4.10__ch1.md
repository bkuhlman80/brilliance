# Prediction for Ch 2

## Duration
Moderate length, 700-1000 words. Will dwell extended on a single moment or object — not a montage. The voice pauses to examine.

## Register
Shift downward from the warmth of Ch 1's close. The celebratory register (the toast, the laughter) will not carry forward. Instead: clipped, closer to the bone. The expansiveness of the speech will not repeat; the voice will move into flatter description and observation.

## Concreteness
Stays concrete but narrows its frame. Will name small physical things — a room's corner, a hand on something, a sound — rather than sweep across systems. The bot will likely recede. Objects will be mundane: a door, a timer, a screen, a face.

## Interiority
Inner state will be present but unnamed in the third person. Burns (or possibly another voice) will feel something — strain, doubt, the weight of the moment after — but the narrative will show it through silence and small refusals rather than naming it. "He did not say" is more likely than "he felt tired."

## Attention
Will linger on what happens in the room after the toast. Either the immediate aftermath (people gathering coats, the awkward shift from ceremony to dispersal) or the next morning, the space before work restarts. Will pass quickly over dialogue or explanations. What lingers will be the texture of waiting or the cost of having said something true.

## Silence
Will not explain what the funder situation is. Will not tell us who won this bid or whether the hard quarters have names. Will not show Cheryl on tape or what she sees. The voice maintains its opacity about the future.

## Signature
Short sentences return as control mechanism. Repetition of "the" + noun phrase as a way to pin things down (the lab, the room, the moment after). Verbs will shift to static or negative ones: stayed, remained, did not move. The rhythm will tighten from the gift-like ease of the speech.

## Confidence
**Medium-High** — the pattern of contraction after speech is strong, and the voice's refusal to explain itself is consistent. The shift from ceremony to its aftermath is the shape this voice tends to follow. 

**Basis**: Ch 1 opens mid-meeting (already inside), ends in a moment of earned grace, then laughs. The next move is always away from laughter, back into the weight of the thing itself. The voice is built on that oscillation — toward something, then the cost of having reached it.
