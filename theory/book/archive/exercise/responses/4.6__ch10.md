# Prediction for Chapter 11

## Duration
A single sustained beat, not rushed. The speaker will take her time and won't hurry through it. Likely moderate length — not a short report, but not the expansive narratives of Chapters 4 and 6. The pacing will favor the slow approach she's favored in recent chapters: a turn of the bottle, a pause, then what comes.

## Register
Plain. Clipped at the sentence level, expansive in the patience between them. The register here stays honest and reportorial. Not colored, not decorated. The words will be simple and the doing will be in the accumulation, not in any reaching for language.

## Concreteness
Physical and grounded. Jasmine speaks from the body — from what her hand feels on a surface, what she sees from the kitchen chair, what she hears in a relay click. The next chapter will stay at that level: specific objects, specific rooms, specific positions in space. The abstraction — if any — will arrive only after the concrete has been laid down.

## Interiority
Interior states will be present but constrained. When they appear, they'll belong to Mira or the machine, not to Jasmine herself. Jasmine's own interior — her fatigue, her thinking, her slowness to the task — will be readable only in what she does and doesn't say, in the gaps between her words. She will not name her own feeling.

## Attention
The eye stays close. It lingers on the small and repeatable — a hand on a back, a knob turned, a moment where the child checks before reaching. It does not linger on crisis or drama (those belong to earlier chapters). What it dwells on now is the ordinary work of living with the machine, the texture of cohabitation when the crisis is past.

## Silence
Jasmine will decline to explain what the changes mean, what they cost, whether they are good or bad. She will set the facts down and let others interpret. She will be especially silent about Mira's interior — about what the child is carrying, what she has learned, how she's changed. She will note the behavior (the checking, the hesitation) and stop there.

## Signature
Short sentences with long gaps between them. The bottle as a pause-mark — "She turned the bottle a quarter and did not drink it" — appears in nearly every chapter as her rhythm-keeper. Repetition of simple conjunctions: "and," "so," "because." The habit of understating by stating plainly: "It is a new one, baby," or "That's the whole of what changed." Minimal metaphor; when metaphor appears, it's grounded in the household itself (the warm spot, the doorway, the shelf where things live).

## Predicted Content

The speaker will give a report on the quarter that focuses on the machine's ordinary functioning and the household's adaptation to having it as a permanent resident. She will likely touch on:

1. **A plain behavioral observation** — something the machine is doing better, or differently, or still not doing. Probably related to a knob, or to movement through the house, or to how it responds to Mira's direction.

2. **Mira's relationship to the machine** — not the emotional crisis of Ch. 9, but the new normal after it. How the child has settled with the fact of the machine's newness, its forgetting. How she treats it now.

3. **A moment of household life** — mundane, repeatable, almost incidental. The machine in its spot. Someone passing it. The cat. The warm place.

4. **A quiet observation about the old one** (the one on the other side of the valley, the cleaner, the baseline). A marker of how far the new one has come.

5. **One specific piece of evidence** — a detail the speaker has been turning over, the way she does. Not a crisis, not a triumph. Just something she noticed and has been sitting with.

## Opening
The chapter will open with Lisa giving Jasmine the floor, or Jasmine taking it from the chair. A plain summary statement: "It was a good quarter" or "The house has settled," or something that sits between. Then the bottle, then the first concrete observation.

## Ending
The chapter will not resolve anything. It will end with an image — Mira and the machine, or the speaker in the kitchen, or a comparison between the two Brambles. It will be quiet. Someone else may speak after, but the weight of the chapter will rest in Jasmine's last word.

## Confidence and Basis
**Medium confidence.** The basis is the pattern of interiority, concreteness, and patience that Jasmine has established across ten chapters. She speaks from one room, from one body, from the slow accumulation of days. The threads of her voice are consistent enough to predict: no crisis language, no metaphor-reaching, no interiority beyond behavior. What's harder to predict is *which* detail she will have been sitting with, what the machine will have learned or forgotten or repeated, how Mira will have changed. The shape of the chapter is stable; the content is less so.
