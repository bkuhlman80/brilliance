# Prediction for Ch 2

**Duration:** Moderate length, likely 60-75% of Ch 1's span. Will dwell longest on a single concrete object or organism; will move through other examples more quickly than Ch 1 does.

**Register:** Clipped where Ch 1 was expansive. Sentences will shorten. The colored, reflective passages ("There is something tender about this") will appear but less frequently. More declarative. Less discursive.

**Concreteness:** High. Will open with a physical thing the narrator is holding, examining, or observing. Will stay close to surfaces and behaviors. Abstract framing will arrive later, if at all, and will be brief.

**Interiority:** Sparse. Inner states will be named only when unavoidable (the way "astonishment" appears in the *Deinococcus* section). Mostly will show the narrator's attention settling on what the thing does, not what the narrator feels about it. When feeling appears, it will be stated flatly, almost as a fact.

**Attention:** Will linger on a gap or boundary — the place where a system's design stops matching its behavior, or where an organism's capability exceeds what its structure should allow. Will move past secondary details fast.

**Silence:** Will not circle back to the thermostat. Will not directly address the question posed in the title. Will decline to moralize or resolve the tension opened in Ch 1's closing. Will avoid the word "alive" or any synonym.

**Signature:** Short declarative openings. Subordinate clauses stacked tightly. Technical terms embedded without apology. A repeated syntactic move: "The X is not Y. The X *is* Z." Moments of direct address to the reader embedded as asides. Will end not with resolution but with a fact left standing.

**Opening prediction:** Will begin with a narrator action (looking at, finding, or receiving something) rather than a general statement. Something smaller than a bacterium, or something the narrator has made or modified.

**Confidence: Medium-High.** The pattern of concrete observation + embedded abstraction + self-aware restraint is very stable across Ch 1. The signature syntax and the refusal to resolve are strong fingerprints. What is harder to predict: which specific system or object will anchor the chapter, and whether interiority will recede further or hold steady.
