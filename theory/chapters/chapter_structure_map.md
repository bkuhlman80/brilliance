# Chapter Structure Map

Section-by-section extraction across all files in `theory/chapters/`. For each heading: the basic topic, a rough narration-vs-dialogue split, and who is speaking.

A recurring structural note applies to every chapter: the dated scene-breaks and vignette dividers inside sections are horizontal rules (`---`), **not** markdown headings. Where a single heading covers many distinct vignettes (e.g. "Under Her Breath," "Two Old Sentences," "In Her Own Hand"), that is flagged inline. Each chapter uses two H1 headings — the chapter title and the "Notes on the Nth Retrospective" divider — with everything else at H2.

Two narrator conventions hold throughout: **Cheryl is the first-person narrator** of every one-on-one lunch scene and every closing "Reflection: What is …?" essay. Each lunch is Cheryl with one lab member. The lunch sections are titled by their **venue**, not by a character — "Fisher Brewing Company," "Urban Hill," "Pho Tay Ho," "The Roof," "Arlo," "Chuck-A-Rama," "Gourmandise," "Zest," "Water Witch," "Coffee Garden" are all restaurants/bars. Lunch pairings: ch_2 Burns, ch_3 Marge, ch_4 Jasmine, ch_5 Lisa, ch_6 Marge, ch_7 Jasmine (at Arlo), ch_8 Mira, ch_9 Bart, ch_10 Marge, ch_11 Lisa, ch_12 Burns.
