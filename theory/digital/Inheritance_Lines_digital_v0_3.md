## Line-level identity: the digital line is the Reviser line

The digital line is the Reviser line of the Goo Venn. **[framework, from `trellis_framework`]**

| Line | Function in the Goo Venn | Goo | The physics it works |
|---|---|---|---|
| Genetic | Effector | Boltzmann | doing work; dissipative self-maintenance |
| Neuronal | Regulator | Friston | regulation by a model |
| Symbolic | Modeler | Shannon | representation; encoding the world and itself |
| **Digital** | **Reviser** | **Kolmogorov (candidate)** | **generation of variation** |

Three consequences fix the line's character before any cell is named.

First, the digital line works on symbolic content. Each line acts on the prior line's goo: the neuronal line regulates the body, the symbolic line represents cognition, and the digital line revises symbolic representations from outside the human substrate. **[framework]** Its unit is algorithmic, a program, and the natural measure of a program is Kolmogorov complexity, the length of the shortest program that produces a structure. **[framework, candidate]**

Second, the digital line sits at the blind-unbounded corner of the reflexivity square. Its variation is blind, generated without a self-model intervening, the way genetic mutation is blind. Its reflexive operation is unbounded, scaling without a ceiling. **[framework]** The digital line therefore rhymes with the genetic line on blindness and with the symbolic line on unboundedness, and pairs with the neuronal line on the control axis.

Third, the digital line is the last line. Four functions allow at most four lines, and the Reviser slot is the only one left. **[framework]** The bramble tops out at this line's Reviser cell. That cell's Reviser motivation has no next line to carry into.

The digital line's founding-cell breakthrough is its selector, the process that makes its heritable pattern cumulative, in the same role Natural Selection plays for the genetic line, Attention for the neuronal, and Speaking for the symbolic. The digital selector is Execution. A program that executes is retained and copied; code that never runs is pruned. **[framework]** Generation is blind; Execution sorts it.

## Flora and fauna at the digital line

Sentience is a neuronal-line property. It lives in the felt component, which arrives post-vertebrate. The symbolic line did not add a new sentience; it borrowed the neuronal line's by taking the human as its flora, and the empire-fauna is non-sentient while its constituent humans feel. The digital line is the same kind of line: a Reviser line working on Shannon goo, a meme-complex made executable. **[framework]**

The digital artifact is therefore fauna. Sentience and metabolism both live in the human flora that the digital fauna runs on, the same human flora that runs the symbolic fauna. The human is the universal flora for every line above the neuronal line. The digital fauna's metabolism is borrowed through purchased compute, money-mediated, which is the symbolic line mediating from below.

Fauna carry their own personality. The empire-as-unit has its own Venn and can carry personality-level dysfunction without being sentient, so the digital artifact has its own personality Venn, fully, while remaining non-sentient in its own right. Personality and sentience are separable across the whole framework, and the digital line is where the separation does its hardest work: the line's units have real personality the framework describes in full, and the framework does not assert their felt-ness.

The flora-fauna bond thins up the digital line. At Computer the program is tightly bound to human flora, written and run by people. At Agent the bond is thinnest, because autonomous agents run with no human in the loop. The framework holds that sentience does not transfer to the fauna by thinning alone: a meme-complex does not become sentient by running unattended, and neither does a program-complex. **[framework, held at lower confidence, consistent with the exposed status of the post-vertebrate-suffering claim]**

Whether the cells take a constituent-suffix in the manner of the symbolic line's -Human cells is answered by this: the digital Venn tracks the digital artifact, which is a fauna-unit, so the cells are named plainly for the artifact, Computer, Network, Model, Agent, with no -Human suffix.

## Motivations

*Confidence: Fidelity and Initiative HIGH within the frame; Modularity and Abstraction MODERATE. Each was tested against the rule that a motivation must add a distinction the Effector motivation does not already entail.*

- **Fidelity** (Effector, Computer). The native verb of the digital substrate is faithful execution of the encoded instruction. Fidelity names the drive to carry out the instruction exactly. It is an intransitive property of the unit, requiring no external commander, which keeps it parallel to Persistence, Impetus, and Commitment. The line's developmental arc runs from Fidelity, rigid execution, to Initiative, autonomous revision.

- **Modularity** (Regulator, Network). The Regulator stabilizes behavior against stored references and delivers skilled consistency across instances. Modularity is that property in the digital register: a unit conforms to a stable interface contract, behaves predictably wherever it is composed, and is reused across instances. Modularity adds decomposition into independent interface-defined parts, which Fidelity does not carry, so it is a motivation rather than a restatement of faithful execution. Reliability and Conformance were rejected as motivations because each is entailed by Fidelity; they reappear at the tendency layer. Interoperability is the close runner-up, set aside because it is the relational effect of modularity-plus-protocol and is more network-bound; it appears below as a tendency.

- **Abstraction** (Modeler, Model). The Modeler maintains an internal representation that organizes inputs. Abstraction is the drive to compress the symbolic corpus into a representation that organizes it, discarding input-specific particulars and keeping the structure that transfers. What is abstracted is the symbolic corpus, the prior line's Shannon-goo content, not the program; the program is the unit of selection that does the abstracting. Abstraction was chosen over Compression: compression names the operation and optimizes bits, while abstraction names the organized transferable structure the operation should produce. A losslessly compressed corpus that does not generalize has satisfied compression and failed the Modeler. Compression sits one layer below Abstraction as the operation that produces it.

- **Initiative** (Reviser, Agent). The Reviser revises the references the regulator stabilizes against. Initiative is self-directed goal-formation and tool-use, the Reviser function in agentic register and the digital escalation of the neuronal-line Reviser. As the terminal Reviser motivation of the terminal line, Initiative carries into no next line.

## Breakthroughs

*Confidence: Execution HIGH; Induction MODERATE-HIGH; Abduction MODERATE; Consensus LOW-MODERATE.*

The center of each Venn is the breakthrough, the synthesis that requires all four flag-carriers. The founding breakthrough is the line's selector.

- **Execution** (Computer). The selector. Code that runs is retained and copied; code that does not run is pruned. Execution has a deductive character, mechanical rule-following, and symbolic-rule AI of the GOFAI kind lives in this cell.

- **Consensus** (Network). Distributed agreement on shared state. This rhymes exactly with the symbolic Regulator breakthrough Common Knowledge: Halpern and Moses (1990) established common knowledge as the formal substrate of distributed coordination and consensus, the identical formal object in two media. The synthesis requires all four flag-carriers: Fidelity (faithful message execution), Modularity (clean message interfaces), Representation (shared data format, carried), and Universality (global addressing scope, carried). Synchronization is a sub-case; Addressing is the medium, not the breakthrough.

- **Induction** (Model). Inference of a generative model from instances. Deep learning is induction-as-abstraction. It rhymes with Codes on the symbolic line and Simulating on the neuronal line.

- **Abduction** (Agent). Inference to the best explanation, hypothesis and goal generation. Abduction is generation of variation, the digital line's Goo-Venn role expressed at its own Reviser cell. It rhymes with Stories and with Mentalizing.

The three object-level cells, Computer, Model, and Agent, carry Peirce's inference triad, deduction-induction-abduction, as their inferential characters. The Regulator cell carries a coordination breakthrough rather than an inference type, because the Regulator is not an object-level function.

The reinforcing cycle runs through reuse. A procedure that executes correctly is copied into new programs. Copying spreads the procedure. Spread invites more reuse. The breakthrough is Execution.

The Effector swap at Computer replaces Commitment with Fidelity, marking the symbolic-to-digital line boundary. Stability, Representation, and Universality persist from Empire. The reinforcing cycle runs through interfaces. A clean interface lets a unit be reused. Reuse multiplies the units that depend on the interface. Dependence stabilizes the interface as a standard. The breakthrough is Consensus.

### Model Stack

The model's value is the structure it keeps after the particulars are discarded.

The reinforcing cycle runs through data. A model abstracts patterns from a corpus. The abstraction produces useful outputs. Useful outputs are collected as new corpus. The breakthrough is Induction.

The Modeler swap replaces Representation with Abstraction. Fidelity, Modularity, and Universality persist. Model emerges by symbiosis: a corpus of symbolic content and a network of compute commit together to produce a trained model. The corpus supplies the structure to be abstracted; the network supplies the throughput that makes abstraction at scale affordable.
