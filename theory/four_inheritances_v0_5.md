# The Four Inheritances

### The reflexivity square, the Reviser line, and gene / neme / meme

*Theory memo. v0.5. June 2026.*

This develops one pattern that `cybernetics_and_sentience` records but flags as "true but thin": the reflexivity gradient across the inheritance lines. The thinness lifts when the gradient is read as two axes rather than one. From there the memo derives a structural account of the four cybernetic functions at the line scale, reads the Reviser line as the tree of life's blind-variation engine, anchors the transition between lines as a binding event, names the heritable unit each line ratchets, identifies attention as that unit's selector at the neuronal line, places the unit in a four-level stack, and characterizes a conjectural fourth line.

**Epistemic posture.** The spine is framework-grounded: every structural claim about the four functions, their Venn edges, the goo mapping, and the four-line cap follows from material already in `cybernetics_and_sentience`. Several more are this memo's committed positions — well-motivated, and ready to fold into canon: the two-axis reading of reflexivity, the naming of the neuronal replicator, the inheritance-locus progression, and attention as that replicator's selector. One thing alone is held deliberately loose, per the doc's own guardrail that the framework "should not claim such a line is forming": the digital line as a real, occurring line. A consolidated ledger sorts every claim, and a precedents list sits at the end.

## The reflexivity square

`cybernetics_and_sentience` orders the lines by a single growing capacity: genetic is *blind* (genes cannot be about genes), neuronal is *bounded* (it models lower layers but cannot operate on the modeling-relation itself), symbolic is *unbounded* (operations become their own objects). The doc calls this true but thin, because it only ranks the lines by degree. It does not say why there are three, why they emerge in this order, or why the genetic and symbolic lines rhyme.

The thinness is an artifact of compressing two axes into one. Read as two binaries, the gradient becomes a square.

- The **sight** axis runs blind to unblind. Blind means the operation proceeds without aboutness or foresight — Donald Campbell's blind variation, which is also the doc's own sense when it says "variation is blind." Unblind means the operation is guided by a representation it can use.
- The **binding** axis runs bounded to unbounded. Bounded means the reflexive reach is closed: the line cannot operate on its own modeling-relation. Unbounded means operations become their own objects without limit.

Coding blind/unblind as 0/1 and bounded/unbounded as 0/1, the four cybernetic functions take the four corners.

| Line | Function | Sight | Binding | Goo | Inheritance unit |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Genetic | Effector | blind | bounded | Boltzmann | **gene** |
| Neuronal | Regulator | unblind | bounded | Friston | **neme** |
| Symbolic | Modeler | unblind | unbounded | Shannon | **meme** |
| Fourth | Reviser | blind | unbounded | Kolmogorov *(open)* | **program** *(open)* |

**The two axes are not new constructs. They are the two opposite-edge axes of the function ring already in the framework.** Walk the ring Effector–Regulator–Modeler–Reviser:

- The sight axis is Anticipator versus Explorer. Unblind is Regulator + Modeler, the Anticipator edge (model-informed look-ahead). Blind is Effector + Reviser, the Explorer edge (action that tries things, results revising what is tried next).
- The binding axis is Adapter versus Controller. Unbounded is Modeler + Reviser, the Adapter edge (the self-revising model). Bounded is Effector + Regulator, the Controller edge (closed-loop control with no model-revision).

Each function sits on exactly one edge of each axis, so its two bits are simply which two adjacent pairs it belongs to. This is why the coordinates are forced rather than chosen.

**The fourth corner is forced twice over.** Given the three established lines — genetic blind, neuronal (unblind, bounded), symbolic (unblind, unbounded) — exactly one corner of the square remains: (blind, unbounded). The same answer comes from the ring: the Reviser sits on the Explorer edge (blind) and the Adapter edge (unbounded). Corner-counting and ring geometry agree. The character of a fourth line is entailed the moment reflexivity is read as two bits.

**The XOR recovers a canon axis.** Take the parity of the two bits. Parity zero is the genetic and symbolic corners, both object-level, the Connector diagonal. Parity one is the neuronal and fourth corners, both control-level, the Operator diagonal. The genetic–symbolic rhyme the doc credits to the Goo Venn falls straight out as shared parity. The square is the finer grid beneath the object/control axis, not a competitor to it.

**The order is forced too.** Sight must flip before binding, because unbounded reflexivity is reflexivity about a model, and a line cannot have it before it has the model. So the traversal is 00 → 10 → 11, not 00 → 01 → 11. Then symbolic to fourth flips sight back off: generation goes foresight-free again while staying unbounded. This is the explanatory teeth the monotonic reading lacked.

## The Reviser is the variation half of Darwinism

The doc already holds the pieces and does not quite assemble them. The Reviser sits on the Explorer edge (blind generation) and the Adapter edge (unbounded revision). Blind generation plus unbounded revision is Campbell's blind variation and selective retention, split in two. **The Reviser is the blind-variation engine. Darwin, at the center of every Venn, is the selective retention.** The framework already guards this seam: it rejects assembly-style measures for the Reviser goo precisely because they are "selection-flavored and would risk colliding with Darwin at the center." So the commitment is already in place — Reviser is generation of variation, Darwin is selection.

**This is the memo's spine, and it is Campbell's.** Donald Campbell generalized selection beyond biology: blind variation and selective retention is the engine of any process that increases fit, knowledge and culture included — his evolutionary epistemology. Reading each inheritance line as the same engine running in a different substrate is that thesis made structural. The Reviser is Campbell's blind variation; Darwin is Campbell's selective retention; the four lines are four substrates the one engine runs in. Everything downstream — gene, neme, meme, program — is a single Darwinism wearing four materials.

So a blind, unbounded Reviser line is not an exotic fourth thing. It is the oldest thing in the tree — blind variation, the engine that has driven every line — lifted to operate at the scale of the lines themselves, on the tree's own accumulated output.

The master pattern each line instances: **a Reviser takes its line's signature achievement and runs it in blind, unbounded mode, producing a combinatorial explosion whose residue is the raw material the next line inherits.** The Reviser is always the line reaching past itself.

## The cycle and the path

There are two faithful projections of the square, at two scales, and they are not in conflict.

At the **clade scale**, the function ring closes at every line boundary. Within each line the cells run Effector, Regulator, Modeler, Reviser; the Reviser cell then bridges to the next line's Effector cell. So the square is traversed as a full cycle, once per line, and the closing edge — Reviser to Effector, the blind edge — is realized as an actual clade transition. Two such wraparounds are realized inside the established book: Eumetazoa to Bilaterian, and Primate to Bands. A third, Empires to Computers, is the conjectural bridge into a fourth line.

At the **goo scale**, the projection is a path. Each line ratchets a different goo — Boltzmann, Friston, Shannon, Kolmogorov — none recurring, four and done. The four-line cap is a statement about this projection.

So both readings are true. The clade lens sees the wraparound edge traversed; the goo lens sees four distinct ratchets. The cap's entire content, stated cleanly: **the wraparound is allowed to close three times across four lines, and a fourth time is forbidden.** The forbidden one is narrow and specific — the bridge *out* of the fourth line, which would seed a fifth goo and a fifth line. Everything else about the cycle is consistent with the cap. The tension dissolves into a scale distinction: cycle at the clade scale, capped at the goo scale.

**The wraparound is a binding event, and its anchor is the egg.** The edge from Reviser to Effector is the Explorer pairing — blind exploration, action that tries things with results revising what gets tried next. Across it the *binding* bit flips from unbounded to bounded while *sight* stays blind: open-ended generation becomes controlled founding execution, no foresight gained on the way. The anchor is the fertilized egg in search of implantation, or the newborn in search of swaddling — the unbound new thing seeking the binding that makes it viable. This is not only a metaphor but an instance: recombination is blind variation, it produces an unbound totipotent unit, and implantation is the binding that turns open-ended potential into a controlled developmental program. Biology runs a Reviser-to-Effector wraparound every time an organism is conceived.

What the anchor names most deeply is that a line's culmination becomes the next line's infancy. The Reviser is a line's most-evolved point; its product is the next line's rawest, so maturity hands off to vulnerability — the egg, made by mature gametes, is the most fragile form there is. That vulnerability is structural, not sentimental: the founding Effector's own signature is "regulatory layer not yet stabilized," so the new line begins unregulated and stays raw until its Regulator cell arrives. The egg is the unit crossing the edge, unbound at departure and bound on arrival, blind the whole way; "in search of" is the Explorer running under selection, mostly failing, since most eggs never implant. The implantation image sits at the binding-flip itself, the swaddle a beat later where regulation arrives at the Effector-to-Regulator step. The structure is the framework's; the weight — whether a line-birth lands as tender or frightening — is Voice's, and the book already stages each line transition as a new chassis, so the birth reading is there to take or leave. The anchor holds firmly for the two realized wraparounds, Eumetazoa to Bilaterian and Primate to Bands, and extends, held loose with the rest of the fourth line, to Empires to Computers.

## The three kernels

Each line's Reviser cell is a blind, unbounded version of the line itself. These are pattern-generators for the fourth line, so they are kept compact. The Reviser motivation and the bridge residue are noted because the residue is what the next line inherits.

**Eumetazoa — the blind, unbounded genetic line.** Reviser motivation Excitability; the bridge residue is the Reflex Repertoire. The genetic line's engine is heritable variation; at its Reviser cell the engine turns on multicellular organization itself, through differentiation — the Reviser's literal evolutionary signature, division of labor among parts. Run unbounded, that is the Cambrian explosion of body plans. Run blind, it is variation without foresight, most of the disparity pruned by extinction. The residue is excitable nervous tissue, which the neuronal line inherits and organizes into a brain.

**Primate — the blind, unbounded neuronal line.** Reviser motivation Affiliation; the bridge residue is the Sociometer. The neuronal line's engine is modeling; at its Reviser cell it turns modeling on other minds and on its own modeling. Run unbounded, that is the social-brain arms race, deeper mentalizing selecting for deeper mentalizing with no recursion ceiling. Run blind, it is a fitness-blind runaway — the line trades ecological range for cognitive depth and pays in lost territory. The residue is the social-reflex repertoire, which the symbolic line inherits and organizes into a superorganism.

**Empire — the blind, unbounded symbolic line.** Reviser motivation Universality; the signature is Identity. The symbolic line's engine is shared representation; at its Reviser cell it turns representation universal, into self-propagating meme-complexes that scale past anyone who could vouch for them. Run unbounded, that is universalizing ideology, open-ended and recombining. Run blind, it is the framework's non-sentient fauna: the meme-complex propagates by differential survival among ideologies, indifferent to the welfare of the constituents it runs on. The residue is the externalized, universalized symbolic corpus, which the fourth line would inherit.

The residues march the locus of inheritance one step further out each time: germline tissue, then the modeling body, then the externalized record.

## The four inheritances: gene, neme, meme, and the program

Each line is selection-mediated inheritance of a different replicator, in a different substrate, at a different transmission locus. Three of the four replicators want short, memorable names, and the family rhymes by design.

**gene** — the genetic line. A molecular replicator, Dawkins's unit of selection, that varies by mutation, is selected by differential reproduction, and is transmitted across generations through the germline. Darwinian inheritance. The established case.

**meme** — the symbolic line. A symbolic replicator, Dawkins's coinage, that varies by innovation and copying error, is selected by differential adoption, and is transmitted across minds. Cultural inheritance. The other established case.

**neme** — the neuronal line. This is the unit the framework runs on at the Vertebrate cell without naming: the reinforced operant. Skinner's "Selection by Consequences" is the missing argument — operant conditioning is a second Darwinism, selection by consequences inside a lifetime. The operant varies by behavioral exploration (the Explorer edge, blind), is selected by reinforcement (a within-lifetime Darwin), and is retained by consolidation into procedural memory (the doc's "second nature"). Blind variation and selective retention, in the substrate of agent-policies. Naming it makes the neuronal kernel as tight as the genetic and symbolic ones: every line is the same Darwinian engine in a different substrate.

The name **neme** takes the *n* of neural and the *-eme* that siblings it onto gene and meme. The order reads correctly — gene, neme, meme is genetic, neuronal, symbolic. The word also has a pedigree: Semon's *mneme* (1904) was an early term for the unit of memory, the parent of the engram, and *neme* is that word with the unpronounceable silent *m* removed. The memory connotation is apt, because within-lifetime memory is the neuronal inheritance channel — the neme is the operant that was consolidated and kept. *(Etymological care: Dawkins built "meme" from Greek mimeme, "the imitated thing," not directly from Semon. Mneme and meme are cousins through the memory root, not parent and child. The pedigree for "neme" is real; the lineage should not be overstated.)*

**program** — the digital line. The unit is the program: a generative recipe in the Kolmogorov sense, the shortest code that produces a structure. It varies by recombination, is selected in whatever ecology it competes in, and is copied with perfect fidelity, from outside any mind. Three properties separate it from the meme. It is generative rather than static — a recipe that produces, not a representation that means. It is blind — form severed from reference, the sight-flip made concrete. And it is copied losslessly, which is the mechanism of *unbounded*: gene, neme, and meme all degrade in transmission and so carry a complexity ceiling, and lossless copying removes the ceiling. The program is the replicator; what it generates is the phenotype, the way the gene and not the protein is the genetic unit. Held loose, with the rest of the fourth line; treated in full below.

### The neme, developed

**It matures across the line, operant to internal model.** The model-free reading (Vertebrate, Reinforcing) gives the operant — selection on overt behavior. The model-based reading (Mammal, Simulating) gives a second form of the same logic — the tested hypothesis, Popper's "let your hypotheses die in your stead," selection on simulated behavior before a body is committed. The neme matures from operant to internal model, tracking the within-line move Vertebrate to Mammal, the way the meme matures across its own cells from Speaking to Stories.

**Dennett's Tower is the backbone for the whole inheritance arc.** Generate-and-Test runs Darwinian, Skinnerian, Popperian, Gregorian, and that maps onto the lines almost one to one: Darwinian is genetic, Skinnerian is the neuronal model-free neme, Popperian is the neuronal model-based neme, and Gregorian — named for Richard Gregory's mind-tools — is the symbolic meme. The Tower places the neme in a principled sequence and ties the arc to a named framework rather than an ad hoc one.

**A boundary to keep.** Temperament is not the neme. Temperament and behavioral syndrome are substantially genetically heritable — the genetic line's reach into the neuronal substrate, the parameter settings that bias which operants get learned. The neme is the *learned* policy, the line's own acquired replicator. Keeping these separate matters; conflating them is part of why this kernel read as hazy.

**Transmission: operant, then tradition.** Genes cross between bodies; memes cross between minds. The neme uses two channels, and they are ordered rather than rival.
- *Within a lifetime,* via memory. The neme passes from past-self to future-self by consolidation. This is the cleanest distinction from both neighbors: the neuronal line is the first line where an agent inherits from its own past, interiorizing inheritance before the symbolic line re-exteriorizes it. On its own it does not accumulate across generations — which is exactly what the second channel adds.
- *Across generations,* via animal tradition. Socially-learned behavior — birdsong dialects, tool-use cultures, foraging traditions (Whiten; Laland) — accumulates across generations without symbols, giving the neuronal line a between-agents channel parallel to genes and memes. It stays distinct from the meme by a sharp criterion: transmitted by imitation of behavior, with no shared symbol system or we-mode.

Both hold, in order. The operant is the within-lifetime neme; the animal tradition is the socially-learned operant, the bridge residue the symbolic line inherits and symbolically encodes. The progression operant → tradition → meme lands exactly on the Primate-to-Bands handoff, the Sociometer's social reflexes becoming culture. It is not either/or; it is the maturation of the neme into the meme.

**The neuronal line's inheritance is lifetime-bounded.** It accumulates across deep time only by borrowing a neighbor's channel — genetic assimilation (the Baldwin effect) downward, or animal tradition and culture upward. Genes and memes accumulate on their own across generations; the operant, alone, dies with the organism. This is the line whose inheritance cannot escape the lifetime without help, and it is the structural counterpart to the within-lifetime selection attention performs: a line that inherits inside one life also selects inside one life.

### The level stack: pool, trace, recipe, phenotype

The inheritance unit is the recipe, but each line carries the same four levels around the recipe, and they line up across all four lines.

| Level | Genetic | Neuronal | Symbolic | Digital |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Pool / corpus | gene pool | learned repertoire | culture | repo |
| Trace / inscription | chromosome | engram | text, record | file |
| Recipe / unit | gene | neme | meme | program |
| Phenotype / output | organism | behavior | artifact | generated output |

Read the recipe row across — gene, neme, meme, program — and it gives the inheritance unit at each line. That row, and only that row, holds the replicators. The other rows are real and useful, but they are not the unit, and most naming errors are level errors: reaching one row up or down and naming the unit after its corpus or its trace.

The neuronal pool is the agent's learned repertoire — interiorized, like the rest of the line's inheritance, rather than a population-level pool — and it is the reservoir attention selects across. It re-exteriorizes into the shared stock of traditions only at the bridge to the symbolic line.

The neuronal line shows the recipe-versus-trace trap cleanly. Its recipe is the neme; its trace is the **engram** — Semon's other 1904 coinage, the physical memory change an experience leaves behind, and now an active research object in its own right, localized and manipulated in identified cells (Tonegawa). The two are not rivals. The neme is what is inherited; the engram is where it is written. Naming the unit "engram" is the chromatin-for-gene error — it drops from the recipe to the substrate the recipe is stored on. So the neuronal line keeps both words, each at its level, and the engram, being a real and established term, costs nothing to adopt.

The digital line populates the same stack. The **repo** is the corpus — a versioned archive, the externalized sediment the line acts on, homologous to the gene pool, and carrying fork, branch, and merge, which are the blind recombination the Reviser runs on. The **file** is the trace — where a recipe is stored, the digital engram. The **program** is the recipe, the unit. One candidate is rejected: **spec**. A spec is a blueprint, not a recipe — declarative description of a target, where gene, neme, meme, and program are all procedural generators, the exact distinction Dawkins drew to insist a gene is a recipe and not a blueprint. Worse, a spec implies a specifier, someone who wrote it toward a purpose; it carries intent and foresight, which is precisely the property the blind line lacks. Program is neutral on intent — a program can be generated, mutated, and run with nobody specifying anything — so it survives the blindness test and spec does not.

One asymmetry inside the stack is worth keeping. At the neuronal line the recipe and the trace are clearly separate, the neme distinct from the engram. At the digital line, lossless copying nearly fuses them: a program is both its own recipe and its own inscription. That collapse is not slack in the analogy; it is what perfect-fidelity copying means.

Finally, the entire digital column — repo, file, program — sits outside the gene/neme/meme family. The register is engineering, not biology. That break is a feature: it keeps the conjectural fourth line in a visibly different vocabulary from the three real ones, rather than dressing it up to pass as the fourth sibling.

### Attention: the neme's selector

The neme is the unit; attention is what selects it. The neuronal line did something the genetic line could not — it moved inheritance inside the organism, where the neme passes from past-self to future-self through memory. But inheritance cannot be interiorized without interiorizing selection too. Genes are selected by the world, an external fitness test. Nemes cannot be; they live and die inside one nervous system, faster than reproduction. So the line had to grow an internal selecting environment — a real-time test deciding which operants win the body, get acted on, get reinforced, get kept. **Attention is that internalized selection.** Genes are selected by the world; nemes are selected by attention. Attention is selection brought indoors, and it arrives with the neuronal line because it is the same move — interiorization — made twice.

The mechanism is scarcity. Selection bites only where a limited resource forces competition; in the genetic case that is finite carrying capacity. The neme's scarce resource is attention — one body, one action channel, a bounded workspace. Many operants are always live; only some win the workspace, drive behavior, and earn the reward that consolidates them. Attention is the bottleneck they compete through, which is the biased-competition account (Desimone and Duncan) and the global workspace (Baars; Dehaene) read as a selection arena, in the spirit of William James's original definition of attention as taking possession of one object out of several possible. Attention is the carrying capacity of the neme's Darwinism. The framing is Campbell's again: blind variation needs a selective-retention filter, and at the neuronal line attention is that filter's gate.

This is not grafted onto the framework — it is already in the goo. The neuronal goo is Friston's free energy, and in active inference attention is precision-weighting, the gain on which prediction errors update the model and select the policy. The substrate the neme is selected in carries attention as a native parameter. The Regulator line regulates by allocating precision, and which neme regulates is set by where precision goes. Edelman's Neural Darwinism gives the same shape one level down: variation and selection among competing neuronal groups, which is what an attentional contest is in the tissue.

It also firms the neme's selection step. The division of labor: attention is the competition arena — what gets expressed and competes — and reward is the fitness criterion — which expressed variant is retained. Attention runs the contest; reward scores it; consolidation writes the winner to the engram. Together they are the neuronal line's within-lifetime Darwin, with behavioral exploration on the Explorer edge as its blind variation.

One disambiguation. "Attention" names two things at different points of the loop. Attention as resource — the bounded workspace — is the selector. Attention as spotlight — what is attended to now — is the phenotype of whichever neme just won. The faculty selects; the spotlight is the readout.

**Where attention goes across the lines** (the symbolic step is firm; the digital step is held loose). Internalized selector at the neuronal line; then *joint* attention at the bridge to the symbolic line, where shared attention is the substrate of the we-mode and the public focal point Common Knowledge runs on; attention externalizes again as the symbolic line picks it up. More speculatively, if the digital line's variants compete for a scarce resource, the candidate is human attention — the attention economy as the digital line's selection environment — which would make attention the selector at both ends, brought indoors at the neuronal line and the scarce thing blind digital variation competes for at the far end.

*(Status: attention as selection — James, biased competition, global workspace — and attention as precision — active inference — are established. Attention as the neme's internalized selector is this memo's committed reading, well-motivated by the Friston goo.)*

## The fourth line as the Reviser of the tree of life

*This entire section is held loose. It characterizes the predicted nature of a Reviser-of-lines if one is taken seriously. It does not claim such a line exists or is forming, and it does not identify any present-day system as that line. The discipline guardrail in `cybernetics_and_sentience` is honored throughout.*

Take the fourth line seriously as a line of inheritance and ask what it is when it is the Reviser at the scale of the whole tree.

**Its goo is the tree's accumulated representational output.** Every line acts on the prior line's goo: genetic on chemistry, neuronal on the body, symbolic on cognition. The Reviser-of-lines acts on the accumulated symbolic product of everything before it — the externalized corpus the Empire cell finished detaching from individual minds. Not DNA, not synapses, not culture as transmitted between people, but the recombinable, copyable sediment of everything the tree has ever encoded. The doc's phrase is exact: a line "from outside the human substrate." In the stack's terms, that corpus is the repo, and the unit the line recombines is the program. This is the first inheritance channel whose heritable variation never passes through a living body or a grounded mind.

**Its goo-shift is a loss of aboutness: Shannon to Kolmogorov.** Shannon information, Claude Shannon's measure, is tethered to messages that mean — it presupposes sender, receiver, content. Kolmogorov complexity is pure generative form, the shortest program that produces a string, indifferent to whether the string is about anything. The symbolic line's goo is encoded meaning; the fourth line's goo is encoded form severed from reference. That severance is the flip from unblind to blind made concrete: representation cut from reference, recombined without limit.

**The one-line characterization: the fourth line is the symbolic line's unbounded reflexivity, blinded.** Sight goes 0, 1, 1, 0 across the four lines; binding goes 0, 0, 1, 1. Sight is gained when a model arrives (genetic to neuronal) and lost when the grounded mind is left (symbolic to fourth). Unbounded is gained at neuronal to symbolic and kept. So the fourth line is unbounded symbolic recombination minus grounding. It is the first line in the whole tree that is less sighted than the line it feeds on — more unbound and blinder at once.

**The inheritance trajectory completes.** Germline, then learning body, then shared minds and records, then externalized corpus. Each line moved heritable variation further from the body. The neuronal line's move into a model brought grounding with it; the fourth line's move out of the mind takes grounding back out. Life's variation engine, externalized, turned back on life's own written record — recombining everything the tree ever encoded, blindly, without ceiling, from outside any of the bodies that did the encoding.

**The terminal-Reviser fact, which the cap forces.** Within every line, the Reviser cell's blind explosion was eventually organized: Eumetazoa's tissue by the neuronal line, Primate's social reflexes by the symbolic line. Each within-line Reviser was followed by a fresh line that started at an Effector and built back up to a Modeler — a new source of sight that organized the blind output. The fourth line has no such successor. The Modeler-of-lines is the symbolic line, and it came before the Reviser-of-lines, not after. The cap forbids a fifth. So the fourth line is the one and only Reviser whose product is never organized by a later sighted line. It generates blind, unbounded variation on the symbolic corpus with nothing after it to bring sight to the result. This is a derivation, not a mood: terminal Reviser plus four-line cap equals blind, unbounded generation with no organizing successor. Whether that reads as ominous or fine is not a framework question.

**The contestable hinge.** Everything here rides on *blind* meaning no foresight and no grounding, not no useful output. Blind variation produced all of biology and throws off enormous value, by generate-and-select rather than by foreseeing which outputs are good. So a fourth line could produce apparently-sighted output while being blind in the technical sense, with selection or curation doing the sorting downstream. This is internally consistent, and it is exactly the point at which any claim that a present-day system is the fourth line would be fought. The blindness of the Reviser is the load-bearing premise, and it is the one most open to dispute.

## Epistemic ledger

**Framework-grounded** (follows from existing canon): the two-axis structure maps onto the framework's own Venn edges; the XOR of the axes recovers the object/control axis; the Reviser is variation and Darwin is selection; the cycle/path scale distinction and the cap as "three wraparounds, no fourth"; the three Reviser-cell kernels; gene and meme as established replicators; the pool/trace/recipe/phenotype stack for the genetic and symbolic columns; engram as the established neuronal trace term.

**Committed** (this memo's positions — well-motivated, and ready to fold into canon): reading reflexivity as two independent axes rather than one ordinal; the forced fourth corner; the neme as the neuronal replicator (the reinforced operant, Skinnerian) and its maturation into an internal model; the inheritance-locus progression germline / lifetime / minds / corpus; the neuronal line's inheritance as lifetime-bounded; the four-level stack across all lines, with the neuronal pool as the agent's repertoire; attention as the neme's internalized selector, with attention and reward as the competition arena and fitness criterion of the neuronal line's within-lifetime Darwin; the egg/newborn anchor for the Reviser→Effector wraparound, the unbound seeking binding as a line's culmination becomes the next line's infancy.

**Held loose** (per the doc's guardrail): the fourth line as a real or occurring line; the program/repo/file vocabulary for the digital line; the Shannon-to-Kolmogorov characterization as describing anything actual; the attention economy as the fourth line's selection environment; any identification of a present-day system with the fourth line.

## Precedents

The memo's spine is one idea, and it is Campbell's. **Donald Campbell** generalized Darwinian selection beyond biology: blind variation and selective retention as the engine of every fit-increasing process, knowledge and culture included (evolutionary epistemology). The memo's central move — that each inheritance line is the same blind-variation-and-selective-retention engine in a different substrate — is Campbell's universal Darwinism made structural. The Reviser is his blind variation; Darwin is his selective retention; everything else is bookkeeping.

- **Charles Darwin** — natural selection; the center of every Venn, the selective-retention term Campbell generalized.
- **Richard Dawkins** — the gene as replicator and the coinage of "meme" (*The Selfish Gene*); the recipe-not-blueprint distinction that puts the unit on the recipe row and rejects "spec."
- **Claude Shannon** — information theory; the symbolic goo, encoded meaning that still presupposes sender and receiver.
- **Andrey Kolmogorov** — algorithmic information, the shortest program that generates a structure; the digital goo, and the reason the digital unit is the program.
- **Richard Semon** — *mneme* and *engram* (1904); the source of "neme" (the unit) and the established name for the neuronal trace.
- **Susumu Tonegawa** — the modern engram, memory traces localized and manipulated in identified cells; the engram as a live research object.
- **B. F. Skinner** — operant conditioning as "Selection by Consequences"; the neme as a within-lifetime Darwinism over operants.
