**1. **2. Default units and overrides: accepted, with one instruction.** The memetic unit is the group — band, settlement, city, empire — and the constituent human is where a meme rests, not the unit. Sociology's vocabulary is written at person scale (socialization, role conflict, impression management, self-concept), and an author holding the stated unit will have to write the group doing something to or through a constituent. That is correct and we want it. Where a term cannot be written at group scale without invention, the brief already says: write the card that says so, CONFIDENCE contested. Please tag those at the kind gate as *unit mismatch* and read them anyway. Our prediction for that set is that they read without the Member — they are nemetic-line constructs that sociology inherited — and that is a finding about sociology's vocabulary, not about the test.

Persistence at .20 is the floor; these are the line's home vocabularies and the characteristic-item claim predicts more.
