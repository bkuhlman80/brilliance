*Four rulings. No brief change. One small source for the middle clades, optional.*

**The result first.** Line alignment on three public lists the brief was never tuned against: Member votes zero of 132 at Primate and Mammal on a behavioural-ecology vocabulary, with the Modeler present there at .39 on exactly the terms it should be (allogrooming, reciprocal altruism, tolerated scrounging, parental investment, mate choice); Member at .75 on the anthropology and sociology lists, Modeler .62. ## 1. Reciprocity: (a), and the theory should say so

The readers are right and the prediction was wrong about which operation "reciprocity" names. The source definitions describe the goods flow — no one tallies, the giver does not name a return — and a goods flow carries no code: what crosses is a good, re-emittable but not tokened. That reads as exchange with the other party in the store, Connector or Workspace, and it reads the same at Band as at Primate, which is what the Band twin of costly signaling also showed. The two reciprocity cards that took the Member are the two whose mechanism routes through told stories a newcomer can repeat.

That is the theory's own position, stated more sharply than the theory had stated it. The memetic line's replicator is the telling; the group is held together by what is said — gossip, named debts, told rules, common knowledge — and an institution is memetic *by its tellings, not by its goods*. The exchange is nemetic machinery; the account of the exchange is what makes it an institution. So the ruling is (a): the Member is a code-channel test, correctly; a card of the goods flow reads as exchange; the Member fires where the account is in the mechanism. We add no sentence to the brief, and we add one to the theory, which is where the error was. Option (b) would pull in tolerated scrounging and food sharing exactly as you say, and would make the Member a test of social embedding rather than of code, which is the thing the design removed. The same reading covers your residue (i): the behavioural-ecology glossary's human-band terms are written as behaviour, and the readers found the Member only where a gesture, a told rule or a shared judgment was in the mechanism. That is the test working.

## 2. Member without Modeler is a permitted composition: it is the Herald

E×R×Me is a slot, defined as accountable action by protocol with no revisable store — trafficking in a shared code, held to a standard, with nothing represented in between, because where the partner is stable a protocol costs less than a picture. Socialization, cultural transmission, religious ritual, degradation ceremony, assimilation and redlining read at group scale as exactly that: the group enacting a code by rule against a held standard, with the group's own store unrevised. The constituent who is socialized revises; the group that socializes does not, and the unit is the group. Twenty-six sociology terms at the Herald is a finding about institutions, and a good one.

The confusion to head off: the theory says the Member is *born* after the Modeler, which is a claim about lines — no Member on the thread before the Modeler's store runs as a replicator. It does not say every Member-bearing operation requires the Modeler. Nothing to pre-register against; if anything, pre-register that institutional-enactment terms land at the Herald.

## 3. Drop the unit-mismatch tag

It told us nothing the readers used, and my prediction for the person-scale set was wrong: enculturation, rite of passage, shamanism, stigmatization and doing gender are code operations whatever the grammatical subject of their definition, and the readers read the machinery. The group ruling is about what the *unit* is on the memetic line; it is not a filter on which terms carry a code. Drop the tag; keep the authors holding the stated unit, which costs nothing.

The persistence claim is already carried at both ends of the memetic line at n over 100, and the middle clades are not where the risk lives. If you want them scored, the Human Relations Area Files' Outline of Cultural Materials is a public category list with definitions, and its subsistence, settlement, property and community-organization sections address to Settlement by keyword; your seeding note already names it as a Band fallback. City is covered by Seshat's polity rows. We would not build anything bespoke for either.
