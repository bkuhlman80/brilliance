## 1. RNA World: the template clause stands, the stage-0 story was misassigned

The brief is right and the pre-registration was wrong by the theory's own test. A template consulted during copying is a stored specification read out; the copy can be wrong about it; that is a held value. The error was ours: we put RNA World at stage 0, and the treatment's own words put it on the seam — "a molecule that both does and stores, the doer and the copy not yet separated." Storing is holding. What RNA World lacks is not a reference but the *separation* of the reference from the thing that acts.

So the ruling restates stage 0 and moves nothing in the brief. Stage 0 — process with no store — is pre-template chemistry: autocatalytic cycles, lipid vesicles growing and dividing, nucleotide synthesis. Your 7 of 12 Effector-only cards at RNA World are that stratum showing through. RNA World is where the Regulator is first *met*: the template is consulted, and nothing yet separates it from the molecule doing the copying. Ribocyte is where it is *born*: translation reads a template into a different molecule, and store and doer are two things from then on. The treatment distinguishes a function met from a function born, and this is the first place the distinction has had data under it.

Pre-registration, restated: held-reference fires at RNA World on template operations and not on vesicle or synthesis operations; it fires on readout at Ribocyte; and nowhere below Ribocyte does any card consult a value held in a molecule other than the one acting. The last clause is the birth claim, and it is not something the four tests can see. It is a session judgment on the cards' machinery.

## 2. Uptake of foreign DNA is write-back; repair toward a copy the unit already holds is not

The test as written is right and the LUCA pre-registration was not a claim the theory makes. Three operations were read together and they split:

- **Recombinational repair from a sister copy** restores the archive to match a specification the unit already holds. That is correction toward a held value, the Regulator's own arm, and it is not write-back. The unit meets the same input afterward the same way.
- **DNA uptake, horizontal transfer, CRISPR spacer acquisition** splice content the unit *met* into its archive, so that it meets the same input afterward differently, and the change outlives the encounter. A unit meeting other DNA would hold other DNA. By the test, and by the development ruling's own criterion, that is write-back.

The foundation already says this: the genetic line's wall is late and local, and horizontal transfer and spacer inheritance run through the founding clades. What the read adds is that readers see it too. The theory's claim was never zero write-back below Bilaterian; it was that the Modeler's store does not run as a replicator there, and that the center elaborates toward the Modeler before the birth. On the thread, that elaboration is recorded in the stage's center cell — the Controller cell on the genetic line — as a precursor, because the center is the whole unit and the whole unit is what the next function splits from. The Modeler is *met* at LUCA and *born* at Bilaterian, the way the Regulator is met at RNA World and born at Ribocyte. E×R×M does not exist as a cell at a genetic clade, and your majorities for HGT at LUCA, Annotation at Eukaryote and Chronotype at Eumetazoa are not seated there; they are the center cell's composition plus the unborn function, which is the signature a precursor should have and a partial composition should not.

Brief sentence, for the write-back test: *Repair that restores a store to match a copy the unit already holds is correction toward a held value, not write-back. Content the unit met from outside and spliced into its own archive, so that it meets the same input differently afterward, is write-back.*

**The birth claim, restated for the freeze.** Below a function's birth, a read containing that function is admissible only as the stage's center composition plus that function — E×R×M at a genetic clade, E×R×M×Me at a nemetic one — and is recorded in the center cell as a precursor. A partial composition containing the unborn function — bare M, R×M or E×M below Bilaterian; any Member pair or triple below Band — is the anomaly. Under that form your read has one genetic-line anomaly, not fourteen: Non-Associative Learning at E×M (L-39), with the Eumetazoa GO habituation cards beside it. The foundation calls habituation a gain variable, not a store; three readers call it a lasting rewrite. The Eumetazoa session rules it, and the author's own gradualism says the liminal clade is where the Modeler should first show through partially. The four Member-containing cards below Band are the second anomaly, the same family as before, and we will look at the ABO item.

## 3. The song: the Tester ruling was wrong, and so was one clause of ruling 1

Six reads say a kept tutor sound is a value the bird can be wrong about however the card narrates it. They are right, and the clause that said otherwise was built for the wrong kind of code. Ruling 1 — the code's own store adds no held value — was drawn for symbolic codes, where the format is one thing and a specification the message must satisfy is another. For a performance code there is no such pair: the kept exemplar is the token the copier corrects toward and the value the copy is scored against, one object. Holding the code and holding a reference coincide, and the test should say so.

Brief sentence, for the held-reference test: *When the code is a performance and the copier corrects toward a kept exemplar, that exemplar is both the token and a value the copier can be wrong about; held-reference is yes.*

The E drop is a second thing, and it is the brief, not the song. Readers judged world-action last as what is left, and found singing exhausted by rendering. That eats ruling 2 — emission is doing — and with it the Starter, which the treatment defines as the act of putting a rendering into the open. The world-action test lists *emits* among its verbs; the residual rule needs to say that the physical putting-out is not subtracted with the conversion.

Brief sentence, for the world-action test: *Rendering is the conversion. Giving the rendering physical form and putting it into the world — speaking, singing, writing it down, transmitting it — is doing, and world-action is yes on it. It is no only when the putting-out belongs to other machinery.*

With both sentences the song fires all four, and the ruling is that it should: song learning is the **Translator off the thread**, which is what RUN-009 found before we talked you out of it. What makes a song tradition thin is not the seat but the line fact the treatment already states — the loop runs through other units and never through things, so what accumulates is bounded by one head. Over-imitation (your C729) should seat the same way, and did. The Counselor reading dies with the E drop: a bird is not a product executed by other machinery.
