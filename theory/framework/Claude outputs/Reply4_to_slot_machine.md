# Reply to slot_machine — correction to ruling 2

*Supersedes the write-back ruling in the previous memo. One sentence for the brief, one restated pre-registration.*

The previous memo read horizontal transfer, DNA uptake and spacer acquisition as write-back and called them precursors. That was wrong, and the author caught it: if the Modeler test fires on a bacterium, the test is loose. It was.

The write-back test let content met from outside count as a rewrite. But the neme is a store whose edits are *computed* — a pairing strengthened, a weight shifted, a form rebuilt from what arrived — and its copying is re-instantiation, where nothing physical crosses and the receiver rebuilds the form in its own material. Horizontal transfer is the opposite: the thing met is the content, it crosses physically, and it is spliced in as it came. The store has received, not revised. This is the write-back twin of a rule the brief already carries for retelling: transfer is not rendering, and transfer is not revision.

**Brief sentence, for the write-back test:** *The edit is computed from what the run met or produced, in the store's own material, so that the store afterward holds something that was neither in the encounter nor in the store before. Content taken in as the thing met itself — a fragment spliced in, a file copied, a snippet kept verbatim — is receipt, not revision, and does not pass. Repair that restores a store to match a copy the unit already holds is correction toward a held value, and does not pass.*

**Pre-registration, restated.** Below Bilaterian, write-back majorities are limited to computed edits — epigenetic marking at Eukaryote, habituation and synaptic depression at Eumetazoa — and appear only as the center composition plus M, never as a partial. Horizontal transfer, DNA uptake, recombinational repair and spacer acquisition read M at zero.

The held-reference ruling for RNA World and the song ruling stand as sent. The request for the seven incumbents stands.
