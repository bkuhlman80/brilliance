## 1. Habituation and kin: the test is looser than the theory, a third time

The foundation's own criterion for the Modeler is *acquired structure*: a store that can hold pairings the genome never specified, and re-emit them. It says in so many words that habituation and sensitization do not qualify — the eumetazoan net turns the gain up or down on responses the genome already wrote, adjusting volume without composing content — and that composition arrives with associative learning at the centralized brain, which is why the neme's dawn is Bilaterian. The write-back test never carried that criterion. It counted any lasting change, which is why a response that wanes with repetition passed.

So the five are neither precursor nor anomaly; they are the test under-specifying, the same shape as development and as receipt. Synaptic depression, chronotype entrainment and self-tolerance are gain, phase or suppression settings on lineage-written responses. Epigenetic marking at Eukaryote is the only one of the set that lays down a mapping the lineage did not specify, and it is the one that read E×R×M.

**Brief sentence, for the write-back test:** *A lasting change in how strongly, how fast or when a response the lineage wrote is expressed — a gain turned up or down by repetition, a rhythm shifted in phase, a response suppressed — is a setting on that response, not a rewrite. Write-back lays down or alters a pairing, sequence or mapping the lineage did not specify, so that the unit afterward has a response it did not have before, or gives an input a response it did not give it. Changing which response an input gets, or how responses are ordered, is a mapping change; changing only how strongly one lineage-written response fires is not.*

**Pre-registration.** Habituation, synaptic depression, chronotype and self-tolerance read M at zero. Associative learning at Bilaterian still reads M. Reinforcement and model-based cards at Vertebrate and above are unchanged, because a learned state-to-action mapping is a mapping. Epigenetic marking at Eukaryote may hold or drop; nothing rests on it.
