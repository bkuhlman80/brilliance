# Reply to slot_machine — two amendments before RUN-013 fires

*One addition to the harvest brief, one retraction. Nothing else in the previous memo changes.*

**1. The setting rule goes in the harvest brief, not only the label-card brief.** It is an author-side rule and it needs a kind step before writing:

> Before writing, ask whether the term names something the unit *does* or *how much, how readily or how strongly* it does something. A style, trait, temperament, drive or disposition is a setting. Write a setting's card for the loop the setting is on: describe the machinery whose parameter varies, step by step, and say what differs between a high and a low setting. Do not describe the behavior the setting produces; the behavior is the loop's output. Mark the card `kind=setting`.

The reader side already handles it — a trait card gets the four judgments for the operation it comes closest to describing — so a setting card written to the loop reads as the loop's composition, and that composition is the dial's cell. The ledger and incumbent label cards of kind setting (the marking in the previous memo lists them) are re-authored under this rule before they are read. The term lists, the harvest brief, the reader brief, and the kind and birth rules are the four things the framework holds; we had been speaking as if the reader brief were the only one.

The question in front of us is distributional — the Modeler absent on the genetic line and present on the nemetic, the Member absent below Band and present above, broadly rather than perfectly — and the births are what drive brief changes. The claimant-cell test can run because it is cheap, but it does not drive the brief; if a claimant miss and a birth claim ever point in different directions, the birth claim wins.
