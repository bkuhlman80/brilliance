- *RNA World.* Populations of replicating RNA, possibly enclosed in lipid vesicles. Template-directed copying by ribozyme polymerases; ribozyme catalysis of metabolism; vesicle growth and division by physical forces. No translation, no DNA, no proteins. The doer and the copy are one molecule, so the frame's prediction is Effector-only: every operation is process, nothing consults a value it could be wrong about. - *Ribocyte.* The ribosome and the genetic code: RNA templates read out into proteins by translation. Store and doer separate for the first time — a sequence consulted, a product made from it — so the frame's prediction is that held-reference first fires here, on readout, while proofreading is primitive and heredity is still communal. Lenses: ribosome evolution, the origin of the code, early translation.
- *Progenote.* A DNA archive emerging alongside RNA: reverse transcription, DNA replication and repair, cell division, with horizontal transfer dominating vertical heredity. Prediction: held-reference fires broadly (repair toward a stored sequence), the Controller composition begins to appear (a gradient held and spent by acting), nothing fires write-back. Lenses: Woese on the progenote, early DNA replication and repair, the lipid divide.
- *LUCA.* Three cells. Effector: self-organization, division, motility. Regulator: homeostasis, DNA repair, the genetic code consulted.
