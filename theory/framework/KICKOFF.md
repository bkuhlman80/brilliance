# Kick-off — orientation to the Trellis Framework repo

---

## What this repo is

A theory of personality and capacity called the Trellis Framework, plus the specs for a novel that instantiates it.

The core object is a Venn that grows. Each line of inheritance gives birth to one function. The new function splits off from the center of the Venn as it stands, so the Venn has three slots on the genetic line, seven on the nemetic, and fifteen on the memetic. Each earlier Venn sits inside the later one as a region, still running. The Venn is drawn at each of twenty clades: seven genetic, five nemetic, and eight memetic, which run in two media.

---

## The object

### The four functions

| Function | What it does | Born on | Its store |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Effector** | executes: spends the unit on a change in the world | the prework, as process; named on the genetic line | none of its own |
| **Regulator** | holds a stored reference and consults it — a value the unit can be wrong about, walled from what the carrier goes through | genetic | the gene |
| **Learner** | rewrites a store from the unit's own traffic, at the grain of what it met, so the unit meets the same input differently next time | nemetic | the neme |
| **Member** | holds a code in common with other units and renders between its own store and that code — outward on emission, inward on uptake | memetic | the meme |

A function is a role in a process, defined by what the unit would lack if that doing stopped. One piece of machinery can play more than one role. The four doings are doing, holding, revising and rendering. No function is named for the shape of its store or for a property of the center it split from: every regulator contains a model, so the nemetic newborn is not the model but the revising of it, and the Member is named for what holding a code makes a unit. Every function is complete at what it does and incomplete on its own:

- The Effector has no unit of attempt in its medium.
- The Regulator has no reference it did not make itself.
- The Learner has no room to enumerate the cases.
- The Member has no access to the uptake: the rendering's effect is computed inside a receiver it cannot open.

**The Member.** A code is held in common when it has three properties:

- **Re-emittable:** any user can put out its forms.
- **Tokened:** users correct toward its forms. A performance is tokened when the copier reproduces the form where it departs from what produced the result; a performance with no result for its form to depart from is not shown to be tokened.
- **Shared only as code:** it works with a receiver who shares nothing of the sender's but the code.

A form that works only because the receiver shares the sender's internals is readout or transfer, not rendering. That includes a call fixed in both lineages and a gene carried across. A display, a groom or a reconciliation is traffic between model-based animals, and it contains no Member.

The bare Member's drive is **Legibility**: the urge to make what is held receivable by another. *Membrum* is a limb, a part of a body. At memetic clades the unit is the group. The person is where a meme rests between expressions, which is the store's role, as the genome is on the genetic line.

### The ladder

| Stage | Line | Newborn | What it adds | The center at this stage | Renamed when the next function leaves it |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | prework | — | process: chemistry that keeps itself going | process | Effector |
| 1 | genetic | Regulator | memory: a stored reference the process is held to | Body | Controller |
| 2 | nemetic | Learner | revisable memory: a reference the unit rewrites from its own experience | Workspace | Conservator |
| 3 | memetic | Member | transmissible memory: a reference rendered so another unit can take it up | Translator | — |

**The rule.** Each new function splits off from the center of the Venn. The old center is renamed for what it keeps doing without the function that left it.

The center is the whole unit running, and that is where each line's test bites.

No function splits from the Translator, so it keeps one name.

### The slots

The slots are the non-empty subsets of the functions that exist: one, then three, then seven, then fifteen. A slot is new if and only if it contains the newborn function. Every slot at one stage has exactly one descendant at the next: itself plus the newborn.

The six pairs are all loops. Each one is the answer to two lacks, and no pair is privileged by where it is drawn. What a pair has instead of a position is an age: the line it was born on and the lines it has run in.

A slot's count fixes its composition and nothing else. A slot seats any operation that requires exactly its functions, and no kind of content is reserved to a count. A **setting** — a trait, style, drive or disposition — is a parameter on a loop and has no composition of its own; it is seated at the loop its description reads. A triple is defined by the function it runs without. The center at a clade is the whole unit, and what the clade first did with its newborn function is found there, not assumed.

**Genetic** (three slots)
- **Effector** (E).
- **Regulator** (R).
- **Controller** (E×R): act, compare, correct, and the outcome rewrites nothing. The model-free loop; a consolidated habit runs here whatever built it. At stage 1 this slot is the center, the Body.

**Nemetic** (adds four)
- **Learner** (Le).
- **Anticipator** (R×Le): regulation run ahead of its errors. A held reference informed by the revisable store, with the loop closing inside the unit.
- **Connector** (E×Le): action driven by the revisable store, and the store revised by the action's outcome, with no held value scoring it. The clean case is latent learning.
- **Conservator** (E×R×Le): act, reference and revision all in play, and nothing rendered — the arena with no exit. At stage 2 this slot is the center, the Workspace, where reinforcement runs in its full form, where the nemetic test bites, and where attention and the self are seated.

**Memetic** (adds eight)
- **Member** (Me).
- **Adapter** (Le×Me): the store revised by renderings that arrive from outside. Learning from others' renderings lives here.
- **Operator** (R×Me): a held reference rendered so a second party can hold the unit to it. Accountability; the promise.
- **Starter** (E×Me): the act as emission. A rendering is put into the open, receivers the unit cannot see decide its fate, nothing returns to revise it, and no reference shapes it. It begins what others carry on.
- **Tester** (E×Le×Me, runs without the Regulator): engages another party on the other's ground and revises from what comes back. It holds nothing of its own, so the references it tests are the other's.
- **Herald** (E×R×Me, runs without the Learner): accountable action by protocol. It acts, is held to a reference, and renders, with no picture of the receiver. Where the partner is stable, a protocol costs less than a picture.
- **Counselor** (R×Le×Me, runs without the Effector): a rendering prepared and screened against reference and store before any act, executed by someone else. Design, advice and drafting live here.
- **Translator** (E×R×Le×Me, the center): the unit whose loop closes through other units. It renders outward, sees uptake, revises and acts.

**Nesting.** The three-slot Venn is the region of the fifteen that contains neither the Learner nor the Member. The seven-slot Venn is the region that contains no Member. The stage-1 unit's whole Venn sits inside the stage-2 unit's as the Controller, and the stage-2 unit's whole Venn sits inside the stage-3 unit's as the Conservator. Covered layers keep running, and in this architecture that is a picture rather than a sentence.

### Seating

A mechanism is seated at the smallest slot whose functions its operation requires. Four tests decide whether a function is required:

- **World-action, for the Effector.** The mechanism does something whose description is not exhausted by holding, revising or rendering. It is judged last, as the doing left over. Correcting toward a held value — repairing a store to its copy, pumping to a setpoint — is doing, and seats with the reference at the Controller.
- **Held-reference, for the Regulator.** The mechanism holds a stored value it can be wrong about and consults it — matches against it, reads it out, scores by it; comparison is one form of consulting, not the test, and a value read out and returned, copied or rendered is consulted whether or not anything is checked against it. A gradient maintained by pumping is a held state, not a held value. A sequence that another molecule is checked against by pairing is a held value; a fold that fits its substrate by shape is the catalyst's own structure and holds nothing; a shape that enforces an arbitrary assignment the lineage settled holds a value, and a shape that fits what fits holds nothing. A revisable store or a shared code is a held value when the mechanism scores something against it — an outcome against an expectation, a performance against a kept exemplar, a partner's act against a record — and the mismatch drives the correction or the rewrite; a store merely read out to drive action, or a code merely rendered into, adds no Regulator. A reference can be a scalar or a map; a map that tracks and is restored but is never revised by the unit's own outcomes is a Regulator's.
- **Write-back, for the Learner.** A store inside the unit is rewritten by what the unit's own acts go through, and the change outlives what produced it. Content written without passing through a run of the unit is a reference-write. Selection inside the unit among parts of its own store by what the encounter met — junctions kept or cut, clones expanded or lost — is an edit at the grain of the encounter and is write-back; selection that keeps or drops the whole store on its outcome is not. The rewrite is at the grain of the encounter: what was met chooses where the edit lands. A change that only sets how much, how soon or whether a lineage-wired response is expressed — a gene opened or closed at a site the lineage tied to that signal — is a setting, however long it lasts, and rewrites nothing.
- **Retelling, for the Member.** What leaves or arrives is in a code that is re-emittable, tokened and shared only as code. Whether a given rendering is in fact retold is a fact about a line, not about the seat.

Seat the operation, not its history. Seat by requirement, not by mention. For a triple, the check is that the mechanism runs *without* the absent function.

### The twenty clades

| Line | Clades, in order |
|---|---|
| **Genetic** | RNA World · Ribocyte · Progenote · LUCA · Prokaryote · Eukaryote · Metazoa |
| **Nemetic** | Eumetazoa · Bilaterian · Vertebrate · Mammal · Primate |
| **Memetic, symbolic medium** | Band-Human · Settlement-Human · City-Human · Empire-Human |
| **Memetic, digital medium** | Computer · Network · Model · Agent |

Below the genetic line sits the prework: self-sustaining chemistry with no store, a Venn of one slot — autocatalytic cycles, vesicles that grow and divide, activated nucleotides and untemplated polymers forming from simpler molecules. The genetic line begins at RNA World, the first address where a stored reference runs as a replicator: a template consulted during copying is a value the copy can be wrong about, and nothing the strand does while it catalyzes edits its own sequence, so the reference is held and walled in the same molecule that acts. The Regulator is born there and the Venn has three slots. What the later deep clades add is found in their cells and is not a birth: at the Ribocyte the store is read out into a different molecule, at the Progenote a DNA archive is kept apart from the working copies while heredity is still communal, and at LUCA a lineage's heredity is first its own, so that the organism is the carrier the genetic test touches.

| Line | What is copied | Whose store | What the test touches | The test |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| genetic | the gene | the Regulator's, walled from its carrier | the strand, and from LUCA the organism | survival and reproduction under finite carrying capacity |
| nemetic | the neme | the Learner's, rewritten by its carrier's traffic | the performance | the outcome of expressed behavior: a route kept by being re-met at the founding clade, and scored by the unit from where scoring installs |
| memetic | the meme | the Member's: the rendering itself | the telling; in the digital medium, the run | uptake: taken up and retold, or invoked, copied and depended on |

The genetic test is uncomputed: nothing scores a gene, and no edit is computed from the outcome. Each line's test is uncomputed at the line's founding clade, where the new store runs with nothing in the unit scoring it: a route laid down by pairing persists by being re-met and decays otherwise, as a gene persists by being copied. The machinery that scores the carrier inside the unit is a later clade's install, and from there the test is computed. On the nemetic line it installs one clade above the founding one: at Bilaterian an expectation held in the unit is compared with the outcome that arrives, and the mismatch drives the rewrite. On the memetic line it is in the founding clade's center: a band scores a claim by how many say it back and how few bring a case that does not fit, and the stock of claims is rewritten by the count — read on one card at Band, and held to that until Settlement reads more. Write-back is the rewrite, scored or not; a route laid down by pairing is write-back before anything scores it.

**The digital medium is the memetic line's second medium, with the same fifteen-slot Venn.** What it adds is an executor built to the rendering's terms, so a rendering runs without a mind to interpret it. The program is the meme in a medium built to run it. Its store is separable from its host, as a gene is, and open to write-back, as an engram is. In this medium, renderings can be screened before they are ever copied.

**Each line's medium precedes the line.** The medium is laid down by the prior line's last clade: activated nucleotides and untemplated polymers in the prework, the synaptic toolkit at Metazoa, mentalizing and tool-making at Primate, formal systems whose execution is still interpretation at Empire-Human. A line begins at its **founding clade**, where a replicator first runs in the new medium. The last clade of a line, the **bridge clade**, is one where the next medium exists and no replicator yet runs in it.

**The boundaries are gradual.** No human was born of a primate, and no eumetazoan of a sponge. Every clade transition is a continuum when you zoom in. The line transitions are where that matters most, which is why Metazoa and Primate are the liminal clades.

**The thread is one; the tests are not.** The table follows a single line of descent. A mechanism off the thread that meets a function's test is seated at that function, and is convergent: the same role played by different parts. A line runs only where the function's store runs as a replicator. A birth is the function splitting from the center on the thread. On the thread no function's test is met below its birth; a read that meets one is the definition leaking, and the definition is the first suspect.

## The vocabulary

Terms every doc uses as known.

**Layer, covered.** The unit of accretion. A covered layer keeps running, less separable from the rest rather than less present.

**Install.** A clade lays down a new layer at a slot, covering what was there. How many slots install at a given clade is a matter of evidence.

**Birth.** A function splits from the center at a line's founding clade, bringing into being every slot that contains it.

**Slot.** One of the Venn's positions, tracked across clades, as against the layer sitting in it at any one clade. A slot's claim at a clade is indexed to the thread alone. It claims no exclusivity against off-thread lineages, no priority over them, and no absence below.

**Setting.** A parameter on a loop, running from a high pole to a low pole; it has no composition of its own and is seated at the loop its description reads.
- The **high pole** runs the clade's own bias.
- The **low pole** is **eclipsing**: the newest layer going dormant and the predecessor's bias showing through, unweakened, under the current clade's name. It is not a smaller amount of the same thing.

**Unit, store, carrier, replicator.**
- A **unit** is a process with functions: the thing that has a Venn.
- A **store** is a part playing the holding role of the Regulator, the Learner or the Member. Detached from its process, a store is inert.
- A **carrier** is the token the test touches: an organism, a performance, a telling, a run. The Effector mints carriers.
- A **replicator** is what is copied. It sits in a line, not on the ladder.

## How the material is cut

The theory is stated in eight documents and nothing else.

- **This kick-off** — the architecture, the vocabulary, the working agreements, and the results.
- **The growing Venn** — the ladder, the slot space, the seating tests and the lines, stated in full.
- **The selectionist foundation and the replicators** — what a selection word may mean, where bias lives, the gene, the neme and the meme each developed at the unit's own grain. Every other treatment conforms to its selection vocabulary.
- **The slot definitions** — one standard wording per slot, composition-transparent at sixty of sixty.
- **The structural map** — every clade's Venn, every cell's label, the composition it was read at, the cards and the note. Twelve clades ruled; the four digital clades unruled and marked so.
- **The re-sort ledger and the nemetic label kinds** — the working record of what the old cells held and how each label was marked and seated.
- **What a placement claims** — the readings a clade address does not license, the branch-point test, and why a negative finding is held to a higher bar than a positive one.
- **The line failures** — disease, disorder, disorganization.

The novel's specs are parked beside them and are not framework; they instantiate an architecture in a running fiction and are rebuilt from the map when the novel needs them.

The correspondence between the framework and the structures it borrows from sorts into three kinds:

- **Coordinates** were placed by composition or by countable geometry, and a count catches them if they are wrong.
- **Aspects** describe an occupant already placed rather than placing one.
- **Rhymes** are matched against structures that came from outside.

A rhyme can sit wherever a reader needs a mnemonic or the shape of an idea-complex. What keeps it useful is that it is read as a rhyme and not as a coordinate.

The rhymes in current use:

- **Damasio's four steps sit on two registers and the three centers.** Non-neural life regulation is the genetic Regulator's register. The proto-self is the nemetic Regulator's register, a neural map of the body's state now, held and not revised. The core self is the Workspace, which becomes the Conservator. The autobiographical self is the Translator. They nest the way the renaming rule nests them.
- **Jung's figures sit on Member-era slots.** The Persona is the Member, the Ego the Operator, the Shadow the Connector, and the Anima/Animus the Herald. The Self is the whole Venn.
- **The four triples are the mutable signs.**
  - Tester is Gemini, the Trickster.
  - Herald is Sagittarius.
  - Counselor is Pisces, the Senex/Sophia.
  - Conservator is Virgo.
  - Gemini and Virgo share Mercury, and those two triples share the Connector. Sagittarius and Pisces share Jupiter, and those two share the Operator.

## Working agreements

The author's, and they matter more than any content below.

- **Do not guard the page.** The goal is never to protect what is written, always to improve it. Confirming that a doc is fine is the least useful thing available.
- **Check before assuming.** The tree changes between turns, sometimes mid-turn. Read the current state; do not work from what a doc said earlier in the conversation.
- **Count, don't recall.** Verify matrix claims across all twenty clades against the structural layer map, not against remembered examples. An arithmetic claim was wrong once for exactly this reason.
- **No pointers.** Reference ideas, not locations. No filenames in prose, no version numbers, no part numbers, no index of what lives where. If a sentence needs content from elsewhere, state the content. The repo has been cleared of pointers twice; the second sweep found that two thirds of one review memo's items had already been silently resolved, which is the failure mode exactly.
- **No provenance.** Every doc states what is true now and nothing about how it got there.
  - When an item closes, delete it: no Closed section, no archive, no changelog.
  - When a correction lands, write the corrected claim positively and leave no trace of what it replaced. A correction that turned up a real result is written as the result; one that turned up nothing is written as nothing.
  - Backwards compatibility is never a goal, so nothing is owed to the superseded reading.
  - *The one exception is this list*, because a working agreement may name the mistake that earned it. That is a fact about how to work, not a doc narrating its own edits.
  - Note the asymmetry with the architecture: in the trellis nothing is deleted, because covered layers keep running. Superseded text is not a covered layer.
- **Be opinionated.** Say which option is better and why. Disagreement is more useful than agreement.
- **Mark epistemic status.** Distinguish canon from framework inference from authorial commitment, and be honest when something is asserted rather than derived.
- **Sort every selection word.** Each use of *selection* or *selector* is exactly one of three things:
  - a population statistic, which no agent, organ, or clade may hold;
  - a mechanism inside a unit, which selects only among candidates one level below itself;
  - or unredeemable, in which case it is deleted.

  A whole family of errors entered as sentences that gave the statistic to an entity.
- **Name the replicator or downgrade.** A selection claim states what gets copied. If nothing is copied, the claim is a covariance — worth reporting, never an explanation. Selectionist vocabulary fits any adaptive system for free, which is why fitting it proves nothing.
- **Costs, never payments.** State what a thing costs in joules, risk, or forgone alternatives. Never name a payer, a price, or a rate of conversion. A term survives if it names a stock, a flow, or a priority; it dies if it needs a counterparty. An entire economics was once built on a payer no one could identify.
- **Cui bono assigns lineages.** Wherever something seems to evolve, ask who benefits from the trait being there. The answer names the lineage; if nothing benefits, nothing is evolving.
- **The definition is the first suspect.** When the readers find a function on a line where that function's store cannot be the replicator, the question written was broader than the theory meant, and the clade is the last thing to move. Every tightening of the write-back test — development, receipt, settings, persistent settings — came from this rule, and none has failed.

## The one claim the sorting is held to

The framework is a sorting procedure: give it any list of phenotypes — anatomy and physiology, the cell, evolutionary psychology, sociology and anthropology — and each item seats at the composition its operation requires. The one outcome the sorting is held to is **line alignment**: an item seats at a composition whose functions are all born at or below the line of the unit that has it. A genetic-line phenotype carries no Learner and no Member; a nemetic one no Member; and each line's characteristic phenotypes do use the function born on it. The claim is about absence below a birth and presence somewhere on the line, not presence at every clade: a computer running fixed code does not learn, and a line's later medium acquires its functions where its units' operations require them. And it is about the operation described, not the phenomenon it belongs to: an institution is memetic by its tellings, not by its goods. A card of the goods that change hands in reciprocity reads as exchange, at Band as at Primate; the Member fires where the account — gossip, named debts, told rules — is in the mechanism, and that account is what makes an exchange an institution. A new mode of digestion that changes what an animal eats and how it feels, and nothing about how it learns, seats at the Effector and the Regulator; the same digestion with a gut–brain effect on what the animal does next brings the Learner in, and should. That is the level at which a seat is scrutinized. Nothing else about where items land — which slots fill, what kind of content a count holds, whether a label sits where it was argued to — is forced; it is found.

## Results established — do not re-derive, do check

- **The fourth circle is made visible by rendering into a code another unit takes up.**
  - In a three-arm blind run over provenance-stripped mechanism cards, a rendering definition alone made the fourth circle visible, more than doubling the alternative reading's engagement.
  - A confirmation run under a repaired protocol with invented-item controls passed its validity gate overwhelmingly. The engagement gap replicated above criterion in all four media tested, and the alternative reproduced its three-singles floor.
  - The run's definition was rendering across a boundary of terms. The retelling test sharpens that definition and the Member carries it. This is the best-evidenced structural claim in the repo.
- **The instrument's findings constrain every placement claim.**
  - Redescription moves roughly forty percent of items to a different slot.
  - Re-reading the identical description moves about a quarter: eighty-four cards read twice by independent blind readers kept their modal slot on 75%. No single-description placement is trusted anywhere.
  - The kind labels are fully derivable from slots and give no orthogonal test.
  - Five of the fifteen slots went essentially unexercised by the innovations corpus. The label corpus has ratified occupants at two of them, the Counselor and the Center.
- **The instrument's verdicts are about compositions.** Its cards and packets carry no slot names, so its verdicts stand under any naming of the slots.
- **Twelve clades are ruled cell by cell on the instrument's cards**, the genetic line, the nemetic line and the symbolic medium, and the map is their record. What the sessions found:
  - Each genetic clade added one kind of held value — template, code, archive, setpoint, threshold, address, body plan — and the center at each is the loop that runs on it. The readers produced that sequence; the theory did not go in with it.
  - A founding clade's novelty is its newborn: Eumetazoa installs no new kind of held value, and Band's new held value is the custom the group holds its members to.
  - The nemetic test is uncomputed at its founding clade and computed from Bilaterian, where an expectation held in the unit scores the rewrite; the memetic test is computed at its founding clade, where a band scores a claim by how many say it back.
  - Every setting seats where its loop card reads, and the loop cards for Temperament, Coping Style and Inhibition read as the Effector: a tendency is a dial on the doing.
  - The arena the center's definition names — candidates for one body, one chosen against a held value, the choice revised by outcome — reads at Mammal and not below.
  - The Member, the Adapter and the Counselor are intake cells, reachable only by cards that end before any act; one hearer with two facts varied — kept, matched — gives the Counselor, the Member and the Adapter, which is the two-roles clause and the write-back definition working as a pair of switches.
  - Writing is the Starter at City — emission into a code whose readers the writer cannot see — and the written record consulted is the Herald.
  - The nemetic Learner single is filled at Eumetazoa, Bilaterian and Vertebrate and owed above, because every card of learning at the higher clades carries the act; the symbolic medium's intake cells are filled at Band and owed above, because its sources author the polity from the emission side.

## The most useful posture

The framework is dense, internally consistent, and rewards arithmetic. The grid settles what the record says; the corpus tests what the world says. When a claim about the matrix comes up, count it.

The other productive move is reading two documents against each other. A disagreement between two treatments is always a finding for one side or the other. Once the Bramble specs are rebuilt from the ratified map, they and the framework become two statements of one architecture at different resolutions, and that pairing is the richest one the repo has.

The status file states the state at close: what is ruled, what is owed, what is unruled, and what stands as measured residue.
