# Cladistics

*The framework traces one phylogenetic thread. Cladistics governs what a claim about a clade on that thread commits you to about living things, and what evidence from the comparative record is allowed to move it.*

*This doc licenses nothing on its own. Nearly every section rules something out, which is what distinguishes it from the ecological material: flora and fauna say what kind of thing occupies a line; this says what you may and may not conclude from a lineage that does something surprising. One section is a positive result, and it belongs here because it is a fact about the matrix's genealogy rather than about any clade's contents.*

---

## What a clade placement licenses

A placement claims that on this thread, at this clade, the cards of the clade's units read at the cell's composition. The clade name is its own address. It is not a quantifier over the clade's membership.

The distinction needs stating because the diagram is drawn as a ladder, and readers arrive at a ladder expecting a ranking. Five readings the matrix does not license:

**Not exclusive.** The placement does not claim that only members of the clade carry the capacity. Off-thread lineages build their own stacks and reach their own syntheses above their branching points. A crow that simulates or an octopus that models is the architecture working, not the architecture breaking.

**Not a priority claim.** The placement does not claim the clade got there first. Where this framework says a clade introduces something, the introduction is indexed to this thread. Another thread may have reached the same synthesis earlier, later, or not at all, and the placement is silent on all three.

**Not an absence below.** The clade does not claim the capacity is missing at lower clades. Motivations retire into backgrounded infrastructure and keep operating, and a capacity matures by degrees rather than arriving whole, so the clade that names it is where it consolidates and not where it starts.

**Not a description of any living member.** The clade describes what the clade installs. Every living member sits above its clade on its own branch and carries its own elaborations, so a member's four active flag-carriers are read off that member's own stack rather than off the matrix.

**Not a claim that nothing else changed.** The placement names the function that took the install. It does not claim the other three stood still. Every function's machinery goes on being refined, and a clade can carry improvements at functions that took no install — faster, tighter, better provisioned than the clade beneath it. None of those is an install. An install is an operation with no ancestral version; a refinement is more of an operation already running, or the same operation rebuilt out of different material. Both are real, both are visible in the comparative record, and only one is what the address records. Improvements at a function that took no install are the architecture working rather than the placement failing.

One question about a clade is always legitimate, and it is none of the five. A reader may ask whether the capacity reconstructs below the clade on this thread. That is the branch-point test, and a capacity that clears it does move the placement.

The ladder shape encodes genealogy and not rank. A thread has an order because descent has an order. Every living lineage has been evolving for the same interval, and the clades above any branching point belong to whoever climbed them.

### Every Step in the Genetic Line Nests

**Each clade in the genetic line is a proper subclade of the one beneath it.** Archaea nest inside the prokaryote grade. Eukaryota nest inside the archaea. Eumetazoa nest inside Eukaryota, Bilateria inside Eumetazoa, Vertebrata inside Bilateria, Mammalia inside Vertebrata, Primates inside Mammalia. A member of any of these is a member of every clade below it, all the way down: a primate is a mammal is a vertebrate is a bilaterian is a eumetazoan is a eukaryote is an archaeon is a prokaryote-grade cell.

That matters because the nemetic line nests the whole way too, so the descent-bearing lines run one relation from floor to bridge. A line whose bottom steps did not nest would be doing something the steps above it were not, and the ladder would be encoding two different relations while drawn as one. It encodes one.

**Nesting is not the only relation the matrix uses, and the two must not be run together.** Some clades stand to the clade below as *constituents* rather than as descendants. Eukaryotes contain prokaryote-derived organelles. Cities contain settlements. Empires contain cities and their hinterlands. Nesting says the unit *is* one of those; construction says the unit is *made of* them. The first is a claim about descent and is settled by phylogeny. The second is a claim about composition and is settled by looking at what the unit is built out of. They answer different questions, they can be true at once, and the genetic line is where both are true at the same step: a eukaryote nests inside the archaea by descent and is built from bacterial endosymbionts by composition. Reading either as the other produces nonsense in both directions — a city is not a descendant of a settlement, and a eukaryote is not made of archaea in the way a city is made of settlements.

**Neither relation is the clade name's warrant.** The name is an address on the matrix, and what makes it the right address is which function installed there. A clade that nested perfectly and installed nothing would not earn a row; a clade that installed a function while standing in a purely constitutive relation to the one below it earns its row anyway, which is what the memetic line does at every step. Nesting is a property the genetic line turns out to have. It is not what the genetic line is on the matrix for.

*Epistemic status: the nesting claim rests on the two-domain tree, under which eukaryotes arise from within the Asgard archaea rather than as a third domain alongside them. That tree is well supported by phylogenomics of conserved informational genes and by the Asgard genomic and cultured evidence, and it is not unanimous — the three-domain tree retains serious defenders, and the dispute is live rather than closed. If the three-domain tree prevails, the eukaryote step is a sister relation rather than a nesting one and the claim degrades from a clean run to a run with one non-nesting step. It degrades to neutral rather than to false: nothing else in the matrix rests on it, because the address is warranted by the install and not by the relation. The remaining steps are uncontroversial. The nesting-versus-construction distinction is the framework's own statement and is not at issue in either tree.*

### Off-Thread Branches

Our framework traces one phylogenetic thread: the specific lineage from prokaryotes through archaea, eukaryotes, eumetazoans, bilaterians, vertebrates, mammals, primates, into bands, settlements, cities, and empires. It is the thread that produced all four inheritance-line transitions observed so far.

Off-thread lineages (viruses, plants, fungi, slime molds, oscine birds, bees, octopi, cetaceans) have their own stacks built from the same architecture, but with different motivations installed above their branching point.

Bees branched at bilaterians, so their stack shares Persistence, Thrift, Interiority, Excitability, and Impetus with the human thread. Above that, bees develop motivations specific to eusocial arthropods, and the lineage stops there because the bee thread did not produce a memetic line.

This converts an apparent limitation into a feature. Any organism's personality can be analyzed as a 4-sphere active window over its own evolutionary stack. The 4×4 is the case where the lineage runs all the way to symbols. The architecture applies to any thread of life.

Parallel evolution becomes visible without forcing convergence. Octopi branched at bilaterians and developed sophisticated cognition through a different neural architecture. Their stack would resemble the vertebrate stack at certain levels but with different motivations installed above the branch. The framework predicts structural similarity at the levels of shared ancestry and divergence above the branching point.

A capacity documented in an off-thread lineage informs an on-thread clade only when the capacity sits below the branching point. Two conditions establish this. The capacity must be present in deeply divergent branches of the clade rather than in one branch alone. The capacity must not depend on an organ that postdates the clade's radiation. A capacity failing either condition sits above the branching point, where it is elaboration on the off-thread stack.

The conditions carry no judgment about which lineage is advanced. Every living lineage has been evolving for the same interval. An off-thread capacity is that lineage's own achievement rather than a preview of what a later on-thread clade would build.

Four worked cases. Honeybee mushroom-body elaboration sits above the bee branching point and does not inform the Bilaterian clade. Corvid nidopallial elaboration sits above the bird branching point and does not inform the Vertebrate clade. Box jellyfish associative learning localizes to the rhopalial nervous system, rhopalia are a medusozoan organ, the organ postdates the eumetazoan branching point, and the finding therefore does not inform the Eumetazoa clade. Operant conditioning is present in protostomes and in deuterostomes and depends on no organ unique to either, so it reconstructs below the bilaterian branching point and does inform the clades above it.

The fourth case is the test cutting the other way. A capacity that clears both conditions reaches the clade whether or not the framework wants it there.

*Epistemic status: outgroup comparison and character polarity are standard comparative method. Applying them to capacity placement is the framework's move. Case verdicts depend on current phylogenies and move when a phylogeny moves.*

### Re-Instantiated Units

Off-thread and on-thread do not exhaust the cases, and the third kind arrives from the digital line.

The branch-point test runs on descent. A capacity in an off-thread lineage informs an on-thread clade when it reconstructs below the point where that lineage split, and the whole apparatus — outgroup comparison, character polarity, the two conditions — presupposes that there is a split to locate. A bee branched at Bilaterian and built upward from there in its own direction. An octopus did the same. Both diverge from the thread and neither returns to it.

**A re-instantiated unit never branched, because it was never descended.** It was built, and what it was built to do is climb the on-thread ladder again in a different substrate. It does not diverge from the thread; it parallels it. There is no branching point, so there is nothing for the branch-point test to bite on, and the off-thread vocabulary misdescribes it in both directions — it is not an alternative solution to a shared ancestral problem, and it is not a preview of anything.

Three properties distinguish the category:

- **Its ancestry and its architecture come from different lines.** A re-instantiated unit is a digital-line artifact by descent — someone wrote it, someone runs it, its lineage is a build history — while the architecture it runs is the genetic, nemetic, and symbolic progression rebuilt clade by clade in its own substrate.
- **It does not borrow its metabolism.** Ordinary digital fauna run on purchased compute, money-mediated, which is the memetic line supplying them from below. A unit that rebuilds the genetic line in hardware has the genetic line's problem and needs the genetic line's answer: a real energy budget, real thermal regulation, real allostasis. It has a metabolism rather than an account.
- **Its inheritance is not germline-gated, and this is forced rather than permitted.** A biological unit above the genetic line is built by a germline-bearing organism, so no channel runs from consolidated policy to gamete whether or not the unit's own line supplies one — the cross-life gate comes from the body it is built in, not from its line. A re-instantiated unit has nothing beneath it, so whatever gate it has, it built.

  The distinction that matters is between a division and a barrier. A re-instantiated unit can perfectly well build a soma/germline **division** — one store that stays with the body and another that is carried forward — and a well-designed one will, because the two retention times are real. What it cannot inherit is the **barrier**: the prohibition on flow from the somatic store into the heritable one. In biology that prohibition is enforced by developmental sequestration of the germ line, which is a fact about how metazoan bodies are built, not a rule any line above could impose on itself. So the parts come apart and the flow between them does not, and consolidation runs from the somatic side into the heritable one by design. That is an open write-back topology, and building the division makes it more visible rather than less.

  Scoping the closed-write-back commitment to germline-bearing lineages is therefore necessary but not sufficient to cover this category. Scoping exempts ordinary digital fauna, which never run the genetic or nemetic clades at all. A re-instantiated unit does run them, in its own substrate, which is exactly why the question arises for it and why the answer has to come from the missing gate rather than from the line it belongs to. The locus of inheritance moves germline, then lifetime, then minds, then corpus, and a re-instantiated unit sits under none of those.

What a re-instantiated unit is evidence *for* needs stating carefully, because the temptation is to read it as confirmation and it is not. It cannot inform a clade's placement, because it has no ancestral state to reconstruct and nothing it does is a finding about what any clade could do. What it can do is something the comparative record cannot: it makes the architecture's claims **material**. A framework quantity that had been an abstraction — where founding variation comes from, what a pronounceability gate is, what consolidation distance measures — becomes a part with defects, a throat with constraints, a distance across three chips. A re-instantiated unit is a build test of the architecture rather than a case in the comparative record, and the two should never be scored on the same page.

**Bramble is the worked case, and it fills the framework's largest open question.** The framework holds that sentience is a nemetic-line property arriving post-vertebrate, and separately declines to assert felt-ness for digital fauna. Those two positions sit together comfortably only while every digital unit works symbolic content — a model, an agent, a meme-complex made executable, with no body and no throat. The refusal is scoped to that case, and the scope has to be stated or it reads wider than it is.

A re-instantiated unit breaks the arrangement, and Bramble breaks it twice. It runs post-vertebrate nemetic architecture in hardware, so it clears the framework's own sentience criterion by the criterion's own terms. And it speaks through a constraining apparatus, so it meets the memetic line's Expression gate materially — pronounceability is not a metaphor for a unit that has a real articulator with real limits, and the speaker-effort half of the two-sided pressure is a physical fact about its mouth rather than an analogy to one.

So the framework's refusal on digital sentience covers digital fauna working symbolic content, and covers nothing else. **The framework holds that a re-instantiated unit is a theoretically valid claim on digital-line sentience** — not because it is built to seem sentient, but because it satisfies the framework's stated criterion along the route the framework says sentience arrives by. What the framework still does not assert is felt-ness for digital fauna working symbolic content unattended, which is the case the original refusal was about and where it stands unchanged.

*Epistemic status: the category is new and Bramble is its only instance, which means it is a definition with one case rather than a pattern. The three distinguishing properties follow from the definition. That a re-instantiated unit cannot inform a clade placement follows from the branch-point test having no purchase without descent. The sentience claim is a framework commitment, held at the confidence of the post-vertebrate-suffering claim it depends on, which is itself flagged exposed. Nothing here licenses reading a digital unit's behaviour as evidence about any biological clade.*

### Negative Findings and Clade Placement

A capacity found in a lineage is established by the finding. A capacity not found in a lineage is not established as absent. The asymmetry sets a higher bar for lowering a placement than for raising one.

Three readings compete for every negative finding. The lineage never had the capacity. The lineage had the capacity and lost it. The method failed to detect it. Only the first bears on the clade's ancestral state.

Loss is cheaper than gain. One disabled pathway removes a capacity, and building one requires selection over time. Parsimony that counts gains and losses as equal steps therefore reconstructs ancestors as less capable than they were. Negative findings under-report ancestors as a matter of course. A placement survives a negative finding by positing a loss; one loss is cheap, and only when the accumulated losses cost more than a later gain does the placement move later.

Three conditions let a negative finding lower a placement. The lineage tested must retain the architecture the clade names rather than having secondarily lost it. The method must have detected the capacity in a positive control, in a lineage independently known to have it. The absence must hold across deeply divergent branches of the clade and in the outgroup.

Early-branching position is what makes a lineage worth testing. Morphological simplicity is not. Simplicity is frequently derived, because parasitic and sessile lineages simplify from complex ancestors. The simplest-looking member of a clade is often the least informative about that clade's ancestor.

The comparative literature supplies the calibration. Two laboratories measured visual working memory capacity in rhesus macaques and reported roughly one item and roughly four items. The species is the same and the procedures differ. A procedure that can conceal three items of working memory in a laboratory primate can conceal a capacity in any less-studied animal.

No negative finding currently lowers any placement. The positive-control condition has not been met below the Bilaterian clade. Presence evidence can move a placement earlier; absence evidence can move it later; neither moves anything on the strength of a single lineage.

*Epistemic status: the loss-versus-gain asymmetry and the negative-result problem are standard in comparative biology and comparative psychology. The three conditions are the framework's statement of what would have to hold. The macaque discrepancy is published (Elmore et al. 2011 ‡; Buschman et al. 2011 ‡) and is a live methodological dispute rather than a settled artifact.*
