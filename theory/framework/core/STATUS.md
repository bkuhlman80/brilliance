## Standing rulings

Each was written into the definitions and sent to the instrument, and each stands.

- *One store can play two roles.* A revisable store or a shared code is a held value when the mechanism scores something against it and the mismatch drives the correction or the rewrite; a store merely read out to drive action is the Learner's alone. Prediction-error learning is the Anticipator.
- *Correcting toward a held value is doing.* Repair, pumping to a setpoint, a body moved to where the setpoint can be met: act, compare, correct, nothing rewritten, the Controller by definition. The Regulator alone is holding and consulting with nothing done about it.
- *A shape that enforces an arbitrary assignment the lineage settled holds a value; a shape that fits what fits holds nothing.*
- *Sequence complementarity is a held reference; shape complementarity is not.*
- *A filter phrased as a standard is not a held value.* An error threshold is a population statistic nothing holds or consults.
- *Write-back needs no scorer.* A route laid down by pairing is write-back before anything scores it.
- *RNA World holds a reference, and the Regulator is born there.*
- *Transfer is not write-back.* A store that takes in the thing met itself has received, not revised.
- *Development is not write-back.*
- *A setting is not write-back, and a setting that persists is still a setting.* The difference between a setting and a rewrite is who chose where the edit lands.
- *Selection inside the unit is write-back.* Junctions kept or cut, clones expanded or lost, by what the encounter met; selection that keeps or drops the whole store on its outcome is not.
- *A threshold compared against is a held value whether or not pairing moves it.*
- *Song learning is the Translator off the thread, convergent.*
- *Emission is doing.* The Member, the Operator, the Adapter and the Counselor are intake cells, and only a card that ends before any act reads at them.
- *An institution is memetic by its tellings, not by its goods.* The Member fires where the account is in the mechanism, and a told account the author wove in is the Member in the read.
- *The Herald is permitted without the Learner.*
- *A performance is tokened only where form and outcome come apart in the mechanism.*
- *A founding clade's novelty is its newborn.* Eumetazoa installs no new kind of held value.
- *The nemetic test is uncomputed at its founding clade and computed from Bilaterian.* The anemone's route persists by being re-met; at Bilaterian an expectation held in the unit scores the rewrite.
- *The memetic test is computed at its founding clade.* A claim firmed by how many say it back and how few contradict it, the stock of claims rewritten by the count — one card at Band, and the city's precedent files and the empire's binding rulings read the same loop above it.
- *Settings seat where their loop cards read, not by count.* Every nemetic setting was authored as the loop its dial sits on and scored for that loop's composition; each sits where it read.
- *The center is the arena where candidates for one body compete, and that is read at Mammal* — a choice among available acts scored against a held threshold and revised by outcome, E×R×Le. At Bilaterian the competition reads as the Effector; attention in its intake form is Anticipator content from Bilaterian up.
