# The Selectionist Foundation and the Three Replicators

What selection is in this framework, what each line runs, what a selection word is allowed to mean, and the three units of inheritance — the gene, the neme, the meme — each developed at its own grain, with the meme developed twice, once per medium, because the program is the meme in a medium built to run it. Every other treatment conforms to the selection vocabulary stated here.

Each line names a unit of inheritance — the smallest thing the line's test sorts. The gene and the meme arrive thoroughly theorized, and for those the framework's work is placement rather than theory: one frame, applied at one grain. The neme is the framework's own coinage and carries its full development here.

The frame is re-instantiation. All copying is a store persisting while machinery reads it to stamp out tokens — there is no other kind — and the three units, and the meme's two media, differ not in whether they copy but in the geometry around the copying: where the store sits, whether it can exit its carrier, whether the carrier's adventures write back into it, how cleanly its trials individuate, and how blind the generation of variants runs. Those differences, graded rather than typed, are what the units' different horizons, replicator grades, and explanatory territories all trace back to.

---

## What a selection word may mean

A Darwinian process is any process meeting three conditions: variation among the members of a population; differences among them that affect how many descendants each leaves; and descendants that resemble their sources. Nothing in the conditions mentions biology. Any substrate meeting them evolves, and the three lines, with the memetic line's two media, are four substrates that meet them — differently well, which is what the trajectory reading measures.

The vocabulary discipline follows from what the conditions are. **Selection is a statistic**: the covariance between what a member carries and how many descendants it leaves. A statistic is held by nothing — no agent, no organ, no clade — and it acts on nothing; things are born and die, and the covariance is what that amounts to over a population. **A selector, where the word appears, is a mechanism inside a unit**: an arena that sorts candidates one level below itself — actions, not animals; utterances, not speakers; procedures, not machines. A mechanism never sorts units of its own kind. There is no third legitimate use.

Selectionist description is cheap. The accounting identity beneath it fits any adaptive system by construction, so fitting it proves nothing; reinforcement learners and Bayesian updaters fit it too. What converts description into explanation is the replicator: the named thing that gets copied and the mechanism that copies it. A selection claim in this framework names its replicator or calls itself a covariance — worth reporting, never an explanation.

## Vicarious selectors, and where bias lives

An inner selector is any mechanism that discards candidates against a stand-in criterion instead of letting the world discard them. Vision is a vicarious trial of locomotion; thought is a vicarious trial of action; the workspace contest is a vicarious trial of behavior. The world's own removal — the fall, the predator, the silence after a telling — is the criterion all the stand-ins proxy.

A vicarious selector is necessarily biased, because only a proxy can deviate from the criterion it proxies. The world's test has no weighting apart from the payoff, so it cannot be biased about anything. Biases therefore live in mechanisms that estimate, and the framework's four heuristics — recency, familiarity, potency, novelty — are the biases of the first implemented estimator, which arrives with the nemetic line. What look like biases on the genetic line live on the generation side, as mutational and developmental bias, and belong to the generator; the generator's biases are uncorrelated with the test, which is what blind means.

The criterion of an inner selector is not derived by the unit that carries it. It is a fossil of outer verdicts: units whose proxies misfired were removed, and what remains is a proxy whose settings record what removed the lineage. Calibration runs twice, at two scales. Ancestor removal sets the coarse structure — the innate alarms, and what counts as primary reward, which is the scoring rule everything else runs on. Within-life learning then tunes the fine structure on that inherited rule. The organism-side statement of the whole arrangement is Popper's: the inner selector lets hypotheses die in the unit's stead, converting fatal errors into recoverable ones.

## Parallax, and the crane

One object, two true readings. From inside the unit, an inner selector is an arena: candidates compete, most are discarded, one is expressed. From the level below — from the test the unit's own lineage answers to — that entire arena collapses into generation: one deliberated candidate offered to the world, its production biased by fossilized verdicts. One level's selection is the level below's biased generation. That reading-dependence is parallax, and it makes directed search nothing but generation run through a vicarious selector — not a fourth ingredient beside variation, test, and heredity.

A crane is an inner selector whose verdicts are written back, so that its bias compounds. Without write-back an arena only prunes: variation and differential success without heredity can sort what exists and build nothing, because every contest starts from scratch and a thousand trials explore a thousand points near the origin. With write-back, the store that generates tomorrow's candidates is edited by today's outcome; search becomes a path rather than a scatter of independent samples, and reachable design scales with time instead of being bounded by single-trial luck. Heredity is the pawl of the ratchet, and a crane is a ratcheted gain whose function is to raise the rate of ratcheting — built by the ground process, then accelerating it.

A line boundary is a mature crane opening a new medium. Attention and conversation are arenas built by the line below each of them, and execution is the arena the memetic line's symbolic medium built for its digital one; each accelerates search in a medium the ground process could not search: within-lifetime search over actions, cross-mind search over tellings, mechanical search over procedures. What crosses a boundary is not a seed looking for soil; it is a built search-accelerator arriving in a medium that never had one.

## The terminator

For a trial to register, an attempt must end. The terminator is what closes one carrier token so it counts: the death of the organism, the action completing in an outcome, the telling ending as the floor passes, the invocation halting. It individuates attempts; it never sorts them. It is distinct from the death of the unit — the unit's lineage may run indefinitely, while its carriers must end, every time, or there are no trials at all.

Countability is graded, and the grade does work. How cleanly a medium's attempts individuate predicts how much analytic traction the unit's-eye view has there: turns and invocations count cleanly, lives count well, performances blur. Where attempts blur, selection vocabulary loosens with them, and the framework says so rather than forcing precision the medium does not offer.

On the nemetic line the trial's close and the inheritance event are the same moment: the outcome lands, and the store that produced the performance is rewritten in the landing. A line without a wall between carrier and store learns in an afternoon what a walled line learns in a thousand generations — that identity of trial-close and write-back is the structural reason for the speed.

## The test, sorted by opponent

Disturbances divide by whether they can adapt to the answer they meet. Erosion cannot; a phage can. A test made of non-adapting disturbance is met by upkeep — hold the reference, repair toward it — and a test made of adapting disturbance is met by defense, because the opponent updates against whatever is holding. The cut is recognized independently in four literatures: Red Queen against Court Jester in paleobiology, adversarial against stochastic control in engineering, requisite variety against a variety source that grows, and worst-case against average-case robustness in machine learning. It is the same cut the genetic founding clade makes — an interested ending is the moment part of the test starts adapting back — and it is the boundary of the stances: the physical stance suffices for weather, and opponents demand the intentional stance, because an adapting test carries free-floating rationales of its own.

One strain, held openly: interiors are also overwritten by mere salience, and salience has no opponent behind it. Either the sharp criterion is the genetic line's case and the higher lines run a looser version, or some of what looks like defense above the genetic line is something else. Unresolved.

## The somatic engine

One mechanism inside the body outranks the neme on the replicator row, and it is worth stating exactly because it is not a line. Adaptive immunity is a full Darwin engine run somatically, on the genetic medium: V(D)J recombination and somatic hypermutation generate blind variation in receptor genes, antigen binding is the test, and clones that bind expand — template copying, true replication — with memory cells retaining the winners. Affinity maturation in germinal centers is cumulative selection, iterated rounds of mutation and expansion improving binding a hundredfold within weeks. Ordinary somatic turnover has none of this: a skin cell's daughters carry the same specification, and no test differentially amplifies functional variants. The engine also runs feral: a tumor is the same somatic evolution without the organismal license — one engine, domesticated and undomesticated.

It founds no line, and the reasons are the line criteria rather than the geometry: it opens no new medium of accumulation, its record dies with the body, and nothing later builds on antibody-space as groundwork. It is the genetic line's somatic crane — evolution harnessed as an organ. The historical footnote is instructive: neural Darwinism was this engine exported to the brain by an immunologist, and the export lost the replicator in transit; the framework's neme is the honest accounting of what actually survives the trip.

The immune case also carries the framework's cleanest molecular statement of accretion — the parts of defense are ancient and the deployments are new: animal cGAS-STING descends from bacterial anti-phage systems, and TIR-domain enzymes and viperin are prokaryotic in origin. Covered layers running, named in proteins. And it is where *keep* first becomes a verdict: per-encounter discrimination lets an encounter end in admission rather than exclusion, endosymbiosis is the keep-verdict that built a clade, and tolerance — regulatory T cells, secretory IgA, the maternal-fetal machinery — is defense's expensive mode, not its absence.

*Epistemic status: clonal selection, affinity maturation, the bacterial origins of the named immune components, tolerance-as-machinery, and tumor evolution as somatic Darwinism are established biology. The opponent sort's four external anchors are established in their fields. The crane reading, the not-a-line ruling, the keep-verdict framing of endosymbiosis, and the stance-boundary reading of the opponent sort are framework commitments.*

## The lines, stated as populations

A line is a Darwinian population plus its medium, and there are as many lines as there are named replicators — three today because three replicators are named, with nothing in the geometry capping the count. The memetic line runs in two media, and a medium is not a line: the digital medium adds an executor to the meme's test and no replicator of its own. The three conditions, stated per line with the test described locally — no line's test is a thing, and nothing owns it:

- **Genetic.** What varies: nucleic-acid sequence, by mutation, recombination, and transfer, blind to the test. The test: survival and reproduction under finite carrying capacity. What is copied: the gene, template-read at high fidelity.
- **Nemetic.** What varies: performances around the stored form — topography, timing, vigor — with the variance itself a reinforceable dimension, so the line's variation rate is a trait under its own test. The test: the outcome of expressed behavior, scored by reward prediction error. What is copied: the behavioral form, performance to performance, through the serially rewritten engram.
- **Memetic, symbolic medium.** What varies: retellings — error, recombination, deliberate innovation, partly directed. The test: uptake — taken up and retold, or let die in the room. What is copied: the meme, digitized by the word; a mis-heard form is corrected to the nearest word, which restores fidelity the medium would otherwise lose.
- **Memetic, digital medium.** What varies: edits and generated candidates, mostly directed — the writer consults a model of the test. The test: being run and kept — invoked, copied, depended on. What is copied: the program, the meme rendered in a code a machine executes, bit-perfect.

And the replicator-status row is the framework grading its own units: the neme sits in a strongly Darwinian process with the weakest replicator, the meme in its digital medium is the strongest replicator in the least Darwinian process, and both are locations rather than failures.

## Lines as trajectories

The lines, and the two media of the last, are not instances of one process. Each occupies its own region of the space of Darwinian processes, graded on ordinal axes, and each drifts across its own four clades toward the comprehending corner.

| | **Genetic** | **Nemetic** | **Memetic, symbolic medium** | **Memetic, digital medium** |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Heredity fidelity | high | low | moderate | maximal |
| Generation | blind | blind, variance tunable | partly directed | mostly directed |
| Tester location | external | substantially internalized | public and internal | external arena, comprehending designers |
| Comprehension in the loop | none | none to proto | partial | high |
| Trial countability | high | low | high | maximal |

The drift matters more than any single coordinate. Selectionist description has the most traction at the blind, walled, countable corner and loses traction as comprehension enters the loop — the meme's-eye view is a good lens on gossip and a poor one on peer review, and the program's-eye view is today mostly the designer's-eye view in costume, gaining independent traction only as generation de-comprehends. Mechanisms are graded, never lines: every line contains mechanisms at different positions, and a clade is a stage in a population's machinery, not a rung on a ladder.

Two facts anchor the gradient reading from opposite ends. The genetic line's famous wall is late and local — sequestered germlines are a metazoan invention, horizontal transfer and CRISPR spacer inheritance run through the founding clades, and plants never build the wall at all — so even the paradigm line is graded inside itself. And the digital medium's comprehension breaks midway: the trained model is the medium's first organ that is not written but fit, which is the clade where it stops being fully auditable by the humans in the loop. A gradient that runs through the interior of both end-member lines is a gradient, not a typology.

## The Gene

The gene is a heritable set of instructions in nucleic acid: the paradigm replicator, and the standard the other two are graded against. Its tokens are the copies carried in organisms; its store is the sequence itself; its population is the gene pool; its fitness is copy number across generations. The theory arrived finished — Williams's gene selectionism, Dawkins's replicator, Hull's replicator-interactor split — and the framework adds placement, not theory.

Read in the re-instantiation frame, the gene copies the way everything copies: a template persists and machinery reads it to stamp out new tokens, polymerase and ribosome doing for the sequence what the motor system does for the engram. What makes the gene the paradigm is not a different kind of copying but three properties held at their extremes. Fidelity is near-perfect, because proofreading and repair hold the error rate to something a deep-time horizon can be built on. The store exits its carrier: the germ line is smuggled out of every organism before the organism dies, which is the mechanical fact behind the gene's horizon — the engram is trapped in its body, and the gene never is. And the write-back topology is nearly closed: in lineages that sequester a germline, what the body goes through does not reach the store — though the closure is late and local, with horizontal transfer and CRISPR spacer inheritance running through the founding clades and plants never sequestering at all, so even the paradigm is graded rather than typed.

Two units, not one. The gene is the heritable unit, the smallest thing the line's test sorts. The subjective unit — what carries the Venn at each clade, what has motivations, tendencies, and a breakthrough — is the cell or organism. The gene is what is copied; the organism is what does the copying and carries its cost. The two are different levels and the framework needs both, which is Hull's replicator and interactor stated as the matrix's own bookkeeping.

The triple. What varies: sequence, by mutation, recombination, and transfer, blind to the test. The test: survival and reproduction under finite carrying capacity, held by nothing. What is copied: the gene, template-read at high fidelity, with the organism's death as the terminator that closes each trial and lineage extinction as the death of the unit.

What the gene's-eye view buys is everything the organism's-eye view cannot see: kin altruism, where a gene rescues copies of itself housed in relatives; junk DNA riding the channel for free; meiotic drive and imprinting conflict, where the interests of genes sharing one genome come apart. Those explananda set the standard the other two units are held to — a unit's-eye view earns its keep only where it explains what the level above it cannot.

*Epistemic status: everything here but the placement is established evolutionary biology — Williams, Dawkins, Hull, Hamilton on kin selection, the genomic-conflict literature. The re-instantiation reading of template copying, and the graded write-back topology, are the framework's frame applied to received theory.*

## The Neme

**The neme is a behavioral form that reproduces by re-instantiation**: a stored form, read out as performances, whose consequences rewrite the store that emits them. Its tokens are performances; its store is the engram, the synaptic policy that persists between performances; its population is the emission stream of a lifetime; its fitness is emission rate. A behavior emitted once a day before reinforcement and a hundred times a day after has multiplied, in the only sense multiplication ever has: more tokens of the form, descended from fewer.

This is not a weaker copying than the gene's. A gene is not copied by magic — a template persists and machinery reads it to stamp out new tokens; an engram persists and the motor system reads it to stamp out new tokens. Copying-as-re-instantiation is what all copying is. Where the lines differ is the write-back: the genetic store is walled off from what its carrier goes through, and the nemetic store is built so that what the carrier goes through is exactly what edits it. The outcome lands, and the store that produced the performance is rewritten in the landing — on this line the close of a trial and the inheritance event are one moment, which is the structural reason the line is fast.

The triple, stated plainly. What varies: performances around the stored form — topography, timing, vigor — and the variance is itself a reinforceable dimension, so the line's variation rate is a trait under its own test (Neuringer). What the test is: the outcome of expressed behavior, scored by reward prediction error. What is copied: the form, performance to performance, through the serially rewritten engram.

Replicator status, held without flinching: the gene is the paradigm replicator, and the neme is a marginal Darwinian population — amplified through a store rather than copied template to template. A marginal case is a location, not a failure, and the demotion costs nothing the framework uses, because the ratchet survives it: variation regenerates around the reinforced mean, so the search is cumulative — a hill-climber, not a one-pass filter. Anchors: Skinner's selection by consequences; Dennett on why the Law of Effect will not go away; McDowell's evolutionary theory of behavior dynamics, in which a population of behaviors reproducing under selection fits the matching law at individual-subject precision; the dopamine reward-prediction error as the write-back's scoring signal.

Because the nemetic line interiorized inheritance into a single life, it had to interiorize selection too. Attention is the arena: the contest in which candidate behaviors compete for expression, scored by reward and retained by consolidation into an engram. Attention and reward are the competition-arena and the fitness-criterion of a within-lifetime Darwinism, the way the environment and differential survival are the genetic line's. This is the framework's evolution-as-learning equivalence, run inside one agent. The arena sorts candidate actions — one level below the animal — and it runs at every nemetic clade, founding at the Bilaterian and holding from there to the Primate. The arena is bounded and the weighting across it is normalized, so weight given to one candidate is weight not given to another. Candidate behaviors carry weight in proportion to their goal-relevance; the candidate with the most weight wins expression, and the margin by which it wins is set by how concentrated or spread the weighting is across the full candidate set. What loses the contest is not consumed. What is discarded before a candidate wins expression is possibility foregone rather than a consequence suffered. The losing operants stay live and remain eligible at a later turn. Losing in the world is irreversible; losing in the workspace is deferral. This is a structural claim, not a metaphor: the same competition-and-normalization dynamic recurs at every nemetic clade from Bilaterian through Primate, sharpening and generalizing but never changing kind. *Epistemic status: the competition/normalization framing is a framework theoretical commitment, consistent with contemporary selection-based accounts of attention (Logan 2026) but not itself requiring their endorsement — Bramble instantiates this claim as fictional architecture, not as a settled empirical verdict. Non-depletion in the workspace follows from where consequences land: only expressed behavior meets the world.*

It matures across the line, operant to internal model. The model-free reading (Vertebrate, Reinforcing) gives the operant, selection on overt behavior. The model-based reading (Mammal, Simulating) gives a second form of the same logic, the tested hypothesis, Popper's "let your hypotheses die in your stead," selection on simulated behavior before a body is committed. The neme matures from operant to internal model, tracking the within-line move Vertebrate to Mammal, the way the meme matures across its own clades from Conversation to Stories.

The neme is what is inherited. The engram is where it is written: Semon's other 1904 coinage, the physical memory change an experience leaves behind, and now an active research object in its own right, localized and manipulated in identified cells (Tonegawa). The two are not rivals, and the line keeps both words. Naming the unit after the engram would name it after the place a thing is stored rather than after the thing, which is the error that calls a gene chromatin.

A boundary to keep. Temperament is not the neme. Temperament and behavioral syndrome are substantially genetically heritable, the genetic line's reach into the nemetic machinery, the parameter settings that bias which operants get learned. The neme is the *learned* policy, the line's own acquired replicator. Keeping these separate matters.

Transmission: operant, then tradition. Genes cross between bodies; memes cross between minds. The neme uses two channels, and they are ordered rather than rival.
- *Within a lifetime,* via memory. The neme passes from past-self to future-self by consolidation. This is the cleanest distinction from both neighbors: the nemetic line is the first line where an agent inherits from its own past, interiorizing inheritance before the memetic line re-exteriorizes it. On its own it does not accumulate across generations, which is exactly what the second channel adds.
- *Across generations,* via animal tradition. Socially-learned behavior (birdsong dialects, tool-use cultures, foraging traditions; Whiten, Laland) accumulates across generations without symbols, giving the nemetic line a between-agents channel parallel to genes and memes. It stays distinct from the meme by a sharp criterion: transmitted by imitation of behavior, with no shared symbol system or we-mode.

Both hold, in order. The operant is the within-lifetime neme; the animal tradition is the socially-learned operant, the bridge residue the memetic line inherits and encodes. The progression operant to tradition to meme lands exactly on the Primate-to-Band handoff, social reflexes becoming culture. It is not either/or; it is the maturation of the neme into the meme.

The nemetic line's inheritance is lifetime-bounded. It accumulates across deep time only by borrowing a neighbor's channel: genetic assimilation (the Baldwin effect) downward, or animal tradition and culture upward. Genes and memes accumulate on their own across generations; the operant, alone, dies with the organism. This is the line whose inheritance cannot escape the lifetime without help, and it is the structural counterpart to the within-lifetime selection attention performs: a line that inherits inside one life also selects inside one life.

The bound comes from the body, not from the neme. A nemetic-line unit is built by a germline-bearing organism, and no channel runs from consolidated policy to gamete, so the operant has nowhere to go even when it is fully consolidated. Consolidation retains within a life; the germline retains across lives; the two gates do different work. A nemetic architecture instantiated with no germline beneath it has only the first gate, and its consolidated policy passes directly to the next unit.

### Instantiation, and the dawn

The neme is instantiated in brains. Its store is written in nervous tissue, and the requirement beneath the word is specific: a store that can hold *acquired structure* — pairings the genome never specified — and re-emit it. Habituation and sensitization do not qualify: the eumetazoan net turns the gain up or down on responses the genome already wrote, adjusting volume without composing content. Composition arrives with associative learning at the centralized brain, which is why the neme's dawn is Bilaterian, with the arena, and why the bare associative form is already a neme — acquired, stored, re-instantiated as performances, serially rewritten by prediction error. The operant at Vertebrate is its maturation, not its birth.

One empirical hostage, flagged rather than hidden: associative conditioning has been reported in box jellyfish, run on rhopalia and a ring nerve with no centralized brain. Cubozoans are off the counted thread, so under the placement discipline the finding does not indict the Bilaterian address — the capacity may be independently built there — but if it generalizes, "brains" softens to "nervous systems that store acquired structure," and the dating on the counted thread stands. A built unit can mimic the neme's shape on another substrate — a stored form re-instantiated and serially rewritten — without membership: nemes are instantiated in brains, and the mimic is a program running close.

### The boundary: what is not a neme

Reflexes and instincts are not nemes, and the ruling is Skinner's before it is the framework's. His founding cut split respondent behavior, elicited by antecedent stimuli, from operant behavior, emitted and shaped by consequences; his phylogeny-and-ontogeny distinction assigned the two their provenances — behavior shaped by contingencies of survival against behavior shaped by contingencies of reinforcement — and his selection-by-consequences paper made them two levels of one causal mode. That is gene against neme in 1966 vocabulary: same selectional logic, different level, different store. Reflex and instinct differ from each other only in complexity — the knee-jerk's short stimulus-bound arc against the fixed action pattern's released, sequenced, motivation-gated program — and both fall on the genetic side, because the boundary runs between stores written by ancestry and stores written by the life, never between simple and complex.

Two questions assign any behavior to its unit: which store is its form written in, and which channel does its heritable variation travel? A yawn's form is developmentally wired — fetal, cross-vertebrate, brainstem-generated, present in anencephaly — so every answer says gene. Contagious yawning changes nothing: contagion is a trigger channel, not a copying channel — activation propagates, structure does not, no store anywhere is written — the imitation channel at fidelity zero, groundwork for the channel that later carries nemes as tradition. Bacterial chemotaxis gives the same verdict from the other side: reflex-shaped control run in molecular hardware, with genuine short-term adaptation — the methylation comparison of now against seconds ago — that resets and inherits nothing. Adaptation without inheritance is a gain variable, not a store, which is why the neme's criterion is write-back to a persistent store and not mere within-life state.

Provenance, never topography, individuates the unit. Skinner's own warning: topographically similar behavior can be phylogenic in one case and ontogenic in another, and only the history tells you which — the blink and the wink are physically near-identical, and one is a reflex while the other is a move in a game. The layered cases are the instructive ones. An infant's cry is a genetic-line signal in its form; instrumental crying — deployment shaped by what crying reliably produces — is a neme laid over the reflex, running the same muscles. Continence is a neme trained over the defecation reflex, and the norms around it are memes over the neme: three lines stacked in one behavior. The Brelands' instinctive drift is the layers fighting in a training lab — raccoons compulsively washing the tokens they were being operantly trained to deposit, the phylogenic store bleeding through the ontogenic one. Eclipsing, observed in 1961.

Two words to hold apart, and one implication to refuse. *Instrumental* has a teleonomic sense — behavior that accomplishes something — which applies to nearly everything alive, kinesin included, and a learning-theory sense — behavior acquired and maintained by its within-life consequences — which is rare and datable; the neme's criterion is stated as write-back rather than usefulness because usefulness is everywhere and free-floating. And the neme implies no sentience: instrumental learning runs in the transected spinal cord — an isolated cord acquiring and retaining an outcome-shaped flexion policy with no brain participation — so the neme requires store, arena, write-back, and an estimator, and machinery entails nothing about experience.

*Epistemic status: the respondent-operant cut, the phylogeny-ontogeny distinction, instinctive drift, fetal and anencephalic yawning, chemotaxis adaptation, instrumental infant crying, and spinal instrumental learning (Grau) are established. The two-question assignment rule and the fidelity-zero reading of contagion are framework commitments; the blink-wink individuation point is Ryle's.*

### Neme-complexes

Memes assemble into meme-complexes — coadapted memes traveling together because each raises the others' fitness — and the neme has the same level above it: coadapted learned policies that co-occur, recruit each other, and protect one another from extinction. The psychoanalytic tradition described the object before the vocabulary existed. Jung's feeling-toned complex — a cluster of associations around an emotional core that activates as a unit, resists dissolution, and behaves like a partial personality — is a neme-complex named sixty years early. The modern anchors are stronger: Beck's schemas and their maladaptive developments in schema therapy, organized self-maintaining clusters of learned appraisal and response; Bowlby's internal working model; behavioral chaining for the mechanics of policies linking into units. Cui bono runs clean at this level: the complex's persistence serves the complex, its members shielding each other from the disconfirming contexts that would extinguish them singly — the memeplex's self-protection run on the line below. Transference is the complex's reproduction event: the whole cluster re-instantiated onto a novel object.

Two connections discipline the concept. Downward: the behavioral syndrome is the heritable correlated bundle — temperament's bundle, the genetic line's reach into the machinery — and the neme-complex is the learned bundle: same shape, different line, the temperament boundary extended one level up. Upward: nemetic-line failure is constellational — configured rather than broken, which is why experience-driven relearning treats it and molecular medicine does not — and a constellation is a maladaptive neme-complex, which closes the loop between the unit and the failure taxonomy.

*Epistemic status: schemas, internal working models, chaining, and the constellational reading of personality disorder are established or already canon; Jung's complex is a historical anchor read charitably. The neme-complex as a level, and the identification of constellation with maladaptive complex, are framework commitments.*

### The disposable self

The self is the neme's disposable soma, and the claim is a proportion: **the self is to the repertoire what the body is to the genome.** A soma is not built by one gene; it is the shared vehicle of the whole genome, maintained as far as it serves the store's propagation and discarded while the store persists. The self is not one neme's carrier; it is the shared soma of the unit's whole engram-store — built from the stores, spent in their service, dissolved while they persist. The scope is cut deliberately: the claim covers the self — the felt owner-and-author, the homuncular ghost — and not consciousness. What dissolves in the contemplative cases is the subject; experience continues.

The register is specific: the self is the *Popperian* neme's soma. At the model-free register the neme spends performances. When the neme matures into the internal model, its trials move into simulation — and a simulated trial needs a simulated trier. Hypotheses die in your stead, and the self is the stead: the protagonist built to absorb the imagined deaths so the body doesn't. That is why the self-model installs at Mammal, the clade of Simulating, and not before — the soma arrives with the trials that need it.

Disposability runs in three registers, all observed. Spent per trial: the simulated self dies in imagined futures all day. Cleared per night: the running self is discontinued every sleep and re-instantiated from the stores each morning. Dissolvable under attention: at the Learner dial's dormant pole the self-model goes quiet while the unit acts at full capacity — flow, and the contemplative states, are the narration off and the repertoire running.

The disposable-soma signature — maintained only as far as it serves the store — holds empirically at three points. The self-referential machinery runs during planning, autobiographical projection, and mental time-travel, and stands down during absorbed execution: built for the trials, idle otherwise. Re-introducing it degrades consolidated skill — choking under explicit self-monitoring is the soma getting in the repertoire's way. And the repertoire persists without it: the amnesic whose episodic and self-narrative machinery was destroyed still acquired and ran procedural nemes, improving across sessions he could not remember.

The minimal self — the felt doer, Mammal machinery — is the neme's soma: the simulation protagonist. The meme's soma is the group, and the same proportion runs one line up: the group is to the meme-store what the body is to the genome. It is built by the memes it carries, maintained exactly as far as it propagates them, and discarded while the store survives — a congregation dies and the faith migrates, a guild dissolves and the craft is carried on, a village empties and the language keeps being spoken elsewhere. Persons are replaceable components of that vehicle, which is what somatic cells are. The two somas sit at two scales rather than at two registers of one thing. One strain, held openly: the narrative self — named, storied, reputation-bearing, and what Dennett's center of narrative gravity and Blackmore's selfplex describe — is not the meme's vehicle, and where it does sit is unruled.

The convergence at the bottom is the vocabulary discipline discovered introspectively. The meditator hunts the thinker — the entity that picks the thoughts — and finds only the contest: candidates arising, one winning, no owner anywhere. No selector, only selection, stated from inside. And the whole claim carries its rationale the licensed way: units whose simulations had protagonists chose better, so the self was kept — a free-floating rationale, represented nowhere until written down.

*Epistemic status: default-mode dynamics, choking under self-focus, the amnesic dissociation, and flow are established at the level used here. The proportion, the Popperian-register placement, the three disposals, and the group as the meme's vehicle are framework theoretical commitments; the no-thinker convergence is a reading of the contemplative reports, not an empirical claim.*

### Attention, developed

The mechanism is scarcity. Selection bites only where a limited resource forces competition; in the genetic case that is finite carrying capacity. The neme's scarce resource is attention, one body, one action channel, a bounded workspace. Many operants are always live; only some win the workspace, drive behavior, and earn the reward that consolidates them. Attention is the bottleneck they compete through, which is the biased-competition account (Desimone and Duncan) and the global workspace (Baars; Dehaene) read as a selection arena, in the spirit of William James's original definition of attention as taking possession of one object out of several possible. Attention is the carrying capacity of the neme's Darwinism. The framing is Donald Campbell's: blind variation needs a selective-retention filter, and at the nemetic line attention is that filter's gate.

The arena is a vicarious selector: it discards candidates against a stand-in criterion instead of letting the world discard them, and the criterion is a fossil of outer verdicts. Ancestor removal set its coarse structure — the innate alarms, and what counts as primary reward, which is the scoring rule the whole loop runs on — and within-life learning tunes the fine structure on that inherited rule. Losing here is cheap by design: a candidate discarded in the workspace is deferred, and a candidate discarded by the world is spent. Popper's hypotheses dying in our stead is this arrangement stated from the organism's side. Seen from the genetic line's test, the whole arena reads the other way: everything the workspace does, deliberation included, collapses into generation — one candidate offered to the world, its production biased by fossilized verdicts. Both readings are true at once, and the pairing is the parallax every inner selector shows.

The arena is also where bias enters the world. Only a mechanism that estimates can be biased — a bias is a gap between a weighting and a payoff, and the world's test has no weighting apart from the payoff — and this line carries the first implemented estimator. Its four credit-assignment heuristics, one per function position, are the Pavlovian associability heuristics: eligibility trace (recency), blocking (familiarity), overshadowing (potency), latent inhibition (novelty). The genetic line has none, because nothing on it estimates; the memetic line's heuristics ride in the adopting mind, which is this machinery re-aimed at tellings.

This is not grafted onto the framework. The machinery the neme is selected in carries attention as a native parameter: in active inference attention is precision-weighting, the gain on which prediction errors update the model and select the policy. The Regulator line regulates by allocating precision, and which neme regulates is set by where precision goes. Active inference supplies the arena's scoring rule and no account of what competes or what persists; that division of labor is the framework's, and it is why the two are complements rather than rivals.

It also firms the neme's selection step. The division of labor: attention is the competition arena (what gets expressed and competes) and reward is the fitness criterion (which expressed variant is retained). Attention runs the contest; reward scores it; consolidation writes the winner to the engram. Together they are the nemetic line's within-lifetime Darwin, with behavioral exploration on the Connector — the store revised by acting, with no reward scoring it — as its blind variation.

One disambiguation. "Attention" names two things at different points of the loop. Attention as resource (the bounded workspace) is the arena. Attention as spotlight (what is attended to now) is the phenotype of whichever neme just won. The faculty sorts; the spotlight is the readout.

**Where attention goes across the lines.** Internalized arena at the nemetic line; then *joint* attention at the bridge to the memetic line, where shared attention is the groundwork of the we-mode and the public focal point Common Knowledge runs on; attention externalizes again as the memetic line picks it up. The trail ends there. The digital medium's arena is execution — a program that runs is retained and copied, and code that never runs is pruned — so human attention meets that medium's output rather than sorting its variation. The attention economy is a selection environment the digital medium's products compete in, downstream of the arena and not the arena itself.

*Epistemic status: attention as selection (James, biased competition, global workspace), attention as precision (active inference), vicarious selection (Campbell), and the associability heuristics are established. Attention as the neme's internalized arena, the calibration reading, and the extension into joint attention at the memetic bridge are framework theoretical commitments.*

### The machinery by clade

The four functions are cross-clade structural roles, and the machinery implementing them changes by clade: homeostatic correction at LUCA, state-dependent gating at Prokaryote, associative learning at Bilaterian, reinforcement learning at Vertebrate, generative modelling run offline at Mammal, gossip-tracked social debt at Band, code-tracked obligation at City. The role is what carries. The machinery is what the clade had available.

Active inference is the line-native machinery at Mammal. Within-lifetime timescales. The Regulator is the precision-weighted prior structure; the Learner is the generative model that predicts incoming sensory data; the Effector is the motor system. There is no fourth function at this clade. Expression and display — the body made legible to conspecifics — are traffic between model-based animals with one another in the store, and they run on the Connector and the Workspace; active inference does not natively model them either, and the gap is recorded rather than smoothed. Other clades in the nemetic line run other line-native machinery — state-dependent gating, associative learning, reinforcement learning — each implementing the cybernetic dynamic in the form available to the clade.

## The Meme

The meme is a unit of culture copied between minds — Dawkins's coinage, Dennett's workhorse. Its tokens are tellings; its store is the brains that hold it, and later the text; its population is everything currently being retold; its fitness is retellability — not truth, and not host benefit. The meme and the meme-complex are different levels, a unit and the assembly built of units, not competing definitions.

The meme acts like a neme with an escape route. Its copying is re-instantiation in the strictest sense: nothing physical crosses between minds — a telling prompts the hearer to rebuild the form from their own material, performance to performance through stores that never touch. That is the neme's mechanism run between bodies instead of within one, and the continuity is real rather than analogical: animal tradition, the socially learned operant, is the bridge — a neme crossing between conspecifics by imitation before any symbol exists. What the memetic line adds is the escape. A form that hops hosts outlives every host, so the meme recovers the deep-time horizon the neme lost: the engram is trapped in its body, and the meme is never in only one.

Rebuilding-from-prompt is also the meme's standing vulnerability, and the field's central dispute. Sperber's objection: reconstruction is not copying — each rebuild is drawn toward the attractors of the host mind, so culture is shaped by attraction rather than selection. Dennett's answer: language digitizes — a mis-heard form is snapped to the nearest word in the lexicon, so the medium error-corrects, restoring the fidelity that reconstruction loses. The framework holds the dispute as a graded fact rather than a war. Where the medium digitizes and attempts individuate sharply — the turn, the written word — selection dynamics dominate and the meme's-eye view has traction; where forms blur, attraction dominates. One dispute, resolved per medium rather than won.

The test is uptake — taken up and retold, or let die in the room — and the adopting mind that administers it is nemetic machinery, which is why the meme's biases are the estimator's heuristics re-aimed at tellings; the transmission-bias literature (content, prestige, conformity) is the anchor register. The test's structure is doubled: speaker effort pulls forms toward simplification, hearer recovery pushes them back apart, and surviving forms sit in the trade-off.

Cui bono is the meme's whole payoff. Fitness-as-retellability explains the class of culture unintelligible as host adaptation — martyrdom, celibate clergy propagating the faith that forbids their own reproduction, chain letters, earworms — and the mundane versions, easy-to-repeat beating accurate. Dennett's wild and domesticated memes give the unit its trajectory: feral at the founding clade, where gossip spreads on its own fitness, increasingly domesticated up the line as comprehending hosts breed their memes on purpose — the line drifting toward the comprehending corner, read at the unit.

*Epistemic status: the meme (Dawkins), its development (Dennett, Blackmore), the attraction objection (Sperber), the digitization answer (Dennett), and the transmission-bias literature (Boyd and Richerson) are established positions in an unsettled field. The neme-to-meme continuity through animal tradition is a framework commitment; resolving the selection-attraction dispute by grading media on how sharply their attempts individuate is the framework's position.*

## The Program: the meme in the digital medium

The program is the meme rendered in a code a machine executes, and it is the strongest replicator form in the matrix sitting in the least Darwinian regime. It is the same unit as the told meme because it passes the same test: source and serialized weights are re-emittable by any user of the code, tokened — a machine corrects a malformed form to nothing, which is digitization at its limit — and read by executors that share nothing of the author's but the code. What the digital medium adds is the executor: a rendering prepared and screened against reference and store, the Counselor's product, run by machinery built to its terms instead of by a mind that interprets it. Its tokens are running processes; its store is code; its reproduction is literal — install, fork, dependency — and bit-perfect, the only copying in the matrix that loses nothing. Its test is being run and kept: a program that runs is retained and copied, and code that never runs is pruned. The invocation's halt closes each trial; deletion, and never being invoked again, is the death of the unit.

The program acts like a neme at both ends of its life. Its run is a performance read off a stored form, re-instantiated at every invocation the way the neme is re-instantiated at every emission. And its store is serially rewritten in place — patched, versioned, retrained — an open write-back topology with no wall between what the deployment goes through and what the next release carries. The trained model is the sharpest case: weights are not written but fit, a store rewritten by the outcomes of its own performances, which is the neme's shape executed on the digital substrate — and it is the clade where the medium stops being fully auditable by the humans in the loop.

What keeps the regime un-Darwinian is generation: mostly directed, the writer consulting a model of the test, which is the comprehending corner. The proof that the substrate supports the full engine anyway is the artificial-life record: give programs blind variation and native copying — Ray's Tierra, the Avida platform — and complete Darwinian evolution runs, parasites, arms races, and the evolution of complex features included. Ordinary software differs from a digital organism only in where its generation sits on the blind-to-directed axis, and that position is drifting: auto-generated code and models trained on engagement de-comprehend the generator, so the program's-eye view — today mostly the designer's-eye view in costume — gains independent traction exactly as fast.

Cui bono at this unit reads institutional: survival by being load-bearing. The unkillable legacy core, the file format nobody can abandon, the dependency everything calls — each is a program whose fitness is the cost of deleting it. Malware is the view running feral and honest, and it is where the replicator reading was first taken literally: a computer virus is a program whose whole phenotype is its own copying.

*Epistemic status: bit-perfect copying, dependency lock-in, and the artificial-life results (Tierra; Avida, including the evolved-complex-features result) are established. Dawkins on computer viruses as literal replicators is the placement's oldest anchor. The neme-shaped reading of the trained model, and the drift claim about generation de-comprehending, are framework commitments.*

## The tower, read across the units

Dennett's Generate-and-Test tower (an intuition pump) runs Darwinian, Skinnerian, Popperian, Gregorian, and it maps onto the Trellis as such: Darwinian is genetic, Skinnerian is the nemetic model-free neme, Popperian is the nemetic model-based neme, and Gregorian, named for Richard Gregory's mind-tools, is the meme. The tower has no digital floor, and the digital medium does not add one: the program enters not as a smarter creature but as the first form of the meme whose generator was comprehending from birth — the tower climbed from the far end.
