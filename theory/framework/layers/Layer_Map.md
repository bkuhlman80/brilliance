# The Clade Venn Layer Map

---

## The matrix

The Venn has three slots on the genetic line, seven on the nemetic, fifteen on the memetic. A function is born at its line's founding clade and runs at every clade after it.

| Function | Born at | Its store | Line test |
|-----|---------|----------|---------|
| **Member** | Band-Human (100 KYA), language | the meme | uptake: taken up and retold; in the digital medium, run and kept |
| **Learner** | Eumetazoa (~700 MYA), the nerve net | the neme | the outcome of expressed behavior: a route kept by being re-met at the founding clade, scored inside the unit from where scoring installs |
| **Regulator** | RNA World (Hadean, before ~4 BYA), the template consulted in copying | the gene | survival and reproduction under finite carrying capacity |
| **Effector** | the prework, as process | — | — |

| Line | Clades, in order |
|---|---|
| **Genetic** (3 slots) | RNA World · Ribocyte · Progenote · LUCA · Prokaryote · Eukaryote · Metazoa |
| **Nemetic** (7 slots) | Eumetazoa · Bilaterian · Vertebrate · Mammal · Primate |
| **Memetic, symbolic medium** (15 slots) | Band-Human · Settlement-Human · City-Human · Empire-Human |
| **Memetic, digital medium** (15 slots) | Computer · Network · Model · Agent |

Twenty addresses: 21 genetic cells, 35 nemetic, 120 memetic — 176 in all. Each clade is a category of units sharing an architectural signature, named for a representative form and containing internal diversity. The name is an address on the matrix. What a placement at that address does and does not claim about the living members of the clade is a question of evidence rather than of naming, and this table settles only the naming.

Every cell carries either a positive label naming the operation read there, or "owed". A label is an install read at that clade; nothing is written into a cell from the clade below, since covered layers keep running under every cell without being named twice. On the genetic line the Controller cell is the stage's center, and on the nemetic line the Conservator cell is; those cells carry the clade's breakthrough and, where a setting was also seated there, the setting is listed with it.

**Marks.** "Owed" marks a cell whose install is still to be found: a cell no card at the clade reads at its composition. Where a cell lists more than one label, they are claimants, and the session chooses.

## The prework

Below the genetic line sits the prework: chemistry that keeps itself going, with no store, a Venn of one slot — autocatalytic cycles, vesicles that grow and divide, activated nucleotides and untemplated polymers forming from simpler molecules. It is described and not tabulated, because a one-slot Venn has no addresses. It lays down the nucleic medium, and the genetic line begins at RNA World, the first address where a stored reference runs as a replicator: a template consulted during copying is a held value the copy can be wrong about, walled because nothing the strand does edits its own sequence, and the Venn there has three slots.

---

## What each clade name covers

A clade is a category of units sharing an architectural signature. The clade name is not a single named entity. Each clade is named for a representative form and contains internal diversity.

- **RNA World** names populations of RNA replicators, possibly encapsulated in fatty-acid vesicles, with all catalysis by RNA — the doer and the copy one molecule.
- **Ribocyte** names the RNA–protein cell: an RNA genome read by a ribosome into peptides that do the catalysis.
- **Progenote** names the integrating cell: a DNA archive emerging, with genes still passing freely between cells, so that heredity is communal and no lineage yet owns its store.
- **LUCA** names the last universal common ancestor: not one cell but the population from which bacteria and archaea both descend, carrying the genetic code, the ribosome, the rotary ATP synthase and recombinational repair.
- **Prokaryote** names the clade containing bacteria and archaea — single cells with no nucleus, whose DNA sits loose inside and whose ribosomes read it directly. Archaea sit inside it as a proper subclade and hold no address of their own.
- **Eukaryote** names the clade containing protists, fungi, plants, and animals. All share the organized-interior architecture with membrane-bounded organelles.
- **Metazoa** names the clade containing all animals, sponges included: multicellular, with differentiated cell types and the postsynaptic scaffold, and without neurons at its base.
- **Eumetazoa** names the clade containing all animals with a nerve net or nervous system — cnidarians and bilaterians.
- **Bilaterian** names the clade containing all bilaterally symmetric animals with centralized nervous systems.
- **Mammal** names the clade containing all mammals, with their many brain region elaborations.
- **Band** names a foraging group of a few dozen. **Settlement** names a sedentary village of near-strangers.
- **City** names the clade containing classic city-states, urban-centered kingdoms, and other forms of polity organized around an urban administrative core. The city-clade contains both the city-as-polity (Sumerian Uruk, Greek polis, Italian Renaissance city-states) and the larger state-with-central-city forms (Pharaonic Egypt, Han China, the Achaemenid satrapies). Settlements supply the population and agricultural-surplus base. Trade networks supply the connectivity that lets the city aggregate goods, people, and information beyond what any single settlement can produce.
- **Empire** names the clade containing territorial empires, maritime empires, and ideological empires. All share the move of binding multiple cities, and often colonies, under a universalizing framework.

At memetic clades the unit is the group — the band, the settlement, the city, the empire — and the person is where a meme rests between expressions, which is the store's role.

**Each clade stands in one of two relations to the clade below it, and the matrix uses both.** Some clades *nest*: the clade above is a proper subclade of the one below, and its members are members of both. Prokaryotes descend from LUCA, Metazoa nest inside Eukaryota, Eumetazoa inside Metazoa, Bilateria inside Eumetazoa, Vertebrata inside Bilateria, Mammalia inside Vertebrata, Primates inside Mammalia. Other clades are *built from* the clade below as constituent components: eukaryotes contain prokaryote-derived organelles, cities contain settlements, empires contain cities and their hinterlands. Nesting says the unit *is* one of those; construction says the unit is *made of* them. Neither relation is the clade name's warrant — the name is an address, and what makes it the right address is what the evidence says installed there.

## Eras and coinciding events

Each clade's emergence date anchors in geological or cultural time. Where a major event coincides with the clade's emergence, the event is named. The nemetic-line dates track extinctions and faunal radiations. The memetic-line dates track climatic and technological transitions. The genetic line is event-poor at deep-time resolution, and three of its clades rest on first-fossil, biomarker, or molecular-clock evidence without a near-coincident event.

| Clade | Date | Geological or Cultural Anchor | Coinciding Event |
|------|------|-------------------------------|------------------|
| RNA World | Hadean, before ~4 BYA | ordered, not dated | No fossil or molecular anchor; the stratum is inferred from the ribosome's RNA core and ribozyme chemistry |
| Ribocyte | Hadean, before ~4 BYA | ordered, not dated | No anchor beyond its place between all-RNA catalysis and the DNA archive |
| Progenote | Hadean–Eoarchean | ordered, not dated | No anchor beyond its place before LUCA; inferred from the non-homologous DNA replication machinery of bacteria and archaea |
| LUCA | ~4 BYA | Hadean–Eoarchean boundary | Molecular-clock anchor; no discrete event (weakest dated clade in the matrix) |

### Genetic line — three slots

#### RNA World

| Position | Layer |
|----------|-------|

*References: Gilbert 1986 on the RNA world; Joyce and Szostak on ribozyme polymerases and primer extension; Szostak, Bartel & Luisi 2001 and Chen, Roberts & Szostak 2004 on fatty-acid vesicle growth, division and competition. Under the current blind read, every card at this address that reads with the Regulator is a sequence consulted by base-pairing — primer extension, checking the newest base against the template, templated ligation, a ribozyme's guide region pairing with its substrate — and every card that reads as the Effector alone is vesicle growth and division, diffusion, product release, energy coupling or catalysis by fold shape. #### Ribocyte

| Position | Layer |
|----------|-------|
| Effector | Biosynthesis |
| Regulator | Genetic Code |
| Controller, the center | Translation |

*References: Woese 1998 and Fox 2010 on the RNA–protein cell; Noller on the ribosome as a ribozyme; Cech 2000; Ibba & Söll 2000 on aminoacyl-tRNA synthetases and editing. The Genetic Code is the second kind of reference on the line: not a sequence copied against itself but a mapping, each triplet to one amino acid, held in the shapes of the charging enzymes, arbitrary in that any other assignment would have served, and one the cell can be wrong about — a mischarged carrier — and in some enzymes consults twice, at an editing site that destroys a near-miss. Under the blind read every step that matches a carrier to a message or an amino acid to a carrier is E×R. Division gated on a size mark also reads E×R here, so a setpoint-type reference is first read one clade below LUCA.*

#### Progenote

| Position | Layer |
|----------|-------|
| Effector | Assembly |
| Regulator | Archive |
| Controller, the center | Gene Exchange |

*References: Woese & Fox 1977 and Woese 1998 on the progenote and communal heredity; Leipe, Aravind & Koonin 1999 and Forterre on the non-homologous DNA replication machinery of bacteria and archaea; Lane on the stepwise arrival of DNA. The Archive is a reference kept with a second copy and corrected toward itself: mismatch repair reads the old strand against the new, break repair reads the sister or a partner, excision repair reads the opposite strand. ## What is anchored and what is not

Two clades carry no external anchor at any slot: **Computer** and **Model**. Those are the thinnest addresses in the matrix and the gap is recorded here rather than smoothed over — and they sit side by side, so the digital medium is anchored at half its clades while the other lines are anchored at all of theirs. City-Human lost its one anchor when the label it anchored was deleted for failing to render at group scale.

The density is uneven in a way worth seeing. Mammal, Primate, and Band-Human are the three densest addresses above the genetic line, which tracks the fact that three mature empirical literatures — attachment, primate social cognition, and the self-conscious emotions — happen to sit there. The genetic line is anchored at all seven clades, though the anchoring runs from near-certain at the ribosome and the rotary ATP synthase to moderate at prototrophy and at the LUCA date, and the three clades below LUCA are ordered by inference from surviving machinery and not dated at all.
