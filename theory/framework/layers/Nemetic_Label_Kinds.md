# Nemetic labels — operation or setting

*Pre-registration for the claimant-cell test. Every label at Eumetazoa, Bilaterian, Vertebrate, Mammal and Primate on the map or in the ledger, marked by kind before its session scores it. An operation has a composition and is scored by the four tests. A setting is a dial on a loop and has no composition of its own; its card must describe the loop it sits on, not the animal's behavior, and it is scored only for whether the loop it describes has the composition of the cell it is seated in. A card for a setting that narrates what the animal does will read as the workspace, and that read says nothing about the dial.*

*A label is marked by what it names: something the unit does is an operation; how much, how readily or how strongly it does it is a setting. Nothing follows from the count of the cell a label was seated in.*

## What this changes in the claimant test

Of the thirteen claimant cells, eight are pair cells, and every incumbent in a pair cell is a setting. For those cells the test is not "do the claimants land on the pair" but "does a card that describes the loop land on the pair." That is the internal-operation harvest's question, and the setting cards owe a re-authoring under the loop rule before they can be scored at all. The five cells whose incumbents are operations — the four nemetic centers and the Bilaterian Learner — are scored as before.
