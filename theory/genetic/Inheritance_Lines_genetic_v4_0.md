*Bramble: A Brief History of Personality — Inheritance Lines and Capabilities — genetic line (cells Protocell, Prokaryote, Eukaryote, Eumetazoa). Each line file carries the full shared framework; see also the neuronal and symbolic line files.*

*Working document. v4.0. May 2026.*

## Scope and Content Types

The framework is a theory of personality and capacity. Both are read off a single Venn at each cell. There is one Venn per cell. Its regions are not all the same kind of thing. They sort into five content-types:

- **Motivations** sit at the four single-motivation regions. A motivation is one of the unit's primal directives.
- **Tendencies** sit at the four adjacent pairs. A tendency is the dispositional expression of two adjacent motivations together.
- **Signatures** sit at the two opposite pairs. A signature is the level-defining coupling of two motivations across the Venn.
- **Capacities** sit at the four triples. A capacity is a synthesis of three motivations the unit can perform.
- **Breakthroughs** sit at the center. A breakthrough is the unique synthesis that requires all four motivations.

Personality and capacity are content types occupying different regions of one diagram, not two parallel diagrams.

The 4-circle Venn formalism follows from the four cybernetic functions: Effector, Regulator, Modeler, Reviser. The regions carry different content types because of the Port-Royal comprehension and extension gradient. A region defined by more attributes has richer comprehension and smaller extension. A one-attribute region holds a single motivation. A two-attribute region holds either a tendency or a signature: an adjacent pair holds a tendency, an opposite pair holds a signature. A three-attribute region, a triple, holds a capacity. A capacity is a synthesis of three motivations the unit can perform. The four-attribute center holds the breakthrough, the unique synthesis that requires all four. The gradient is developed in `trellis_framework doc`.

The framework is structural-explanatory first. Specific testable predictions are extracted in the journal-paper pipeline rather than enumerated here.

ABHOP's three lines (genetic, neuronal, symbolic) instantiate the bio-psycho-social model that medicine, psychology, and sociology have converged on across the twentieth century. Bio is the genetic line. Psycho is the neuronal line. Social is the symbolic line. This is a descriptive mapping rather than a relabeling. The bio-psycho-social framing leaves underspecified what the line-specific machinery is at each line, and ABHOP supplies that structural detail.

### Row functions

Row function definitions live in `trellis_framework doc`. The architectural summary:

- **Effector** executes the line's native verb (persist in genetic, seize in neuronal, commit in symbolic). In selection, weights recency.
- **Regulator** stabilizes behavior against stored references. In selection, weights familiarity.
- **Modeler** maintains internal representation that organizes inputs. In selection, weights potency.
- **Reviser** revises the references the regulator stabilizes against. In selection, weights novelty.

The four functions emerge in order at every line because each depends on the prior. There is no Regulator without an Effector to regulate. There is no Modeler without something worth representing. There is no Reviser without a model worth updating.

### Adjacent pairs, triples, and opposite pairs

Adjacent pairs instantiate four loop-functions. They carry tendencies, the dispositional pair-expressions of two adjacent motivations:

- **Controller** (Effector × Regulator): closed-loop control, action plus stabilization.
- **Anticipator** (Regulator × Modeler): model-informed regulation.
- **Adapter** (Modeler × Reviser): self-revising model.
- **Explorer** (Reviser × Effector): exploratory action.

"Tendency" is a permissive umbrella term. At genetic-line cells the adjacent pairs hold genetic-line dispositional capabilities such as Throughput Type and Life-History Type. At neuronal and symbolic cells the adjacent pairs hold personality traits such as Coping Style and Conscientiousness. The umbrella term spans the lines. The specific named constructs differ by cell and by the literature anchoring them.

The adjacent-pair labels follow a naming convention by line. The genetic-line adjacent pairs tend toward Type suffixes. The neuronal-line adjacent pairs tend toward Style suffixes. The symbolic-line adjacent pairs tend toward -ness or related nominal suffixes. The convention is descriptive of the empirical literatures the constructs anchor in, not prescriptive.

Triples (one motivation absent) instantiate four synthesis-functions. They carry capacities, syntheses of three motivations the unit can perform:

- **Reactor** (Modeler absent): action and stabilization and revision without internal model. Model-free regulated action.
- **Conservator** (Reviser absent): action and stabilization and representation without revision. The mature loop running without meta-update.
- **Planner** (Effector absent): regulation and representation and revision without execution. Strategic deliberation.
- **Pursuer** (Regulator absent): action and representation and revision without stabilization. Goal-pursuit without rule-enforced inhibition.

The two opposite pairs carry signatures, the level-defining couplings of two motivations across the Venn. The Effector × Modeler pair hosts Lineage, Host, Temperament, Self-Model, Self-Reference, and Self-Evaluation across the cells. The Regulator × Reviser pair hosts Organism, Reflex Repertoire, Behavioral Syndrome, Sociometer, Self-Construal, and Identity. Each signature persists for two consecutive cells, the cell where it is introduced plus the next. It then shifts when one of its two contributing motivations is eclipsed. See `trellis_framework doc`.

The 4-circle Venn has 15 regions: 4 single-motivation regions (motivations), 4 adjacent pairs (tendencies), 2 opposite pairs (signatures), 4 triples (capacities), and 1 center (the breakthrough).

### The Breakthrough

The center of each Venn is the breakthrough. It is the synthetic capability that emerges when all four flag-carriers operate together. It is not a fifth motivation. It is what the four jointly produce. Examples are Natural Selection at Protocell, Attention at Bilaterian, Speaking at Band-Human, Codes at City-Human, and Stories at Empire-Human. Each line's founding-cell breakthrough is that line's selector, the process that makes its heritable pattern cumulative: Natural Selection on the genetic line, Attention on the neuronal line, Speaking on the symbolic line. The framework uses Bennett's Reinforcing, Simulating, and Mentalizing for the upper three neuronal cells, and names the founding neuronal breakthrough Attention, of which Bennett's steering is the bilaterian expression. Per-cell breakthroughs live in the Per-Cell Treatments below.

### Adjacent-pair, triple, and opposite-pair persistence

Each level transition introduces 6 new labels: 2 new adjacent pairs, 3 new triples, and 1 new signature. These are the regions that include the just-introduced motivation. It persists 4 labels from the cell below: 2 adjacent pairs, 1 triple, and 1 signature, the regions that do not include the new motivation. Persistence is exact. Any cell whose count breaks indicates a hole in the label library.

Protocell is the founding cell and has no cell below to persist from. It carries an Effector × Modeler signature, Lineage, but its Regulator × Reviser signature is empty, since a Regulator × Reviser signature requires a prior introduction. Every cell from Prokaryote onward carries both an Effector × Modeler and a Regulator × Reviser signature.

A vocabulary shift runs at the genetic-to-neuronal boundary. Genetic-line labels are drawn from cell biology and developmental biology. Neuronal-line labels are drawn from behavioral ecology and personality psychology. The constellations that bridge the boundary carry different labels on either side. This is a deliberate vocabulary shift, not a persistence failure. The disciplines that built the vocabularies anchored at different cells.

## Cell Names as Clade Shorthand

Each cell name is shorthand for a clade. A clade is a category of units sharing the cell's architectural signature. The cell name is not a single named entity. The cell name picks out the representative form. The clade contains internal diversity.

The convention applies across lines:

- **Eukaryote** names the clade containing protists, fungi, plants, and animals. All share the organized-interior architecture with membrane-bounded organelles.
- **Eumetazoa** names the clade containing all multicellular animals with differentiated tissues.
- **Bilaterian** names the clade containing all bilaterally symmetric animals with centralized nervous systems.
- **Mammal** names the clade containing all mammals, with their many brain region elaborations.
- **City** names the clade containing classic city-states, urban-centered kingdoms, and other forms of polity organized around an urban administrative core. The city-clade contains both the city-as-polity (Sumerian Uruk, Greek polis, Italian Renaissance city-states) and the larger state-with-central-city forms (Pharaonic Egypt, Han China, the Achaemenid satrapies). Cities emerge from the symbiosis of settlements and trade networks. Settlements supply the population and agricultural-surplus base. Trade networks supply the connectivity that lets the city aggregate goods, people, and information beyond what any single settlement can produce. The city is the merger product.
- **Empire** names the clade containing territorial empires, maritime empires, and ideological empires. All share the move of binding multiple cities, and often colonies, under a universalizing framework.

Each clade is built from instances of the prior clade as constituent components. Eukaryotes contain prokaryote-derived organelles. Eumetazoa contain eukaryotic cells. Cities contain settlements as their agricultural and demographic base, with trade-network connectivity threading through them. Empires contain cities and their hinterlands. The constituent is the clade's structural innovation. The clade name picks out the level of organization built from that constituent.

## Per-Cell Treatments

The twelve filled cells of the matrix each get one section below. Each section opens with structural commentary that covers the cell's breakthrough, virtuous cycle, and any cell-specific notes about opposite-pair signatures or bridge-entity status. One unified Venn table follows.

### Genetic Line

The genetic line ratchets Boltzmann goo through nucleic-acid inheritance. Its pre-line groundwork is Deacon's homeodynamic, morphodynamic, and teleodynamic processes, the pre-biotic ratchets that prepared chemistry for life. The genetic line's primaries at the bottom of the stack (Dissipation, Self-Organization, Self-Production) are this pre-line groundwork carried forward as the founding cell's working motivations. Persistence, the line's Effector motivation, is what Darwin selects when ratchets exist.

Genetic-line cells have no documented personality-trait structure. Trait-level individual differences in dispositional response, the content personality psychology studies, are not documented at any cellular or multicellular level below the brain-bearing organism. The tendencies at the adjacent pairs of a genetic-line Venn are dispositional capabilities of the cell-as-unit, not personality traits. The signatures at the opposite pairs are cell-level couplings of the same kind.

#### Protocell Stack

The founding form of the genetic line. The line-defining move is the move from chemistry to inheritance. The defining mechanism is autocatalytic ratcheting: RNA sequences that catalyze their own replication, where information and function are the same molecule. Once ratchets exist, *Persistence* operates as an attractor immediately. Any sequence that replicates more reliably outcompetes any sequence that doesn't. Selection pressure on replication fidelity is what the rest of biology runs on. This is recency in differential-reproduction terms: the sequence that keeps replicating under present conditions outcompetes one built for conditions that have already passed.

The reinforcing cycle requires three conditions to hold jointly: a medium (water), a container (lipid vesicle), and periodic energy gradients (tidal, thermal, redox cycles). When all three hold, autopoiesis closes the loop. The breakthrough is Natural Selection itself, named at the level of what the Protocell-as-unit can do that nothing below can: ratchet pattern persistence through differential survival.

The three pre-line groundwork processes carry flags at Protocell. Dissipation is self-maintenance against thermodynamic decay. Self-Organization is form-building dynamics. Self-Production is the work-doing dynamics that prepared chemistry for ends-directed behavior. Each relabels one of Deacon's pre-line groundwork processes: Dissipation the homeodynamic, Self-Organization the morphodynamic, Self-Production the teleodynamic.

The Effector × Modeler pair introduces Lineage, the framework's first signature. Lineage names the persistent replicating-form chain, the continuity of the replicating form across copies. The Regulator × Reviser pair is empty at Protocell, since a Regulator × Reviser signature requires a prior cell to carry one from.

The Explorer (Reviser × Effector) carries Acquisition Type, which spans the genetic line's exploratory uptake of exogenous functional content: horizontal gene transfer, plasmid uptake, transformation, conjugation, transduction, endosymbiosis, and viral integration. The label names the broad phenomenon across the Protocell-Prokaryote-Eukaryote span rather than any one prokaryote-specific mechanism.

#### Prokaryote Stack

Prokaryotes introduce stored regulation in the genetic register. The genome no longer just specifies form. It specifies *responses*. The defining mechanism is state-dependent gating: switching behavioral programs based on internal and external conditions. *Coordination* operates as the attractor through this gating. A cell that can foreground different metabolic and motility programs in different conditions outsurvives one that can't. This is familiarity in differential-reproduction terms: canalization defends the developmental program already established against invasion by new variants.

The reinforcing cycle runs through state-dependent gating (one genome holding many program states), taxis tightening the loop between a sensed gradient and directed movement, and sequential responding exploiting the temporal order wired into the regulatory network. An early stimulus pre-induces the genes a reliably later one will need, so the cell readies the next program before its condition arrives. The breakthrough is Predation. Prokaryotes establish inter-organism interaction as a capacity layer, in the predator-prey sense and in the broader sense of one organism's behavior conditioned on another's state.

The Regulator swap at this cell replaces Dissipation with Coordination. The other three primaries (Persistence, Self-Organization, Self-Production) persist from Protocell.

The Effector × Modeler pair carries Lineage forward from Protocell. The Regulator × Reviser pair introduces Organism, the signature that names the bounded coordinated work-doing unit.

#### Eukaryote Stack

Eukaryotes introduce organized interiority in the genetic register: a cell with a defended interior containing organelles that maintain their own operating conditions. The nucleus protects DNA. The mitochondrion runs an electrochemical gradient at conditions hostile to the rest of the cell. The defining mechanism is endosymbiosis: capturing another organism and operating it as a component. *Interiority* operates as the attractor in the literal sense. Maintaining the interior against the exterior is what staying alive consists of. This is potency in differential-reproduction terms: the strongest environmental regularity dominates what the interior comes to model.

The bioenergetic transition through mitochondria is Nick Lane's territory. The eukaryotic cell is the bioenergetic platform that scaling everything else up depends on. The breakthrough is Scale & Size. Eukaryotes are large compared to prokaryotes and complex compared to prokaryotes. The difference traces to Production Type at the SW Adapter position.

The reinforcing cycle runs through mating and meiosis (sexual recombination plus the cell-division machinery that supports it), memory beyond moment-to-moment adaptation, and a hierarchical behavioral repertoire (nested programs invokable under different conditions, all running inside one cell). The Modeler swap at this cell replaces Self-Organization with Interiority. Persistence, Coordination, and Self-Production persist.

The Planner-region capacity is Checkpoint Control. The eukaryotic cell withholds a major transition, such as division or replication, until an internal check confirms the represented conditions are met. It represents the internal state, verifies it, and gates the transition, without itself executing the step it gates. The cell-cycle checkpoint system is the standard mechanism, conserved across eukaryotes; a few highly derived lineages have lost it secondarily.
The Effector × Modeler pair introduces Host, the signature that names the chimeric unit constituted by its incorporated symbiont, canonically the mitochondrion. The Regulator × Reviser pair carries Organism forward from Prokaryote.

#### Eumetazoa Stack

Eumetazoa are the high-variance bet of the genetic line. They are cells that stop competing with each other and start cooperating, with differentiated tissues specialized to different functions. This is not the default outcome of selection. Defection from cooperation is constantly selected for, and most attempts at multicellularity fail or remain marginal. The defining mechanism is differentiated tissues, including the first nervous tissues. The Reviser motivation that arrives at this cell, *Excitability*, names the rapid propagable response to stimulus that nervous tissue makes possible. This is novelty in differential-reproduction terms: disruptive selection favors the rare variant precisely because it is not yet common.

The reinforcing cycle runs through sleep-wake regulation of arousal, allostatic regulation (set points predicted by the brain rather than fixed homeostatically; Sterling), reflexes, habituation, and sensitization. All of these are forms of stored excitability tuning. The breakthrough is Neurons. The nervous tissue that arises here is what no eukaryote has, and it is the goo every neuronal-line capability will be built from. Eumetazoa is the cell where the breakthrough is the neuron itself.

This is the bridge cell to the neuronal line. The bet pays off because the nervous tissue animals require for coordination becomes the brain that founds the next line. Excitability persists as Reviser of Bilaterians, the founding cell of neuronal. The Reviser swap at Eumetazoa replaces Self-Production with Excitability. Persistence, Coordination, and Interiority persist.

The Effector × Modeler pair carries Host forward from Eukaryote. The Regulator × Reviser pair introduces Reflex Repertoire, the signature that names the stored set of coordinated excitable response patterns.

## 3D Bramble Visualization

### Core idea

The 4×4 matrix visualizes one phylogenetic thread, but the underlying architecture is a bramble growing up the trellis. Four parallel relay teams (Effector, Regulator, Modeler, Reviser) advance simultaneously, with each team's flag passing from one motivation to the next at handoffs. Genetic founds with four motivations (Persistence, Dissipation, Self-Organization, Self-Production) and ends with four different ones (Persistence, Coordination, Interiority, Excitability) after three handoffs. Each subsequent line founds with its own new Effector motivation and then runs through three more handoffs. The full chain of motivations introduced across the diagram is the bramble's growth across the trellis.

An interactive model would let users:

- Click any cell in the 4×4 to see the Venn from above (top view).
- Rotate 90° to see the bramble from the side, with the four active flag-carriers visible at the current rung and the retired motivations stacked below them as consolidated memory.
- Click an adjacent cell to watch a handoff: one team's flag passes from its current carrier to the new one; the prior carrier retires below.

### What the rotation reveals

**Top view (Venn).** The four flag-carrying spheres are semi-translucent. At their four-way intersection sits the breakthrough for the cell, rendered as its own distinct token rather than as a color mixture of the four primaries. The center is a capacity at the cell, not a motivation, and the rendering should reflect that.

**Side view (bramble).** The four flag-carrying spheres sit at the active rung, with the retired motivations stacked beneath them as consolidated memory. The breakthrough shows at the four-way intersection, not as an additional sphere. It is what the four jointly produce, not a fifth primary.

### The handoff

Exactly four spheres are flag-carriers at any cell, one per team. Move up one rung and one team's flag changes hands: the new motivation takes the flag from its same-color predecessor; the predecessor drops into consolidated memory. The other three teams' flag-carriers stay the same. The center of the new Venn, the new breakthrough, emerges as the four-way intersection of the new flag-carrying constellation. The handoff is the framework's central pedagogical moment.

### Phase change markers

Two transitions within the bramble mark inheritance-line changes:

- Eumetazoa to Bilaterians (genetic to neuronal, Effector flag passes from Persistence to Impetus).
- Primates to Bands (neuronal to symbolic, Effector flag passes from Impetus to Commitment).

**Recommended visualization:** inheritance-line bands plus bridge-entity rendering. Bands identify each line (GENETIC, NEURONAL, SYMBOLIC) alongside the bramble with subtle tinting. Bridge-entity rendering makes the parent-of-next-line relationship visible in the geometry. The top sphere of each line extends into or merges with the bottom sphere of the line above. Spacing rhythm (vertical gaps scaled to temporal interval) is interesting but loads heavy visual weight for one specific claim. Hold it as a togglable secondary layer.

### Zoom levels

- **Zoomed out:** three inheritance-line bands and two phase changes, the macro story.
- **Zoomed in:** the four flag-carriers and their breakthrough at center, the micro story.

Both are visible from the same 3D model by changing zoom.

### The bottom of the bramble

The bramble bottoms out at Protocell, with flag-carriers Persistence, Dissipation, Self-Organization, and Self-Production. The breakthrough at Protocell is Natural Selection. There is no line below. The genetic founding is the start of the diagram. The framework's most empirically speculative entry is the assignment of four discrete primaries to the RNA-world bramble. That assignment is a structural inference, not a measurement.

## Literature Context and Contributions

### The four-paper anchor

ABHOP's cleanest position in the contemporary methodological literature is *upstream* of a cluster of four papers in personality psychology and psychopathology that converge on a single argument when read together:

- **Eronen & Bringmann (2021)** diagnoses the field's theory crisis: too few robust phenomena, weak construct validity, fat-handed interventions that move many things at once.
- **Bleidorn (2024)** applies that diagnosis to lifespan personality development and finds the field rich on phenomena, weak on causes.
- **Bareinboim et al. (2020)** supplies the formal causal-inference apparatus that distinguishes layer-1 (observational), layer-2 (interventional), and layer-3 (counterfactual) claims.
- **Ringwald (2025)** calls for *mechanistic traits*: stable person-environment dynamics measurable through intensive longitudinal designs over actuarial risk prediction.

ABHOP is not in dialogue with these four papers. It is the architectural-explanatory framework their field-level diagnoses imply the field is missing. The framework's lead move is retrodictive (Layer 1): explaining why the Big Five, Dark Triad, HiTOP, and p-factor took the structural shapes they did. Its secondary move is forward-looking but disciplined to where it has traction: predicting the structure of the mechanistic-trait taxonomy as Ringwald's program develops.
