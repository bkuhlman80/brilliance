# Handoff Packet Template

*Convention: markup tags as follows. `MANDATORY:` = locked; voice has no discretion to omit or substantially alter. `VERBATIM:` = exact wording locked; reserved for carefully crafted explanations of complicated ideas. `LOAD-BEARING:` = canonical default; voice has discretion to change, but doing so triggers downstream effects (noted in parens after the content); flag back if changed. `EDITABLE ORDER` / `MANDATORY ORDER` at a block top sets sequence rules. Background information appears as a prose block at scene or packet level, not as an inline tag.*
