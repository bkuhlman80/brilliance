# The Knob-Name Tuning Fork — v3.1

*The single naming authority. Supersedes v3.0. The IFTTT is now keyed to **who is speaking, where in the
chapter they are, and what is happening** — because that is how the question actually arrives at the
page. Verified against `Inheritance_Lines_*_v4_0` and `bramble_specs_v1_4`.*

---

## The law, in one line

**The Venn is on the table → Tendency. The robot is on the table → Proper Name. The setting → volume up
/ volume down. The children, from Ch 9 → the rebrand, carrying -mode.**

Everything below is that sentence, made checkable.

## D. Use the Tendency instead of the Proper Name when…

The four triggers, and there are only four:

1. **The clade block is running** — the Venn, the circles, the seams, the corners.
2. **The board's geometry is the subject** — which seam a knob sits on, which pair is adjacent, what
   holds the opposite pair. *A knob rides a tendency; the tendency is the seam, the knob is the part.*
3. **Comparing the same architectural position across lines** — the Anticipator seam carried Foraging
   Type, then Neuroticism, then Extraversion. Proper Names cannot do this work; the seam is the constant.
4. **A character is speaking the literature** — Marge citing the construct, Lisa naming the clinical
   label, anyone reading the stamp.

Outside those four: **Proper Name.** If the sentence involves a hand — turned, thumbed, installed,
lifted, set in the groove, wound — it is always the Proper Name, with no exceptions.

## E. The hard nevers

- A **rebrand never enters narrator prose or a callback.** Prose says *Leading*; the child says *Boss-mode*.
- A **Tendency is never the knob's name on the page.** The board reads *Extraversion*; the knob is *Leading*.
- **The Proper Name is never derived from the Tendency.** Extraversion/*Extraverted*, Agreeableness/
  *Agreeable* — a name built by shaving the tendency's suffix is wrong by construction, and the tell is
  that it sounds right. Knob 6 (Coping / Coping Style) is the one legitimate echo and it is what makes
  the rest look plausible.
- **No rebrand exists before Ch 9.** The naming game originates at Band-Human and extends backward.
- **A descriptive phrase is never a name.** *The reading knob, the room dial, the give-way knob* — all
  coinages, all struck. **Test: could it be capitalized without looking odd? Then it is a name, and the
  wrong one.**
- **Behavior never anchors the clause where the volume belongs.** No: *"verb up on the face when it leans
  in."* Yes: *"verb up when the volume's high."*
- **Verbs are turn / wind.** Never flip, switch, toggle.

# Traps

**1. Neuroticism inverts.** A Proper Name names its knob's volume-**up** pole; a Tendency names the
clinical construct, whose high end on knob 7 is volume **down**. *High Neuroticism = Believer = volume
DOWN.* "Risking volume up" and "high neuroticism" are opposites. Only knob 7 does this.

**2. Coping and Hunting read backwards and are correct.** *Little* (not Beast) is knob-6 up; *Deep* (not
Wide) is knob-9 up. Confirmed twice — the fork's pole list and the specs' Lab column. Do not "fix" these.

**3. All twelve Proper Names are gerunds** — a permanent collision with ordinary English (*the going was
slow*, *he was leading*). Always capitalized; on first use in a scene, attach to *knob* or *volume* so
the frame lands before the ambiguity.

**4. Several rebrands are plain English** — Deep, Wide, Real, Little, Warm, Live. The **-mode** suffix is
what disambiguates them. It is the tell, not decoration.

**5. "Dial" is left alone in exactly three places:** the toy disc's own dial, belief-and-intensity
metaphors (*the dial that never quite hits zero*), and mechanism terms as description. Anywhere else,
a dial is a knob and should say so.

**6. Naming accuracy is a gradient, and it is the point.** Symbolic names are sharp because Bramble
reports from inside; the tetrapod's are borrowed; the vacuum's are guesses; *Live/Dead* is the bluntest,
because the crank has the least inside of anything in the lab.

**7. Naming the dead.** A sticker on a knob that retired before Ch 9 is still *minted* Ch 9 or later.
Retirement date ≠ naming date.

# Open conflicts

**C1 — Knob shape. The most expensive unresolved question in the project.**
This fork says all eleven non-crank knobs are two-pole volume knobs: *"no third stop,"* volume down *"not
a low dose."* `bramble_specs` Part III says knobs **6–12 are graded dials**, and the drafted prose is on
the specs' side — *"thumbed the knob a notch," "wound the knob down into the one thing and back out
across the whole field," "volume all the way up."* All three describe a range with positions in it.
**If the two-pole unification stands, these are not naming fixes — they are staged demos that have to be
re-blocked**, because a sweep across a field is not performable on a two-position switch.

**C2 — Do the Lab poles get to be setting-names?**
This fork says mechanism terms may appear but never as the setting's name. The specs say of knob 2:
*"The team — Bart included — calls it aerobic/anaerobic throughout."* That is the mechanism term serving
as the name, by the whole team, for eight chapters, and it collides with Bart's coin-nothing rider.
**Chs 1–8 need some setting-vocabulary before the rebrands exist** — that gap is real and wants deciding
on its own terms.

**C3 — Does the stamp carry the Tendency or the Proper Name?**
The specs say a knob is known by its clinical tendency label until the naming game reaches it, and that
Mira's stickers *cover the clinical labels*. Proposed ruling, three layers on one object: **stamped =
Tendency** (factory, permanent) · **spoken = Proper Name** (Marge's coinage, which is why it gets
attributed at all) · **stuck over the stamp, Ch 9+ = the rebrand.** This resolves why the sticker covers
something: the spoken name was never on the hardware.

**C4 — Face-screen caps.** BOSS and VIBE appear in caps in Ch 11. If the screen displays a rebrand, a
child's coinage has climbed onto Lisa's readout — forbidden in prose, ungoverned on hardware. Unverified;
worth a look.
