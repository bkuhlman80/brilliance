# The Tendency Knobs — Specimen Cards, second instrument

*Companion to the Body Sheet. The Body Sheet renders the surface a naturalist reads; this sheet
renders the twelve settings underneath it. Same specimens, same discipline — but where the Body Sheet
asks* could a reader draw her, *this one asks* which way is each knob turned, and what on the body
lets you read it without being told.*

---

## What this is, and the one thing that organizes it

The twelve knobs are **Bramble's** — literally. On him they sit on the outside, one arriving per
chapter, turnable by any hand in the household. He is the only character in the book whose tendencies
are external dials. **Every human carries the identical twelve, turned invisibly, on the inside.** 
So **Bramble's section is the key** — it defines each knob in its pure, drawable form. 
The seven human sections are the same twelve instruments played where you cannot see the knob, 
only the needle.

Bart, Marge, Lisa, and Burns are typed in canon —
Bart's symbolic-lind slideshows run one *shape* apiece up the whole ladder, and the tape assigns them:
Reactor (Bart), Conservator (Marge), Planner (Lisa), Pursuer (Burns). Those four are the four
cybernetic slots — Effector, Regulator, Modeler, Reviser — each person built around the drive that
leads.

## The twelve knobs — pole legend

Volume **up** is the first word; volume **down** is the second. 

| # | Knob | Up ↔ Down | What it is the volume *of* |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Going** | Live ↔ Dead | Vitality. How wound-up the animal runs — animation against depletion. Winds up, drains across the week. |
| 2 | **Effort** | Sprint ↔ Sloth | Energy tempo. Fast-and-hot-and-spent against slow-and-frugal-and-lasting. |
| 3 | **Explore** | Dance ↔ March | Foraging. Roaming for the new against working the proven groove. |
| 4 | **Action** | Busy ↔ Warm | Excitability at rest. Restless-and-reactive against settled-and-anchored. |
| 5 | **Orienting** | Think ↔ Buzz | Credit horizon. Looks-before-it-leaps and blames only what repeats, against acts-on-the-nearest-thing. |
| 6 | **Coping** | Beast ↔ Little | Mastery under challenge. Takes it head-on against yields-and-makes-itself-small. |
| 7 | **Attention** | Wide ↔ Deep | Experiencing. Scans-the-whole-room against tunnels-on-one-thing-at-full-resolution. |
| 8 | **Agreeable** | Respect ↔ Savage | Affiliation. Keeps-the-bond against takes-the-other-for-a-thing-that-does-something. |
| 9 | **Neurotic** | Cancel ↔ Clutch | Flaking. *Up = wobbles/aborts when it counts; down = commits and holds.* |
| 10 | **Conscientious** | Preppy ↔ Messy | Order. Tidy-planned-rule-kept against improvised-and-undone. |
| 11 | **Extraverted** | Boss ↔ Vibe | Representation. Drives-the-room against goes-with-the-flow. |
| 12 | **Honest** | Real ↔ Flex | Integrity. *Up = plays it straight and humble; down = bends the rule, works the angle.* |

# BRAMBLE — the key. The knobs made external.

Not a profile. Bramble doesn't *have* settings the way the humans do — he has the actual knobs, and the
household turns them, and which way they're turned changes across the book. His column is where the
reader learns to read all twelve, because on him each one is answerable to the last screw. The setting
below is the **default the body ships with** at the knob's arrival chapter — the pole the hardware
leans to before a hand has touched it.

| # | Knob | Arrives | Ships leaning | The drawable form |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Going** | Ch 1 | wind-up, drains to Dead | The crank. Wind it Live, it drains across the week to Dead. Mira solves mortality by buying a spare. |
| 2 | **Effort** | Ch 2 | Slow tank | Slow tank and fast tank; Loud setting (Sprint) darts-and-quits, has to sit and recover. |
| 3 | **Explore** | Ch 3 | Marches the worn groove | The board wears a route by use. March re-runs it; Loud setting (Dance) tries a new one. |
| 4 | **Action** | Ch 4 | settles Warm | Warmth and chill. Loud setting (Busy) sends it to wherever the action is. |
| 5 | **Orienting** | Ch 5 | short, Buzz | Blames whatever was nearest when the thing landed. Loud setting (Think) it won't charge a thing till it repeats. |
| 6 | **Coping** | Ch 6 | Little | Turned little and it hangs back. The loud setting (Beast) it runs the face a mile a minute, chases the cat. |
| 7 | **Attention** | Ch 7 | Deep | Deep would hold one thing and drop the rest. The loud setting (Wide) holds `REPLAYING [ CHASE MIRA ]` at two a.m. |
| 8 | **Agreeable** | Ch 8 | Savage | Savage is practical about others. The loud setting (Agreeable) is bonded to others. |
| 9 | **Neurotic** | Ch 9 | Clutch | The throat learning to talk. Cancel aborts the commitment; Clutch holds it. |
| 10 | **Conscientious** | Ch 10 | Messy | Messy lets it go. Loud setting (Preppy) flags the trash fifteen minutes late. |
| 11 | **Extraverted** | Ch 11 | Vibe | Voluntary drawing arrives. Vibe lets the involuntary readout show the truth. Loud setting (Boss) takes the whole screen and performs. |
| 12 | **Honest** | Ch 12 | Flex | Bramble speaks his mind. Flex yields reliable (biased) hallucinations. Loud setting (Real) yields unreliable (different each time) candor. |

**The joke and the thesis in one line:** he cannot reach his own knobs. Everyone in the household can
turn them; he can't. Ch 10 he learns to reach *around behind the earlier bodies* and set the vacuum's
and the tetrapod's — sets controls on every version of himself except the one he's wearing.

# BURNS — Pursuer (Reviser). *Goes toward. Keeps going toward. The brake it never grew.*

The Pursuer is the shape with nothing turning it off — worm to empire, the same errand at every rung,
only the stakes changing. Bart ran it last, with his father's face on the worm, and did the one thing
he'd never done in three years of these: *said something about someone,* flat, in the voice for a
tolerance come back out of spec. The tragedy of the card is that Burns is a genuinely warm man whose
Pursuer floor is the wanting with no brake — and the boy drew it for a joke in four lines. His loud
knobs are the reach; his spine is the two the slideshow exposes.

| # | Knob | Setting | Reading |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Going | **Live** | Knobs to 11 — overcommits the reach, chest-laughs a beat before the others, already mid-sentence. Maximum animation. |
| 2 | Effort | **Sprint** | Hot and generative. Can't let a new thing in without explaining it back out; the reach is the default and it spends. |
| 3 | Explore | **Dance**, high | Finds a whole continent of wanting. The eco-spirituality, the once-a-decade desert Confluence, the *isn't it strange that* that opens a frame. |
| 4 | Action | **Busy** | Leans forward when something matters, sometimes too far. Hands can't stay still during a pitch — when they stop, he's stopped thinking. |
| 5 | Orienting | **Buzz**, mid | The reach is impulsive — the hands are already at the thing before the slow head-turn arrives. Acts, then finds out. |
| 6 | Coping | **Beast** | Leads from the head of the table, confronts, overcommits the reach. Takes the room head-on. |
| 7 | Attention | **Wide** | Cosmic-scale, frame-opening. Laughs hardest at the joke that opens a frame, not the one that closes it. |
| 8 | Agreeable | **Respect** surface, **Savage** floor | Genuinely warm — married into staying, the chest-laugh, the gratitude — but the Pursuer floor means the wanting overrides the bond when they collide. The tragic bass note with Bart. |
| 9 | Neurotic | **Clutch** | Comes back from the Confluence *more himself* — gathered, not rewritten. Steady, not fragile. |
| 10 | Conscientious | **Messy**, quiet | Organizing via throwing stuff out. Field boots at the retrospective, clothes field-casual — but quietly good and old, a thirty-year oxford resoled not replaced. Disordered surface, real care under it. |
| 11 | Extraverted | **Boss**, high | The generative extravert. Leads from the end of the table, works the room, the reach as social default. |
| 12 | Honest | **Real** surface, **Flex** floor | Sincere, materialist, no soul-doctrine — but the Pursuer's own knob, and the Ch 12 point: *the brake's the one part it never grew.* The uninstrumented wanting the boy drew, boxer shorts and all. |

# JASMINE — no cartoon. The body that knows.

Not one of Bart's four — she's the counterweight to the four theorizing, the one who works with her
hands inside the bottom-up process while the others argue about the inside. Her voice is self-anaphora,
laying a plain thing down a beat at a time until the plainness is the weight, and her one recurring move
is to name the appearance and refuse the inside in the same breath. The single most striking thing about
her tuning: **her Going knob is the one human dial that visibly winds and drains like Bramble's crank** —
the depleted arrival, in from a 2 a.m. labor, quietly truer than the fresh academics and read only as
"tired." Nobody ever names the rhyme.

| # | Knob | Setting | Reading |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Going | **Live** | Energy varies by whether she's in from a birth or a nap after one. Truer when it's low. The one human whose knob-1 reads like Bramble's. |
| 2 | Effort | **Sloth**, high | Endurance — thirty-six-hour stretches, the midwife's sustained frugal output. |
| 3 | Explore | mid, tilt **Dance** | Eclectic belief — moved by the Chopra/Oprah register, reincarnation-curious, committed to none. Roams belief without settling anywhere. |
| 4 | Action | **Warm** | Knows how to be quiet in a room. Her home-spot is a person — she orients toward Mira, the way Mira lands on whoever's nearest. |
| 5 | Orienting | mid | Folk-empirical — sometimes ahead of the literature, sometimes behind, and not always sure which. Works off the body's memory, not a rule. |
| 6 | Coping | **Beast** | Takes up the whole room when she needs to; hands inside a crisis while the rest of her catches up. Confronts a body head-on. |
| 7 | Attention | **Deep** | Hands are the whole of her when she works. Embodied, one-thing focus — the forearms stronger than the soft sweaters let on. |
| 8 | Agreeable | **Respect**, high | Warm and full-bodied, laughs at her own kid and her own mistakes. The room's easiest warmth. |
| 9 | Neurotic | **Clutch** | Talks through the exhaustion, steady across the long nights. The depletion never becomes wobble. |
| 10 | Conscientious | tilt **Messy** | Never took records-removal seriously enough to bother — technically still on the rolls out of pure indifference. Plain, not tidy-driven. |
| 11 | Extraverted | mid, tilt **Vibe** | Quiet in a room until she needs the whole of it. The self-anaphora accumulates rather than commands. |
| 12 | Honest | **Real**, high | *It looks like somebody deciding; I know it isn't; it looks like it.* The refusal to pretend the inside is there — the plainest voice doing the most work. |

# MIRA — young John Steinbeck.

The narrator types her like every other specimen — the whole card below is what the naturalist's watching
already sees. What's true and narrower: she doesn't run the framework the adults run, which is exactly why
she says the thing the theory is dancing around. The young-Steinbeck read is a **deliberate watcher in an
impulsive small body.** The eye is already a naturalist's — she lies on the floor in front of Bramble and doesn't
move for fifteen minutes, works out that *asleep* ends at the crank and *stopped* might not, thinks about
it a day, and solves mortality the sensible way. The gaze is old; the body is five. And she's the one
knob-set that **moves across the book** — she's the only human who grows, so half these settings are
winding as you read them.

| # | Knob | Setting | Reading |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Going | **Live** | Full-body and uncontainable at four; by seven, a kid who's noticed laughter can be a choice. Vitality maxed, learning the volume. |
| 2 | Effort | **Sprint** → learning control | Total-body scramble, moving before the thought that would've stopped her finishes. Matures toward a held burst by the end. |
| 3 | Explore | **Dance**, high | Forages literally — into cupboards, into pockets, into whatever's not locked. The young-Steinbeck rove. |
| 4 | Action | **Busy** early | The most impulsive body in the house at five; can't sit in a chair the way a chair wants sitting in. Warms only under the watch. |
| 5 | Orienting | **split**, climbing to Think | The signature. No inhibition in the body at five (Buzz) — but the fifteen-minute watch and the day spent on mortality are pure Think. Impulsive limbs, deliberate eye. |
| 6 | Coping | **Beast** | Solves death by acquiring a spare — doesn't retreat from the hard thing, takes it head-on the sensible way. |
| 7 | Attention | **Deep**, high — *the Steinbeck knob* | Lies still and watches at length; at seven, cross-legged, hands in her lap, doing the same. The long deep watch is her whole instrument. |
| 8 | Agreeable | **Respect**, with a **Savage** truth-streak | Warm — but says the thing the theory is dancing around. Unfiltered enough to blurt the true, blunt, simple thing the adults are avoiding. |
| 9 | Neurotic | **Clutch** | Kid-steady and stubborn — the too-small frog boots she'll never admit are too small; the argument about the pajamas nobody's won. |
| 10 | Conscientious | **Messy**, high — *climbing* | Destroys the hair by ten a.m., scabbed knees, one Velcro strap always soft from never quite being fastened. First order-mark is the Ch 10 sticker-lettering callus. |
| 11 | Extraverted | **Vibe → Boss** — winds across the book | Speaks rarely early, and the room shifts when she does. By Ch 11 she deputizes herself, schedule-enforcer's helper — the one knob that visibly winds up. |
| 12 | Honest | **Real**, maxed | The truth-pole of the whole book. Children unfiltered by theory say the thing the theory dances around; she hasn't learned to Flex yet, and the book needs her never to. |

# CHERYL — Anthony Bourdain as a lesbian. The narrator; in-room only at Ch 12.

The instrument is aimed everywhere but herself for four hundred pages, then turns around. The
Bourdain-lesbian read: restless appetite, sensory-maximalist, allergic to sentiment and to bullshit,
generous to the overlooked and the working, wit worn as armor over a cool commanding surface over a hot
self-lacerating core — and, per the card's sharpest line, **a loud woman who trained herself silent.**
Not reticence; a built-for-volume voice held down on purpose. **Two knobs carry the whole book, and both
are hers:** the *Attention* split, which is the spine, and *Honest*, aimed inward, which is the
confession.

| # | Knob | Setting | Reading |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Going | **Live** | High animation and appetite up top, the depletion running underneath the performance — the wind-up you can hear starting to unwind. |
| 2 | Effort | **Sprint** | Hot, fast, spent. The appetite reaches before it checks. |
| 3 | Explore | **Dance**, maxed | The rover — has to taste everything, the sensory-maximalist, the outsider who keeps moving. The Bourdain knob. |
| 4 | Action | **Busy** | Never settles. The observer's restlessness — in every room in the book and at home in none until the last one. |
| 5 | Orienting | **split** — Buzz over Think | Appetite wants it now (Buzz), then reconstructs three years off the tape with forensic patience (Think). The narrator device is a Think act laid over a Buzz temperament. |
| 6 | Coping | **Beast**, high | Unflinching, cold and total, spares no one — including herself. The gaze that doesn't look away. |
| 7 | Attention | **Wide on everyone, Deep on Marge** — *the spine* | The whole book is this one knob split. Wide, total, ordinary naturalist attention on every body — and Deep, the useless details with zero forensic function, on exactly one. The asymmetry *is* what the reader notices, and eventually what she names. |
| 8 | Agreeable | **Savage** voice, **Respect** heart | Cruel-tongued, bullshit-allergic — and generous to the overlooked, and the whole withheld tenderness pointed at the one woman she can't render enough of. Brutal on the surface, soft on the unloved. |
| 9 | Neurotic | performs **Clutch**, hides **Cancel** | Total control in the room — the woman built for volume, describing herself at last with complete command. The Cancel is the inhibition under it: the loud one who trained herself silent. |
| 10 | Conscientious | **Preppy** surface, **Messy** floor | Well-dressed, expensive jewelry, hair and nails done regularly — a curated, maintained surface over an appetite that keeps no rules. |
| 11 | Extraverted | **Boss**, held down | Naturally loud-mouthed, commands the room, the narrator's whole authority — turned up, and kept down by a hand on the knob. The card's own pairing: not a quiet woman, a loud one trained silent. |

## Two readings to carry off this sheet

**The four shapes are the four slots, and they sit at one table.** Reactor / Conservator / Planner /
Pursuer are Effector / Regulator / Modeler / Reviser — Bart, Marge, Lisa, Burns — and Bart's quarterly
slideshows are the book teaching the reader to read the very knobs this sheet tabulates. When he runs a
shape up the ladder, he's running one *column* of this grid, worm to empire.

**Cheryl's Attention knob is the book.** Everything the Body Sheet says about Marge drawing more of the
gaze than anyone else is, in this instrument, a single setting: Wide on all, Deep on one. If the two
sheets ever disagree, that's the line they have to agree on.
