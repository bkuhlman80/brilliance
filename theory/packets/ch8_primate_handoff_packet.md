## Behavior cluster

- **Dominance is a separate personality factor in every studied non-human primate but folds into Extraversion in humans — an unexplained anomaly placing a primate-specific dimension just below the human boundary** *(King & Figueredo, 1997; Weiss et al., 2015)*. A trait that almost-but-not-quite maps across the species line. *[segments: tendencies, motivations/signatures, capacities: Standing]*
- **Chimp AVPR1A microsatellite length predicts affiliation and social-bond strength — the same gene tied to vole pair-bonding, here in a species that doesn't pair-bond** *(Hopkins et al., 2012)*. A molecular handle on Affiliation. *[segments: motivations/signatures (Affiliation), Sociometer]*
- **Réale's five dimensions of animal temperament** *(Réale et al., 2007; pace-of-life syndrome framework)*. The standard axes for animal personality: **Boldness** (reaction to risky but familiar situations — predators, danger), **Exploration** (reaction to novel objects and environments), **Activity** (general level of movement), **Aggressiveness** (agonistic response to conspecifics), and **Sociability** (non-aggressive tendency to associate with conspecifics). A cross-clade temperament frame, not Primate-unique: the framework rides Boldness/Exploration/Activity/Aggressiveness on the Vertebrate Behavioral Syndrome (Koolhaas), and sources Sociability from Experiencing Style (Modeler/Autonomy) from Mammal on. *[segments: tendencies, motivations/signatures, behavior]*
- **Primate cognition and attention (Tomasello)** *(Hare, Call & Tomasello, 2000/2001)*. Great apes read others as goal-directed agents and track what a competitor can and cannot see — a subordinate chimp goes for food the dominant can't see, adjusting to the rival's line of sight. Perspective-taking and attention-reading as the working core of Mentalizing; it feeds Tactical Deceit directly. Boundary marker: the richer *shared* intentionality and joint attention behind human cooperation are a later, symbolic-line development. *[segments: breakthrough, capacities: Tactical Deceit, behavior]*

## Breakthrough material cluster (Mentalizing)

- **Substrate: granular PFC (dorsolateral) plus an expanded temporoparietal junction, together enabling models of other minds with distinct intentions, beliefs, and goals** *(clade_reference)*. The hardware story. *[segments: breakthrough, clade card]*
- **Mentalizing is the first nervous system that can hold a model of another agent's model — theory of mind in strong form, including false-belief representation** *(clade_reference)*. The breakthrough in one line; grounds Tactical Deceit and Standing. *[segments: breakthrough]*
- **False-belief tasks are the purest behavioral signature; great apes pass under some conditions, standard-condition performance contested** *([INFERRED]; not from Smithers)*. The cleanest behavioral test. *[segments: breakthrough, behavior]*
- **The breakthroughs are a cumulative stack, not a replacement series: Attention → Reinforcing → Simulating → Mentalizing, each conserving the prior and adding one operation** *(clade_reference; Bennett)*. Relay-as-stack framing for Lisa's line-complete beat. *[segments: breakthrough, Trellis]*
- **Social Brain Hypothesis: primate brain expansion driven by social complexity, not foraging or tools; troop-size pressure as the engine of mentalization** *(Barton & Dunbar)*. Why brains grew — sociality. *[segments: breakthrough, deep-time]*
- **"Big brains for big social groups" is shakier than it sounds** *(DeCasien et al., 2017 — MODERATE, APA reconstructed)*. The popular story says primates grew big brains to keep track of big social groups. But re-run carefully, the link barely holds — the math could "predict" a group size anywhere from about 2 to 520, which is hardly predicting at all — and what a primate *eats* predicts brain size just as well as how social it is. Honest version: sociality is part of the story, not all of it. *[segments: breakthrough; epistemic-status beat]*

## Capacities cluster

- **Triadic Awareness (Reactor): the Ngogo chimpanzee fission traced to one public submission grunt** *(Sandel, 2026, AAAS podcast transcript)*. The morning the rising male first received submission from the deposed alpha was the day the western chimps fled, beginning a polarization that produced lethal raids three years later. A single public, mutually-witnessed signal flips a coalition: Mentalizing lets a chimp register not just "the alpha changed" but that the others present saw it change too, and the troop restructures around that. *[segments: capacities: Triadic Awareness, breakthrough]*
- **Tactical Deceit (Pursuer): the tactical-deception record — deceptive maneuvers that depend on modeling what another individual does and doesn't know** *(Byrne & Whiten)*. The Pursuer capacity, unique to Primate. *[segments: capacities: Tactical Deceit]*
- **Standing (Planner): proto-reputation as social-comparison tracking in monkeys and apes — precursor to the gossip-mediated reputation systems of Band-Human** *(clade_reference)*. Reputation before language. *[segments: capacities: Standing]*
- **Autonomous Cognition (Conservator), captive: two chimp groups trained on different equally-good puzzle-box methods each kept its own; shown a competent chimp using the alternative, they largely declined to revise** *(Whiten et al., 2005)*. Mature cognition resisting outside revision. *[segments: capacities: Autonomous Cognition]*
- **Autonomous Cognition (Conservator), field: nut-cracking, ant-fishing, and grooming-posture traditions vary by community and persist across generations without crossing boundaries** *(Whiten et al., 1999)*. The Conservator prior stabilized by the whole troop — harder to dislodge because socially mirrored, not just individually held. *[segments: capacities: Autonomous Cognition]*

## Sociality and the Sociometer cluster *(clade-specific)*

- **The Sociometer, three findings in one gauge: a fundamental drive to form and keep bonds; self-esteem as an internal monitor of relational value that drops when inclusion is threatened; exclusion recruiting affective machinery so that falling value registers as aversive** *(Baumeister & Leary, 1995; Leary et al., 1995 / Leary & Baumeister, 2000; Eisenberger et al., 2003 — substrate pending-Smithers)*. The Operator signature; reputation-tracking as the readout of the belonging drive. *[segments: motivations/signatures (Sociometer), constitutive-risk premium]*
- **What Primate adds to inherited mammalian attachment affect is scaling — dyad to coalition — through mentalization** *(clade_reference, Sociometer kinship)*. Why belonging goes group-sized here. *[segments: Sociometer, breakthrough]*
- **The strong shared-neural-substrate reading of social pain is contested; the robust finding is simply that exclusion is aversive** *(clade_reference — pending-Smithers)*. The careful version of "rejection hurts" — and the spine of the constitutive-risk-premium beat. *[segments: constitutive-risk premium; epistemic-status beat]*
