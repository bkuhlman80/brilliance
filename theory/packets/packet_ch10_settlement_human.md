### Clade cards, breakthrough, capacities (Bart + Burns)

- **MANDATORY — clade card.** Settlement-Human: the constituent human parameterized by sedentary village life. A fixed-location community whose cohesion rests on accumulation, storage, and recurring structured obligation, rather than the band's mobile reciprocal exchange. The defining mechanism is agriculture — sedentary food production that permits population density, surplus storage, and permanent dwellings. The collective the human runs on is the settlement as a norm-generating collective. *(Ethological: the form and what it is for, not the robot.)*

- **MANDATORY — breakthrough: Common Knowledge** (Pinker / Lewis / Schiffer; **not Bennett**). Synchronized cross-stranger mutual awareness: each party knows that the others know, and knows that they know that they know. Speaking (Band-Human) enabled face-to-face coordination within a band; settlement architecture introduces durable public focal points — town squares, monuments, marketplaces — that scale common knowledge across strangers. Norms presuppose common knowledge (Bicchieri); the cognitive achievement is more fundamental than the normative one, so the breakthrough position names it. It is the multi-agent "across" counterpart to Band-Human's inward Self-Reference loop, and it is **not** a strange loop ("strange loop" stays reserved for Self-Reference).

- **MANDATORY — the four capacities** *(named here; illustrative examples in Section D):*
  - **Reactor → Farming.** Subsistence agriculture, the daily practical action of settlement food production. Commitment × Stability × Affiliation, without modeling (Autonomy absent).
  - **Conservator → Norms.** Stored regulatory references followed without individual revision — Bicchieri-style conditional preferences for compliance, given the expectation that others comply. Commitment × Stability × Autonomy, without Reviser-side update (Affiliation absent). *(Norms is the Conservator capacity, one slot out from the Common Knowledge breakthrough.)*
  - **Planner → Wealth.** Strategic deliberation about durable resources, accumulation, and intergenerational transfer. Stability × Affiliation × Autonomy (Commitment absent). Settlement-level surplus storage is what makes Wealth structurally possible.
  - **Pursuer → Alloparenting** (carried from Band-Human). Cooperative childcare, elaborated into extended-family caregiving and communal child-rearing at settlement scale. Commitment × Affiliation × Autonomy (Stability absent).

- **MANDATORY — Burns's deep-time context** *(framework context; empirical menu in Section D):* the Neolithic transition, from the end of the Younger Dryas (~11.7 ka) through the first cities (~5.5 ka). Holocene climatic stability is the enabling pressure — a warm, remarkably stable climate that makes predictable agriculture possible for the first time. Agriculture arises in multiple independent centers (Fertile Crescent, Yangtze and Yellow River, Mesoamerica, the Andes, New Guinea, and others). Göbekli Tepe (~11 ka) shows monumental architecture *preceding* farming, a challenge to settled-life-causes-monuments framings. Anchor sites: Göbekli Tepe, Jericho (~10 ka), Çatalhöyük (~9 ka).

### Environment and tendencies (Lisa + Burns)

- **MANDATORY — environmental specifics.** Holocene climatic stability following the Younger Dryas cold snap; a warm, stable climate enabling predictable agriculture. Rivers and floodplains as agricultural cores (Tigris-Euphrates, Nile, Indus, Yellow, Yangtze). The Fertile Crescent's mix of high-quality domesticable plants and animals gives it an early lead. The reinforcing cycle: agriculture produces surplus; surplus enables labor specialization; specialization produces the recording technologies (writing, law, calendars); recording enables larger-scale coordination; larger scale requires more stability. *(Empirical menu in Section D.)*

- **MANDATORY — tendencies in functional language.** Norm-following behavior: calendar-bound task execution; following stored norms without renegotiating them each time; sedentary labor coordination; intergenerational wealth accumulation. These are what falls out of the architecture once stored regulation arrives in the symbolic register.
