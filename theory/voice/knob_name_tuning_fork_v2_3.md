*The scientific term carries the page; the bench word is seasoning; the kid's word is a symbolic-line event held in dialogue. First devotion is to the reader, not the characters.*

## The ranking — read first

A knob can be named three ways, and the names are **not co-equal**. They are ranked, and the ranking is the whole instrument:

1. **The scientific term is the default.** The Big Five names and their construct kin — *Agreeableness, Conscientiousness, Extraversion, Neuroticism, Coping Style.* This carries the page, owns the narrator's prose, and owns every callback. It is what the reader navigates by, because it is precise *and* recognizable *and* survives distance. Most appearances of a knob, in most registers, are this word. Across the genetic line the scientific tier *is* the plain functional word — *Going, Breathing, Foraging, Home.* Plainness here is the tier's whole point, not a demotion from it: these stand beside *Extraversion* and *Conscientiousness* by right, and none is ever handled as one of Bart's bench words.
2. **Bart's bench word is occasional seasoning** — *give-way, the keeping knob, the room knob.* Tolerated because the characters are flamboyant and headstrong, never because the reader needs it. It earns a few spots a chapter (Move 1, Move 2) and nowhere else. A flourish on top of the default term, never a replacement for it.
3. **The kid's word does not exist before the symbolic line, and after it, it lives in dialogue only.** Pre-symbolic there is no third term at all; in the symbolic line the Bramble-and-Mira co-naming game runs as a dramatized event in the characters' mouths, and it never touches the narrator's prose or the callbacks. (Move 3.)

Reader first means the clear term is the default and the vivid terms are the indulgence. Never let the bench word run as the page's default; that inversion — the opaque term load-bearing, the clear term demoted to a footnote — is the confusion this fork exists to kill.

## The donor is Ch 1, read correctly

The Trellis scene is the model, and its lesson is *reader-first word selection,* not "pick the plainest word." When Marge asks *can we call them tendencies* and Lisa feels the relief of dropping "dynamical attractor," the team chooses the word that best serves the reader over the in-group word. For the personality dimensions that word **is the scientific term** — precise and navigable at once. The bench nickname is the in-group word to be subordinated, exactly as "dynamical attractor" was.

The card-boxing ritual is the diegetic apparatus, and what gets boxed is **the scientific term.** No sidebar, no narrator gloss, no textbook box. A character catches the real word with a pen; that is the whole machine. Donor of the apparatus, Ch 1 is also its first user: *Going* gets caught and boxed here like every word the team settles. Being the source of the machine is no excuse to run it on every knob but knob-1's.

## Move 1 — anchor on the scientific term; coin the bench word once beside it, then let it yield

At a knob's first appearance the scientific term lands and is the thing that persists. Bart coins his bench word *next to it,* once, in the act of preferring his own — the refusal characterizes him and costs the reader nothing, because the real word is right there and is the one that stays.

The name names the *dimension* the knob governs across its line, not the sophistication of this quarter's mechanism. A first implementation that is a single binary crank, a bare switch, a dial with no positions yet takes the name at first appearance all the same. Knob-1 is the case most easily got wrong: the protocell knob is a wind-up crank, go or no-go, and *Going* is its scientific name precisely because go/no-go is the dimension it governs. *Mira's the knob* and *not a dial yet* are true of the mechanism and change nothing about the name. Pre-dial is no exemption — the default lands in the knob's own chapter, the first one included.

> *Illustration only — Voice's to discard.* *Marge stamps it Agreeableness, and she's right. On the bench I call it give-way, because that's the whole of what it does — when its want and the kid's point two ways, the dial sets whether the body gives or holds.*

The scientific term is named and honored; the bench word gets its single coining; the page goes forward on **Agreeableness.**

**The designated site.** A chapter offers two candidate first-appearances, and the book will install the full coining in both if left alone. Split the job; do not duplicate it:

- **The build log owns the bench word and the *why*.** Bart's filed log opens the chapter; it is the home for *give-way, because that's the whole of what it does,* naming the scientific term **once**, as *Marge's word, and a fine one.* The current build logs already run this formula (Ch 8, Ch 10, Ch 11) — so the work is to stop it recurring *after,* not to add it.
- **The framework / card scene owns the scientific term's official entry.** When Lisa or Marge boxes the term, that is where the scientific word enters the record. Echo the plain phrase if useful, but do not re-weld the *X-is-Marge's-word-and-Bart-calls-it-Y* pair a second time. The build log already did it.

One full coining, split clean across its two sites. Nothing between them re-welds.

## Move 2 — the page runs on the scientific term; the bench word returns only where the voice earns it

After the anchor, the scientific term carries everything by default. The bench word comes back in only here:

- **In Bart's own mouth, demonstrating live mechanism** — a quality-control demo where the physical action is the point (*give-way set, it yields the warm; take set, it holds the spot*). The bench word names the motion he is performing in real time.
- **As an occasional flourish in his dialogue** where the scientific term would sound wrong coming from him — a handful of times a chapter, never the running default.

Everywhere else the page runs textbook: the narrator's prose, Marge's mouth, the household sections that are not a live Bart demo, and **all callbacks.** A reference back to a knob from an earlier chapter is always the scientific term — the one the reader met as the real thing and can carry across distance.

Hold the ceiling. Where a bench word is running as the default verb across a stretch — the symbolic-line reflections lean on *keeping* where *Conscientiousness* should carry — thin it to the scientific term and leave a flourish or two where Bart is most himself.

## Move 3 — the kid's word: the pronoun meter, and the symbolic divide

The book already runs this meter for pronouns. **The narrator holds *it*, always; Mira's *he* is hers — earned by the relationship, absent at first contact.** Knob-names take the same meter on a second axis: the narrator's prose and every callback hold the scientific term; the kid's word is the relational one, the children's, and it never climbs into the narrator's register. The reader navigates by the prose-level constant no matter what the children invent above it.

**Before the symbolic line (Ch 1–9): there is no kid knob-name.** Mira may read a knob in folk terms in the moment she meets it (*he's being careful*), but no such reading hardens into a recurring name and no callback ever rides one. The naming is two-name throughout — scientific default, bench seasoning.

*pushy-and-hangs-back* is the one place a pre-symbolic kid-name has already hardened (coined in Ch 6, callback-quoted in Ch 8). Convert it. The knob is **Coping Style**; its poles are **proactive** and **reactive**; that vocabulary is the default that carries its first appearance and its Ch 8 callback. Mira's "pushy / hangs-back" survives only as a one-time in-scene reading at first contact, never as a term used again.

**In the symbolic line (Ch 10–12): the Bramble-and-Mira co-naming game runs as a dramatized event.** Once Bramble has language it draws and coins, and the two of them work names out together off-camera and hand them up ratified — *the children,* plural, with the lab's silence for consent (*Boss* turned up, *Vibe* turned down; *we're not keeping the room dial*). This is character, part of the twins' coming-of-age, and it overrules Bart's bench word **in the characters' mouths.** Coin it once and dramatize it; it is a scene, not a running label.

**The game stays in dialogue.** The kid-word never becomes the narrator's term or the callback term. The prose says *Extraversion*; the children say *Boss.* Dialogue holds the game; prose and callbacks hold the anchor. This is why the chapter with the most nicknames stays the most anchored — the anchor lives in a register the children do not touch, exactly as *it* survives every *he* Mira spends.

## Move 4 — Cheryl is reserved for the character-gloss, never the term-gloss

The narrator never defines a framework word. Her barge-in is the Ch 3 line-376 register: she stops the tape to notice the *person* — that Bart's contempt for the textbook word is itself a theory he doesn't know he holds; that the word he won't say is the one that, in the things with someone home, does the most of the loving. Spent on definitions she becomes a glossary and flattens every idiolect into her diction. Hold her at the gap. The table does the teaching.

---

## The law, in one line

The scientific term is the default and owns the prose and the callbacks; Bart's bench word is a flourish his voice earns a few times a chapter; the kid's word does not exist before the symbolic line and lives only in the children's dialogue after it; the narrator holds the anchor the way she holds *it*, and stays at the gap. First devotion is to the reader.
