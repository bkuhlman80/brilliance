## Clade block

### Clade cards, breakthrough, capacities (Bart + Burns)

- **MANDATORY — what the cell is.** A City-Human's personality constellation is parameterized by urban life: a dense, occupationally differentiated settlement whose cohesion rests on legal codes, market exchange, and institutional representation rather than face-to-face normative pressure. The city forms as a symbiosis of settlements and trade networks: settlements supply population, surplus, and the agricultural base; trade networks supply the connectivity that lets the city aggregate goods, people, and information past any single settlement. The city is the merger product, and this is the first symbiosis cell on the symbolic line. The merchant emerges as a new constituent-human type alongside the band-forager and the settled farmer: a symbolic-line nomad whose life-form runs along trade routes between cities.
- **MANDATORY — the breakthrough: Codes.** Written, portable, enforceable rule-sets that coordinate strangers who share no face-to-face norm-maintenance history. No Bennett attribution: the word "breakthrough" follows Bennett, the capability Codes does not. Anchored to Goody and Scott: Goody supplies the administrative reading (writing scales administration, law, and accounting past oral memory); Scott supplies the legibility reading (a state governs at scale by making its population legible through standardized records, and writing originates as fiscal-accounting technology rather than literature). [Goody/Scott pending verification.] The reinforcing cycle: records enable taxation, taxation funds specialists, specialists produce more records. Archaeological anchor: the Urban Revolution, Uruk and Mohenjo-daro; the first writing is accounting, not poetry (examples in Section D).
- **MANDATORY — the four capacities** (`Inheritance_Lines` names them; fuller examples in Section D).
  - *Distributing (Planner).* Strategic deliberation about how surplus moves through a network of strangers. City example: Rome's annona grain distribution.
  - *Crime (Pursuer).* Goal-pursuit without rule-enforced inhibition, made viable because the city created the structural conditions (strangers, surplus, gaps in oversight) under which code-violation pays. **LOAD-BEARING — crime-is-code-violation.** Crime is the negation of Stability by structural definition (`Inheritance_Lines` line 245), and Stability is exactly the order Codes institutionalize. So the breakthrough installs the very thing one of the cell's own capacities is defined by violating; there is no organized crime in a forager band because there is no code yet to break. This is the clade-side anchor for the Bramble-block written-law beat.
  - *Farming (Reactor).* Accumulate, store, manage, now at categorically larger scale, feeding strangers through irrigation and distribution networks the farmer never sees.
  - *Standardizing (Conservator).* Slow maintenance of shared references (the silver shekel, the cubit, standardized brick); a city of strangers cannot trade at scale without them.
- **MANDATORY — Burns's deep-time context** (`burns_deep_time`; examples in Section D). From the Uruk period and the first cities (~3500 BCE) to the first large multi-region empires at Akkad (~2300 BCE); Bronze Age into Iron Age. Mesopotamia first (Uruk), then Egypt, the Indus, China, Mesoamerica, the Andes, and the Aegean. Cuneiform from ~3200 BCE. The signature evidentiary signal is monumental urban architecture, writing systems, social stratification visible in differentiated burials, and mass-produced standardized pottery such as the Uruk bevel-rim bowl.

### Environment and tendencies (Lisa + Burns)

- **MANDATORY — environmental specifics.** Floodplain civilizations on the Tigris-Euphrates, Nile, Indus, and Yellow rivers, with secondary cities strung along trade routes and the Mediterranean as a connecting inland sea. Climate is mid-to-late Holocene, cooler and drier than the early-Holocene optimum; the 4.2 ka drought event coincides with the Akkadian collapse.
