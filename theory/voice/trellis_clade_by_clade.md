# Trellis Framework — Clade by Clade


---


# Genetic line


## 1. Protocell  

**Line:** genetic · **Date:** ~4 BYA · **Function introduced:** Effector  
**Era:** Hadean-Archean boundary (Eoarchean) · **Coinciding event:** End of Late Heavy Bombardment; oceans stabilize


**Overview.** The first things that count as alive — not cells full of machinery, but bare bundles of self-copying chemistry. A protocell is little more than a fatty bubble holding strands of RNA that copy themselves. There are no proteins-as-machines yet: the molecule that carries the information is also the molecule that does the work.


**Virtuous cycle.** A strand that copies itself a little faster or a little more accurately leaves more copies, and the better copiers crowd out the rest — natural selection switching on for the very first time.


### Motivations

_The four base drives — one per cybernetic function._


- **Persistence** (Motivation · Effector) — Persistence is the drive to hold a pattern in existence against everything that breaks it down, the genetic line's founding push and the one new at this cell. It is what selection rewards: a strand that copies and lasts wins, one that does not is gone.
  
  _Cite:_ [Darwin](https://search.worldcat.org/title/7897536)

- **Dissipation** (Motivation · Regulator) — Dissipation is the coupling to an outside energy gradient that keeps a system far from equilibrium, the original way of staying alive without running down. It was present before life, not invented here. At alkaline vents, a natural acidity difference across mineral walls drove the first proton-powered chemistry.
  
  _Cite:_ [Lane & Martin](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cell.2012.11.050)

- **Self-Organization** (Motivation · Modeler) — Self-organization is the tendency of matter to fall into ordered patterns on its own, with no template directing it, groundwork life inherited rather than invented. Fatty acids dropped in water self-assemble into hollow spheres, the same bubble shape every cell membrane still uses, built by chemistry alone.
  
  _Cite:_ [Hanczyc, Fujikawa & Szostak](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.1089904)

- **Self-Production** (Motivation · Reviser) — Self-production is a system making and remaking its own parts, closing the loop so the thing produces the very components that produce it. The idea, called autopoiesis, is groundwork present from the start. A living bubble whose chemistry builds the membrane that holds that chemistry is doing it.
  
  _Cite:_ [Maturana & Varela](https://search.worldcat.org/title/5726379)


### Tendencies

_Adjacent-function blends (each pairs two neighbouring drives)._


- **Throughput Type** (Tendency · Effector × Regulator) — Throughput type is how fast a system runs energy and matter through itself, fast and hot or slow and frugal, pairing the drive to act with the drive to stabilize. A protocell's rate is set from outside: the gradient and temperature at a vent fix how quickly its chemistry turns over.

- **Aging Type** (Tendency · Regulator × Modeler) — Aging type is how a system accumulates damage and wears down, pairing the drive to stabilize with the drive to model. A bare protocell has no repair crew: strands break and membranes leak until it divides or degrades. Aging here is just unrepaired chemistry.

- **Enclosure Type** (Tendency · Modeler × Reviser) — Enclosure type is what holds the system together as a unit, pairing the drive to model with the drive to revise. Early protocells had a choice of walls: lipid bubbles or pores in vent minerals. Rainwater can even grow a tough skin on RNA droplets, slowing leakage from minutes to days.
  
  _Cite:_ [Agrawal et al.](https://doi.org/10.1126/sciadv.adn9657)

- **Acquisition Type** (Tendency · Reviser × Effector) — Acquisition type is how a system gets the materials it needs, pairing the drive to revise with the drive to act, the protocell's founding Effector knob. It takes up building blocks from its surroundings, fatty acids and RNA letters from vent chemistry, some of it delivered ready-made from space.
  
  _Cite:_ [Glavin et al.](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41550-024-02472-9)


### Signatures

_Cross-function pairings that mark the clade._


- **Lineage** (Signature · Effector × Modeler) — Lineage is this level's defining coupling on the vertical: not the single bubble but the unbroken line of copies it throws forward. It links the drive to act with the drive to model. What survives is never one protocell but the line, the pattern carried copy to copy down to every cell alive.
  
  _Cite:_ [Moody et al.](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41559-024-02461-1)

- _Signature · Regulator × Reviser — not present at this clade._


### Capacities

_Defined by the one function that is *absent*._


- **Operational Closure** (Capacity · without Effector) — Set the action drive aside and the other three close on themselves: a unit that makes its own parts, sets its own internal rules, and takes no program from outside. A fatty-acid vesicle grows by absorbing more fatty acid, divides under stress, and copies whatever is inside, with nothing directing the steps.
  
  _Cite:_ [Maturana & Varela](https://search.worldcat.org/title/5726379)

- **Precision Machinery** (Capacity · without Regulator) — Drop the stabilizer and the other three give precision machinery: molecules that copy and build with high accuracy. At this level it is the bare ribozyme. QT45 folds precisely enough to copy itself and its mirror image, accurately enough for selection to act on, with no enzymes wrapped around it yet.
  
  _Cite:_ [Gianni, Kwok et al.](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.adt2760)

- **Phenotypic Plasticity** (Capacity · without Modeler) — With no inner model, a protocell still behaves differently in different conditions: warm it and replication speeds up, cool it and strands re-pair. The same protocell in two settings is two functional things. The TPR ribozyme depends on this, replicating open-endedly only under pH swings and freeze-thaw cycling.
  
  _Cite:_ [Attwater et al.](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41557-025-01830-y)

- **Reproduction** (Capacity · without Reviser) — With revision switched off, the unit runs on what already works and copies it faithfully. Every living thing makes energy the same way: protons flowing across a membrane drive a tiny rotor that builds fuel. Life inherited it from the natural acid gradient at alkaline vents and has never replaced it in four billion years.
  
  _Cite:_ [Lane & Martin](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cell.2012.11.050)


### Breakthrough

_The framework-center concept unlocked at this rung._


- **Natural Selection** (Breakthrough (framework center)) — Natural selection is the engine under all of evolution: whatever copies itself faster or more accurately leaves more copies, and the better copiers crowd out the rest. It switches on the moment chemistry can replicate with errors. QT45, a 45-letter RNA selected in a test tube, copies itself and its mirror strand, crossing that threshold on its own.
  
  _Cite:_ [Gianni, Kwok et al.](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.adt2760)

## 2. Prokaryote  

**Line:** genetic · **Date:** ~3.5 BYA · **Function introduced:** Regulator  
**Era:** Paleoarchean (Archean Eon) · **Coinciding event:** First stromatolite-building microbial mats


**Overview.** Bacteria and their cousins the archaea: single cells with no nucleus. They were the only life on Earth for well over a billion years and are still the most numerous organisms today. The DNA floats loose inside, and tiny machines called ribosomes read it to build proteins.


**Virtuous cycle.** One set of genes can run many programs depending on conditions, so a cell that switches to the right program at the right moment out-survives one that can't — and sharper sensing pays for sharper switching.


### Motivations

_The four base drives — one per cybernetic function._


- **Persistence** (Motivation · Effector) — Persistence is the founding drive to keep a pattern going, carried here from the Protocell. A bacterium expresses it by dividing fast and copying faithfully: keep the line going, flood the world with copies, let selection sort them.
  
  _Cite:_ [Darwin](https://search.worldcat.org/title/7897536)

- **Coordination** (Motivation · Regulator) — Coordination is the drive to track and respond to other cells, not just the physical world, the new motivation here. Bacteria release molecules their neighbors sense, a chemical headcount called quorum sensing: when the crowd is big enough, the signal flips the whole population into new behavior at once.
  
  _Cite:_ [Waters & Bassler](https://doi.org/10.1146/annurev.cellbio.21.012704.131001)

- **Self-Organization** (Motivation · Modeler) — Self-organization is matter falling into ordered patterns with no template, groundwork present from the beginning and still running. Bacteria organize into structured biofilms, and microbial mats layer themselves into stromatolites, order built from the bottom up.

- **Self-Production** (Motivation · Reviser) — Self-production is a system making its own parts and closing the loop, the idea called autopoiesis, present from the start. A bacterium continuously rebuilds its own membrane, enzymes, and machinery from within.
  
  _Cite:_ [Maturana & Varela](https://search.worldcat.org/title/5726379)


### Tendencies

_Adjacent-function blends (each pairs two neighbouring drives)._


- **Respiration Type** (Tendency · Effector × Regulator) — Respiration type is how a cell makes energy, pairing the drive to act with the drive to stabilize. Bacteria run an enormous menu: some breathe oxygen, others sulfur, iron, or light. Cyanobacteria invented oxygen-making photosynthesis and slowly rusted the oceans, then filled the air with oxygen.

- **Life-History Type** (Tendency · Regulator × Modeler) — Life-history type is how a lineage spends what it gathers, pairing the drive to stabilize with the drive to model. Some bacteria live fast: grab resources and divide quickly, booming then busting, the copiotroph or r end. Others live slow and thrifty, doing more with less and persisting through lean times, the oligotroph or K end. The setting is the lineage's standing bet about its world, fixed by selection rather than chosen by the cell.
  
  _Cite:_ MacArthur & Wilson; [Pianka](https://doi.org/10.1086/282697); [Malik et al.](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41396-019-0510-0)

- **Enclosure Type** (Tendency · Modeler × Reviser) — Enclosure type is what holds the cell together as a unit, pairing the drive to model with the drive to revise. A bacterium wraps itself in a wall and membrane, and many add a slime capsule or share walls in a biofilm, choosing how hard or soft a boundary to keep.

- **Acquisition Type** (Tendency · Reviser × Effector) — Acquisition type is how a cell gets what it needs, pairing the drive to revise with the drive to act. Bacteria are versatile feeders: some eat sugars, some fix carbon from air, some pull energy from rock. They also take up loose DNA from their surroundings, acquiring new abilities ready-made.


### Signatures

_Cross-function pairings that mark the clade._


- **Lineage** (Signature · Effector × Modeler) — Lineage is the defining coupling on the vertical: the surviving unit is the line of copies, not the single cell. Bacteria blur it further by swapping genes sideways across species, so a cell can pick up a new trait in one generation. The line, not the individual, carries forward.
  
  _Cite:_ [Moody et al.](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41559-024-02461-1)

- **Organism** (Signature · Regulator × Reviser) — Organism is the defining coupling on the horizontal: what counts as one individual. For bacteria the answer is fuzzy. Genes flow sideways so freely, and cells coordinate in biofilms and quorums so tightly, that the boundary of a single organism is more a working agreement than a hard wall.


### Capacities

_Defined by the one function that is *absent*._


- **Sequential Responding** (Capacity · without Effector) — Set the action drive aside and the other three read order: act on what reliably comes next, not on what is here now. In the gut, lactose normally arrives before maltose, so E. coli switches on its maltose machinery the moment it tastes lactose. The sequence is wired into the regulatory network, an early cue pre-inducing what a later one will need, and it is hardwired into the lineage, not learned.
  
  _Cite:_ [Mitchell et al.](https://doi.org/10.1038/nature08112); [Tagkopoulos et al.](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.1154456)

- **Precision Machinery** (Capacity · without Regulator) — Drop the stabilizer and the other three give precision machinery. A bacterium runs thousands of ribosomes, RNA-and-protein factories that read code and build proteins one amino acid at a time, about one error in ten thousand. The ribosome's core has barely changed since LUCA: the machine that built the first proteins still builds yours.
  
  _Cite:_ [Moody et al.](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41559-024-02461-1)

- **Taxis** (Capacity · without Modeler) — With no inner model, a bacterium still steers. E. coli climbs an attractant gradient by run-and-tumble: run straight, tumble to a new heading, and hold the run longer while things improve. The CheA kinase reads many chemical signals into one choice, swim or tumble, with sensing wired straight to movement and no map in between.
  
  _Cite:_ Van Duijn

- **State Gating** (Capacity · without Reviser) — With revision off, the unit runs a mature loop of stored programs and switches among them by condition. Starve Bacillus subtilis and it runs a tough-it-out program, wrapping into a dormant spore that can wait years; feed it and the spore wakes. The cell does not decide, the environmental state flips the gate.
  
  _Cite:_ [Errington](https://doi.org/10.1038/nrmicro750)


### Breakthrough

_The framework-center concept unlocked at this rung._


- **Predation** (Breakthrough (framework center)) — Predation is the moment the main threat to a living thing stops being the weather and becomes other living things. For the first time, organisms are selection pressures on each other. The oldest arms race is with viruses: even LUCA carried CRISPR, a gene-based immune memory, so cells were fighting invaders before any animal existed.
  
  _Cite:_ [Moody et al.](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41559-024-02461-1)

## 3. Eukaryote  

**Line:** genetic · **Date:** ~2 BYA · **Function introduced:** Modeler  
**Era:** Paleoproterozoic · **Coinciding event:** Post-Great Oxidation Event; Lomagundi-Jatuli excursion ends ~2.06 BYA


**Overview.** Cells with an organized interior — a nucleus that guards the DNA and little engine-organs like mitochondria, which began when one cell swallowed another and kept it alive inside. This clade holds the protists and everything built from them: fungi, plants, and animals.


**Virtuous cycle.** A protected, powered interior lets the cell grow larger and do more, and being larger and more capable makes that interior even more worth protecting and powering.


### Motivations

_The four base drives — one per cybernetic function._


- **Persistence** (Motivation · Effector) — Persistence is the founding drive to keep a pattern going, carried from the Protocell. Eukaryotes express it through faithful copying of a now-huge genome, guarded in the nucleus and proofread far more carefully than a bacterium's.
  
  _Cite:_ [Darwin](https://search.worldcat.org/title/7897536)

- **Coordination** (Motivation · Regulator) — Coordination is the drive to track and respond to other cells, carried from the Prokaryote. A eukaryotic cell coordinates not only with neighbors but with the tenant it absorbed: host and mitochondria run two genomes that must keep step, a partnership two billion years old.

- **Interiority** (Motivation · Modeler) — Interiority is the drive to build a genuine inside: not just a wall, but compartments with their own chemistry, rules, and history, the new motivation here. A eukaryotic cell guards its DNA in a nucleus and runs powered compartments within it, an organized interior no bacterium has.
  
  _Cite:_ [Lane](https://search.worldcat.org/title/921868945)

- **Self-Production** (Motivation · Reviser) — Self-production is a system making its own parts and closing the loop, the idea called autopoiesis, present from the start. A eukaryotic cell rebuilds its own membranes, organelles, and machinery continuously, now across many compartments at once.
  
  _Cite:_ [Maturana & Varela](https://search.worldcat.org/title/5726379)


### Tendencies

_Adjacent-function blends (each pairs two neighbouring drives)._


- **Respiration Type** (Tendency · Effector × Regulator) — Respiration type is how a cell makes energy, pairing the drive to act with the drive to stabilize. Eukaryotes run respiration inside mitochondria, burning fuel with oxygen far more efficiently than fermentation, which is what bankrolls their larger size.
  
  _Cite:_ [Lane](https://search.worldcat.org/title/921868945)

- **Foraging Type** (Tendency · Regulator × Modeler) — Foraging type is how a cell hunts and gathers, pairing the drive to stabilize with the drive to model. Physarum, one giant cell, grows tubes toward food and prunes the unused ones; dropped on a map of a city's food sources, it can grow a network resembling the real rail system.
  
  _Cite:_ [Kramar & Alim](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2007815118)

- **Production Type** (Tendency · Modeler × Reviser) — Energy-production type is the kind of power plant a cell runs, pairing the drive to model with the drive to revise. The eukaryotic answer is the mitochondrion, the swallowed bacterium turned permanent generator. Some lineages swallowed a second tenant, a photosynthesizer, and became algae and plants.
  
  _Cite:_ [Lane](https://search.worldcat.org/title/921868945)

- **Acquisition Type** (Tendency · Reviser × Effector) — Acquisition type is how a cell gets what it needs, pairing the drive to revise with the drive to act. The eukaryotic way is engulfing: instead of absorbing molecules through a wall, the cell wraps around whole particles and prey and takes them inside.
  
  _Cite:_ [Cavalier-Smith](https://doi.org/10.1099/00207713-52-2-297)


### Signatures

_Cross-function pairings that mark the clade._


- **Host** (Signature · Effector × Modeler) — Host is this level's defining coupling on the vertical: the cell is no longer one lineage but a merger, a host and the guest it took in and never let go. The mitochondrion in every eukaryotic cell descends from a free-living bacterium, a second lineage on its own genome, half a stranger still.
  
  _Cite:_ [Lane](https://search.worldcat.org/title/921868945)

- **Organism** (Signature · Regulator × Reviser) — Organism is the defining coupling on the horizontal: what counts as one individual. A eukaryotic cell looks like a clean answer, one bounded cell, but it is really a confederation, a host genome plus mitochondrial genomes cooperating so tightly they read as a single self.
  
  _Cite:_ [Lane](https://search.worldcat.org/title/921868945)


### Capacities

_Defined by the one function that is *absent*._


- **Checkpoint Control** (Capacity · without Effector) — Set the action drive aside and the other three give checkpoint control: the cell holds a big step back until an inner check clears it. A eukaryote ready to divide waits at its cell-cycle checkpoint, refusing to split until its chromosomes are copied clean, and only a passing check lets it go. It reads its own state and gates the move, without taking the step itself.
  
  _Cite:_ [Saigusa et al.](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.100.018101)

- **Phagocytosis** (Capacity · without Regulator) — Drop the stabilizer and the other three give phagocytosis: engulfing another cell whole, wrapping the membrane around it and pulling it inside. An amoeba does this to bacteria all day. It is also how complex life began: an ancestral cell swallowed a bacterium, did not digest it, and kept it as a power plant that still runs every animal cell.
  
  _Cite:_ [Cavalier-Smith](https://doi.org/10.1099/00207713-52-2-297)

- **Taxis** (Capacity · without Modeler) — With no inner model, a eukaryotic cell still steers, and with more finesse than a bacterium. An amoeba or a neutrophil reads the gradient across its whole body and crawls straight at the source, building a leading edge where the signaling lipid PIP3 piles up, balanced by the enzymes PI3K and PTEN. Sensing wired to movement, now resolved in space.
  
  _Cite:_ [Devreotes & Janetopoulos](https://doi.org/10.1074/jbc.R300010200); Iglesias & Devreotes

- **Intracellular Transport** (Capacity · without Reviser) — With revision off, the unit runs the haulage that keeps a big cell alive. A eukaryotic cell is too large for things to drift where needed, so motor proteins, kinesin and dynein, walk step by step along internal tracks carrying cargo across the cell. Billions are walking inside you right now.
  
  _Cite:_ [Vale & Milligan](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.288.5463.88)


### Breakthrough

_The framework-center concept unlocked at this rung._


- **Scale & Size** (Breakthrough (framework center)) — Scale and size is the jump to bigger, more complex cells, paid for by a new power supply. A cell that swallowed a bacterium and kept it alive gained an internal generator, the mitochondrion, and with energy to spare could carry a far larger genome and grow a bigger body. Complexity became affordable.
  
  _Cite:_ [Lane](https://search.worldcat.org/title/921868945)

## 4. Eumetazoa  

**Line:** genetic · **Date:** ~800 MYA · **Function introduced:** Reviser  
**Era:** Tonian (Neoproterozoic) · **Coinciding event:** Predates Cryogenian glaciations; molecular-clock anchor


**Overview.** The first true animals — bodies of many cells that stopped competing and specialized into tissues, including the first nervous tissue. Jellyfish, corals, and sea anemones are living examples: they sense and act as one body rather than as a crowd of separate cells.


**Virtuous cycle.** Cells that cooperate and divide the labor can do what no single cell can, and that payoff selects for tissues that coordinate the cooperation better — nerves being the sharpest of those.


### Motivations

_The four base drives — one per cybernetic function._


- **Persistence** (Motivation · Effector) — Persistence is the founding drive to keep a pattern going, carried up from the Protocell. In an animal it becomes the whole body's drive to stay alive and leave descendants, the oldest push still running under the new nervous one.
  
  _Cite:_ [Darwin](https://search.worldcat.org/title/7897536)

- **Coordination** (Motivation · Regulator) — Coordination is the drive to track and respond to others, carried from the Prokaryote. In the first animals it turns inward: millions of cells that stopped competing now coordinate as one body, a feat the nerve net exists to manage.

- **Interiority** (Motivation · Modeler) — Interiority is the drive to build an organized inside, carried from the Eukaryote. An animal scales it to a body with an inside and an outside, tissues with their own jobs, and a nerve net that begins to hold a rough internal sense of how the body is doing.

- **Excitability** (Motivation · Reviser) — Excitability is the fast, arousable responsiveness of nervous tissue, the new motivation here and the one that carries the genetic line into the neuronal line. Voltage-gated channels let a nerve net fire and a whole jellyfish flinch as one. The same channel proteins are the direct ancestors of every one in your brain.
  
  _Cite:_ [Burkhardt & Jékely](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.conb.2021.11.002)


### Tendencies

_Adjacent-function blends (each pairs two neighbouring drives)._


- **Respiration Type** (Tendency · Effector × Regulator) — Respiration type is how the body makes energy, pairing the drive to act with the drive to stabilize. Early animals breathed by simple diffusion across thin tissues, no lungs or gills yet, part of why the first ones stayed small and flat until the seas held more oxygen.

- **Foraging Type** (Tendency · Regulator × Modeler) — Foraging type is how the animal hunts and gathers, pairing the drive to stabilize with the drive to model. A hydra forages by waiting: it anchors in place and lets prey drift into its ring of stinging tentacles, an ambush that needs no chase and no map.
  
  _Cite:_ [Beckmann & Özbek](https://doi.org/10.1387/ijdb.113472ab)

- **Home Type** (Tendency · Modeler × Reviser) — Home type is the kind of place an animal keeps as its own, pairing the drive to model with the drive to revise. Many early animals settle: corals and anemones anchor to rock and stay for life, building a fixed home, while jellyfish drift, carrying no home at all.

- **Chronotype** (Tendency · Reviser × Effector) — Chronotype is when an animal's active hours fall, pairing the drive to revise with the drive to act, a toggle between more active and more at rest across the day. Sleep begins here: even a brainless jellyfish settles, stops answering, and rests on a daily clock no one set.
  
  _Cite:_ [Nath et al.](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cub.2017.08.014)


### Signatures

_Cross-function pairings that mark the clade._


- **Host** (Signature · Effector × Modeler) — Host is the defining coupling on the vertical, carried from the Eukaryote: the body is still a merger of lineages. Every cell in an animal, including every neuron in its nerve net, runs on the descendants of that one swallowed bacterium, powering its signals with a two-billion-year-old guest.
  
  _Cite:_ [Lane](https://search.worldcat.org/title/921868945)

- **Reflex Repertoire** (Signature · Regulator × Reviser) — Reflex repertoire is this level's defining coupling on the horizontal: the fixed set of stereotyped responses a nerve net can fire, the body's built-in moves. A hydra's contract-and-retract, a jellyfish's swim pulse, a sea anemone's close, each runs as a wired reflex, the same way every time, no learning required.
  
  _Cite:_ [Christensen](https://search.worldcat.org/title/71812675)


### Capacities

_Defined by the one function that is *absent*._


- **Allostatic Regulation** (Capacity · without Effector) — Set the action drive aside and the other three give allostatic regulation: holding different normal states for different situations instead of one fixed setting. A Cassiopea jellyfish, with no brain, sleeps, slowing its pulse at night and waking by day, and sleeps harder after a night kept awake. The baseline shifts with context.
  
  _Cite:_ [Nath et al.](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cub.2017.08.014)

- **Ingestion** (Capacity · without Regulator) — Drop the stabilizer and the other three give ingestion, eating the way animals eat: capturing prey with coordinated muscle and digesting it in a dedicated gut. A hydra fires tiny toxin harpoons on threads when something brushes a tentacle, hauls the catch to its mouth, and digests it inside. Mouths and guts start here.
  
  _Cite:_ [Beckmann & Özbek](https://doi.org/10.1387/ijdb.113472ab)

- **Non-Associative Learning** (Capacity · without Modeler) — With no inner model, the simplest learning a nerve net can do: turning a response up or down. Poke a hydra gently over and over and it stops reacting, it has habituated to a harmless thing. Shock it hard first and a later light touch makes it overreact, sensitized. No stored associations, just tuned excitability.
  
  _Cite:_ Rushforth

- **Intracellular Transport** (Capacity · without Reviser) — With revision off, the unit runs the haulage a body now stretches to its limit. The same motor proteins that carry cargo inside one cell now haul it down the length of a nerve cell, which in a big animal can run meters. The machinery did not change; the distances did.
  
  _Cite:_ [Vale & Milligan](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.288.5463.88)


### Breakthrough

_The framework-center concept unlocked at this rung._


- **Neurons** (Breakthrough (framework center)) — Neurons are the breakthrough here: cells specialized to signal with electricity in milliseconds, letting a whole body respond as one. A hydra has no brain, just a net of nerve spread evenly through it, yet that net already uses the same signaling molecules, glutamate and GABA, that run a human brain. The hardware is ancient; only the wiring changed.
  
  _Cite:_ [Burkhardt & Jékely](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.conb.2021.11.002)

# Neuronal line


## 5. Bilaterian  

**Line:** neuronal · **Date:** ~550 MYA · **Function introduced:** Effector  
**Era:** Late Ediacaran (Neoproterozoic) · **Coinciding event:** Immediately precedes Cambrian explosion (~540 MYA)


**Overview.** Animals with a head and a tail, a left and a right, and a brain at the front where the senses gather. This is the body plan of nearly every animal you can name, from worms and insects to fish and us. It appears just before the Cambrian explosion, when animal life suddenly diversified.


**Virtuous cycle.** A body that moves forward pays to put sensing and deciding at the front end, and a better front-end brain makes bolder forward movement worth the risk.


### Motivations

_The four base drives — one per cybernetic function._


- **Impetus** (Motivation · Effector) — Impetus is the drive to act and take risks, the neuronal line's founding push and the new motivation here. It is already a measurable trait in the simplest brains: a roundworm with just 302 neurons comes in bold and shy versions, the same individuals crossing a barrier toward a tempting smell while others hold back, trial after trial.
  
  _Cite:_ [Stern et al.](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cell.2017.10.041)

- **Coordination** (Motivation · Regulator) — Coordination is the drive to track and respond to others, carried up from the Prokaryote. In an animal with a centralized nervous system it becomes coordinating the body's own parts toward one aim, many muscles and segments pulling together instead of each reacting on its own.

- **Interiority** (Motivation · Modeler) — Interiority is the drive to hold an organized inside, carried from the Eukaryote. A bilaterian carries a faint one: an inner state, hunger or arousal, that colors how it reads the world and reaches out to mark whatever was around when things went well or badly.

- **Excitability** (Motivation · Reviser) — Excitability is the fast responsiveness of nervous tissue, carried up from the Eumetazoa where nerves began. It is the oldest neuronal drive, the raw readiness to fire and react that everything else here is built on.


### Tendencies

_Adjacent-function blends (each pairs two neighbouring drives)._


- **Affect** (Tendency · Effector × Regulator) — Affect is how an animal's inner state runs, pairing the drive to act with the drive to stabilize, set on two axes: good-to-bad and calm-to-aroused. A roundworm already has both, a valence axis and an arousal axis, the bare scaffolding of mood running on 302 neurons.
  
  _Cite:_ [Stern et al.](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cell.2017.10.041)

- **Foraging Type** (Tendency · Regulator × Modeler) — Foraging type is how an animal searches for food, pairing the drive to stabilize with the drive to model, from roaming widely to settling in one spot. Roundworms split along it: some rove and sample, some dwell and dig in, and the split holds across individuals.
  
  _Cite:_ [Stern et al.](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cell.2017.10.041)

- **Home Type** (Tendency · Modeler × Reviser) — Home type is the kind of place an animal keeps as its own, pairing the drive to model with the drive to revise. Early bilaterians range from burrowers holding a fixed tunnel to wanderers that carry no home at all, settling or roaming by lineage.

- **Orienting Style** (Tendency · Reviser × Effector) — Orienting style is whether the animal locks onto the freshest signal or lets it go — the exploratory setting of the new associative brain, and how it decides which cue among many is worth reacting to now. Bilaterians run it through four heuristics every later nervous system inherited: latent inhibition filters the cue seen so often it reads as background, overshadowing hands attention to the loudest of several cues at once, the eligibility trace holds only what happened a heartbeat before the outcome, and blocking refuses a new cue once an old one already predicts it. Tuned short, the animal orients off a single coincidence and chases the novel; tuned long, the recency layer goes quiet and the older pull toward warmth and activity shows through.


### Signatures

_Cross-function pairings that mark the clade._


- **Temperament** (Signature · Effector × Modeler) — Temperament is this level's defining coupling on the vertical: a standing bias in how an animal acts, stable across days, linking the drive to act with the inner state. Bold worms and shy worms share the same genes and the same 302 neurons, yet each forages a little differently and keeps to its style, a temperament you could name if you watched long enough.
  
  _Cite:_ [Stern et al.](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cell.2017.10.041)

- **Reflex Repertoire** (Signature · Regulator × Reviser) — Reflex repertoire is the defining coupling on the horizontal, carried from the Eumetazoa: the fixed set of wired moves the nervous system fires the same way every time. A worm's rapid withdrawal from a poke, a startle that snaps the body away, run as built-in reflexes underneath whatever the animal learns on top of them.


### Capacities

_Defined by the one function that is *absent*._


- **Allostatic Regulation** (Capacity · without Effector) — Set the action drive aside and the other three hold different normal states for different situations. Even a fruit fly sleeps in the full sense: less active, harder to rouse, and sleeping harder after a night kept awake. The fly does not decide to sleep; its regulatory circuits flip the state when context calls for it.
  
  _Cite:_ [Shaw et al.](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.287.5459.1834)

- **Steering** (Capacity · without Regulator) — Drop the stabilizer and the other three give steering: a nervous system that can point the whole body at a goal and close on it, or turn and flee, instead of only reacting where it sits. It needs a front end, a head where the senses gather, aimed the way the animal moves. A jellyfish set an inch from food can lose it on the way in; a worm with a front end does not.
  
  _Cite:_ [Bennett (2023)](https://books.google.com/books/about/A_Brief_History_of_Intelligence.html?id=s506zwEACAAJ)

- **Associative Learning** (Capacity · without Modeler) — With no inner model, a bilaterian can still learn that one thing predicts another. A sea slug touched on the siphon and shocked on the tail soon yanks its gill hard at the touch alone: it has linked the two. Eric Kandel traced this to changes between named neurons, work that won a Nobel. Pairing events so one predicts the next is what bilateral nervous systems made possible.
  
  _Cite:_ [Hawkins et al.](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.6294833)

- **Inhibition** (Capacity · without Reviser) — With revision off, the unit can hold a strong default back instead of always firing it. A crayfish has a giant nerve cell that flips its whole body backward in a fraction of a second when startled, but a layer of inhibition decides when not to fire it: mid-meal or mid-swim, the escape is held. Being ready and choosing not to act is what this adds.
  
  _Cite:_ [Edwards et al.](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0166-2236(98)01340-X)


### Breakthrough

_The framework-center concept unlocked at this rung._


- **Attention** (Breakthrough (framework center)) — Attention is the breakthrough here, and it founds the neuronal line the way natural selection founds the genetic one: it is the contest in which candidate behaviors compete for expression, the winner scored by reward and kept by memory. That is selection moved inside a single animal — a within-lifetime Darwinism, so a body can learn in an afternoon what a lineage would need generations to. Its bilaterian expression is steering: a front-ended animal picking one target out of many and aiming the whole body at it, where a jellyfish can only react where it sits.
  
  _Cite:_ [Bennett (2023)](https://books.google.com/books/about/A_Brief_History_of_Intelligence.html?id=s506zwEACAAJ)

## 6. Vertebrate  

**Line:** neuronal · **Date:** ~420 MYA · **Function introduced:** Regulator  
**Era:** Early Devonian (Paleozoic) · **Coinciding event:** Devonian Age of Fishes onset; jawed-fish radiation


**Overview.** Animals with a backbone and a bony skull wrapped around a larger brain — fish first, then everything descended from them: amphibians, reptiles, birds, and mammals. The clade rises to prominence in the Devonian, the 'Age of Fishes.'


**Virtuous cycle.** Learning which actions pay off lets the animal get steadily better at getting what it needs, and the better it gets, the more worthwhile that learning machinery becomes.


### Motivations

_The four base drives — one per cybernetic function._


- **Impetus** (Motivation · Effector) — Impetus is the drive to act and take risks, carried from the Bilaterian. Drop something new in a tank and fish sort into two: one noses straight up to it and owns the corner within a minute, another hangs at the back wall and watches. Bold and wary, the same split found in trout, rats, pigs, and monkeys.
  
  _Cite:_ [Bell et al.](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.anbehav.2008.12.022)

- **Mastery** (Motivation · Regulator) — Mastery is the drive to get steadily better at one's own moves, the new motivation here. It arrives with reward-based learning: a fish fed for the right action repeats and refines it until the move is smooth and sure. Getting good at what works becomes its own pull.

- **Interiority** (Motivation · Modeler) — Interiority is the drive to hold an organized inside, carried up the line. A vertebrate's is richer: a gut that senses its own contents and an inner economy of hunger, stress, and energy that the brain reads and weighs before acting.

- **Excitability** (Motivation · Reviser) — Excitability is the fast responsiveness of nervous tissue, the oldest neuronal drive, carried up from the first nerve nets. In a vertebrate it runs through a centralized brain and spinal cord, but the raw readiness to fire is the same ancient thing.


### Tendencies

_Adjacent-function blends (each pairs two neighbouring drives)._


- **Coping Style** (Tendency · Effector × Regulator) — Coping style is how an animal meets stress, pairing the drive to act with the drive to stabilize, on a dial from proactive to reactive. Proactive fish hold territory and habituate fast; reactive ones freeze and stay wary. The same split shows up across fish, rodents, pigs, and primates.
  
  _Cite:_ [Koolhaas et al.](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0149-7634(99)00026-3)

- **Development Rate** (Tendency · Regulator × Modeler) — Development rate is how fast an animal grows up and lives out its life, pairing the drive to stabilize with the drive to model, from fast-and-furious to slow-and-steady. A pace-of-life idea predicts bold animals should also burn hot and develop fast, but the individual-level link is shaky in the data.
  
  _Cite:_ [Royauté et al.](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00265-018-2472-z)

- **Home Type** (Tendency · Modeler × Reviser) — Home type is the kind of place an animal keeps as its own, pairing the drive to model with the drive to revise. Fish split along it: some defend a fixed territory or nest site for a season, others shoal and roam open water, keeping no home at all.

- **Orienting Style** (Tendency · Reviser × Effector) — Orienting style is the horizon a reward system works over, pairing the drive to revise with the drive to act — how far back it reaches to tie an action to what followed. In vertebrates it rides the basal-ganglia machinery that tracks value across delay, tuned short-to-long: some fish swing to the freshest payoff and move on, others weight a longer run of outcomes before committing. The quick reactor and the slow accumulator are the same circuit at different settings.


### Signatures

_Cross-function pairings that mark the clade._


- **Temperament** (Signature · Effector × Modeler) — Temperament is the defining coupling on the vertical, carried from the Bilaterian: a standing behavioral bias stable across time. In fish it is the bold-or-wary axis, and it holds: the bold individual that explored the new object this week is the one that explores it next week too.
  
  _Cite:_ [Bell et al.](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.anbehav.2008.12.022)

- **Behavioral Syndrome** (Signature · Regulator × Reviser) — A behavioral syndrome is this level's new coupling on the horizontal: separate behaviors that travel together. Measure boldness, activity, and aggression in individual fish and they correlate, the bold ones also the active, aggressive ones, across situations. The links are weak but consistent enough for selection to act on the whole bundle.
  
  _Cite:_ [Sih et al.](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.tree.2004.04.009)


### Capacities

_Defined by the one function that is *absent*._


- **Gut Sense** (Capacity · without Effector) — Set the action drive aside and the other three give gut sense: a read on the body's own inner economy. A vertebrate has a second nervous system lining its gut, around 500 million neurons in a human, more than the spinal cord, that monitors food and chemistry and signals up to the brain, shaping mood and energy in ways the thinking part never sees directly.
  
  _Cite:_ [Furness](https://doi.org/10.1038/nrgastro.2012.32)

- **Steering** (Capacity · without Regulator) — Drop the stabilizer and the other three give steering, now with a dedicated circuit. When a vertebrate sees something good, dopamine neurons deep in the midbrain fire and pull it toward the thing. The same pathway runs in a guppy, a guinea pig, or a person, and individual fish vary in how strongly it drives them to investigate.
  
  _Cite:_ [Wise](https://doi.org/10.1038/nrn1406)

- **Temporal Credit** (Capacity · without Modeler) — With no inner model, a vertebrate can still credit an action for a reward that comes seconds later. A fish bumps a lever, food drops three seconds on, and by then it has done six other things. The basal ganglia solve it with dopamine, which fires above baseline when reward beats expectation and shifts that signal back onto the action that earned it.
  
  _Cite:_ [Schultz et al.](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.275.5306.1593)

- **Procedural Memory** (Capacity · without Reviser) — With revision off, the mature loop runs stored motor programs on autopilot. A goldfish learns a maze and, after enough trials, swims it without thinking through each turn, the same kind of memory behind a bird's song or a person on a bicycle. The structures that hold it, basal ganglia and cerebellum, run from sharks to humans.
  
  _Cite:_ [Squire (2004)](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.nlm.2004.06.005)


### Breakthrough

_The framework-center concept unlocked at this rung._


- **Reinforcing** (Breakthrough (framework center)) — Reinforcing is the breakthrough here: a brain that can work out which of its recent actions earned a reward that arrived seconds later, and tune toward it. The machinery is the basal ganglia, nearly unchanged from a lamprey, a jawless fish, all the way to a human. Every animal you can train with food rewards is running this same circuit.
  
  _Cite:_ [Bennett (2023)](https://books.google.com/books/about/A_Brief_History_of_Intelligence.html?id=s506zwEACAAJ)

## 7. Mammal  

**Line:** neuronal · **Date:** ~200 MYA · **Function introduced:** Modeler  
**Era:** Triassic-Jurassic boundary (Mesozoic) · **Coinciding event:** End-Triassic extinction; Pangaea breakup begins


**Overview.** Mammals — from mice to whales to humans. The clade is defined by many new brain regions layered over the older vertebrate brain, plus warm blood, fur, and milk. They appear near the Triassic–Jurassic boundary and spend roughly 135 million years small and nocturnal before the dinosaurs' exit opens room to diversify.


**Virtuous cycle.** A bigger model-building brain pays for a costly warm body by finding food more cleverly; better foraging funds a still bigger brain; and sleep consolidates what the day's model learned.


### Motivations

_The four base drives — one per cybernetic function._


- **Impetus** (Motivation · Effector) — Impetus is the drive to act and take risks rather than play safe, the neuronal line's founding push. It first entered at the Bilaterian and is carried up to here. In mammals it is a measurable, repeatable trait: some rats reliably explore an open, exposed space that their cage-mates will not leave the walls of.
  
  _Cite:_ [Sih, Bell & Johnson (2004): Behavioral syndromes](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.tree.2004.04.009)

- **Mastery** (Motivation · Regulator) — Mastery is the drive to get steadily better at one's own moves through practice. It entered at the Vertebrate, when reward-based learning let an animal tune a skill toward what works. A rat refining its run through a familiar maze, faster and surer each trial, is this drive carried into the mammal.

- **Autonomy** (Motivation · Modeler) — Autonomy is the drive to run on your own inner map instead of only reacting to what is in front of you. It is the model-building motivation, new at this clade and the one colored circle in the flower. Tolman's rats showed it: allowed to wander a maze with no reward, they built a map anyway, then beat reward-trained rats the moment cheese appeared.
  
  _Cite:_ [Tolman (1948): Cognitive maps in rats and men](https://doi.org/10.1037/h0061626)

- **Excitability** (Motivation · Reviser) — Excitability is the fast, arousable responsiveness of nervous tissue: how readily the system fires up and reacts. It is the oldest of the four drives still active here, carried all the way up from the first nerve nets at the Eumetazoa. A startled mammal's instant freeze or bolt runs on it.


### Tendencies

_Adjacent-function blends (each pairs two neighbouring drives)._


- **Coping Style** (Tendency · Effector × Regulator) — Coping style is how an animal meets stress, charging at it or hanging back, pairing the drive to act with the drive to stabilize. It runs on a dial from proactive to reactive. Rodents sort cleanly into the two: proactive copers build bold, fixed routines, reactive copers stay cautious and flexible, and each style holds across situations.
  
  _Cite:_ [Koolhaas et al. (1999): Coping styles in animals](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0149-7634(99)00026-3)

- **Experiencing Style** (Tendency · Regulator × Modeler) — Experiencing style is how attention is spent, diving deep into one thing or sampling broadly across many, pairing the drive to stabilize with the drive to build a model. It runs on a Deep-to-Meander dial. A foraging mammal lives on it: exploit one rich patch thoroughly, or keep roving to sample what else is out there.

- **Remembering Style** (Tendency · Modeler × Reviser) — Remembering style is what the inner model keeps and what it lets fade, pairing model-building with revision, which together are what learning is. It is actively managed, not passive. In a resting rat, place-cell replay runs recent routes back over themselves, fixing some into lasting memory while others are allowed to decay.
  
  _Cite:_ [Foster & Wilson (2006): Reverse replay in hippocampal place cells](https://doi.org/10.1038/nature04587)

- **Orienting Style** (Tendency · Reviser × Effector) — Orienting style in mammals is the same horizon dial stretched longer, pairing revision with action across minutes-long chains rather than seconds. Prefrontal extensions of the dopamine system let the animal tie an action to a reward far downstream, so the setting now spans the impulsive short view and the patient long one. A rat runs a whole maze before the food and still binds the outcome back along the chain — how far that reach extends is the disposition.


### Signatures

_Cross-function pairings that mark the clade._


- **Self-Model** (Signature · Effector × Modeler) — Self-Model is this level's defining coupling on the vertical: the inner model now contains the animal itself. It links the drive to act with the drive to build a model. A rat that tracks its own uncertainty, opting out of a test when it senses it does not know, is reading a model that includes its own states.
  
  _Cite:_ [Foote & Crystal (2007): Metacognition in the rat](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cub.2007.01.061)

- **Behavioral Syndrome** (Signature · Regulator × Reviser) — A behavioral syndrome is a stable bundle of correlated behaviors, bold-and-aggressive or shy-and-careful, holding across many different situations. It is the level-defining coupling on the horizontal. Measure boldness, aggression, and exploration in individual rodents and the same individuals score high or low together, across contexts and over time.
  
  _Cite:_ [Sih, Bell & Johnson (2004): Behavioral syndromes](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.tree.2004.04.009)


### Capacities

_Defined by the one function that is *absent*._


- **Reflective Cognition** (Capacity · without Effector) — Set the action drive aside and the other three give reflective cognition: thinking that steps back and checks its own thinking, strategy without immediate doing. Rats show a version of it. Given a hard memory trial, they will decline the test they are unsure of and take a smaller guaranteed reward instead, tracking when they do not know.
  
  _Cite:_ [Foote & Crystal (2007): Metacognition in the rat](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cub.2007.01.061)

- **Algorithmic Cognition** (Capacity · without Regulator) — Drop the stabilizer and the other three run a chain forward from a starting point: try the trajectory in the model, then act. A rat at the mouth of a maze it knows shows this. Its place cells fire down the route it is about to take, ahead of the body, as if testing the path before it steps.
  
  _Cite:_ [Pfeiffer & Foster (2013): Place-cell sequences depict future paths](https://doi.org/10.1038/nature12112)

- **Temporal Credit** (Capacity · without Modeler) — Without an inner model, the unit can still link an action to a reward that arrives much later, working out which earlier move earned the payoff. A mammal's midbrain solves it with dopamine: those neurons fire when a reward beats expectation, then shift their firing back onto the earliest cue that reliably predicts it as learning sets in.
  
  _Cite:_ [Schultz, Dayan & Montague (1997): A neural substrate of prediction and reward](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.275.5306.1593)

- **Autonomous Cognition** (Capacity · without Reviser) — With revision switched off, the mature loop runs on the model it already holds and resists being overwritten from outside. Blocking shows the mechanism: once a rat has learned that one cue predicts a shock, a second cue paired with the same shock gains no power of its own, because the existing expectation blocks the update.
  
  _Cite:_ [Kamin (1968): Attention-like processes in classical conditioning](https://search.worldcat.org/title/1345627728)


### Breakthrough

_The framework-center concept unlocked at this rung._


- **Simulating** (Breakthrough (framework center)) — Simulating means running the inner world model offline, playing out an action as if on a screen before the body performs it. It needs all four drives at once. A resting rat shows the machinery: its place cells replay routes forward and in reverse while the body holds still, rehearsing sequences off the clock.
  
  _Cite:_ [Pfeiffer & Foster (2013): Place-cell sequences depict future paths](https://doi.org/10.1038/nature12112); [Foster & Wilson (2006): Reverse replay in hippocampal place cells](https://doi.org/10.1038/nature04587)

## 8. Primate  

**Line:** neuronal · **Date:** ~66 MYA · **Function introduced:** Reviser  
**Era:** Paleocene (Paleogene, Cenozoic) · **Coinciding event:** K-Pg extinction (Chicxulub impact)


**Overview.** The clade of lemurs, monkeys, apes — and us. Primates live in trees with grasping hands, forward-facing eyes for judging distance, and an outer brain layer expanded for life in complicated social groups. They spread out right after the dinosaurs disappear.


**Virtuous cycle.** Living in a group means tracking who is who and who did what, and the brainpower that tracks the group pays off as the group grows more complex — feeding back into still more social brainpower.


### Motivations

_The four base drives — one per cybernetic function._


- **Impetus** (Motivation · Effector) — Impetus is the founding drive to act and take risks, carried up the whole neuronal line. In a primate it plays out socially as much as physically: who approaches a tense situation, who challenges a rival, who hangs back and watches.

- **Mastery** (Motivation · Regulator) — Mastery is the drive to get steadily better at one's own moves, carried from the Vertebrate. A young chimp spends years getting good at cracking nuts or fishing for ants, practicing a skill long past bare competence.

- **Autonomy** (Motivation · Modeler) — Autonomy is the drive to run on one's own inner map rather than the room, carried from the Mammal. A primate holds a rich model of its world and its place in it, and can act from that model even against the immediate pull of the group.
  
  _Cite:_ [Tolman](https://doi.org/10.1037/h0061626)

- **Affiliation** (Motivation · Reviser) — Affiliation is the drive to bond and belong, the new motivation here, replacing raw excitability in the reviser slot. Primates run their lives on it: grooming, alliances, and friendships lasting years. In chimps a single gene tied to social bonding, the same one linked to pair bonds in voles, predicts how strongly an individual affiliates.
  
  _Cite:_ [Hopkins et al.](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1601-183x.2012.00799.x)


### Tendencies

_Adjacent-function blends (each pairs two neighbouring drives)._


- **Coping Style** (Tendency · Effector × Regulator) — Coping style is how an animal meets stress, pairing the drive to act with the drive to stabilize, proactive or reactive. The same split runs in primate troops as in fish and rodents: bold confronters who habituate fast, and wary watchers who stay vigilant.
  
  _Cite:_ [Koolhaas et al.](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0149-7634(99)00026-3)

- **Experiencing Style** (Tendency · Regulator × Modeler) — Experiencing style is how attention is spent, pairing the drive to stabilize with the drive to model, deep focus on one thing or broad sampling across many. A foraging primate lives on the dial, working one rich tree thoroughly or ranging widely to check what else the forest offers.

- **Agreeableness** (Tendency · Modeler × Reviser) — Agreeableness is how warmly an animal handles others, pairing the drive to model with the drive to revise, from tolerant and cooperative to pushy and combative. It shows up as a real personality dimension in chimpanzees and bonobos, measurable and stable across individuals well below the human line.
  
  _Cite:_ [King & Figueredo](https://doi.org/10.1006/jrpe.1997.2179)

- **Certainty** (Tendency · Reviser × Effector) — Certainty is how an animal plays the social game, pairing the drive to revise with the drive to act, from working alliances to going it alone. Primate troops hold both: coalition-builders who trade favors and climb through partners, and loners who win or lose on their own.


### Signatures

_Cross-function pairings that mark the clade._


- **Self-Model** (Signature · Effector × Modeler) — Self-Model is the defining coupling on the vertical, carried from the Mammal: an inner model that includes the self. A primate's is socially loaded, a sense of itself as seen by others. A monkey does not only know it lost a fight, it knows the others watched it lose, and marks its own worth down by what the watchers now think.
  
  _Cite:_ [Foote & Crystal](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cub.2007.01.061)

- **Sociometer** (Signature · Regulator × Reviser) — Sociometer is this level's new coupling on the horizontal: an inner gauge of how accepted or valued one is, read off others' regard. Tellingly, dominance is its own separate personality dimension in every non-human primate measured, a standing axis of how hard you push and how often you win, hinting at the social-rank machinery the gauge runs on.
  
  _Cite:_ [King & Figueredo](https://doi.org/10.1006/jrpe.1997.2179)


### Capacities

_Defined by the one function that is *absent*._


- **Standing** (Capacity · without Effector) — Set the action drive aside and the other three track standing: where one sits relative to others. Show a capuchin monkey her partner getting a grape for the same task that earns her a cucumber slice, and she throws the cucumber back, refusing a fine food because a peer got better. She is tracking her place, not her hunger.
  
  _Cite:_ [Brosnan & de Waal](https://doi.org/10.1038/nature01963)

- **Tactical Deceit** (Capacity · without Regulator) — Drop the stabilizer and the other three give tactical deceit: pursuing a goal by managing what others know. Faced with food and a dominant rival, a subordinate chimp preferentially goes for the piece the dominant cannot see. It is tracking what the other knows and using the gap, the cognitive root of politics and bluff.
  
  _Cite:_ [Hare, Call & Tomasello](https://doi.org/10.1006/anbe.2000.1518)

- **Triadic Awareness** (Capacity · without Modeler) — Without modeling anyone in the moment, a primate still tracks not just its own rank but who outranks whom across the whole group. A chimp registers not only that the alpha changed but that everyone present saw it change. One public submission grunt, witnessed by all, has split a troop along the new fault line and led, years later, to lethal raids.

- **Autonomous Cognition** (Capacity · without Reviser) — With revision off, the mature loop runs on what it already knows and resists being overwritten, now hardened by the group. Teach two chimp troops different ways to crack the same nut, then show each an expert using the other way; they watch, understand, and keep doing it their own way. The prior is not just in one head, it is mirrored by everyone around.
  
  _Cite:_ [Whiten et al.](https://doi.org/10.1038/nature04047)


### Breakthrough

_The framework-center concept unlocked at this rung._


- **Mentalizing** (Breakthrough (framework center)) — Mentalizing is the breakthrough here: holding a model of another mind, what it knows, wants, and does not know. A low-ranking chimp wanting food the boss is sitting on clocks that the boss cannot see behind a rock, and takes the long way into the blind spot. Whether apes pass the strictest false-belief tests is still argued, part of why it sits here as contested.
  
  _Cite:_ [Bennett (2023)](https://books.google.com/books/about/A_Brief_History_of_Intelligence.html?id=s506zwEACAAJ)

# Symbolic line


## 9. Band-Human  

**Line:** symbolic · **Date:** ~100 KYA · **Function introduced:** Effector  
**Era:** Late Pleistocene; Middle Stone Age · **Coinciding event:** Behavioral modernity; symbolic artifacts (Blombos cave)


**Overview.** Modern humans living the way our species lived for almost all of its history: in small mobile bands of a few dozen, hunting and gathering, bound together by language. The breakthrough here is full speech — whole sentences, not just calls and signals.


**Virtuous cycle.** Talking lets a band pool what each member knows and act as one, and a band that coordinates better survives to pass on the language that let it.


### Motivations

_The four base drives — one per cybernetic function._


- **Commitment** (Motivation · Effector) — Commitment is the drive to keep one's word and stay reliable to the group, the new motivation that opens the symbolic line, replacing raw boldness. It is load-bearing because bands run on it: across 32 hunter-gatherer societies, most people sharing a camp are not close kin, so cohesion rests on reciprocal commitment, not blood.
  
  _Cite:_ [Hill et al.](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.1199071)

- **Mastery** (Motivation · Regulator) — Mastery is the drive to get steadily better at one's own moves, carried up from the Vertebrate. A forager spends years mastering tracking, knapping, and the hundred skills of getting a living from the land, refining each well past bare competence.

- **Autonomy** (Motivation · Modeler) — Autonomy is the drive to run on one's own inner map rather than the room, carried from the Mammal. A band has no boss; each forager judges where to hunt and when to move, holding their own read of the land against the group's.

- **Affiliation** (Motivation · Reviser) — Affiliation is the drive to bond and belong, carried up from the Primate. In a band it is the warmth that holds a few dozen people together day after day, the friendships and loyalties that make sharing and cooperation feel natural rather than forced.


### Tendencies

_Adjacent-function blends (each pairs two neighbouring drives)._


- **Reputation** (Tendency · Effector × Regulator) — Reputation is how others' regard accrues to you, pairing the drive to act with the drive to stabilize. Language makes it scalable: a band of thirty keeps track of everyone's standing in everyone's eyes through talk about people not in the room, a social ledger no ape without speech could hold.
  
  _Cite:_ [Wiessner](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1404212111)

- **Experiencing Style** (Tendency · Regulator × Modeler) — Experiencing style is how attention is spent, pairing the drive to stabilize with the drive to model, deep focus or broad sampling. A forager lives on the dial, reading one set of tracks intently or scanning the whole landscape for what has changed.

- **Agreeableness** (Tendency · Modeler × Reviser) — Agreeableness is how warmly a person handles others, pairing the drive to model with the drive to revise, from cooperative and trusting to combative. It is one of the most reliably found human personality dimensions, and in a small interdependent band the warm, cooperative end is heavily favored.

- **Neuroticism** (Tendency · Reviser × Effector) — Neuroticism is how strongly a person feels and reacts to threat and setback, pairing the drive to revise with the drive to act, from calm to easily rattled. It is a core human personality dimension, and in a dangerous foraging world a quick, anxious read of risk can be life-saving rather than a flaw.


### Signatures

_Cross-function pairings that mark the clade._


- **Self-Reference** (Signature · Effector × Modeler) — Self-Reference is this level's defining coupling on the vertical: holding a stable picture of how others see you, linking the drive to act with the drive to model, turned on the self. It is the groundwork of shame and pride, feelings that only make sense once you can model your own standing in another's eyes.

- **Sociometer** (Signature · Regulator × Reviser) — Sociometer is the defining coupling on the horizontal, carried from the Primate: an inner gauge of how accepted you are, now read off reputation in a talking community. In a band where everyone's standing is tracked in gossip, the gauge reads the whole group's regard, not just whoever you face directly.


### Capacities

_Defined by the one function that is *absent*._


- **Standing** (Capacity · without Effector) — Set the action drive aside and the other three track standing: where one sits in others' eyes. Language scales it. Polly Wiessner found Ju/'hoansi daytime talk is practical, but night talk around the fire turns to stories and gossip about absent people, the band quietly updating everyone's reputation, even those not there.
  
  _Cite:_ [Wiessner](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1404212111)

- **Alloparenting** (Capacity · without Regulator) — Drop the stabilizer and the other three give alloparenting: raising a child through a network of helpers, not the mother alone. A Hadza infant is held by around fourteen caregivers in its first weeks, siblings, aunts, unrelated band members rotating through. Shared childcare needs constant tracking of who can be trusted with a child.
  
  _Cite:_ [Hrdy](https://search.worldcat.org/title/1153314912)

- **Meat Pooling** (Capacity · without Modeler) — With no central planner, the band still solves the gamble of the hunt: meat shared out, not hoarded. A hunter brings down big game rarely and gives most of it away, taking 40 to 60 percent of his lifetime calories from others' kills in return. Pooling averages the wild luck of hunting across many tables.
  
  _Cite:_ [Kaplan et al.](https://doi.org/10.1002/1520-6505(2000)9:4<156::aid-evan5>3.0.co;2-7)

- **Collaborative Fission** (Capacity · without Reviser) — With revision off, the group keeps its peace by splitting cleanly when it grows too big or tense, then staying linked. Foragers marry across bands by reciprocal exchange, so a band that divides keeps alliances with its offshoots, extending the safe coalition far past any one camp's fighting strength.
  
  _Cite:_ [Walker et al.](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1002598107)


### Breakthrough

_The framework-center concept unlocked at this rung._


- **Speaking** (Breakthrough (framework center)) — Speaking is the breakthrough here, and it founds the symbolic line the way attention founds the neuronal one: it is the contest in which candidate utterances compete to be repeated, the winner scored by uptake and kept in shared memory. That is selection moved out of any single mind and into a corpus that outlives the speaker — so a band can hold what one lifetime would lose. Its band expression is full open-ended language, whole sentences rather than fixed calls that make gossip, reputation, and shared plans work across a whole band, not just between two groomers. The date is argued from material traces, since talk leaves no fossil: the oldest stand-ins are abstract scratch-marks on ochre at Blombos Cave, roughly 75,000 to 100,000 years old.
  
  _Cite:_ [Henshilwood et al.](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-018-0514-3)

## 10. Settlement-Human  

**Line:** symbolic · **Date:** ~10 KYA · **Function introduced:** Regulator  
**Era:** Early Holocene; Neolithic · **Coinciding event:** End of Younger Dryas; Neolithic Revolution


**Overview.** Humans who stopped roaming and stayed put — the first farming villages, where people who were not close kin had to live side by side all year. Holding a settlement together takes shared norms: rules everyone knows that everyone else is keeping too.


**Virtuous cycle.** Staying in one place lets people store food and build, which supports more people, which makes the shared rules that keep near-strangers cooperating even more necessary and more worth enforcing.


### Motivations

_The four base drives — one per cybernetic function._


- **Commitment** (Motivation · Effector) — Commitment is the drive to keep one's word and stay reliable, carried from the Band. In a fixed village of near-strangers living side by side all year, it hardens into dependability: the neighbor who honors obligations across seasons is the one a settlement can build on.
  
  _Cite:_ [Hill et al.](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.1199071)

- **Stability** (Motivation · Regulator) — Stability is the drive to keep things consistent and in order, the new motivation here, replacing skill-mastery as the main pull. Farming rewards it: crops do not wait for inspiration and stored grain needs tending on a schedule, so the person who shows up reliably, season after season, out-survives the brilliant improviser.
  
  _Cite:_ [Roberts et al.](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1745-6916.2007.00047.x)

- **Autonomy** (Motivation · Modeler) — Autonomy is the drive to run on one's own inner map, carried from the Mammal. A farmer holds a private plan for the land and the year, and works it even when it differs from how a neighbor would do it.

- **Affiliation** (Motivation · Reviser) — Affiliation is the drive to bond and belong, carried from the Primate. In a settlement it widens past kin to the standing relationships of village life, the households one cooperates, trades, and shares ritual with year after year.


### Tendencies

_Adjacent-function blends (each pairs two neighbouring drives)._


- **Conscientiousness** (Tendency · Effector × Regulator) — Conscientiousness is the trait of being reliable, orderly, and hardworking, debuting here. Agricultural life selects hard for it: it predicts survival outcomes more strongly than IQ or wealth, worth roughly seven added years of life, and the farmer who plants, weeds, and harvests on time out-lasts the improviser.
  
  _Cite:_ [Roberts et al.](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1745-6916.2007.00047.x)

- **Assertiveness** (Tendency · Regulator × Modeler) — Assertiveness is how forcefully a person stakes a claim, pairing the drive to stabilize with the drive to model. In a settlement it works as norm-negotiating rather than open challenge: the farmer secures position within a fixed hierarchy through reliable performance and vocal claim-making, not by fighting strangers.

- **Agreeableness** (Tendency · Modeler × Reviser) — Agreeableness is how warmly a person handles others, pairing the drive to model with the drive to revise. In a settlement it is the trait that keeps near-strangers cooperating across years of shared fields, wells, and walls, where a falling-out has nowhere to walk away to.

- **Neuroticism** (Tendency · Reviser × Effector) — Neuroticism is how strongly a person feels threat and setback, pairing the drive to revise with the drive to act. Settled life offers new things to dread, a failed harvest, a stored surplus that could be lost, and a watchful, anxious temperament reads those slow risks as readily as a forager reads a fast one.


### Signatures

_Cross-function pairings that mark the clade._


- **Self-Reference** (Signature · Effector × Modeler) — Self-Reference is the defining coupling on the vertical, carried from the Band: holding a stable picture of how others see you. In a settlement of fixed neighbors who watch year-round, that picture becomes a steady, named reputation rather than a shifting camp-fire one.

- **Self-Construal** (Signature · Regulator × Reviser) — Self-Construal is this level's new coupling on the horizontal: whether one reads oneself as an independent individual or as part of an interdependent web. Fixed property and household boundaries first make this a stable question. Strikingly, a history of paddy-rice farming, with its shared irrigation, leaves cultures more interdependent than wheat farming does.
  
  _Cite:_ [Talhelm et al.](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.1246850)


### Capacities

_Defined by the one function that is *absent*._


- **Wealth** (Capacity · without Effector) — Set the action drive aside and the other three give wealth: holding value across time. A forager band has little to inherit, but a village stores grain, herds animals, and passes land rights to children. Across 21 small-scale societies, farming and herding families stay rich or poor for generations, where foragers do not.
  
  _Cite:_ [Borgerhoff Mulder et al.](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.1178336)

- **Alloparenting** (Capacity · without Regulator) — Drop the stabilizer and the other three give alloparenting, now through fixed kin. The rotating helpers of the mobile band give way to the extended-family household: across 45 small-scale societies, a living maternal grandmother markedly improves a child's odds of surviving to five.
  
  _Cite:_ [Sear & Mace](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.evolhumbehav.2007.10.001)

- **Farming** (Capacity · without Modeler) — With no central planner, the settlement runs the opposite gamble from the band: store, do not share out. Where foragers pool meat that would spoil, farmers hoard grain that keeps, and stored surplus becomes the first lasting and the first unequal wealth. Same accumulating capacity, inverted social logic.
  
  _Cite:_ [Roberts et al.](https://doi.org/10.1037/a0031109)

- **Norms** (Capacity · without Reviser) — With revision off, the settlement holds together on shared rules everyone keeps and expects others to keep. Çatalhöyük housed up to 8,000 people with no palace and no clear elite, every house the same size: a town running on normative coordination rather than a boss. Norms are the village's way of cooperating without a ruler.
  
  _Cite:_ [Bicchieri](https://search.worldcat.org/title/967410281)


### Breakthrough

_The framework-center concept unlocked at this rung._


- **Common Knowledge** (Breakthrough (framework center)) — Common knowledge is the breakthrough here: not just that everyone knows a rule, but that everyone knows everyone knows it, and so on up. Settlement architecture, the square, the monument, the marketplace, gives strangers durable shared focal points that lock that mutual awareness in. Norms then rest on it. The archaeology is inferred, not directly sourced.
  
  _Cite:_ [Pinker](https://search.worldcat.org/title/154308853)

## 11. City-Human  

**Line:** symbolic · **Date:** ~5 KYA · **Function introduced:** Modeler  
**Era:** Holocene; Early Bronze Age · **Coinciding event:** Urban Revolution; invention of writing


**Overview.** Humans organized around a dense urban center — the first cities and city-states, like Uruk, where far more people mix than anyone could ever know by face. The breakthrough is the written code: laws, records, and accounts kept on clay and tablet rather than in memory.


**Virtuous cycle.** Writing lets a city track and coordinate far more people and goods than memory ever could, and the larger the city grows, the more it leans on the records that hold it together.


### Motivations

_The four base drives — one per cybernetic function._


- **Commitment** (Motivation · Effector) — Commitment is the drive to keep one's word, carried up the symbolic line. In a city it shifts onto contracts and codes: reliability is now owed not just to known neighbors but to written agreements enforceable among strangers.
  
  _Cite:_ [Hill et al.](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.1199071)

- **Stability** (Motivation · Regulator) — Stability is the drive to keep things consistent and in order, carried from the Settlement. A city runs on it at scale: schedules, records, and routines that hold steady so hundreds of thousands of unrelated lives can mesh.

- **Representation** (Motivation · Modeler) — Representation is the drive to be recognized within an institutional order, the new motivation here, replacing autonomy. In a city of strangers, what matters is less your own inner map than your standing on the record: your title, your role, your registered place in an order that includes people you will never meet.

- **Affiliation** (Motivation · Reviser) — Affiliation is the drive to bond and belong, carried up the line. In an anonymous city it narrows to chosen circles, the guild, the congregation, the neighborhood, islands of belonging inside a sea of strangers.


### Tendencies

_Adjacent-function blends (each pairs two neighbouring drives)._


- **Conscientiousness** (Tendency · Effector × Regulator) — Conscientiousness is the trait of being reliable, orderly, and hardworking, carried from the Settlement. City life leans on it through the clock and the calendar: the shift, the deadline, the appointment, all reward the person who is dependable on schedule.
  
  _Cite:_ [Roberts et al.](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1745-6916.2007.00047.x)

- **Extraversion** (Tendency · Regulator × Modeler) — Extraversion is how much a person seeks out and draws energy from social contact, debuting here. The city builds the ecology for it: merchant, lawyer, priest, and soldier are all roles that pay off for approaching and asserting in front of strangers, a context bands and villages rarely offered.

- **Righteousness** (Tendency · Modeler × Reviser) — Righteousness is aligning oneself with the codes as a moral authority, pairing the drive to model with the drive to revise. It is the first moral-identity trait in this slot: where the villager accommodated the known neighbor, the city dweller internalizes the rule itself, judging self and others by the code.
  
  _Cite:_ [Ashton & Lee](https://doi.org/10.1177/1088868306294907)

- **Neuroticism** (Tendency · Reviser × Effector) — Neuroticism is how strongly a person feels threat and setback, pairing the drive to revise with the drive to act. The city offers fresh worries, competition, debt, and judgment by strangers, and a reactive temperament reads those social risks as sharply as older ones read physical danger.


### Signatures

_Cross-function pairings that mark the clade._


- **Self-Evaluation** (Signature · Effector × Modeler) — Self-Evaluation is this level's new coupling on the vertical: a single underlying sense of one's own worth and competence, binding self-esteem, self-efficacy, and emotional steadiness. In a city it collapses to one question, can I hold my institutional position against strangers who want it too, since the reference group is now an institution, not a known band.
  
  _Cite:_ [Judge et al.](https://doi.org/10.1037/0021-9010.83.1.17)

- **Self-Construal** (Signature · Regulator × Reviser) — Self-Construal is the defining coupling on the horizontal, carried from the Settlement: independent versus interdependent self-reading. A city pushes it toward the independent end, where one is a portable individual with a role and a name that travel, recognized by strangers rather than embedded in one fixed web.
  
  _Cite:_ [Talhelm et al.](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.1246850)


### Capacities

_Defined by the one function that is *absent*._


- **Distributing** (Capacity · without Effector) — Set the action drive aside and the other three give distributing: moving surplus through a network of strangers on purpose. Imperial Rome ran the annona, grain shipped from Egypt, inventoried at the port, hauled upriver, and handed free to some 200,000 people on a schedule. Granaries, markets, and supply chains all sit here.
  
  _Cite:_ [Erdkamp](https://search.worldcat.org/title/469657894)

- **Crime** (Capacity · without Regulator) — Drop the stabilizer and the other three give crime: pursuing a goal with the rule-following switched off, viable only because the city created the conditions for it. A band of thirty has no organized theft; everyone watches everyone. Density brings anonymity, surplus brings targets, and gaps in oversight let code-breaking pay.
  
  _Cite:_ [Glaeser](https://search.worldcat.org/title/650211168)

- **Farming** (Capacity · without Modeler) — With no central planner, the city still rests on the settlement's storing-and-accumulating engine, now feeding many who never farm. The grain one household once stored for winter becomes the surplus that funds scribes, soldiers, and priests who never touch a field.

- **Standardizing** (Capacity · without Reviser) — With revision off, the city holds together by making things uniform: one set of weights, measures, coins, and bricks everyone uses. A shekel means the same across the city and a brick is the same size in every wall, so strangers can transact and build without renegotiating the units each time.
  
  _Cite:_ Powell


### Breakthrough

_The framework-center concept unlocked at this rung._


- **Codes** (Breakthrough (framework center)) — Codes are the breakthrough here: written, portable, enforceable rules that coordinate strangers who share no face-to-face history. The first writing at Uruk is not poetry but accounting, ledgers of grain and beer rations. A city is too big for everyone to know everyone, so the rule on the tablet does the work that watching neighbors did in a village.
  
  _Cite:_ [Scott](https://search.worldcat.org/title/990684513)

## 12. Empire-Human  

**Line:** symbolic · **Date:** ~3 KYA · **Function introduced:** Reviser  
**Era:** Holocene; Iron Age · **Coinciding event:** Late Bronze Age Collapse; Iron Age; Axial Age universalizing ideology


**Overview.** Humans bound together across many cities and peoples under one overarching frame — territorial empires held together not only by force but by shared stories: religions, ideologies, and myths that let strangers across vast distances count themselves as one people.


**Virtuous cycle.** A story anyone can join lets an empire unite people no shared kinship or single city ever could, and the wider it spreads the more powerful and self-reinforcing the story becomes.


### Motivations

_The four base drives — one per cybernetic function._


- **Commitment** (Motivation · Effector) — Commitment is the drive to keep one's word, carried the whole length of the symbolic line. At empire scale it attaches to the abstract order itself: the oath of loyalty, the duty to the state or the faith, binding the individual to something far larger than any neighbor.
  
  _Cite:_ [Hill et al.](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.1199071)

- **Stability** (Motivation · Regulator) — Stability is the drive to keep things consistent and in order, carried from the Settlement. An empire demands it across territories that never meet, through uniform law, currency, and administration holding steady from one edge of the realm to the other.

- **Representation** (Motivation · Modeler) — Representation is the drive to be recognized within an institutional order, carried from the City. In an empire it scales to the abstract: the citizen, the subject, the believer, a registered place in an order spanning peoples one will never see.

- **Universality** (Motivation · Reviser) — Universality is the drive to belong to something vast and impersonal, the new motivation that closes the symbolic line, replacing face-to-face affiliation. An empire-scale person identifies not with people they can see but with an imagined order, Rome, the faith, the cause, that feels cosmically legitimate and binds millions who will never meet.
  
  _Cite:_ [Anderson](https://search.worldcat.org/title/71266698)


### Tendencies

_Adjacent-function blends (each pairs two neighbouring drives)._


- **Conscientiousness** (Tendency · Effector × Regulator) — Conscientiousness is the trait of being reliable, orderly, and hardworking, carried up the symbolic line. An empire of records and offices rewards it heavily: the bureaucracy, the tax roll, and the standing army all run on people dependable across long chains of duty.
  
  _Cite:_ [Roberts et al.](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1745-6916.2007.00047.x)

- **Extraversion** (Tendency · Regulator × Modeler) — Extraversion is how much a person seeks and draws energy from social contact, carried from the City. Imperial life rewards it in courts, assemblies, and far-flung postings, wherever advancement runs through working a room of strangers.

- **Integrity** (Tendency · Modeler × Reviser) — Integrity is honesty and fairness even when unwatched, debuting here, where warmth toward the known and alignment with the code give way to principle held for its own sake. It rises across adult life and matters most where defection is both more tempting and more visible, exactly the empire's condition.
  
  _Cite:_ [Ashton & Lee](https://doi.org/10.1177/1088868306294907)

- **Intellect** (Tendency · Reviser × Effector) — Intellect is openness to ideas, abstraction, and inquiry, the last trait to arrive, replacing Neuroticism in this slot. It needs imperial conditions, scribal culture, philosophy, contact between civilizations, to assemble: tellingly, of the big personality dimensions, this is the one that fails to show up reliably across the world's languages, as if not yet a universal human baseline.
  
  _Cite:_ [De Raad et al.](https://doi.org/10.1037/a0017184)


### Signatures

_Cross-function pairings that mark the clade._


- **Self-Evaluation** (Signature · Effector × Modeler) — Self-Evaluation is the defining coupling on the vertical, carried from the City: one underlying sense of one's worth and competence. At empire scale it is measured against an abstract order, whether one is succeeding or failing by the standards of a vast institution rather than a face-to-face crowd.
  
  _Cite:_ [Judge et al.](https://doi.org/10.1037/0021-9010.83.1.17)

- **Identity** (Signature · Regulator × Reviser) — Identity is this level's new coupling on the horizontal, replacing self-construal: labeling oneself by a vast imagined category, Roman, Han, Muslim, Brahmin. These are cosmological, not face-to-face: the referent community is imagined, and the self-label is carried by the cultural memory that outlasts any living chain of talk.
  
  _Cite:_ [Assmann & Czaplicka](https://doi.org/10.2307/488538)


### Capacities

_Defined by the one function that is *absent*._


- **Stories** (Capacity · without Effector) — Set the action drive aside and the other three give stories: the shared narratives, myths, and ideologies an empire builds and keeps so that strangers who will never meet count themselves one people. Because an empire outlasts any living memory, the story is carried in sacred texts, monuments, and law rather than in talk — a community imagined into being and maintained on the page. Around 800 to 200 BCE, in several civilizations not in contact, universalizing ethical frameworks arose at once.
  
  _Cite:_ [Anderson](https://search.worldcat.org/title/71266698)

- **Extracting** (Capacity · without Regulator) — Drop the stabilizer and the other three give extracting: building systems to pull resources out of conquered land and people. The Spanish ran the Potosí silver mountain for three centuries, forcing roughly 14,000 laborers a year into the mines, and the silver flowed across the world. Oil, lithium, and cobalt run the same template today.
  
  _Cite:_ [Bakewell](https://search.worldcat.org/title/10779199)

- **Conquering** (Capacity · without Modeler) — With no central planner of the whole, the empire still runs the band's accumulating engine turned inside out: not pooling but seizing. In 80 years the Mongols took roughly a quarter of the world's land, concentrating grain, gold, and captives at the center. Where foragers shared meat to spread risk, empires hoard what others accumulated.
  
  _Cite:_ [Mann](https://search.worldcat.org/title/59246575)

- **Standardizing** (Capacity · without Reviser) — With revision off, the empire holds together by enforcing one standard across peoples who never meet. When Qin Shi Huang unified China in 221 BCE he standardized the script, cart axles, weights, and coins, not inventing them but forcing one version across a territory the size of a country, backed by inspectors and governors.
  
  _Cite:_ [Lewis](https://search.worldcat.org/title/436034177)


### Breakthrough

_The framework-center concept unlocked at this rung._


- **Trust** (Breakthrough (framework center)) — Trust is the breakthrough here: a stranger hands another stranger something that matters and expects it to come back. An empire must extend reliability to people who share no kinship, language, or local history, and it does so through universalizing frameworks — a currency, a law, a faith, a shared identity — that supply the trust kinship cannot. Coin, contract, and creed each let reliance travel past the reach of anyone you will ever meet.
  
  _Cite:_ [Seabright](https://search.worldcat.org/title/52639182)
