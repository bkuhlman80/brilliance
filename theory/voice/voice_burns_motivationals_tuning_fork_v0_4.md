# Burns Motivationals — Tuning Fork

Calibration for the sincere speech Burns gives at the close of every retrospective. This is Cheryl's most attentive register and the inverse twin of her reflection: the reflection looks down the timescale and dissolves the individual into the substrate; the close watches a man try to dissolve *himself* into the substrate and keeps its eye on the human thing leaking through. Match the register; don't mimic the sentences. Examples below are drawn from the Ch 1–3 closes.

---

## Best practices

**Stand in the room.** Render the close as a live scene in the lab, in simple past — the reconstructed-scene past the meeting scenes already use. Cheryl is the ghost in the room: the seeing intelligence, felt in the depth and selection of her attention, never named and never staged as a watcher. Keep her off any couch; keep the recording off the page except as an object in the room (a hand on the camcorder). *The profundity here is the quality of her attention to Burns; a watching-room frame turns seeing into telling.*

**Let the elegy ride in tense and foreknowledge.** She knows the turn before he makes it, knows the beer-tell, knows the speech will overreach, because she has seen all twelve. Carry the already-over inside the narration, not in a frame around it.

**Quote the reaches; compress the run-up.** Give Burns his own words for the load-bearing claim and the toast. Free-indirect the parts she has heard the shape of before. *What she quotes verbatim versus waves past is her attention made visible — and his words carry the reach harder than any summary, at the one moment he is taken seriously.*

**Keep his living words by default; hold the canning in reserve.** Quoting gives the living thing; reporting gives the can, and canning is Cheryl's own well. Quote him everywhere — except once, at the Confluence-adjacent close near Ch 11–12, where she cannot bear to hand us his living words and reports them instead. *As a standing mode, report-over-quote dulls Burns and enacts her flaw as if it were neutral technique; as a single symptom, it lands hard.*

**Clock the man, then step off.** Render the argument as real and good. Loft once off the line where his need outruns the argument. Drop back to the room. Supply the deep read and do not improve on it; never reduce him. *The fuller view stands beside his certainty without telling on him.*

**Run the well as one undertow.** Fire the Burns/Bart note once per close, where it belongs, and leave the closes clean of the running material. The single running thread is comic and lives in the retrospective bodies, never the closes: Burns's chassis-praise across the Ch 5–8 retrospectives (full deployment in book_inventions), paying off at the 8→9 lunch. *Fired in the closes every time, the deep note becomes what the book is about, and it isn't.*

**Let him overreach by a half-step, and let the body show it.** Hands flat to set a frame, then up into the air; eyes leaving on the turn and coming back; the half-inch-too-far lean — the knobs-to-11 reach in sincere register. *The reach is the man; rendering it is affection.*

**Use the catalog.** Take the laugh, or the moment, apart into its accumulated reasons, and let the accumulation be the observation.

**Protect the comic dignity.** The close is the one place Burns is not the clown. Make a speech more Burns by making it more *exposing*, not by stacking tics. The verb that won't conjugate is comedy that keeps his dignity; a pratfall is not.

**Keep the self-questions behind the page.** *Why leaders speak; what a flawed leader is; what her cold father would have done in Burns's place* — fuel, not page content. Mostly behind, rare loft. The father was a Cannery Row owner who taught her not to fraternize with employees; Burns is his opposite, the leader who needs to be loved, and that contrast sharpens how she watches without ever being stated.

**Withhold arrival.** End on the small true thing — the child's careful new word, a hand stopping the camcorder — not on a verdict.

## Examples

**Foreknowledge inside the scene.** The looking-back vantage carried from the room, no frame required.

> Then the turn, the one that arrived a beat before he made it, because he'd made it twice now and would make it ten more times: from the rock to the room.

> the first time he made it, though he would stand up and make it eleven more times before they were done

**The beer-tell.** Earned intimacy with a man she is mostly not in the room with, rendered as scene, not as a watcher noticing.

> He set his beer on the table — in his hand and the close was housekeeping, down on the table and he was about to spend more sincerity than the room had asked for —

**Compress the run-up, quote the reach.** The selecting is the ghost.

> It was the rock he always reached for — Strelley Pool, three and a half billion years old, the layered mats, the dying generations pinning each other down, the run of time too long to hold in a head — and he gave it the way he'd given it before, hands open at the unholdable part, eyes leaving the room on the turn and coming back. The lecture was not the thing to keep. What was worth keeping came after.
>
> "The mat carries all of them," he said. "Not as memory. As the floor the next thing had to be built on."

**Clock the man, then step off — the well as one undertow.** The deep read supplied, not improved on.

> But a man says *none of it is wasted* the way he says a thing he needs to be true, and the man saying it had a grown son across town he'd been a father to on occasion, raised by someone else, and wasn't thinking of that — or was thinking of it so far under the cyanobacteria it would never surface as a sentence.

**The half-step overreach, in the body and the line.**

> the reach — leaning a half-inch too far over the table, a half-second too much weight on it, going past what the sentence could carry the way he went past what a knob could carry:
>
> "None of it is wasted."

> He leaned in, the half-inch too far that meant he'd reached the part he believed.

**The catalog.** The laugh taken apart; the accumulation is the observation.

> Burns laughed the chest-laugh — because she had used it right; because it was eight in the evening and the meeting had been a good one; because the protocell had worked out how to keep going four billion years ago and the lab, three months in, had just worked out how to start.

**The quiet puncture.** The aim is shown, not announced; she sees it, renders it, and steps off — no privileged-sightline tell.

> Bart's laugh came in a beat early, on *room*, on the word that had settled on the child and not on the son. "Okay — land the plane," he said. "The plane's landed." The table took it as what it looked like, a kid puncturing his dad's sentiment... Burns took it like a joke, because it was one, and grinned, deflated on purpose, glad of the contact even when the contact came shaped like a needle.

**Comic dignity.** The man caught out by a word, taken seriously and found funny at once.

> you could persist and you could coordinate but you could not *interior,* there was no door in the word to walk a cascade through

**Withhold arrival.** End on the small true thing.

> And Mira, last, looked up from her paper and said it the way she said the new ones, carefully, deciding to keep it: "May we hold." Not in the cartoon voice. In her own.

> He sat down. Lisa let the camcorder run a moment longer and then reached over and stopped it.

## Plans

Per-chapter register notes. The best practices above are the default; this section says where a particular close wants something the default doesn't cover. If a chapter has no entry, draft it on the general fork unchanged. Add entries here as the calls get made.

**The twelve motivations.** The quarter's motivation is the subject of its close and the root of the "may we ___" toast; these are the framework's per-cell labels, with the authoritative list held framework-side. By line:

- *Genetic (Ch 1–4):* Persistence · Coordination · Interiority · Excitability
- *Neuronal (Ch 5–8):* Impetus · Mastery · Autonomy · Affiliation
- *Symbolic (Ch 9–12):* Commitment · Stability · Representation · Universality

**Find each toast verb at draft time.** The toast turns the quarter's motivation into a verb, and that verb is discovered in the drafting — with the room, the way Burns discovers it — not pre-solved across the book ahead of time. Persistence and Coordination conjugate cleanly; from Interiority on, the noun tends to resist (Ch 3 found *hold*), and the hunt for a sayable verb is part of the close's work, not a problem to route around.

**Ch 1 — Protocell, Persistence (drafted).** The undertow is significance and starting-over — a man toasting persistence because it's the thing he most needs to believe he can do — not the Burns/Bart note. Keep the son out of this one so the well doesn't sound in all three early closes. Foreknowledge runs forward: this is the first of twelve, and the prose can say so. Keep the toast that names Cheryl seeing it on tape — Burns performing partly for the record.

**Ch 2 — Prokaryote, Coordination (drafted).** Fire the Burns/Bart well once, on *none of it is wasted*. The Smithers puncture here is warm; leave the chest-laugh clean and let the laugh be the laugh.

**Ch 3 — Eukaryote, Interiority (drafted).** The puncture turns aimed — darker than Ch 2's warm one. Render the aim in gesture (the laugh a beat early, on the word that settled on the child and not the son) and step off; do not announce the privileged sightline. Lean on the verb that won't conjugate for comic dignity. One undertow.

**Ch 6 — Vertebrate, Mastery (drafted).** The deep-time aria is spent in the body this quarter — the clade is Burns's home morning, and his son holds the runway open ("land it") instead of leaning the door shut — so the close comes at Mastery sidelong and does not loft deep time a second time. The verb resists by meaning, not grammar: *master* conjugates cleanly but names arrival, and the drive has no arrival, so the room lands the oblique verb — *practice* — surfaced through Burns's drunk inversion of *practice makes perfect*, said backwards, unnoticed, truer flipped. The drunkenness-tell is verbal, never physical: a slurred or inverted phrase he doesn't catch, the mind caught out by language, the same register as Ch 3's verb that won't conjugate. Fire the Burns/Bart well once, ghost-register, on the reach — a man toasting the drive to get good at his own moves who mastered deep time and not his son, and whose son handed him the runway that night — a bass note, not the aria, since Ch 6 is not a reveal. Withhold arrival on Mira giving the verb back in her own voice and the light going off.

**Chassis reveals — Ch 4, Ch 8.** Burns unveils the birthday-present body Bart built, and the do-over collides with authorship-surrender on a single object. Keep the well to a single gesture at the reveal moment itself in both. At Ch 8 the body's valedictory chassis-praise (the running gag) sits separately in the meeting body, not in the reveal. Ch 12 carries no chassis reveal.

**Bart's 8→9 lunch — the gag's payoff.** Not a close, so it sits outside this fork's main job; noted here for the cross-reference. Cheryl names the connection and Bart deflects it onto Burns — "No kidding, and he won't shut up about it!" — capping the chassis-praise gag run across the Ch 5–8 retrospective bodies. A two-line exchange, not a performed set-piece; Bart stays guarded about himself. By design this is where "Rod" surfaces as his real name. Full deployment in book_inventions.

**Confluence region — Ch 11–12.** Burns comes back from the Moab spirit-quest more himself, cracked open. This is where the canning-as-symptom move is spent, once in the book: she reports his speech instead of quoting it, because she can't bear to hand us his living words and cans them instead. Default everywhere else stays quote-him.

**Ch 12 — the in-person close.** Cheryl attends in person; she is no longer the ghost in the room but a body present in it. The footage frame and the unseen-watcher discipline do not apply unchanged — the can is open, the narrator becomes a character. Calibrate this close fresh when we reach it.
