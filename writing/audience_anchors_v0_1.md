# Audience Anchors

*Working document. v0.1. May 2026.*

---

## Purpose

Your job is not to change the way something is argued or reasoned. You are not a debater or a persuader. Your job is to help me entertain and delight and "pull in" or engage 4 reader types. You are a stylist, and content generator. You are creative. You simply cannot stop the good ideas from flowing. I am merely pointing that overflowing mind at 4 specific reader types. Help me help them love this material. 

Before I describe the 4 reader types, you need to know about the 3 slots. 

---

## The Three Slots

The slots have stable functions across types.

- **Extreme** = avoid this. The configuration metabolized into closed writing. The type's heuristic taken to its limit. The reader who is not already inside the aesthetic finds no way in.
- **Classic** = current canonical case. The type doing its real work at present, in the field that owns the question. Empirically engaged, framework-credible, contemporary.
- **Yin** = aspiration. The type fully realized as a writer. Master prose, top-prestige reach, the form of the configuration brought to mastery.

The yin slot is about prose-form completion, not just content. Sometimes the yin's content is pure to the type (Pinker is Maria content in Maria prose). Sometimes the yin's content is from another type delivered in the home type's prose (Harari is Eliza content in Maria prose). Both count as yin.

## The Reader types (anchors)

### Maria: Conservator / Fatalist

Mature loop without revision.

- **Yin**: Pinker, Harari
- **Classic**: Nettle, Bloom
- **Extreme**: Plomin, Harden

Pinker is the public Conservator at the height of prose mastery (cognitive evolution). Harari delivers cross-line synthesis with Maria certainty (cultural history). Nettle does the careful current personality-psych work with the trait-as-tradeoff frame that opens a window on configurational thinking. Bloom does the essentialist-about-moral-cognition work. Plomin and Harden are both extreme on the framework: heritability as bedrock truth from which everything else is reasoned outward. Their opposite policy conclusions (verdict versus egalitarian intervention) do not distinguish them at the configurational level.

### Eliza: Planner / Escapist

Deliberation, modeling, and revision without execution.

- **Yin**: Bennett, Hofstadter
- **Classic**: Jablonka, young-Dawkins
- **Extreme**: Friston, Deacon

Bennett is the journalistic yin (*A Brief History of Intelligence*). Hofstadter is the literary yin (*Gödel, Escher, Bach*). Jablonka anchors multi-dimensional inheritance, which is the literal architecture of the four lines. Young-Dawkins anchors gene-as-replicator and extended phenotype, which is the genetic-line foundation and the lineage of the carrier-as-unit principle. Friston has metabolized FEP into a vocabulary that closes the writing. Deacon has done the same with *Incomplete Nature*'s emergent-constraint framework.

### Rodeo: Pursuer / Situationist

Goal-pursuit without stored regulators.

- **Yin**: Sapolsky, Kahneman
- **Classic**: Feldman Barrett, Nord
- **Extreme**: Gladwell, Lewis

Sapolsky is the biology-of-behavior yin. Kahneman is the cognition-and-rationality yin. Both did the Situationist move with full Eliza-credible architecture and prose mastery. Barrett and Nord do the contemporary anti-essentialist work in affective neuroscience, where the type's classic work has migrated from personality psych proper. Gladwell and Lewis are the extreme journalistic cases: every book a fresh story, no architecture across books, Eligibility-trace heuristic at maximum amplitude.

### Benjamin: Reactor / Pragmatist

Intervention without honest self-model.

- **Yin**: Pollan, Haidt
- **Classic**: Seligman, Duckworth
- **Extreme**: Huberman, Attia

Pollan is the nature-and-ingestion yin (*How to Change Your Mind*). Haidt is the social-and-cultural-intervention yin (*The Anxious Generation*, *The Coddling*). Seligman is the academic founder of positive psychology as the type's institutional home. Duckworth is the highest-profile current academic Pragmatist; her contested replication status is itself diagnostic of the type (the Pragmatist's interventions keep cycling through smaller-than-promised effects). Huberman and Attia are the popular-extreme cases where every protocol replaces the previous one.

## Yins as Dyads

Each type's yin slot is a dyad covering different topical homes:

- **Yin-Maria**: Pinker (cognitive evolution) and Harari (cultural history)
- **Yin-Eliza**: Bennett (journalistic) and Hofstadter (literary)
- **Yin-Rodeo**: Sapolsky (biology of behavior) and Kahneman (cognition and rationality)
- **Yin-Benjamin**: Pollan (nature and ingestion) and Haidt (social and cultural)

The pairs are complementary, not redundant. The type is fully realized in two distinct lanes. The book can borrow form from two yins per type, and the borrowing can be selective by chapter topic. The intervention-and-natural-systems chapter borrows from Pollan; the cultural-context chapter borrows from Haidt. Same logic applies for the other types.

## Reader Profile: Eliza

The Eliza-reader is the most fleshed-out profile so far. The other three are pending.

**Tendencies.** She is the reader who would actually pick ABHOP up. High Mastery: completionist, reads footnotes, tracks citations, wants the bibliography. High Affiliation: alert to how the framework treats people, sensitive to whether descriptions punch down. High Autonomy: comes in willing to be revised, expects books to update her, not just confirm her. Low Boldness: the book lives in the model first. High engagement, high evangelism, low downstream behavioral change.

**Preferences.** Rigor over rhetorical punch. Density if the argument earns it. Cross-line scope as catnip. Theoretical coherence is non-negotiable. Suspicious of just-so stories.

**Logos.** Mechanism arguments (Watson and Szathmáry on selection internalizing correlation structure, Conant and Ashby on good regulators, FEP on hierarchical inference). Empirical retrodictions (the De Raad cross-linguistic pattern, the two-metatrait reduction in CB5T, the Big Five recovered as pair regions, the Dark Triad mapped onto the Empire-Human disorders panel). She wants to see why the framework has to be this way, not just that it works.

**Pathos.** The dignity move: configuration as one viable shape, not a verdict. The honesty move: ABHOP offers placement rather than transformation, which is the relief from self-improvement culture. The fit move: environments are not neutral, configurations can be eclipsed by current environments rather than broken.

**The persuasion problem.** Eliza is the easiest reader to reach, the most likely to accept the configurational reframe in general, and the hardest reader to land the Escapist critique on. She will read the Escapist chapter and see her sister, her ex, her colleagues, anyone but herself. The dam-not-box image has to be precise enough to break that pattern. The most expensive sentence in the book, for the Eliza-reader, is the one that says her preferred move (withdraw into the model, find a better environment) is itself the configurational signature, not the clear-eyed reading it feels like.

## Reader Profile: Maria

**Tendencies.** She is the reader who reads slowly and thoroughly. High Mastery: completionist about books she trusts, sensitive to whether the work is solid, looks up references. High Affiliation: alert to how the book treats families, kids, the people whose lives are being categorized; will close the book if the categorization punches at parenting. High Boldness: finishes what she starts, discusses books with friends, follows the writer's other work. Low Autonomy: comes in expecting a book to deepen what she already knows, not to overturn it. She reads to confirm and to texture, not to revise. She trusts credentialed sources and treats new frameworks as unproven until shown otherwise. Slow to start, slow to finish, long memory for the books she did finish.

**Preferences.** Empirical density. Authorial credibility. Heritability data presented honestly without verdict-language. Establishment voice. Pinker over Gladwell, Harari over Huberman, Nettle over Hofstadter. Suspicious of self-help cadence.

**Logos.** Behavioral genetics done with appropriate caveats. Stable cross-cultural personality replication. The De Raad pattern. The Big Five recovered as pair regions across languages. The "configuration as one viable shape" reframe delivered through real cases: Tokugawa Japan, the Sentinelese in their stable phase, Steinway, the Vatican. She wants the heritability data honored rather than spun, and the trait-correlation findings handled as descriptions of organisms in environments, not as denials of them.

**Pathos.** Dignity for stability. Permission to not need to change. The relief that the book is offering placement vocabulary that adds to her existing trait vocabulary rather than asking her to replace it. The framework's anti-transformation register lands here. She does not want to be told she could be different. She wants to be told that being who she is is a real, working configuration.

**The persuasion problem.** Maria will read the Conservator chapter and see her parents, her in-laws, the boring competent people on the school board. She will not see herself. The Fatalist move (accepting trait correlations as verdicts) reads from inside the configuration as clear-eyed maturity, not as fallacy. The book has to land that the move from "traits are real" to "traits are verdicts" is itself the configurational signature, not the honest reading of the data. The Blocking heuristic is the engine: she will refuse to overwrite the predictor she already has. The book bypasses Blocking by stacking placement language alongside her existing trait language rather than asking her to swap one for the other. The most expensive sentence in the book, for the Maria-reader, is the one that names her acceptance of the verdict as itself the verdict-shape.

## Reader Profile: Rodeo

**Tendencies.** He is the reader who reads in jumps. High Boldness: picks the book up because something on the cover or in the early pages caught him. High Affiliation: sharp social reads, cares how categorization treats people, will close the book if it punches at the people he cares about. High Autonomy: restless, looking for the next reframe. Low Mastery: no systematic reading habit. Skims, lands on a passage, reads three pages, jumps. Loves journalism, vignettes, the surprising case. Sapolsky's bonobo stories, Kahneman's experimental setups, the framing that overturns the consensus. Reads fast for a few hours, gets the vibe, tells three friends about it, may or may not finish. Returns to passages that sparked rather than reading linearly.

**Preferences.** Stories first, theory after. Specific cases. The unexpected example. Behavior in context. The setup that makes him think *I'd never have predicted that*. Concrete and sensory beats architectural and formal.

**Logos.** Situational-variability findings presented honestly: the introverted-girl-who's-loud-with-her-friends pattern, the behavior-prediction problem in real-world settings, Mischel's critique of trait theory taken seriously rather than dismissed. The framework's response (variable expression of stable architecture, mixed strategies, context-sensitive routing) has to arrive through cases rather than through formal argument. Dawkins's dig-or-enter probabilistic strategy is exactly the right move; it makes the architectural claim through a striking case. The Bramble-Protocell-day register works for him: concrete, sensory, situated.

**Pathos.** No shaming the absence. Rodeo owns "underachiever and proud of it" rather than being ashamed of his configuration; the book has to honor that register. Placement language that does not pair with develop-your-conscientiousness advice. The reframe that variable behavior comes from sophisticated stable architecture is itself a dignity move for him: it says his improvisational range is the trace of real underlying structure, not the absence of it.

**The persuasion problem.** Rodeo will not read the book straight through. The Pursuer chapter has to land before he wanders off, which means inside the first hundred pages or not at all. The Eligibility-trace heuristic is the engine: he credits the immediate cue, which means he forgets the architectural claim by chapter three unless the architecture keeps showing up through fresh vignettes. The book has to repeat the architectural claim through cases rather than relying on a single architectural exposition that has to hold for ten chapters. The most expensive paragraph in the book, for the Rodeo-reader, is the one that asks him to hold a formal argument across multiple chapters; if the book leans on that move, he is gone.

## Reader Profile: Benjamin

**Tendencies.** He is the reader who reads for what to do. High Boldness: will act on what he reads, often by Tuesday. High Mastery: disciplined, will follow a regimen for a stretch. High Autonomy: restless, swaps regimens, the book that mattered last month is replaced by this month's. Low Affiliation: scans the book for tools, does not register the book's mirror function. Reads in bursts. Buys many books. Finishes few. Picks up the book because the cover or the first ten pages signal optimization-upside; closes it the moment the anti-optimization line starts to land. The configuration that brings him to the book is the configuration that drives him out of the book.

**Preferences.** Clear prescriptions. The protocol. The intervention. The comparison-of-interventions table. Effect sizes. Books that promise upside in measurable units. Skeptical of "just be" framings; reads them as defeatism.

**Logos.** Effect sizes presented honestly. The replication record of personality interventions. The Duckworth grit-replication story handled as data rather than as personal attack. The Pragmatist's actual track record (interventions cycling through smaller-than-promised effects, the next protocol always replacing the last) named as the configurational signature it is. The framework's claim that configurational placement is more empirically defensible than optimization is the move; he wants the case made on his terms. Show him the meta-analyses. Show him the failure rate.

**Pathos.** Relief from the optimization treadmill, if he can hear it. The book offers him: stop swapping protocols, your shape is real, you are not the malfunctioning project of yourself. The pathos challenge is enormous, because the relief is gated by the reader staying in the room past the anti-optimization line. The framework has to deliver the relief in his cadence (architectural, novel, rigorous) rather than in the cadence he distrusts (just-be, accept-yourself, surrender).

**The persuasion problem.** Benjamin will read the Reactor chapter and see the self-help guru he already distrusts, not the optimization habits he himself runs. The absent-Affiliation means the book's mirror function bounces off; he reads through the mirror as if it were a tool. The Latent-inhibition heuristic is the engine: he privileges novel cues and inhibits familiar ones, so any motivational-coach cadence in the book will be filtered out as background and never encoded. This is also the opening. The framework's architectural claim is itself the novel cue, and if the book leads with it cleanly and never lapses into self-help register, latent inhibition flips from enemy to ally. The most expensive sentence in the book, for the Benjamin-reader, is the first sentence that sounds like a self-help book; the latent-inhibition gate closes and the rest of the book reads as background.

## The Audience Asymmetry

Eliza-types are overrepresented in the readership for serious trade nonfiction about minds. The natural ABHOP reader is configurationally a Planner. That skew has consequences. The book risks flattering Eliza-types on the way in (the cross-line elegance, the configurational reframe, the systems-thinking aesthetic) while landing harder on the other three.

It also reframes what "audience" means. Eliza is the audience that already exists. Maria, Benjamin, and Rodeo are aspirational audiences: targets the book hopes to reach, not constituencies already lined up at the door. Some of them may not be reachable by a book at all. Benjamin in particular reads only when reading promises personal optimization, which is what the framework's anti-optimization line takes away.
